# 시즈야마 마시로 인연 스토리

- 등장: 시즈야마 마시로, 선생(샬레)

1. **「시즈야마 마시로 인연 스토리 1」**

본문 대사는 수록되어 있지 않다.

2. **「시즈야마 마시로 인연 스토리 2」**

- **시즈야마 마시로**: Oh, Sensei. It's good to see you again.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Oh, my skirt? I borrowed a sewing kit from Hasumi so I could stitch it up.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: The seam might rip again if I move too much... It will have to do until the new one arrives.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I would borrow one, but Hasumi's would never fit, and Tsurugi's are always covered in the blood of her enemies.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Getting replacement Justice Task Force uniforms can be a real pain, even within Trinity.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Are you curious as to our uniforms are black, whereas the regular students wear white?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: It's so the blood we spill of our unrighteous enemies—like those from Gehenna—won't be noticeable on our uniforms. Again, take a good look at Tsurugi's uniform.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I can slaughter my enemies without worry so long as I'm wearing this uniform.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: That...was supposed to be a joke. Did I push it too far?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I'm well aware that I have not been gifted with a sense of humor. People tend to be terrorized whenever I crack jokes.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I wonder why that is.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: That is one possibility. Heh...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I should clarify that we do not wear black uniforms for fear of bloodstains.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Our club places importance on hygiene. If blood is to get on our uniform during a mission, we're to quickly wash it off.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: No, I was being serious...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: There's a price to pay for attaining justice after all. Haha...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Hmm. When I'm joking, you take it seriously, and when I'm serious, you think I'm joking.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I really don't have any sense of humor, do I?

3. **「시즈야마 마시로 인연 스토리 3」**

- **시즈야마 마시로**: Justice... It rolls off the tongue so easily. Is there any better word in existence?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: The Justice Task Force has always striven to uphold justice in Kivotos, and those who commit injustice are thoroughly punished.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I once fought believing everything my upperclassmen did was just and that the opposite was true for our enemies from Gehenna...but lately, I've had doubts.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Th-That isn't to say I've lost faith in my upperclassmen!
- **시즈야마 마시로**: However, once they graduate, the Justice Task Force will be left to me, and I'll have to use my own judgment to determine what is just.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: To do so, I need to have a clear understanding of what justice is.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: So I went out and picked up a copy of this best selling classic: "Justice: It's the Right Thing to Do".
- **시즈야마 마시로**: It was everything I hoped it would be. It covered several examples of justice and injustice and how they applied to several situations.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: For example, bullies who torment others are unjust, whereas doctors who save others from painful disease are just. Delicious food that brings joy to people is just, but bizarre food that causes stomach pain is unjust.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Everything felt so clear and straightforward. It spoke to my personal sense of justice.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: But a few of the examples I still struggle to understand due to my inexperience.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Would you mind listening to them and telling me what the right thing to do is?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Okay. Here's the first example.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: "You are a train engineer. One day, you see five people tied to the track you're about to enter."
- **시즈야마 마시로**: "If you switch to another track, you can save the five people, but as a result, you will hit one other person. What, in this case, would be the right thing to do?"
- **시즈야마 마시로**: It's difficult to answer. Switching to another track would be the right thing if I'm trying to save more people, but doing so requires sacrificing someone else who was otherwise not in harm's way.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: What's more important? Saving lives or not willfully harming another?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: It says...that this is a thought experiment, so there is no answer.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Justice without an answer? What nonsense.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Justice should always have a clear, singular answer...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Hmm. Is there no way to save them all?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I can't accept that answer, even if it goes against the spirit of the scenario.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I would do my best to save all of them, no matter what it took.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: That is the way of justice!

4. **「시즈야마 마시로 인연 스토리 4」**

- **시즈야마 마시로**: It's said snipers are soldiers of opportunity. Patience being a virtue is no truer for anyone than it is for a sniper.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Once you have your target, you can't move a centimeter until the enemy enters the line of fire.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: No matter what temptations or aches you may face!
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Of course. I've had to skip several meals before due to being on a mission.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I hardly think so. It simply comes with the territory.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I also wouldn't go so far as to call myself "incredible".
- **시즈야마 마시로**: In fact, there is one thing that tests even my patience when I'm on a mission.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: The, um...bugs.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Now that the weather is warmer, there are more bugs on campus and on the roof. They crawl up my arms when I'm in position.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: It's a creepy feeling, and they affect my aim.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Of course not! You have to control every action you take when you're in position—even your breathing. A few bugs is no excuse to break concentration.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: It would be a disgrace to any good sniper!
- **시즈야마 마시로**: But I haven't trained myself well enough to not have them bother me.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: That's why I wanted to ask you for help.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I'm going to stand still and try to maintain my composure. I'd like for you to tickle me the way the bugs do.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: All right. I'm ready whenever.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: *breathes in*
- **시즈야마 마시로**: This is nothing. It will take more to break my composure.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Gah. My face? No. An elite sniper would not let this faze her...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Ahhh! Wh-What are you doing, Sensei?!
- **시즈야마 마시로**: You can't just tickle my neck for the sake of training!
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I thought...you stood for justice, Sensei!

5. **「시즈야마 마시로 인연 스토리 5」**

- **시즈야마 마시로**: Strength is the key to today's training session!
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Most people are surprised to hear snipers undergo muscle training. They think all we do is sit around and wait for our target.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: While it is true that we spend a lot of time sitting, we also have to climb up and down a lot of stairs in order to find the perfect location.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Sensei, would you like to try carrying my rifle?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Correct. The average sniper rifle weighs in excess of 20 kilos.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: It takes extraordinary fitness to be able to run up and down stairs with it in tow.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: So for today, I'm going to climb 30 flights of stairs in five minutes!
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I'll carry the gun. Would you mind carrying the cartridge as you follow me?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: This is no time to be lazy!

6. **「시즈야마 마시로 인연 스토리 6」**

- **시즈야마 마시로**: Hmm. They're late. They should be passing by any minute now...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: But I can't let my guard down. I'll only have a split second to get the perfect shot on the target!
- **시즈야마 마시로**: What? Do you think it's cruel to kill a target in a single shot? Even if they're guilty of terrible crimes?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: You needn't worry. This gun is loaded with tranquilizers that will only put the target to sleep.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: As soon as they're out, we'll take them into custody and request an investigation from President Tsurugi.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: ― Whew... It's unseasonably hot today.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: ― I can handle heat like this if that's what it takes to get the perfect shot.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: ― But I do hope...that I don't get too sweaty.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: ― I envy the other students' white uniforms on hot summer days like this.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: ― Sensei. You're staring at me.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: ― Shouldn't you keep your eyes out for the target?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Huh? A call from the Justice Task Force...?
- **Justice Task Force Member**: Mashiro? Where are you and what are you doing right now?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Hasumi told me yesterday that the head of the Black Market would be passing by soon... I've been waiting at a nearby position.
- **Justice Task Force Member**: Oh, dear. I'm afraid you've been wasting your time.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Huh? What do you mean?
- **Justice Task Force Member**: President Tsurugi arrested that criminal herself just last night.
- **Justice Task Force Member**: You know how she is. Always going off on her own.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Then...I've been doing all of this for nothing?
- **Justice Task Force Member**: You can return to the clubroom now. Sorry for the misunderstanding.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: *sigh* Understood.

7. **「시즈야마 마시로 인연 스토리 7」**

- **시즈야마 마시로**: Sensei? What are you doing here?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: You happened to see me while you were walking down the street?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I see. I must have been too preoccupied to notice.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: You want to know if this is a sniper mission? No, this is just how I like to pass the time.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: You can see a lot of the city from up here.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: The cars coming and going from downtown, the people hard at work, and even the students at school...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I find it hard to believe that injustice could exist in this city when I see how orderly it operates.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: But I know there are people lurking in Kivotos waiting to break the peace.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I belong to the Justice Task Force. I should be doing more...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Do I? I think my efforts are average at best.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: And I'm only doing what I can to keep up with the other members of the task force. If I didn't...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Still. If it pleases you, then perhaps being average isn't so bad. Heh...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I usually don't stay as long as I have.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: But I wouldn't mind staying a while longer if I have you with me.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: So? Will you humor me...for just a little while longer?

8. **「시즈야마 마시로 인연 스토리 8」**

- **시즈야마 마시로**: Oh, Sensei.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I wasn't aware that you were visiting Trinity. If you had informed me, I would have come to greet you.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: ...I suppose it doesn't matter, since we've run into each other here.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Oh, my book?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I-It's a bit embarrassing, but...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: It's...this...kind of book.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: It teaches various proverbs and four-character idioms.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: ...It looks interesting to you?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: ...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Fufu, yes. I do think it's very compelling.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I recommended it to Hasumi recently as well.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: I don't think she read it, though...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Yes, I really do. The proverbs and four-character idioms are nice, but what really engrosses me are old stories.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Old stories contain lessons that have been passed down through generations.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: It's like seeing the future through the past, as they say. Old stories can guide us to the correct path.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: And the best part of it all is that...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: ...most proverbs contain messages of the inevitability of justice and the reward of good deeds!
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Yes, it's a universal truth that justice always prevails.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: That's why I've always liked proverbs, ever since I was young.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Even during my club activities, I often quote proverbs or sayings...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: They tease me for being ancient or just completely ignore me when I do, though...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: But I knew you'd understand me.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: ...You're interested, and you'd like to borrow it?
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Yes, of course!
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Heehee...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: To think that you'd enjoy the same thing I enjoy. It makes me feel...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: ...
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Um... It's an amazing feeling.
- **시즈야마 마시로**: Y-Yes, pretty amazing.
