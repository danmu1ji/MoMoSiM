# 우스바 리츠 인연 스토리

원문(bluearchive.wiki) 인연 스토리 5편. 선생과의 개인 기억이므로 시간창 없이 열람할 수 있다(개인 지식).

- 등장: 우스바 리츠, 선생(샬레)

1. **「우스바 리츠 인연 스토리 1」**

본문 대사는 수록되어 있지 않다.

2. **「우스바 리츠 인연 스토리 2」**

- **우스바 리츠**: Oh! It's Sensei!
- **우스바 리츠**: Hello!
- **우스바 리츠**: Oh! I'm the Student Rep today, right?
- **우스바 리츠**: Yeah! This rock spoke to me!
- **우스바 리츠**: Yes! Every rock has its own shape underneath.
- **우스바 리츠**: This one told me that it had true beauty lying within!
- **우스바 리츠**: Which is why...
- **우스바 리츠**: ...I broke it!
- **우스바 리츠**: Its true form can only come out after you chip away at the outer shell.
- **우스바 리츠**: So I had to work through the rough and bumpy surface to reveal its true form!
- **우스바 리츠**: Hm? Of course n—
- **우스바 리츠**: ...
- **우스바 리츠**: So, it's good to see you, Sensei!
- **우스바 리츠**: Can you boost me up?
- **우스바 리츠**: Higher!
- **우스바 리츠**: Heehee. I wasn't tall enough to hit the top of the rock!
- **우스바 리츠**: Now I can sculpt properly! Thank you, Sensei! Close your eyes!
- **우스바 리츠**: Okay, just have to refine this part...!
- **우스바 리츠**: And, finally, this side needs a little more...
- **우스바 리츠**: Oh my. Sensei, are you okay? Your face is covered in dust! Don't lick it!
- **우스바 리츠**: Wh-What should we do?
- **우스바 리츠**: Ah! What if we rotate? I'll sit on your shoulders, but you face the other way?
- **우스바 리츠**: Then the dust will fall on your back!
- **우스바 리츠**: Eh, really? I thought it'd be a good idea.
- **우스바 리츠**: I'll wrap this up, then!
- **우스바 리츠**: Okay! And my work is...done!
- **우스바 리츠**: Thank you, Sensei. Heehee.
- **우스바 리츠**: Are you trying to tell me how you feel about it?
- **우스바 리츠**: You don't have to do that!
- **우스바 리츠**: You just saw it for the first time, and I'm right here.
- **우스바 리츠**: I think that would make speaking your feelings difficult, huh?
- **우스바 리츠**: Give yourself time to think about how it makes you feel.
- **우스바 리츠**: Especially how it makes you feel when I'm not staring at you!
- **우스바 리츠**: And, however long it takes you to think it out is fine. Once you have the right words...
- **우스바 리츠**: ...Then you can tell me how you feel, Sensei!
- **우스바 리츠**: Speaking of which, would you like me to move it? I can put it in your office.
- **우스바 리츠**: Ah...
- **우스바 리츠**: ...Thank you, Sensei.
- **우스바 리츠**: I think this has been a very fulfilling day!
- **우스바 리츠**: ...Ah.
- **우스바 리츠**: ...Wah!

3. **「우스바 리츠 인연 스토리 3」**

- **우스바 리츠**: Sensei! Sensei!
- **우스바 리츠**: Your window's locked! Should I break it?
- **우스바 리츠**: Secured!
- **우스바 리츠**: Yeah! Your elevator was SOOOO slow!
- **우스바 리츠**: Hmm...?
- **우스바 리츠**: But this was shorter. It was the most direct.
- **우스바 리츠**: Oh! I told them that I wanted to go buy some marble. For sculpting.
- **우스바 리츠**: The Assembly is suuuuper strict about going outside...
- **우스바 리츠**: But they're a liiiitlle nicer if you have art-related reasons.
- **우스바 리츠**: And that's why I visited you after I went to the quarry!
- **우스바 리츠**: Yeah! It's on the ground floor, near the elevator. Do you want me to bring it up?
- **우스바 리츠**: Ah, sure! That's not what's important.
- **우스바 리츠**: Here, Sensei! For you!
- **우스바 리츠**: Yeah! Remember how you got covered in stone dust when you were helping with my sculpture last time?
- **우스바 리츠**: I figured that it ruined your shirt...
- **우스바 리츠**: So I asked my friend who's majoring in Fashion to make you a new one.
- **우스바 리츠**: Yeah. See, I thought we should make the shirt in your favorite color...
- **우스바 리츠**: But I didn't want to impose on her artistic vision. So I left it up to her.
- **우스바 리츠**: Ah! Is that your THING, Sensei?
- **우스바 리츠**: Yeah, yeah! Try it on! Let's see how it looks!
- **우스바 리츠**: Hm? What do you mean?
- **우스바 리츠**: ...
- **우스바 리츠**: Ah, feeling shy, Sensei?
- **우스바 리츠**: How about I change into my work clothes right next to you?
- **우스바 리츠**: Huh.
- **우스바 리츠**: What do you think? Is it comfortable? Is the fit okay?
- **우스바 리츠**: See, I think...it really...
- **우스바 리츠**: ...doesn't work for you!
- **우스바 리츠**: Ah, it looks good on YOU, Sensei! In isolation.
- **우스바 리츠**: But I don't think it gels with your pants! And shoes!
- **우스바 리츠**: But still, it's okay!
- **우스바 리츠**: We can shop for some coordinating pieces next time!

4. **「우스바 리츠 인연 스토리 4」**

- **우스바 리츠**: Sensei! Over here!
- **우스바 리츠**: Sensei, I thought you said you were busy!
- **우스바 리츠**: Oh! I know what you are!
- **우스바 리츠**: You're a "lost cause."
- **우스바 리츠**: But what matters is that you'll hang with me, right?
- **우스바 리츠**: In that case, I'm fine with you being a "lost cause."
- **우스바 리츠**: Let's go, Sensei! I know this street up and down.
- **우스바 리츠**: Tada! We're here, Sensei!
- **우스바 리츠**: We have casual, business casual, formal, you name it! It's all here on one street!
- **우스바 리츠**: See! They even have those transparent skirts and pants you like! Right around the corner!
- **우스바 리츠**: If you don't like that, then follow me. Over here!
- **우스바 리츠**: Let's start with reasonable clothes at reasonable prices, right?
- **우스바 리츠**: Hm, I think we bought a good assortment.
- **우스바 리츠**: What do you think, Sensei?
- **우스바 리츠**: Heehee! I'm glad we ventured out, then.
- **우스바 리츠**: Sensei, can you just wait here a minute?
- **우스바 리츠**: Right on. You always know what's up.
- **우스바 리츠**: I'll be right back.
- **Suspicious Store Clerk**: Hm? Welcome, bud! What're you looking for?
- **Suspicious Store Clerk**: That's great! We got some fresh arrivals!
- **Suspicious Store Clerk**: Here! Put this on! It'll look great!
- **Suspicious Store Clerk**: Nah, that's a waste of time. This is made for you! Look in my eyes and tell me you don't trust my taste!
- **Suspicious Store Clerk**: I'm telling you to try it on. Here!
- **Suspicious Store Clerk**: Wow! I knew it! It's perfect. Don't you think so?
- **Suspicious Store Clerk**: Don't worry! There's a tailor nearby. They're amazing. We'll shorten them. Just wait a minute, will you?
- **Suspicious Store Clerk**: You know, it's foolish to hesitate. This is a rare find. It'll be gone in a day.
- **우스바 리츠**: Sensei, what's going on over here?
- **Polite Store Clerk**: Oh! B-Big sis?!
- **우스바 리츠**: What are you doing? You weren't...giving them the "hard sell" again, were you?
- **Polite Store Clerk**: No! I was....just offering to help them find options that may be better suited for their figure....next time!
- **우스바 리츠**: That's what I thought. Cool! I'll be taking Sensei, then.
- **Polite Store Clerk**: Love it! Be well!
- **우스바 리츠**: It seems you really need me around, huh, Sensei?
- **우스바 리츠**: Heehee.
- **우스바 리츠**: All I think is...if you need to go somewhere new, you should bring me with you.
- **우스바 리츠**: Does that sound good?

5. **「우스바 리츠 인연 스토리 5」**

- **우스바 리츠**: Sensei, over here!
- **우스바 리츠**: Shh.
- **우스바 리츠**: No one can find out what we're doing, Sensei.
- **우스바 리츠**: Now then... Hyup!
- **우스바 리츠**: Let's go, Sensei!
- **우스바 리츠**: Tada! These are the underground ruins I found when looking for a new smuggling route!
- **우스바 리츠**: Wildhunt is SUPER old.
- **우스바 리츠**: They say there are tons of forgotten ruins under it.
- **우스바 리츠**: And, best of all...I think this is the only entrance!
- **우스바 리츠**: Over here, Sensei.
- **우스바 리츠**: Once you squeeze past this tunnel...
- **우스바 리츠**: ― Ah, ah.
- **우스바 리츠**: ― So cool.
- **우스바 리츠**: ― Isn't this place great?
- **우스바 리츠**: ― It looks like a cistern that was built a long time ago.
- **우스바 리츠**: ― They dug out the ground, placed dozens of stone pillars...
- **우스바 리츠**: ― and carved each and every one of them personally...
- **우스바 리츠**: ― Isn't it beautiful?
- **우스바 리츠**: ― I really wanted to show this to you, Sensei.
- **우스바 리츠**: ― Come over here.
- **우스바 리츠**: ― We should enjoy this scenery together.
