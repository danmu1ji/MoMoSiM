2. **「KnightsHospitaller 스토리 2화」**

- **아사가오 하나에**: The Remedial Knights have arrived!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Where are the patients? If you're not feeling well, please raise your hand and yell out loud!
- **스미 세리나**: Shh... Hanae, you're being way too loud. There are a lot of sick people here, so please lower your voice.
- **아사가오 하나에**: I don't agree with you there. Worry may be the root of all ailments, but laughter is the universal cure, right?
- **아사가오 하나에**: If everyone smiles and sings loudly together, they'll be healthy again in no time!
- **Patient A**: Haha. True, true. Thanks to Hanae's uplifting energy, life in the hospital isn't as boring as it could be.
- **Patient B**: Just looking at Hanae's smile gives me energy.
- **스미 세리나**: Heehee. I'm glad to hear you feel that way.
- **스미 세리나**: Oh, Shiroyagi. That's the bread from lunch. Didn't you finish your meal?
- **Shiroyagi**: I just don't have much of an appetite lately. I don't know why I'm not hungry...
- **스미 세리나**: But that's no good... If you wanna be healthy, you have to get enough nutrition!
- **스미 세리나**: Here, I'll feed you. Open wide!
- **Patient C**: No fair, that's special treatment! Feed me too, Serina!
- **Patient D**: Me too, me too!
- **스미 세리나**: Everyone, quit acting like children or I'll tell the head nurse! Looking at me with those sad eyes is not gonna work!
- **아사가오 하나에**: If there's a patient with injured hands, I'll feed them some bread myself!
- **Patient A**: Even though they are busy with schoolwork, they still volunteer at the hospital every weekend to take care of patients... The Remedial Knights really are angels!
- **Patient B**: That's right. Whenever I look at them, I feel like my heart is full... I bet even an evil spirit would turn into a cute little lamb in front of them.
- **Patient A**: Yeah, totally!
- **스미 세리나**: Head Nurse, I've finished going around the third floor. All of the patients are doing well.
- **Head Nurse**: Great job. Did any patients give you a hard time?
- **아사가오 하나에**: No! Everyone was nice and kind to us. I really enjoyed meeting them!
- **Head Nurse**: Glad to hear. Now that I think of it, the atmosphere in the hospital has become much more positive, and the number of complaints from patients has gone way down. It's all thanks to the Remedial Knights.
- **스미 세리나**: We're just doing our job, heh heh.
- **아사가오 하나에**: Please let us Remedial Knights know when people need us!
- **스미 세리나**: By the way...I have a question.
- **Head Nurse**: What is it, Serina?
- **스미 세리나**: We only go up to the first three floors when we visit. Are there any patients on the fourth floor?
- **스미 세리나**: The map in the central lobby shows that there's also a ward on the fourth floor...
- **Head Nurse**: That's because...a bad rumor has spread among the patients there...
- **아사가오 하나에**: A bad rumor?
- **Head Nurse**: They say that after the midnight bell rings...a ghost wrapped in bandages appears!
- **아사가오 하나에**: A-A ghost? Is that true?
- **Head Nurse**: I don't believe there's a real ghost...but so many of the patients were anxious about it, we had no choice but to relocate the ward.
- **Head Nurse**: So now the fourth floor remains unused.
- **스미 세리나**: Th-That's a scary rumor... I-I'll stay away from the fourth floor, then...
- **아사가오 하나에**: But even if it's a ghost... If it has bandages on its body, that means it's hurt, right?
- **아사가오 하나에**: If Captain Mine finds out we turned away an injured patient because we were scared, I'm sure she'll be disappointed!
- **스미 세리나**: B-But, the Remedial Knights only help living patients. We're not a group that deals with ghosts!
- **스미 세리나**: W-We really shouldn't mess with them...
- **아사가오 하나에**: We won't know the real story unless we find out if the rumors are true or not!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Besides, if the fourth floor can't be used because of the ghost, the hospital won't be able to take in as many patients. This is for the patients' sake too!
- **스미 세리나**: Ugh...why do you have to be so logical in a situation like this?!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Heehee. I guess you agree with me. Let's meet at eleven tonight and go check on the ghost in the empty ward!
- **스미 세리나**: I-If there's a real ghost, I'm gonna leave! I'll run away and never look back!

3. **「KnightsHospitaller 스토리 3화」**

- **???**: Heh heh... I hate you... I hate you...!
- **???**: I'll have my revenge...
- **???**: ...Ugh. No one's even around to see me. Maybe I should just drop the ghost act?
- **Fake Ghost**: Ha! The plan worked! I didn't expect they'd close down the ward so quickly... All I had to do was act like a ghost!
- **Fake Ghost**: The patients, doctors, nurses...nobody will even come close to this floor because they're so afraid of the "ghost." Heehee! Now I can do whatever I want!
- **Fake Ghost**: Now let's see if there's anything I can sell to make some easy cash...
- **Fake Ghost**: Wait! Are those footsteps?
- **스미 세리나**: H-Hanae...are you really gonna keep going? I-I don't think there's anything here...
- **아사가오 하나에**: We can't go back until we prove there's no ghost in this ward! The patients are still terrified of this rumor!
- **스미 세리나**: Why are you only brave in situations like this? Ugh...
- **Fake Ghost**: Who were they? I don't think they're nurses... Volunteer students, maybe?
- **Fake Ghost**: Whoever they are, I won't allow any uninvited guests to interrupt my plans. All I have to do is act like a ghost to scare them away!
- **스미 세리나**: *gasp* D-Did you see that, Hanae? I saw something move behind the curtain!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Huh? I didn't see anything... Maybe a cat came in through the window?
- **아사가오 하나에**: Here, kitty kitty! Ps-ps-ps!
- **Fake Ghost**: Heh heh... I hate you... I hate you...!
- **스미 세리나**: Ahhh! Gh-Gh-Ghost! It's a ghost!
- **Fake Ghost**: Leave this place or I'll steal your soul!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Load the bullets with the blue mark into the magazine! Those are the silver bullets I got from the Sisterhood!
- **스미 세리나**: I was hoping I wouldn't have to use this...
- **스미 세리나**: I-I'm sorry, Mr. Ghost! Please rest in peace!
- **Fake Ghost**: Wait, what?! You're shooting a gun at me?! What the heck?!
- **Fake Ghost**: Don't you know you're in a hospital?! How could you bring such a dangerous weapon in here? You could really injure someone!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Huh? A gun is very useful in a hospital. It can be used as a crutch when someone has an injured leg. It also comes in handy when a patient doesn't wanna get up and needs a little support!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Guns are almost like magic. I'd say they're an essential tool in any hospital!
- **스미 세리나**: But since you're a ghost, you wouldn't get hurt if we shot you, right?
- **Fake Ghost**: Well...Th-That's true, but...
- **Fake Ghost**: Wh-Whoa! Wait!
- **Fake Ghost**: Silver exorcism bullets can be dangerous to the living too! People can get hurt or even die if they get hit by mistake!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Huh? I thought ghosts were already dead. How can you die again?
- **스미 세리나**: I understand you're not ready to cross over to the other side, but you don't belong here! It's time for you to rest in peace!
- **Fake Ghost**: Stop, stop! I can't do this anymore!
- **Fake Ghost**: I-I surrender! Please stop!

4. **「KnightsHospitaller 스토리 4화」**

- **아사가오 하나에**: Wait, what? You're not a real ghost?
- **Fake Ghost**: Yeah! That's what I keep saying!
- **스미 세리나**: But all the special effects... And your acting was so good... I thought you were a real ghost!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Why were you wandering around pretending to be a ghost? Were you playing hide-and-seek in the empty ward?
- **Fake Ghost**: Why would I pretend to be a ghost for a dumb reason like that?! I snuck in here to scare people away so I could steal things!
- **스미 세리나**: Steal things? Stealing is bad!
- **Fake Ghost**: I-I know that! B-But, I had no choice... It was the only way to find my father's lost keepsake!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Your father's keepsake? Why would you search for something like that here in the hospital?
- **Fake Ghost**: My father went into surgery at this hospital last year. But when it came time to leave, I forgot all about his necklace. That's how I lost it...
- **Fake Ghost**: I didn't want to harm the other patients...but the hospital wouldn't let me up here, so I had no other choice!
- **스미 세리나**: So that was why... I understand.
- **아사가오 하나에**: We'll help you find your father's keepsake!
- **Fake Ghost**: Really? But I pretended I was a ghost so I could steal things... Are you sure?
- **스미 세리나**: It's the motto of the Remedial Knights to help anyone in trouble. Just promise us that you won't touch anything that doesn't belong to you!
- **Fake Ghost**: Of course I won't! I promise I'll leave as soon as I get my father's necklace!
- **스미 세리나**: Okay! Hanae, let's look around and see if we can find anything important!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Okay!
- **아사가오 하나에**: There's an iron box on the shelf over here! I bet it contains a bunch of valuable stuff... Can you help me move it?
- **스미 세리나**: Oh! Hold on!
- **스미 세리나**: Ugh! Phew, that was heavy. I wonder what's in here.
- **Fake Ghost**: Hand it over!
- **Fake Ghost**: Give me that safe!
- **스미 세리나**: Why are you doing this, Mr. Ghost?!
- **Fake Ghost**: Hahaha, I fooled you, dummies!
- **Fake Ghost**: I made up the whole story about my father's keepsake! I broke into this hospital to make some money for myself!
- **Fake Ghost**: And you volunteered to help me. Trinity students are so naive! Hahaha!
- **스미 세리나**: What? How can you take advantage of other people's kindness? That's so mean!
- **Fake Ghost**: It's your fault for believing me. I'm not responsible for your dumb decisions!
- **Fake Ghost**: This safe is all mine now!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Hey! Are you okay?
- **스미 세리나**: I-I'm fine. But we have to get that stolen safe back from the ghost...
- **아사가오 하나에**: Don't worry. That box isn't an ordinary safe. It's an autoclave used in hospitals!
- **스미 세리나**: Huh? What's an autoclave?
- **아사가오 하나에**: Well, it does contain a lot of valuable equipment, so it's like a safe, but it's also full of high-pressure steam. If you force the door open, the whole thing will explode with a loud bang!
- **Fake Ghost**: Ahhh!
- **스미 세리나**: ...
- **아사가오 하나에**: ...
- **스미 세리나**: Hanae, please tell me that kind of stuff earlier next time. Someone's been seriously hurt.
- **아사가오 하나에**: I will!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Ladies and gentlemen, the Remedial Knights have arrived!
- **스미 세리나**: Good morning, everyone. How did you sleep last night?
- **Patient A**: I couldn't sleep at all because of the noise from upstairs...
- **Patient B**: I heard a bunch of shooting noises and then I heard something that sounded like a bomb going off! It was crazy!
- **Patient A**: Come to think of it, you two took over watch duties for the nurses last night. Did you see anyone suspicious?
- **스미 세리나**: Well...we didn't even go close to the fourth floor because we were too afraid of the ghost. I... I have no idea about that bomb noise you heard.
- **Patient A**: What? You can't be afraid of ghosts if you're gonna handle a night shift at the hospital!
- **Patient B**: But that's what makes Serina so cute! Don't you agree, newbie?
- **Fake Ghost**: Uh, yeah. Hahaha.
- **스미 세리나**: Oh, Mr. Ghost. There you are!
- **아사가오 하나에**: I've been looking for you ever since you disappeared without saying a word!
- **Patient A**: What are you talking about, Hanae? This patient just came in this morning. Have you two met already?
- **Patient B**: How come this newbie's already gotten to know Hanae?! I hate it!
- **Patient C**: By the way, Mr. Ghost is a weird nickname. How did you get it?
- **아사가오 하나에**: Oh, that's because Mr. Ghost pretended to be a ghost in the empty ward on the fourth floor...
- **Fake Ghost**: No, that's not true! People call me that because they think I look scary... Like a ghost!
- **Patient C**: What? That's pretty boring. I was expecting something more interesting as a backstory than that...
- **Fake Ghost**: What is wrong with you guys?! Who are you? Why do you keep following me around and harassing me?!
- **스미 세리나**: Us? We're the Remedial Knights! We go wherever the patients are.
- **아사가오 하나에**: We're not trying to harass you. We wanna help you! You don't have to be afraid of us!
- **Fake Ghost**: But how can I know for sure you're not holding a grudge over what happened yesterday?
