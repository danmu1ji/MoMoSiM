- **아사히나 피나**: It's an idea that was captured perfectly in Loyal Cat's Fist!

- **카와와 시즈코**: Whiteboard, check. Dry erase marker, check. All good to go!
- **카와와 시즈코**: The stage is yours, Pina!
- **아사히나 피나**: Yes, ma'am! We've received the president's blessing, so it's time!
- **아사히나 피나**: Let us commence with the meeting of the Festival Operations Department!
- **카와와 시즈코**: Heehee. Heeheehee!
- **카와와 시즈코**: We may have been thwarted by the Inner Discipline Club's unwelcome intrusion last time...
- **카와와 시즈코**: But things will not be the same this round! No, indeed!
- **카와와 시즈코**: Why you ask? Because this time we have an ace up our sleeve.
- **카와와 시즈코**: The one and the only, Umika has returned!
- **아사히나 피나**: Yes! The best ace up our sleeve we could ask for!
- **아사히나 피나**: Welcome, Umika! I've eagerly awaited your return!
- **사토하마 우미카**: It's good to be back, Boss!
- **사토하마 우미카**: I've missed you too, Pina!
- **카와와 시즈코**: Call me president, not boss!
- **카와와 시즈코**: *sigh*
- **카와와 시즈코**: Just how many times do I need to hammer that into your head?!
- **카와와 시즈코**: Moving on to business! We are scheduled to host a festival in the Foxy Shopping District next month!
- **카와와 시즈코**: Now, Our dearest Umika embarked on a grand festival expedition to learn more about the trade!
- **카와와 시즈코**: If you will, please share the stories of your exploits and the lessons you've learned! Armed with your new business acumen, we'll storm the shopping district and bring about a festival renaissance!
- **아사히나 피나**: A renaissance!
- **사토하마 우미카**: Yes, yes! I have returned with many proposals.
- **사토하마 우미카**: To begin, I learned about the Bloody Crimson Festival of Gehenna and found a treasure trove of inspiration.
- **카와와 시즈코**: (Bloody crimson sounds... Oh. She means the Gehenna Tomato Festival.)
- **카와와 시즈코**: (I've only read about it, but the students gather together and throw tomatoes at each other to create the impression of a blood bath. It suits Gehenna.)
- **카와와 시즈코**: (A festival of that scale... I wonder...)
- **카와와 시즈코**: W-Wonderful!
- **카와와 시즈코**: Please enlighten us on what this treasure trove you discovered is.
- **사토하마 우미카**: Yes, it's very enlightening.
- **사토하마 우미카**: The origins of this festival can be traced far back in time. To call it a "bloody history" is rather apt, as the participants would shoot each other with live rounds until their bodies were soaked crimson red with blood!
- **카와와 시즈코**: What?!
- **아사히나 피나**: That... That sounds like a violent conflict between two warring organizations...
- **아사히나 피나**: As expected of Gehenna! How horrifying!
- **카와와 시즈코**: No, no, no. There's no way that's the actual origin! Who told you that?!
- **사토하마 우미카**: Hm? It was a kind member of the Helmet Gangsters I happened to meet.
- **사토하마 우미카**: They offered to tell me the story of the secret, unknown origins of the bloody crimson festival for just 1,000 yen. It was rather generous of them.
- **카와와 시즈코**: Umika! That was the most obvious scam of the century. Didn't I tell you to be careful of tourist traps?
- **사토하마 우미카**: Yes? And I was?
- **사토하마 우미카**: To return to my story, this bloody practice was eventually replaced with today's more peaceful iteration.
- **사토하마 우미카**: It's become a wonderful festival where they throw tomatoes instead of bullets, but with the Gehenna touch of course!
- **사토하마 우미카**: They implement all kinds of throwing devices, such as tomato catapults, tomato launchers, tomato grenades, armor-piercing fin-stabilized discarding tomato sabots...
- **사토하마 우미카**: I was truly inspired by their limitless imagination.
- **사토하마 우미카**: I could wile away weeks, no, months watching the different ways they project tomatoes at each other!
- **카와와 시즈코**: What in the world are armor-piercing fin-stabilized discarding tomato sabots?!
- **사토하마 우미카**: There are even rumors that the massive scale of this festival is so impressive that Millennium is entering into a partnership with them!
- **사토하마 우미카**: And thus, I would like to propose that we recreate the very same Bloody Crimson Festival in Hyakkiyako, Boss!
- **카와와 시즈코**: R-Recreate it?! Here?
- **사토하마 우미카**: You see, there is much more to the Gehenna Tomato Festival than throwing tomatoes.
- **사토하마 우미카**: It's also environmentally friendly, as it is a chance to use up surplus tomatoes in a culturally enriching, non-wasteful method.
- **사토하마 우미카**: Just imagine the possibilities, Boss!
- **사토하마 우미카**: If we can recreate it here...
- **사토하마 우미카**: Hyakkiyako will purchase all the surplus tomatoes from surrounding farms...
- **사토하마 우미카**: And the Festival Operations Department will be the leading force in revitalizing our markets!
- **사토하마 우미카**: It'll also be an opportunity to expand our traditional cuisine and discover new tomato dishes. Don't you think so?
- **사토하마 우미카**: This way, surplus tomatoes won't be left to simply rot in the fields.
- **사토하마 우미카**: We'll cooperate with local restaurants to introduce these new tomato dishes to festival attendees.
- **사토하마 우미카**: And Hyakkiyako will consume even more tomatoes!
- **사토하마 우미카**: As an added bonus, the clean-up for the festival will require professional cleaners and laundromat services.
- **사토하마 우미카**: These local industries will generate more business, which in turn will boost our economy.
- **아사히나 피나**: Umika, you're truly an inspiration!
- **아사히나 피나**: Your journeys have proven fruitful in broadening your knowledge of our trade!
