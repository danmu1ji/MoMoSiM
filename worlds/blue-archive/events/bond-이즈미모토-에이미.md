2. **「이즈미모토 에이미 인연 스토리 2」**

- **이즈미모토 에이미**: *sigh* Great job again, Sensei.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Now, then...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Hmm? I'm taking my clothes off. Why?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Why am I taking them off...? Well, because it's hot.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I can't just take my clothes off because it's hot? How else am I suppose to cool off, then?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: The most efficient way to dissipate heat inside one's body is to undress.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Embarrassed...? Is embarrassment a valid reason to not successfully carry out a mission?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Then what would you suggest I do to cool down without undressing?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Hmm...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Ice cream? Yes, ingesting something cold may work... That should prove an effective way of lowering my internal temperature.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Very well, on the condition that I buy you your ice cream.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Why...? Because that's the sensible thing to do.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Heehee... We should hurry, Sensei. Staying here will only make us overheat more.

3. **「이즈미모토 에이미 인연 스토리 3」**

- **이즈미모토 에이미**: You're here, Sensei.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I'm glad you could make it. I know you must be busy.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Well, honestly...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: What do you think...girls are like these days?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Well, because you're always surrounded by girls.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: So I thought you'd be an expert...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Was I mistaken?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Hmm...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Well...I've been having a hard time seeing eye to eye with my friends.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: For example, they wanted to go to a new candy shop that just opened near the school.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: If junk food is what they're after, wouldn't it be more economical to buy cheap greasy fries rather than expensive candy?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: That also doesn't account for the time spent standing in line. I just can't understand their reasoning.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: That is a valid point...but there are other instances.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: For example, why do girls find occult things so interesting? You know, like tarot cards, horoscopes, and fortune telling?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Why in the world would anyone trust some fortune teller on TV? I mean, there's no logic there. I just don't get it.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: So, there are some things you don't know about girls. Color me surprised.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Maybe I do have a chance, after all...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Hmm? Oh, I didn't say anything.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Anyway, I need to know what girls like if I'm to be friends with them.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Does that make me sound like I'm not a real girl my age? I could hardly blame you if it did.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Sometimes I forget that I'm a student. Sensei, you strike me as more a...comrade than a teacher.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: But thinking like that will only make graduating harder. And that wouldn't be efficient.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Now, Sensei. Let's read that magazine I brought and ponder the nature of girls.

4. **「이즈미모토 에이미 인연 스토리 4」**

- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Hello, Sensei. I just discovered something new.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: According to my school friends, singing together at karaoke has become quite popular.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I don't really understand how singing together in a small room could be fun...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: ..but it'd be inefficient to jump to conclusions before trying it myself.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Now, Sensei. Let us go to karaoke together.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: That isn't an option. Karaoke is a dangerous place.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: It's a small, confined, soundproofed space. We'll be cut off from the outside.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: If we were to be attacked there...I wouldn't stand a chance.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: So it's only right that I go with Sensei, my trustworthy comrade.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Now, let's go sing... I mean, investigate this karaoke stuff.

5. **「이즈미모토 에이미 인연 스토리 5」**

- **이즈미모토 에이미**: It's been a while since I've been to a boutique. I don't buy new clothes until mine wear out, so I don't shop for clothes often.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Let's see... Huh, front-hook bras come in a lot of colors these days. It used to be easy to choose.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Front-hook bras are a lot easier to wear and take off. Undoing the hook behind your back is a lot harder than it looks.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Sensei, you don't look well. Are you uncomfortable?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Huh? I told you in my text. I wanted you to help me pick out some clothes.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I'm sorry. Do you have more important business to attend to?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Does underwear not also constitute as clothing? I can't possibly understand your confusion, Sensei.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: If you wear the wrong underwear, it can throw your whole balance off. Proper undergarments are extremely important for efficiency.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Well, my friends tell me you have to wear cute underwear. You know, just in case...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: What does "cute underwear" look like, anyway? Can you pick some out for me?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: ― Sensei, what about this one? Does this suit me?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: ― Underwear is just a type of clothing. I don't see why you can't show other people.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: ― This underwear looks a bit small... I wonder if there's a bigger size.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: ― Ah... This won't do. I'm just going to try it on, and then we can see how it looks.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: ― Wait, I can't change here? Ugh... Fitting rooms are always so cramped...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Thanks for today, Sensei. I got everything I needed thanks to you.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: So, just to be sure, I shouldn't take off my clothes in the store next time?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I know, I know. You kept telling me not to.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: It's not as efficient, but I'll take your word for it. I promise.

6. **「이즈미모토 에이미 인연 스토리 6」**

- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I said I'm fine...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: You can relax.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: It's normal for one to bleed when scratched! It simply needs time to heal.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Is that not a suitable response? Hmm...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: But more caution would lead to a loss of efficiency.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: You can't be afraid of getting hurt. You have to charge forward and get the job done...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Ouch!
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: You could have warned me before applying the disinfectant, Sensei.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Yes, I'm well aware it'll get infected if we don't disinfect it properly...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I'm a little embarrassed...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Fine. I'll try to be more careful.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: (If Sensei takes care of me like this all the time, maybe I should get hurt more often...)
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Hmm? Oh, nothing.

7. **「이즈미모토 에이미 인연 스토리 7」**

- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Hm...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Hmmm...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: ...Oh.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: No, Sensei. No need to worry about me.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Though the investigation is not very successful, that's not unexpected.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: In fact, just knowing that there is nothing abnormal here is its own gain.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I'm fairly used to strenuous outdoor activities for extended periods of time.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I'm more worried about you, Sensei...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Are you okay? Your pace has gotten much slower.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: That's true. The climate here can be very taxing.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: No shade whatsoever, strong and direct sunlight, and no wind...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: It's easy to get exhausted, even if we're well hydrated.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I get hot easily too. My condition is fine, but I'm already soaked in sweat right now.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: In such situation, it'd be best to take our clothes off to help regulate the heat and sweat...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: ...I know you'd stop me. I've learned from past experiences.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: But there's nothing to worry about.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I've told you before, right? I prepare for everything, so that nothing is ever unexpected.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: And here I have...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: ...Don't judge a machine by its cuteness, Sensei.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I brought it because it was the best choice in terms of efficiency.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: It's slightly modified for situations like this.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: If I flip the switch on like this...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: In extreme situations, extreme measures must be taken.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I knew we wouldn't have too much idle time, so it had to be strong enough to cool down our bodies quickly. The stronger the wind, the less time we'd need to spend fanning.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: So...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Come here, Sensei.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Despite its strong output, it's still a pocket electric fan. The area of effect isn't very big.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: To efficiently cool off both of us, we must be close to each other.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Which means, you should move in as close as you can. Understood?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: ...? Sensei?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Why aren't you moving? I asked you to come closer.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: ...You're okay?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: It'd be better to not be too close? Why?
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: ...I fail to understand your logic. Whatever your reason may be, cooling you down is more important. We must prioritize efficiency right now.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Um...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: You need to come closer.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: ...Come, Sensei.
