# 아카시 준코 인연 스토리

- 등장: 아카시 준코, 선생(샬레)

1. **「아카시 준코 인연 스토리 1」**

본문 대사는 수록되어 있지 않다.

2. **「아카시 준코 인연 스토리 2」**

- **아카시 준코**: Welcome to L'Oiseau Bleu!
- **아카시 준코**: Sensei? What in the world are you doing here?
- **아카시 준코**: You just came to buy snacks? This place is pretty pricey for an impulse purchase, though...
- **아카시 준코**: I guess that's the advantage of being an adult with a credit line.
- **아카시 준코**: You don't know how good you have it. Adults can have dessert whenever they want!
- **아카시 준코**: What do you mean me? What am I doing here?
- **아카시 준코**: Isn't it obvious? I work here part-time.
- **아카시 준코**: I need it to fund my spending habits. Breakfast, lunch, dinner, not to mention all the snacks between meals...
- **아카시 준코**: Why do high-quality ingredients have to be so expensive? How is a student supposed to feed herself?!
- **아카시 준코**: I don't work part-time because I want to...but that's the price I have to pay for epicureanism.
- **아카시 준코**: Not particularly. It's not like I'm the one doing the baking.
- **아카시 준코**: Besides, L'Oiseau Bleu lets its employees take the leftover sweets home.
- **아카시 준코**: So I get to eat sweets I'd never be able to afford for free. What more of a reason do I need to work here?
- **아카시 준코**: Recommendations? Let's see...
- **아카시 준코**: The whole menu is good if you ask me, but my personal favorite is the Mont Blanc right in front of you.
- **아카시 준코**: We don't sell many due to the price, but it's worth trying for the rich flavor of creamy chestnut paste in harmony with the crispy meringue!
- **아카시 준코**: I was lucky enough to get a leftover for free...and it changed my life!
- **아카시 준코**: Great choice. You won't regret it!
- **아카시 준코**: Come again? If I sell too many, there won't be any leftovers for me to have at the end of the day, and then I'd be taking a net loss?
- **아카시 준코**: Th-That's a good point. In that case, I should slack off so that there are more leftovers...
- **아카시 준코**: No, that's not right! If I don't make any sales, then I won't get paid and can't afford to buy the other foods I want!
- **아카시 준코**: Hmm. But I do want those leftovers. What should I do...?
- **아카시 준코**: Huh...? But you bought this for yourself, Sensei.
- **아카시 준코**: You want me to take it as a gift for working so hard? Th-Thanks...
- **아카시 준코**: Still, I know it's not right to slack off and take advantage of people's kindness.
- **아카시 준코**: So I'll work extra hard today as my way of paying you back!

3. **「아카시 준코 인연 스토리 3」**

- **아카시 준코**: You're late, Sensei!
- **아카시 준코**: I offer you delectable food, and you have the nerve to be ten minutes late? This is death penalty-worthy!
- **아카시 준코**: You're lucky that letting you live is the only way you'll be able to try what I brought.
- **아카시 준코**: You're pardoned just this once, but it's the firing line next time!
- **아카시 준코**: I hope you weren't thinking I meant real seafood. I can't afford that with my kind of paycheck.
- **아카시 준코**: What we have here today is...a tasty substitute that's going to deceive your taste buds!
- **아카시 준코**: The human tongue is very sensitive. Surely you know what it's like to experience a new flavor by combining several different ingredients.
- **아카시 준코**: According to my online research, it should be possible to imitate the flavor of expensive seafood by combining much cheaper ingredients.
- **아카시 준코**: Allow me to introduce you to...imitation sea urchin eggs!
- **아카시 준코**: Exactly. From what I read, pudding with soy sauce on top tastes just like sea urchin eggs.
- **아카시 준코**: Don't ask me how or why...but if the combo works, it works! I'm sure we're in for an interesting flavor!
- **아카시 준코**: Here, Sensei, you try it first!
- **아카시 준코**: How is it? Can you taste the sea urchin egg?
- **아카시 준코**: That's weird. People swore it tasted just like it... *slurp*
- **아카시 준코**: ...
- **아카시 준코**: Salty... Sweet... Weird...
- **아카시 준코**: What the heck?! This doesn't taste anything like sea urchin eggs!
- **아카시 준코**: Ugh... Why would anyone go on the internet and lie? Who does that?
- **아카시 준코**: Th-That's okay! I still have plenty of recipes to try!
- **아카시 준코**: Here's the second one on deck.
- **아카시 준코**: Same basic concept. They say if you eat avocado with soy sauce on it, it should taste like tuna sashimi!
- **아카시 준코**: Avocado is fatty just like tuna is, so this one HAS to work!
- **아카시 준코**: All right... Here goes nothing!
- **아카시 준코**: ...I feel like an idiot.
- **아카시 준코**: I've been bamboozled! What the heck was THAT?! None of this tastes like what people said it would!
- **아카시 준코**: Avocado and soy sauce for tuna sashimi, tangerine and soy sauce for salmon roe... They all come out tasting gross!
- **아카시 준코**: The only person who'd like this stuff is Izumi!
- **아카시 준코**: *sigh* All I wanted was luxury seafood at an affordable price...
- **아카시 준코**: Wait. You think I could have afforded seafood with the money I used on all those different ingredients?
- **아카시 준코**: Let me do the math... Pudding set, soy sauce, avocados, tangerines...
- **아카시 준코**: You're right! That comes out to the same price as going to a conveyor belt sushi bar!
- **아카시 준코**: I got tricked again! Why would people go on the internet and just tell lies? Who does that?!

4. **「아카시 준코 인연 스토리 4」**

- **아카시 준코**: I'm back...
- **아카시 준코**: Ugh. That investigation was the worst. I was looking forward to going to a buffet with all-you-can-eat wagyu beef.
- **아카시 준코**: Nobody told me that they're closed today! How was I supposed to know?!
- **아카시 준코**: I skimped out on lunch to make room for bulgogi. Ugh, I'm starving...
- **아카시 준코**: Would you happen to have anything to eat, Sensei? I'm so hungry...
- **아카시 준코**: You do? Let me see... Mitoshin's chicken ramen?
- **아카시 준코**: I've heard of this brand but never tried it. This is the instant ramen you put in a cup and pour hot water over, right?
- **아카시 준코**: I know I said I was starving, but I'm supposed to be a gourmet. Eating cheap instant food would be a little...
- **아카시 준코**: I-I never said I didn't want it! Give it back!
- **아카시 준코**: Hmm. Put the noodles in a cup, pour boiling water, and wait for three minutes...
- **아카시 준코**: It doesn't even have a broth base or garnish. It can't possibly be tasty...
- **아카시 준코**: (What in the...?! It's delicious!)
- **아카시 준코**: (The fried noodles have an al dente texture. I can't believe my taste buds...)
- **아카시 준코**: (Not to mention the combination of noodles and sauce DO make for a savory broth... It exceeds my wildest expectations!)
- **아카시 준코**: I-It's edible, I suppose! Even if it falls short of the lofty standards of a gourmet like myself! H-Haha!
- **아카시 준코**: Not to mention it was hardly enough to be considered filling. You wouldn't happen to have more, would you?
- **아카시 준코**: D-Don't get the wrong idea! I'm not asking for more because I like it or anything!
- **아카시 준코**: This is purely because I require calories! Don't you dare get it twisted!

5. **「아카시 준코 인연 스토리 5」**

- **아카시 준코**: Still not enough...
- **아카시 준코**: I have to try harder...!
- **아카시 준코**: Oh, Sensei.
- **아카시 준코**: No. It's not that I was unhappy with the food. The cake was rather delicious, and they had so many different kinds...
- **아카시 준코**: The real problem is...I couldn't eat nearly as much as my clubmates!
- **아카시 준코**: I was full after my third serving, but Akari shoveled down dozens of plates' worth of cake!
- **아카시 준코**: What if it had been a buffet I paid to enter? If I couldn't eat nearly as much as the others, I'd be getting less value for my yen.
- **아카시 준코**: This isn't fair! I want to eat as much as everyone else!
- **아카시 준코**: What should I do, Sensei?
- **아카시 준코**: Train my stomach? Hmm... By nature, the stomach can expand to accommodate large amounts of food...
- **아카시 준코**: So in theory, consistently overeating would train it like a muscle and I'd be able to eat as much as I want!
- **아카시 준코**: But what would be a good training food for this?
- **아카시 준코**: Hmm... I know! Dried kelp will do the trick.
- **아카시 준코**: I can eat dried kelp endlessly, and since it's low in calories, I don't have to worry about gaining weight!
- **아카시 준코**: Let's start with a little test run...
- **아카시 준코**: Ugh...
- **아카시 준코**: I've never been so stuffed with kelp. I think I'm going to be sick. It feels like my stomach is going to explode.
- **아카시 준코**: I can't do this, Sensei...
- **아카시 준코**: Bleughhh!

6. **「아카시 준코 인연 스토리 6」**

- **아카시 준코**: Whew! That was so good.
- **아카시 준코**: This street lives up to the hype. Everything here is so delicious!
- **아카시 준코**: Fried rice, char siu, meat dumplings... They're all winners!
- **아카시 준코**: It was worth coming all the way here for. Heehee.
- **아카시 준코**: Huh? Don't I want to take a break after all of that?
- **아카시 준코**: No way! We haven't even gotten to half of the restaurants!
- **아카시 준코**: If we don't pick up the pace, we won't be able to taste every dish by the end of the day!
- **아카시 준코**: Still...I do feel a little queasy after all of that greasy food.
- **아카시 준코**: Okay. Why don't we head to a cafe for a break?
- **아카시 준코**: ― Huh? Wh-What? Why are you looking at me like that?
- **아카시 준코**: ― (Seriously...)
- **아카시 준코**: ― We're going to the spots after we're done taking a break, right? What should we eat next?
- **아카시 준코**: ― Delicious! ♪
- **아카시 준코**: ― What?! Drink it together?
- **아카시 준코**: ― Don't be silly! Do you have a death wish?
- **아카시 준코**: ― (Sensei must be teasing me, right? Right? There's only one straw...)
- **아카시 준코**: ― (But if it's with Sensei...maybe I wouldn't mind...)
- **아카시 준코**: ― Forget it! No juice for you!
- **아카시 준코**: Ah... That was good!
- **아카시 준코**: Today was the best. I got to have so much good food!
- **아카시 준코**: Being with you made it taste even better...
- **아카시 준코**: W-Wait, don't get the wrong idea! I can eat delicious food by myself like it's nothing!
- **아카시 준코**: But I'll admit that...it tastes better...with someone else there with me.
- **아카시 준코**: Will you grab a bite with me again sometime?
- **아카시 준코**: You will? Then it's a promise! I'll hold you to that!

7. **「아카시 준코 인연 스토리 7」**

- **아카시 준코**: Heeheehee. Prepare yourself!
- **아카시 준코**: It's...a foodie notebook for the Shiba Town area!
- **아카시 준코**: Yup! It's a collection of foodie spots.
- **아카시 준코**: I've pulled them from all the restaurants I've been to.
- **아카시 준코**: I sadly can't visit every single restaurant in Kivotos...
- **아카시 준코**: So I've been dividing it up by area.
- **아카시 준코**: And, look! I finished all the eateries of Shiba Town!
- **아카시 준코**: Um... So, the truth is, I haven't actually been to every single one.
- **아카시 준코**: ...Do NOT repeat this, but I've got a trick to save time and money.
- **아카시 준코**: I only go to restaurants that Haruna hasn't blown up.
- **아카시 준코**: It's a good first test to see if a restaurant's worth it.
- **아카시 준코**: A-Anyway!
- **아카시 준코**: The point is, I've got all the popular eateries in Shiba Town under my belt. And in my notebook!
- **아카시 준코**: But you know, they say experience is the best teacher. No offense, Sensei. Heeheehee.
- **아카시 준코**: Online reviews can be really unreliable. You never know when someone's being paid for a review or trying to get in on a viral trend.
- **아카시 준코**: And, even if the reviews are genuine, they're still based on someone else's tastes.
- **아카시 준코**: And there's no guarantee that those tastes will match yours.
- **아카시 준코**: But my notebook is a one-of-a-kind resource—a list of restaurants tailored just for me!
- **아카시 준코**: To celebrate the completion of my notebook...It's time to visit the #1 ranked restaurant in the neighborhood!
- **아카시 준코**: And you're coming too. Get ready, Sensei!
- **아카시 준코**: Of course! But don't worry, I'm buying tonight.
- **아카시 준코**: Wow. That was delicious! What did you think, Sensei?
- **아카시 준코**: Mmm...I'm glad you enjoyed it so much.
- **아카시 준코**: For me, rather than being the best, it's a place where you can get really good value.
- **아카시 준코**: This is just my opinion, but I think a restaurant has to really satisfy diners to be good. You should leave feeling full.
- **아카시 준코**: So that's why a good value is important.
- **아카시 준코**: But it can't just be cheap, of course. You need to balance value with flavor.
- **아카시 준코**: Okay, let's get going! Hey, excuse me! Can we get the check?
- **아카시 준코**: What's that?
- **아카시 준코**: Oh, you want me to share my notebook?
- **아카시 준코**: Uh... But today's the day that I pay for you.
- **아카시 준코**: If you pay, that throws everything off.
- **아카시 준코**: Sometimes you gotta let me pay...
- **아카시 준코**: It makes me uncomfortable if I never get to return the favor.
- **아카시 준코**: ...
- **아카시 준코**: Hahaha, come on...
- **아카시 준코**: Well then, I guess I have no choice!
- **아카시 준코**: Who could outwit a teacher?
