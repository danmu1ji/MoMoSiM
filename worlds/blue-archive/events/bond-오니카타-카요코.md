2. **「오니카타 카요코 인연 스토리 2」**

- **오니카타 카요코**: (The Prefect Team won't be happy if they find out I'm from Gehenna...)
- **오니카타 카요코**: (I'm in big trouble now.)
- **Cop**: Hmm? And who might you be?
- **오니카타 카요코**: Advisor?

- **Cop**: Hmm... Everything here seems to check out. So you do work for Schale.
- **오니카타 카요코**: ...Why did you help me?
- **오니카타 카요코**: I looked like I was in trouble? Well, I guess I was in a tight spot...
- **오니카타 카요코**: I'm used to people calling me suspicious, though. It's nothing to get worked up about.
- **오니카타 카요코**: Being interrogated by the police is practically a Tuesday...
- **오니카타 카요코**: I guess I don't give off a great first impression. I look pretty intimidating, so that lands me in hot water.
- **오니카타 카요코**: What, you wanna know what I was actually doing here?
- **오니카타 카요코**: Too bad. It's none of your business.
- **오니카타 카요코**: ...
- **오니카타 카요코**: *sigh* Fine. I was feeding a stray cat. Though it ran away as soon as the cops showed up.
- **오니카타 카요코**: Why didn't I just tell them that?
- **오니카타 카요코**: Because they would have thought I was lying, obviously.
- **오니카타 카요코**: You can hardly believe it yourself, I bet.
- **오니카타 카요코**: Do I look like the kind of girl who feeds stray cats for fun?
- **오니카타 카요코**: Wait...you don't think it's strange? Oh, uh...thanks.
- **오니카타 카요코**: I guess I owe you one, even if I don't want to.
- **오니카타 카요코**: But I'll make good and pay you back. Promise.

3. **「오니카타 카요코 인연 스토리 3」**

- **오니카타 카요코**: Hmm...? Sensei?
- **오니카타 카요코**: You're here early.
- **오니카타 카요코**: I would have done some cleaning by now if I knew you were going to be here so early.
- **오니카타 카요코**: They'll be coming in a little later. We were up late last night doing some work.
- **오니카타 카요코**: What about me? I'm fine. I never get a lot of sleep.
- **오니카타 카요코**: That's why I usually get here first and clean the office.
- **오니카타 카요코**: What, you think it'd be easier if I waited for the others so we could clean together?
- **오니카타 카요코**: I guess it would be...
- **오니카타 카요코**: I dunno. They'd probably think I was being too bossy if I said we should clean together. Besides, it'd be a pain in the ass.
- **오니카타 카요코**: Aru and Haruka do a decent job of cleaning up after themselves, so that makes it easier.
- **오니카타 카요코**: I'm good doing it solo rather than asking for help.
- **오니카타 카요코**: Were you not listening to a word I said, Sensei?
- **오니카타 카요코**: This is Problem Solver's problem. It has nothing to do with you.
- **오니카타 카요코**: Are you trying to keep me in your debt or something?
- **오니카타 카요코**: You're not? You genuinely just wanted to help?
- **오니카타 카요코**: But...
- **오니카타 카요코**: Never mind. Arguing over something like this is stupid.
- **오니카타 카요코**: I'm not usually the one to go ask for help, but I know it doesn't make sense to turn it down if someone offers.
- **오니카타 카요코**: It's not all that different than how Problem Solver operates now that I think about it. Heh.
- **오니카타 카요코**: Here. You can take care of the heavy lifting.

- **오니카타 카요코**: Sensei? Something wrong?

- **오니카타 카요코**: Here. Your umbrella.

- **오니카타 카요코**: I swear, I'm surrounded by klutzes...

- **오니카타 카요코**: Well...there's someone else who needs my help on rainy days like these.
- **오니카타 카요코**: It's not important. Don't worry about it.
- **오니카타 카요코**: What...? You want to tag along?
- **오니카타 카요코**: What...? You want to tag along?
- **오니카타 카요코**: Well, it's not like I particularly mind or anything.
- **오니카타 카요코**: But don't blame me if you end up thinking it's a waste of time...
- **오니카타 카요코**: Especially with all this rain. It's gonna be muddy.
- **오니카타 카요코**: *sigh* You're really not going to let this go, huh?
- **오니카타 카요코**: Fine. Do whatever you want.
- **오니카타 카요코**: ― You really didn't need to follow me all this way, Sensei.
- **오니카타 카요코**: ― I guess it's not here...
- **오니카타 카요코**: ― I only came here because it was raining.
- **오니카타 카요코**: ― It'll be fine. Cats can look after themselves.
- **오니카타 카요코**: ― Not being tied down by someone, not being hurt by others...can be a good thing...

- **오니카타 카요코**: ― That's all there is to it. Got it, Sensei?
- **오니카타 카요코**: ...The rain stopped.
- **오니카타 카요코**: Giving the umbrella back already?
- **오니카타 카요코**: Don't forget flash floods can strike out of nowhere. Hold on to it for now.
- **오니카타 카요코**: Besides, we see each other often enough. You can always return it later.
