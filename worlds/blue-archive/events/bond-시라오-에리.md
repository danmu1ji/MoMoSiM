2. **「시라오 에리 인연 스토리 2」**

- **시라오 에리**: Welcome, Master!
- **시라오 에리**: I was debating what we ought to do while we take a break together.
- **시라오 에리**: And then the spellbook told me...
- **시라오 에리**: ...we should paint together at the atelier today!
- **시라오 에리**: I'm sure we'll make an incredible masterpiece together.
- **시라오 에리**: And on that note, off we go!
- **시라오 에리**: I'm not even going to pick a theme to paint today.
- **시라오 에리**: Everything I put on this canvas will be pure emotion, raw artistic impulse. <br/>It'll burst like magic!
- **시라오 에리**: On a day like today, when I can feel my mana resonate...<br/>it's best to let my heart take charge.
- **시라오 에리**: Now, please focus your attention here!
- **시라오 에리**: We are! This is how we bring the magic into it!
- **시라오 에리**: Nyuhuhu... Which Arcana will reveal our fate today...?
- **시라오 에리**: Mm. The Upright Tower.
- **시라오 에리**: This card represents disaster and destruction. That can only mean...
- **시라오 에리**: I'll take this brush and STRIKE it across the canvas!
- **시라오 에리**: Just like THIS!!
- **시라오 에리**: Nyuhuhu. Now, to bring forth the next stroke...
- **시라오 에리**: Our guide shall be...?
- **시라오 에리**: Huh? Reversed Death?
- **시라오 에리**: A fixed state. Unchanging...
- **시라오 에리**: Aha! Then another strike it is!
- **시라오 에리**: It's all right! Believe in the power of magic!
- **시라오 에리**: Even if the canvas does rip,<br/>that only represents the power I've put into it!<br/>This is art! There's nothing to fear!
- **시라오 에리**: Now, then! Let us continue!
- **시라오 에리**: Ooh, look at this.
- **시라오 에리**: Enchanting! I can hardly believe I made it myself!
- **시라오 에리**: Behold! By the resonance of mana, and in the presence of my Master, I have created...
- **시라오 에리**: I call it "The Eternal Storm"!
- **시라오 에리**: If I can channel THIS much power, perhaps I can even master the 10th Circle, and spin entire worlds out of pure will!
- **시라오 에리**: Nyuhuhu, isn't it, though?
- **시라오 에리**: As they say, "Art is an explosion!"
- **시라오 에리**: I'm glad I brought you here, Master.<br/>I couldn't have brought this piece together on my own.
- **시라오 에리**: If I can keep up this pace, that next practical exam will be no trouble at all!
- **시라오 에리**: ...Oh, my. I must have burned through my reserves...
- **시라오 에리**: Master! Please, recharge my mana!
- **시라오 에리**: Oh my, those look delicious!
- **시라오 에리**: But...hmm, my hands are all covered in paint...
- **시라오 에리**: Nyuhuhu, well, only one thing to do about it. You'll have to feed me, Master.
- **시라오 에리**: I'm glad we understand each other.
- **시라오 에리**: Thank you for the snacks!
- **시라오 에리**: So good to have you on hand for such things, Master.
- **시라오 에리**: Nyuhuhu, certainly not.
- **시라오 에리**: Consider it the will of fate.

3. **「시라오 에리 인연 스토리 3」**

- **시라오 에리**: Ugh... Master! Good, you're here.
- **시라오 에리**: I don't know what to do.
- **시라오 에리**: I have a practical exam this afternoon...with a NOTORIOUSLY nitpicky proctor...
- **시라오 에리**: ...but they told me I can't bring anything magic-related, not even my witch hat!
- **시라오 에리**: "Don't wear the hat"?! They might as well tell me to walk around naked!
- **시라오 에리**: Ugh, what should I do?
- **시라오 에리**: I don't WANT to walk around naked, Master!
- **시라오 에리**: Wait, so YOU want me to go without the hat?
- **시라오 에리**: Y-You want me to be all embarrassed?
- **시라오 에리**: The only way I can fix this is by having a different proctor.
- **시라오 에리**: ...Hmm, I COULD curse the one proctor to have an all-day stomachache...
- **시라오 에리**: Nothing's impossible with magic. If I sneak some pills into their food...
- **시라오 에리**: U-Ugh...!
- **시라오 에리**: You're right, you're right. I shouldn't do that.
- **시라오 에리**: ...So there's no way to change the proctor, then.
- **시라오 에리**: I'll have to walk into that exam room completely unarmed.
- **시라오 에리**: Then I'll run out of mana, and they'll who-knows-what to me...!
- **시라오 에리**: Right now?
- **시라오 에리**: ...Well, 'if not now, when,' right?
- **시라오 에리**: *exhale*
- **시라오 에리**: All right. I'll take it off, here and now. I might as well be stripping naked, but I'll do it.
- **시라오 에리**: ...Look the other way for a moment, if you could, please.
- **시라오 에리**: *exhale*
- **시라오 에리**: Honestly, I'd rather wear nothing BUT the hat...
- **시라오 에리**: I-I know! It was a joke, just a joke!
- **시라오 에리**: Anyway...I suppose you can open your eyes now.
- **Department of Painting Student A**: Oh hey, Eri.
- **Department of Painting Student B**: Hi, Eri! No hat today, huh? It's been a while.
- **Department of Painting Student A**: We're practicing for the exam.
- **Department of Painting Student B**: What about you? Same?
- **Department of Painting Student B**: Well, let's all ace it together. I'll be rooting for you!

4. **「시라오 에리 인연 스토리 4」**

- **시라오 에리**: Hello, Master.
- **시라오 에리**: Wow, it's been a while since I've left the academy.
- **시라오 에리**: Hm. How fun...
- **시라오 에리**: Well, I am.
- **시라오 에리**: At least, compared to how things have been lately.
- **시라오 에리**: I can still remember it. That abomination I created on canvas in the classroom...
- **시라오 에리**: Aah! It's in my head again! This calls for memory-erasing magic...
- **시라오 에리**: Whew...
- **시라오 에리**: A different kind of magic, then, perhaps.
- **시라오 에리**: What do you suggest, Master? How should I recover my mana?
- **시라오 에리**: Ah, you've piqued my interest.
- **시라오 에리**: Let's test that spell. Lead on.
- **시라오 에리**: Hm, what is this place?
- **시라오 에리**: One of Master's favorites, hm?
- **시라오 에리**: ...I see.<br/>I can sense the traces of mana with the tip of my nose.
- **시라오 에리**: Yes, this will sate my hungry soul.
- **시라오 에리**: And my stomach. Let's have a seat.
- **시라오 에리**: Is there a particular dish you'd recommend, Master?
- **시라오 에리**: Hmm. Ramen that extracts thick mana from the essence of bones.
- **시라오 에리**: Yes, that'll be quite suitable.
- **시라오 에리**: Tonkotsu ramen it is, then.
- **시라오 에리**: Phew, I can feel my mana rushing back.
- **시라오 에리**: A library, you say.
- **시라오 에리**: I do have an interest in the occult, of course, but there is one certain book, banned in our academy, that I'd love to get my hands on most of all.
- **시라오 에리**: If I could only find it, my magic would evolve even further. Nyuhuhu.
- **시라오 에리**: It's called "Practical Rope Knotting Techniques."
- **시라오 에리**: If I could master such a skill, the Prefectural Council would never catch me again.
- **시라오 에리**: Oh, no. The reason is...far stranger.
- **시라오 에리**: Apparently, there was a performance artist who tied themselves with knots they'd learned from the book and walked around the streets.
- **시라오 에리**: Turtle shaped knots? Something like that. That's all I know.
- **시라오 에리**: You...wouldn't know anything about that, would you, Master?
- **시라오 에리**: Well, I suppose I'll find out myself when I read the book.
- **시라오 에리**: Nyuhuhu, ah, the anticipation.
- **시라오 에리**: Phew, this has been quite a joy.
- **시라오 에리**: Thank you, Master. It's been a wonderful day.
- **시라오 에리**: Perhaps a memento is in order.
- **시라오 에리**: What would you say to a photo together?
- **시라오 에리**: Nyuhuhu, allow me, then. Look over here...
- **시라오 에리**: One, two, three!
- **시라오 에리**: Let's see...
- **시라오 에리**: Ah, it came out well.
- **시라오 에리**: ...
- **시라오 에리**: Photographs certainly are convenient.
- **시라오 에리**: To capture an entire scene with nothing but the push of a button.
- **시라오 에리**: You know, when the camera was first invented, many painters fell into despair, or so I've heard.
- **시라오 에리**: Why spend hours or days painting an image when a photograph can do it in a flash?
- **시라오 에리**: But those painters didn't give up. Not ever.
- **시라오 에리**: They turned inward, painting the "scenery of the mind," the fields of their emotions, things only painters could express, and only on canvas.
- **시라오 에리**: A photograph can capture what's there, but a painting can capture the invisible.
- **시라오 에리**: When I read about those artists, and saw their work...I wanted to do the same.
- **시라오 에리**: Of course! I love the artistry as much as I love the occult.
- **시라오 에리**: Mark my words, one day, I'll be a renowned artist, and my paintings will hang on the walls of every home.
- **시라오 에리**: What? Right now?
- **시라오 에리**: I-I can't make you one RIGHT now...but if you can wait, I'll be sure to gift you a masterpiece!
- **시라오 에리**: ...Once I have the time and the inclination to sit still, I mean.
- **시라오 에리**: Thank you, Master. You helped me remember why I started art in the first place.
- **시라오 에리**: I'll do my best to live up to your belief in me!
- **시라오 에리**: Heeheehee...
- **시라오 에리**: It's fascinating, isn't it?
- **시라오 에리**: I was sour and exhausted today until you came along, and now I'm all refreshed.

5. **「시라오 에리 인연 스토리 5」**

- **시라오 에리**: Ooh! This way, Master!
- **시라오 에리**: Everything's all ready. Let's begin.
- **시라오 에리**: Correct.
- **시라오 에리**: I plan on drawing a portrait. With you as the model, Master.
- **시라오 에리**: Nyuhuhu, you should. We're painting under special circumstances today.
- **시라오 에리**: I'll be drawing using only the mana I have inside me.
- **시라오 에리**: A-Are you asking me to strip, Master...?
- **시라오 에리**: ...Well, I will take it off if that's what you ask of me. But not with all these people around.
- **시라오 에리**: Nyuhuhu, perhaps...
- **시라오 에리**: Nyuhuhu, yes. I do mean the hat.
- **시라오 에리**: Now then, without further ado, let's be off to the atelier.
- **시라오 에리**: Ah, not today, it isn't.
- **시라오 에리**: Because today, THIS will be our atelier!
- **시라오 에리**: I'll paint your portrait right here, the spot with the most foot traffic in all of Wildhunt Academy!
- **시라오 에리**: Painting here, in this crowd, should boost my confidence and then some.
- **시라오 에리**: Now then, please sit right there, if you would. I'll just break out the canvas...
- **Prefectural Council Member A**: Hm? Oh, she's practicing outdoors?
- **Prefectural Council Member B**: I'm not busy. You want to go check it out?
- **시라오 에리**: A-As expected. Nothing to worry about it.
- **시라오 에리**: ...Let's begin.
- **시라오 에리**: Here, in public, observe my magic, as I spin a whole world with my will!
- **시라오 에리**: ― ...This reminds me of the first time I ever picked up a brush.
- **시라오 에리**: ― Back then... I was so fresh and pure...
- **시라오 에리**: ― ...when I faced that canvas.
- **시라오 에리**: ― But now... I don't feel that way anymore.
- **시라오 에리**: ― I lost the magic of my inspiration...
- **시라오 에리**: ― and with it, I lost my confidence.
- **시라오 에리**: ― But then,
- **시라오 에리**: ― you told me, Master...
- **시라오 에리**: ― ...that dreams are a magic all their own,
- **시라오 에리**: ― right here in my heart.
- **시라오 에리**: ― That those dreams could keep me
- **시라오 에리**: ― from getting lost on the path.
- **시라오 에리**: ― Master...if you don't mind,
- **시라오 에리**: ― would you like to walk the path with me?
- **시라오 에리**: ― The path of magic,
- **시라오 에리**: ― full of colorful dreams!
- **시라오 에리**: How do you feel, Master? Did my heart reach you?
- **시라오 에리**: Heeheehee...
- **시라오 에리**: Not yet! I'll show you when I finish!
- **Department of Painting Student A**: Oh, it's Eri!
- **Department of Painting Student B**: Whoa, look at her picture, though...
- **시라오 에리**: E-Everyone? Wh-Wh-When did you get here?!
- **Department of Painting Student A**: That's a REALLY impressive portrait. And I can see it's got your color scheme!
- **Department of Painting Student B**: Don't be shy. It looks amazing!
- **시라오 에리**: Y-You mean it?
- **Department of Painting Student A**: Ah, sorry! I shouldn't distract you. I'll see you later!
- **Department of Painting Student B**: You rock, Eri! Keep it up!
- **시라오 에리**: Ehe, eheehee...
- **시라오 에리**: They really are wonderful.
- **시라오 에리**: ...And I was so worried over nothing.
- **시라오 에리**: I'd best keep at it, then!
- **시라오 에리**: Spinning these worlds of my own design!
