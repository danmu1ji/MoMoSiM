- **키리후지 나기사**: *sigh*

- **키리후지 나기사**: If anyone has any news or concerns to share, the floor is open.
- **Tea Party Member A**: Yes, President Nagisa. To start, there was a shootout last evening in the street near a cafe.
- **Tea Party Member A**: Witnesses confirmed that the dispute was over who should get the strawberry on top of a limited-time dessert.
- **키리후지 나기사**: A strawberry...?
- **키리후지 나기사**: Such a conflict over a single strawberry...?
- **Tea Party Member A**: Justice Task Force Vice President Hasumi was off duty, so President Tsurugi was dispatched. She successfully subdued the conflict.
- **키리후지 나기사**: ...If it was President Tsurugi, then the Remedial Knights must be busy.
- **Tea Party Member A**: Yeah, so many cans—I mean, students—were squashed that the Remedial Knights provided emergency services through the night.
- **키리후지 나기사**: Duly noted. The Remedial Knights likely exhausted considerable supplies...
- **키리후지 나기사**: Kindly remind the Knights to submit an emergency expense report if they require additional inventory.
- **키리후지 나기사**: Please approach. What is your report?
- **Tea Party Member B**: This is regarding the aforementioned Remedial Knights...
- **Tea Party Member B**: There was an incident where multiple patients fled in hysterics upon observing a medical-grade chainsaw in a treatment kit...
- **Tea Party Member B**: ...which led to a complete shutdown of a treatment room.
- **키리후지 나기사**: Ugh... Why are they parading that dreadful thing around...? *sigh*
- **키리후지 나기사**: ...For that, please conduct a full inspection of the Remedial Knights's medical equipment.
- **키리후지 나기사**: Now then... Is there anything else?
- **Tea Party Member C**: At 1 AM, multiple witnesses reported seeing a naked student loitering on the main building's rooftop.
- **Tea Party Member C**: Although only a few students spotted them, rumors are spreading that this figure was actually a ghost. We may need to address it.
- **키리후지 나기사**: ...
- **키리후지 나기사**: Fufu...
- **키리후지 나기사**: Naked...you say?
- **키리후지 나기사**: In the middle of the night... On the rooftop... Naked...
- **키리후지 나기사**: Are you so...certain it wasn't a phantasm? A ghost?
- **Tea Party Member C**: Miss Nagisa...?
- **키리후지 나기사**: I would indeed...greatly prefer...it to have been a ghost...
- **키리후지 나기사**: Then what could I do? How could I address that? It's out of my hands! Naked! Bare naked hands!
- **키리후지 나기사**: ...Heehee.
- **키리후지 나기사**: Heehee... Heeheehee... Heeheeheehee...
- **Tea Party Member A**: Miss Nagisa?!
- **Tea Party Member B**: She's out of it! Girl down!
- **Tea Party Member C**: Knights! You, call in the Remedial Knights! Hurry!
- **미소노 미카**: Nagi collapsed from working too hard?!
- **유리조노 세이아**: Yes. To be more precise, they diagnosed it as "burnout."
- **유리조노 세이아**: Fortunately, there's no problem with her physical health, but...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...I tried serving her generic iced black tea, poured from a PLASTIC bottle, and she didn't protest at all.
- **미소노 미카**: What? Hold on, this sounds dire!
- **유리조노 세이아**: Indeed. She was prescribed a mandatory vacation away from work in hopes that rest would restore her mental health.
- **미소노 미카**: "Restore her mental health"...
- **미소노 미카**: Would getting some limited-time desserts help? It's strawberry season!
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Given the meeting notes, that might make things worse.
- **유리조노 세이아**: The number-one priority is getting Nagisa away from work.
- **유리조노 세이아**: She's not going to get any rest as long as she's in school.
- **미소노 미카**: Hmm, so we need to find a place where Nagi can relax...outside of Trinity.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Yes. I believe that would be for the best.
- **미소노 미카**: Sure...
- **미소노 미카**: Outside of Trinity... A place that Nagi might enjoy... Refreshing...
- **미소노 미카**: Ah!
- **유리조노 세이아**: Mika?
- **미소노 미카**: Let's go to Shanhaijing!
- **유리조노 세이아**: Shanhaijing Academy...? Why?
- **미소노 미카**: There's a famous black tea workshop in the Shanhaijing outskirts!
- **미소노 미카**: I heard that if you sign up for a tour, you can participate in taste testing and tea blending!
- **미소노 미카**: Nagi likes black tea, and it's creative... I think it might help her feel better!
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...It's been a while since you came up with a logical idea, Mika.
- **미소노 미카**: Hm? What do you mean "it's been a while"?
- **미소노 미카**: Seia, just what DO you think of me...?
- **유리조노 세이아**: I acknowledge that it is a good idea.

3. **「티파티 스토리 3화」**

- **키리후지 나기사**: Where are you...taking me?
- **미소노 미카**: Oh, you're going to love the place, Nagi. ☆
- **키리후지 나기사**: A place...I...love...?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Apparently there is a famous black tea workshop on the outskirts of Shanhaijing, Nagisa.
- **유리조노 세이아**: We're taking you there right now.
- **미소노 미카**: Seia! Why did you spoil the surprise?!
- **유리조노 세이아**: We are trying to foster inner peace, not mental strain.
- **키리후지 나기사**: Inner peace...
- **키리후지 나기사**: In that case, you could have...just left me to recuperate quietly in the dorm...
- **미소노 미카**: Nagi, I KNOW you would just be rifling through documents in your bed.
- **미소노 미카**: So, none of that. You're going to kick back and take it easy today. ☆
- **미소노 미카**: ...With lots of aromatic black tea!
- **키리후지 나기사**: Black...tea...

- **미소노 미카**: Wow. What a workshop!

- **Black Tea Workshop Owner**: You'll learn all about the process of making black tea before embarking on a taste testing and curating adventure!
- **Black Tea Workshop Owner**: Some blends are only available in the workshop, so make sure to try everything you can!
- **미소노 미카**: Sounds great! Let's go, Nagi!
- **키리후지 나기사**: ...
- **Black Tea Workshop Owner**: Now, here's a wonderful place to start.
- **Black Tea Workshop Owner**: To make black tea, we start by dehydrating the tea leaves so that they wither. And to do so, we must...
- **Black Tea Workshop Owner**: Now then, shall we experience the joys of blending?
- **Black Tea Workshop Owner**: While we all enjoy a pure black tea, we will also explore the process of producing aromatic blends by mixing in other tea varietals, spices, and even fruits.
- **Black Tea Workshop Owner**: In these baskets, you'll find some of our many blending ingredients.
- **Black Tea Workshop Owner**: Feel free to get up close and personal. Bask in the aroma!
- **키리후지 나기사**: ...
- **키리후지 나기사**: *sniff*
- **키리후지 나기사**: ...
- **Black Tea Workshop Owner**: Smells nice, right?
- **키리후지 나기사**: ...Fufu.
- **Black Tea Workshop Owner**: Ah?!
- **유리조노 세이아**: Nagisa?!
- **미소노 미카**: She buried her face in the tea leaves...!
- **미소노 미카**: Nagi! Are you okay?! Can you breathe?
- **키리후지 나기사**: ...
- **키리후지 나기사**: Pff...
- **키리후지 나기사**: I don't wanna...
- **키리후지 나기사**: I want to stay in here...
- **키리후지 나기사**: I'll be black tea...
- **키리후지 나기사**: I wish to be steeped in hot water...and flow far, far away...
- **미소노 미카**: Nagi's broken...!
- **미소노 미카**: ...I mean, I guess she's sometimes like this, but...!
- **유리조노 세이아**: Calm down, Mika.
- **유리조노 세이아**: We'll buy the tea leaves that she's buried her face in.

- **미소노 미카**: Hmm, I see, Seia.

- **키리후지 나기사**: ...?
- **미소노 미카**: This reminds me of the playground we used to play in when we were little.
- **미소노 미카**: Except it's empty and full of weeds...
- **미소노 미카**: Nagi! Nagi! Over here!
- **키리후지 나기사**: I'm...going to...stay in...
- **미소노 미카**: Nope! ☆
- **키리후지 나기사**: Eh?
- **키리후지 나기사**: ...
- **키리후지 나기사**: (Need to sit...)
- **키리후지 나기사**: (Ah, a slide.)
- **키리후지 나기사**: Aaaah?!
- **키리후지 나기사**: It's hot...!
- **미소노 미카**: Ahaha! Classic Nagi, always hogging the slide!
- **키리후지 나기사**: Why are you laughing? Do you have any idea how hot the sun made this metal? Such a hazard should be...outlawed!
- **키리후지 나기사**: To think such a conveyance is designed for children! They would be roasted and seared. Outrageous!
- **미소노 미카**: Well, Nagi, at least you seem to be doing better!
- **키리후지 나기사**: What? Uh, um...
- **키리후지 나기사**: I am marginally recovered... I suppose?
- **미소노 미카**: I'm glad, Nagi.
- **미소노 미카**: I was so worried. You were confused and languishing like an abandoned fawn.
- **키리후지 나기사**: What kind of absurd analogy is that...?
- **키리후지 나기사**: ...Nevertheless, I suppose it's difficult to feel lethargic upon entering a playground.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Precisely. Let's keep your energy up.

- **키리후지 나기사**: ...?
- **키리후지 나기사**: Isn't this...highly-processed junk food?
- **유리조노 세이아**: I bought it from that convenience store while you two were bickering.

- **키리후지 나기사**: They already made the inside too sweet, then coated it in more sugar.

- **키리후지 나기사**: This last one reeks of artificial smoke and tastes overwhelmingly savory.
