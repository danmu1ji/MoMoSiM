- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Anyway, thanks again for coming all the way here.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: I was just about to head to the cafe for dessert when I thought of you.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Knowing how busy you've been, I thought you could use some sweets to perk you up.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Heehee.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Huh? Order?
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Oh, I already took care of that, Sensei.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: I was trying to save you time since your schedule is so packed!
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Our order should be out any minute now.
- **Clerk**: Order up! We have one cafe mocha, one mint chocolate chip latte, one slice of chocolate tiramisu, and one chocolate ganache cake.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: I'll get it, Sensei. You just wait here.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Ta-da!
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: I mainly ordered chocolate desserts for you, Sensei...
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Well...I heard it's good for fatigue.

- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Wow, really? That's good to hear.

- **쿠리무라 아이리**: I was worried that you wouldn't like it.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: That brain needs all the high-quality sugars it can get, you know!
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: I also eat a lot of sweets when I'm studying for exams. Heehee.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: All right, then!
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Let's dig in!

- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Oh, you're just out for a walk?
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Does that mean you're finally free?
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Yay! I've missed you, Sensei! Heehee.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: That's so like you, Sensei.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: I've been great, as usual.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: O-Oh, no. Don't worry about it.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: I guess I was just excited to see you again.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: I know how busy you've been, so I hope you're all right.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: I was wrapped up studying for exams, so I couldn't even come visit you.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Huh? Then what am I doing here?
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Like I said, I WAS wrapped up. It's all done now.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: I can finally go on a walk like I've been wanting to.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: ...
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: If I had known I'd run into you...
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Huh?
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: Oh, no. It's just, I had no idea I'd see you today.
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: So I don't have any desserts prepared...

- **쿠리무라 아이리**: ― I'm enjoying myself because...
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: ― Well, you know. It's just the two of us here...
- **쿠리무라 아이리**: ― My heart feels like it's going to leap out of my chest.
