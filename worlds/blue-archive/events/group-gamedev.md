2. **「GameDev 스토리 2화」**

- **사이바 모모이**: Ugh...argh!
- **사이바 미도리**: What are you up to, Sis?
- **사이바 모모이**: Don't distract me, Midori! This is a critical moment!
- **사이바 모모이**: One more hit... Just one more hit and I'll be victorious!
- **사이바 미도리**: Oh, Student Fighter 2! You've been playing this game a lot lately, huh?
- **사이바 모모이**: Yup, they just added online matchmaking in the last update. Now I can play in real time with people at the arcade!
- **사이바 모모이**: I was starting to get bored because I was only finding easy opponents...
- **사이바 모모이**: But after a while, I finally ran into a worthy challenger!
- **사이바 모모이**: Good, I got 'em cornered! Now I just have to use a command grab to cancel their guard...
- **사이바 모모이**: What the heck? They did a guard evasion into an uppercut?!
- **사이바 모모이**: Followed by a big combo...and a finisher too?!
- **사이바 모모이**: What, seriously?! I made one tiny mistake at the end and I got the tables turned on me!
- **사이바 미도리**: So close. It was by a whisker!
- **사이바 모모이**: Nothing you can do about it, though. A match is a match.
- **사이바 모모이**: I should send them a thank-you message for that good game...
- **사이바 모모이**: "Peach_Fighter: Good game, Sukeban312. Let's have a rematch sometime!"
- **사이바 모모이**: Wh-What? That's completely uncalled for!
- **사이바 모모이**: "Peach_Fighter: Come on, what's with that attitude? You're acting like a child!"
- **사이바 모모이**: Granny?!
- **사이바 모모이**: "Peach_Fighter: I almost beat you, remember?!"
- **사이바 모모이**: Ugh! ...Where do you live?! Give me your address right now!
- **사이바 모모이**: ...
- **사이바 미도리**: ...Ooh boy, that's rough.
- **사이바 모모이**: What the heck...?
- **사이바 모모이**: What the heck is with this toxic player?!
- **사이바 모모이**: Well, since they did that...
- **사이바 모모이**: We're going on a real-life hunt!
- **하나오카 유즈**: What? Us too...?
- **Aris**: You want us to come with you?
- **사이바 모모이**: Of course! Intimidating the opponent is everything!
- **사이바 모모이**: I'm a hundred percent sure that we're dealing with a video game nerd, so if we show up in a big group, they'll get scared and apologize!
- **하나오카 유즈**: Aren't we just as nerdy as they are, though...?
- **사이바 미도리**: Do you really have to get so worked up, Sis? It's only a game. You lost and they trash talked you. That's all.
- **사이바 모모이**: Just a game?! This is a matter of the Game Development Department's dignity!
- **사이바 모모이**: That person not only trampled over my gesture of sportsmanship, but also called me a granny!
- **사이바 모모이**: A granny... Ugh!
- **사이바 모모이**: Absolutely unforgivable!
- **사이바 미도리**: You're angrier about being called a granny than losing the match?
- **사이바 모모이**: Actually, toxic players are a bad influence on the fighting game scene.
- **사이바 모모이**: If this kind of unpleasant experience becomes the norm every time someone plays a fighting game, who will ever play them?
- **사이바 모모이**: For the sake of a clean gaming environment, we must teach this toxic player a lesson!
- **Aris**: I agree. When I was playing an MMORPG a few days ago, my character got killed by a max-level player. It was so painful that I just wanted to quit the game completely...
- **Aris**: But since then, I caught up to their level and have already had my revenge!
- **하나오카 유즈**: You've already reached max level? Seriously, your obsessiveness scares me sometimes...
- **사이바 미도리**: But don't you taunt the opponent sometimes when you win, Sis?
- **사이바 미도리**: As soon as you finished a game of Fruitjellies with me, you taunted me! You said, "I could beat you using my feet"!
- **사이바 모모이**: Uh, well... That's...um...
- **사이바 모모이**: It's okay to trash-talk people you know! It's much more rude to say things like that to total strangers.
- **사이바 모모이**: Whatever! Anyway, when I searched this person's match history, I found out that they logged in ten minutes ago from the nearby arcade.
- **사이바 모모이**: Which means that we can find them if we head over there right away. Let's track them down and get them to apologize for what they said!
- **사이바 미도리**: I happen to have some business at the arcade myself... I guess I wouldn't mind joining you.
- **Aris**: It is like a quest to defeat the general of a demon lord! A four-player raid!
- **하나오카 유즈**: Uh...I don't have any business at the arcade, and I don't wanna get in a fight, either...
- **하나오카 유즈**: ...Can I sit this one out?
- **사이바 모모이**: No! In a group boss battle, each and every member has a critical role!
- **사이바 모모이**: Zone control works best when there are four players, each blocking a side!
- **하나오카 유즈**: But still...
- **사이바 모모이**: Oh, yeah! The new console that you've been waiting for should be on display there... Aren't you curious about what kind of new functions it has?
- **하나오카 유즈**: ...A new console?!
- **사이바 모모이**: You never go out on your own anyway. Let's take this opportunity to go see it together. What do you say?

3. **「GameDev 스토리 3화」**

- **사이바 미도리**: It's much bigger than the last time we were here. Did they renovate the building or something?
- **사이바 모모이**: Seriously. Whoa, they brought in the newest Space Cat machine!
- **Aris**: Yuzu, there is the new console display!
- **하나오카 유즈**: Whoa... Finally, the fifteenth generation of consoles is here...
- **하나오카 유즈**: Let's see... They added a new cooling mechanism on the side. That will definitely improve the problem that the previous gen had.
- **하나오카 유즈**: Not only that, the response rate is much faster now, thanks to this high-end gaming memory...
- **사이바 모모이**: Haha... Yuzu always gets so excited about gaming stuff.
- **사이바 미도리**: Let's go play Space Cat, Sis!
- **사이바 모모이**: Okay! But I won't go easy on you, so brace your—
- **사이바 모모이**: ...Wait, this isn't what we came here for!
- **사이바 모모이**: Did everyone forget why we're here? We have to find that toxic player right now!
- **사이바 미도리**: Actually, I came to buy a new controller.
- **Aris**: We have a lot of monsters to slay, Momoi. Which game should we play first?
- **하나오카 유즈**: I'd much rather just stay here and look at the new console than fight someone...
- **사이바 모모이**: No! Even at this very moment, that player might be trash talking others!
- **사이바 모모이**: If they keep doing so, and more and more people do the same...there might not be a single fighting game player left!
- **사이바 미도리**: Aren't you being a little too pessimistic there?
- **Aris**: This is a sacred battle to save the world...
- **Aris**: Heroes, it is high time that we head to the demon lord's den!
- **사이바 모모이**: Right, let's hurry to the section where the Student Fighter 2 machine is!
- **사이바 미도리**: There aren't as many people here as I expected. Maybe the guy already left?
- **사이바 모모이**: No, there's no way. Check the IDs on the screens.
- **사이바 모모이**: Hmm... It's not this player...
- **사이바 모모이**: Not this player, either...
- **Mean Gamer**: Yahoo, another win! I'm on a winning streak!
- **Defeated Gamer**: Ugh, I lost again...even though I used my secret move!
- **Mean Gamer**: You mean that clunky combo was your secret move? Are you serious?
- **Mean Gamer**: My grandma is better than you!
- **Defeated Gamer**: What? It's just a game! You can't say stuff like that!
- **Defeated Gamer**: Take that back!
- **Mean Gamer**: Why should I? You're a moron who can't even play the game right!
- **Defeated Gamer**: Ugh. I'm gonna tell my mom!
- **사이바 미도리**: Yikes...I can't believe that there's a player out there who'd trash talk in real life like that.
- **사이바 모모이**: I found it! The same ID I saw earlier!
- **Mean Gamer**: Hmm? What, you guys want to challenge me too?
- **사이바 모모이**: You're Sukeban312, right? You're the one who called me a granny online!
- **Mean Gamer**: You mean...you're Peach_Fighter?
- **Mean Gamer**: Haha, look at you. You're just as wimpy as I expected.
- **사이바 모모이**: What did you just say?!
- **사이바 미도리**: You're so rude! I can't believe you're still saying that stuff face-to-face.
- **Aris**: Momoi, let us cleanse this evildoer with the Sword of Light!
- **사이바 모모이**: If you don't apologize for your abusive language, I will show you hell! Apologize, right now!
- **Mean Gamer**: Nope, not gonna do it.
- **사이바 모모이**: Why not?!
- **Mean Gamer**: I don't apologize to people weaker than me.
- **Mean Gamer**: It would hurt my pride to bow to an inferior player.
- **사이바 모모이**: Why, you little...!
- **사이바 미도리**: Should I shoot, Sis?
- **Aris**: Hero, I am ready to draw my sword. Just say the word.
- **Mean Gamer**: Heh heh. If you think that's so unfair, then why don't we play Student Fighter 2 right now to settle the score? If you can get one round off of me, I'll apologize for what I said!
- **사이바 모모이**: Okay, fine! But I won't go easy on you this time...
- **사이바 모모이**: Get ready to get on your knees and apologize!
- **Mean Gamer**: Well, then...
- **Momoi & Mean Gamer**: Game on!
- **Mean Gamer**: Pfft! Wow, you lost again after talking such a big game!
- **Mean Gamer**: What a total scrub! Heh...
- **사이바 모모이**: Hold on! You can't just sit in the bottom left corner and wait! That's cheating!
- **사이바 모모이**: How could you fight me only using pokes?!
- **Mean Gamer**: What are you talking about? These moves are built into the game, so they're completely fair. If you don't want to get hit by them, then come up with a way to counter them. I mean, aren't you embarrassed for whining about some made-up rules?
- **사이바 모모이**: Ugh...dammit!
