# 야스모리 미노리 인연 스토리

- 등장: 야스모리 미노리, 선생(샬레)

2. **「야스모리 미노리 인연 스토리 2」**

- **Labor Student A**: It's been ages since I've had a job. I was ready to write off this whole month.
- **Labor Student B**: The timing couldn't be more perfect. There are some new goods in the shop that I was eying, and I need the cash.
- **Labor Student A**: I just hope it's nothing too hard this time. I hate lugging sand and cement around.
- **Labor Student B**: I hear you. Maybe some pipe fitting, or putting together a little home extension... That would be nice.
- **야스모리 미노리**: I need five for scaffolding, ten for steel framing!
- **Labor Student A**: Me! I can do it!
- **Labor Student B**: Oh, me too, me too!
- **야스모리 미노리**: All right. They're expecting us at the job site, so everybody in!
- **Labor Student A**: Hey, you! Stop lollygagging!
- **Labor Student B**: You planning to pay us if we miss this gig?
- **야스모리 미노리**: Everybody in? Here we go!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Good morning, laborers of Kivotos!
- **야스모리 미노리**: I am Yasumori Minori of Red Winter Academy. I am responsible for managing today's work site!
- **야스모리 미노리**: This is the first job in a while for most of us, so let's show them what we can do!
- **야스모리 미노리**: And remember, a safe environment and fair wages are the right of every worker! If you feel you haven't received that here, come see me at the office here on site!
- **Labor Student**: Yeah!!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Before we begin, I'll lead you in a cheer.
- **Labor Student**: Woo!!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Safety first!
- **Labor Student**: All right! All right! All right!
- **야스모리 미노리**: All right! Everyone stay safe out there!
- **야스모리 미노리**: ...Oh?
- **야스모리 미노리**: [USERNAME] Sensei? What are you doing here?
- **야스모리 미노리**: Ah, so you were swept up by this morning's laborers...
- **야스모리 미노리**: I'm sorry for bringing you here against your wishes. The Labor Party uses that bus stop as a gathering spot in the morning.
- **야스모리 미노리**: But now what do we do? I can't spare any vehicles to take you home, and we aren't close to any bus stops here...
- **야스모리 미노리**: Oh? Would you like to take a tour while you're here, then?
- **야스모리 미노리**: I can't replace the time that you've lost, but after the workday, I'll treat you to an extra-large katsudon from a place nearby.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Their sauce is thick and the meat is plenty, so it's a hearty reward for a hard day's work.
- **야스모리 미노리**: If you ask me, the workday isn't over until you have a katsudon or a beef bowl to fill your stomach.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Now, before we begin... Put this on.
- **야스모리 미노리**: A hard hat and a hi-vis belt. The first most important thing on a job site is safety! And the second is also safety!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Some of the equipment we use here is dangerous even for the sturdy students of Kivotos.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Stay away from any structure that looks unstable. And no matter what, watch your step!
- **야스모리 미노리**: All right!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Now then... Let's get to work!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Welding team! Get those safety handrails up!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Plaster team! We need 3 bags of mortar here! I want all those gaps in the plywood filled!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Impressed? Huh. Everyone seems a little slow today, if you ask me...
- **야스모리 미노리**: Don't be so sure. The reason the Labor Party has so much trouble getting jobs is because we're slower than other groups.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Of course, our work quality is second to none in all of Kivotos...
- **야스모리 미노리**: And I think the students here work at a fine pace, so I'm not sure why we're always behind schedule.
- **Boss**: Ah, President Minori, hello! I believe this is our first time meeting in person, yes?
- **Boss**: I'm the president of Magma Construction. It's a pleasure, a real pleasure.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Ah, so you're our client. Thank you for giving us this opportunity.
- **Boss**: Oh, it's nothing, nothing at all. Why, I should thank you for coming here on such short notice!
- **야스모리 미노리**: ...What brings you to the job site? Would you like to inspect our work?
- **야스모리 미노리**: I can personally vouch that we are following safety regulations to the letter, so you have nothing to worry about.
- **Boss**: Oh, no, it's nothing like that...
- **Boss**: To be quite frank with you, dear... I had a teeny, tiny favor to ask of you.
- **야스모리 미노리**: A favor.
- **Boss**: You see—and oh, this is dreadfully embarrassing—I had an unexpected expense, and my funds aren't exactly liquid just now.
- **Boss**: I just don't know if I have the funds to pay the Labor Party wages today.
- **Boss**: Oh, but don't worry! I'll pay you back with interest tomorrow, so if we could just keep this between us—
- **야스모리 미노리**: Attention, everyone! Stop work! Shut it down!
- **야스모리 미노리**: No labor for no pay!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Labor is the fight for human survival! There can be no labor without fair and equal payment!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Anyone who comes to me, trying to get free work with honeyed words...
- **야스모리 미노리**: ...is a capitalist pig who doesn't know the true value of labor!
- **Boss**: I-I never said I wouldn't pay! I only need more time...

3. **「야스모리 미노리 인연 스토리 3」**

- **야스모리 미노리**: Hmm...
- **야스모리 미노리**: Oh, Sensei? What are you doing at the academy library?
- **야스모리 미노리**: I hope you're not looking for the academy's administrative records...
- **야스모리 미노리**: I wouldn't bother. Most of Red Winter Academy's records are redacted and changed whenever we have a coup, so there aren't many originals left.
- **야스모리 미노리**: You might have better luck checking the Knowledge Liberation Front's selection. Those are mostly intact.
- **야스모리 미노리**: They even have old, out-of-print novels. So if you have a book in mind, you should stop by.
- **야스모리 미노리**: What am I doing. Here. In the library.
- **야스모리 미노리**: I'm here to read books. I thought it'd be obvious...
- **야스모리 미노리**: You don't think I'm like that child from the Secretariat, who hides out in the library because it's a good place for a nap?
- **야스모리 미노리**: I guess the Labor Party has kind of a rough image. But to do good work, you need good manners and learning.
- **야스모리 미노리**: And how are we supposed to understand our work contracts if we aren't decent readers?
- **야스모리 미노리**: That's why it's important for me to keep up my reading. As president of the Labor Party.
- **야스모리 미노리**: When I'm not working or striking, I'm often here in the library, reading. It helps that it's warmer than most of the other buildings on campus.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Well, sci-fi and essays always get me thinking. But my favorite would have to be class literature.
- **야스모리 미노리**: You know. Literature written for the enlightenment and emancipation of the working class! Literature that agitates against the capital elite!
- **야스모리 미노리**: As a worker, I find it very relatable. And it teaches me how to deal with capitalists!
- **야스모리 미노리**: There are so many books to choose from, but let's see... My favorites...
- **야스모리 미노리**: The Fox and the Crane.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Ah, you've heard of it! Well, it is in the library of most schools, so that makes sense.
- **야스모리 미노리**: It's a classic of guerrilla literature, in which the working-class crane uses its wits to wage a revolution against the sly capitalist fox.
- **야스모리 미노리**: The fox promises to share its food equally, but it serves the food on a flat plate that the crane can't eat out of, thereby hoarding resources for itself...
- **야스모리 미노리**: Yes, it is.
- **야스모리 미노리**: But the crane learns from this, see? So when the fox comes to the crane's house, it serves dinner in a narrow jug. The fox can't get at the food, thus ensuring victory for the crane!
- **야스모리 미노리**: It's a fable, see? The moral of the story is that you must lure your enemy into an environment where you hold the advantage!
- **야스모리 미노리**: What else... Oh, right, The Three Little Pigs!
- **야스모리 미노리**: This book is essential reading for any worker in construction. It deals with lessons about on-site safety, recording two cases of failure and one case of success.
- **야스모리 미노리**: The first and second pigs ignored civic codes and used cheap materials, and their houses collapsed just from a strong wind! The third pig, however, used approved materials, and his house was safe.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Of course, it would have been better if he framed out the house in rebar and concrete first...
- **야스모리 미노리**: But it's a children's story, so it can be forgiven for going light on the details.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Ah, and I can't forget about The North Wind and the Sun!
- **야스모리 미노리**: The winds and the sun conspire to take a poor traveler's cloak through a combination of hard and soft tactics, just like how the capitalist class divides workers and tricks them into fighting each other!
- **야스모리 미노리**: The moral is that the worker must never, ever let her guard down, and always keep the fighting spirit of—

4. **「야스모리 미노리 인연 스토리 4」**

- **Labor Party Member A**: Oh? Isn't that Schale's Sensei?
- **Labor Party Member B**: What brings you to the labor office?
- **Labor Party Member A**: You aren't here for a tour of the job site, are you? Work was canceled today 'cause of bad weather.
- **Labor Party Member A**: Wow! These are those limited-edition chocolates from Angel 24!
- **Labor Party Member B**: Oh, look, there's custard pudding too! And it's not watery, like the pudding we get from the Secretariat...
- **Labor Party Member A**: Thanks, Sensei!
- **Labor Party Member A**: Good question. She always seems to vanish whenever work gets canceled unexpectedly...
- **Labor Party Member B**: She usually goes to the work site to make sure all of our materials and equipment are secure.
- **Labor Party Member A**: Wow, President Minori sure works hard!
- **Labor Party Member B**: I don't know if she went out there today, though. It's cold enough to give you frostbite out there...
- **Labor Party Member A**: Yeah, you'd have to be crazy to go out and work on a day like today. I bet she went to the library, instead.
- **Labor Party Member A**: Already?
- **Labor Party Member B**: Thanks for the snacks, Sensei. Come visit us on the job site sometime!
- **Labor Party Member A**: Oh, and be careful not to get lost! Red Winter Academy is pretty big.
- **???**: Who's butchering my name...?
- **야스모리 미노리**: Oh, [USERNAME] Sensei! What are you doing here?
- **야스모리 미노리**: I'm sure I sent a notice on MomoTalk that work was canceled... Didn't you get it?
- **야스모리 미노리**: I'm only doing what I was contracted to do. Extra rewards like this are too much...
- **야스모리 미노리**: Now that I think of it, the Red Winter Office has never sent us snacks before. Nor have our clients...
- **야스모리 미노리**: It looks like you're the only one looking after laborers like me, Sensei.
- **야스모리 미노리**: I'm just walking the grounds and doing some safety inspections.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Because work was canceled so suddenly, some of our equipment and tarps and things were left laying around. I have to secure it all, so it doesn't fly away in the wind.
- **야스모리 미노리**: I can't think of any better way to enjoy a rare free day like this...
- **야스모리 미노리**: Ah, there's actually something I want to show you.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Mind following me? Up this way.
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― Hahaha... On a cold, brisk day like this, I can see forever.
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― Sensei, don't just stand there. Come and take a look.
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― There's nothing to worry about.
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― This scaffolding is so sturdy it can hold an entire elephant.
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― Come to think of it, this scaffolding
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― is a lot like the Labor Party.
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― Construction is impossible without it, but
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― it must disappear
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― like it never existed once the work is done...
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― Maybe that's why...
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― I love standing up here on the scaffolding
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― and looking down at the job site from the top of the world.
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― This is proof of the hard work of everyone
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― who stood on the edge and built these walls with their own hands.
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― ...This proof, nowhere to be found on a finished building,
- **야스모리 미노리**: ― is what I wanted to share with you, Sensei.
- **야스모리 미노리**: So, what did you think? Looking down from so high up?
- **야스모리 미노리**: Surely you've been higher up than that before...
- **야스모리 미노리**: Hm. Maybe a little height is scarier, since your brain can understand it, compared to the view from a tall building...
- **야스모리 미노리**: I know! Let's find a spot too high up for your brain to even comprehend.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Let's go to the very top, Sensei!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Up there, I bet you'll be able to take your time and really enjoy the view!

5. **「야스모리 미노리 인연 스토리 5」**

- **야스모리 미노리**: Hmm...
- **야스모리 미노리**: Hmmmmmmmm...
- **야스모리 미노리**: Ah, sorry! I'm not trying to sabotage Schale's work with my hmm-ing.
- **야스모리 미노리**: I just...had something on my mind.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Well, it actually has to do with you, [USERNAME] Sensei.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Remember when we were talking on MomoTalk about how to make everyone in Kivotos happy?
- **야스모리 미노리**: You know, society's suffering can be traced back to the bourgeoisie monopolizing precious resources, like pudding.
- **야스모리 미노리**: But there's only so much pudding, and some resources are more...intangible.
- **야스모리 미노리**: I was wondering how a resource like that should be distributed.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Do you know how many students want to be on Schale's duty roster?
- **야스모리 미노리**: Duty here means spending alone time with you, Sensei, and the work itself is pretty light. So many students sign up to be included.
- **야스모리 미노리**: In a way, Schale's duty roster is an intangible resource that makes people happy!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Of course, your time isn't limitless. So duty days aren't equally distributed among the students, and only some students get to take more than one.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Isn't this a kind of monopoly? A small group get all of your attention. It's social inequality!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Unlike pudding, your time cannot be equally distributed.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Even if we ensured that Schale duty was equally split, there are so many students that some will graduate before they even get to meet you.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Alternatively, we could assign a couple hundred students to Schale every day...
- **야스모리 미노리**: That's true. Is it worth it to make everyone happy if it means Schale can't do its work?
- **야스모리 미노리**: But all of this is an issue because there's only one of you, and there are many of us.
- **야스모리 미노리**: The ideal solution would be to divide Sensei into multiple pieces and distribute you...
- **야스모리 미노리**: Hahaha! I kid, I kid.
- **야스모리 미노리**: No one would be happy with a solution like that.
- **야스모리 미노리**: If it were so easy to make everyone happy, there'd be no need for my comrades at Red Winter Academy to continue the good fight.
- **야스모리 미노리**: No, that isn't possible!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Even I feel more at ease, and more happy, when I'm around you.
- **야스모리 미노리**: If I could, I'd bring you with me to the job site every day—
- **야스모리 미노리**: Ah, what am I saying?!
- **야스모리 미노리**: I know this is a private conversation, but as president of the Labor Party, I need to set an example to the proletariat...
- **야스모리 미노리**: And here I am, thinking of monopolizing you like some dirty bourgeois!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Lately, I've been spending more time with...capitalists. Am I being infected by their greed?
- **야스모리 미노리**: Sensei, I must perform a self-criticism session!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Please sit there and observe. Do not hesitate to reprimand me with utmost severity for what I am about to confess!
