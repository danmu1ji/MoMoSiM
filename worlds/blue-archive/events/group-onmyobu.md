2. **「Onmyobu 스토리 2화」**

- **쿠와카미 카호**: The next item on the agenda...
- **쿠와카미 카호**: The Yin-Yang Club will be holding a concert called "A Hyakkiyako Thanksgiving" for the festival.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: This festival is an important tradition for us all, but there's no need to feel any pressure.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: It should be routine, so I'll handle directing and organizing it.
- **와라쿠 치세**: Wait, please.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Yes, Chise? Did you have any questions?
- **와라쿠 치세**: Mmm...
- **와라쿠 치세**: I want to be in charge.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Of the concert?!
- **아마치 니야**: Director Chise? Nyahaha!
- **아마치 니야**: I like the sound of that.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Y-You do? ...I mean, I appreciate the offer, Chise, really!
- **쿠와카미 카호**: But there's not much work to be done regardless. We'll be reusing old equipment and props...
- **와라쿠 치세**: It's a special concert.
- **와라쿠 치세**: I want to do it, okay?
- **쿠와카미 카호**: (Sh-She's blindingly cute!)
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Ahem! In that case...
- **쿠와카미 카호**: I'll be there with you every step of the way. I'll help you with buying any additional props, staging...
- **쿠와카미 카호**: I'll make sure this experience is smooth as silk for you, Chise.
- **아마치 니야**: Aren't you going a little overboard?
- **아마치 니야**: It'll be totally fiiine.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: This falls under my purview as the vice president of the Yin-Yang Club.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: I've always coordinated our events and concerts...
- **쿠와카미 카호**: (It's partially because I would feel terrible if Chise undertook my responsibilities...)
- **쿠와카미 카호**: (I also... I do trust Chise, but I can't help but worry a little bit...)
- **와라쿠 치세**: It's okay.
- **와라쿠 치세**: This time, Kaho can take a break.
- **와라쿠 치세**: The sun is out. I have heard sunbathing is fun.
- **와라쿠 치세**: You sunbathe, and I'll direct.
- **와라쿠 치세**: Okay. I'm going shopping now.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Wait, Chise! Come back!
- **아마치 니야**: Oh my. Just like that, she's gone.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: This is entirely out of character for Chise!
- **쿠와카미 카호**: It couldn't be... I-Is Chise going through her rebellious phase?!
- **아마치 니야**: Nyahaha! I don't think that's possible for her. Why not just hand over the reins on this one, Kaho?
- **아마치 니야**: Chise's not the type to do something without intention behind it.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: I-I know that! It's not that I don't think she would do a good job...
- **쿠와카미 카호**: B-But what if she gets lost at the mall?
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Or she could get kidnapped! Again!
- **아마치 니야**: Worrywart Kaho rears her fearsome head!
- **아마치 니야**: We're really never going to live down the "Princess Waraku" incident, are we? She's just going shopping.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: How do you know it won't happen again? Hm?!
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Exactly! You do not! And so, I will be...keeping an eye on her!
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Hurry up, Niya!
- **아마치 니야**: Why am I getting dragged into this?
- **Umbrella Shop Owner**: Welcome!
- **Umbrella Shop Owner**: What a surprise! We're honored to serve the one and only Chise here.
- **와라쿠 치세**: Hi. Thank you.
- **아마치 니야**: Don't you feel like a stalker right now?
- **아마치 니야**: Maybe we'll get arrested! Nyahaha!
- **쿠와카미 카호**: We'll only get caught if you keep making so much noise!
- **쿠와카미 카호**: I am not stalking her! I'm respecting her independence while still keeping her safe, okay?
- **아마치 니야**: Oops. Did she just turn her head? She's gonna hear you, Kaho.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Ugh!
- **아마치 니야**: Eh, well we're already here. Might as well see what happens.
- **Umbrella Shop Owner**: I'm guessing you're here to get props for the concert.
- **Umbrella Shop Owner**: Then let me recommend this...
- **Umbrella Shop Owner**: Ah. Found it!
- **Umbrella Shop Owner**: Kaho loves using this indigo umbrella for her concerts.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Great choice, Chise!

3. **「Onmyobu 스토리 3화」**

- **Chise Fan A**: I'm so excited! Chise's directing the concert this time!
- **Chise Fan C**: I cleared space on my camera's memory card just for today!
- **아마치 니야**: Today's the big day! And we still have no idea what her plan is.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: It seems that's the situation.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: I've long since learned how tight-lipped Chise is when she wants to be.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: So I've come to terms with the fact that my role today is not to be a director or coordinator.
- **아마치 니야**: Why're you watching from backstage?
- **아마치 니야**: You don't want to go out there?
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Today is Chise's day to shine.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Her actions make no sense to me at all, but I'll support her regardless.
- **아마치 니야**: Secret agents, working in the shadows!
- **아마치 니야**: Nyahaha. It's got a nice ring to it.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: I don't think we're exactly on the same page...
- **쿠와카미 카호**: But we're both here for Chise.
- **Host**: Thank you all for coming to the "A Hyakkiyako Thanksgiving" concert!
- **Host**: This Thanksgiving Festival is a long-held tradition at Hyakkiyako. It is a day for us to pause and show gratitude to those who keep our community running.
- **Host**: And we have a very special treat for the concert! Today's star is none other than Hyakkiyako's beloved idol, Princess Waraku Chise!
- **Host**: Let's give Chise a warm welcome!
- **와라쿠 치세**: Hello.
- **Host**: First, we have the umbrella dance.
- **Host**: Traditional Hyakkiyako umbrellas are dyed with traditional methods, passed down through generations, our dignified history represented in the indigo—
- **Host**: A-Ah! I mean...pink? Umbrella? It, um...is just like the cherry blossoms we all so enjoy! Wonderful!
- **Host**: Onto the next stage! You'll see our traditional kokeshi...
- **Host**: Ahem. Fox and Daruma doll!
- **아마치 니야**: We're not the only ones she didn't explain anything to.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: It doesn't follow the usual script at all! Our Thanksgiving traditions...
- **쿠와카미 카호**: The audience must be utterly confused!
- **Chise Fan A**: Chise really is the cutest!
- **Chise Fan B**: Chise always knocks it out of the park!
- **Chise Fan C**: That's our idol!
- **쿠와카미 카호**: ...They don't care! I'm grateful, but how is this working?!
- **Host**: It's finally over... Ahem! I mean, we've come to the end of the "A Hyakkiyako Thanksgiving" concert.
- **Host**: Our star of the evening, Chise, will say a few parting words.
- **Host**: Take it away, Chise!
- **Host**: Preferably by following the script!
- **와라쿠 치세**: Thank you for watching my performance.
- **와라쿠 치세**: Did anyone notice that I made changes to the concert?
- **와라쿠 치세**: Yes. I did it to show gratitude to my important people.
- **Host**: A-Ah, yes! Of course that's the reason!
- **Host**: Could you tell us who they are, Chise?
- **와라쿠 치세**: My important people are...
- **와라쿠 치세**: ...President Niya and Vice President Kaho.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: D-Did I hear that correctly?!
- **아마치 니야**: Me?
- **와라쿠 치세**: Without the president and vice president, I would have never gotten to become Princess Waraku.
- **와라쿠 치세**: So today I wanted them to have fun being in the audience.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: That's why you changed the props and colors?
- **쿠와카미 카호**: You did it for us...
- **아마치 니야**: Nyahaha! So all the pieces fall into place.
- **아마치 니야**: Chise wanted the pink umbrella, because Kaho's favorite color is pink.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: And, instead of a Kokeshi doll, you picked dolls that looked like Niya and me.
- **아마치 니야**: What do you mean "looked like Niya"? Do you really think so, Kaho?
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Moving along... Look, Niya. You drink that brand of orange juice almost every morning.
- **쿠와카미 카호**: All of this merch Chise got is of my favorite character!
- **쿠와카미 카호**: That's even mine and Niya's favorite pudding flavor...
- **아마치 니야**: Ah, yes. Wow! Amazing! I'm truly impressed, Chise.
- **아마치 니야**: But before we get all mushy gushy...
- **아마치 니야**: Tell me. In what ways do I look like the Daruma, like—
- **쿠와카미 카호**: Chise! *sob* How can I ever thank you?
- **쿠와카미 카호**: I'm sorry I stalked you and doubted your directing choices!
