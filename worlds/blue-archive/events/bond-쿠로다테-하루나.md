# 쿠로다테 하루나 인연 스토리

- 등장: 쿠로다테 하루나, 선생(샬레)

2. **「쿠로다테 하루나 인연 스토리 2」**

- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh heh heh. The dining hall is closed. There will be no more students to interfere.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Good thing the hall still has electricity and running water after hours.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: This is the perfect spot for a little epicurean research. No one will bother us here!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Is that a rhetorical question?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: I need an adult here to take responsibility in case we're caught by the Prefect Team.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh. No one said life is fair. Now, let's get down to business.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Today's epicurean subject is coq au vin!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: You should already know, but allow me to explain coq au vin...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Coq au vin.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: It's an easy stew made by boiling chicken and vegetables. You just put the ingredients in the pot and boil it all together.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: However...if it were really that simple, it wouldn't be worth researching.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: What makes coq au vin different is that instead of water, wine is used to boil the chicken.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Yes, as in the alcohol.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Naturally, it's off limits here. No student in Kivotos can buy wine.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: The upscale restaurant that serves this dish doesn't allow students inside...so there's no way for me to try it for myself.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Instead...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...I prepared ethyl alcohol and grape juice as a substitute!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh heh heh. I've been searching for a combination that would act as a good substitute for the taste of wine.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: All I need is grapes and alcohol, no? This should be perfect. With this combination, I can make my own coq au vin.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Just watch, Sensei.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: First, grill the bacon in the frying pan.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Next, fry the breaded chicken in the bacon grease.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: After that, put the cooked chicken, mushrooms, carrots, and onions in the pot.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Then, pour the grape juice.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh heh. Don't worry, Sensei. The plan is foolproof.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Finally, it's time for the alcohol...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Hmm... How much should I put in?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: I should have thought about this beforehand...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Well, I'm sure it'll be fine. The book I read said that alcohol evaporates when it's boiled...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: I'll just dump it in!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh heh heh. A true epicure is never stingy with ingredients. I like to put in more than enough...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Okay, maybe I went a little overboard...but I'm sure it will be fine..
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Let's turn up the heat...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Huh?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...?!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Yes, I-I'm fine...but the coq au vin is...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: We set off the fire alarm?!
- **Gehenna Student Voice 1**: There was an explosion in the closed dining hall!
- **Gehenna Student Voice 2**: Could it be an attack?! Report to the Prefect Team right away! Have them converge all forces on the dining hall!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Whoops... Looks like we're busted.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: B-But...what about the food?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Oh... This is bad.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: It wasn't a big explosion, thankfully...but now the secret research lab is gone.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: *sigh*
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...It's okay. I can always find another place.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: The ratio of alcohol played a bigger part than I thought. I'll have to be more careful next time.

3. **「쿠로다테 하루나 인연 스토리 3」**

- **쿠로다테 하루나**: I've been waiting for you, Sensei.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh. Research time is important, so I wanted the best. I spare no expense when it comes to research.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: We can research in peace—the entire place is ours until midnight.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: What better place than a teahouse late at night?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Let's get started, then.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Today's epicurean research subject is instant cup ramen.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Today's epicurean research subject is instant cup ramen.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Huh? Why not have a tea ceremony?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: There's been a misunderstanding. I never said we'd be researching tea ceremonies today.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: All I said was that tea ceremonies teach the ultimate form of etiquette.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Consider the following. If posture, atmosphere, demeanor, and mindset determine how we perceive what we taste...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...what would happen if we apply the same philosophy when eating cup ramen?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: In other words, instant cup ramen...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...is worthy of being researched!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh heh. Very good! I knew you'd understand its profoundness!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh heh. Very good! I knew you'd understand its profoundness!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Now, let me explain what we'll be using for this experiment.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: This pottery was made by an artisan. I'll be using it to boil our water.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: This is artesian water from over 500 meters underground in Gehenna's volcanic region.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: It's very expensive mineral water. And I mean very.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: This is charcoal made from oak—yet another expensive item. It will help the water boil to an even temperature.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: I also have a stopwatch to measure the time, a thermometer to measure the temperature of the water, a pair of the finest bamboo chopsticks, and, finally, a drip pot to pour the water...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh heh. Everything looks good. Shall we start?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Allow me to boil the water.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Let's see...how do I light the charcoal?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: *cough* *cough* Huh? There's no fire—just smoke...!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Is this how a furnace is supposed to work? How am I supposed to use the pottery...?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh. Thanks for waiting. It's ready to go.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Slow down. We're only getting started!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Slow down. We're only getting started!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: We still have to decide how to pour the water and if the hot water should touch the noodles or the powdered seasoning first!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: I was thinking of going with the hand-drip method. There are different patterns for pouring water, such as the spiral, circle, and dots, but I'll go with the spiral pour...!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: This is how I first learned to make hand-drip coffee, so it should work.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: *pant* *pant*
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Pouring water requires so much concentration.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: That should do. I'll set three minutes on the stopwatch...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Here you go, Sensei!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh heh... What did you think of today's research?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: I thought it was rather tasty—different from ordinary ramen.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Eating ramen in an elegant space like a quiet and peaceful teahouse...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: I wondered if what I felt in that moment was enlightenment.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: I think we can perfect the Tao of cup ramen with a little more experimentation.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh... But that's enough for today. See you later!

4. **「쿠로다테 하루나 인연 스토리 4」**

- **Citizen 1**: What happened? The building exploded!
- **Citizen 2**: That's where that famous restaurant is...
- **Citizen 3**: What's going on?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...Heh. Sensei! I didn't expect to see you here.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Oh, yeah. It's a long story.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Oh, yeah. It's a long story.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Let's get out of here.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Phew. We should be fine here.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Like I said, it's kind of a long story... But...
- **Manager**: Welcome to Pierre Garnish. Do you have a reservation?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Yes. Table for Kurodate Haruna.
- **Manager**: Very well. Right this way.
- **Manager**: Allow us to take your bag.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Oh, thank you.
- **Manager**: There's something you must know. Our restaurant specializes in full multi-course meals, so we don't accept single customers.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Huh. I didn't know that was your policy. I reserved a table for one, though...
- **Manager**: It was implemented just yesterday.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...
- **Manager**: But as you said, you made the reservation before the policy changed, so we'll make an exception.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Wow, thank you! In that case, what should I order...?
- **Manager**: I recommend the Pierre. It's the best set on our menu.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Oh... Okay. I'll get that.
- **Manager**: Please wait a moment.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...Hmm.
- **Waiter**: Here's your bread, served with a foie gras appetizer.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Hmm...
- **Waiter**: And here is the oyster salad, part of your entree.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Mmm...
- **Waiter**: Here's your main course, the chateaubriand steak.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...
- **Waiter**: And for dessert, the fruit sorbet.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...
- **Manager**: ...Yes. Here is your receipt. Thank you.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...
- **Manager**: How was your dinner? Did you enjoy each dish?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: The dishes...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...Heh heh heh heh heh heh.
- **Manager**: ...?
- **Manager**: We'll bring you a to-go box.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh. You don't need to do that.
- **Manager**: Pardon?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: *strains*
- **Manager**: A d-detonator?
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: It wasn't just because of that, Sensei.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: When you serve food, it's not just about the taste.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Their arrogance when they said they didn't accept reservations for one.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Their audacity when they didn't allow me, the customer, to choose my own meal.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: There was no aperitif—no cocktail before my meal. There wasn't even an amuse-bouche. A meal NEEDS a bite-sized appetizer.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: They didn't even ask how I'd like my steak cooked. How rude!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Everyone knows chateaubriand has to be cooked medium rare!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: I expect the service AND food to be luxurious at a gourmet restaurant.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: ...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: But I'll give them one thing. I don't enjoy eating good food alone.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heh heh.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: I should go back and take a shower. I smell like smoke...
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: I feel like overcooked yakitori!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Okay, then...Sensei. I'll be a new me next time we meet.

- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Same here. I'm glad I ran into you.
