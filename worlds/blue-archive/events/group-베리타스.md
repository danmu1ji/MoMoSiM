# 베리타스 그룹 스토리

2. **「베리타스 스토리 2화」**

- **코누리 마키**: *sigh*...
- **오마가리 하레**: Why the heavy sigh, Maki? Is something the matter?
- **코누리 마키**: I was just thinking about the physical exam we took today.
- **코누리 마키**: Say, Hare. Did you notice anything strange about physical? Like, do you think weight was off?
- **오마가리 하레**: What do you mean? Did something happen?
- **코누리 마키**: For some reason, the scale said my weight was way higher than it should be!
- **코누리 마키**: It doesn't make any sense! I even stopped snacking and worked out for an entire week to get ready for the physical!
- **코누리 마키**: I put in all that work, but I didn't lose any weight at all...
- **오토세 코타마**: Data never lies, Maki.
- **오토세 코타마**: If the output doesn't exceed the input, the data will reflect that. Just as the scale did.
- **오토세 코타마**: I saw you reward yourself for pulling an all-nighter with soda and potato chips.
- **코누리 마키**: Ack!
- **코누리 마키**: What about you, Kotama? How did it go compared to last year?
- **오토세 코타마**: Th-That hardly matters to me.
- **오토세 코타마**: One's physical form is sure to change over time and it's a fool's errand to attempt to control any more than you can. I'm more focused on the virtual world.
- **코누리 마키**: Y-You're starting to scare me. You sure sound like you care.
- **코누리 마키**: Anyway, I refuse to stand for this...this invasion of privacy! Why does the school even care how much we weigh?!
- **오마가리 하레**: I agree with Kotama. There's no point in worrying about it.<br/>Human weight is a fluctuating data point.
- **오마가리 하레**: If you're that concerned about it, then simply cut back on the late night rewards.
- **코누리 마키**: I can't do that! How do you expect me to live my life knowing that me getting fat will be carved into Millennium's records forever!
- **코누리 마키**: Everyone whisper about it behind my back, and it'll follow me for the rest of my life.
- **오마가리 하레**: I somehow doubt anyone cares that much.
- **오마가리 하레**: Regardless, what's done is done—the record has already been made. Where are you going with this?
- **코누리 마키**: Heh heh. Who do you think you're talking to?
- **코누리 마키**: We're Veritas: the righteous truth-defending and knowledge-liberating hacking club!
- **코누리 마키**: Changing Millennium's records will be a breeze with skills like ours!
- **오마가리 하레**: Like "ours"?
- **오토세 코타마**: Are you referring to us?
- **코누리 마키**: C'mon. Don't act like you haven't thought about it before.
- **코누리 마키**: Just imagine how proud Sensei will be when they see your shining physical exam results.
- **오토세 코타마**: I guess I wouldn't be setting a very good example if I refused such a sincere request from my adorable underclassman.
- **오토세 코타마**: Just for the record, my acceptance of this request has absolutely nothing to do with Sensei's opinion of me!
- **오마가리 하레**: My opinion remains unchanged. I'm not interested either way.
- **코누리 마키**: Hey, Hare. Weren't you just telling me about your new hacking drone?
- **코누리 마키**: Don't you want to give a real challenge to see what it can do? Like say...Millennium's security system?
- **오마가리 하레**: It's true that I've yet to gather any field data since completing development...
- **오마가리 하레**: Fine. I'll go along with this.
- **코누리 마키**: Great! Then we're all in agreement. No backing out now!
- **코누리 마키**: Here's to the defense of truth and the freedom of knowledge!
- **오토세 코타마**: Shouldn't you being saying, "Here's to forging our physical exam records"?
- **오마가리 하레**: I'll stick with, "Here's to practical testing of my latest drone."
- **코누리 마키**: Veritas, it's time to roll out!

3. **「베리타스 스토리 3화」**

- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Yuuka, I've returned with the scale used in the physical exams.
- **하야세 유우카**: Thank you, Eimi.
- **하야세 유우카**: I'm relieved my fears were unfounded. Thankfully, no disgruntled students caused a scene about the exam results this time.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: "This time"?
- **하야세 유우카**: It's happened before. Millennium has a lot of...eccentric students, let's just say. Some have brought their inventions to their physicals and caused complications in the past.
- **하야세 유우카**: There was that one student who tried to wear jet propulsion shoes while being weighed. And another time, one of them brought an entire exoskeleton suit for the strength test.
- **하야세 유우카**: I wish they'd see we're only measuring their personal physical development.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: I had no idea. That's incredible.
- **하야세 유우카**: It's not that I can't empathize with some of them. Frankly, I had the nerve to unload an entire magazine into the scale after being weighed this year.
- **하야세 유우카**: It's the Game Development Department's fault! I always end up eating too much junk food when I'm around them.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Could you not just modify your own record with your authority?
- **하야세 유우카**: I-I would never do that! A Seminar executive mustn't abuse her power for her own gain!
- **하야세 유우카**: I may be treasurer, but I have to adhere to the same rules as everyone else.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: If you say so.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Actually, I'm about to enter the data for the student records. What was the password again?
- **하야세 유우카**: I don't remember changing it at any point. It should be the same.
- **하야세 유우카**: Try "1q2w3e4r".
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: That worked. Thanks.
- **오토세 코타마**: We're in.
- **코누리 마키**: Way to go, Kotama!
- **코누리 마키**: How'd you figure out the password to the student records server?
- **오토세 코타마**: It's still the default password assigned at setup.
- **코누리 마키**: Are you telling me that tightwad treasurer hasn't changed the password even once? How can she be so oblivious to basic cybersecurity?!
- **오마가리 하레**: It only makes our job easier in the end.
- **오마가리 하레**: I'll take it from here. Moving my drone to the server room now.<br/>Accessing the internal network from there will be a cinch after my latest adjustments.
- **오마가리 하레**: Done. We should have full access now.
- **오마가리 하레**: Are you ready, Maki?
- **코누리 마키**: I'm on it!
- **코누리 마키**: Scanning the device node Hare's drone is connected to...
- **코누리 마키**: Neutralizing proxy with an overload...
- **코누리 마키**: And using stolen password to takeover admin privileges...
- **코누리 마키**: Bingo! Authorization granted, and now these records are all mine!
- **코누리 마키**: Now let's see here...
- **코누리 마키**: First, I'll decrease my weight by 10 kilos and increase my height by 10 centimeters. Now how about I bump my chest up to 90 centimeters...
- **오마가리 하레**: Is there a body type with that kind of weight? You're being a little unrealistic.
- **오토세 코타마**: Maki, change mine too.
- **코누리 마키**: Heehee. Your wish is my command!
- **코누리 마키**: Oh? What do we have here? It's our beloved Seminar treasurer Yuuka's physical records.
- **코누리 마키**: I've never really cared for her—too much of a meddler for my tastes. What do you say we have some fun?
- **오마가리 하레**: How so?
- **코누리 마키**: Would you look at that? Yuuka is suddenly up to 100 kilos!
- **코누리 마키**: Wait until she sees this. If only I could see the look on her face.
- **오마가리 하레**: This is pettier than petty.
- **코누리 마키**: Wh-What's going on? I'm getting kicked out!
- **오토세 코타마**: We're being back-hacked from beyond the server! We have less than 10 seconds before we're traced!
- **오마가리 하레**: Maki, erase the log and disconnect. Now!
- **코누리 마키**: B-But we worked so hard to get here. Ugh, fine!
- **코누리 마키**: Wait! If there's another node nearby... Got it, lemme copy it over!
- **오마가리 하레**: What are you doing?!
- **코누리 마키**: What? I'm just backing up the data real quick before we lose access.
- **오마가리 하레**: Look closer! You're uploading the entire Millennium student body records to a public social networking server!
- **코누리 마키**: Uh-oh...
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: This...can't be right.
- **하야세 유우카**: What's wrong, Eimi? It's rare to see anything ever faze you.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: The students' physical exam results shouldn't be public, correct?
- **하야세 유우카**: Of course not. Our students' personal information is kept under the tightest of security protocols. Not even Veritas would be able to worm their way into that system.
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Then why has it been posted all over social media?
- **하야세 유우카**: Wh-What? You're kidding...
- **하야세 유우카**: You're not! B-But who would do this? And why?!
- **이즈미모토 에이미**: Yuuka.

4. **「베리타스 스토리 4화」**

- **오토세 코타마**: Malware infection due to code injection confirmed. Backdoor installed.
- **오토세 코타마**: Another hacking attempt incoming.
- **코누리 마키**: Fix it, fix it, fix it, fix it, fix it...!<br/>Fix it, fix it, fix it!
- **오마가리 하레**: What do you take me for? Some all-knowing super computer?
- **코누리 마키**: But at this rate, we'll go from the hackers to the hackees!
- **코누리 마키**: I'm begging you here! You're our only hope!
- **오마가리 하레**: *sigh* Step aside. Writing defensive code isn't my specialty, but I'll do what I can.
- **오마가리 하레**: I’ll produce a packet to force them off my trail. Huh? What?! They already overrode it.
- **오마가리 하레**: What if I induce an overload by planting a logic bomb inside of a vulnerable folder...? No good. It's already blocked.
- **오마가리 하레**: This is bad. Even I might not be able to get us out of this one.
- **코누리 마키**: Y-You're kidding me! But you're the best there is...
- **오마가리 하레**: I'm being cornered from all sides. It's as though they can predict my every move.
- **오마가리 하레**: It's hard to believe there's someone this talented outside of Veritas.
- **오토세 코타마**: Traffic is increasing due to the prolonged connection.
- **오토세 코타마**: We'll be discovered by Seminar if we don't disconnect immediately.
- **오마가리 하레**: You're right. I'm sorry, Maki, but we can't risk anymore edits on the student records.
- **코누리 마키**: Ugh... Fine. The last thing I want is to sit through another of Yuuka's lectures.
- **코누리 마키**: I'm out. Your drone is clear to withdraw, Hare.
- **카가미 치히로**: It's me.
- **코누리 마키**: Oh. Hey...
- **카가미 치히로**: What's wrong? It feels like a funeral in here.
- **오마가리 하레**: We were forced to come face-to-face with our limitations today.
- **오토세 코타마**: No matter how good you may be, there's always someone better out there.
- **코누리 마키**: An anonymous hacker managed to back-hack us this morning.
- **코누리 마키**: They even ran circles around Hare. I'm still in shock.
- **카가미 치히로**: Did they? I'm begrudgingly impressed.
- **카가미 치히로**: Speaking of hackers, I came across a rather talented one checking on the student records server earlier.
- **카가미 치히로**: They were snooping around the physical exam results. They were so fast that even I had trouble dealing with them.
- **코누리 마키**: Th-The student records server?
- **오마가리 하레**: No wonder they were such a challenge.
- **오토세 코타마**: Our opponent was the vice president?!
- **카가미 치히로**: Are you serious? You three were the ones causing all that trouble by hacking into the student records earlier?
- **카가미 치히로**: I should have known. I knew there was something familiar about those techniques...
- **오토세 코타마**: We were fighting a losing battle from the beginning.
- **오마가리 하레**: I was disappointed in myself for losing to an anonymous hacker, but now I can take some solace in knowing that it was Chihiro.
- **코누리 마키**: I knew it! There's no one better than Veritas, so this explains everything!
- **카가미 치히로**: Wipe that grin off your face! Have I taught you nothing? How could you treat other people's personal information so carelessly?
- **카가미 치히로**: All of you are in for some harsh re-education on hacking ethics. You had better prepare yourselves.
- **코누리 마키**: R-Re-education?! That's just a codeword for punishment! Go easy on us!
- **Millennium Student A**: Hey, did you hear?
- **Millennium Student B**: Hasn't everyone? Yuuka weighing 100 kilograms is, like, the biggest gossip news of the year!
- **Millennium Student A**: She doesn't look it, though... How do you think she's hiding it?
- **Millennium Student B**: Maybe she developed some kind of hologram projector that's on her at all times?
- **하야세 유우카**: That's ridiculous! Who would put so much effort into making something like that? It'd be easier to just work out!
- **하야세 유우카**: The info that leaked on social media was forged! How does this rumor keep spreading?!
- **하야세 유우카**: If I ever get my hands on who did it, I'll make them regret this for the rest of their lives!
