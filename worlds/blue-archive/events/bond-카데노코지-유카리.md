1. **「카데노코지 유카리 인연 스토리 1」**

본문 대사는 수록되어 있지 않다.

2. **「카데노코지 유카리 인연 스토리 2」**

- **카데노코지 유카리**: Fear not, Sensei! I, Yukari, have arrived!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Yes! Because of you, Sensei. You suggested that we all take a "breather".
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I, Yukari, wished to learn more about this task you gave us.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: So here I am!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Of course! Enthusiasm is just one of my many strengths!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: So...where do we find this "breather", Sensei?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I, Yukari, shall seize it without fail!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Of course! I'll go prepare some supplies for our mission!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Heehee.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Heeheehee.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Heh?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Uh, Sensei...what's a breather?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: ?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Ahh! I must look like a fool!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I thought "taking a breather" was slang for some kind of secret mission!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Really? I was certain it had a deeper, hidden meaning.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Forgive me, Sensei! I've troubled you with my impudence.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I'm sorry!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: So taking a breather is resting...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: But it can also mean spending time with others to clear your mind.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Interesting. I can see the wisdom in that, Sensei.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: But where would I take this breather?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Hm? It should be somewhere I want to go? Or something I've always wanted to do?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Let me think about that...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Hmmmm!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I'm sorry, Sensei. I can't think of anything. The idea of pure leisure is very new to me.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Huh?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Sensei, what is all this?!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Arcade, a strange name for a strange place.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: It's so loud and flashy.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Ah! Look, Sensei! The people onscreen are all screaming and squirming! What's happened to them?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Do they need medical attention?!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Is this one of those video games that are popular right now? I just have to shoot these creatures to win right?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: All right! Let's go!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Ha ha! Bullseye! Next!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: More ill citizens?! Wait...no! That must be the boss! They look fearsome!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Too bad. I, Yukari, will not lose!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Phew! We are victorious!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: What a fascinating game!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Oh.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Sensei, what's this over here?!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: So you become a UFO pilot and abduct innocent plush creatures?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: How mysterious!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Ooo, let's try this one next!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Phew! That game was fun as well!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: So was the shooting game, the game where you had to move right to defeat the enemy, and that one where you had to step in time with the music... I even enjoyed the motorcycle race game!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Who knew there were so many different, wonderful games!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Of course!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I don't usually get to play around like this . I spend most of my free time at home or with the Hyakkaryouran.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: So this is what you meant by taking a breather.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I, Yukari, quite enjoyed it!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Me too!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: O-Of course! I was only able to relax because...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: It wasn't just the games, Sensei. It was also...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Oh nothing!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Enough chatter! Let's move onto the next game!

3. **「카데노코지 유카리 인연 스토리 3」**

- **카데노코지 유카리**: Ah! Over here, Sensei!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Our MomoTalk today was quite the culture shock for me!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Don't tell me that kind of salty snack actually exists!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Hm. You wish to know what desserts I treat myself with?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I do enjoy nice ice cream after meals...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Sometimes, our household staff buy me wagashi from the shopping districts of Hyakkiyako as well...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Ah, I also get a lot of sweets as presents. Once I was sent Red Winter Academy's pudding. I've even tasted Trinity's special rollcake and baklava.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Huh? You're...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: ...!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: So you brought me here in order to teach me about salty snacks?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Junk...food?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: So this is where you buy your junk food, Sensei?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Whoa, That's a huge crowd over there!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Is that—?!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: An entire wall of individually packaged snacks?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: And look! There's a glass jar full of candies on the counter!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I see deep fried snacks, bottled beverages, and...umbrella-shaped chocolate?!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: What's that over there?! Are those fireworks and shuriken spinning tops?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: They even sell things like that?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I, Yukari, am both vexed AND baffled!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: There's so much... What should I buy first?!
- **Store Owner**: Thank you as always!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Thank you...as always?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Sensei, are you a regular here?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: A corn stick? It's just a crispy-feeling chip in a wrapper.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Wait. The label here says that it's straightforward junk food yet also the road to junk food paradise, where lies the GOAT of junk food. What does that even mean?!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Is not knowing what ingredients are mixed in part of being junk food?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I, Yukari, am vexed, baffled, AND confused. But if Sensei recommends it...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: It's good! Such a sweet, spicy, yet savory taste!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: There's a Corn Potage with Mixed Fruits Mega Max Flavor?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Is that even a real thing?!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Yes! This one's a thrilling, edge of your seat type of flavor!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Let's try this one next. It's a candy that changes the color of your tongue.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Ah! My tongue is blue!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Sensei, over here!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Snacks here, snacks there. There are so many delicious snacks everywhere!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I never could've dreamed of something like this. This is beyond culture shock. It's a 100 volt electric storm!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: This place has more than junk food?!!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Wait, why are those people gathering around a game machine at the back of the store?
- **Child A**: Everybody come here! We're gonna run a fighting game tournament!
- **Child B**: Oh yeah! I think I could take it all! Who's up for a money match?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: An impromptu fighting game tournament? Here?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Look how those children's eyes are sparkling. They must be having so much fun.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I see... This atmosphere is a part of the junk food experience as well.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Oh, uh...um...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Heehee.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: This is all very new to me. It's a lot to take in...but...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Compared to the arcade, this place is dazzling in a completely different way!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: It's so lively, yet peaceful and cozy at the same time.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: It reminds me of this strange feeling inside my chest lately...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Anyway— This is great! I always learn so much when I'm with you, Sensei!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: *gasp*
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Of course! I, Yukari, solemnly swear...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: That I shall learn all I can!

4. **「카데노코지 유카리 인연 스토리 4」**

- **카데노코지 유카리**: I've arrived! I hope I didn't keep you waiting, Sensei.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Sure thing! I'll wait right here!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Don't mind me.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Hm hmm. ♪
- **카데노코지 유카리**: What shall I learn from Sensei today?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Heehee, I'm so excited.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Hm? What's that sound?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Oh...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Sensei... It's raining...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I guess we won't be going out on any adventures then.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Don't worry about it. I mean, I didn't check either.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: As for today...I've got it all figured out.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I shall keep Sensei company while they work!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: But what?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Do you feel like you've wasted my time because we can't go outside?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Oh, Sensei.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: You feeling that way would actually make me sad.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: I came here today because I was excited to spend time with you, Sensei.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: We don't have to go anywhere, or do anything special.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Getting to be out in the world with you is enough.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Every experience you show me is new.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: And every day feels unique.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: If we can't go out today, we'll have tomorrow.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: If tomorrow proves too troublesome, we can look forward to the next time.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: What tale will Sensei tell me tomorrow? What surprising lesson will Sensei teach me next week?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: That anticipation makes every day feel dazzling.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: So if you're ever scared that you wasted my time...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Don't be! You're looking at it all backwards.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Every moment with you is precious, Sensei!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Oh my! I was just speaking from the heart!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Thanking me for something so obvious... You're making me blush!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Heeheehee. ♪
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Me too.
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Thank—
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Wait... How I truly feel?
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Every moment with you is precious, Sensei!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: ...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: ...
- **카데노코지 유카리**: (Wait... D-Did I just—?!)
- **카데노코지 유카리**: (I was so caught up in the moment. What did I just say?!)
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Ah... Yes...!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Wow... S-Sensei's sure got a way with words!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: Oh! I must've eaten too much junk food. That must be why my heart feels like it's going to explode!
- **카데노코지 유카리**: This was a lovely talk, Sensei.
