# 엔지니어부 그룹 스토리

원문(bluearchive.wiki) 그룹 스토리 스크립트 4화를 한국어 정본 이름으로 옮긴 기록이다.

- 등장: Helmet Gangster A, Helmet Gangster B, Helmet Gangsters A & B, Millennium Student, Pizza Delivery Guy, 네코즈카 히비키, 미카모 네루, 사이바 모모이, 시라이시 우타하, 이치노세 아스나, 토요미 코토리

1. **「엔지니어부 스토리 1화」**

본문 대사는 수록되어 있지 않다.

2. **「엔지니어부 스토리 2화」**

- **토요미 코토리**: Hmm. By reducing the weight of this lower bearing connected to the drive shaft, the speed will be...
- **토요미 코토리**: Oh, it's lunchtime already. Utaha! You can do the engine maintenance this afternoon. Let's eat before you get your hands dirty!
- **시라이시 우타하**: Okay, give me a moment. I'm almost done welding the seams. Let me finish this, and we'll go to the cafeteria together.
- **네코즈카 히비키**: But we have soot all over our work clothes. I was so focused that I didn't even notice...
- **시라이시 우타하**: I know. It would be pretty rude of us to set foot there wearing these rags... So, should we get lunch delivered to the workshop like yesterday?
- **토요미 코토리**: Great idea! I've been craving greasy fast food with lots of ham and cheese.
- **네코즈카 히비키**: This calls for fast food that has a lot of variety and can be easily shared with minimal use of our hands...
- **토요미 코토리**: That would be pizza!
- **네코즈카 히비키**: I agree. There's nothing like getting a pizza delivered to the workshop.
- **시라이시 우타하**: Ha. You two never get tired of pizza.
- **시라이시 우타하**: Well, I can't deny I've been craving it too. We'll get the usual pepperoni and cheese.
- **Pizza Delivery Guy**: Delivery from Kamehame Pizza! Did you order one pepperoni and cheese pizza?
- **토요미 코토리**: Yes, please put it on the table.
- **Pizza Delivery Guy**: Got it. Thanks for choosing Kamehame!
- **토요미 코토리**: *inhale* Oh, that rich, cheesy smell... I haven't even tasted it, and I'm already hungry!
- **네코즈카 히비키**: Let's dig in.
- **토요미 코토리**: Mmm. Pepperoni pizza is the best! Nothing beats savory cheese and salty pepperoni!
- **네코즈카 히비키**: I appreciate a good cheese pizza. The simple, light flavor of the cheese is enough for me.
- **시라이시 우타하**: Is that right? Too much cheese makes me feel kind of sick...<br/>Kotori, could you pass the hot sauce?
- **토요미 코토리**: Shouldn't it be in the pizza box? It's not here.
- **네코즈카 히비키**: There's nothing but grated cheese in the box. The delivery guy must have forgotten the sauce.
- **토요미 코토리**: What? That's unforgivable. Everyone knows you don't forget the heat!
- **토요미 코토리**: Let's blow up the review section of the delivery app and teach that delivery guy a lesson!
- **시라이시 우타하**: Calm down, Kotori. We all forget things, especially when we're busy. Mistakes happen, so let's forgive him.
- **시라이시 우타하**: There should be some hot sauce left in the club room cabinet from last time.<br/>I can just go get it.
- **시라이시 우타하**: Huh? I was sure I left the sauce somewhere around here... Where'd it go?
- **토요미 코토리**: That cabinet is always full of junk. Maybe someone took it out when they were getting a tool?
- **시라이시 우타하**: Bah. Then I suppose we won't be having hot sauce with our pizza this time after all...
- **네코즈카 히비키**: I wonder if anyone's ever made an invention that gives you sauce anytime, anywhere you want...
- **토요미 코토리**: ...
- **토요미 코토리**: Say, Utaha. We order pizza all the time. Why don't we modify a bottle of hot sauce to make it wearable?
- **시라이시 우타하**: A wearable sauce bottle? How?
- **토요미 코토리**: For example...we could install hot sauce dispensers on our personal weapons so that we'll always have them on hand!
- **네코즈카 히비키**: You would risk shooting hot sauce instead of bullets just for some pizza?
- **시라이시 우타하**: That's the most ridiculous and frivolous idea I've ever heard.
- **네코즈카 히비키**: ...
- **시라이시 우타하**: ...
- **시라이시 우타하**: Let's get to work.
- **네코즈카 히비키**: (nod)
- **토요미 코토리**: I knew it was a good idea!
- **토요미 코토리**: Let's start by reverse engineering the hot sauce bottle!

3. **「엔지니어부 스토리 3화」**

- **시라이시 우타하**: Are you ready, Kotori?
- **토요미 코토리**: Of course! I've been waiting for this moment!
- **네코즈카 히비키**: Allow me to do the honors, then.
- **토요미 코토리**: Ta-da! It's finally complete. This is the Engineering Department's newest invention:<br/>a submachine gun with a hot sauce dispenser!
- **시라이시 우타하**: It looks a lot more like an ordinary submachine gun than I was expecting. Does it work?
- **토요미 코토리**: You bet it does! The difference is that you have to use special bullets mixed with pepper and vinegar instead of regular bullets.
- **네코즈카 히비키**: When you put a special bullet in the magazine and pull the trigger, gunpowder causes the red pepper to be fermented in an instant.
- **토요미 코토리**: And the hot sauce will come out of the barrel like this!
- **시라이시 우타하**: Let's see here...
- **시라이시 우타하**: It really is genuine hot sauce! It's completely indistinguishable from the pizza place's sauce.
- **토요미 코토리**: We've conducted multiple experiments to recreate the taste. This gun is the pinnacle of Millennium's latest engineering!
- **네코즈카 히비키**: Plus, if you change it to full auto mode, you can dispense two pints of hot sauce per second.
- **토요미 코토리**: Heh. Now that we have this gun, we can spray as much hot sauce as we want anytime, anywhere! We'll never have to worry about spicing our pizza again!
- **시라이시 우타하**: Simply marvelous. It's exceeded my every expectation.
- **시라이시 우타하**: This gun is just so...so...
- **시라이시 우타하**: ...pointless.
- **토요미 코토리**: Th-That's true. It'd be much cheaper and more convenient to carry hot sauce packets instead of carrying this gun.
- **네코즈카 히비키**: Besides, the sauce gets all over the barrel when you use it, which is a pain to clean. So there's a hygiene issue too...
- **시라이시 우타하**: Ha. Still, it was entertaining to see it put to practice, so that's quite all right.
- **시라이시 우타하**: Not every invention needs to satisfy practicality. If you can enjoy what you're making, then great achievements naturally follow.
- **토요미 코토리**: That's right! This experiment allowed me to put our liquid-dispensing capabilities to the test! That data is sure to come in handy in the future.
- **네코즈카 히비키**: They say that failure is the prelude to success.
- **시라이시 우타하**: By the way... Whose gun did you modify?
- **토요미 코토리**: Huh? I thought this was your gun, Utaha.
- **시라이시 우타하**: No, this is much too large to be my gun. I use a burst pistol without a stock. This is a submachine gun.
- **토요미 코토리**: Well, it's not my gun.
- **네코즈카 히비키**: Not mine either.
- **시라이시 우타하**: Wait. Look at this gaudy dragon pattern.
- **토요미 코토리**: The only person who would put this kind of stupid design on a gun is...
- **미카모 네루**: Finally done with the repair job, I see. You Engineering Department geeks usually don't take so long.
- **토요미 코토리**: N-Neru? Wh-What are you doing here?
- **미카모 네루**: Huh? What do you mean? I'm here to pick up my gun. You got a problem with that?
- **토요미 코토리**: O-Oh, right! I-I forgot about that. Haha...
- **미카모 네루**: Huh? But it's sitting right there. How could you forget? Well, whatever. I'll be taking it back if you're done.
- **토요미 코토리**: H-Hold on. There's still one little problem with this gun...
- **미카모 네루**: What the...? How's it still not done after all this time? I could hear you say something is "finally complete" before I walked in.
- **미카모 네루**: ...Don't tell me you guys messed with my gun! What did you do?!
- **토요미 코토리**: N-Nothing...! There's nothing wrong with your gun!
- **미카모 네루**: Yeah? Fine. I'll be taking it back, then.
- **토요미 코토리**: O-Okay. T-Take care...
- **미카모 네루**: Whatever. Thanks, I guess.
- **토요미 코토리**: What do we do, Utaha?! Neru's gun was...!
- **네코즈카 히비키**: She's sure to be saucy when she finds out her gun is nothing but a sauce dispenser now.
- **토요미 코토리**: We're toast. It won't be long before Neru comes charging in here in a rage and throws us into a barrel of hot sauce!
- **시라이시 우타하**: All of you calm down. She still doesn't know.
- **시라이시 우타하**: If we can get Neru's gun back, we can fix it without her ever realizing it was modified.
- **토요미 코토리**: Take Neru's gun without getting caught? That's like walking into the lion's den!
- **네코즈카 히비키**: We're dead no matter what we do.
- **시라이시 우타하**: Nonsense. First, I'll make a decoy that we can swap for Neru's gun. In the meantime, you two need to make sure Neru doesn't touch the trigger.
- **토요미 코토리**: But...what if Neru finds out that there's something wrong with her gun before then?
- **시라이시 우타하**: I'd rather not think about that possibility.
- **시라이시 우타하**: Let's just pray that she stays patient.

4. **「엔지니어부 스토리 4화」**

- **토요미 코토리**: I don't think she's noticed anything off about her gun yet.
- **네코즈카 히비키**: It's too soon to relax. We're busted the second she pulls the trigger.
- **토요미 코토리**: Don't worry. Neru may be hot-tempered, but that also means that all of Millennium goes out of its way not to provoke her.
- **토요미 코토리**: Neru won't be in any hurry to pull the trigger unless there's, "someone stupid enough to pick a fight," as she puts it.
- **사이바 모모이**: Oh, there you are, Neru!
- **미카모 네루**: Huh? You're one of the twins from the Game Development Department. What do you want with me?
- **사이바 모모이**: Well, just take a look at this broken console!
- **사이바 모모이**: The last time that Helmet Gang was causing trouble on campus, you went berserk trying to catch them and hit the console with a stray bullet. You're responsible for this!
- **미카모 네루**: Are you kidding me? How is that my fault?
- **사이바 모모이**: The console would have been fine if you hadn't been firing at random! Do you have any idea how expensive this thing is? You should be the one paying for it!
- **미카모 네루**: Why, you little...! You'd better back off!
- **토요미 코토리**: Wait! Let us take care of this!
- **사이바 모모이**: Huh? What's the Engineering Department doing here?
- **네코즈카 히비키**: Wait, Momoi. Can I take a look at that console for a moment?
- **토요미 코토리**: Hmm. What we have here is a minor malfunction caused by a broken access terminal. Fixing it with the parts we have on hand will be no sweat!
- **사이바 모모이**: Really? That's a relief...
- **미카모 네루**: Hmph. Are we done? I'm outta here.
- **토요미 코토리**: W-Wait a moment, Neru!
- **네코즈카 히비키**: We'll fix your console if you leave it in our workshop. We're a little busy right now...
- **토요미 코토리**: Hibiki, let's follow Neru before anyone else picks a fight!
- **사이바 모모이**: Fight? Just what is the Engineering Department up to?
- **이치노세 아스나**: Say, President. I've always wondered where you got that tiny sukajan you wear. From the children's clothing department?
- **미카모 네루**: The hell did you just say? I'll kill you!
- **토요미 코토리**: Wait, Neru! Please don't be mad! I'm sure Asuna didn't mean any offense by it!
- **네코즈카 히비키**: ...Right. Still, don't you think you should apologize to Neru, Asuna?
- **이치노세 아스나**: Hmm. Was that really offensive?
- **이치노세 아스나**: Sorry if I hit a sore spot. I just thought your sukajan was cool...
- **미카모 네루**: Hmph. You're unbelievable. Thank the Engineering Department that I'll let it slide, but I'll kill you if you mention the children's clothing department again!
- **Millennium Student**: Neru, could you shoot the lock off of this door with your gun? I forgot the keys.
- **미카모 네루**: That's easy. Put the barrel to the handle, and...
- **토요미 코토리**: W-Wait! If you just need the door unlocked, then leave it to us!
- **네코즈카 히비키**: Opening that door without destroying the handle will be easy for us.
- **Millennium Student**: Wow. You picked the lock with just some wire like they do in the movies. That's amazing!
- **토요미 코토리**: We're experts on this kind of thing! There's no reason to use a gun to open a locked door!
- **미카모 네루**: ...
- **미카모 네루**: What the hell...are you all up to?
- **토요미 코토리**: Wh-What do you mean? W-We're the Engineering Department!
- **미카모 네루**: You think I don't know that?!
- **미카모 네루**: I want to know why you guys keep following me around and butting into my business!
- **토요미 코토리**: W-We just like to help students in need! H-Haha!
- **네코즈카 히비키**: And we're...staunch advocates for peace! If our technology can be used to prevent violence, why wouldn't we help?
- **미카모 네루**: I guess you have a point, but still...something's fishy here.
- **미카모 네루**: You didn't do something to my gun, did you?
- **Helmet Gangster A**: There you are, Mikamo Neru!
- **Helmet Gangster B**: We've been looking for you since you attacked us!
- **미카모 네루**: Huh? What do you bastards want? You got a lotta nerve showing your faces around here!
- **Helmet Gangster A**: Haha. You may have caught us off guard before, but this time, we came prepared!
- **Helmet Gangster B**: All of our gear is now bulletproof, including our helmets! Your little peashooter gun will never take us down!
- **Helmet Gangster A**: So, get ready, Little Miss C&C President! It's time for our revenge!
- **미카모 네루**: Ha. You guys are pretty serious about this.
- **미카모 네루**: All right. Have it your way!
- **토요미 코토리**: W-Wait, Neru! We turned your gun into...!
- **Helmet Gangster A**: Hahaha! Behold the power of the new bulletproof helmets... Huh?
- **Helmet Gangsters A & B**: Aaarrrgh!
- **Helmet Gangster A**: Wh-What is this? Why do the bullets smell spicy and tangy...?<br/>Is this hot sauce?
- **Helmet Gangster B**: It's seeping through the gaps of the helmet and getting into my eyes and nose!<br/>*cough* *cough* I can't breathe! Someone help me!
- **Helmet Gangster A**: *cough* *cough* Y-You little she-devil! I didn't think you'd stoop to chemical warfare. C&C has no mercy! *cough*
- **미카모 네루**: I mean...I...
- **Helmet Gangster A**: Retreat! We'll get you next time! *cough* *cough*
- **토요미 코토리**: Wow, that was close. I thought Neru was going to have her undefeated record snapped by the Helmet Gang's bulletproof gear.
