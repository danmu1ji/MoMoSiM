- **이타가키 카노에**: Now then, let's begin.
- **이타가키 카노에**: First...I add the boiled tomatoes.
- **이타가키 카노에**: Heehee, I'm a well-prepared woman.
- **이타가키 카노에**: Next, the stir-fried marinated beef, with a mix of seasoning...

- **이타가키 카노에**: One to inspire love and memories, indeed.
- **이타가키 카노에**: Now, we add the rest and bring it to a boil...
- **이타가키 카노에**: Here... It's ready.
- **이타가키 카노에**: A potion of love across lifetimes.
- **이타가키 카노에**: Tomato beef stew, filled to the brim with my adoration.
- **이타가키 카노에**: Oh, it certainly is. Try it, and you'll be flooded with the understanding of my true, deepest feelings.
- **이타가키 카노에**: Come now, don't be shy... To come to your senses, you must first indulge your senses.
- **이타가키 카노에**: Heehee, what do you think? Does it take you back?
