# 코누리 마키 인연 스토리

- 등장: 코누리 마키, 선생(샬레)

1. **「코누리 마키 인연 스토리 1」**

2. **「코누리 마키 인연 스토리 2」**

- **코누리 마키**: Waaaaaah! Sensei!
- **코누리 마키**: The police arrested me even though I'm innocent...
- **Cop**: You know exactly why we arrested you!
- **코누리 마키**: Waaah, Sensei! The cops are bullying me!
- **Cop**: Are you this child's guardian?
- **Cop**: Are you aware of what this young lady has been doing?
- **코누리 마키**: I didn't do anything wrong! I did what I had to do!
- **Cop**: Why you!
- **Cop**: This girl was caught trespassing on private property...
- **Cop**: ...not to mention vandalizing it!
- **코누리 마키**: Graffiti isn't vandalism. It's an art form!
- **Cop**: Try explaining that to the property owner, young lady.
- **Cop**: Precisely—or "graffiti" as this miscreant refers to it. She was caught painting images on the property's wall.
- **코누리 마키**: But the owner said it was fine!
- **Cop**: He only said that he forgave you, not that you were given permission in the first place. This also isn't the first instance.
- **Cop**: The overpass on the KV highway, the back alley of the Kuro Neko market...
- **Cop**: You're always tagging places where there aren't a lot of people around. Do you have any idea how hard it is to clean up the messes you leave behind?
- **코누리 마키**: They're not messes—they're art!
- **Cop**: She should be the one apologizing, not you.
- **코누리 마키**: What?! All I'm doing is adding some liveliness to this gloomy city!
- **코누리 마키**: But... Ugh...
- **코누리 마키**: Fine. I'm...sorry about all of this.
- **Cop**: Hmph. I'll let you off with a warning this time. Don't think you'll get so lucky if I catch you again!
- **코누리 마키**: Hey, Sensei. Are you...angry with me?
- **코누리 마키**: Don't lie. You are angry, aren't you? Um...
- **코누리 마키**: But that gloomy gray wall was calling out to me! It wanted to be tagged in bright, pretty colors!
- **코누리 마키**: I-I know that tagging someone else's property isn't right, okay...?
- **코누리 마키**: But I couldn't contain the burning artistic passion inside me...
- **코누리 마키**: I'm sorry. The last thing I wanted was to make you angry.
- **코누리 마키**: Graffiti means a lot to me but not more than you do.
- **코누리 마키**: This is a predicament. How can I continue expressing myself with graffiti but not make Sensei angry...?
- **코누리 마키**: ...A huge canvas?
- **코누리 마키**: Wait, I get it. If we stacked several canvases together, I could use that instead of the side of a building!
- **코누리 마키**: Maybe it won't be the same as using a real wall...but anything is fun as long as I'm doing it with you, Sensei.
- **코누리 마키**: So...if I put a huge canvas together, will you be there to help me paint?
- **코누리 마키**: You promise, right? Thanks, Sensei!

3. **「코누리 마키 인연 스토리 3」**

- **코누리 마키**: I don't think anyone can see me. This place is a little creepy, though...
- **코누리 마키**: I didn't want to resort to coming here...but there's no other choice, given the circumstances.
- **코누리 마키**: I made sure to buy the software I need beforehand... Now I just have to follow through with the plan.
- **코누리 마키**: Perfect. I have access to every kiosk in the area thanks to the control program. Now to just install the software.
- **코누리 마키**: Let's see how the web version runs, shall we?
- **Clerk**: Welcome. How can I help you?
- **Customer**: One bulgogi lunch box, please.
- **Clerk**: Coming right up. Swipe your card at the kiosk to pay.
- **Clerk**: Wh-What's going on? Why is there a retro game on the screen...?
- **Customer**: D-Demons from space!
- **Clerk**: C-Calm down! It's just a game.
- **Customer**: Why is this happening? Am I supposed to play the game to check out or something?
- **Clerk**: No, it must be some kind of error. This has never happened before. I couldn't tell you why this is happening.
- **Customer**: But how am I supposed to pay for my food now...?
- **코누리 마키**: Hahaha! They have no idea what to do. BOOM is the best!
- **코누리 마키**: Eek! Sensei? How long have you been standing there?
- **코누리 마키**: What am I doing...? Isn't it pretty obvious?
- **코누리 마키**: I'm hacking the kiosks and installing BOOM on them!
- **코누리 마키**: Huh? Sensei, you don't know what BOOM is?
- **코누리 마키**: It's only the best retro action game of all time!
- **코누리 마키**: It's a game where a space soldier that people call "BOOM Guy" takes out aliens with a shotgun. A fad has been spreading among hackers to install BOOM on all sorts of devices!
- **코누리 마키**: Phones, kiosks, LCD signs, and even calculators! As long as it has a screen, it can run BOOM!
- **코누리 마키**: What do you think? Isn't it awesome, Sensei?
- **코누리 마키**: But...seeing people's reactions is so interesting. I don't want to stop now.
- **코누리 마키**: I was looking forward to putting BOOM on the sign above the KV highway next...
- **코누리 마키**: Ugh... Fine. I'll stop for today if that's really what you want, Sensei.
- **코누리 마키**: Tch. I just wanted to impress everyone with something really cool. Why did you have to tell me to stop, Sensei...?
- **코누리 마키**: ...I need to find something that won't cause trouble for anyone else? How am I supposed to do that?
- **코누리 마키**: Something that would make people happy? What could even do that...?
- **코누리 마키**: I got it! I could hack in and cast a self-improvement video for everyone to watch!

4. **「코누리 마키 인연 스토리 4」**

- **코누리 마키**: Studying has nothing to do with me failing the test...
- **코누리 마키**: Millennium's tests are too hard to begin with! None of the problems are meant for average high school students!
- **코누리 마키**: Sensei, you're starting to sound like a teacher... Wait. I guess you are one.
- **코누리 마키**: Besides, studying the textbooks doesn't help me understand the questions.
- **코누리 마키**: Especially with literature! How am I supposed to know what the narrator is feeling just from reading a paragraph? I can't read minds, you know!
- **코누리 마키**: What? How can I be such a good hacker if I can't answer such simple questions?
- **코누리 마키**: I don't get what you mean. Hacking is way easier than this.
- **코누리 마키**: Pouring too much water in a bucket will make it overflow. When you don't have the key you need to open something, you ask the person who does. It's all so simple!
- **코누리 마키**: But solving literature questions is supposed to be easier than hacking? Let's see about that...
- **코누리 마키**: Oh, so this part means that the character is happy. Right, I think I get it!
- **코누리 마키**: What about this part, Sensei? What does this mean...?
- **코누리 마키**: Thanks, Sensei! I'm going to ace the make-up exam at this rate.
- **코누리 마키**: It's amazing how easy you make it to understand complex questions.
- **코누리 마키**: Huh? You think it's because I'm a fast learner?
- **코누리 마키**: Haha. Don't flatter me.
- **코누리 마키**: I may not be the smartest Veritas member, but the last thing I want is to fall behind the curriculum.
- **코누리 마키**: And it's not like Veritas sees me as a troublemaker. It's one of the only places where I feel like my talents are understood.
- **코누리 마키**: Besides, you mean a lot to me too, Sensei. So... I'm going to try harder.
- **코누리 마키**: Haha! That came out way too embarrassing. Forget I said anything!
- **코누리 마키**: Ahem. Did I memorize the notebook you gave me?
- **코누리 마키**: Here it says the notebook is 100 sheets of 80-gram A-type white paper cut to 297 by 210 millimeters...
- **코누리 마키**: Hmm? What am I reading? It's the beginning of the notebook you gave me.
- **코누리 마키**: *gasp* I'm not supposed to memorize that part? I thought you told me to memorize everything inside...
- **코누리 마키**: E-Excuse me? You can't tell whether I'm brilliant or brainless? What's that supposed to mean, Sensei?!

5. **「코누리 마키 인연 스토리 5」**

- **코누리 마키**: Yay! Thanks for buying me ice cream, Sensei.
- **코누리 마키**: I feel like I'm the one who owes you a gift for tutoring me...
- **코누리 마키**: But I could I never turn down food from you, Sensei!
- **코누리 마키**: I just remembered, Sensei. Did you know there's a new clothing store nearby?
- **코누리 마키**: It looked like they had a lot of trendy clothes for people your age when I passed by.
- **코누리 마키**: I'm sure we'll find something perfect for you. I'll help you pick, so let's go!
- **코누리 마키**: La la la! Shopping with Sensei! ♪
- **코누리 마키**: Hmm. What's that smell?
- **코누리 마키**: Ugh. Someone threw their trash out along that wall again.
- **코누리 마키**: Yeah. Since that spot is out of sight and covered in graffiti, it's a popular place for people to dump their garbage.
- **코누리 마키**: It's like they have no remorse at all just because the wall is so dirty.
- **코누리 마키**: Maybe people wouldn't be so willing to litter if it were cleaner...
- **코누리 마키**: Yup. It's big trouble...
- **코누리 마키**: ...
- **코누리 마키**: La la la! ♪ That takes care of all the trash. This place should stay a lot cleaner now.
- **코누리 마키**: Ahhh! S-Sensei, when did you get here?
- **코누리 마키**: I swear I wasn't doing anything suspicious...
- **코누리 마키**: W-Well...the wall is all dirty and old... I thought I could get people to stop littering in front of it if I painted something pretty on it.
- **코누리 마키**: I know I'm breaking the promise I made. I'm sorry.
- **코누리 마키**: Are you angry with me Sensei? *groan*...
- **코누리 마키**: Huh?
- **코누리 마키**: Sensei... You're the best!
- **코누리 마키**: Gah, fine. I guess I do owe them that much.
- **코누리 마키**: Still, I'm glad that you're going to join me. I don't want to get arrested by myself again.
- **코누리 마키**: Would you mind helping me clear this last pile of junk out of the way, Sensei?
- **코누리 마키**: Hah. Thanks.
- **코누리 마키**: I hope people stop dumping their litter here.

6. **「코누리 마키 인연 스토리 6」**

- **Passerby**: Young lady! You with the red hair!
- **코누리 마키**: Hmm? Me?
- **Passerby**: Yeah, you're the one who spray-painted that wall, right?
- **코누리 마키**: Oh, well...
- **Passerby**: I'm the owner of that building.
- **코누리 마키**: I'm so sorry!
- **코누리 마키**: I wasn't trying to cause trouble. It's just, the alley was so dirty—
- **코누리 마키**: Please, don't call the police!
- **Passerby**: I wasn't going to.
- **Passerby**: I've been so tired of people dumping trash back there and tagging the wall with ugly graffiti.
- **Passerby**: But there's been less litter and tagging ever since you painted it with your art. I don't know how to thank you.
- **코누리 마키**: O-Oh. I only did it because i-inspiration struck. H-Haha...!
- **Passerby**: Would you be interested in painting the other side too?
- **Passerby**: You don't have to if you don't want to, though...
- **코누리 마키**: Of course I want to! I'd be happy to!
- **코누리 마키**: Haha! I can't believe I have the green light to do whatever I want with this wall.
- **코누리 마키**: Graffiti artists are almost never given the freedom to paint whatever they want for however long they want. It's so exciting!
- **코누리 마키**: Where's this coming from, Sensei? You promised to be my apprentice, remember?
- **코누리 마키**: You're going to be painting with me, obviously. Here, take my spray can, Sensei.
- **코누리 마키**: Don't overthink it. Let your heart paint the lines, then fill in the colors.
- **코누리 마키**: You can always paint over any mistakes... So don't worry. Just pick up the spray can and get started.
- **코누리 마키**: ― So, is this good enough?
- **코누리 마키**: ― What do you think, Sensei?
- **코누리 마키**: ― Hmph... At least try to look impressed.
- **코누리 마키**: ― I need you to give me feedback like a professional art critic!
- **코누리 마키**: ― ...
- **코누리 마키**: ― So what did you paint, Sensei?
- **코누리 마키**: ― Uh...
- **코누리 마키**: ― Hahaha! What is that supposed to be? It looks so silly!
- **코누리 마키**: ― Aren't I scared of heights? Not when I have you by my side.
- **코누리 마키**: ― And I'll be by yours whenever you need me.
- **코누리 마키**: ― The wall, Sensei. I'll paint anything if it's for you!
- **코누리 마키**: I hope this wall stays here forever so I can always remember this moment with you.
- **코누리 마키**: I, uh... Pretend you didn't just hear that!
