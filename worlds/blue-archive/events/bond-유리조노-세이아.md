# 유리조노 세이아 인연 스토리

원문(bluearchive.wiki) 인연 스토리 5편. 선생과의 개인 기억이므로 시간창 없이 열람할 수 있다(개인 지식).

- 등장: 유리조노 세이아, 선생(샬레)

1. **「유리조노 세이아 인연 스토리 1」**

본문 대사는 수록되어 있지 않다.

2. **「유리조노 세이아 인연 스토리 2」**

- **???**: ...Mmm...
- **???**: Mmm...
- **???**: ...Oh.
- **???**: Sensei...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ― ...I didn't know you were here.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ― It may not seem like it, but I assure you, I'm quite surprised.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ― Oh, don't misunderstand. Surprises can be wonderful things.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ― Even things you anticipate can be surprises when they happen.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ― ...For example...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ― Something like you, Sensei, suddenly showing up in front of me...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ― Let's see. Today was...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ― Ah, yes.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ― You came here for my request.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ― ...For that, I apologize. There's no particular occasion.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ― I simply...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ― ...I...simply wanted to talk with you. That's all.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Perhaps my stubbornness has troubled you.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Though perhaps it's a bit late to say so. Heehee.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Ah, so you're untroubled? No worries, then?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Of course you would say so, gracious as you are.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Yes, I suppose my messages could have been interpreted that way.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Though you'd likely visit even if I simply said, "I wish to see you."
- **유리조노 세이아**: I can be quite the troublesome brat sometime, can't I?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Perhaps I simply wanted to see your face, in a confusing and awkward situation.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And then I slept through it, without a care in the world.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Such folly. Fufu.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Does it surprise you that I played the "official Tea Party business" card to fulfill my personal motives?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Or did you already know, Sensei?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Unlike others, I have no qualms about such things. I have my privileges, and I use them. Unless it would cause trouble for Trinity or the Tea Party, of course.
- **유리조노 세이아**: After all, what harm could there be in doing it just once?
- **유리조노 세이아**: But, now that it's been done...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...I shan't call on you again in such a way.
- **유리조노 세이아**: There may be times when I enjoy your consternation, but I have no wish to make you distrust or dislike me.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Though I may yet find other ways to fluster you.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...I haven't worn you out already, have I?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Very good. That is precisely what I wanted to hear.
- **유리조노 세이아**: It must be hard for you, dealing with a brat like me.
- **유리조노 세이아**: On a somewhat-related topic...
- **유리조노 세이아**: You haven't asked me how I feel yet, Sensei. Most people start their conversations that way.
- **유리조노 세이아**: You know, "How are you feeling," "Are you well," "Are you hurting," that sort of thing.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Fufu.
- **유리조노 세이아**: But of course, you would know that I find such small talk entirely unnecessary.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Excellent. Then this gamble of mine was entirely worth it.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Now, Sensei, since you have taken the time to come all the way here...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...I do have an official request, as a former Tea Party Host.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Would you...help me with my "nap task" by nodding off over there for a bit?

3. **「유리조노 세이아 인연 스토리 3」**

- **유리조노 세이아**: You're here, Sensei. Apologies for bringing you all this way.
- **유리조노 세이아**: As you may have guessed, I have no particularly pressing issues at the moment. I hope I made that clear enough by asking you to come by "when you were free."
- **유리조노 세이아**: Still, if I might justify my invitation after the fact...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...perhaps this will serve as a reason.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Those who exhaust themselves with work must replenish their sugar levels. The Tea Party had these snacks specially ordered.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Since I've taken your time and energy, I may as well help you restore the latter.
- **유리조노 세이아**: It must seem like a bit of a contradiction...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...the way I wish to trouble you, while also providing for your naps and snacks and such.
- **유리조노 세이아**: But so be it. Allow me to ask for your understanding.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And if you find this preamble too long, please, go on and help yourself.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Sensei? Are those...?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Oh. But why...?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Surely, if you saw through the ruse, you wouldn't have bothered with such a thing?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Are you telling me you went out of your way to bring treats for a sick person, despite knowing that I'm not actually sick?
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Hmm? The "sickness" was a mere excuse to bring them for me?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Aha... I see.
- **유리조노 세이아**: So we both brought desserts, then. Different brands, of course, but nearly identical in substance.
- **유리조노 세이아**: One would think we were both here for a picnic, rather than a visit to the sick.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And of course, you knew from the start why I called you here.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Fufu. Is that so?
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...What a shame. It seems my idea wasn't quite so unique as I thought it was.
- **유리조노 세이아**: What? You still want it?
- **유리조노 세이아**: You actually like it...? Why would...?
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Indeed.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And only you could make such a thing so pleasant, Sensei.
- **유리조노 세이아**: It's impressive. One simple phrase from your lips, and all my disappointment simply vanished. In fact, this went far better than I could have expected.
- **유리조노 세이아**: We both understood the pretense from the start.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And here I am, expecting you to play along for my benefit. Truly I ought to be content to enjoy your company and share a treat or two.
- **유리조노 세이아**: How should I put this...?
- **유리조노 세이아**: The way I see myself developing, I fear the word "brat" may be an understatement soon.
- **유리조노 세이아**: But have no fear. I'm fully capable of drawing my own boundaries.
- **유리조노 세이아**: You're the only one I treat this way.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And, in that spirit, there are lines I will make certain never to cross.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Does that put you at ease?
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Fufu.
- **유리조노 세이아**: I can't help but laugh. Your company is as enjoyable as it is unpredictable.
- **유리조노 세이아**: But back to the point: let's indulge in these scrumptious desserts.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Naturally.
- **유리조노 세이아**: The treats I prepared are already enough for the two of us. And they're unwrapped, so it only makes sense to eat them first.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And forgive me for saying so, but all the treats you brought are mine now.
- **유리조노 세이아**: I'll savor them myself, on my own good time.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Such are the rules in my room. Even you have to follow them, Sensei.
- **유리조노 세이아**: In this room...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...my word is Trinity's law.

4. **「유리조노 세이아 인연 스토리 4」**

- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Ah, Sensei.
- **유리조노 세이아**: What brings you here? I don't believe I specifically asked for you to come...
- **유리조노 세이아**: No, of course I'm happy. Your visits are always a pleasure.
- **유리조노 세이아**: In fact, it's wonderful to see you here unprompted.
- **유리조노 세이아**: As it happens, I am...here by myself all day.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Fufu, indeed.
- **유리조노 세이아**: This is the exclusive property of Trinity's Tea Party. Only members and their guests are welcome here.
- **유리조노 세이아**: In fact, it's not the only Tea Party-exclusive building on campus.
- **유리조노 세이아**: There are more than just the three of us in the Tea Party, after all. There are many of us, in fact.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Regardless, as you can see, this place is very quiet.
- **유리조노 세이아**: A few students do drop by from time to time, but they typically come for class materials and such.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Not many of them read for pleasure, and those who do tend to read in their own rooms.
- **유리조노 세이아**: You ask why I read here, then?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Fufu, I made my room too comfortable for reading books. I rarely read there these days.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Were I to try, I would likely nod off, and the book would slip from my hands.
- **유리조노 세이아**: So when it comes to reading, a bit of discomfort can actually help.
- **유리조노 세이아**: You haven't seen it from me yourself, but in truth, I read so much that people often check for me here before they try my own room.
- **유리조노 세이아**: I would love to find others to share my books with...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...but even for such a widespread hobby, bringing people together for it can be extraordinarily difficult.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And, were I to bring my peers to the library, I would inevitably feel as if I were burdening them.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Another complication is my own social standing. It's difficult to discuss books freely when one of us has higher status.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Were I to express my opinion of a book in front of regular students, they might feel compelled to agree.
- **유리조노 세이아**: So I could never bring myself to follow through with such a thing. I've often thought about it, but I've always stopped myself.
- **유리조노 세이아**: I would love to share conversations through a book, but without troubling anyone.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...I understand where you're leading me with that.
- **유리조노 세이아**: But you needn't worry. My life is not lonely or lacking.
- **유리조노 세이아**: My reading hobby is rather solitary, but I do have fine relationships with other students.
- **유리조노 세이아**: That said... Um...
- **유리조노 세이아**: Perhaps you might be willing to accompany me on a trip through a book sometime.
- **유리조노 세이아**: We would both enjoy it. At least I think so.
- **유리조노 세이아**: I wouldn't have to worry about dragging you about anyplace or burdening you with my own opinions.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...This already sounds greedy of me. But what would you say to it?
- **유리조노 세이아**: It wouldn't be frequent, mind you, only occasional...but I would love to have you as a reading partner now and again.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Fufu. Wonderful.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Every day we meet, you find a new way to make me happy.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Now, the question is, would you like to pick a book? Or would you like me to recommend you one?
- **유리조노 세이아**: This place has at least one copy of every book known to the school.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Unless you're looking for some ancient tome, in which case you'd want the old library, you can find it here.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And if you ARE looking for something from the old library, I can give them a call for you.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...That's a joke. As I mentioned before, I wouldn't abuse my power that way. Not that our school's Library Committee president would let me.
- **유리조노 세이아**: But I would like to recommend you a book. Several, actually, if you'd like to see this list I've prepared.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Go on. Choose whichever one you like.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Mmm, mm. I see. So you're going with...that one.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Ah.
- **유리조노 세이아**: N-Nothing. Merely a...minor problem.
- **유리조노 세이아**: That book isn't here right now. Someone already borrowed it.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And that someone is me. The book is in my room.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Now why are you looking at me like that? Do you not believe me?
- **유리조노 세이아**: I said I RARELY read books in my room, not that I NEVER do. I promise you, it's there as we speak.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And, by that happy coincidence...
- **유리조노 세이아**: I suppose we'll have to head back to my room so that I can give it to you.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Again, this is pure coincidence. A lucky, delightful coincidence.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Lucky, also, that I happened to think of borrowing more books today...
- **유리조노 세이아**: Fufu, well, let's just say this coincidence isn't the only stroke of luck that could have happened here.
- **유리조노 세이아**: There's always the chance it could happen again. One must be prepared for such things.
- **유리조노 세이아**: That said, I do believe I've checked out too many books to carry.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Sensei?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Would you mind helping me?

5. **「유리조노 세이아 인연 스토리 5」**

- **???**: ...Sensei?
- **유리조노 세이아**: What are you doing here...?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Ahh. Of course, you mentioned you were here on business. I didn't join the meeting because it wasn't pertinent to me...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...but I'd forgotten you would be there. Perhaps I should have joined after all.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Then again, it would be a bit awkward to sit through an entire meeting I wasn't a part of, just to meet you.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And I ended up meeting you anyway, so it must be my lucky day.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Hmm? What was I doing here?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Merely walking, as you can see. It's pleasantly quiet out here. No gossip, no guns, no grenades.
- **유리조노 세이아**: I pulled a few strings and reserved the whole place for myself.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Fufu, no, I'm joking.
- **유리조노 세이아**: I simply thought you might walk by. Oh, please don't look at me like that.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Chirping...?
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Aha.
- **유리조노 세이아**: No, I had no plan to lure you here, if that's what you're implying.
- **유리조노 세이아**: All I wanted was to see you passing by.
- **유리조노 세이아**: The chirping, hmm...
- **유리조노 세이아**: You want to know if it came from this little one? Perhaps it did.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...One would be hard pressed to say for sure.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Fufu, I do enjoy a mystery, but that's not my intention at the moment. You see...
- **유리조노 세이아**: Many of these little ones live here in the park, and some often come to my window. A few even perch on me now and again, though I'm never sure which is which.
- **유리조노 세이아**: So the chirp you heard could have come from this one, or perhaps another one entirely.
- **유리조노 세이아**: All I can say for certain is that all of them have lovely singing voices.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And whichever one charmed you enough to bring you here, well, I sincerely appreciate it.
- **유리조노 세이아**: I'm sure the little ones would love to know they sprinkled a bit of curiosity into your day. And I would say the same.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Thank you. That is, allow me to accept your compliment on their behalf.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Incidentally...
- **유리조노 세이아**: I do enjoy a good song. Sometimes, when I'm alone, I even sing myself.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Fufu.
- **유리조노 세이아**: I love the surprise on your face, when I told you that just now.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And I would love to seize the moment and sing you a song...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...but I ought to hold back. The first song I sing for you should come at a special place and time.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Oh, don't look at me that way.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Singing has a very special meaning for me, Sensei.
- **유리조노 세이아**: I used to be incredibly shy and awkward when I spoke to others. More so than I am now, even. People would feel uncomfortable whenever I opened my mouth.
- **유리조노 세이아**: It was difficult to change that, and I wasn't always sure if I even needed to.
- **유리조노 세이아**: But there were rare occasions when I needed to give people the clear, unvarnished truth.
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...And singing was my medium for that.
- **유리조노 세이아**: To the people of the Tea Party, the Sisterhood, and Remedial Knights...even to other students at Trinity.
- **유리조노 세이아**: And even in occasional exchange with students from other schools, I would sing to express my thoughts when absolutely necessary.
- **유리조노 세이아**: My life was full of excuses and talking in circles. I was hypocritical, and couldn't be honest with myself...
- **유리조노 세이아**: Singing was my only way of saying "not this time."
- **유리조노 세이아**: So I can't sing to you just because I wanted to. That's not the way I've taught myself.
- **유리조노 세이아**: You're looking very disappointed. Is it your turn to be the brat, perhaps?
- **유리조노 세이아**: Hmm, though for some reason...
- **유리조노 세이아**: I am starting to feel like I want to sing for you. At some point.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Ah, you always read my intentions so well. My pretenses never fool you. All I can do is play along with your playing along.
- **유리조노 세이아**: With that said, you may never have the chance to hear me sing.
- **유리조노 세이아**: Wouldn't that be a shame?
- **유리조노 세이아**: ...Oh, you've seen through me again.
- **유리조노 세이아**: There is one truth I want to tell you. Though by this point, I suppose it's hardly a revelation.
- **유리조노 세이아**: I'll sing it to you, then...
- **유리조노 세이아**: Sensei...
- **유리조노 세이아**: This is my...
- **유리조노 세이아**: ..."song of thanks" for you.
