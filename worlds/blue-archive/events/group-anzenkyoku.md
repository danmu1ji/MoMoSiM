2. **「anzenkyoku 스토리 2화」**

- **네무가키 후부키**: *sigh* Relaxing. So relaxing...
- **네무가키 후부키**: There are no open cases or special requests today at all. I can even nap while I'm on the clock. This is heaven!
- **네무가키 후부키**: I wish Kivotos could be this peaceful every day...
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: No, that can't happen!
- **네무가키 후부키**: Huh? Do you not like peace, Kirino?
- **네무가키 후부키**: Are you saying that you want Kivotos to always be in chaos, with villains committing crimes every day and the students constantly fighting amongst themselves?
- **네무가키 후부키**: You can be so evil sometimes, Kirino...
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: Of course that's not what I'm saying.
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: But there wouldn't be anything for us to do if it were always peaceful.
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: We wouldn't be able to show our worth to the police school higher-ups, and we'd have to stay in the Public Safety Bureau with no hopes of ever being promoted!
- **네무가키 후부키**: I like the Public Safety Bureau.
- **네무가키 후부키**: It doesn't receive any dangerous or troublesome cases. All we have to do is keep the peace.
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: That's my LEAST favorite thing about it!
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: I came to Valkyrie Police School because I wanted to catch bad guys like the cops I admired. But all I'm doing is helping lost children and directing traffic at crosswalks.
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: I know it's important work and has its own merits.
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: And it does feel indescribably good to be thanked by a lost child, but still! It's not enough for me!
- **네무가키 후부키**: You really are a busybody, Kirino.
- **네무가키 후부키**: Maybe it's because I'm getting older, but I always run out of energy for this job by the afternoon.
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: But you're a first-year just like me, Fubuki! You need to get on your feet and be more enthusiastic!
- **네무가키 후부키**: Don't be so anxious. You said it yourself: We're first-years, so we still have plenty of time to prove our worth.
- **네무가키 후부키**: You never know. There might be a major case that needs our help soon—
- **네무가키 후부키**: I was nearly about to blame my big mouth, but that's not us.
- **네무가키 후부키**: And the donut shop on 2nd Avenue... Do they mean Master Donuts? That place is a classic.
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: I'm going to call the Security Bureau to find out what's going on!
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: Director, this is Kirino from the Public Safety Bureau!
- **Security Director General**: Yes, Kirino? What's the matter? We're busy preparing for dispatch.
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: I just heard the emergency call. Did something happen at the donut shop?
- **Security Director General**: It's not a big deal. A petty thief is holding the place up and demanding money.
- **Security Director General**: Your department doesn't have anything to worry—
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: Let's go, Fubuki! We have to get there before the Security Bureau and stop the criminal so we can prove our worth!
- **네무가키 후부키**: Huh? But I don't wanna...
- **네무가키 후부키**: Plus, we aren't even equipped to handle something like that—literally. We don't have the weapons, so if something goes wrong, we're going to be taken hostage too.
- **네무가키 후부키**: Why not just leave it to them so we can kick back and relax?
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: Innocent civilians are being held hostage as we speak! Now isn't the time to argue about whether it's the responsibility of the Public Safety Bureau or the Security Bureau!
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: And we may even receive a commendation from the higher-ups if we solve this case! Or better yet...maybe more vacation days?
- **네무가키 후부키**: Vacation days? Now you're talking my language.
- **네무가키 후부키**: Fine. But I'm hightailing it out of there if the situation gets too dangerous.
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: Don't worry. Justice will always prevail no matter who stands in the way!

3. **「anzenkyoku 스토리 3화」**

- **Donut Shop Owner**: E-Eek! Help me!
- **Hostage Taker**: Hahaha! Hand over all your dough if you don't wanna get hurt!
- **Hostage Taker**: Taking the shop owner hostage, threatening people so they don't come near, and then taking all the money before the police arrive... Another perfect plan!
- **Hostage Taker**: Valkyrie Police School may pack some serious heat, but you can't use it if you're so slow! We'll be long gone by the time they arrive on the scene. Hahaha!
- **Hostage Taker**: Now, time to crack open the safe...
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: Police! Everyone put your hands up and take a step back!
- **Hostage Taker**: What?! How are they already here?! It hasn't even been five minutes since the police were called!
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: Don't compare us to the slowpokes at the Security Bureau! The Public Safety Bureau knows all the shortcuts in this area thanks to our regular patrols!
- **Hostage Taker**: The Public Safety Bureau?
- **Hostage Taker**: You're with Public...Safety? Hahahaha!
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: Wh-What's so funny?
- **Hostage Taker**: You're what's funny! Shouldn't you be directing traffic somewhere? You don't have the muscle to take us on!
- **네무가키 후부키**: Even criminals are looking down on us...
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: Ugh... Just shut up and get caught already!
- **Donut Shop Owner**: A-Ahhh! What are you doing?! You can't just start shooting like that!
- **Hostage Taker**: Y-You brat! Were you even looking at where you were aiming?! Are you trying to ice the hostage too?!
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: *sigh* I knew my marksmanship got low marks on my entrance exam,<br/>but I didn't think it would still be plaguing me at this point...
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: Fubuki, would you mind being my trigger woman?
- **네무가키 후부키**: I dunno. I'm not confident in my shot at this distance.
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: The Security Bureau will arrive and snatch away our commendation if we keep wasting time like this. We need to figure something out soon...
- **네무가키 후부키**: Wouldn't it be better to just leave the arrest up to them at this point?
- **네무가키 후부키**: We don't have sniper rifles to shoot with from this distance or explosives that can break up their barricade. The situation might deteriorate if we try to force things.
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: This is so frustrating! If only we could take down their barricade blocking the entrance. Then it'd be easier to get in...
- **네무가키 후부키**: Hey, Kirino. Isn't that barricade made of the sacks of flour they use to make the donuts?
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: I can't see it clearly from this far away. Why? What's that got to do with anything?
- **네무가키 후부키**: Don't get used to me coming up with plans like this, but I think I just came up with a way to neutralize the culprit.
- **네무가키 후부키**: Hear me out.
- **Hostage Taker**: Whew, it's quiet now. Looks like those cops have given up.
- **Hostage Taker**: Now, back to opening the safe...
- **Hostage Taker**: Wh-What are those loud gunshots?! Are they firing warning shots?
- **Hostage Taker**: Their marksmanship is still terrible. The bullets aren't even making it this far, they're just shooting at the barricade I made from the flour sacks.
- **Hostage Taker**: Hey, idiots! What are you shooting at? That's just flour!
- **Hostage Taker**: What do you hope to accomplish, huh?!
- **Hostage Taker**: *cough* *cough* Where is this smoke coming from? Wait a second...
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: You guessed it! That's flour you're breathing in!
- **네무가키 후부키**: Those shots were never meant for you. Our goal was to fill the store with flour.
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: All it takes it just one small spark to ignite this much flour in the air. In other words...
- **네무가키 후부키**: ...it's a flour bomb!
- **Hostage Taker**: You idiots.
- **Hostage Taker**: The entire block will be blown away if you do that—
- **Donut Shop Owner**: My shop... The neighborhood...
- **Hostage Taker**: Our plan was ruined by these idiots. I can't believe it...
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: Hurray! We caught the culprit!
- **네무가키 후부키**: And we managed to blow up both the culprit and the barricade without even using bombs.
- **네무가키 후부키**: My plan worked perfectly.
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: Now the higher-ups at Valkyrie will have to recognize our achievement, right?
- **Security Director General**: Police! Everyone raise your hands and step...
- **Security Director General**: We were told there was a hostage situation here, but this looks more like a terrorist attack...
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: Look, Director! We caught the culprit before anyone else even got here!
- **Security Director General**: You're Kirino and Fubuki from the Public Safety Bureau, right? What in the world happened here? Did the culprit set off a bomb?!
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: No, ma'am. We destroyed the barricade they had set up!
- **네무가키 후부키**: It was with flour instead of gunpowder, but still very effective.
- **Security Director General**: ...
- **나카츠카사 키리노**: So, what do you think? I can be transferred to the Security Bureau with this kind of achievement under my belt, right?
- **네무가키 후부키**: I don't need to be transferred, but I wouldn't mind about two weeks of vacation.
- **Security Director General**: Blowing up an entire store to solve a problem that could've been solved by one sniper...
- **Security Director General**: Return to your station and be prepared to submit a written apology!
- **Everyone**: What?!
