# 아마미 노도카 인연 스토리

원문(bluearchive.wiki) 인연 스토리 6편. 선생과의 개인 기억이므로 시간창 없이 열람할 수 있다(개인 지식).

- 등장: 아마미 노도카, 선생(샬레)

1. **「아마미 노도카 인연 스토리 1」**

본문 대사는 수록되어 있지 않다.

2. **「아마미 노도카 인연 스토리 2」**

- **???**: Waaait!
- **아마미 노도카**: *pant* *pant* ...Sensei, that's my coin!
- **아마미 노도카**: No, I didn't drop it, but...
- **아마미 노도카**: I found it first!
- **아마미 노도카**: From one kilometer away!
- **아마미 노도카**: Yeah! If I look through this telescope, I can even see ants crawling from hundreds of kilometers away!
- **아마미 노도카**: My morning horoscope was auspicious, so I was looking through my telescope the entire morning to see if something good might happen today...and I got lucky!
- **아마미 노도카**: Supply distribution is terrible at Spec Ops No. 227, so we don't get enough snacks...
- **아마미 노도카**: But 500 yen will buy me a pile of pastries and drinks from the convenience store!
- **아마미 노도카**: So, Sensei, please give me the coin!
- **아마미 노도카**: Why, Sensei?!
- **아마미 노도카**: Why, Sensei?!
- **아마미 노도카**: What? It doesn't matter who saw it first? Whoever picked it up first is the rightful owner?
- **아마미 노도카**: You're mean! You're stingy! You're unreasonable!
- **아마미 노도카**: Wh-What? Isn't L'Oiseau Bleu that...fancy sweets store where the cheapest cake is over 1,000 yen?
- **아마미 노도카**: A-Are you sure?
- **아마미 노도카**: Th-Thank you.
- **아마미 노도카**: Seriously...this is one lucky day.

3. **「아마미 노도카 인연 스토리 3」**

- **아마미 노도카**: That's weird... Sensei was sitting in the Executive Office earlier...
- **아마미 노도카**: Did Sensei go to the restroom?
- **아마미 노도카**: Agh! S-Sensei?
- **아마미 노도카**: Wh-When did you get here? You were in Schale just a minute ago...
- **아마미 노도카**: You came because you were worried after seeing my email?
- **아마미 노도카**: Ugh, if I knew this would happen, I wouldn't have sent that message...
- **아마미 노도카**: Uh, so...I wasn't watching you through the telescope...
- **아마미 노도카**: But I can't help it. I need my daily dose of Sensei to be motivated!
- **아마미 노도카**: This is all because it's so hard to see you, Sensei!
- **아마미 노도카**: I can call you directly if I want to see your face?
- **아마미 노도카**: Th-Thank you...but I'd rather not do that.
- **아마미 노도카**: Your face shines brighter when I'm spying through a telescope than when I'm looking straight at you!
- **아마미 노도카**: I love watching you from afar as you go about your day... *sigh*
- **아마미 노도카**: Ah! That was kind of messed up... F-Forget what you just heard!

4. **「아마미 노도카 인연 스토리 4」**

- **아마미 노도카**: Oh, Sensei. You're here.
- **아마미 노도카**: Why am I so down?
- **아마미 노도카**: I-It's nothing.
- **아마미 노도카**: The thing is...I don't think we'll be able to see the meteors this year.
- **아마미 노도카**: According to the weather forecast, Kivotos is going to be cloudy all day.
- **아마미 노도카**: You can only see the Leonids meteor shower once every thirty years...
- **아마미 노도카**: It's so upsetting to lose an opportunity like this. It only comes two or three times a lifetime.
- **아마미 노도카**: I was going to make a wish... A wish that you make on shooting stars is supposed to come true, you know.
- **아마미 노도카**: But I guess a wish is a wish...because it doesn't always come true.
- **아마미 노도카**: Huh? But even with the best telescope, if the clouds are thick, you won't be able to see the meteors.
- **아마미 노도카**: Huh? But even with the best telescope, if the clouds are thick, you won't be able to see the meteors.
- **아마미 노도카**: So if the clouds are covering the meteors...we should go above the clouds?
- **아마미 노도카**: But a building like that in Kivotos...
- **아마미 노도카**: Are you referring to the building where the Schale Executive Office is located?
- **아마미 노도카**: That's a great idea! The roof of the Schale building goes above the clouds, so we can definitely watch the meteor shower from there!
- **아마미 노도카**: But if you want to enter the Schale building outside of business hours, you'll have to get permission from that scary Rin from the Student Council...
- **아마미 노도카**: W-Would it be okay...
- **아마미 노도카**: Haha... I'm glad to hear that.
- **아마미 노도카**: Haha... I'm glad to hear that.

5. **「아마미 노도카 인연 스토리 5」**

- **아마미 노도카**: I've been waiting for you, Sensei!
- **아마미 노도카**: I was so excited for today that I couldn't sleep... I almost overslept.
- **아마미 노도카**: I'm so glad that I'm able to watch the meteor shower like this.
- **아마미 노도카**: Come on, let's hurry to the roof before it's too late!
- **아마미 노도카**: Phew, it looks like it hasn't started yet.
- **아마미 노도카**: Are you cold, Sensei?
- **아마미 노도카**: It's quite chilly late at night, so it's a good idea to bundle up.
- **아마미 노도카**: Heehee. Would you like a cup of tea?
- **아마미 노도카**: For some reason...I feel a bit shy being alone with you like this in the middle of the night.
- **아마미 노도카**: We won't need the astronomical telescope I brought with me to observe the meteor shower...
- **아마미 노도카**: Wh-What should we talk about to pass the time?
- **아마미 노도카**: ...What?
- **아마미 노도카**: ― Wow, it's a shooting star! Oh... Over there! And over there!
- **아마미 노도카**: ― The sky is full of shooting stars! Meteors are falling like rain!
- **아마미 노도카**: ― I can't believe I'm seeing this with my own two eyes... It's like a dream.
- **아마미 노도카**: ― A wish? No... I'm okay for today.
- **아마미 노도카**: ― I'm content enjoying this beautiful view with someone important...
- **아마미 노도카**: ― If I wish for something more, the stars might grow angry with me...
- **아마미 노도카**: ― I'm totally satisfied for now.
- **아마미 노도카**: But I wonder how the story of wishing on a shooting star came to be.
- **아마미 노도카**: It's a beautiful concept, but it's just a celestial event that has nothing to do with people's wishes...
- **아마미 노도카**: What do you think, Sensei?
- **아마미 노도카**: Hey, you can't fall asleep here!
- **아마미 노도카**: Seriously! Sensei!

6. **「아마미 노도카 인연 스토리 6」**

- **아마미 노도카**: Oh! Hello, Sensei. Are you on your way to work?
- **아마미 노도카**: The weather is so nice today. If I go up somewhere high on a day like this, I'd be able to see everything!
- **아마미 노도카**: Red Winter Academy isn't on this route?
- **아마미 노도카**: Ahaha... Yes, you're right. Actually, I wasn't going to the academy. I was going to a station past the mountains to see the clouds.
- **아마미 노도카**: When I woke up in the morning and looked up at the eastern sky, I saw a beautiful cloud hanging on the mountainside.
- **아마미 노도카**: I thought the view would be prettier from the top of the mountain, so I was on my way over there with my telescope.
- **아마미 노도카**: This one?
- **아마미 노도카**: Of course it's heavy, but I can't fully admire the beautiful scenery without it.
- **아마미 노도카**: As long as I can see grand views with the telescope, the weight of it isn't a concern.
- **아마미 노도카**: Sensei, would you like to go with me to see the clouds?
- **아마미 노도카**: Oh, are you too busy with work at Schale?
- **아마미 노도카**: Well, I guess there's nothing I can do about that!
- **아마미 노도카**: Instead, I'll feast my eyes on all the beautiful sights I can see!
- **아마미 노도카**: Oh, thank you, Sensei.
- **아마미 노도카**: Now then, on to the station platform!
