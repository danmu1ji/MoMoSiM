2. **「RedwinterSecretary 스토리 2화」**

- **렌카와 체리노**: Hmm...
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: May I assist you, President Cherino?
- **렌카와 체리노**: Have you ever heard of a character named Peroro?
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Peroro?
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Ah, yes. I remember a few students had a strange bird plush with that name.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: It was drooling and flapping its wings. I thought it unfit for a student of Red Winter, so I confiscated all of them. Is there something the matter?
- **렌카와 체리노**: You see, I went to downtown Kivotos last weekend, and a limited edition Peroro was being sold at an auction.
- **렌카와 체리노**: Everyone was dying to get this oversized, ugly, useless stuffed character plush. They were even willing to pay double for it!
- **렌카와 체리노**: Millennium, Gehenna, and even the highbrow Trinity students were all desperate for Peroro.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Haha! How pathetic! The students at those schools are brainwashed by the freedom of information!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Red Winter Federal Academy controls such silly media in order to keep our students from being manipulated in the same way.
- **렌카와 체리노**: Hmm. But a wise leader learns even from fools.
- **렌카와 체리노**: I, the smart and wise Great Leader, have found inspiration from their idiocy and come up with a brilliant idea to strengthen our authority!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Please illuminate me, Great Leader.
- **렌카와 체리노**: It's...to bring Peroro to Red Winter Federal Academy!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Excuse me?
- **렌카와 체리노**: What?! Do your ears not work, Chairman Marina?
- **렌카와 체리노**: I said, I want to bring Peroro to Red Winter Federal Academy.
- **렌카와 체리노**: The bird may be a drooling dullard, but Kivotos students are undeniably obsessed with it.
- **렌카와 체리노**: So, if we bring Peroro here, it's sure to attract applicants from all over Kivotos, right?
- **렌카와 체리노**: An academy's power is proportional to its enrollment... If our numbers increase because of Peroro, Red Winter Academy will turn into a super academy the likes of which Trinity and Gehenna wouldn't hold a candle to!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: However...Peroro has a rather small fan base.
- **사시로 토모에**: A perfect plan, Great Leader!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Chief Secretary Tomoe?
- **사시로 토모에**: Promotion through mascots is propaganda 101! It's only a matter of time before students flock here if we have a charming mascot of our own!
- **사시로 토모에**: You came up with such a great plan so quickly... Excellent judgment, President Cherino!
- **렌카와 체리노**: I-Is that so?
- **렌카와 체리노**: Ahaha! Yes, of course! Naturally I, the student council president, Beautifier of Lands, Great Leader, and Athletics Club Captain would come up with a brilliant plan!
- **사시로 토모에**: Peroro's snow-white feathers will match perfectly with the Red Winter Academy's brand. We could put him in a uniform and make him our PR manager.
- **사시로 토모에**: And adding a big white mustache will push it right over the top. What do you think? Word of Red Winter Academy's might will spread all over Kivotos.
- **렌카와 체리노**: Yes, that was my idea exactly! Excellent, Tomoe!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: But...Peroro isn't a student...or real, for that matter.
- **렌카와 체리노**: Chairman Marina, what are you mumbling about in the corner?<br/>Please, speak up!
- **렌카와 체리노**: Or...do you have complaints about my great plan?
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: O-Of course not! It's a...wonderful idea!
- **렌카와 체리노**: Isn't it? My plans are always perfect!
- **렌카와 체리노**: The only thing we have to figure out is how to get Peroro transferred here...
- **렌카와 체리노**: Chairwoman Marina! It'll be your responsibility to transfer Peroro to Red Winter Academy!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: What?!
- **렌카와 체리노**: Are you not happy? You should be honored by the chance to execute my plan!
- **렌카와 체리노**: I shall award you with a Golden Mustache Medal once you successfully bring Peroro to Red Winter!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: B-But how am I supposed to register a fictional character as a student?
- **렌카와 체리노**: The disrespect, Chairman Marina!
- **렌카와 체리노**: I saw Peroro dancing and singing in downtown Kivotos with these very eyes!
- **렌카와 체리노**: Are you saying you don't believe the words of Red Winter Academy's president, the Beautifier of Lands, Great Leader, et cetera?
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: N-No, it's not that...
- **사시로 토모에**: Allow me to intervene. This is written in the Red Winter Academy rules.
- **사시로 토모에**: Red Winter Federal Academy Rules, Article 1: President Cherino is always right.
- **사시로 토모에**: Red Winter Federal Academy Rules, Article 2: Any person who makes a statement that violates Article 1 must be purged.
- **사시로 토모에**: I most certainly hope you are not violating the rules, Chairman Marina.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: O-Of course not, but...
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: (Th-This is bad! I'm going to be purged, at this rate!)
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: (But how am I supposed to make a fictional character into a student?)
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: (Hold on. I can't make the fictional Peroro real, but what if I turn something real into Peroro?)
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: (Heehee... Okay! I can make this work!)
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: You can count on me, President. I will bring Peroro to Red Winter Academy as you ordered!
- **렌카와 체리노**: Excellent. I'll be counting on you, Chairman Marina.
- **렌카와 체리노**: *yawn* Is it time for a nap already?
- **렌카와 체리노**: Tomoe... I'm sleepy...
- **사시로 토모에**: Your bed is already prepared. Please come this way.

3. **「RedwinterSecretary 스토리 3화」**

- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Therefore, you shall become Peroro.
- **아마미 노도카**: What do you mean, "therefore"?! That seems like a gigantic leap!
- **아마미 노도카**: Here I was, celebrating that I was finally leaving the Former Institute,<br/>thrilled my suspension was at last over...
- **아마미 노도카**: ...and then I find out the only way I can leave is if I become Peroro?<br/>What's going on?!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: You ARE finally leaving. If you accept, you'll live comfortably in the main institute. You can even have pudding.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: All you have to do is wear this Peroro costume and say "Pero" at the end of your sentences.
- **아마미 노도카**: How desperate do you think I am?!
- **아마미 노도카**: Ask some of your Security Council cronies! I don't wanna wear that stupid costume!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I already tried. It's too small for any of them.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: The very success of this operation hinges on a tiny kid like you. Doesn't that make you feel good?
- **아마미 노도카**: No! I don't feel good about it, and I don't feel good about being called a tiny kid!
- **아마미 노도카**: And anyway, this whole thing doesn't make any sense! Peroro isn't real!
- **아마미 노도카**: I don't care if it's the president's order. Just tell her it's impossible!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I mean, you're not wrong...
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Wait a moment. Comrade Nodoka. Are you implying President Cherino is wrong?
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: How dare you blatantly break the rules in front of the security chair!<br/>I'll have you purged!
- **아마미 노도카**: Hmph. I'm already suspended, so your petty threats won't work!
- **아마미 노도카**: I don't care if I get to come back to the main institute. I'm not gonna wear your stupid costume or do your stupid lines!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: *sigh*
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I truly didn't want to resort to this, but it seems you leave me no choice.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Security Council, bring you-know-what!
- **아마미 노도카**: This is...premium pudding from L'Oiseau Bleu!
- **아마미 노도카**: Are you trying to bribe me with this 1,000-yen premium pudding?
- **아마미 노도카**: I-It's not that I'm not tempted...but don't think I'll be bribed so easily.<br/>You're wrong...I think!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: What are you going on about?
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Suspended students don't deserve bribes. I'm going to torture you and break your spirit!
- **아마미 노도카**: You're gonna torture me with the pudding? H-How?!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: *nom*
- **아마미 노도카**: Y-YOU'RE eating it?!
- **아마미 노도카**: How cruel. I understand now. You know I've been living here in poverty and can no longer remember the sweet taste of pudding...
- **아마미 노도카**: ...but this kind of suffering is nothing compared to what I've already been through.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Hmph. Very well, then.
- **아마미 노도카**: Y-You're throwing it away?!
- **아마미 노도카**: H-How dare you?! You only took one bite! And then you waste it, like, like...<br/>a waster!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Do you think I didn't do my homework before coming here?
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: You've lived in the Former Institute for a while, which means you've become thrifty. There's now nothing more horrendous and painful to you than food being wasted.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Now, if you don't want to endure true suffering, I recommend you cooperate.
- **아마미 노도카**: You truly are President Cherino's dog. Merciless and cruel, Chairman Marina.
- **아마미 노도카**: But I've strengthened my will while living in the Former Institute!<br/>You'll never break me OR convince me to wear your stupid costume.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I can't help but admire your spirit as a fellow Red Winter student.<br/>I accept your challenge, then.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Security Council. Bring the next item!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I see. So, this is your cherished telescope... Indeed, it's equipped with an expensive, high-quality lens.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I haven't washed my hands all day. Maybe I'll just run my sticky fingers all over the lens. Is that okay with you?
- **아마미 노도카**: G-Get away from my telescope, you monster!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Next! This is a soft feather I had specially airlifted from the Secretariat.<br/>How long will you last against my tickle attack?
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: *tickle tickle tickle*
- **아마미 노도카**: Arrrgh! H-Haha...hahahaha!
- **아마미 노도카**: O-Okay, fine! H-Haha! I-I'll...I'll do whatever you want! Hahaha!<br/>Just stop t-tickling me!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I don't think so! You don't sound like Peroro to me!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Do you want to try this deep-fried pineapple with a mint-choco drizzle?
- **아마미 노도카**: Please end my suffering...
- **아마미 노도카**: *sigh*
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I believe that should be enough.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Let me ask you again. What is your name?
- **아마미 노도카**: Nodo... I mean, P-Peroro.
- **아마미 노도카**: Not a bear. Not a rabbit. Peroro. Pero...pero...
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Excellent, that's the spirit!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I should be able to trick—I mean...report to President Cherino now!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I'll transfer this fake Peroro, receive the special Golden Mustache Medal,<br/>and be one step closer to being the next president!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Now, Nodoka...or should I say, Comrade Peroro? Let's go.

4. **「RedwinterSecretary 스토리 4화」**

- **렌카와 체리노**: Late!
- **렌카와 체리노**: Where's Security Chairman Marina? The Secretariat's weekly meeting started ten minutes ago!
- **사시로 토모에**: It technically started 30 minutes late because of your nap.
- **렌카와 체리노**: Shut up! I'm allowed to delay meetings as much as I want! I'm Red Winter Academy's student council president and Great Leader!
- **렌카와 체리노**: A mere security chair can't make me wait. This is unacceptable!
- **렌카와 체리노**: I'll purge her!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I sincerely apologize for making you wait, President Cherino!
- **렌카와 체리노**: Late!
- **렌카와 체리노**: I had to wait an entire ten minutes! Come here so I can purge you!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I'm sorry, President. It took me longer than I thought to prepare my report...
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: However, I have good news.
- **렌카와 체리노**: Good news?
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Yes. As you ordered, I have transferred Peroro to Red Winter Federal Academy!
- **렌카와 체리노**: You have? Really?
- **렌카와 체리노**: Heehee. Excellent work, Chair Marina! Now students from all over Kivotos will swarm to our school!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I'm honored to be of service to you, President!
- **렌카와 체리노**: So, where is Peroro? Quickly, show me!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Yes, of course.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Nodo...ahem. Comrade Peroro, come forward!
- **???**: Pero pero...
- **Mascot Nodoka**: P-Peroro!
- **렌카와 체리노**: What is this terrifying, gigantic bird? Is it drooling?!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: Th-This is Peroro, just as you requested.
- **렌카와 체리노**: Ugh, I suppose it is. But I didn't expect it to be so ugly in person!
- **렌카와 체리노**: I only saw it from a distance in the city. On closer inspection...it's not as cute as I thought.
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: B-But, Peroro is very popular all over Kivotos, so if it stands in front of the Secretariat with a mustache, it will draw a huge crowd?
- **렌카와 체리노**: How dare you suggest that? Why would I put my precious symbol of authority on something so ugly?
- **렌카와 체리노**: Chief Secretary Tomoe may have said something like that, but we were clearly led astray!
- **렌카와 체리노**: I can't stand the thought of having to see this unpleasant thing every time I go in and out of the Secretariat!
- **렌카와 체리노**: Marina, I have a new job for you! Purge it! Send it to the Former Institute immediately!
- **Mascot Nodoka**: Former institute Spec Ops No. 227, pero?
- **렌카와 체리노**: Did that thing just talk?
- **Mascot Nodoka**: Even if the Former Institute is a cold and barren place, it's dear to each of us.<br/>And you think of it as a giant trashcan?
- **아마미 노도카**: I don't care if you're the president—I won't stand for this, pero!
- **렌카와 체리노**: Why did this exiled pervert come out of Peroro? It was...a fake?!
- **아마미 노도카**: Are you stupid, pero? Peroro is a fictional character!
- **렌카와 체리노**: Ugh... How dare you try to trick me, Marina?!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I-I'm sorry, President!
- **이케쿠라 마리나**: I was just trying to fulfill your impossible orders...
- **렌카와 체리노**: No excuses! You conspired with a suspended Spec Ops student to deceive me! Unforgivable!
- **렌카와 체리노**: Purge all of them! Take the pervert and former security chair to the Former Institute immediately!
- **아마미 노도카**: You can't scare a suspended student with the threat of purging, pero!
- **아마미 노도카**: It's a Pero-revolution, pero!
- **아마미 노도카**: You've been purged too, Marina! Rise up! Let's fight against this dictator!
