# 고료 나구사 인연 스토리

원문(bluearchive.wiki) 인연 스토리 5편. 선생과의 개인 기억이므로 시간창 없이 열람할 수 있다(개인 지식).

- 등장: 고료 나구사, 선생(샬레)

1. **「고료 나구사 인연 스토리 1」**

본문 대사는 수록되어 있지 않다.

2. **「고료 나구사 인연 스토리 2」**

- **고료 나구사**: Hup... Haa!
- **고료 나구사**: ...Oh, Sensei.
- **고료 나구사**: I haven't kept you waiting, have I?
- **고료 나구사**: Forgive me. I was immersed in my training.
- **고료 나구사**: I see... Very good, then.
- **고료 나구사**: Though you've caught me in a bit of a disheveled state...
- **고료 나구사**: If you're willing to wait, I could stop and wash up.
- **고료 나구사**: But...
- **고료 나구사**: Curious, you say?
- **고료 나구사**: Well, there's nothing extraordinary about our training techniques.
- **고료 나구사**: We simply shoot, replace the target, and repeat. It may not be exciting to watch.
- **고료 나구사**: ...Sadly, I lack my predecessors' talent at it.
- **고료 나구사**: So you may come away disappointed, Sensei.
- **고료 나구사**: ...Thank you for saying so.
- **고료 나구사**: Very well...allow me to demonstrate our basic forms.
- **고료 나구사**: Oh, I don't recommend you watch from behind, on account of all the stray bullets.
- **고료 나구사**: Well, as the name implies, the Resolution Council often takes on the task of resolving disputes between students. That often pits a single one of us against a full mob.
- **고료 나구사**: And of course, this is Kivotos, where most people own guns. A shot could come from anywhere at any time.
- **고료 나구사**: Hyakkaryouran Marksmanship was developed over the years to counter that.
- **고료 나구사**: Through its forms, one can evade and snipe multiple enemies at once.
- **고료 나구사**: Essentially, the goal is to calculate the enemy's line of fire, slip around it, sight each target, and respond with precise, rapid shots.
- **고료 나구사**: Of course, enemies may come from behind, or from out of sight, so we prepare for that as well.
- **고료 나구사**: I know little of kung fu, myself...though if I ever have the chance to visit Shanhaijing, I would love to learn more.
- **고료 나구사**: Now, shall I begin?
- **고료 나구사**: Let us suppose that the four targets around me are enemies, and shoot them in order.
- **고료 나구사**: I do recommend you keep your distance.
- **고료 나구사**: *inhale*
- **고료 나구사**: ...Haa!
- **고료 나구사**: Was that acceptable? Pardon me if it seemed a bit unpolished. I don't often perform for an audience.
- **고료 나구사**: ..."How?" I simply shot them as they approached. Why do you ask?
- **고료 나구사**: Well, to explain step by step...
- **고료 나구사**: The enemy at twelve o'clock moved first, so I sidestepped and fired at three o'clock, then turned and fired under my armpit to neutralize the enemy at nine o'clock.
- **고료 나구사**: I then executed a quarter turn and threw my gun, neutralizing the enemy behind me. That caused them to fumble their rifle, so I simply flipped into the air, caught it, shot the enemy at twelve o'clock while upside down, then landed.
- **고료 나구사**: Four enemies neutralized.
- **고료 나구사**: Of course, that method only works for that particular formation. There are other techniques, depending on the enemy's number and position.
- **고료 나구사**: Oh, no need to flatter me, Sensei.
- **고료 나구사**: Though your compliment does give me a certain...well, rush, one might say.
- **고료 나구사**: We have time, if you'd like to see a few more examples.

3. **「고료 나구사 인연 스토리 3」**

- **Merchant A**: Try our special-sauce yakisoba! We also have okonomiyaki!
- **Merchant B**: Melon bread, filled with real Ebisu melon cream!
- **Merchant C**: Special cotton candy! Chise's favorite!
- **Hyakkiyako Club Member A**: The Rakugo Fitness Club will be holding a performance in the plaza soon. Come join us!
- **Hyakkiyako Club Member A**: A perfect chance to burn 500 calories with a single Rakugo session! Great for losing weight!
- **고료 나구사**: Ah, [USERNAME] Sensei. You're here.
- **고료 나구사**: Did you enjoy the festival?
- **고료 나구사**: You wouldn't have. I was so busy grilling skewers and moving things about, I hardly left the stall.
- **고료 나구사**: But it was worth it to see the customers enjoy the food I'd made for them.
- **고료 나구사**: Everyone worked their hardest today to make it all a success.
- **Merchant B**: Oh, my. Thank you for all your hard work today, Miss President!
- **고료 나구사**: Uhm, I'm actually...
- **Merchant B**: Oh, are you the vice president? Well, regardless, we couldn't have done it without you. Would've been a disaster!
- **Merchant B**: When our grill volunteer called in sick, I thought we'd have to sit the whole festival out.
- **Merchant B**: But thanks to you, we got to serve all kinds of customers.
- **Merchant B**: And your culinary skills are amazing. You said it was your first time? Did you learn it from somewhere?
- **고료 나구사**: I hadn't made skewers before, but I do enjoy eating them.
- **고료 나구사**: Honestly, mine weren't nearly as good as some others I've had. You have my apologies for that.
- **Merchant B**: Haha! And you're humble on top of it all.
- **Merchant B**: Oh, hey, I almost forgot to tell you...
- **Merchant B**: We're having an afterparty. If you don't have any plans, would you like to join us?
- **Merchant B**: Everyone's excited that it went so well today, after all the work we put into it.
- **Merchant B**: It'd be even more fun with you there, Nagusa!
- **고료 나구사**: In that case...I...
- **고료 나구사**: ...Oh, I'm sorry. I do have plans. I don't think I can make it.
- **Merchant B**: You do? Ah, well, that's a shame.
- **Merchant B**: In that case, please drop by our shop later when you can. The skewers are on us!
- **고료 나구사**: I'll certainly look forward to that.
- **고료 나구사**: Truthfully...no.
- **고료 나구사**: I lied. I simply didn't wish to attend the party.
- **고료 나구사**: No, not at all. In fact, I enjoy that sort of rowdy atmosphere.
- **고료 나구사**: But...my own rather cold demeanor is not the best fit for it.
- **고료 나구사**: I rarely know what to say, and people often misread my expressions and take my coldness personally.
- **고료 나구사**: When it comes to helping other people enjoy themselves, I'm...inexperienced at best.
- **고료 나구사**: In that kind of atmosphere, warm as it is, I would only lower the temperature. So I declined.
- **고료 나구사**: Hm?!
- **고료 나구사**: B-But...
- **고료 나구사**: ...I hadn't thought of it that way.
- **고료 나구사**: Would you pardon me, Sensei?
- **고료 나구사**: Let me catch up with them and ask if I can still join.

4. **「고료 나구사 인연 스토리 4」**

- **고료 나구사**: Excuse me, why are you loitering in the park so late?
- **Wandering Student**: ...None of your business.
- **고료 나구사**: You are, of course, free to be here as a student...
- **고료 나구사**: ...but this place is remote, and potentially unsafe at this hour. If you must be out, consider a family restaurant or a cafe instead.
- **Wandering Student**: Nah. I might run into my sister there.
- **고료 나구사**: ...Your sister?
- **Wandering Student**: ...Nah.
- **Wandering Student**: And I definitely didn't just run off to noplace 'cause I had a fight with her!
- **고료 나구사**: Hm.
- **Suspicious Student**: H-Huh? Wh-Who's there?
- **고료 나구사**: What exactly are you up to?
- **Suspicious Student**: Uh, construction. We're doin' construction.
- **Suspicious Student**: We're workin' on the sewers— I mean the waterworks!
- **고료 나구사**: Odd. I haven't heard of any waterworks project in this area.
- **고료 나구사**: Certainly nothing scheduled for this hour of the night.
- **고료 나구사**: Would you mind if I check your equipment?
- **Suspicious Student**: Ugh...
- **Mouryo A**: Damn it, they're onto us! Run!
- **Mouryo B**: Hold up— there's still Hyakkaryourans walking around here?
- **Mouryo A**: So much for our bomb plot...
- **고료 나구사**: Oh, you're not getting away.
- **Drunk Resident**: Home? *hic* Anyplace I can shtretch out under the shky's my home!
- **Drunk Resident**: This whole WORLD'SH my home. What'sh the problem with that?
- **Drunk Resident**: What?! You're gonna hand me over to those brats?!
- **Drunk Resident**: I'm not goin' down without a fight! Bring it on!
- **고료 나구사**: ...Sensei, would you like me to "take care" of this one?
- **고료 나구사**: We did well, Sensei.
- **고료 나구사**: Tonight was a rougher night than usual.
- **고료 나구사**: And it's well past midnight...if we waited a bit, we could see the sunrise.
- **고료 나구사**: Just a moment, Sensei.
- **고료 나구사**: This is the darkest time of night, with the moon having set.
- **고료 나구사**: You might stumble and sprain your ankle on the way there. Let us rest until the darkness fades.
- **고료 나구사**: Besides, I wouldn't mind a breather.
- **고료 나구사**: ...It's so quiet.
- **고료 나구사**: This teahouse is so rowdy with customers in the day. But right now...it's as if everything has stopped breathing.
- **고료 나구사**: This isn't the first time I've done this.
- **고료 나구사**: Rested here and waited for dawn, I mean. After a long patrol.
- **고료 나구사**: ...Nothing in particular.
- **고료 나구사**: Well, one thing. It makes a good hiding place...
- **고료 나구사**: ...from my fear of the dark.
- **고료 나구사**: ― This moment, just before the sun rises...
- **고료 나구사**: ― is when the darkness dominates.
- **고료 나구사**: ― This pitch-black darkness... I've always feared it.
- **고료 나구사**: ― I don't shine enough to lift that darkness.
- **고료 나구사**: ― Nor could I...endure...
- **고료 나구사**: ― through the passing of the night.
- **고료 나구사**: ― I had no choice but to hide,
- **고료 나구사**: ― pretending to be someone else, blending into the shadows, feigning composure.
- **고료 나구사**: ― But not anymore.
- **고료 나구사**: ― I know that the night will pass with time...
- **고료 나구사**: ― and my shadow will cast over the earth.
- **고료 나구사**: ― So...
- **고료 나구사**: ― I declare my name to the spirit realm.
- **고료 나구사**: ― ...I am the President of the Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council...
- **고료 나구사**: ― Goryou Nagusa!
- **고료 나구사**: Ah, and now the sun rises.
- **고료 나구사**: ...Quite a night, wasn't it?
- **고료 나구사**: A night when Mouryos ran wild, in all sorts of strange forms...
- **고료 나구사**: It's fitting for the name of "Hyakkiyako." "The Night Walk of a Hundred Ghosts."

5. **「고료 나구사 인연 스토리 5」**

- **고료 나구사**: Apologies for my lateness, Sensei.
- **고료 나구사**: I got a bit lost on my way here...
- **고료 나구사**: No... I'm all right.
- **고료 나구사**: (grimace) I'm all right. What can I help with?
- **고료 나구사**: Nothing happened, I merely... I stepped off the train at the wrong station on the way here, and got a bit lost, as I said.
- **고료 나구사**: And it's...quite sunny out there.
- **고료 나구사**: No, no. I came here to help, not to cause you more troub—
- **고료 나구사**: ...Excuse me. I'll take a small break before I start, then. Just a small one.
- **고료 나구사**: Phew... I feel like myself again.
- **고료 나구사**: Thank you, Sensei. I do feel much better now.
- **고료 나구사**: Well, not "heat advisory" hot, per se, but...
- **고료 나구사**: Well...hot weather is a weakness of mine.
- **고료 나구사**: Cold weather I can stand, but summers always make me sick.
- **고료 나구사**: When I was younger, people often called me "the Snow Maiden."
- **고료 나구사**: Flattering, in a sense. The myths always describe the Snow Maiden as a mysterious beauty. I suppose I could see the resemblance.
- **고료 나구사**: All the same, I never liked those stories.
- **고료 나구사**: Because they never end happily.
- **고료 나구사**: The Snow Maiden always tries to express her affection to someone, but no matter how strongly she feels it, no matter how poetic her words, that someone always rejects her because of her cold appearance.
- **고료 나구사**: So I didn't always appreciate being compared to a lovelorn maiden who vanishes or melts when the world rejects her.
- **고료 나구사**: Even if the stories are...true in a sense.
- **고료 나구사**: When winter comes, and the ground is barren, all most people can feel about it is...longing for the summer sun.
- **고료 나구사**: Indeed. You put in so much ice, it's still chilling my hands.
- **고료 나구사**: I don't disagree, but why do you bring that up?
- **고료 나구사**: ...Perhaps you're right. I've never heard stories of a Snow Maiden living through the summer.
- **고료 나구사**: But here I am. And perhaps I'm not without hope for it.
