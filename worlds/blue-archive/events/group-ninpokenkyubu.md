2. **「NinpoKenkyubu 스토리 2화」**

- **치도리 미치루**: Yay! We're finally done filming!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: W-wonderful work, P-President... Would you like some water?
- **치도리 미치루**: Thanks, Tsukuyo.
- **오노 츠쿠요**: I also b-brought a towel to wipe your b-brow... and some h-homemade lemon honey!
- **치도리 미치루**: Wow! Really?
- **치도리 미치루**: Heehee! Don't mind if I do!
- **치도리 미치루**: We really make a great team, don't we? Like we were meant to be together.
- **치도리 미치루**: And you were way more prepared than I expected, Tsukuyo!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Oh, it was n-nothing... That's what I'm h-here for...
- **치도리 미치루**: Nonsense! You were so helpful at today's shoot!
- **치도리 미치루**: If it weren't for your expert skills with that light reflector...
- **치도리 미치루**: ...we never would have caught that incredible scene!
- **치도리 미치루**: The way the shadows wrapped around the ninja prisoner... Ah!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Th-That's true... Still... I wish I could be m-more helpful during filming...
- **오노 츠쿠요**: The best I can ever h-hope to be is a filming assistant...
- **오노 츠쿠요**: And I c-can't even do that well... Like this morning...
- **치도리 미치루**: Oh that...
- **치도리 미치루**: Tsukuyo! Yes, just hold the reflector right there! Perfect!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: O-Okay! Hold the reflector. Simple...
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Huh? Oh, um...
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Hello, there. P-Please don't stand that close.
- **오노 츠쿠요**: I'm doing something v-very important.
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Weaving around my l-legs like that makes it hard for me to do my j-job.
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Please! I don't want to get in t-trouble!
- **Park-Visiting Child A**: Whoa! Look at all those cats over there!
- **Park-Visiting Child B**: It's like some kind of cat family reunion!
- **Park-Visiting Child A**: That's so cool! I'm gonna take a picture!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Ah! P-Please don't take pictures!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: W-We're in the middle of filming!
- **치도리 미치루**: Haha! That was quite a disaster, wasn't it?
- **오노 츠쿠요**: *sniffle* It... It was another 30 minutes before... before we could start shooting again...
- **오노 츠쿠요**: I-I'm so sorry, P-President!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: It's all my f-fault!
- **치도리 미치루**: It's not your fault, Tsukuyo.
- **치도리 미치루**: They probably just saw all that sunlight and thought it would be a great place to nap.
- **오노 츠쿠요**: *sniffle* Then... I-I'll have to bring treats to distract them n-next time.
- **치도리 미치루**: Next time?! I doubt it'll happen again. There's no need to be that well-prepared!
- **치도리 미치루**: We'll be just fine without cat treats.
- **치도리 미치루**: What do you say, Dame Tsukuyo?
- **오노 츠쿠요**: H-Huh? D-Dame?
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Yeah! Isn't that the proper title for a ninja?
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Well... Y-Yes, sure!
- **치도리 미치루**: Let's forget about this morning and focus on what's next!
- **치도리 미치루**: It's time to edit the video we captured today!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: U-Understood!
- **치도리 미치루**: Hm...
- **치도리 미치루**: Hmm...
- **치도리 미치루**: Agh!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: P-President! What's wrong?
- **치도리 미치루**: I hate to say it, but...
- **치도리 미치루**: ...This isn't good enough.
- **오노 츠쿠요**: It's n-not?
- **치도리 미치루**: Not even close. I envisioned perfect composition, camera work, edits...
- **치도리 미치루**: Between my eye and your skills, I thought...
- **치도리 미치루**: Well, I thought it would have more bang!
- **치도리 미치루**: And flash! It was going to be so cool!
- **치도리 미치루**: But this... It doesn't have that panache I was going for. I can't upload this...
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Oh no...
- **치도리 미치루**: It's fine, I suppose. It happens to the best of us.
- **오노 츠쿠요**: I'm so s-sorry! I hope it's not because of m-me!

3. **「NinpoKenkyubu 스토리 3화」**

- **쿠다 이즈나**: Wow!
- **쿠다 이즈나**: So this is the Ninjutsu Research Club! I've always wanted to see it!
- **치도리 미치루**: Pst! Tsukuyo!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Huh?
- **치도리 미치루**: Tsukuyo! What do we do? She's cute, but she's so nosy!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: You t-think she's suspicious?
- **치도리 미치루**: Of course I do! Nobody knows about this place but us!
- **치도리 미치루**: And she just shows up out of nowhere looking to join? It's fishy...
- **치도리 미치루**: Extremely fishy!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: But... That's how I j-joined...
- **치도리 미치루**: No! You're different! I recruited you personally, Tsukuyo. Remember?
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Oh, that's right...
- **치도리 미치루**: She must be some kind of spy from the Yin-Yang Club or a Hyakkaryouran assassin!
- **치도리 미치루**: It's unlikely, but still...
- **오노 츠쿠요**: She makes me nervous.
- **오노 츠쿠요**: We know nothing about her.
- **오노 츠쿠요**: B-But... This could be our first step to becoming a r-real club!
- **치도리 미치루**: Yeah, I know I said I wanted a new club member. But she's just some random person who walked in off the street. Hm...
- **치도리 미치루**: Ah! I know what to do.
- **치도리 미치루**: We'll conduct a special interview!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: S-special interview?
- **치도리 미치루**: Yep! Then I'll know if she's a good fit for the Ninjutsu Research Club!
- **치도리 미치루**: You don't mind doing an interview, do you Izuna?
- **쿠다 이즈나**: Of course not! You have to make sure an amateur ninja wouldn't be a liability during missions.
- **쿠다 이즈나**: I completely understand!
- **치도리 미치루**: Yes, uh... Exactly.
- **치도리 미치루**: Tsukuyo, why don't you get a drink for our guest?
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Right!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: U-Um...
- **오노 츠쿠요**: H-Here. I h-hope you like it.
- **쿠다 이즈나**: Oh, thank you!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: I'm s-sorry we can't offer you m-more.
- **쿠다 이즈나**: It's okay! Nin nin!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Nin nin? No, it's not—I'm not sure...
- **오노 츠쿠요**: I'm sorry it's not tea or anything f-fancy.
- **오노 츠쿠요**: It's just t-tap water...
- **쿠다 이즈나**: Tap water?
- **오노 츠쿠요**: I'm sorry! I can't believe we're offering t-tap water to a g-guest!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: I don't mean to be r-rude! It's just hard to find t-tea leaves here.
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Especially without club f-funds... *sniffle*
- **치도리 미치루**: It's okay, Tsukuyo!
- **치도리 미치루**: We're lucky to have such a good water supply system! We should be proud to serve tap water!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: I d-don't know about that, President!
- **쿠다 이즈나**: Incredible!
- **치도리 미치루**: Huh?
- **오노 츠쿠요**: What?
- **쿠다 이즈나**: I saw a ninja documentary about this ages ago.
- **쿠다 이즈나**: A true ninja should always be satisfied with chilled water. Anything more is too lavish!
- **쿠다 이즈나**: To see you practicing this in daily life... You're so much more dedicated than the rumors say! I'm impressed!
- **치도리 미치루**: Yeah...
- **치도리 미치루**: Ahem... Anyway, let's start the interview.
- **치도리 미치루**: How did you find our club, Izuna?
- **치도리 미치루**: We're quite far removed from the Hyakkiyako area.
- **치도리 미치루**: There's not much in the way of transportation or amenities here.
- **치도리 미치루**: (In fact, we chose this location to stay under the Yin-Yang Club and Hyakkaryouran's radar...)
- **오노 츠쿠요**: I'll admit it was hard to find. And if I miss the bus, it will be nearly impossible to get to school.
- **오노 츠쿠요**: And the b-building is old, so the water and electricity g-get cut off sometimes. Plus, there's not a single c-convenience store in sight...
- **치도리 미치루**: What are you trying to say, Tsukuyo?!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: N-Nothing! This is a g-great location!
- **오노 츠쿠요**: Y-You can see all the stars at night...it's perfect for filming the president's ninjutsu...
