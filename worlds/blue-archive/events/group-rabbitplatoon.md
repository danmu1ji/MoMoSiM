- **소라이 사키**: W-Well, what do you want us to do? Eat it?!
- **츠키유키 미야코**: EOD missions such as eliminating duds and IEDs are also part of SRT's duties.
- **츠키유키 미야코**: We can't afford to let this abominable bio-bomb fall into anyone else's hands.
- **소라이 사키**: Miyako, are you...
- **카제쿠라 모에**: Are you serious right now?!
- **츠키유키 미야코**: It's our duty to properly eliminate this seafood can!

- **츠키유키 미야코**: We have to render the explosive inoperable without detonating it.
- **츠키유키 미야코**: Since most of the odorous components of fish are water-soluble, let's begin by slowly degassing the canned food in water.

- **츠키유키 미야코**: Right, Saki. I'm counting on you.
- **소라이 사키**: What in the hell makes you think I'm gonna do it?
- **츠키유키 미야코**: You're the only one out of us who's completed the EOD training, Saki.
- **소라이 사키**: That's not true. Moe did too.
- **츠키유키 미야코**: Well, but... I mean, Moe and explosives...
