2. **「Class227 스토리 2화」**

- **아마미 노도카**: It's so cold today!
- **아마미 노도카**: And all this snow... I could very well turn into a snowman and die out here!
- **아마미 노도카**: Heehee. Good thing I've got a bundle of dry branches and lots of fruit!
- **마요이 시구레**: You look happy, Nodoka. Things went well?
- **아마미 노도카**: Shigure!
- **아마미 노도카**: It's true! Just look at my haul!
- **아마미 노도카**: With all this snow, I thought I wouldn't find a single dry branch. But it turns out I'll have a nice cozy feast next to the fire.
- **마요이 시구레**: I'm glad to hear it.
- **마요이 시구레**: You need to take better care of yourself, though. Your face is red.
- **아마미 노도카**: R-Really? I guess I got a little carried away searching for supplies.
- **마요이 시구레**: Let's get you out of the cold. I'll make us a fire.
- **아마미 노도카**: *sigh* The fire's warming me up inside and out.
- **마요이 시구레**: Hot cocoa, Nodoka?
- **아마미 노도카**: Thank you, Shigure. I don't know what I'd do without you.
- **마요이 시구레**: Me? You're the one who gathered wood for us. I'm grateful for you.
- **마요이 시구레**: And that adorable smile gives me the energy to keep going.
- **마요이 시구레**: We might be stuck here, but I'm happy to be stuck here with you, Nodoka.
- **아마미 노도카**: Sh-Shigure, I'm blushing...
- **마요이 시구레**: Nodoka...
- **아마미 노도카**: Shigure....
- **???**: Stop, stop! Stop right now!
- **아마미 노도카**: Wh-What in the heck is this, Meru?!
- **아마미 노도카**: Why are Shigure and I protagonists in a romance novel?
- **히메키 메루**: What do you mean? I'm writing a story about the life of Former Institute<br/>No. 227 students, just as you asked.
- **히메키 메루**: I may have spiced up some of the details a little, but it's based on your lives!<br/>Heehee.
- **아마미 노도카**: A little?! Other than the fact that we're cold and hungry, everything is made up! I want to describe our lives here in these horrible conditions, not write an original story!
- **아마미 노도카**: It didn't matter how loud we were. The Secretariat never responded... So we thought maybe if other students petitioned...
- **히메키 메루**: Hmm. No one cares about just your lives, though.
- **히메키 메루**: Sometimes you need an interesting hook to get the readers reading.
- **아마미 노도카**: It's a petition! Not some story! And your version sucks anyway!
- **마요이 시구레**: I kinda liked it.
- **마요이 시구레**: Especially the scene in front of the fire... It was romantic.
- **아마미 노도카**: Shigure. Please. Do you think all this work has been for a literary contest?
- **아마미 노도카**: This is supposed to be about how awful and scary our lives are here!<br/>Not your fantasy teenage romance world.
- **아마미 노도카**: Imagine if we sent this to the Secretariat! That stupid Cherino would be like, "They're supposed to be in punishment, but they're happy and in love! Suspend them forever!"
- **히메키 메루**: I think you're being a bit judgmental. This could be the next masterpiece in the history of Kivotos.
- **아마미 노도카**: Then do it on your own time! Right now, what we need is a petition to save us from our inevitable fate of starving to death in the cold at the Former Institute!
- **아마미 노도카**: Please write it again—non-fiction this time!
- **히메키 메루**: Fine. I'll re-write it.
- **히메키 메루**: "Dear Great Leader. Our comrades of Spec Ops No. 227 are suffering from a serious shortage of supplies. We've even been forced to split our jawbreakers into two!"
- **아마미 노도카**: Yes! Yes! That's perfect.
- **아마미 노도카**: Please include some examples so they understand our suffering.
- **히메키 메루**: "Nodoka was pitifully savoring her hard-earned candy..."
- **아마미 노도카**: H-Huh? I don't like the direction this is going!
- **히메키 메루**: "Shigure walked in to see her beloved Nodoka eating candy, all alone. She knelt down next to Nodoka and gently traced her mouth..."
- **아마미 노도카**: Wait, stop! STOP!
- **아마미 노도카**: Why are you doing this?!
- **히메키 메루**: Because...
- **히메키 메루**: It's fun?
- **아마미 노도카**: We don't need fun right now! Ugh! I can't deal with the perverted librarians of the Knowledge Liberation Front!
- **아마미 노도카**: I never should have placed my hopes and dreams in your hands.
- **아마미 노도카**: Please leave our home!
- **히메키 메루**: Hmph. Your loss. When my masterpiece becomes a hit, I'll be sure to forget you!
- **아마미 노도카**: I don't care about your stupid, lying story!
- **마요이 시구레**: What a shame. I wanted to see how it ended.
- **아마미 노도카**: I've said it before: We'll never escape this horrible place with your lackadaisical attitude.
- **아마미 노도카**: We need a petition that pulls at the Secretariat officers' heartstrings and makes them cry!
- **아마미 노도카**: I won't let them keep neglecting us and our existences!
- **아마미 노도카**: We need this appeal to work so we can go back to our regular lives.
- **마요이 시구레**: Yeah but...who are more capable writers than the Knowledge Liberation Front?

3. **「Class227 스토리 3화」**

- **아마미 노도카**: Why are they fighting with each other in a petition?
- **아마미 노도카**: Mint chocolate and karaage won't improve our situation!<br/>How dare they put their selfish interests in our petition!
- **마요이 시구레**: Well, Red Winter students are notoriously interested in themselves.
- **아마미 노도카**: Ugh. It won't be as effective if another club writes in anyway. I'll just have to do my best...
- **야스모리 미노리**: Hiya, Spec Ops No. 227!
- **아마미 노도카**: M-Minori? Why are you here?
- **아마미 노도카**: W-We paid all construction costs and haven't infringed on the rights of the Labor Party at all!
- **야스모리 미노리**: We're not always just running around going on strike, you know.
- **야스모리 미노리**: I should explain... The Labor Party naturally hears many rumors floating around the student community and helps those same students get various jobs.
- **야스모리 미노리**: Now we've heard that Spec Ops No. 227, with whom we once planned a revolution, have been looking for help.
- **아마미 노도카**: We are...but not fixing things or anything like that. We want to write a petition to submit to the Secretariat.
- **아마미 노도카**: I asked students from clubs all over to write petitions for us to help capture our struggles, but it hasn't been going well. So, I guess that's that.<br/>I did my best...
- **야스모리 미노리**: Hmm. Well, I'm an expert on that kind of writing!
- **야스모리 미노리**: A good project is completed by unified workers, worker unity is achieved through good speeches, and good speeches are achieved through good writing!
- **아마미 노도카**: R-Really? Then do you think I could ask you to help us, Minori?
- **야스모리 미노리**: Of course! I'd love to.
- **야스모리 미노리**: "The students of Spec Ops No. 227 are suffering in the harsh weather due to poor bedding and heating."
- **야스모리 미노리**: "This is an overly harsh environment to endure, even considering their past transgressions. I implore you to take the steps to revoke their suspension and return all of them to the main institute."
- **아마미 노도카**: Wow. You're much better than I expected.
- **야스모리 미노리**: That's a little rude, you know. I always get an A on my writing tests.
- **야스모리 미노리**: The important thing about writing is to clearly convey your purpose.
- **야스모리 미노리**: And, of course, you have to keep your reader interested with a bit of rhetoric.
- **아마미 노도카**: Thank you! This petition will help us make the Secretariat understand how horrible our lives are!
- **아마미 노도카**: And you did it so fast! I'm so impressed, Labor Party President!
- **마요이 시구레**: It is pretty good...but do you really think President Cherino will read it?
- **마요이 시구레**: Knowing her, she won't even look at it.
- **아마미 노도카**: You're right, Shigure! That irresponsible brat will probably burn it without even opening it!
- **아마미 노도카**: How do we get her to read it?
- **야스모리 미노리**: I have an idea!
- **야스모리 미노리**: I'll just add this to the front of the envelope...
- **아마미 노도카**: That's perfect! She won't be able to resist after seeing it!
- **야스모리 미노리**: Let's add this underneath so she knows exactly who it is too.
- **아마미 노도카**: You're a genius, Minori!
- **아마미 노도카**: All that's left is to send our petition to the Secretariat...
- **아마미 노도카**: Then our life in the Former Institute will finally be over!
- **사시로 토모에**: President, it's time for the weekly report.
- **사시로 토모에**: As usual, you have received many letters from different clubs.
- **렌카와 체리노**: Heehee. I already know what it's about!
- **렌카와 체리노**: They're all praising my greatness and incredible mustache!
- **사시로 토모에**: Actually, if you'll allow me to summarize... Ehem!
- **사시로 토모에**: "A kid who doesn't appreciate mint doesn't deserve to be the president!"<br/>"We're sick of mustaches!"<br/>"The dictator kid needs to wake up!"
- **렌카와 체리노**: What did you just say?!
- **렌카와 체리노**: How dare they hide behind their letters to challenge the authority of my mustache?!
- **렌카와 체리노**: Give me all the letters, Tomoe! I'll purge every last one of them!
- **렌카와 체리노**: What's this? There's red writing on the envelope.
- **렌카와 체리노**: "If you don't read this letter, you'll be cursed"?
- **렌카와 체리노**: Tomoe! Help! What do I do? I'm going to be cursed!
- **사시로 토모에**: Don't worry. It's likely just a chain letter.
- **렌카와 체리노**: A chain letter...?
- **사시로 토모에**: Yes. A manipulative message that convinces people to pass it around to others. They've been a popular prank among the students recently.
- **사시로 토모에**: It's likely just full of fake, unfortunate events.
- **렌카와 체리노**: I see. It does talk about a lot of misfortunes.
- **렌카와 체리노**: "The students of Spec Ops No. 227 are suffering in the harsh weather due to poor bedding and heating facilities..."
- **렌카와 체리노**: How strange. Even though it's a prank, it makes me think of something I feel like I've forgotten...
- **렌카와 체리노**: Chief Secretary Tomoe. Accompany me during my nap today.
- **렌카와 체리노**: I-It's not that I'm scared! The mind of a great leader is simply always busy!
- **사시로 토모에**: Of course, President. I will stay with you as long as you need.
- **사시로 토모에**: If you would please, I can burn the chain letter so it no longer affects you.
- **렌카와 체리노**: That's a good idea, Tomoe.
- **렌카와 체리노**: All right, let's move on!
