# 와니부치 아카리 인연 스토리

원문(bluearchive.wiki) 인연 스토리 5편. 선생과의 개인 기억이므로 시간창 없이 열람할 수 있다(개인 지식).

- 등장: 와니부치 아카리, 선생(샬레)

1. **「와니부치 아카리 인연 스토리 1」**

본문 대사는 수록되어 있지 않다.

2. **「와니부치 아카리 인연 스토리 2」**

- **와니부치 아카리**: Sensei, we don't have much time left before the eating contest downtown!
- **와니부치 아카리**: This time, it's gonna be a hamburger-eating contest. I've never challenged myself to competitively eat hamburgers before, so I'm a bit nervous about this one.
- **와니부치 아카리**: I can't give up yet, though! I need to keep my winning streak going for the third year in a row!
- **와니부치 아카리**: That's why I'm going to practice eating burgers in advance, here at the Gehenna dining hall.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Though... There's a risk of not pacing myself right if I do this solo. Please help set my pace and make sure I don't overextend myself!
- **와니부치 아카리**: Huh? You've never had to set pace before?
- **와니부치 아카리**: In marathons, there are runners who run alongside others just to keep them on an even pace.
- **와니부치 아카리**: If you only focus on running fast without considering the remaining distance, you'll run out of steam too quickly.
- **와니부치 아카리**: An eating contest is just like a marathon. If you don't keep your pace and start off inhaling food, you'll get tired of its taste sooner. Even worse, you might get an upset stomach!
- **와니부치 아카리**: That's why I want you to keep up with me when I'm eating burgers, Sensei!
- **와니부치 아카리**: Don't worry! I knew you'd say that, so I prepared some sliders for you instead.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Every time you eat one of these tiny burgers, I'll eat a big one. That way I can match my pace with yours.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Well, then, let's get started!
- **와니부치 아카리**: That's thirty-one hamburgers down!
- **와니부치 아카리**: Come on, time for hamburger number thirty-two...
- **와니부치 아카리**: Sensei, haven't you finished your slider yet? It's bite-sized, so just swallow it whole.
- **와니부치 아카리**: We still have a long way to go, and I need you with me. Our goal is a hundred! Let's move, chop-chop!
- **와니부치 아카리**: ...You wanna know what the world record for hamburger eating is?
- **와니부치 아카리**: If I remember correctly, there was a competitive eater who ate thirty burgers in one go, and that's still the world record. Since that monumental occasion, burgers have never been selected as the subject of an eating contest.
- **와니부치 아카리**: What, do you think we should stop since I already set a new record?
- **와니부치 아카리**: No way! We should give our all in every competition, regardless of records!
- **와니부치 아카리**: You have to understand...this battle is a battle against myself!
- **와니부치 아카리**: Come on, Sensei! Less whining and more eating! I'm still far from finished.
- **와니부치 아카리**: If you can't feed yourself, then I'll just feed them to you. Open your mouth, okay?
- **와니부치 아카리**: All right, Sensei. Say "Ahhh..." ♥

3. **「와니부치 아카리 인연 스토리 3」**

- **와니부치 아카리**: Hmm, hmm... Hmm... ♪
- **와니부치 아카리**: Ah, today's lunch was delicious. I got to try a tasty new restaurant, and it put me in a great mood.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Did you eat bread for lunch again?
- **와니부치 아카리**: That's not okay. You ought to fill up for lunch if you want to stay focused through the whole afternoon. You should eat something more nutritious than that.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Even if you don't want to, you ought to eat at a restaurant instead of a convenience store. I know some good places near Schale you can try!
- **와니부치 아카리**: Eh? How did you know?
- **와니부치 아카리**: That's right, I had a bowl of ramen for lunch. There's this new ramen restaurant that just opened in front of the Schale building.
- **와니부치 아카리**: It says on the menu that 10 servings of the jumbo ramen are free if you finish them within 30 minutes. I ate two bowls in a row, and they were great!
- **와니부치 아카리**: They served authentic tonkotsu ramen, with rich broth and plenty of toppings.
- **와니부치 아카리**: ...By the way, how did you know that I ate ramen for lunch, Sensei?
- **와니부치 아카리**: Wait...did you smell garlic on me?
- **와니부치 아카리**: Wow... I brushed my teeth as hard as I could, but I guess the garlic scent really clings to me.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Now that I think about it, there were three whole bulbs of garlic in the ramen, and I ate it all... No wonder I smell like garlic.
- **와니부치 아카리**: I don't want to smell like garlic in front of you, but I don't want to eat tonkotsu ramen without garlic, either... Oh, what to do?
- **와니부치 아카리**: But this is a serious issue for me!
- **와니부치 아카리**: I love eating all kinds of delicious foods, but I don't want you to remember me as that one student who smelled like garlic.
- **와니부치 아카리**: I need to come up with a solution...
- **와니부치 아카리**: Welcome, Sensei! I've been waiting for you!
- **와니부치 아카리**: I looked up solutions on the internet yesterday, and apparently the human nose cannot distinguish a smell if it's exposed to a specific one for too long.
- **와니부치 아카리**: So, my solution is this: I'll bombard you with garlic smell, so much so that you won't smell anything odd when I'm nearby!
- **와니부치 아카리**: All right, now. I've made some food with plenty of garlic in it for you, Sensei.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Garlic bread, garlic fried rice, garlic dumplings, garlic pickles...
- **와니부치 아카리**: If we eat lots of garlic together, its scent will be nothing to us!

4. **「와니부치 아카리 인연 스토리 4」**

- **와니부치 아카리**: Which restaurant should we go to today?
- **와니부치 아카리**: My wallet's pretty light today, so I think I'll go to an all-you-can-eat restaurant and load up.
- **와니부치 아카리**: For tonight's dinner...it's not all-you-can-eat without yakiniku, and it's not yakiniku if it isn't all-you-can-eat!
- **와니부치 아카리**: It's a bit early, but I'm gonna go have some grilled short ribs.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Hello. I'd like one order of short ribs, please.
- **Yakiniku Restaurant Staff**: Welco—*gasp*
- **Yakiniku Restaurant Staff**: (Isn't that Wanibuchi Akari, the competitive eating champion?!)
- **Yakiniku Restaurant Staff**: (I heard that she devoured thirty servings of short ribs in an instant at the last event...)
- **Yakiniku Restaurant Staff**: (This is bad... If I let a customer like her in, our restaurant will go out of business for sure!)
- **Yakiniku Restaurant Staff**: Uh, um...I'm terribly sorry, ma'am, but we've run out of short ribs for today. We can't accept any more customers.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Really? But I just saw another customer walk in.
- **Yakiniku Restaurant Staff**: That customer was the last one we could let in... I'm so sorry!
- **와니부치 아카리**: Well, I guess there's nothing I can do if you ran out. Man, I was really hoping to have some yakiniku today.
- **와니부치 아카리**: I should look for something else to eat...
- **와니부치 아카리**: All right, there should be an all-you-can-eat sushi joint nearby...
- **와니부치 아카리**: Yakiniku is still my top priority...but I don't get to eat sushi so often these days.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Tonight's dinner shall be sushi!
- **와니부치 아카리**: Hello, one order of tuna sushi please!
- **Sushi Restaurant Staff**: (I-Is that Wanibuchi Akari, the competitive eating champion?!)
- **Sushi Restaurant Staff**: (This is bad! If I serve her, we'd need a whole tuna and then some to satisfy her appetite!)
- **Sushi Restaurant Staff**: I-I'm sorry! Our tables are fully booked for today. We can't accept any walk-in customers!
- **와니부치 아카리**: Really? No way...
- **와니부치 아카리**: Well, if you're booked up, I guess that's that. I'll find another place...
- **와니부치 아카리**: Ugh...I'm starving...
- **와니부치 아카리**: For some reason, every place I stopped by today was either out of food or fully booked.
- **와니부치 아카리**: I guess that makes sense... But seriously, did the all-you-can-eat restaurants band together against me or something?
- **와니부치 아카리**: Ah, Sensei. Are you here to grab dinner?
- **와니부치 아카리**: I was planning to fill up at an all-you-can-eat place, but every place I went refused to serve me...
- **와니부치 아카리**: So I still haven't gotten to sit down at a restaurant and eat dinner.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Unfortunately, that means I won't be able to have a full meal considering how light my wallet is at the moment.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Urgh... I'm so hungry, I can hardly move... I should give up on finding an all-you-can-eat place and just get whatever I can from a regular restaurant.
- **와니부치 아카리**: You mean it? Yes, of course! I'm a huge fan of conveyor sushi.
- **와니부치 아카리**: But you promised that you'll let me eat as much as I want... You really think you can handle it, Sensei?
- **와니부치 아카리**: *chuckle* A promise is a promise. I won't go easy on you. ♥
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― Mmm. Delicious!
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― I could eat this sushi
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― every day!
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― How much sushi have I eaten so far?
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― Heehee.
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― Sensei, do you remember how many pieces of sushi you've eaten in your life?
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― I think I've had about a hundred plates...
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― But I've been eating less than usual.
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― I'm not even half-full yet!
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― Are you sure you don't mind if I eat more?
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― You've been sweating. I see beads of sweat dripping down your forehead!
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― Ooh... I wonder what the sushi
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― on this golden plate would taste like!
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― Sensei, are you sure you're okay?
- **와니부치 아카리**: ― I'm gonna eat this 1,000-yen sushi if that's okay with you!
- **와니부치 아카리**: Thanks for the meal, Sensei!
- **와니부치 아카리**: I didn't expect them to run out of all the sushi they'd prepared for the day. Who would've thought?
- **와니부치 아카리**: Have you had enough, Sensei? I feel like I could still take down fifty more pieces...
- **와니부치 아카리**: Should we go to another sushi joint?
- **와니부치 아카리**: Haha! I'm just kidding. I don't wanna put your wallet in jeopardy.
- **와니부치 아카리**: I don't think it'll be enough to pay you back, but I'll cover as much as you want to eat next time.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Wait, what? You mean one meal won't cover how much you spent on me today?
- **와니부치 아카리**: Haha! You're right about that. Your portion isn't nearly as huge as mine, so paying for only one of your meals wouldn't be fair.
- **와니부치 아카리**: So then...we'll have to eat a whole bunch of meals together. ♥

5. **「와니부치 아카리 인연 스토리 5」**

- **와니부치 아카리**: Wow, Sensei! What a coincidence. Are you here to grab lunch?
- **와니부치 아카리**: Cool, same here! This place has a special "gourmand challenge" where you can eat as much mapo tofu as you want.
- **와니부치 아카리**: If you're up for it, would you like to try the mapo tofu gourmand challenge with me?
- **와니부치 아카리**: Wait, you just want a regular order of mapo tofu? But eating a mountain of mapo tofu is pure euphoria...
- **와니부치 아카리**: If you're not interested, suit yourself. Should we order now?
- **와니부치 아카리**: We'll have a regular mapo tofu and a gourmand mapo tofu, please.
- **Chinese Restaurant Staff**: Is the gourmand order of mapo tofu for the adult here?
- **와니부치 아카리**: No, it's for me. I may not look it, but I'm no slouch when it comes to gourmand challenges!
- **Chinese Restaurant Staff**: Haha. Our mapo tofu makes for a tough challenge no matter how much you normally eat...
- **Chinese Restaurant Staff**: Please give us a moment. Your food will be ready soon.
- **와니부치 아카리**: (Is it just me...or did the server have a strange expression there?)
- **Chinese Restaurant Staff**: Thanks for waiting. Here's the regular mapo tofu and gourmand mapo tofu you ordered.
- **Chinese Restaurant Staff**: If you can finish this ten-serving mapo tofu in thirty minutes, we won't charge you for it. If you finish before the time is up, you can eat even more. That's an unlimited amount!
- **Chinese Restaurant Staff**: ...Take your time and enjoy.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Whoa...I can smell that spicy aroma already. I can tell how delicious it is just by looking at it!
- **와니부치 아카리**: Time to dig in, then!
- **와니부치 아카리**: ...Hmm? How odd. This mapo tofu doesn't taste the same as I remember.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Sensei, do you mind if I have a bite of your regular mapo tofu?
- **와니부치 아카리**: Nom, nom. ...I see what's going on here. It's a bit disappointing to see that there are still some restaurants that resort to cheap tricks like this.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Compared to yours, mine has an excessive amount of spicy sauce on it. This must be their way of rigging the gourmand challenge in their favor.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Because I can eat so much of anything, people tend to forget that I'm still a member of the Gourmet Research Society. I'm a tough critic when it comes to the quality of food.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Even in a gourmand challenge, where the focus is on the amount of food served, there's a baseline quality a dish should meet. And this mapo tofu...falls below the line.
- **와니부치 아카리**: If someone plays dirty tricks with their food to make a quick buck...I'll punish them harshly.
- **와니부치 아카리**: ...Thanks for the grub! And I'll have another bowl of mapo tofu, please.
- **Chinese Restaurant Staff**: (Th-This is impossible. Not even ten minutes have passed since she started, and she's done with her third bowl already?)
- **Chinese Restaurant Staff**: (Each gourmand order has ten servings of mapo tofu!)
- **와니부치 아카리**: Hello...are you listening? One more bowl of gourmand mapo tofu, please!
- **Chinese Restaurant Staff**: Uh, um, ma'am...you already completed your challenge. You ate the full ten servings of mapo tofu... Shouldn't you consider stopping now?
- **와니부치 아카리**: Excuse me? What do you mean by that? Didn't you clearly state that the challenger can eat an unlimited amount of gourmand mapo tofu within the time limit?
- **와니부치 아카리**: For a gourmand challenge to be fair for both parties, the initial rules must be strictly followed. Wouldn't you agree?
- **와니부치 아카리**: And I didn't even complain about the extremely spicy sauce they poured all over the gourmand mapo tofu... Heeheehee.
- **Chinese Restaurant Staff**: I-I'm so sorry! I won't charge you two for the meal... Please forgive us!
- **와니부치 아카리**: No! I came here to stuff myself with mapo tofu, not to listen to your apologies.
- **와니부치 아카리**: Bring me my next bowl of mapo tofu, please!
