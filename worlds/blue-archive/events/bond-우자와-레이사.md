- **우자와 레이사**: What? What does that mean?
- **우자와 레이사**: ...Um...I dunno... I don't think people have really said anything notable to me recently...

- **우자와 레이사**: And the Vigilante Crew too... Suzumi said she would take my patrols if I needed.
- **우자와 레이사**: But... I said no, obviously. Haha.
- **우자와 레이사**: I-Is that so? Um... Oh. Am I overworking myself?
- **우자와 레이사**: Haha... Well, now that I think about it, I guess I definitely am... That's weird.
- **우자와 레이사**: But still. Would it really be okay?
- **우자와 레이사**: What if my friends think I don't wanna hang out with them? What if the Vigilante Crew thinks I'm slacking off?
- **우자와 레이사**: Haha... It's not that your advice doesn't make sense, but... Why do you always have to ask me to do such scary stuff?
- **우자와 레이사**: What if... What if it ruins everything...
- **우자와 레이사**: ...But...
- **우자와 레이사**: No. That's not right. 'Cause everything you've asked me to do before has turned out well.
- **우자와 레이사**: So...it must be the same this time! Yeah, that's right.
- **우자와 레이사**: ...!
- **우자와 레이사**: Okay!
- **우자와 레이사**: Um...Uh...Whoa...
- **우자와 레이사**: Uhh... It's just...I feel like all the tension just left my body...
