- **아사가오 하나에**: Sensei. I've been thinking about how to give painless injections, like you mentioned before.
- **아사가오 하나에**: But I think giving an injection requires a lot of study.
- **아사가오 하나에**: If I practice dozens, maybe hundreds of times, I'd be as skilled as everyone else, wouldn't I?
- **아사가오 하나에**: Heehee! I want to get better at giving quick injections.
- **아사가오 하나에**: Heehee! I want to get better at giving quick injections.
- **아사가오 하나에**: For that...
- **아사가오 하나에**: Sensei, I want you to be my practice partner for today!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Sensei! Don't run! Act like an adult!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Don't worry about it. I've replaced the medicine with vitamins.
- **아사가오 하나에**: You'll become super healthy!
- **아사가오 하나에**: All you need to do is lend me your arm.
- **아사가오 하나에**: Please, Sensei. It's for the students of Kivotos... Help me out just this once, please?
- **아사가오 하나에**: Thank you, Sensei!
- **아사가오 하나에**: I'll do my best not to hurt you!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Now, take a deep breath... And here it comes...
- **아사가오 하나에**: ...
- **아사가오 하나에**: Huh? Is that a Peroro plush flying around?
- **아사가오 하나에**: Dropped your guard!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Tsk, I failed...
- **아사가오 하나에**: What? I was trying to distract you because the nurse manual told me to make the injection while the patient is unaware...
- **아사가오 하나에**: But I failed, heh.
- **아사가오 하나에**: It's fine. Vitamins can be absorbed wherever they're injected into the body.
- **아사가오 하나에**: Now, shall we go for a second injection?

3. **「아사가오 하나에 인연 스토리 3」**

- **아사가오 하나에**: Welcome, Sensei!
- **아사가오 하나에**: I've prepared a blanket for you to sleep on, since you didn't sleep well last night!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Even if it's only thirty minutes, a little nap on a comfy blanket should clear all of your fatigue.
- **아사가오 하나에**: Napping is very healthy for you!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Naps dispose of fatigue for people that lack sleep, and they also increase concentration.
- **아사가오 하나에**: So don't worry and sleep tight!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Okay! Lie down so that I can help you sleep.
- **아사가오 하나에**: Now focus...on the carotid artery...
- **아사가오 하나에**: Sensei, don't move! I almost hit you in the face by mistake!
- **아사가오 하나에**: What? I'm just trying to knock you out so you can sleep tight.
- **아사가오 하나에**: I read in a book that if you hit a person's carotid artery hard, they'll faint right away without any pain.
- **아사가오 하나에**: I'll wake you up when lunch is over, so you can sleep peacefully!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Another method would be...
- **아사가오 하나에**: Another method would be...
- **아사가오 하나에**: Okay, why don't you use this anesthetic? It's powerful enough to knock out an elephant instantly!
- **아사가오 하나에**: The injection is gonna sting a little, but it'll be super effective! ☆
- **아사가오 하나에**: You don't want that either? Then the only thing left is herbal tea to help you sleep tight...
- **아사가오 하나에**: But there are so many better ways! Are you sure you want tea?
- **아사가오 하나에**: Then I'll get some herbal tea right away. Please give me a moment!

4. **「아사가오 하나에 인연 스토리 4」**

- **아사가오 하나에**: It's time for the radio workout!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Sensei! Hurry up and get up!
- **아사가오 하나에**: No! If you insist on sleeping every minute of your off-time, you'll become a cow.
- **아사가오 하나에**: You may feel sluggish now, but once you move your body you'll feel much better!
- **아사가오 하나에**: ...And if you keep being lazy, I'll give you a big kick in the butt!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Now let's start the radio workout! Follow the rhythm and do as I do!
- **아사가오 하나에**: First is stretching! One, two!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Not like that, Sensei! Stretch out your arms and put them next to your head!
- **아사가오 하나에**: If you do it half-heartedly, I won't give you a gold star.
- **아사가오 하나에**: Now, from the beginning! One, two!

5. **「아사가오 하나에 인연 스토리 5」**

- **아사가오 하나에**: Thank you for helping me move the medicine today, Sensei.
- **아사가오 하나에**: There was way more missing from the documents than I thought, so it would have taken me all day if I had to organize the warehouse myself.
- **아사가오 하나에**: I was able to stop and catch my breath thanks to you! Heehee.
- **아사가오 하나에**: By the way, Sensei. It's slippery because the floor was mopped earlier, so be careful...
- **아사가오 하나에**: S-Sensei! Are you okay?
- **아사가오 하나에**: But your knee is bleeding! We need to get you treated quickly...
- **아사가오 하나에**: We'll have to amputate your leg immediately to prevent infection!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Huh? What's the problem, Sensei?
- **아사가오 하나에**: If we don't amputate soon, the infection will spread and cause the wound to fester.
- **아사가오 하나에**: That's what I read in an old medical book!
- **아사가오 하나에**: I'm not sure. Maybe...500 years old?
- **아사가오 하나에**: Don't worry! According to the book, a skilled doctor can finish the amputation in under a minute...
- **아사가오 하나에**: We gotta do this right away before it gets worse!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Are you sure?
- **아사가오 하나에**: Then at least let me disinfect the wound for you first. Please sit still.
- **아사가오 하나에**: ― All right, I'm going to disinfect the wound,
- **아사가오 하나에**: ― so take off your sock and don't move.
- **아사가오 하나에**: ― If I don't disinfect your wound properly,
- **아사가오 하나에**: ― you might get an infection. This is dangerous stuff.
- **아사가오 하나에**: ― It might sting a little, but I know you can do it!
- **아사가오 하나에**: ― Here we go! Let's get this cleaned up!
- **아사가오 하나에**: ― All right, good job.
- **아사가오 하나에**: ― We're almost done.
- **아사가오 하나에**: ― How about I
- **아사가오 하나에**: ― cast a spell on you, Sensei? It'll help your wound heal quickly.
- **아사가오 하나에**: ― Pain, pain, go away!
- **아사가오 하나에**: ― There! It doesn't hurt at all, right?
- **아사가오 하나에**: Put some medicine on the wound...cover it with a bandage...
- **아사가오 하나에**: Finished! Good job, Sensei.
- **아사가오 하나에**: Be careful not to let the bandage fall off!
- **아사가오 하나에**: Yes, it's my favorite. Not only is it pretty, if you rub the top it smells sweet.
- **아사가오 하나에**: However, it can't be attached to large wounds or amputated areas because it's too small.
- **아사가오 하나에**: Because of that, cutting off your leg would have been bad.
