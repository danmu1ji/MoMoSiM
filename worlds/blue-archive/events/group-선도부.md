2. **「선도부 스토리 2화」**

- **아마우 아코**: Now it's time for us to start the regular weekly Prefect Team meeting.
- **소라사키 히나**: *sigh*
- **소라사키 히나**: Ako, I have an important meeting with the Pandemonium Society today, so let's get this over with quickly.
- **아마우 아코**: Fine. The next meeting is in fifteen minutes, so I think we can finish before then.
- **아마우 아코**: First, we need to go over the report on the unidentified explosion that occurred in Zone 97-44.
- **아마우 아코**: We've confirmed that the explosion in Zone 97-44 was caused by a dual-purpose high explosive used for blasting.
- **아마우 아코**: This is certainly the work of the Hot Springs Department... The suspects are currently on the run, and three squads are pursuing them.
- **소라사키 히나**: What's their motive?
- **아마우 아코**: Hot springs development, as always.
- **소라사키 히나**: Wait... Isn't Zone 97-44 in the middle of the freeway? They detonated a high-power explosive to develop a hot spring there?
- **아마우 아코**: That's correct. They have no guilt when it comes to the development of hot springs...
- **히노미야 치나츠**: So, I have to ask...why are you doing this?
- **Hot Springs Department Member A**: It doesn't matter where we find a hot spring, as long as we find them! Dig 'em all up!
- **Hot Springs Department Member B**: Hahaha! Burn it all! Burn all this useless trash!
- **Hot Springs Department Member C**: Bulldoze everything! It's demolition time! Demolition! Development is all that matters!
- **히노미야 치나츠**: (I have no idea what they're saying... What am I supposed to do?)
- **아마우 아코**: This is the gist of Chinatsu's report. I don't understand it, though...
- **아마우 아코**: I'm guessing they were going to build something like...a freeway rest stop.
- **소라사키 히나**: *sigh*
- **아마우 아코**: The freeway is expected to be backed up for about three months, so that means fifty-four percent of the goods transported...
- **소라사키 히나**: Right. You can leave the rest to the Pandemonium Society. What's next?
- **아마우 아코**: Okay. The next incident is the explosion in a school dining hall...
- **소라사키 히나**: The dining hall explosion... Was it the Gourmet Research Society?
- **아마우 아코**: That's correct, President.
- **소라사키 히나**: And the motive?
- **아마우 아코**: This time, it was because, well.. There was no lemon on the karaage...
- **소라사키 히나**: Lemon...?!
- **아마우 아코**: They took Fuuka, the School Lunch Club president, hostage and argued with the Prefect Team members who came to subdue them.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Okay, let me ask you a very important question, Prefect Team.
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Should lemon go on karaage or not?!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Well, everyone? What do you think?
- **시로미 이오리**: Well, in my opinion...I-I think...there should be lemon...
- **Prefect Team Member**: What? That would make the tempura all soggy! Why would you sprinkle lemon juice on it?!
- **시로미 이오리**: But the refreshing scent of lemon cuts through the greasiness of the tempura!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Haha! All right...all right. This is apparently a serious gastronomic quest! It's exactly what the Gourmet Research Society wants to see!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: What's the right thing to do here? Come on, let's figure out the answer, guys!
- **Prefect Team Members**: *murmuring* So...lemon would cut through...
- **Prefect Team Members**: *murmuring* I mean, then the batter on the tempura would...
- **Prefect Team Members**: *murmuring* Then, what about salt?
- **Prefect Team Members**: *murmuring* Why change the topic all of a sudden? I mean, I prefer soy sauce!
- **쿠로다테 하루나**: Heehee!
- **아이키요 후우카**: (Ugh... I don't care either way... Please, just get me out of here.)
- **아마우 아코**: (The heated debate escalated into a physical fight. Many of the Prefect Team members, including Iori, were hospitalized. After that, the Gourmet Research Society ran away.)
- **소라사키 히나**: *sigh*
- **소라사키 히나**: Ask the School Lunch Club president to figure out the damage and submit a report.
- **소라사키 히나**: If we lose another dining hall, we'll only have rice balls to eat for lunch!
- **아마우 아코**: Got it, President.
- **아마우 아코**: Next up... A group that forged absence letters and sold them for 2,000 yen per sheet has been arrested.
- **소라사키 히나**: Problem Solver?
- **아마우 아코**: That's correct. It was Problem Solver 68... They trespassed into the faculty office and made a copy of the absence letter stamp.
- **아마우 아코**: They shamelessly posted an ad on the academy bulletin board that read, "Want to skip school? Buy one of our absence letters and leave whenever you want!"
- **소라사키 히나**: Absence letters? But no one asks to be absent at our academy...
- **리쿠하치마 아루**: How come no one is buying any of these?!
- **아사기 무츠키**: Ugh! We're doomed, Aru!
- **이구사 하루카**: *fidgets nervously*
- **오니카타 카요코**: *sigh*
- **아마우 아코**: Yep, exactly. So they couldn't even sell one sheet. The crimes here are trespassing and forgery of official documents.
- **소라사키 히나**: *sigh*
- **아마우 아코**: Next...
- **소라사키 히나**: Wait... How many more things are there on the docket?

3. **「선도부 스토리 3화」**

- **아마우 아코**: ...And that sums up the plan for the Prefect Team's tactical training scheduled for next month.
- **하누마 마코토**: This is what you came up with after refusing the plan the Pandemonium Society offered?
- **아마우 아코**: We believe the current plan to be more than acceptable.
- **아마우 아코**: I believe the budget statement has already been submitted, so I would like to request the Pandemonium Society's approval after a review.
- **하누마 마코토**: I did receive and look over your statement, Senior Administrator.
- **아마우 아코**: Then...
- **하누마 마코토**: Your proposal has been rejected.
- **아마우 아코**: Excuse me? That statement should be flawless.
- **하누마 마코토**: Perhaps so.
- **아마우 아코**: Then why...?
- **하누마 마코토**: It's rejected, and that's final.
- **아마우 아코**: We could always adjust the statement if you feel we missed something.
- **하누마 마코토**: It's useless. Don't you understand that the budget will be rejected no matter how you adjust it?
- **아마우 아코**: For what reason? Why won't you tell me?
- **하누마 마코토**: Fine. It's because I've thought for quite some time that the Prefect Team's budget needs to be reviewed more closely.
- **하누마 마코토**: How else would I be able to investigate whether or not our funds are being embezzled?
- **아마우 아코**: (Is she seriously saying this?! No one wastes Gehenna taxpayer money more than the Pandemonium Society.)
- **아마우 아코**: I-I see. That can only mean...
- **하누마 마코토**: That's right. We've decided to conduct an audit of the Prefect Team starting immediately.
- **아마우 아코**: Wait! You can't just do that!
- **하누마 마코토**: This isn't on a whim. As I understand it, there's been some trouble within the Prefect Team lately.
- **하누마 마코토**: For example, a certain administrator aside from the head prefect arbitrarily commanding your troops...or something to that effect.
- **아마우 아코**: ...!
- **하누마 마코토**: Ahaha! The look on your face!
- **아마우 아코**: (She usually ignores anything important we have to say, but of COURSE she would have found out about this. Tch.)
- **하누마 마코토**: Lest you forget, Amau Ako, nothing escapes the Pandemonium Society!
- **아마우 아코**: (You snake!)
- **아마우 아코**: I believe there's been some sort of misunderstanding here, so I'll review the documents once more.
- **아마우 아코**: Ah, I see. There must have been a mix-up.
- **하누마 마코토**: Is that how you want to play it...?
- **하누마 마코토**: Very well! Prepare yourself for this audit, because this time there will be no escape! Hahaha!
- **아마우 아코**: Just great.
- **하누마 마코토**: There's work to be done, Iroha.
- **나츠메 이로하**: What is it, Makoto?
- **하누마 마코토**: Starting now, I am ordering an audit of the Prefect Team! Make sure you dig deep and uncover everything you can about them!
- **하누마 마코토**: Show them what happens when you mess around with our budget. Prove to all of Kivotos just how terrifying the Pandemonium Society can be, Iroha!
- **나츠메 이로하**: If I must.
- **하누마 마코토**: Wh-What was that, Iroha?
- **나츠메 이로하**: *sigh* Nothing. So I just have to conduct an audit, right?
- **하누마 마코토**: Yes! To destroy the Prefect Team!
- **나츠메 이로하**: It's just an audit. How is that supposed to crush them?
- **하누마 마코토**: Heh heh heh... This is our opportunity to shake the Prefect Team to its core and see to it that they never walk all over the Pandemonium Society again.
- **나츠메 이로하**: Whatever you say. Sure.
- **하누마 마코토**: Would you at least listen to me while I'm talking?
- **탄가 이부키**: So...yeah! We're here to conduct an audit of the Prefect Team!
- **나츠메 이로하**: *sigh* What a bother...
- **탄가 이부키**: Makoto trusted Ibuki with this job, so Ibuki is going to give it her very best!
- **시로미 이오리**: W-Wait. We're starting right now?!
- **히노미야 치나츠**: Our training is right around the corner. We'd be on an even tighter schedule if we had to conduct the audit now.
- **시로미 이오리**: What the heck is going on, Ako?! The president isn't even here!
- **아마우 아코**: Tch.
- **나츠메 이로하**: Give up. This is a direct order from the top of the Pandemonium Society.
- **아마우 아코**: This is perfectly fine. We have nothing to hide. You'll find our management to be perfect.
- **아마우 아코**: In fact, once the Pandemonium Society finishes its needless investigation, it will finally understand that the Prefect Team has nothing to hide.
- **아마우 아코**: So do your worst!
- **시로미 이오리**: Ako is taking this a little too personally...
- **히노미야 치나츠**: If anything, seeing her get this animated only makes me more nervous.
- **나츠메 이로하**: Then, let's get started.
- **나츠메 이로하**: The first place we would like to visit is...
- **나츠메 이로하**: ...Prefect Team headquarters.
