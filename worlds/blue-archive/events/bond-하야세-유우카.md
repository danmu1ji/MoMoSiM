- **하야세 유우카**: There you are, Sensei.

- **하야세 유우카**: *sigh* So you agree.
- **하야세 유우카**: Huh. I never thought of it that way.
- **하야세 유우카**: I know it's abstract, but would it kill you to be a little considerate?

- **하야세 유우카**: According to this entrepreneur, it was painted by a genius painter named Matsumoto from Wildhunt Art Academy, and it's worth at least 100 million yen.
- **하야세 유우카**: But no matter how much I stare at it, it looks like it was scribbled by a toddler.
- **하야세 유우카**: I'm aware, but I can't shake my disappointment. That's all.
- **하야세 유우카**: Taking all supplies into account, it couldn't have cost more than 10,000 yen to produce.
- **하야세 유우카**: But it becomes thousands of times more valuable just because the artist was supposedly famous...? I can't fathom why that would be.
- **하야세 유우카**: Then again, I'm only Seminar's treasurer. It's not my job to appraise art. I only wonder whether or not this art qualifies as collateral.
- **하야세 유우카**: If it can't be calculated with a formula, then how DOES one calculate it?
- **하야세 유우카**: Are you saying that when appraising art, you need to examine the artist's effort and creativity?
- **하야세 유우카**: Interesting... I suppose I'd be angry if a fully functional robot and a pile of scrap metal were considered equal in value just because they weighed the same.
- **하야세 유우카**: But that brings us back to square one. Since I can't evaluate art, I want you to appraise this painting.
- **하야세 유우카**: How much do you think it's worth?
- **하야세 유우카**: ...What? How can that be?
- **하야세 유우카**: Wildhunt Art Academy doesn't have a famous artist named Matsumoto?<br/>That would mean this artwork is just a counterfeit?
- **하야세 유우카**: Then why didn't you tell me that from the start?!

- **하야세 유우카**: Since it's handwritten, you wouldn't be able to easily convert it with a computer either.
- **하야세 유우카**: The calculation shouldn't be too difficult. All this will take is a little mental math.
- **하야세 유우카**: I should have this done in about 30 minutes or so.
- **하야세 유우카**: What? Don't believe me? Just you watch.
- **하야세 유우카**: Give me a little time.
- **하야세 유우카**: All done. I've reorganized the inventory according to customary units.
- **하야세 유우카**: I rounded some of the units up or down that couldn't be calculated exactly, so make sure to read my footnotes.
- **하야세 유우카**: Heh heh. What did I tell you?
- **하야세 유우카**: This was nothing. I wouldn't be Millennium's treasurer if I couldn't handle something as simple as that.
- **하야세 유우카**: A document? Is this a...?

- **하야세 유우카**: ...And don't look at me like that!
- **하야세 유우카**: ...
- **하야세 유우카**: *sigh* Fine. I'll just help you out a little bit.
- **하야세 유우카**: I have to stop being such a pushover...
- **하야세 유우카**: Why are you just sitting there? Those other papers have YOUR name on them, so keep your pen moving!

- **하야세 유우카**: A skilled gambler knows how to control their emotions and objectively analyze the probabilities to minimize risks.
- **하야세 유우카**: In other words, the best gamblers are also those that best understand math.
