2. **「매화원 스토리 2화」**

- **스노하라 코코나**: Hello, President Rumi. Good afternoon.
- **아케시로 루미**: Oh, Kokona. Welcome.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Don't just call me Kokona. Call me Instructor Kokona!
- **스노하라 코코나**: I'm also a full-fledged instructor just like you, okay? Seriously... Please don't treat me like a child!
- **아케시로 루미**: Haha, sorry. I'm still not used to calling you that.
- **아케시로 루미**: So, what brings "Instructor Kokona" to the office today?
- **아케시로 루미**: Are you hungry? Would you like me to cook you some fried rice?
- **스노하라 코코나**: No, I'm not here to eat. I'm here to pick up some ingredients.
- **아케시로 루미**: Ingredients?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Yes. Shun asked me to get ingredients for the Plum Blossom children's meals.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Did you not receive the notice?
- **아케시로 루미**: Ah, how could I forget? I must have been so busy with work.
- **아케시로 루미**: Wait here just a moment. I'll be right back.
- **아케시로 루미**: Let's see. I brought cabbage, bamboo shoots, carrots, onions...as well as some dried Chinese noodles and soy milk.
- **아케시로 루미**: Oh, and take some of the flat peaches near the entrance. They're sweet, so they should make for a healthy dessert.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Th-This is a lot, though. Are you sure it's okay?
- **스노하라 코코나**: I thought I was only picking up some leftover ingredients...
- **아케시로 루미**: It's more than fine. Those children are the future of Shanhaijing after all. They need to eat healthy and grow up strong!
- **아케시로 루미**: Oh, but maybe it is a little too heavy for you to carry on your own.
- **스노하라 코코나**: N-No, I can handle it. This is nothing for an instructor like me.
- **아케시로 루미**: Splendid, then.
- **아케시로 루미**: Oh, I almost forgot. Take this as well.
- **스노하라 코코나**: What's in the bag? More food?
- **아케시로 루미**: No, it's a special item that Instructor Shun specifically requested from the Black Tortoise Promenade.
- **아케시로 루미**: It's...
- **아케시로 루미**: ...a secret.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Huh? Seriously?!
- **아케시로 루미**: That's right. In fact, Shun swore me to secrecy over it.
- **아케시로 루미**: Even if you are Shun's younger sister, maintaining the confidentiality of our customers is a matter of trust with Promenade. I'm sorry, but I can't tell you.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Ugh. Now I'm even more curious.
- **스노하라 코코나**: It's something hard, and it makes a rattling sound when you shake it. Is it a toy?
- **아케시로 루미**: Well, you're not entirely wrong. I guess if we want to get technical, it could be called a grown-up's toy.
- **스노하라 코코나**: A grown-up's...toy?
- **아케시로 루미**: Yep. Inside is something that is absolutely not for children—not even ones your age.
- **아케시로 루미**: So you'd better not think of secretly checking what's inside on your way back.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Oh, come on! You and Shun aren't that much older!
- **스노하라 코코나**: I'm also a full-fledged instructor, so for me to be the only one who doesn't know is totally unfair!
- **아케시로 루미**: Some things are better left unknown, Kokona.
- **아케시로 루미**: For example, the officers of Genryumon, Shanhaijing's Student Council, will deal with all the witnesses when a secret is revealed for the sake of maintaining the school's confidentiality. Isn't that right?
- **아케시로 루미**: That's why Shanhaijing students run away if someone tries to reveal to them a secret about Genryumon—so that they don't hear something they shouldn't.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Genryumon?! Is what's inside related to them?!
- **아케시로 루미**: Hmm. I can neither confirm nor deny...but let's just assume that it is.
- **아케시로 루미**: So, Kokona, having said that, I think it would be best if you simply delivered the item. Don't you?
- **스노하라 코코나**: U-Understood!
- **스노하라 코코나**: I promise I won't look at what's inside and deliver it straight to Shun!
- **아케시로 루미**: Very good, then. I'll leave it in your capable hands.
- **아케시로 루미**: H-Haha. That was a little mean, but it looks like she bought it.
- **스노하라 코코나**: The more I think about it, the more curious I am. It's supposed to be a grown-up's toy, right?
- **스노하라 코코나**: But President Rumi said I could get in serious trouble if I look. I should just put it out of my mind and deliver it to Shun.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Oh, Sensei. Hello.
- **스노하라 코코나**: This is a bunch of foodstuff from the Black Tortoise Promenade and...
- **스노하라 코코나**: This is a bunch of foodstuff from the Black Tortoise Promenade and...
- **스노하라 코코나**: ...a, uh, grown-up toy for Shun.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Oh, no! President Rumi said I'm not supposed to tell anyone about that!
- **스노하라 코코나**: This is bad. Those terrifying people from Genryumon could appear and take us away at any moment...
- **스노하라 코코나**: Wait. You're an adult, Sensei, so maybe it's all right for you to know about things like grown-up toys.
- **스노하라 코코나**: I'm not sure either. All I know is that it's supposedly something Shun ordered from the Black Tortoise Promenade.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Judging by the outside of the bag, it's some kind of long, hard device...
- **스노하라 코코나**: President Rumi said that something seriously bad could happen if I were to peek at it, so I have to keep it a secret.
- **스노하라 코코나**: What do you think it could be?

3. **「매화원 스토리 3화」**

- **스노하라 슌**: I usually never have this much free time after work.
- **스노하라 슌**: This calls for some relaxing tea and meditation.
- **Shanhaijing Student A**: That's her, right? The Plum Blossom Garden teacher who bought...you know.
- **Shanhaijing Student B**: I always thought she was so sweet and innocent, but if that's the kind of thing she does in her spare time, who knows what she's into?
- **Shanhaijing Student A**: ...By the way, can you really get stuff like that at the Black Tortoise Promenade?
- **Shanhaijing Student B**: I guess the Promenade really does have everything.
- **스노하라 슌**: Hmm. Why does it feel like other people are staring at me?
- **스노하라 슌**: It's as though all the other students are staring at me any time I walk Shanhaijing's streets these days.
- **스노하라 슌**: I don't think I've done anything wrong. I wonder what's going on.
- **스노하라 슌**: Oh. Hello, Sensei. I haven't seen you in a while.
- **스노하라 슌**: Are you here for some tea?
- **스노하라 슌**: I'm done for the day, actually, so I've been trying to enjoy my down time.
- **스노하라 슌**: Ah. So you can sense that it's been a struggle for me.
- **스노하라 슌**: You're always so sharp, Sensei.
- **스노하라 슌**: It's nothing really—just a minor thing that's been bothering me.
- **스노하라 슌**: Thank you. I'll be honest with you, then.
- **스노하라 슌**: Thank you. I'll be honest with you, then.
- **스노하라 슌**: For some reason, I get the feeling that the other students have been looking at me strangely.
- **스노하라 슌**: Even the ones that are usually the nicest have been keeping their distance. I can't even sit here in the teahouse without sensing that people are gossiping about me.
- **스노하라 슌**: But nothing comes to mind when I try to recall what it is I've done to warrant it.
- **스노하라 슌**: Do you know something, Sensei?
- **스노하라 슌**: Do you know something, Sensei?
- **스노하라 슌**: If there's something I did wrong without realizing it, then please tell me!
- **스노하라 슌**: What?! You think the rumors have do with me purchasing an adult toy?!
- **스노하라 슌**: O-Oh... I may have gotten a little worked up there.
- **스노하라 슌**: M-More importantly, how did such an absurd rumor even start? I don't own that sort of thing!
- **스노하라 슌**: How do you know about that?
- **스노하라 슌**: What?! That's where the rumor started from?!
- **스노하라 슌**: *sigh* I did ask for that item to be delivered in secret, but I never thought it would cause such a misunderstanding...
- **스노하라 슌**: You've got the wrong idea!
- **스노하라 슌**: It's just a... It's a simple...
- **스노하라 슌**: It's a massage roller to help my skin.
- **스노하라 슌**: Yes. I've been particularly worried about my skin and getting wrinkles lately, so I ordered a massage roller from the Promenade that's said to be good for your skin.
- **스노하라 슌**: I thought it would be embarrassing if the students knew I was so concerned about wrinkles, so I wanted to keep it a secret.
- **스노하라 슌**: Never did I expect such a ridiculous rumor to spread all because I tried to hide the truth. Ugh...
- **스노하라 슌**: This is all my fault for trying to keep secrets. I'll have to go around and try to clear up the misunderstanding.
- **스노하라 슌**: Do you know how this rumor started in the first place, Sensei?
- **스노하라 슌**: I can't control everything people believe, but I'd like to know who would start such an insidious rumor!
- **스노하라 슌**: Please tell me everything you know!
- **Plum Blossom Student C**: Kokona. Is it true that Shun has a scary grown-up toy?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Don't just call me Kokona. Call me Instructor Kokona!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Well, it's a secret, but it is true that Shun purchased scary grown-up toy from the Promenade.
- **스노하라 코코나**: It's a creepy-looking toy that will make a child freeze in fear just by looking—
- **스노하라 슌**: Kokona?
- **스노하라 슌**: Didn't you tell me that you didn't see what was inside?
- **스노하라 코코나**: E-Eek!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Sh-Shun? A-Aren't you off duty today?!
- **스노하라 슌**: Yes, but then I heard my cute little sister has been spreading lies about me, so I came back right away.
- **스노하라 코코나**: L-Lies? Then the item inside the bag wasn't a grown-up's toy?
- **스노하라 슌**: Of course not! Why would I order something like that?
- **스노하라 슌**: Are you saying that you didn't look inside at all, and that's why things had gotten so out of hand?
- **스노하라 슌**: Whatever the case, it's unacceptable for a Plum Blossom Garden instructor to spread rumors. What kind of example are you setting for the young children?
- **스노하라 슌**: So I've made a decision. For spreading such rude gossip...
- **스노하라 슌**: ...you'll have to undergo my special lesson!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Anything but that! Please forgive me, Shun!
