2. **「와카사 후유 인연 스토리 2」**

- **Visitor A**: Huh... What a fascinating finale.
- **Visitor B**: It's quite profound. She has such a way of communicating so much without words.
- **Visitor C**: I don't get it. Why is this happening? Are we under attack?
- **Visitor D**: It's performance art! It's cultured!
- **와카사 후유**: Oh, look who it is! Good to see you, Sensei!
- **와카사 후유**: A mess...or a curated wreckage?
- **와카사 후유**: To put it simply...
- **와카사 후유**: Suspending objects, dancing, poetry, music...
- **와카사 후유**: ...are all art in their own ways. Yet they resist simple definition.
- **와카사 후유**: So, I was exploring the ambiguity and embodiment of the personal with the political, the body with the machine...
- **와카사 후유**: It's media art, Sensei. It's a performance, and we're all part of it.
- **와카사 후유**: Yes. I was granted an outing pass for this performance.
- **와카사 후유**: ...Of course, I'm synergizing and multi-tasking with a "side job" too.
- **와카사 후유**: In case you can't tell, making art like this is expensive. I can't be like Miyo and just type in some dark corner.
- **와카사 후유**: I need a constant revenue stream to enable me to procure electronics, tools, things of that nature...
- **와카사 후유**: The money helps, but I have other reasons.
- **와카사 후유**: Anyway, what a shame. You came right as I ended. You missed all the storytelling, the struggle!
- **와카사 후유**: I put a lot of thought into this. You wouldn't believe how much planning it takes.
- **와카사 후유**: Hm? What was this performance about?
- **와카사 후유**: Fufu, it'd be no fun to just TELL you.
- **와카사 후유**: Performance is about SHOWING, about expressing something that can't be told with mere words.
- **와카사 후유**: And if I told you what it meant, it would diminish the experience.
- **와카사 후유**: I have an idea. Try imagining it.
- **와카사 후유**: Look at the wreckage. Every item is in a position—thoughtfully placed, or a random occurrence? Imagine how the audience felt after, the sudden silence, the clearing of smoke...
- **와카사 후유**: Put all that together and imagine what my performance was about.
- **와카사 후유**: You might just discover something about yourself, Sensei!
- **와카사 후유**: Well, that said, it would have been best if you...
- **와카사 후유**: ...could have watched from the front row. Fufu.
- **와카사 후유**: Or, better yet, you can join me on stage next time.
- **와카사 후유**: Haha! I got it! I won't push.
- **와카사 후유**: I respect your preference. But I heard what I wanted...
- **와카사 후유**: ...That you'll be here next time.
- **와카사 후유**: You promised! That said, I think I better get moving.
- **와카사 후유**: I need to sweep up and pack, so you should head back to work, Sensei!
- **와카사 후유**: ...What's that? You want to help with the cleanup?
- **와카사 후유**: You REALLY don't have to... That's not what I was implying.
- **와카사 후유**: Sensei...
- **와카사 후유**: Pfft! Ha!
- **와카사 후유**: ...Very well. Let's clean up together.
- **와카사 후유**: Thank you, Sensei.

3. **「와카사 후유 인연 스토리 3」**

- **???**: Is that Sensei?
- **와카사 후유**: Fancy meeting you here!
- **와카사 후유**: How've you been?
- **와카사 후유**: I'm doing excellently, thanks to you!
- **와카사 후유**: I was just at the hardware shop. SOMEONE broke the catapult, and I figured I better get on it.
- **와카사 후유**: I've also been picking up supplies for an upcoming installation.
- **와카사 후유**: ...Fufu.
- **와카사 후유**: Anyway, what brings you out here on this fine day?
- **와카사 후유**: Based on your empty bag, I figure you're not looking for groceries.
- **와카사 후유**: What? You're buying a necessity?
- **와카사 후유**: A 7-stage fusion super alloy transforming robot...?
- **와카사 후유**: How cool.
- **와카사 후유**: I suppose you could call something like that a "necessity."
- **와카사 후유**: Really? That's a bummer!
- **와카사 후유**: As our Preferred Platinum Client, you should've messaged me that you were looking!
- **와카사 후유**: Discontinued items, sold-out trinkets, potentially lethal robots...
- **와카사 후유**: The Cartel has its ways. We can find it for you.
- **와카사 후유**: Emphasis on the "Sales." It'll cost you. Fufu.
- **와카사 후유**: Uh...
- **와카사 후유**: Hm...
- **와카사 후유**: Well... Um... Hm... That's tough.
- **와카사 후유**: I suppose you could...buy me a meal...
- **와카사 후유**: Or w-we could watch a movie together...
- **와카사 후유**: That's probably too much. It could be short, like just a little treat at a dessert cafe?
- **와카사 후유**: Wh-Wh-Wh-Wh-What?!
- **와카사 후유**: R-R-R-R-R-R-Really?!
- **와카사 후유**: I mean! Yeah! That's what I said. You owe me big time. Um...
- **와카사 후유**: S-Sounds...awesome...

4. **「와카사 후유 인연 스토리 4」**

- **와카사 후유**: Good morning, Sensei!
- **와카사 후유**: I hope you've been doing well!
- **와카사 후유**: No, actually I am. It's all on the record.
- **와카사 후유**: You can check Wildhunt's sign-out list if you like. There was a recent personnel adjustment.
- **와카사 후유**: Sure, that makes sense.
- **와카사 후유**: ...On account of I just changed it.
- **와카사 후유**: There's more to life than money, Sensei.
- **와카사 후유**: Sometimes, we trade goods for...certain privileges.
- **와카사 후유**: Clever as always, Sensei! There you are, solving mysteries again.
- **와카사 후유**: But Sensei, hear me out. Listen. Have a seat.
- **와카사 후유**: This couldn't be helped. It was unavoidable.
- **와카사 후유**: You see, this opportunity could be life-changing.
- **와카사 후유**: And it is....
- **와카사 후유**: ...An exclusive presentation where I recruit Sensei to be the Free Trade Cartel's official advisor!
- **와카사 후유**: Before you say anything, take a look at this proposal. See the graph.
- **와카사 후유**: If you were to become the Free Trade Cartel's advisor, we could diversify our business model, increase productivity...and help the students! It would boost morale!
- **와카사 후유**: Take a look at this list. Can you see what a special partner could bring through Wildhunt's gates? These are essentials that the students need...!
- **와카사 후유**: ...And, so I think we're all in agreement here. The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams.
- **와카사 후유**: So believe in the dream. What do you think, Sensei?
- **와카사 후유**: Ah...! For what...?!
- **와카사 후유**: It's my fault. I must not have explained your extensive compensation and benefits package in a comprehensive manner!
- **와카사 후유**: So, to be clear, if you were our advisor, our partner, our guide...
- **와카사 후유**: I would personally come here and fix most appliances and electronics at NO COST to you.
- **와카사 후유**: ...Fufu.
- **와카사 후유**: Declined even with these conditions...
- **와카사 후유**: Mm, let's see. Ah, right! Let's work to a "yes."
- **와카사 후유**: Like... Did you know that I'm a great cook?
- **와카사 후유**: I can pack lunch, I can roll omelets, I make good miso soup. Nothing beats a good breakfast.
- **와카사 후유**: Um, what else...
- **와카사 후유**: Y-Yeah. I-If you become our advisor...
- **와카사 후유**: Y-You would, if you want, see me every day? You know?
- **와카사 후유**: Is that not okay? C-Can I even say that is a benefit..? Uh...
- **와카사 후유**: Eh.
- **와카사 후유**: R-R-R-Really...?!
- **와카사 후유**: I-I-It can't be helped then! I tried. Rejected. Eh.
- **와카사 후유**: F-Fufu, I'll just come back with a better presentation next time.
- **와카사 후유**: Well then, bye! See ya!
- **와카사 후유**: "Tempting," they said...
- **와카사 후유**: ...Heehee.

5. **「와카사 후유 인연 스토리 5」**

- **Visitor A**: Wildhunt students always make such interesting art.
- **Visitor B**: Fufu! We're in for a treat!
- **와카사 후유**: You read right! A solo feature.
- **와카사 후유**: This is a private exhibition hall, dedicated to immersion into MY world.
- **와카사 후유**: I'd like to escort you in, if you'd please.
- **와카사 후유**: Heehee, you think?
- **와카사 후유**: Well, I didn't get into Wildhunt by sheer coincidence, Sensei!
- **와카사 후유**: So, at least for today, don't think of me as the Free Trade Cartel's exemplary Sales Manager...
- **와카사 후유**: ...but think of me as an artist. Can you do that, Sensei?
- **와카사 후유**: Um, well...
- **와카사 후유**: ...Just Fuyu, I guess?
- **와카사 후유**: Anyway! Shall we head inside?
- **와카사 후유**: Not everyone gets an artist to escort them around her exhibition, you know!
- **와카사 후유**: Let's start here, shall we?
- **와카사 후유**: If we were to think of this exhibition as a collection of poems...
- **와카사 후유**: ...this work can be considered a prologue.
- **와카사 후유**: Precisely!
- **와카사 후유**: That's why I put this at the entrance. It's a bit of a maze in here, but, regardless...
- **와카사 후유**: ...you'll see this piece first!
- **와카사 후유**: Absolutely. I think of the pieces' location, angle, luminosity, hue, brightness, even pathing structure...
- **와카사 후유**: Every element in an exhibition should carry the artist's intent.
- **와카사 후유**: Now then, shall we continue?
- **와카사 후유**: ...And so, you might be finding a recurring motif...
- **와카사 후유**: ...Oh, are you getting hungry, Sensei?
- **와카사 후유**: Fufu, I suppose we must stop.
- **와카사 후유**: As an artist, I can't ignore my VIP's ravenous and loud call of hunger.
- **와카사 후유**: Shh.
- **와카사 후유**: Just follow me, okay? Sen...sei?
- **와카사 후유**: ― We are behind the screens.
- **와카사 후유**: ― Thus, behind the artwork...
- **와카사 후유**: ― A place where only the artist may enter.
- **와카사 후유**: ― The only way to enter this place...
- **와카사 후유**: ― is to get permission from me, the artist.
- **와카사 후유**: ― So, right now, in this place...
- **와카사 후유**: ― There's only you and...me...
- **와카사 후유**: ― Uh no no no no!
- **와카사 후유**: ― Th-That's not what I meant...!
- **와카사 후유**: ― Anyway!
- **와카사 후유**: ― I packed lunch...
- **와카사 후유**: ― Um, if you're fine with it...
- **와카사 후유**: ― ...Would you like to have some?
