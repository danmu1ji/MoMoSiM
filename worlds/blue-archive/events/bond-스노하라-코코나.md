2. **「스노하라 코코나 인연 스토리 2」**

- **Plum Blossom Student A**: Kokona! I want this!
- **Plum Blossom Student B**: What's the snack today, Kokona?
- **스노하라 코코나**: You can't just call me Kokona. It's Instructor Kokona!
- **Plum Blossom Student C**: Kokona, I don't understaaaand! Will you do it for me?
- **Plum Blossom Student D**: Hey, Kokona! Can Riemann zeta function roots really determine the oscillation of a prime-counting function?
- **스노하라 코코나**: How many times do I have to tell you? It's Instructor Kokona!
- **스노하라 코코나**: And what kind of question is that?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Oh, Sensei!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Oh, Sensei!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Say hello to Sensei, everyone!
- **Plum Blossom Students**: Hello, Sensei!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Well, I see they address you as Sensei just fine.
- **스노하라 코코나**: So why do they insist on calling me Kokona?
- **스노하라 코코나**: It's not right!
- **스노하라 코코나**: I'm an accomplished instructor. They should treat me as such!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Ah... I'm sorry. Complaining like this isn't professional.
- **스노하라 코코나**: What?! No! I have to be perfect!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Ugh... I know I shouldn't be so sensitive.
- **스노하라 코코나**: A proper lady is never ruffled by trivial things.
- **Plum Blossom Student A**: Can a proper lady be a picky eater, Kokona?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Y-yes. Even a proper lady has one or two dislikes.
- **Plum Blossom Student B**: Does that mean we don't have to eat stuff we don't like?
- **스노하라 코코나**: No! You still need a balanced diet!
- **Plum Blossom Student C**: But you eat whatever you want! Why can't we?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Because I'm the instructor!
- **Plum Blossom Student D**: Doesn't that mean you should set a good example for us, Kokona?
- **스노하라 코코나**: I—yes, but—
- **스노하라 코코나**: Sensei, will you say something please?!
- **Plum Blossom Student A**: Hey, Sensei said "Instructor Kokona"!
- **Plum Blossom Student A**: Hey, Sensei said "Instructor Kokona"!
- **Plum Blossom Student B**: I thought it's just Kokona...
- **Plum Blossom Student C**: No, Sensei called her that. So it must be right!
- **Plum Blossom Student D**: Well, it's a little weird, but... I guess we should call her Instructor Kokona then.
- **스노하라 코코나**: I must have told you my name a hundred times. Now you remember?
- **Plum Blossom Students**: Instructor Kokona?
- **Plum Blossom Students**: Instructor Kokona!
- **스노하라 코코나**: I-I am... But it's not a big deal.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Ahem! Now you all know.
- **스노하라 코코나**: I'm the Plum Blossom Garden instructor.
- **Plum Blossom Student A**: Instructor Kokona! I need to go potty!
- **Plum Blossom Student B**: Instructor Kokona! I miss my family. When are they coming?
- **스노하라 코코나**: W-Wait!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Kids... Always too excited to listen...
- **스노하라 코코나**: ...What is it, Sensei?
- **스노하라 코코나**: I know it seems like I have a lot on my shoulders.
- **스노하라 코코나**: But it's my job as a Shanhaijing instructor to guide each child to become a great student.
- **스노하라 코코나**: And I love doing that. So I can't really complain.
- **Plum Blossom Student C**: Instructor Kokona! I want a snack!
- **Plum Blossom Student D**: But not YOUR snacks. Those suck.
- **스노하라 코코나**: It's not time for snacks yet.
- **스노하라 코코나**: And just you wait. One day, I'll make something even better than Shun.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Then I'll make everyone eat their words!
- **Plum Blossom Students**: Wow! Kokona is mad!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Why are you calling me Kokona again?!

3. **「스노하라 코코나 인연 스토리 3」**

- **스노하라 코코나**: Oh! Hello, Sensei!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Are you going back to Schale? I was just on my way there.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Shun said she had business near Schale.
- **스노하라 코코나**: I was going to meet her there.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Now that you're here, you have a rare opportunity...
- **스노하라 코코나**: You can escort me like a proper lady!
- **스노하라 코코나**: I SAID it's Instructor Kokona!
- **스노하라 코코나**: I SAID it's Instructor Kokona!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Ugh... I guess I'll let it go, since it's just the two of us.
- **스노하라 코코나**: You bought another cup of coffee?
- **스노하라 코코나**: So many people drink coffee... Is it really that good?
- **스노하라 코코나**: And yet you drink several cups a day.
- **스노하라 코코나**: And yet you drink several cups a day.
- **스노하라 코코나**: There must be a good reason. Is it because it makes you seem sophisticated?
- **스노하라 코코나**: ...S-Sure. Why not? I am a proper lady after all.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Stop smiling at me like that... It's making me nervous.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Okay... Coffee. Yeah. I can drink coffee. No problem!
- **스노하라 코코나**: So this is coffee...
- **스노하라 코코나**: It's darker than the night sky.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Oh, and hot.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Ouch! Hot enough to burn me.
- **스노하라 코코나**: But the smell! It's so... Wait. Is it burnt?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Are you sure I can drink this?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Wh-What?! No, I still want it.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Wh-What?! No, I still want it.
- **스노하라 코코나**: I just—Umm...
- **스노하라 코코나**: I'm just enjoying its scent, okay?!
- **스노하라 코코나**: So? I didn't say I don't like the smell.
- **스노하라 코코나**: I'm an instructor. I can handle a cup of coffee.
- **스노하라 코코나**: I-I don't know. Something!
- **스노하라 코코나**: It does for me, anyway...
- **스노하라 코코나**: So. I'll just go ahead and drink it now...
- **스노하라 코코나**: One. Two. Three!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Ugh. Bleh.
- **스노하라 코코나**: It's bitter and...
- **스노하라 코코나**: Disgusting! It's the worst thing I've ever tasted!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Why do you drink this crap?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Do you LIKE torturing yourself? Are you a masochist?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Is EVERYONE who drinks this a masochist?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Ugh! Coffee is gross. I'd rather have ice cream.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Ugh! Coffee is gross. I'd rather have ice cream.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Hey! You like ice cream, don't you?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Does that mean eating ice cream is sophisticated too?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Please say yes, Sensei. I couldn't bear to give it up.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Huh? There's coffee-flavored ice cream?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Ewwww! I'll never understand THAT.

4. **「스노하라 코코나 인연 스토리 4」**

- **스노하라 코코나**: Oh! Hello, Sensei.
- **스노하라 코코나**: I'm glad I ran into you. I've got a lot on my mind right now.
- **스노하라 코코나**: I've been thinking about ways to get the students to call me Instructor Kokona.
- **스노하라 코코나**: It helped a little when you talked to them, but they went back to just Kokona pretty quickly...
- **스노하라 코코나**: But I hate carrots!
- **스노하라 코코나**: But I hate carrots!
- **스노하라 코코나**: I don't care what you say.
- **스노하라 코코나**: I've never liked them, and I never will!
- **스노하라 코코나**: It's not like I chose to hate them.
- **스노하라 코코나**: I just... How can people stand eating them?
- **스노하라 코코나**: They're bitter. They smell weird. They're hard as rocks...
- **스노하라 코코나**: They're not fit for human consumption.
- **스노하라 코코나**: What even possessed people to eat them in the first place?
- **스노하라 코코나**: It's a clear regression of culture.
- **스노하라 코코나**: We must move forward. We must eat better than that. BE better than that.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Carrots are so...unsophisticated!
- **스노하라 코코나**: And don't even get me started on bell peppers!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Not only are they bitter and smelly—just like carrots—but they're spicy too!
- **스노하라 코코나**: What?!
- **스노하라 코코나**: What?!
- **스노하라 코코나**: What kind of choice is that?!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Carrots or bell peppers? That's it?
- **스노하라 코코나**: I'd rather starve than eat either!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Carrots. And bell peppers. Of course I can.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Ugh. I suppose if I HAD to choose...
- **스노하라 코코나**: Then...
- **스노하라 코코나**: ...I'd pick carrots. Grudgingly.
- **스노하라 코코나**: At least they aren't spicy like bell peppers...
- **스노하라 코코나**: Bleh. Why are you so determined to know?
- **스노하라 코코나**: You want me to eat carrots on purpose? And at a restaurant no less...
- **스노하라 코코나**: You want me to eat carrots on purpose? And at a restaurant no less...
- **스노하라 코코나**: Look, I really appreciate the offer and the fact that you're willing to pay.
- **스노하라 코코나**: But could we go somewhere else? There are nicer restaurants than that one.
- **스노하라 코코나**: And I'd rather have something more sophisticated.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Why go to a carrot restaurant when we could have something better?
- **스노하라 코코나**: I-I know I did, but...
- **스노하라 코코나**: That was only because you made me choose between carrots and bell peppers.
- **스노하라 코코나**: What—Why would you take me to a bell pepper restaurant instead?!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Sensei, you're so mean!
- **스노하라 코코나**: I thought you of all people would understand!

5. **「스노하라 코코나 인연 스토리 5」**

- **스노하라 코코나**: I can't believe you actually brought me here.
- **스노하라 코코나**: Or that a carrot restaurant is even a thing...
- **스노하라 코코나**: Whoa. They REALLY like carrots, don't they?
- **스노하라 코코나**: They have carrots on the curtains, the stairs...
- **스노하라 코코나**: Even the sign is a giant carrot!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Sensei, we don't have to do this. It's not too late to leave.
- **스노하라 코코나**: In fact, you're so determined that I'm starting to worry you've been brainwashed by Big Carrot!
- **스노하라 코코나**: That's it! You've been brainwashed! Don't worry, Sensei! I'll save you!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Wait! Wait!!! Please don't make me go in there! You can't do this to a proper lady!
- **스노하라 코코나**: I know you're trying to be nice, but really—
- **스노하라 코코나**: Waaaahhhh!
- **스노하라 코코나**: Ugh. Literally EVERY dish has carrots in it.
- **Waiter**: What would you like?
- **스노하라 코코나**: Umm...
- **스노하라 코코나**: You should order, Sensei.
- **스노하라 코코나**: You brought me here, which means you know what's good.
- **스노하라 코코나**: So you should order for both of us.

- **스노하라 코코나**: There's no way this is a carrot!
- **스노하라 코코나**: So they steamed them to maintain the sweetness and fried them in butter... Huh!
- **스노하라 코코나**: You want me to try this sauce? Okay...
- **스노하라 코코나**: Oooh! It's so tasty!
- **스노하라 코코나**: ― But...
- **스노하라 코코나**: ― Just so you know...I still hate carrots.
