# 사쿠라이 미요 인연 스토리

- 등장: 사쿠라이 미요, 선생(샬레)

1. **「사쿠라이 미요 인연 스토리 1」**

본문 대사는 수록되어 있지 않다.

2. **「사쿠라이 미요 인연 스토리 2」**

- **사쿠라이 미요**: Hello...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Sakurai Miyo, reporting in for today's Student Rep duties.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: P-Please don't say that. I should be the one thanking you, Sensei.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: You've given me a rare opportunity to go outside...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Ugh... It's been so long since I've experienced something new... Transcendent freedom...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Yes. They...are even more restrictive with Literature majors like me. We have mandatory cloister times.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: After all, you don't need fresh air or sun to write.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: And a lot of great classics were actually written in confinement...and from behind bars...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...You know, maybe I should confess all the Cartel's smuggling activities and get sent to prison. It could do wonders for my writing and focus.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Wh-What are you...?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: S-S-Sensei...?! What are you doing...?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: This is a...lounge?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: And you want me to relax and write? Ooh, such nice stationery!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: And I can help myself to the drink fridge? And you have snacks?!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: B-But I came here as Student Rep today... If I don't help you, then I'm—
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...My only job as a writer is to write?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Well... You're not wrong...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...Thank you, Sensei.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Hmmmm...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: So... How should I reveal that the protagonist has an incurable, beautiful, tragic, crippling disease? How best to twist the knife?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: And the in-laws mobilize a tank unit and launch an offensive to keep the lovers apart... Heh. Oh, this is good.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Okay, maybe a little...homage...from another work?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: "RAAAH! If heaven made you, why did it also make me?!"
- **사쿠라이 미요**: BRR-at-tat-tat! Pew! "Do not give up yet! We still have 200 anti-tank shells!"
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Ha, yes. That's it! It's perfect! I'm a genius!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: And next up is the steamy—
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...Sensei? Do you...require something?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...Were you listening?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: You heard me talking to myself, right? You. Heard. Everything.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: From where?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: That IS everything!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Aaaaaaaaaah...!

3. **「사쿠라이 미요 인연 스토리 3」**

- **???**: Hm? Sensei?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Good morning...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: W-Well...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: I received an offer from a publisher here. I was granted a few hours' leave because this is considered a work meeting.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Yes. You may remember that I was working on a story while visiting you at Schale.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: And...I finished! A romance imprint is interested in my work.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: D-Don't flatter me! Please, I haven't even seen the contract yet.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Oh, when's the meeting?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: It's in 20 minutes, 2 o'clock. And it's in this building. I've been trying not to pace too much. I don't want to look desperate, right?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...What? You'll join me?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: I mean... That's asking so much of you. You undoubtedly had a long day, and...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: That's true. I've never signed a legitimate contract before. And you know so much about them.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: I-In that case...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: I'd be happy to have you, Sensei.
- **Publishing Staff**: Ah! There she is! Our special writer with her ribbons and that eye for romance!
- **Publishing Staff**: But...Schale's Sensei? What are you doing here?
- **Publishing Staff**: Ah, right. So invested! How wonderful. Look at the two of us, encouraging the youthful voices of tomorrow!
- **Publishing Staff**: Since you're all busy, let's get to it. Now, Miyo, you're a smart girl, so you probably know all of this.
- **Publishing Staff**: The industry isn't what it used to be! There's so much overhead. Writing may be easy for you, but publishing is expensive!
- **Publishing Staff**: Sure, there's paper, but also editors, designs, localization, and MARKETING! You wouldn't believe how much marketing costs.
- **Publishing Staff**: And, in the unfortunately possible event that the printed books don't sell, there are storage fees. Climate-controlled warehouses! Armed guards!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Y-Yes. I am familiar with these concepts.
- **Publishing Staff**: Excellent. We're crystal clear. I just wanted to make sure we were on the same page!
- **Publishing Staff**: Business! You're speaking my language, Sensei. Now, let's get to the proposal.
- **Publishing Staff**: Miyo... How would you like to be an equal partner?! We're giving you the opportunity to invest in your OWN book's publication.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...?
- **Publishing Staff**: That's right. We'll publish, and YOU get the privilege of paying for printing, editing, designing, and marketing!
- **Publishing Staff**: Meanwhile, we'll handle the rest!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Wow...!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Oh, I bet that's not all, right?
- **Publishing Staff**: Huh?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: After I pay all that "investment" money and get published, naturally, I'll have to buy 500 or so copies, right? So we can game the Kronos Bestseller List?
- **Publishing Staff**: Oh...! You're familiar!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: And I bet I'd also have to enroll in some of your paid writing courses... You know, to make sure my next book with you is even better? Is that right?
- **Publishing Staff**: Absolutely! You're already up to speed. We're investing in your future, Miyo!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Yes, of course! Wow!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...You scammers need a new playbook.
- **Publishing Staff**: Th-The contract...!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Let's go, Sensei. I have no more business here.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: *sigh*
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Please don't look at me like that, Sensei. It's fine. I'm tougher than I look.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Hm? How did I spot the scam?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: It's simple. I just imagined what I would do if I wanted to...exploit some naive girl with a dream, then worked from there. It's how I come up with our smuggling ideas, too.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: So...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...*sob*
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Why is this what I'm good at? Being scum?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: I can't get anyone to care about my work, my writing, the thing I focus on the most.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...My only talent is crime.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: I'll never be a writer. No one wants my silly romances. I'm just doomed to be...THIS, aren't I?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: How comforting. You have such a way with words.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Avoiding the wrong path...is just as important as finding the right path.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Yeah. I could've been that girl who falls for scams. So cute. So naive. But I'm me.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: It's a benefit. I'm blessed to be so...clever.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...Although I still hate what I am.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: S-Shut up!

4. **「사쿠라이 미요 인연 스토리 4」**

- **사쿠라이 미요**: What's that supposed to mean?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Excuse me. I am exactly on time. Just who do you think I am?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: That is...fundamentally incorrect!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Although...I agree...that I understand certain tropes.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: I have a real job. There's a certain "something" I need to procure for a client. It's at this vintage shop.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ....And, frankly, I could use another pair of eyes. It'd be nice to wrap this up quickly.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...Just one more job. Then I'm done. I'll go legit, I swear.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Ugh...!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Spare me the sarcasm!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Ah... Permission... About that.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Well, the thing is...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...I didn't.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Wildhunt has a Free Outing Club. Unofficial, of course.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: They helped me sneak out.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Well... It's an art school. Isn't that obvious?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: So, artists can be quirky. Everyone is a little...insane, to be honest.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Well, time to get to work!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Look at this typewriter, Sensei! It's the MilkySweet 42 model! Check out that chassis!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Yes. But it's unfortunate, really. It never caught on in the market.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: See the letters? This keyboard has a custom layout. They claimed it was uniquely ergonomic!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: The only problem was that...well, people didn't want to relearn how to type. It's hard being an innovator.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: The model was retired after the initial batch of a million typewriters.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Well, that's why I have one in my collection. So I should be grateful for their ambition!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Huh? Oh! Look, Sensei! A Demington model, and in great condition. Ah! And the matching fountain pen!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: One second, Sensei!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: That's it! Let's go, Sensei.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Ah, it's the...certain "something" I promised the Free Outing Club.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Yes. This is in exchange for them sneaking me out and...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...Ah.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: Uh, ah, u-um...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ...I'm not allowed?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: And am I allowed to sneak out if it means seeing you?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: L-Last time you said that you wanted to see me too, didn't you?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: You said you truly, deeply desired to see me. In MomoTalk!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: D-Do you understand it now? You're the bad one, Sensei. You're the corrupting influence.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: So just stop talking about it. Let's go!
- **사쿠라이 미요**: We still have many typewriters...and so little time.

- **사쿠라이 미요**: ― Could it be that you're not feeling well...?
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ― Hmm, a nightmare.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ― I'm sure this is your punishment.
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ― You've been coming to Wildhunt so often...
- **사쿠라이 미요**: ― Yet you've never once called me.
