# 키누이 레나 인연 스토리

2. **「키누이 레나 인연 스토리 2」**

- **Wildhunt Student A**: Mamma mia! What is this atrocity?!
- **Wildhunt Student A**: Ketchup...on spaghetti?!
- **Wildhunt Student B**: Hm? It's good. Kind of sweet. You want to try?
- **Wildhunt Student A**: Silence! This is an insult to everything Wildhunt stands for! Our storied cuisine...you desecrated it!
- **Wildhunt Student A**: Wait... WHAT is next to it? Some kind of...risotto?
- **Wildhunt Student B**: Oh, this? It's just rice. It's amazing with the pasta sauce. And by that, I mean...ketchup.
- **Wildhunt Student A**: A-Aaaaaah...! To arms, you demon!
- **Wildhunt Student B**: Ahahaha! If you're looking for trouble, you've found it!
- **Prefectural Council Officer**: Both of you... Drop your weapons! No squabbling in front of a guest!
- **Wildhunt Student A**: But she...! She puts ketchup on her spaghetti!
- **Wildhunt Student B**: Well, SHE waters down her espresso. With ICE!
- **Prefectural Council Officer**: The horror...!! Arrest them both!!
- **Prefectural Council Officer**: You are correct. Once more, justice is served!
- **Prefectural Council Officer**: Sensei, are you well? You weren't...hurt, were you?
- **Prefectural Council Officer**: I'm glad to hear that.
- **Prefectural Council Officer**: My apologies for that truly, truly upsetting scene. I will ensure that this never happens again.
- **Prefectural Council Officer**: All right, you lookie-loos! Break it up! Unless you want a lecture on the updated list of restricted items!
- **키누이 레나**: ...?
- **키누이 레나**: Sensei? What are you doing here?
- **키누이 레나**: You never showed up, so I went looking for you!
- **키누이 레나**: Seriously, you can't even check your phone?
- **키누이 레나**: ...Hm? What's wrong? Why are you so sweaty? And hunched over?
- **키누이 레나**: ...
- **키누이 레나**: You're hiding something, aren't you?
- **키누이 레나**: What's behind your back? Show me!
- **키누이 레나**: What do you mean you "can't"?
- **키누이 레나**: If you're trying to pull a prank, then give it up. It's not funny.
- **키누이 레나**: ...
- **키누이 레나**: ...?!
- **키누이 레나**: Wh-Wh-What the?! Right THERE?!
- **키누이 레나**: Y-You could've just said it was t-torn!!
- **키누이 레나**: A-Ahh...!
- **키누이 레나**: Look, just stay right there! Don't...move. I'll be right back.
- **키누이 레나**: Phew. I think we're safe.
- **키누이 레나**: Stop squirming around in that weird pose!
- **키누이 레나**: Sensei, you shouldn't thank me yet.
- **키누이 레나**: Because we're about to get serious.
- **키누이 레나**: *sigh*
- **키누이 레나**: Take it off.
- **키누이 레나**: You're going to make me say it again?
- **키누이 레나**: Take. It. Off! So I can mend it! Obviously!
- **키누이 레나**: So...they ripped when you were trying to flip a table back up?
- **키누이 레나**: You didn't have to do that. The Prefectural Council would've taken care of it. Those girls love a good project...
- **키누이 레나**: Seriously, you're too nice.
- **키누이 레나**: Wh-What?! What are you talking about?!
- **키누이 레나**: ...Ah.
- **키누이 레나**: I-I JUST MESSED UP BECAUSE OF YOU!
- **키누이 레나**: Seriously, needlessly creating more work...! You're just like my roommates!
- **키누이 레나**: If you keep teasing me, I'll sew your pockets closed.
- **키누이 레나**: So you better stop talking and behave.
- **키누이 레나**: I'm dangerous with a needle and thread. *sigh*
- **키누이 레나**: Now, wait quietly so I can focus!
- **키누이 레나**: Here. It's done. Try them on.
- **키누이 레나**: Are you done?
- **키누이 레나**: You sure? I'm not going to see anything weird if I open my eyes, right?
- **키누이 레나**: O-Okay. I'll trust you.
- **키누이 레나**: I'm opening them.
- **키누이 레나**: ...
- **키누이 레나**: So what do you think? How does it feel?
- **키누이 레나**: YOU IDIOT! Why'd you zoom around like that?

3. **「키누이 레나 인연 스토리 3」**

- **키누이 레나**: O-Over here! Sensei!
- **키누이 레나**: Well, the thing is...
- **키누이 레나**: Starting now...we're going to...
- **키누이 레나**: ...investigate a school. An abandoned one. And people say it has ghosts.
- **키누이 레나**: I-I have to!
- **키누이 레나**: I told my seniors, the Occult Society, that I would prove that ghosts don't exist.
- **키누이 레나**: I told them not to.
- **키누이 레나**: I'm sure they would pull some stupid prank again and try to scare me.
- **키누이 레나**: Also... I'm the only one with an approved outing pass.
- **키누이 레나**: But well... It's so late. And it's a little sketchy, you know. So I figured I shouldn't go alone...
- **키누이 레나**: Well, I doubt anything will happen, but still. No ghosts will show up.
- **키누이 레나**: If you want to pay me back for your..."pants incident," this would help.
- **키누이 레나**: *sigh* How did it come to this...?
- **키누이 레나**: Ah. There's the bus.
- **키누이 레나**: The abandoned school is down this street.
- **키누이 레나**: It could be a coincidence, but the rumors say that its halls are plagued with mysterious screaming...and have been since the day the school opened.
- **키누이 레나**: ...They say you can still hear the screams, even though there are no more students.
- **키누이 레나**: A few kids have even passed out while exploring it. That's why no one goes over here anymore.
- **키누이 레나**: N-Naturally! You should always research where you're going.
- **키누이 레나**: We should get to it. Come on, let's prove that there's no such thing as ghosts!
- **키누이 레나**: This school is said to house the "Seven Mysteries."
- **키누이 레나**: First is loud knocking on the window in the middle of the night.
- **키누이 레나**: Second: there's a thirteenth stair that...also doesn't exist.
- **키누이 레나**: Third is an eerie shadow wandering around the faculty hallway.
- **키누이 레나**: A bloodstained cherry blossom pressed against a classroom window—that's the fourth mystery.
- **키누이 레나**: Fifth: the broken classroom speakers play the sound of a ringing bell.
- **키누이 레나**: Sixth, which we've already talked about—is general suspicious screaming originating from the basement's mechanical room.
- **키누이 레나**: And the seventh mystery...
- **키누이 레나**: It's so mysterious that no one knows what it is.
- **키누이 레나**: They say that if you so much as LEARN what this mystery is, you'll be inflicted with a terrible curse.
- **키누이 레나**: R-Right? Just...everyday ghost stories, right?
- **키누이 레나**: And that's why I'll investigate all these silly rumors, take some photos, and show the others.
- **키누이 레나**: And, because NOTHING will happen, I'll finally prove that ghosts don't—
- **키누이 레나**: ...?!
- **키누이 레나**: Wh-Wh-What the...?
- **키누이 레나**: Eek...!
- **키누이 레나**: B-But we're the only ones here!
- **키누이 레나**: ...!!
- **키누이 레나**: I-I wasn't worried!
- **키누이 레나**: F-Fine! L-Let's go see!
- **Crow**: Caw! Caaw...!
- **키누이 레나**: Huh, what? It was just a crow?
- **키누이 레나**: Why was it pecking at the window? It's not like there's any food in here.
- **키누이 레나**: Shyaah! No way!
- **키누이 레나**: Let's take a photo. I need evidence that all this uproar was just a bored crow looking for snacks.
- **키누이 레나**: Here. How's that look? I didn't mess it up or anything, right?
- **키누이 레나**: One down. Let's keep it up and wrap up the alleged Seven Mysteries!
- **키누이 레나**: Phew, that's what I thought. I knew there were no such thing as ghosts.
- **키누이 레나**: They were all misunderstandings originating from stray animals or run-down facilities.
- **키누이 레나**: Good! Since we have everything, we should head back. I can't wait to show my seniors!
- **키누이 레나**: Th-That's...
- **키누이 레나**: ...n-not because I'm scared, or anything!
- **키누이 레나**: I-It's to stop us from falling! It's dark in here! Someone could trip.

4. **「키누이 레나 인연 스토리 4」**

- **키누이 레나**: Ah, Sensei.
- **키누이 레나**: You really came.
- **키누이 레나**: How are you feeling?
- **키누이 레나**: You know... Are you sleeping all right? Any sudden shivers or...night terrors?
- **키누이 레나**: It's nothing... I was just curious.
- **키누이 레나**: My roommates keep yapping about the "consequences" of seeing a ghost, signs of being haunted, things like that.
- **키누이 레나**: *sigh* How annoying.
- **키누이 레나**: Here.
- **키누이 레나**: It's an omamori I made. You know, a protective amulet.
- **키누이 레나**: I was just going to buy one, but my seniors insisted that I make one myself.
- **키누이 레나**: I don't know if it'll protect you from anything, since I made it, and I don't believe in the occult, OBVIOUSLY, but it can't hurt, right?
- **키누이 레나**: Take it.
- **키누이 레나**: Of course. It was easy.
- **키누이 레나**: Are you buying? Ha...
- **키누이 레나**: Yeah! There's actually a snack I've been eager to try.
- **키누이 레나**: ...H-How did you know?
- **키누이 레나**: I don't crave it all the time, you know?
- **키누이 레나**: I-I just happened to be in the mood today!
- **키누이 레나**: Really, Sensei? You also enjoy chocolate-banana crepes?
- **키누이 레나**: U-Ugh... I-I knew it. You're teasing me...
- **키누이 레나**: Well, whatever. Let's go.
- **키누이 레나**: Gross... That's a big one.
- **키누이 레나**: Wh-What was that?!
- **Cafe Manager**: Sensei... It says "PULL."
- **Cafe Manager**: I'm sorry, but you need to cover the replacement cost.
- **키누이 레나**: What's going on...? Why are all these bad things happening?
- **키누이 레나**: Sensei. Hand me the omamori I gave you earlier. Something's off.
- **키누이 레나**: A-Ahh...!!!
- **키누이 레나**: I-I made them in bulk... A-And I think I gave you an empty one! It was a mistake!
- **키누이 레나**: I knew something was weird. No wonder you've been cursed with bad luck!
- **키누이 레나**: I-It's just in case! Only a precaution.
- **키누이 레나**: I'm so sorry I gave you an empty one.
- **키누이 레나**: Hold on for a minute. I'll get you a new one. A good one!
- **키누이 레나**: Here...! This one's fine! Take it!
- **키누이 레나**: Just in case you didn't know...you can't peek inside.
- **키누이 레나**: They say that an omamori loses its power once you open it.
- **키누이 레나**: G-Got it? No matter what, you CANNOT OPEN IT.
- **키누이 레나**: Wonderful. Thank you, Sensei!

5. **「키누이 레나 인연 스토리 5」**

- **키누이 레나**: Ah. Welcome, Sensei.
- **키누이 레나**: The others are out right now.
- **키누이 레나**: They're probably doing something silly, like trying to find occult symbols in a mural, or something.
- **키누이 레나**: And pass up an opportunity to get some work done in peace?
- **키누이 레나**: That was my plan...until I saw that the fabric shop was closed while they scrub off the unauthorized "artwork."
- **키누이 레나**: Seriously, can anyone be normal at Wildhunt? I can't believe I have to survive three more years here.
- **키누이 레나**: I told you not to, but you bought it anyway.
- **키누이 레나**: Well, thank you...
- **키누이 레나**: Hmm! Th-This is good! Where'd you get it from?
- **키누이 레나**: What? R-Really?!
- **키누이 레나**: Yeah... I-It's good...
- **키누이 레나**: ...
- **키누이 레나**: It's...definitely...good.
- **키누이 레나**: Shyaah! I don't know! Why do you have to spring all these questions? What is this, the inquisition?! Idiot Sensei!
- **키누이 레나**: Mmm. Now that I've had some sugar, I can start working.
- **키누이 레나**: A ribbon hair clip.
- **키누이 레나**: Well... It's not like I LOVE them...
- **키누이 레나**: I had one at first, then I added another for balance...and then I ended up covered in them before I even realized it.
- **키누이 레나**: Now, I feel sort of "off" if I'm not wearing a bunch of them when I go out.
- **키누이 레나**: Shyaah! Shut up! It's not like that!
- **키누이 레나**: This is all about the fashion! Fashion!
- **키누이 레나**: Since they're so small, I tend to lose a few if I'm out all day. So...I make a few spares, just to be safe.
- **키누이 레나**: I'm going to start working now, so you should start on your work, too.
- **키누이 레나**: Didn't you come here today because you had some business to take care of?
- **키누이 레나**: ...Huh?
- **키누이 레나**: Th-This is mundane work... So it might be pretty boring to watch.
- **키누이 레나**: But if you still want to observe...
- **키누이 레나**: F-Fine. Feel free to.
- **키누이 레나**: Just don't bother me, okay?
- **키누이 레나**: ― You know, I've always been good with my hands, ever since I was a kid.
- **키누이 레나**: ― I would make things like ribbons and give them to my friends.
- **키누이 레나**: ― I was happy to see that everyone liked them.
- **키누이 레나**: ― ...And that's how I learned
- **키누이 레나**: ― that even something small
- **키누이 레나**: ― can make someone feel happy.
- **키누이 레나**: ― That's why I started dreaming of becoming a fashion designer.
- **키누이 레나**: ― Because it makes me happy to see others enjoy
- **키누이 레나**: ― the clothes and accessories I design.
- **키누이 레나**: ― Sensei, you know how that feels, right?
- **키누이 레나**: ― I think that's also why you keep looking out for me.
- **키누이 레나**: ― Huh...?
- **키누이 레나**: ― How was today...?
- **키누이 레나**: ― Shyaah! D-Don't make me say it! Idiot Sensei!
- **키누이 레나**: Ah... Look at the time.
- **키누이 레나**: Sensei. Don't you have to get going? It's getting dark.
- **키누이 레나**: Yeah. Good luck with your work...and everything.
- **키누이 레나**: Ah, right. Before you leave...
- **키누이 레나**: Here. Come closer.
- **키누이 레나**: Heehee. This ribbon looks good on you!
- **키누이 레나**: Consider it a gift! For keeping me company!
- **키누이 레나**: H-Huh? You don't have to go that far...
- **키누이 레나**: ...!
- **키누이 레나**: Wh-Whatever! Keep it on, if you must!
