# 스미 세리나 인연 스토리

- 등장: 스미 세리나, 선생(샬레)

1. **「스미 세리나 인연 스토리 1」**

2. **「스미 세리나 인연 스토리 2」**

- **스미 세리나**: Hey, Sensei! Fancy meeting you here.
- **스미 세리나**: Are you out for a walk or something? I'm glad to see you're taking care of yourself.
- **스미 세리나**: Oh, what am I doing here? Uh, I was just...
- **Children**: Thank you!
- **Children**: Thank you!
- **스미 세리나**: Heehee. It's nothing. I was just helping these kids out because they looked like they could use it.
- **Children**: She got my balloon out of the tree!
- **Children**: She patched me up when I got hurt playing ball! She's the best!
- **스미 세리나**: H-Haha. This is a little embarrassing. Really, it's nothing...
- **스미 세리나**: I don't think I'm any kinder than the average person. Besides, I'm a Remedial Knight, after all.
- **스미 세리나**: Helping people and putting smiles back on their faces...
- **스미 세리나**: ...is all the satisfaction I need.
- **스미 세리나**: I don't understand it entirely myself. Maybe it's just part of who I am.
- **스미 세리나**: But enough about me. You've seemed fatigued lately. Are you okay?
- **스미 세리나**: You're fine, huh? Nope—I don't think so!
- **스미 세리나**: You can't hide it from me. You work too many hours, you skip meals to eat snacks...
- **스미 세리나**: ...and you're always putting yourself in dangerous situations to help others. You're liable to collapse if you keep this up!
- **스미 세리나**: I always carry first aid supplies, but that doesn't mean I want to use them.
- **스미 세리나**: ...Uh.
- **스미 세리나**: Sorry. I shouldn't be so nosy. I don't mean to bother you, Sensei.
- **스미 세리나**: In fact, I haven't done a very good job of taking care of you, Sensei. I'm ashamed of myself.
- **스미 세리나**: Maybe I tend to neglect you because you're older...
- **스미 세리나**: I'm so disappointed in myself.
- **스미 세리나**: But I told you that being an adult doesn't mean I can neglect you!
- **스미 세리나**: You say you understand, but you still...
- **스미 세리나**: It's not much, but would you like to have this? It's apple juice.
- **스미 세리나**: What's that? This is exactly what you were craving?!
- **스미 세리나**: Well, then. I'm happy to be of service.
- **스미 세리나**: ...Heehee.
- **스미 세리나**: Good luck with the rest of your work! Just don't overdo it!
- **스미 세리나**: Call me if you ever need help, Sensei!
- **스미 세리나**: I'll come right away!
- **스미 세리나**: ...Heehee.
- **스미 세리나**: That's a promise.
- **스미 세리나**: Then I'll keep that in mind! You know where to find me!

3. **「스미 세리나 인연 스토리 3」**

- **스미 세리나**: Hmm hmm. Hmm hmmm!
- **스미 세리나**: Hmm... Huh?
- **스미 세리나**: Oh. You're awake, Sensei?
- **스미 세리나**: You've been working yourself into the ground with overtime. I can't imagine how busy your schedule is.
- **스미 세리나**: You must be exhausted. That's why Serina is here to help you!
- **스미 세리나**: I made a promise, remember? I said I'd be there for you whenever you need me.
- **스미 세리나**: I wish you would have called me before things got to this point, though...
- **스미 세리나**: Not so fast! The towel I placed on your forehead will slip off.
- **스미 세리나**: Also note that I put special pain relief patches on your neck, arms, and waist.
- **스미 세리나**: I'll take them off for you when it's time. You have nothing to worry about, Sensei!
- **스미 세리나**: That's right. I was surprised you slept so soundly. You didn't wake up at all.
- **스미 세리나**: That's right. I was surprised you slept so soundly. You didn't wake up at all.
- **스미 세리나**: I was worried the towel and the patches might cause some discomfort when I put them on you.
- **스미 세리나**: You must have really been exhausted to not have noticed.
- **스미 세리나**: I told you not to overdo it, Sensei! I need you to listen to me!
- **스미 세리나**: Patients who ignore their nurse's advice are the worst, you know. N-Not that I'm technically a nurse, but still...
- **스미 세리나**: Anyway, I'm going to give you an earful if you ignore me again!
- **스미 세리나**: Pardon? My touch is so soft you think you're still asleep...?
- **스미 세리나**: C-Cut it out! Flattery won't get you anywhere, Sensei!
- **스미 세리나**: Sensei...said that I was gentle...
- **스미 세리나**: Huh? N-Nothing—I didn't say anything! I was talking to myself, that's all...
- **스미 세리나**: Wait a minute, Sensei! You're trying to change the subject when I was scolding you!
- **스미 세리나**: You really are the epitome of a bad patient. I'm going to prick you with the biggest needle I have if you end up getting sick.
- **스미 세리나**: ...It's nothing. I was just thinking to myself.
- **스미 세리나**: I know there's nothing you can do about all of your responsibilities.
- **스미 세리나**: I can't imagine you like working overtime until you drop...
- **스미 세리나**: ...but you can't just turn your back on all the people who need you.
- **스미 세리나**: So all I can do is take care of you the best that I can.
- **스미 세리나**: ...
- **스미 세리나**: On the bright side, it was amusing to see how you look when you're sleeping.
- **스미 세리나**: So it's not all bad. In fact, I feel a little privileged. Heehee.
- **스미 세리나**: Oh, it's almost time to take your patches off. I'll take care of it, so sit tight.
- **스미 세리나**: Um... I...
- **스미 세리나**: They were easy to put on, but...it's embarrassing now that you're awake.
- **스미 세리나**: It feels really awkward to be treating your body, so...
- **스미 세리나**: ...if you don't mind, could you pass back out for me, Sensei?
- **스미 세리나**: You don't think you can fall asleep that quickly? Don't worry. I have my ways.
- **스미 세리나**: They say patting a person like this helps them fall asleep...
- **스미 세리나**: You were the one who called me gentle, Sensei, so don't try to resist.
- **스미 세리나**: Now, please relax your body and get in a comfortable position!
- **스미 세리나**: You are feeling veeery sleepy. Close those eyes...
- **스미 세리나**: Just relax and let my hands work their magic. First your shoulders and then your neck...

4. **「스미 세리나 인연 스토리 4」**

- **Building Broadcast**: U-Uh. D-Due to the sudden power outage, we're asking for the understanding of all residents...
- **Building Broadcast**: U-Uh. D-Due to the sudden power outage, we're asking for the understanding of all residents...
- **???**: ...Sensei?
- **???**: Sensei! Are you hurt?!
- **???**: Get up, Sensei! Are you okay?
- **스미 세리나**: It's me, Serina! Can you hear me?
- **스미 세리나**: Slow down. I need to examine you for injuries!
- **스미 세리나**: ...
- **스미 세리나**: Thank goodness. Your knee and arm suffered scratches, but you should be fine.
- **스미 세리나**: You'll be okay once the shock wears off.
- **스미 세리나**: All right. Let's get those scrapes patched up.
- **스미 세리나**: I appreciate it, but I'd rather you stop putting yourself in situations where you need my help.
- **스미 세리나**: It doesn't make me happy to hear you say that while you're hurt.
- **스미 세리나**: Seriously, this is why I told you to contact me if you need help!
- **스미 세리나**: Power outage? What do you mean that there's nothing I can do about a power outage?
- **스미 세리나**: I'll always be there to help you—whether you're fatigued, injured, or in a blackout, it's my job to be there!
- **스미 세리나**: I'm begging you to call me when you're in trouble. I don't want this to keep happening…
- **스미 세리나**: This proves my point. Had I arrived a little later, this would have left a scar. Is that what you want?
- **스미 세리나**: You're lucky I came as soon as I could.
- **스미 세리나**: ...What?
- **스미 세리나**: How did I know...to look for you?
- **스미 세리나**: ...
- **스미 세리나**: I-I don't know. It's a coincidence. I was...just passing by?
- **스미 세리나**: Wh-Who cares how it happened?! You're changing topics again! Bad patient! Bad!
- **스미 세리나**: What? I'm the one changing the topic? N-No I'm not! I would never!
- **스미 세리나**: A-Anyway! Please call me before anything happens next time!
- **스미 세리나**: Okay? Promise me. You. Phone. Me. Call!
- **스미 세리나**: I-I'm done treating you, so...bye!
