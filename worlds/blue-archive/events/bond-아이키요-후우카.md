2. **「아이키요 후우카 인연 스토리 2」**

- **아이키요 후우카**: Oh, Sensei. Y-You came after all.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I-I was planning to go grocery shopping today.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I-I was planning to go grocery shopping today.
- **아이키요 후우카**: ...and I think there's going to be too much for me to carry.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I hate having to rely on a teacher for help with something so minor, but...
- **아이키요 후우카**: Heehee. That's the Sensei I know.
- **아이키요 후우카**: All right, let's get to shopping.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I have a long grocery list to get through! ♪
- **아이키요 후우카**: Wow. Supermarkets always manage to get me excited.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I suppose I do...
- **아이키요 후우카**: There's something about a cool breeze coming down aisles of fresh food that makes my heart race.
- **아이키요 후우카**: All the colorful fruits and vegetables on display are so captivating.
- **아이키요 후우카**: It's like a treasure trove for chefs!
- **아이키요 후우카**: That's how everyone feels, isn't it?
- **아이키요 후우카**: Oh! Look, Sensei. This cucumber still has its spines intact.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I can tell this one is good because it's firm and the tip is still moist.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Oh! This watermelon is a winner too! Just listen to the sound it makes when I tap it!
- **아이키요 후우카**: S-Sorry. I guess I got a little carried away. Ahaha...
- **아이키요 후우카**: I give it my all when cooking meals for the academy...
- **아이키요 후우카**: ...but it's challenging to keep the innate flavors of the ingredients when cooking such large portions.
- **아이키요 후우카**: That's why so many students complain about the food having no taste.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Stop. You're going to embarrass me...
- **아이키요 후우카**: M-More importantly! That was the last of the ingredients I need.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Not at all, Sensei.
- **아이키요 후우카**: The real shopping has only just begun.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Excuse me! I'd like to buy something here.
- **Shop Owner**: How can I help you, ma'am?
- **아이키요 후우카**: I appreciate how fresh your bell peppers and cucumbers are. Good size, good quality...
- **아이키요 후우카**: These are the kinds of ingredients that I know I can make something delicious with.
- **아이키요 후우카**: They're so impressive that I keep coming back for more.
- **Shop Owner**: Oh, so you're a regular. Would you mind if I asked where this is going...?
- **아이키요 후우카**: You see, I'd like to start buying in bulk if possible.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I need enough to feed every student at Gehenna and then some.
- **Shop Owner**: Ah, I see! Did you have a price in mind?
- **아이키요 후우카**: Since I'm trying to buy this many items at once...
- **아이키요 후우카**: How does this price seem to you?
- **Shop Owner**: Uh-huh. I'll have to consult with my manager about that price...
- **아이키요 후우카**: I'll tell you what. At this price, I'll also buy a month's worth of food for our dining hall.
- **Shop Owner**: *gasp*...!
- **Shop Owner**: V-Very well. We have a deal.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Glad to hear it. Pleasure doing business with you.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I'm the president of Gehenna's School Lunch Club. That was nothing!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Well, let's head back, Sensei!

- **아이키요 후우카**: I just so happen...to have heard some gossip about you recently.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I heard Schale keeps you busy all day and all night.
- **아이키요 후우카**: So much so that you rarely eat your meals on time. Is that true?
- **아이키요 후우카**: Bad, Sensei! Bad!
- **아이키요 후우카**: You're already an adult.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Someone your age needs to start taking their health more seriously!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Enough! No excuses!
- **아이키요 후우카**: The situation has gone critical. This calls for the attention of the School Lunch Club president!
- **아이키요 후우카**: In fact, it's the reason we went grocery shopping yesterday.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I was, but getting food for the dining hall was my second objective.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Improving your diet is a top priority for the School Lunch Club!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Rice is the cornerstone to a nutritious meal, and nutrition is the key to good health!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Which brings us to today's meal.
- **아이키요 후우카**: An 18-course meal, Gehenna School Lunch Club style!
- **아이키요 후우카**: I hope you're ready, Sensei!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Let's start with the appetizers.
- **아이키요 후우카**: This is made by mashing red beans and tofu into a white soy paste.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Heeheehee! Next up is...
- **아이키요 후우카**: Heeheehee! Next up is...
- **아이키요 후우카**: ...wonton soup with clam stock.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Slurp it down while it's hot!
- **아이키요 후우카**: You haven't tasted anything yet. Next up is...
- **아이키요 후우카**: You haven't tasted anything yet. Next up is...
- **아이키요 후우카**: This one's up next!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Now try this one!
- **아이키요 후우카**: This dish too, Sensei!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Sensei? You don't look so good...
- **아이키요 후우카**: Huh? Your stomach?
- **아이키요 후우카**: What about your stomach?
- **아이키요 후우카**: S-Sensei?!

4. **「아이키요 후우카 인연 스토리 4」**

- **아이키요 후우카**: I'm sorry for going overboard last time.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Heehee. That's just like you, Sensei.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Say what you will, but I've decided to make it up to you by treating you to something special.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Would you mind going grocery shopping with me again, Sensei?
- **아이키요 후우카**: I realized something after our little incident.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Making you a ton of food isn't the way to go about it.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Instead, I want to make you something special.
- **아이키요 후우카**: That's why I need to go shopping for ingredients.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Heehee. You'll just have to wait and see!
- **Fishmonger**: Welcome! We have plenty of fresh fish available today!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Wow. The mackerel looks so fresh, and the saurel looks good too!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Sensei! Sensei! Take a look at the sea urchin eggs!
- **아이키요 후우카**: They're so fresh that we could sprinkle them on rice and chow down!
- **아이키요 후우카**: S-Sorry. Got carried away.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I get so worked up looking at fresh ingredients.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Thinking about what dishes they're going to go in puts my heart all aflutter...
- **아이키요 후우카**: Haha... I must sound so weird, don't I?
- **아이키요 후우카**: Heehee...
- **아이키요 후우카**: Oooh, this Atka mackerel would be great for grilling.
- **Fishmonger**: My, my. That's a discerning eye you have, young lady!
- **Fishmonger**: Hahaha! That kind of thing is an important trait in a wife!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Me? A w-wife?
- **Fishmonger**: Aahaha! For now! Details, you two. Details.
- **Fishmonger**: It's nice having someone you can rely on, isn't it?
- **아이키요 후우카**: S-Sensei! You're embarrassing me!
- **Fishmonger**: So, young lady, what brings you here today?
- **아이키요 후우카**: Right. Do you have any eel?
- **Fishmonger**: Eel? You bet we do!
- **Fishmonger**: We carry the best eel in all of Kivotos!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Because eel is said to be great for your stamina.
- **아이키요 후우카**: You have to work so many late nights, Sensei...
- **아이키요 후우카**: ...so I thought eel would be the perfect thing to use in a dish made just for you!
- **아이키요 후우카**: This is the best way I can give you my support.
- **Fishmonger**: What a thoughtful young lady. You found yourself a keeper.
- **Fishmonger**: Here you are! The best eel we have on hand!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Thank you so much!
- **아이키요 후우카**: But I hope you don't think I'm going to overpay just because you handed me your best eel.
- **Fishmonger**: My, you know how to hold your own, don't you?
- **아이키요 후우카**: Would you look at this eel? I bet it's going to be delicious!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Heehee. How should I cook it?
- **아이키요 후우카**: I could go with a classic and serve it over rice. Or simply grilling it with salt would be good too!
- **Fishmonger**: Look at how fired up she's getting. Something tells me you won't be sleeping tonight! Hahaha!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Oh, no no no. Sensei needs a good night's rest too!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Your health comes first AND second. Am I understood, Sensei?
- **Fishmonger**: Ha! Can you hear how devoted she is? Now that's true love!
- **아이키요 후우카**: L-Love? Oh, dear...

5. **「아이키요 후우카 인연 스토리 5」**

- **아이키요 후우카**: S-Sensei? Are you all right?

- **아이키요 후우카**: I found you collapsed on the ground at the meeting place.

- **아이키요 후우카**: This...is all my fault.
- **아이키요 후우카**: But it is. I should have taken your schedule with Schale into consideration.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Had I not asked you to meet me for dinner, you wouldn't have fainted.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I'm so sorry...
- **아이키요 후우카**: It's not...?
- **아이키요 후우카**: Every time...
- **아이키요 후우카**: You always say things like that to me.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I've been jumping to conclusions while I waited for you to wake up.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I got so caught up in serving you what I wanted you to eat that I failed to consider what you wanted.
- **아이키요 후우카**: So this time, I want you to choose.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Sensei, is there something you would like to eat?
- **아이키요 후우카**: All you want is a home-cooked meal? Are you sure you don't want anything fancier?
- **아이키요 후우카**: If that's what you want, Sensei. It's up to you.
- **아이키요 후우카**: All right, I'll get right on it!
- **아이키요 후우카**: ― I knew it. You've been eating frozen food again!
- **아이키요 후우카**: ― They're not good for you. You have to think about your health.
- **아이키요 후우카**: ― Making something better won't even take long, so just wait a moment.
- **아이키요 후우카**: ― Seriously, Sensei. Your worst habit is the way you ignore your health.
- **아이키요 후우카**: ― Huh? You want me to cook for you every day?
- **아이키요 후우카**: ― Oh, hahaha. I... If you really want me to, I don't mind...
- **아이키요 후우카**: Let me know if you're ever craving another home-cooked meal.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Feel free to ask as much as you want... I'll always be happy to cook for you.

- **아이키요 후우카**: The students filed another complaint today!
- **아이키요 후우카**: It was only a sip! I had to taste test the recipe!
- **아이키요 후우카**: That's not the problem here, Sensei!
- **아이키요 후우카**: I pour my soul into making delicious meals for everyone!
- **아이키요 후우카**: But cooking large quantities is always harder. The food doesn't turn out as well, and then the students complain.
- **아이키요 후우카**: And, no. Not everyone recognizes my hard work like you do...
- **아이키요 후우카**: I know it's unrealistic to expect everyone to. I accept that.
- **아이키요 후우카**: BUT STILL!
- **아이키요 후우카**: They're the ones who mixed up karashi mustard instead of the regular kind just because they're both yellow!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Where do they get off complaining that my food is too spicy?!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Try cooking for yourself if you're just going to complain everyday!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Don't they realize how thankless being the School Lunch Club president is?!
- **아이키요 후우카**: Wait. I've got an idea.
- **아이키요 후우카**: ...What if I replaced mustard with karashi in every recipe? That would teach them a lesson about complaining.
- **아이키요 후우카**: It would give a whole new meaning to student lunches from Gehenna...
- **아이키요 후우카**: I'm only kidding. It would be blasphemous for the School Lunch Club to sabotage food like that.
- **아이키요 후우카**: In the end, School Lunch Club drama isn't something to lose sleep over.
- **아이키요 후우카**: I choose to cherish the things that make me happy—like when someone tells me they like my food. And best of all...
- **아이키요 후우카**: ...you came to help me, and being with you is enough to warm my heart.
- **아이키요 후우카**: Heehee. Heeheehee...
- **아이키요 후우카**: ...
- **아이키요 후우카**: Zzz...
