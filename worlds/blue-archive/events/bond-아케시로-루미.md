# 아케시로 루미 인연 스토리

- 등장: 아케시로 루미, 선생(샬레)

1. **「아케시로 루미 인연 스토리 1」**

2. **「아케시로 루미 인연 스토리 2」**

- **아케시로 루미**: ...You see, it would be...less than ideal...for Shanhaijing if Black Tortoise Promenade made an official request to Schale.
- **아케시로 루미**: That's why I asked for a student counseling session. So I could ask for your 'personal' help.
- **아케시로 루미**: Sorry, Sensei. I do hope I haven't made trouble for you...
- **아케시로 루미**: We've had some issues getting ingredients for our special sauce. I looked into it myself, and...
- **아케시로 루미**: Apparently the Black Market is involved.
- **아케시로 루미**: If this was purely a Shanhaijing problem, we could have handled it ourselves.
- **아케시로 루미**: But since we're dealing with the Black Market, it's a bit out of our jurisdiction... That's why we need Schale's help.
- **아케시로 루미**: Thank you, Sensei. Now, I know I'm already asking a lot, but I'd appreciate it if you could hurry.
- **아케시로 루미**: We only have about a month's supply of sauce left...
- **아케시로 루미**: But... Sensei, you have a lot on your plate already, don't you?
- **아케시로 루미**: It was hard enough to fit this student counseling session in your schedule.
- **아케시로 루미**: Is there some way I can help you?
- **아케시로 루미**: In other words, you don't think there's anything I can do...
- **아케시로 루미**: Well, that's terribly disappointing. There must be something I could do...
- **아케시로 루미**: Oh, have you had lunch? Surely you have by now, right?
- **아케시로 루미**: ...You're joking.
- **아케시로 루미**: Shush!
- **아케시로 루미**: For your information! Skipping a meal! Is something I will ABSOLUTELY not allow!
- **아케시로 루미**: That settles it. If you won't feed yourself, Sensei, then I will!
- **아케시로 루미**: Let's see here... Onions! They're sprouting and a tad squishy, but I should be able to carve out some edible pieces.
- **아케시로 루미**: These potatoes... How on earth did you get them to green when they were stored in the fridge...?
- **아케시로 루미**: Oh, and this bread... It's not moldy, but it's hard as a rock. Hmm... Maybe if a crush it to add a breaded texture...?
- **아케시로 루미**: And in the freezer, you've got...freezer-burned meat. You could knock someone out with this.
- **아케시로 루미**: Even your sake and soy sauce are about to expire. I've actually never seen sake expire before...
- **아케시로 루미**: Good. I've got everything I need.
- **아케시로 루미**: Don't underestimate the Black Tortoise Promenade, Sensei. Your pantry here is giving me some...challenges...but that just makes it more interesting.
- **아케시로 루미**: Please give me 30 minutes. I'll have a delicious lunch ready for you soon.
- **아케시로 루미**: What do you think? I emptied out your fridge while I was at it.
- **아케시로 루미**: I made plenty, so help yourself.
- **아케시로 루미**: It wasn't easy, but seeing you enjoy a good meal is worth it.
- **아케시로 루미**: When I cook, I try to make as much food as I can, with as much extraordinary flavor as I can, with ordinary ingredients.
- **아케시로 루미**: With each new recipe, I ask myself, "Will this be a hit? Will this grow the business?"
- **아케시로 루미**: It's all a matter of identifying problems in each dish, in terms of flavor, impression, and even cost.
- **아케시로 루미**: That's what it takes to run a restaurant, so that's how Black Tortoise Promenade operates.
- **아케시로 루미**: What are you talking about? Do you know how much we all depend on you? You've got to eat your fill!
- **아케시로 루미**: Look at you! You're all skin and bones!
- **아케시로 루미**: On that note, here, have some more! You skipped lunch, right? I bet you also skipped breakfast!
- **아케시로 루미**: I'd better make you dinner, too, just to be safe!

3. **「아케시로 루미 인연 스토리 3」**

- **아케시로 루미**: Welcome, Sensei. I have been expecting you.
- **아케시로 루미**: Are you excited? Hahaha... Don't be. This is actually kind of embarrassing for me...
- **아케시로 루미**: About that...
- **아케시로 루미**: Normally, I would get help from the others, of course!
- **아케시로 루미**: But everyone's too busy to help me right now. That's why you're here!
- **아케시로 루미**: They're all hard at work studying, but the club is important, too. At least, that's how I feel.
- **아케시로 루미**: Here's the first dish. Please try it and tell me what you think.
- **아케시로 루미**: Don't hold back. I want your honest opinion.
- **아케시로 루미**: Yes, yes, I know it looks a little green. That's...um...that's how it's supposed to look!
- **아케시로 루미**: Okay. That's all right. I'll make a note about this dish...
- **아케시로 루미**: No need to get upset. There are more dishes for you to try.
- **아케시로 루미**: I see... Just as I expected. Okay, the next dish is the last one.
- **아케시로 루미**: Hahaha! Oh, Sensei. You can't back out now.
- **아케시로 루미**: You've already come so far, and you want to give up halfway?
- **아케시로 루미**: In for a penny, in for a pound, as they say! It's only fair...right?
- **아케시로 루미**: Life is full of trials like this one. You're an adult, Sensei. I'm sure you've had your fair share.
- **아케시로 루미**: Finally...success.
- **아케시로 루미**: Bitterness isn't an easy flavor to use well.
- **아케시로 루미**: You could cut it with salt, but then you risk the whole dish becoming too salty.
- **아케시로 루미**: So I tried to balance it with fat and spiciness...
- **아케시로 루미**: The idea is to coat the tongue to cover the bitterness and give your taste buds a little thrill. And it gives the dish a pleasant savor!
- **아케시로 루미**: Then the spiciness helps cut through the grease quickly, giving you a sense of relief.
- **아케시로 루미**: Based on your reaction, I'd say it worked.
- **아케시로 루미**: It is. Maybe it's a bit stubborn of me...
- **아케시로 루미**: Flavor is such a delicate thing. You never know what combinations are going to work out.
- **아케시로 루미**: So that means lots of trial and error.
- **아케시로 루미**: Hahaha... Well, it's not like I want to torture my fellow students.
- **아케시로 루미**: You don't want to be a taste-tester, either? Hm... Well, I still say it all worked out in the end!
- **아케시로 루미**: I've got 5 more recipes in mind, based on that last success.
- **아케시로 루미**: I'm sure they'll all be just as delicious as the dish you just tried.
- **아케시로 루미**: So sit tight, because the next course is coming right up!
- **아케시로 루미**: You don't want any...?
- **아케시로 루미**: But I'm inspired NOW. If I try making these dishes later, it won't be the same...
- **아케시로 루미**: Sensei? Where are you going?

4. **「아케시로 루미 인연 스토리 4」**

- **아케시로 루미**: Thank you for coming, Sensei.
- **아케시로 루미**: What will it be?
- **아케시로 루미**: Ah, so that's what you'd like, hm?
- **아케시로 루미**: As it happens, I HIGHLY recommend the chef's special.
- **아케시로 루미**: Mind you, today's special is something I just made up.
- **아케시로 루미**: Hahaha! Don't worry about it.
- **???**: The hell? Who made this?!
- **아케시로 루미**: Ah... Please excuse me. I need to take care of this.
- **Guest**: Look here! I found a hair in my food!
- **Guest**: Everyone says the Black Tortoise Promenade is so great. Well, how do you explain THIS?!
- **Black Tortoise Promenade Member A**: There's no way... We double- and triple-check every dish before it goes out!
- **Guest**: You sure about that? Then where'd this hair come from, huh?!
- **Guest**: Or are you all too incompetent to catch something this obvious?!
- **Black Tortoise Promenade Member A**: ...Okay, that's enough out of you.
- **Guest**: How dare you! I'm paying customer, aren't I?!
- **아케시로 루미**: One moment, please.
- **아케시로 루미**: As the person in charge of the kitchen, I'd like to apologize.
- **아케시로 루미**: This meal is on the house. I'll personally see to it that this doesn't happen again.
- **아케시로 루미**: I am so, so terribly sorry for any distress this has caused.
- **Guest**: Hmph! At least you're taking this seriously. I'll let it slide this time.
- **Black Tortoise Promenade Member A**: You shouldn't have apologized like that, President! There's no way we made such a stupid mistake!
- **아케시로 루미**: *sigh* You're right, of course. I know how hard you guys work better than anyone.
- **아케시로 루미**: I don't like it, either, but we can't allow any disruption to the meals of our other customers. No matter what.
- **아케시로 루미**: Keep that in mind next time.
- **Black Tortoise Promenade Member A**: Yes, President...
- **Guest**: Heh heh heh...
- **Guest**: That's another free lunch in the bag.
- **Guest**: It works every time.
- **Guest**: Huh? What do you want?
- **Guest**: H-How did you know about that...?
- **Guest**: I mean... You can't prove nothing!
- **Guest**: Wait... You're that person with Schale! That Sensei!
- **Guest**: Ugh!
- **Guest**: S-So what if you heard of me? There's nothing you can do about it!
- **???**: Maybe not alone. But they're not alone...
- **Guest**: Wh-Who's there?
- **아케시로 루미**: Ah, dear customer. It is so good to see you again.
- **Guest**: You're...!
- **Guest**: What're you going to do? I left your little restaurant, didn't I?
- **아케시로 루미**: True, you did. Your whole song and dance about a hair in your food didn't add up, but...
- **아케시로 루미**: As the owner of a restaurant, there's not much I can do but let it go.
- **아케시로 루미**: But I'm not at the restaurant now, am I?
- **Guest**: Wh-What are you doing? Get away from me!
- **아케시로 루미**: Your request is refused!
- **아케시로 루미**: You've made three grave mistakes.
- **아케시로 루미**: First, you disrespected Sensei in front of me, a student.
- **아케시로 루미**: Second, you insulted my juniors in front of me, their upperclassman.
- **아케시로 루미**: Third, you insulted my restaurant in front of me, a chef.
- **Guest**: Aaaah!
- **아케시로 루미**: Whew... Thanks, Sensei. I feel a lot better.
- **아케시로 루미**: If you hadn't tailed that dine-and-dasher, I would've had to let them go...
- **아케시로 루미**: Hahaha... Nothing gets past you, Sensei.
- **아케시로 루미**: As a token of appreciation...
- **아케시로 루미**: I know that.
- **아케시로 루미**: That's why I'm having your meal delivered to Schale. Since you had to waste your lunch break, I threw in a free dessert.
- **아케시로 루미**: What can I say? It's a habit of mine.
- **아케시로 루미**: I am a chef, after all!

5. **「아케시로 루미 인연 스토리 5」**

- **아케시로 루미**: You're here early, Sensei.
- **아케시로 루미**: Good timing, actually. We were just about to start steaming some dumplings.
- **아케시로 루미**: I'm so glad you came. I was worried this would be too early for you...
- **아케시로 루미**: Well then... Shall we begin?
- **아케시로 루미**: Everyone! Light your steamers!
- **아케시로 루미**: It's intense, isn't it?
- **아케시로 루미**: The kitchen practically turns into a sauna whenever we're steaming dumplings.
- **아케시로 루미**: Believe me, I wish I could step out...
- **아케시로 루미**: I need to keep an eye on the steamers.
- **아케시로 루미**: Too much heat and the dumplings rupture, but not enough, and they don't cook all the way through.
- **아케시로 루미**: You can watch from the door, Sensei. But I'm staying right here.
- **아케시로 루미**: What is it, Sensei? Is something wrong?
- **아케시로 루미**: ― Aah... Look at that. It got a little...see-through.
- **아케시로 루미**: ― Oh, don't worry about it. A little steam won't kill me.
- **아케시로 루미**: ― ...If I knew this was going to happen,
- **아케시로 루미**: ― I would have worn cuter underwear.
- **아케시로 루미**: ― It's a real shame, you know?
- **아케시로 루미**: ― Haha! You don't have to look away.
- **아케시로 루미**: ― I don't mind at all.
- **아케시로 루미**: ― I didn't know there was such a side to you, Sensei...
- **아케시로 루미**: ― Haha! I like it!
- **아케시로 루미**: ― It's not like this happens very often, so...
- **아케시로 루미**: ― shall I make you even more uncomfortable...?
- **아케시로 루미**: Whew, all right. The dumplings are done.
- **아케시로 루미**: I made plenty, so please have a bite.
- **아케시로 루미**: Hmm? What's the matter?
- **아케시로 루미**: You see, I tried something called a "sardine pie" at Trinity the other day, and it was pretty good.
- **아케시로 루미**: So I thought I'd try making a sardine dumpling!
- **아케시로 루미**: It turns out that the exact recipe for the pie is some kind of Trinity General School secret...
- **아케시로 루미**: I made this just for you, so please, enjoy!
- **아케시로 루미**: Is there a problem? The recipe supposedly had something to do with Trinity's history and traditions...
- **아케시로 루미**: Come on, just one bite! Do you know how hard it is to get a dumpling into that shape?
