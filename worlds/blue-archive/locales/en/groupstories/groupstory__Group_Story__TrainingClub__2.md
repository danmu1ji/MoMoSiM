- Rei (Athletics Training Club): It's only natural for a batter to yearn for a home run.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): I've been thinking about them ever since I decided to join the baseball club.

- Rei (Athletics Training Club): I could never forget the beautiful arc the ball drew over the fences.
