> Ruins Near Former Institute No. 227
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): It's so cold today!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): And all this snow... I could very well turn into a snowman and die out here!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Heehee. Good thing I've got a bundle of dry branches and lots of fruit!
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): You look happy, Nodoka. Things went well?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Shigure!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): It's true! Just look at my haul!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): With all this snow, I thought I wouldn't find a single dry branch. But it turns out I'll have a nice cozy feast next to the fire.
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): I'm glad to hear it.
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): You need to take better care of yourself, though. Your face is red.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): R-Really? I guess I got a little carried away searching for supplies.
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): Let's get you out of the cold. I'll make us a fire.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): *sigh* The fire's warming me up inside and out.
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): Hot cocoa, Nodoka?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Thank you, Shigure. I don't know what I'd do without you.
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): Me? You're the one who gathered wood for us. I'm grateful for you.
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): And that adorable smile gives me the energy to keep going.
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): We might be stuck here, but I'm happy to be stuck here with you, Nodoka.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Sh-Shigure, I'm blushing...
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): Nodoka...
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Shigure....
- ???: Stop, stop! Stop right now!
> Former Institute No. 227
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Wh-What in the heck is this, Meru?!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Why are Shigure and I protagonists in a romance novel?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): What do you mean? I'm writing a story about the life of Former Institute
No. 227 students, just as you asked.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): I may have spiced up some of the details a little, but it's based on your lives!
Heehee.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): A little?! Other than the fact that we're cold and hungry, everything is made up! I want to describe our lives here in these horrible conditions, not write an original story!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): It didn't matter how loud we were. The Secretariat never responded... So we thought maybe if other students petitioned...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Hmm. No one cares about just your lives, though.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Sometimes you need an interesting hook to get the readers reading.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): It's a petition! Not some story! And your version sucks anyway!
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): I kinda liked it.
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): Especially the scene in front of the fire... It was romantic.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Shigure. Please. Do you think all this work has been for a literary contest?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): This is supposed to be about how awful and scary our lives are here!
Not your fantasy teenage romance world.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Imagine if we sent this to the Secretariat! That stupid Cherino would be like, "They're supposed to be in punishment, but they're happy and in love! Suspend them forever!"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): I think you're being a bit judgmental. This could be the next masterpiece in the history of Kivotos.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Then do it on your own time! Right now, what we need is a petition to save us from our inevitable fate of starving to death in the cold at the Former Institute!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Please write it again—non-fiction this time!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Fine. I'll re-write it.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): "Dear Great Leader. Our comrades of Spec Ops No. 227 are suffering from a serious shortage of supplies. We've even been forced to split our jawbreakers into two!"
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Yes! Yes! That's perfect.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Please include some examples so they understand our suffering.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): "Nodoka was pitifully savoring her hard-earned candy..."
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): H-Huh? I don't like the direction this is going!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): "Shigure walked in to see her beloved Nodoka eating candy, all alone. She knelt down next to Nodoka and gently traced her mouth..."
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Wait, stop! STOP!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Why are you doing this?!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Because...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): It's fun?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): We don't need fun right now! Ugh! I can't deal with the perverted librarians of the Knowledge Liberation Front!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): I never should have placed my hopes and dreams in your hands.
> Screen shakes
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Please leave our home!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Hmph. Your loss. When my masterpiece becomes a hit, I'll be sure to forget you!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): I don't care about your stupid, lying story!
> Screen shakes
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): What a shame. I wanted to see how it ended.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): I've said it before: We'll never escape this horrible place with your lackadaisical attitude.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): We need a petition that pulls at the Secretariat officers' heartstrings and makes them cry!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): I won't let them keep neglecting us and our existences!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): We need this appeal to work so we can go back to our regular lives.
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): Yeah but...who are more capable writers than the Knowledge Liberation Front?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): It doesn't matter!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): A petition needs our sincere feelings, not flowery words.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): We just need to find comrades who are sympathetic to our situation.
It's not about the quality of the writing!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): C'mon, Shigure! Let's find comrades to help us!
