> Engineering Department Workshop
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Are you ready, Kotori?
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Of course! I've been waiting for this moment!
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Allow me to do the honors, then.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Ta-da! It's finally complete. This is the Engineering Department's newest invention:
a submachine gun with a hot sauce dispenser!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): It looks a lot more like an ordinary submachine gun than I was expecting. Does it work?
- Kotori (Engineering Department): You bet it does! The difference is that you have to use special bullets mixed with pepper and vinegar instead of regular bullets.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): When you put a special bullet in the magazine and pull the trigger, gunpowder causes the red pepper to be fermented in an instant.
> *bang*
- Kotori (Engineering Department): And the hot sauce will come out of the barrel like this!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Let's see here...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): It really is genuine hot sauce! It's completely indistinguishable from the pizza place's sauce.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): We've conducted multiple experiments to recreate the taste. This gun is the pinnacle of Millennium's latest engineering!
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Plus, if you change it to full auto mode, you can dispense two pints of hot sauce per second.
> Screen shakes
> *pop* *pop* *pop* *tat*
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Heh. Now that we have this gun, we can spray as much hot sauce as we want anytime, anywhere! We'll never have to worry about spicing our pizza again!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Simply marvelous. It's exceeded my every expectation.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): This gun is just so...so...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): ...pointless.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Th-That's true. It'd be much cheaper and more convenient to carry hot sauce packets instead of carrying this gun.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Besides, the sauce gets all over the barrel when you use it, which is a pain to clean. So there's a hygiene issue too...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Ha. Still, it was entertaining to see it put to practice, so that's quite all right.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Not every invention needs to satisfy practicality. If you can enjoy what you're making, then great achievements naturally follow.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): That's right! This experiment allowed me to put our liquid-dispensing capabilities to the test! That data is sure to come in handy in the future.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): They say that failure is the prelude to success.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): By the way... Whose gun did you modify?
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Huh? I thought this was your gun, Utaha.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): No, this is much too large to be my gun. I use a burst pistol without a stock. This is a submachine gun.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Well, it's not my gun.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Not mine either.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Wait. Look at this gaudy dragon pattern.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): The only person who would put this kind of stupid design on a gun is...
- Neru (C&C): Finally done with the repair job, I see. You Engineering Department geeks usually don't take so long.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): N-Neru? Wh-What are you doing here?
- Neru (C&C): Huh? What do you mean? I'm here to pick up my gun. You got a problem with that?
- Kotori (Engineering Department): O-Oh, right! I-I forgot about that. Haha...
- Neru (C&C): Huh? But it's sitting right there. How could you forget? Well, whatever. I'll be taking it back if you're done.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): H-Hold on. There's still one little problem with this gun...
- Neru (C&C): What the...? How's it still not done after all this time? I could hear you say something is "finally complete" before I walked in.
- Neru (C&C): ...Don't tell me you guys messed with my gun! What did you do?!
- Kotori (Engineering Department): N-Nothing...! There's nothing wrong with your gun!
- Neru (C&C): Yeah? Fine. I'll be taking it back, then.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): O-Okay. T-Take care...
- Neru (C&C): Whatever. Thanks, I guess.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): What do we do, Utaha?! Neru's gun was...!
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): She's sure to be saucy when she finds out her gun is nothing but a sauce dispenser now.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): We're toast. It won't be long before Neru comes charging in here in a rage and throws us into a barrel of hot sauce!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): All of you calm down. She still doesn't know.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): If we can get Neru's gun back, we can fix it without her ever realizing it was modified.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Take Neru's gun without getting caught? That's like walking into the lion's den!
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): We're dead no matter what we do.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Nonsense. First, I'll make a decoy that we can swap for Neru's gun. In the meantime, you two need to make sure Neru doesn't touch the trigger.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): But...what if Neru finds out that there's something wrong with her gun before then?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I'd rather not think about that possibility.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Let's just pray that she stays patient.
