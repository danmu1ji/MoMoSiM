> Veritas Hackers' Lounge
- Maki (Veritas): *sigh*...
- Hare (Veritas): Why the heavy sigh, Maki? Is something the matter?
- Maki (Veritas): I was just thinking about the physical exam we took today.
- Maki (Veritas): Say, Hare. Did you notice anything strange about physical? Like, do you think weight was off?
- Hare (Veritas): What do you mean? Did something happen?
- Maki (Veritas): For some reason, the scale said my weight was way higher than it should be!
- Maki (Veritas): It doesn't make any sense! I even stopped snacking and worked out for an entire week to get ready for the physical!
- Maki (Veritas): I put in all that work, but I didn't lose any weight at all...
- Kotama (Veritas): Data never lies, Maki.
- Kotama (Veritas): If the output doesn't exceed the input, the data will reflect that. Just as the scale did.
- Kotama (Veritas): I saw you reward yourself for pulling an all-nighter with soda and potato chips.
- Maki (Veritas): Ack!
- Maki (Veritas): What about you, Kotama? How did it go compared to last year?
- Kotama (Veritas): Th-That hardly matters to me.
- Kotama (Veritas): One's physical form is sure to change over time and it's a fool's errand to attempt to control any more than you can. I'm more focused on the virtual world.
- Maki (Veritas): Y-You're starting to scare me. You sure sound like you care.
- Maki (Veritas): Anyway, I refuse to stand for this...this invasion of privacy! Why does the school even care how much we weigh?!
- Hare (Veritas): I agree with Kotama. There's no point in worrying about it.
Human weight is a fluctuating data point.
- Hare (Veritas): If you're that concerned about it, then simply cut back on the late night rewards.
- Maki (Veritas): I can't do that! How do you expect me to live my life knowing that me getting fat will be carved into Millennium's records forever!
- Maki (Veritas): Everyone whisper about it behind my back, and it'll follow me for the rest of my life.
- Hare (Veritas): I somehow doubt anyone cares that much.
- Hare (Veritas): Regardless, what's done is done—the record has already been made. Where are you going with this?
- Maki (Veritas): Heh heh. Who do you think you're talking to?
- Maki (Veritas): We're Veritas: the righteous truth-defending and knowledge-liberating hacking club!
- Maki (Veritas): Changing Millennium's records will be a breeze with skills like ours!
- Hare (Veritas): Like "ours"?
- Kotama (Veritas): Are you referring to us?
- Maki (Veritas): C'mon. Don't act like you haven't thought about it before.
- Maki (Veritas): Just imagine how proud Sensei will be when they see your shining physical exam results.
- Kotama (Veritas): I guess I wouldn't be setting a very good example if I refused such a sincere request from my adorable underclassman.
- Kotama (Veritas): Just for the record, my acceptance of this request has absolutely nothing to do with Sensei's opinion of me!
- Hare (Veritas): My opinion remains unchanged. I'm not interested either way.
- Maki (Veritas): Hey, Hare. Weren't you just telling me about your new hacking drone?
- Maki (Veritas): Don't you want to give a real challenge to see what it can do? Like say...Millennium's security system?
- Hare (Veritas): It's true that I've yet to gather any field data since completing development...
- Hare (Veritas): Fine. I'll go along with this.
- Maki (Veritas): Great! Then we're all in agreement. No backing out now!
- Maki (Veritas): Here's to the defense of truth and the freedom of knowledge!
- Kotama (Veritas): Shouldn't you being saying, "Here's to forging our physical exam records"?
- Hare (Veritas): I'll stick with, "Here's to practical testing of my latest drone."
- Maki (Veritas): Veritas, it's time to roll out!
