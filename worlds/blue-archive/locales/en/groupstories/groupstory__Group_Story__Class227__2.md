> Mint Chocolate Liberation Front's Petition
> The Spec Ops students are suffering because they don't get to eat mint chocolate when they want.
> Therefore, it is of utmost importance that a massive amount of mint chocolate be supplied to Spec Ops No. 227.
> Revolutionaries Against Lemon on Fried Chicken Federation's Petition
> Spec Ops students are faced with extreme conditions that violate human rights on a daily basis. They're being forced to eat soggy karaage!
> The word karaage is kinda weird. Where I lived, fried chicken was called "zangi". We should be the "Revolutionaries Against Lemon on Zangi Federation" instead.
> Karaage is karaage. No other terms are allowed. The same goes for tatsutaage.
> Then what about zantare? Can we call it karatare?
> We can't even put lemon juice on it. Why would tare sauce be okay?!
C'mon, are you a spy?!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Why are they fighting with each other in a petition?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Mint chocolate and karaage won't improve our situation!
How dare they put their selfish interests in our petition!
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): Well, Red Winter students are notoriously interested in themselves.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Ugh. It won't be as effective if another club writes in anyway. I'll just have to do my best...
> Screen shakes
- Minori (Labor Party): Hiya, Spec Ops No. 227!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): M-Minori? Why are you here?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): W-We paid all construction costs and haven't infringed on the rights of the Labor Party at all!
- Minori (Labor Party): We're not always just running around going on strike, you know.
- Minori (Labor Party): I should explain... The Labor Party naturally hears many rumors floating around the student community and helps those same students get various jobs.
- Minori (Labor Party): Now we've heard that Spec Ops No. 227, with whom we once planned a revolution, have been looking for help.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): We are...but not fixing things or anything like that. We want to write a petition to submit to the Secretariat.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): I asked students from clubs all over to write petitions for us to help capture our struggles, but it hasn't been going well. So, I guess that's that.
I did my best...
- Minori (Labor Party): Hmm. Well, I'm an expert on that kind of writing!
- Minori (Labor Party): A good project is completed by unified workers, worker unity is achieved through good speeches, and good speeches are achieved through good writing!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): R-Really? Then do you think I could ask you to help us, Minori?
- Minori (Labor Party): Of course! I'd love to.
- Minori (Labor Party): "The students of Spec Ops No. 227 are suffering in the harsh weather due to poor bedding and heating."
- Minori (Labor Party): "This is an overly harsh environment to endure, even considering their past transgressions. I implore you to take the steps to revoke their suspension and return all of them to the main institute."
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Wow. You're much better than I expected.
- Minori (Labor Party): That's a little rude, you know. I always get an A on my writing tests.
- Minori (Labor Party): The important thing about writing is to clearly convey your purpose.
- Minori (Labor Party): And, of course, you have to keep your reader interested with a bit of rhetoric.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Thank you! This petition will help us make the Secretariat understand how horrible our lives are!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): And you did it so fast! I'm so impressed, Labor Party President!
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): It is pretty good...but do you really think President Cherino will read it?
- Shigure (Spec Ops No. 227): Knowing her, she won't even look at it.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): You're right, Shigure! That irresponsible brat will probably burn it without even opening it!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): How do we get her to read it?
- Minori (Labor Party): I have an idea!
- Minori (Labor Party): I'll just add this to the front of the envelope...
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): That's perfect! She won't be able to resist after seeing it!
- Minori (Labor Party): Let's add this underneath so she knows exactly who it is too.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): You're a genius, Minori!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): All that's left is to send our petition to the Secretariat...
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Then our life in the Former Institute will finally be over!
> One week later, at Cherino's executive office in the Red Winter Office.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): President, it's time for the weekly report.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): As usual, you have received many letters from different clubs.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Heehee. I already know what it's about!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): They're all praising my greatness and incredible mustache!
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Actually, if you'll allow me to summarize... Ehem!
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): "A kid who doesn't appreciate mint doesn't deserve to be the president!"
"We're sick of mustaches!"
"The dictator kid needs to wake up!"
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): What did you just say?!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): How dare they hide behind their letters to challenge the authority of my mustache?!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Give me all the letters, Tomoe! I'll purge every last one of them!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): What's this? There's red writing on the envelope.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): "If you don't read this letter, you'll be cursed"?
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Tomoe! Help! What do I do? I'm going to be cursed!
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Don't worry. It's likely just a chain letter.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): A chain letter...?
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Yes. A manipulative message that convinces people to pass it around to others. They've been a popular prank among the students recently.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): It's likely just full of fake, unfortunate events.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): I see. It does talk about a lot of misfortunes.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): "The students of Spec Ops No. 227 are suffering in the harsh weather due to poor bedding and heating facilities..."
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): How strange. Even though it's a prank, it makes me think of something I feel like I've forgotten...
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Chief Secretary Tomoe. Accompany me during my nap today.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): I-It's not that I'm scared! The mind of a great leader is simply always busy!
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Of course, President. I will stay with you as long as you need.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): If you would please, I can burn the chain letter so it no longer affects you.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): That's a good idea, Tomoe.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): All right, let's move on!
> Thus, Spec Ops No. 227's petition came to nothing but a handful of dust...
