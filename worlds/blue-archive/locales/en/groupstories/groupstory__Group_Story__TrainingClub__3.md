> Gehenna District Minor League Stadium
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): It's...
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): ...less crowded in the stadium than I expected.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Yep. It's only a minor league game for rookies, on top of being a Monday.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Don't make that face. You were the one who ran us out to the first game you could find...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): ...That said, it does feel nice to be back in the stands.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Especially at a minor league game. The crowd's small. You can sit right up next to the players.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Quite a few baseball fans only go to minor league games for those reasons alone.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): ...I see.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): Anyway, about that food in your hand...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Hm? It's a hot dog.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): Now Rei... I don't want to enforce a strict diet on you. But processed food with lots of fat can block up your digestive system and significantly lessen the effectiveness of our training.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): If you're feeling peckish, a protein bar is a much better choice. It has a great balance of protein and carbohydrates—
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): No way! We can't come all the way to Gehenna and skip out on their trademark scorching hell hot dogs!
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): A scorching hell hot dog?
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Yes. See, every stadium sells snacks and drinks so you can have something to eat while watching the game.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): They're usually pretty generic, but some stadiums sell delicious treats based on the home team!
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Here in Gehenna, they have scorching hell hot dogs. They're made from a rare, super spicy sausage. They're so good!
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Each academy's stadium has their own delicacy. So not only do you get to see an exciting game, you get to have yummy food too!
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): Well... All right. But try to only have one.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Oh! I forgot to put on some chili sauce.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Although, the sausage is pretty well seasoned, so with the chili, it's extra spicy.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Then after a bite, I wash it all down with a nice ginger ale...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Mmm bmm... Now this is baseball!
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Oh, feel free to order whatever you want, Sumire. I'll treat you!
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): ...One unsalted chicken breast salad please.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Do they even have healthy food like that at a stadium?
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): ...It seems that the game's begun.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): This is my first time watching Gehenna players on the field. Is their club any good?
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Well... With a motto like "Freedom, chaos, and trouble!", as good as they can be.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): They have talented players, but they lack strong teamwork.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): That said, even their minor league team is way better than the Millennium Baseball Club...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): But we're not here to play, we're here to watch. So let's forget our affiliations and focus on cheering!
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Let's go, Gehenna! Break a leg!
- Studio Announcer: The second hitter of Gehenna is at bat. Looks like it's gonna be a fastball...and that's a hit!
- Studio Announcer: The ball's all the way in the middle of the outfield! The batter nabs first base. She's gunning for second...
- Studio Announcer: ...Oh no too greedy! She's out!
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Ugh, that was a chance for a nice two-bagger... What a shame.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): If you need to step on the base before the defender's hand tags you...
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): Can't you just kick the second baseman with a spinning slide kick?
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Of course not!
> The Gehenna baseball club lost their game, in spite of having Rei as a cheerleader...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): ...What a downer of a game. They could've won if the opposing team didn't land that super save at the end.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): I agree.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): If I was the runner, I would have gone past the defender with a shoulder charge and sprinted towards the base.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): And that one Gehenna batter should build up her deltoids more.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Are you sure you understand how baseball works, Sumire?
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): That said, it's been a while since I got to enjoy a game with someone. It feels nice.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): Training is important for a fitness club. But watching sports, seeing others compete with the fruits of their training is great for your own motivation as well.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Is it always training day inside that head of yours?
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): I'm serious, Rei.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): Do you remember that home run you saw?
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): ...Yes.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): The stadium wasn't too crowded then...just like today.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Some folks came for food. Others wanted to take photos with their favorite players. A few were scouting new talent for their teams. Everyone had a different reason to come to the stadium.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): But in the end, all eyes were on the batter when she made that home run.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): The arc the ball drew across the sky was beautiful. But that moment of the batter jogging around the field with nothing in her way? It was so cool.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): It's funny. I had forgotten most of that entire game, despite how much I talked about the home run.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): Having an end goal for your training is important.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): It could be lifting 100 kg or hitting a homerun. But without a goal, exercise is nothing more than self-inflicted pain.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): There has to be a reason for each lift, each swing. Something that drives you to struggle for each step, when common sense begs you to take the easy road.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): Just like how the person who wants to deadlift 100 kg isn't just trying to pick up a number, your goal must be more than simply swatting a ball far away. Otherwise, you won't fight for it.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): As there are many things to enjoy about baseball, there are many things to enjoy about training too.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): One of those things is pursuing a worthy goal. Because instead of dreading each new challenge, it grants you the will to overcome them.
- Sumire (Athletics Training Club): Ready to go back to training with me, Rei?
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): ...Heck yeah!
