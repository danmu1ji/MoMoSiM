> Saint Teresa General Hospital, 4th Floor - Empty Ward
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): Wait, what? You're not a real ghost?
- Fake Ghost: Yeah! That's what I keep saying!
- Serina (Remedial Knights): But all the special effects... And your acting was so good... I thought you were a real ghost!
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): Why were you wandering around pretending to be a ghost? Were you playing hide-and-seek in the empty ward?
- Fake Ghost: Why would I pretend to be a ghost for a dumb reason like that?! I snuck in here to scare people away so I could steal things!
- Serina (Remedial Knights): Steal things? Stealing is bad!
- Fake Ghost: I-I know that! B-But, I had no choice... It was the only way to find my father's lost keepsake!
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): Your father's keepsake? Why would you search for something like that here in the hospital?
- Fake Ghost: My father went into surgery at this hospital last year. But when it came time to leave, I forgot all about his necklace. That's how I lost it...
- Fake Ghost: I didn't want to harm the other patients...but the hospital wouldn't let me up here, so I had no other choice!
- Serina (Remedial Knights): So that was why... I understand.
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): We'll help you find your father's keepsake!
- Fake Ghost: Really? But I pretended I was a ghost so I could steal things... Are you sure?
- Serina (Remedial Knights): It's the motto of the Remedial Knights to help anyone in trouble. Just promise us that you won't touch anything that doesn't belong to you!
- Fake Ghost: Of course I won't! I promise I'll leave as soon as I get my father's necklace!
- Serina (Remedial Knights): Okay! Hanae, let's look around and see if we can find anything important!
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): Okay!
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): There's an iron box on the shelf over here! I bet it contains a bunch of valuable stuff... Can you help me move it?
- Serina (Remedial Knights): Oh! Hold on!
- Serina (Remedial Knights): Ugh! Phew, that was heavy. I wonder what's in here.
- Fake Ghost: Hand it over!
- Fake Ghost: Give me that safe!
> Screen shakes
- Serina (Remedial Knights): Why are you doing this, Mr. Ghost?!
- Fake Ghost: Hahaha, I fooled you, dummies!
- Fake Ghost: I made up the whole story about my father's keepsake! I broke into this hospital to make some money for myself!
- Fake Ghost: And you volunteered to help me. Trinity students are so naive! Hahaha!
- Serina (Remedial Knights): What? How can you take advantage of other people's kindness? That's so mean!
- Fake Ghost: It's your fault for believing me. I'm not responsible for your dumb decisions!
- Fake Ghost: This safe is all mine now!

- Serina (Remedial Knights): I-I'm fine. But we have to get that stolen safe back from the ghost...

- Serina (Remedial Knights): But...the injection is gonna sting, okay?
- Fake Ghost: No, please, anything but a shot! I can't stand needles... Please spare me!
