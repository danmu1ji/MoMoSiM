> Seminar Conference Room
- Yuuka (Seminar): Now that everyone's here...
- Noa (Seminar): We can begin.
- Noa (Seminar): Welcome to the 28th Millennium Budget Review Committee Conference!
- Yuuka (Seminar): As you already know, the Budget Review Committee Conference is a periodic gathering where we review the research and development prospects of each club and allocate funds accordingly.
- Yuuka (Seminar): If a club wants better funding, it is up to us to determine if they can earn it, usually by their ability to create useful products.
- Yuuka (Seminar): The last conference was filled with bizarre items that had no practical purpose whatsoever. As was the conference before that...
- Yuuka (Seminar): I... I still believe in the potential of our students, however.
- Noa (Seminar): We'll begin with...the New Material Development Club's Cushy Cement!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Cushy...?
- Material Development Club Member: Yes! It's a new, innovative approach to cement!
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...What exactly does it mean for cement to be...cushy?
- Material Development Club Member: As you all know, cement is a beloved material in construction due to its malleability while wet and hardness while dry.
- Material Development Club Member: However, cement has one fatal flaw... When it hardens, it becomes way too hard!
- Material Development Club Member: People live in buildings! But according to our research, the average person's body is much weaker than cement, so if someone falls or bumps into cement, they could get injured.
- Material Development Club Member: However, Cushy Cement solves this problem by remaining soft even after it hardens, so not only will you not get hurt, you might even be tempted to lay down on it!
- Noa (Seminar): A building material that's soft to the touch? Truly revolutionary...
- Yuuka (Seminar): Hmm... This is all very interesting, however...
- Yuuka (Seminar): If it's that soft, how exactly would it not bend under the weight of other construction materials, such as steel or bricks?
- Material Development Club Member: Ah... Well, about that... As they say, the best inventions leave room for improvement...
- Yuuka (Seminar): So, to be clear, you took a building material often used for foundations and other purposes reliant on durability and toughness, and made it soft?
> Screen shakes
- Yuuka (Seminar): Rejected!
- Material Development Club Member: N-Nooo!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Next!
- Noa (Seminar): The next item is the Dragon Duster proposed by Murokasa Akane.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Huh? Akane? C&C submitted a research proposal?
- Yuuka (Seminar): *whisper* C&C's budget is already allocated separately based on operational scope... Is that not enough?
- Akane (C&C): Oh, no, this isn't a C&C proposal, it's from me as an individual.
- Akane (C&C): I am a dedicated member of C&C...
- Akane (C&C): But I am also a student of Millennium Science School.
- Akane (C&C): Contributing to the academy's technological advancement by proposing new ideas is just another service I wish to provide!
- Noa (Seminar): What an excellent attitude for a Millennium student! We must all pursue innovation and creativity whenever possible.
- Yuuka (Seminar): I have a bad feeling about this...
- Yuuka (Seminar): Let's hear her out. How is this Dragon Duster different from an ordinary duster?
- Akane (C&C): Dusters are one of my favorite cleaning implements.
- Akane (C&C): However, I often encounter stains that ordinary dusters simply can't remove.
- Akane (C&C): There's obviously a limit to how many cleaning tools I can carry at once, and going out of my way to get another one is cumbersome and time-consuming.
- Akane (C&C): So I came up with the Dragon Duster... A duster equipped with a compressed gas sprayer!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Compressed gas sprayer...?
- Akane (C&C): Not just any gas. Upon activating this function, the compressed gas will ignite as it's sprayed, completely incinerating the target!
- Akane (C&C): No stubborn stains or sticky substance can withstand the Dragon Duster's cleansing flames.
- Noa (Seminar): A tool that spews fire...
- Yuuka (Seminar): Akane, that's a flamethrower.
- Akane (C&C): Oh... But is it a novel flamethrower?
- Yuuka (Seminar): No. I don't think we need to fund a product that already exists.
> Screen shakes
- Yuuka (Seminar): Rejected. Next!
- Noa (Seminar): Next is...
- Noa (Seminar): Oh my... This is a proposal from Momoi of the Game Development Department, who received a special award for the last Millennium Prize.
- Momoi (Game Development Department): Heh heh... Yuuka might be the Cold, Unfeeling Accountant, but she'll be begging to invest once she sees my impeccable proposal.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Rejected.
- Momoi (Game Development Department): W-Wait! You didn't even hear it!
- Yuuka (Seminar): I don't need to. The Game Development Department just plays games all day instead of doing any actual club activities.
- Momoi (Game Development Department): Th-That's unfair! When we work, we deliver!
- Momoi (Game Development Department): Besides, we already came up with a profit model that utilizes advanced technology!
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...A profit model that utilizes advanced technology?
- Momoi (Game Development Department): Yes! This proposal is a game design that implements the latest blockchain technology!
- Momoi (Game Development Department): When you play games, you often earn in-game currency, right? By using this technology, we can just convert this in-game money to real money!
- Momoi (Game Development Department): So, by just sitting down and playing games, you'll make money! Heehee.
- Yuuka (Seminar): It sounds plausible. If you tied it to a cryptocurrency, it would allow players to cash out.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Right, but who would be exchanging cash for this?
- Momoi (Game Development Department): ...Exchange? Cash...?
- Momoi (Game Development Department): Well... Can't Seminar just print it...?
> Screen shakes
- Yuuka (Seminar): I knew it! Rejected!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Is every proposal really going to be rejected again...
- Noa (Seminar): A few proposals seemed promising.
- Noa (Seminar): There was a 37% increase in proposals that planned to use emerging technology compared to last year.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Is that even a good thing? None of their proposed uses of those technologies made any sense!
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...I simply don't understand why everyone is so enamored with such impractical research.
- Noa (Seminar): Our job isn't to decide the direction of the research.
- Noa (Seminar): And who knows? The research may look meaningless now, but it could become the foundation of revolutionary technology in the future.
- Noa (Seminar): ...The Millennium Problem.
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...
- Noa (Seminar): Seminar, what could be considered the heart of Millennium, was originally a gathering of researchers with one goal: to find solutions to the seven unsolvable problems of the modern world.
- Noa (Seminar): Countless experiments, approaches, tests, and more were conducted in order to crack these problems... And as they expanded their efforts, what we know as Millennium Science School came into being.
- Noa (Seminar): Of course, most students these days don't even know what the problems are...
- Noa (Seminar): But each piece of research, each wild experiment, it's all a part of the process of solving those seven problems.
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...I suppose so. As long as there's a sliver of a chance of progress, all thinking and experimentation at Millennium is encouraged and recommended.
- Yuuka (Seminar): However, nobody can know whether these wild ideas will actually bear fruit, but we can know what our budget will look like.
- Yuuka (Seminar): We can encourage students all we want, but if we fund them endlessly, the academy will go bankrupt.
- Noa (Seminar): Heehee. Great researchers often only notice practical things such as time and money once they've run out.
- Noa (Seminar): Millennium School is so lucky to have a treasurer as brilliant as Yuuka.
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...You're messing with me, aren't you?
- Yuuka (Seminar): If you're so worried about Millennium's finances, why didn't you speak up more?
- Yuuka (Seminar): When we were debating the utility the AI Research Department's so-called Smart Bomb, which they claimed could determine when to detonate without human input, it would have been more persuasive if you'd said something, Noa...
- Noa (Seminar): Sorry, but I'm just a secretary.
- Noa (Seminar): A good secretary doesn't interject, no matter the circumstances. We must be as neutral as our records.
- Yuuka (Seminar): What convenient logic...
- Yuuka (Seminar): It sounds like you pick and choose when you want to be Seminar's secretary and when you want to be Ushio Noa...
- Noa (Seminar): Heehee! Whichever I am, it won't change the fact that I like you, Yuuka!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Yes, yes. I guess I shouldn't be surprised...
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...What's this document?
- Noa (Seminar): Did we skip a proposal during the budget review?
- Yuuka (Seminar): Impossible. We heard from every club on the list.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Hmm... The person who wrote the proposal was...
- Yuuka (Seminar): The president of Veritas?!
