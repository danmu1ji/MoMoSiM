> 3rd St. & 2nd Ave. Master Donuts
> Screen shakes
- Donut Shop Owner: E-Eek! Help me!
- Hostage Taker: Hahaha! Hand over all your dough if you don't wanna get hurt!
- Hostage Taker: Taking the shop owner hostage, threatening people so they don't come near, and then taking all the money before the police arrive... Another perfect plan!
- Hostage Taker: Valkyrie Police School may pack some serious heat, but you can't use it if you're so slow! We'll be long gone by the time they arrive on the scene. Hahaha!
- Hostage Taker: Now, time to crack open the safe...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Police! Everyone put your hands up and take a step back!
- Hostage Taker: What?! How are they already here?! It hasn't even been five minutes since the police were called!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Don't compare us to the slowpokes at the Security Bureau! The Public Safety Bureau knows all the shortcuts in this area thanks to our regular patrols!
- Hostage Taker: The Public Safety Bureau?
- Hostage Taker: You're with Public...Safety? Hahahaha!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Wh-What's so funny?
- Hostage Taker: You're what's funny! Shouldn't you be directing traffic somewhere? You don't have the muscle to take us on!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Even criminals are looking down on us...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Ugh... Just shut up and get caught already!
- Donut Shop Owner: A-Ahhh! What are you doing?! You can't just start shooting like that!
- Hostage Taker: Y-You brat! Were you even looking at where you were aiming?! Are you trying to ice the hostage too?!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): *sigh* I knew my marksmanship got low marks on my entrance exam,
but I didn't think it would still be plaguing me at this point...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Fubuki, would you mind being my trigger woman?
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): I dunno. I'm not confident in my shot at this distance.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): The Security Bureau will arrive and snatch away our commendation if we keep wasting time like this. We need to figure something out soon...
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Wouldn't it be better to just leave the arrest up to them at this point?
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): We don't have sniper rifles to shoot with from this distance or explosives that can break up their barricade. The situation might deteriorate if we try to force things.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): This is so frustrating! If only we could take down their barricade blocking the entrance. Then it'd be easier to get in...
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Hey, Kirino. Isn't that barricade made of the sacks of flour they use to make the donuts?
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I can't see it clearly from this far away. Why? What's that got to do with anything?
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Don't get used to me coming up with plans like this, but I think I just came up with a way to neutralize the culprit.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Hear me out.
> Moments later...
- Hostage Taker: Whew, it's quiet now. Looks like those cops have given up.
- Hostage Taker: Now, back to opening the safe...
> *bang* *bang* *bang*
- Hostage Taker: Wh-What are those loud gunshots?! Are they firing warning shots?
- Hostage Taker: Their marksmanship is still terrible. The bullets aren't even making it this far, they're just shooting at the barricade I made from the flour sacks.
- Hostage Taker: Hey, idiots! What are you shooting at? That's just flour!
- Hostage Taker: What do you hope to accomplish, huh?!
> Screen shakes
- Hostage Taker: *cough* *cough* Where is this smoke coming from? Wait a second...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): You guessed it! That's flour you're breathing in!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Those shots were never meant for you. Our goal was to fill the store with flour.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): All it takes it just one small spark to ignite this much flour in the air. In other words...
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): ...it's a flour bomb!
- Hostage Taker: You idiots.
- Hostage Taker: The entire block will be blown away if you do that—
> *boom*
- Donut Shop Owner: My shop... The neighborhood...
- Hostage Taker: Our plan was ruined by these idiots. I can't believe it...
> Screen shakes
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Hurray! We caught the culprit!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): And we managed to blow up both the culprit and the barricade without even using bombs.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): My plan worked perfectly.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Now the higher-ups at Valkyrie will have to recognize our achievement, right?
- Security Director General: Police! Everyone raise your hands and step...
- Security Director General: We were told there was a hostage situation here, but this looks more like a terrorist attack...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Look, Director! We caught the culprit before anyone else even got here!
- Security Director General: You're Kirino and Fubuki from the Public Safety Bureau, right? What in the world happened here? Did the culprit set off a bomb?!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): No, ma'am. We destroyed the barricade they had set up!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): It was with flour instead of gunpowder, but still very effective.
- Security Director General: ...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): So, what do you think? I can be transferred to the Security Bureau with this kind of achievement under my belt, right?
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): I don't need to be transferred, but I wouldn't mind about two weeks of vacation.
- Security Director General: Blowing up an entire store to solve a problem that could've been solved by one sniper...
- Security Director General: Return to your station and be prepared to submit a written apology!
> Screen shakes
- Everyone: What?!
> Kirino and Fubuki were then suspended for a while as punishment for the explosion at the donut shop.
