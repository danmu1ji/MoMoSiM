# Group Story / GourmetClub / 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Group_Story/GourmetClub/3

> (The Gourmet Research Society is paying a visit to Millennium's Veritas Laboratory.)
- Hare (Veritas): And that's why mint tastes like soap or shampoo to people with receptor OR6A4.
- Hare (Veritas): Because it's a single nucleotide polymorphism gene—or an SNP gene.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Fascinating. So, I guess it can't be cured?
- Hare (Veritas): Liking the taste of mint is not a disease. Why try to fix it?
- Izumi (Gourmet Research Society): That's right! You make it sound like it's a flaw or something. That's so mean!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): We're being serious! What if there's something wrong with your nose or tongue?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Think of how it could interfere with your daily life. That's how disorders are formed.
- Hare (Veritas): How bad could it be?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): It all began 30 years ago...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): That's too far back! What are you even talking about?!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): The Prefect Team locked Izumi up yesterday for making and selling mint juice.
- Hare (Veritas): Mint juice...? I've never heard of anything like that.
- Hare (Veritas): It'd be weird to drink, sure, but was it really worth arresting her for?
- Izumi (Gourmet Research Society): Exactly! It's all one big conspiracy. Stop the mint hate!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): She said it was mint juice, but it was actually toothpaste mixed with water. ☆
- Hare (Veritas): Oh, now I get it.
- Izumi (Gourmet Research Society): ...?!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Iori was saying something about tainted food when she took her in.
> (Iori of the Gehenna Prefect Team.)
> (Note: Mint mania.)
- Izumi (Gourmet Research Society): But it tastes just like mint juice. Plus, it's good for your teeth...and cheap.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Who would want it?! You can't peddle something like that just because you like it!
- Hare (Veritas): Anyway, Izumi likes mint because she doesn't have the receptor.
- Hare (Veritas): Otherwise, she would think it tastes like shampoo or soap.
- Hare (Veritas): It's a congenital problem, so nothing will change it.
- Izumi (Gourmet Research Society): That's right. I wouldn't eat shampoo or soap.
- Izumi (Gourmet Research Society): I just eat mint because I like it!
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Hmm. I guess we have no choice.
- Hare (Veritas): That's right.
- Hare (Veritas): Well, since you're all here, why don't we check and see who has the receptor?
- Hare (Veritas): They say the best way to accept others is to better understand yourself.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Oh, my! That sounds like fun! ☆
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): You mean we can find out whether we think mint tastes like shampoo?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): It's also relevant to my pursuit of the ultimate taste. My interest is piqued.
- Hare (Veritas): Now, say, "Aaah..." This won't take long.
> (Moments later...)
- Hare (Veritas): Here are the results. First off, Akari has no receptor.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Oh, really? Then, am I simply picky for not liking mint?
- Hare (Veritas): It wouldn't hurt to try it again with an open mind.
- Hare (Veritas): Next up, Haruna. She does have the receptor.
- Hare (Veritas): Mint must taste like shampoo or soap to you, Haruna.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): I expected this. I treat all flavors equally, but even I struggle with mint.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): To think it was due to my DNA. Still, that is no excuse to discriminate.
- Hare (Veritas): Junko also lacks the receptor.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I like pretty much anything—including mint.
- Hare (Veritas): Next is Izumi.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Isn't it already obvious that Izumi doesn't have the receptor? She loves mint so much.
- Izumi (Gourmet Research Society): Yeah! Mint is delicious! I have no idea why people hate it.
- Hare (Veritas): This says Izumi...does have the receptor.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...?!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Wh-What?!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Oh, my. So mint tastes like shampoo or soap to Izumi?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Huh?
- Izumi (Gourmet Research Society): No way! You mean that's what shampoo tastes like?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): You didn't know?! W-Wait, so you eat mint even though it tastes like shampoo?!
- Hare (Veritas): Taste is subjective so...there could be people out there that enjoy how shampoo tastes.
- Hare (Veritas): I suppose... I mean, there has to be someone...right?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Wh-What are you going on about?! It's totally unnatural!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Why would anyone eat something that tastes like soap or shampoo? That's so dumb!
- Izumi (Gourmet Research Society): Wow... So that's what shampoo tasted like all along?
- Izumi (Gourmet Research Society): (smile) Does this mean I can eat shampoo now?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): DON'T YOU DARE!
> An Unbiased Palate
