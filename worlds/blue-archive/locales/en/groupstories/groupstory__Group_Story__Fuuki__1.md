# Group Story / Fuuki / 1

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Group_Story/Fuuki/1
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


- Ako (Prefect Team): Now it's time for us to start the regular weekly Prefect Team meeting.
- Hina (Prefect Team): *sigh*
- Hina (Prefect Team): Ako, I have an important meeting with the Pandemonium Society today, so let's get this over with quickly.
- Ako (Prefect Team): Fine. The next meeting is in fifteen minutes, so I think we can finish before then.
- Ako (Prefect Team): First, we need to go over the report on the unidentified explosion that occurred in Zone 97-44.
- Ako (Prefect Team): We've confirmed that the explosion in Zone 97-44 was caused by a dual-purpose high explosive used for blasting.
- Ako (Prefect Team): This is certainly the work of the Hot Springs Department... The suspects are currently on the run, and three squads are pursuing them.
- Hina (Prefect Team): What's their motive?
- Ako (Prefect Team): Hot springs development, as always.
- Hina (Prefect Team): Wait... Isn't Zone 97-44 in the middle of the freeway? They detonated a high-power explosive to develop a hot spring there?
- Ako (Prefect Team): That's correct. They have no guilt when it comes to the development of hot springs...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): So, I have to ask...why are you doing this?
- Hot Springs Department Member A (Hot Springs Department): It doesn't matter where we find a hot spring, as long as we find them! Dig 'em all up!
- Hot Springs Department Member B (Hot Springs Department): Hahaha! Burn it all! Burn all this useless trash!
- Hot Springs Department Member C (Hot Springs Department): Bulldoze everything! It's demolition time! Demolition! Development is all that matters!
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): (I have no idea what they're saying... What am I supposed to do?)
- Ako (Prefect Team): This is the gist of Chinatsu's report. I don't understand it, though...
- Ako (Prefect Team): I'm guessing they were going to build something like...a freeway rest stop.
- Hina (Prefect Team): *sigh*
- Ako (Prefect Team): The freeway is expected to be backed up for about three months, so that means fifty-four percent of the goods transported...
- Hina (Prefect Team): Right. You can leave the rest to the Pandemonium Society. What's next?
- Ako (Prefect Team): Okay. The next incident is the explosion in a school dining hall...
- Hina (Prefect Team): The dining hall explosion... Was it the Gourmet Research Society?
- Ako (Prefect Team): That's correct, President.
- Hina (Prefect Team): And the motive?
- Ako (Prefect Team): This time, it was because, well.. There was no lemon on the karaage...
- Hina (Prefect Team): Lemon...?!
- Ako (Prefect Team): They took Fuuka, the School Lunch Club president, hostage and argued with the Prefect Team members who came to subdue them.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Okay, let me ask you a very important question, Prefect Team.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Should lemon go on karaage or not?!
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Well, everyone? What do you think?
- Iori (Prefect Team): Well, in my opinion...I-I think...there should be lemon...
- Prefect Team Member (Prefect Team): What? That would make the tempura all soggy! Why would you sprinkle lemon juice on it?!
- Iori (Prefect Team): But the refreshing scent of lemon cuts through the greasiness of the tempura!
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Haha! All right...all right. This is apparently a serious gastronomic quest! It's exactly what the Gourmet Research Society wants to see!
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): What's the right thing to do here? Come on, let's figure out the answer, guys!
- Prefect Team Members (Prefect Team): *murmuring* So...lemon would cut through...
- Prefect Team Members (Prefect Team): *murmuring* I mean, then the batter on the tempura would...
- Prefect Team Members (Prefect Team): *murmuring* Then, what about salt?
- Prefect Team Members (Prefect Team): *murmuring* Why change the topic all of a sudden? I mean, I prefer soy sauce!
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Heehee!
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): (Ugh... I don't care either way... Please, just get me out of here.)
- Ako (Prefect Team): (The heated debate escalated into a physical fight. Many of the Prefect Team members, including Iori, were hospitalized. After that, the Gourmet Research Society ran away.)
- Hina (Prefect Team): *sigh*
- Hina (Prefect Team): Ask the School Lunch Club president to figure out the damage and submit a report.
- Hina (Prefect Team): If we lose another dining hall, we'll only have rice balls to eat for lunch!
- Ako (Prefect Team): Got it, President.
- Ako (Prefect Team): Next up... A group that forged absence letters and sold them for 2,000 yen per sheet has been arrested.
- Hina (Prefect Team): Problem Solver?
- Ako (Prefect Team): That's correct. It was Problem Solver 68... They trespassed into the faculty office and made a copy of the absence letter stamp.
- Ako (Prefect Team): They shamelessly posted an ad on the academy bulletin board that read, "Want to skip school? Buy one of our absence letters and leave whenever you want!"
- Hina (Prefect Team): Absence letters? But no one asks to be absent at our academy...
- Aru (Problem Solver 68): How come no one is buying any of these?!
- Mutsuki (Problem Solver 68): Ugh! We're doomed, Aru!
- Haruka (Problem Solver 68): *fidgets nervously*
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): *sigh*
- Ako (Prefect Team): Yep, exactly. So they couldn't even sell one sheet. The crimes here are trespassing and forgery of official documents.
- Hina (Prefect Team): *sigh*
- Ako (Prefect Team): Next...
- Hina (Prefect Team): Wait... How many more things are there on the docket?
- Ako (Prefect Team): Eighteen more, President.
- Hina (Prefect Team): What?! These all happened over the course of a week?
- Ako (Prefect Team): No, these only cover the last two days. Since the Intelligence Committee is on vacation, the report for the remaining five days will be available at next week's meeting.
- Hina (Prefect Team): ...Oh, no...
- Ako (Prefect Team): Ugh...
- Ako (Prefect Team): Cheer up, President!
- Hina (Prefect Team): *sigh*
> Weekly Prefect Team Meeting
