> Black Tortoise Promenade Office
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Hello, President Rumi. Good afternoon.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Oh, Kokona. Welcome.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Don't just call me Kokona. Call me Instructor Kokona!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I'm also a full-fledged instructor just like you, okay? Seriously... Please don't treat me like a child!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Haha, sorry. I'm still not used to calling you that.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): So, what brings "Instructor Kokona" to the office today?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Are you hungry? Would you like me to cook you some fried rice?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): No, I'm not here to eat. I'm here to pick up some ingredients.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Ingredients?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Yes. Shun asked me to get ingredients for the Plum Blossom children's meals.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Did you not receive the notice?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Ah, how could I forget? I must have been so busy with work.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Wait here just a moment. I'll be right back.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Let's see. I brought cabbage, bamboo shoots, carrots, onions...as well as some dried Chinese noodles and soy milk.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Oh, and take some of the flat peaches near the entrance. They're sweet, so they should make for a healthy dessert.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Th-This is a lot, though. Are you sure it's okay?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I thought I was only picking up some leftover ingredients...
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): It's more than fine. Those children are the future of Shanhaijing after all. They need to eat healthy and grow up strong!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Oh, but maybe it is a little too heavy for you to carry on your own.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): N-No, I can handle it. This is nothing for an instructor like me.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Splendid, then.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Oh, I almost forgot. Take this as well.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): What's in the bag? More food?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): No, it's a special item that Instructor Shun specifically requested from the Black Tortoise Promenade.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): It's...
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): ...a secret.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Huh? Seriously?!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): That's right. In fact, Shun swore me to secrecy over it.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Even if you are Shun's younger sister, maintaining the confidentiality of our customers is a matter of trust with Promenade. I'm sorry, but I can't tell you.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Ugh. Now I'm even more curious.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): It's something hard, and it makes a rattling sound when you shake it. Is it a toy?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Well, you're not entirely wrong. I guess if we want to get technical, it could be called a grown-up's toy.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): A grown-up's...toy?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Yep. Inside is something that is absolutely not for children—not even ones your age.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): So you'd better not think of secretly checking what's inside on your way back.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Oh, come on! You and Shun aren't that much older!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I'm also a full-fledged instructor, so for me to be the only one who doesn't know is totally unfair!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Some things are better left unknown, Kokona.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): For example, the officers of Genryumon, Shanhaijing's Student Council, will deal with all the witnesses when a secret is revealed for the sake of maintaining the school's confidentiality. Isn't that right?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): That's why Shanhaijing students run away if someone tries to reveal to them a secret about Genryumon—so that they don't hear something they shouldn't.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Genryumon?! Is what's inside related to them?!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Hmm. I can neither confirm nor deny...but let's just assume that it is.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): So, Kokona, having said that, I think it would be best if you simply delivered the item. Don't you?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): U-Understood!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I promise I won't look at what's inside and deliver it straight to Shun!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Very good, then. I'll leave it in your capable hands.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): H-Haha. That was a little mean, but it looks like she bought it.
> Black Tortoise Promenade Street
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): The more I think about it, the more curious I am. It's supposed to be a grown-up's toy, right?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): But President Rumi said I could get in serious trouble if I look. I should just put it out of my mind and deliver it to Shun.
- "Hi, Kokona."
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Oh, Sensei. Hello.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): This is a bunch of foodstuff from the Black Tortoise Promenade and...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): This is a bunch of foodstuff from the Black Tortoise Promenade and...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): ...a, uh, grown-up toy for Shun.
- "...Come again?"
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Oh, no! President Rumi said I'm not supposed to tell anyone about that!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): This is bad. Those terrifying people from Genryumon could appear and take us away at any moment...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Wait. You're an adult, Sensei, so maybe it's all right for you to know about things like grown-up toys.
- "...What exactly IS it?"
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I'm not sure either. All I know is that it's supposedly something Shun ordered from the Black Tortoise Promenade.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Judging by the outside of the bag, it's some kind of long, hard device...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): President Rumi said that something seriously bad could happen if I were to peek at it, so I have to keep it a secret.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): What do you think it could be?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Hmm. I guess it could be.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I guess a gun that's too powerful and complex would need to be kept out of the hands of children.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Maybe it's a terrifying new weapon developed with forbidden technology...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): No way. If it were something that normal, there wouldn't be any need to hide it, right?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Besides, everyone knows Shun's hair is naturally perfectly straight.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): It's gotta be some kind of new terrifying weapon that's too dangerous for children, or maybe it's some kind of bomb...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Hold on. What time is it? I can't believe how late it already is.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I'm going to miss afternoon snacks if I keep dawdling like this. I should hurry.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Sensei, you have to keep Shun's grown-up toy a secret! Promise me!
> Plum Blossom Garden
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Hey, I'm back!
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Goodness, that's a lot of ingredients. Thank you for picking them up.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Yeah, President Rumi gave me a lot more than I was expecting.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Cabbages, bamboo shoots, carrots... She even let us have flat peaches for dessert.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): She also asked me to deliver that thing you ordered separately.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): H-Huh?!
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Oh, Rumi. I told her that I would go pick it up later!
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Ahem... You didn't see what was inside, did you, Kokona?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): No. President Rumi said not to look at it and that it's not meant for children.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): That's good to hear.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Great job, Kokona. I'll bring out the snacks soon, so please gather the students.
- Plum Blossom Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): Kokona, Kokona. What did you get from the Black Tortoise Promenade?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Don't just call me Kokona. Call me Instructor Kokona!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I was just picking up food for everyone. Your snacks will be ready soon, so just be patient, okay?
- Plum Blossom Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): What about that bag you gave to Shun? I heard something rattling inside. Is it also food?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): No, that's not for eating. It's a toy for grown-ups.
- Plum Blossom Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): A grown-up toy? I wanna see too! What kind of toy is it?!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): No! I told you that it's for grown-ups and grown-ups ONLY. Something really bad will happen if kids see it.
- Plum Blossom Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): Why? Is it a scary toy?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Y-Yeah... It's super scary! It's all spiky and bumpy! It's really ugly-looking too, so kids cry the second they see it.
- Plum Blossom Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): (Scary...)
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Oh, and this is a secret, okay? You can never tell anyone you know about it.
- Plum Blossom Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): Yeah, okay.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Good. Aren't you a good listener?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I'm going to go help Shun with the snacks, so sit tight just a little longer.
- Plum Blossom Student B (Plum Blossom Garden): Did you see those bags Kokona and Shun had a little while ago? What was inside?
- Plum Blossom Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): Yeah, Kokona brought food, and Shun's bag had a grown-up toy inside.
- Plum Blossom Student B (Plum Blossom Garden): A grown-up toy? What's that?
- Plum Blossom Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): It's something that Shun secretly bought from the Black Tortoise Promenade. It's an ugly toy with spiky thorns...
- Plum Blossom Student B (Plum Blossom Garden): Scary...
- Plum Blossom Student C (Plum Blossom Garden): What's going on? Are you telling a scary story?
- Plum Blossom Student B (Plum Blossom Garden): Yeah. This is a secret, but Shun...
