# Group Story / RedwinterSecretary / 1

> Red Winter Academy Presidential Office
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Hmm...
- Marina (Red Winter Office): May I assist you, President Cherino?
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Have you ever heard of a character named Peroro?
- Marina (Red Winter Office): Peroro?
- Marina (Red Winter Office): Ah, yes. I remember a few students had a strange bird plush with that name.
- Marina (Red Winter Office): It was drooling and flapping its wings. I thought it unfit for a student of Red Winter, so I confiscated all of them. Is there something the matter?
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): You see, I went to downtown Kivotos last weekend, and a limited edition Peroro was being sold at an auction.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Everyone was dying to get this oversized, ugly, useless stuffed character plush. They were even willing to pay double for it!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Millennium, Gehenna, and even the highbrow Trinity students were all desperate for Peroro.
- Marina (Red Winter Office): Haha! How pathetic! The students at those schools are brainwashed by the freedom of information!
- Marina (Red Winter Office): Red Winter Federal Academy controls such silly media in order to keep our students from being manipulated in the same way.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Hmm. But a wise leader learns even from fools.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): I, the smart and wise Great Leader, have found inspiration from their idiocy and come up with a brilliant idea to strengthen our authority!
- Marina (Red Winter Office): Please illuminate me, Great Leader.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): It's...to bring Peroro to Red Winter Federal Academy!
- Marina (Red Winter Office): Excuse me?
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): What?! Do your ears not work, Chairman Marina?
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): I said, I want to bring Peroro to Red Winter Federal Academy.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): The bird may be a drooling dullard, but Kivotos students are undeniably obsessed with it.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): So, if we bring Peroro here, it's sure to attract applicants from all over Kivotos, right?
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): An academy's power is proportional to its enrollment... If our numbers increase because of Peroro, Red Winter Academy will turn into a super academy the likes of which Trinity and Gehenna wouldn't hold a candle to!
- Marina (Red Winter Office): However...Peroro has a rather small fan base.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): A perfect plan, Great Leader!
- Marina (Red Winter Office): Chief Secretary Tomoe?
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Promotion through mascots is propaganda 101! It's only a matter of time before students flock here if we have a charming mascot of our own!
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): You came up with such a great plan so quickly... Excellent judgment, President Cherino!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): I-Is that so?
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Ahaha! Yes, of course! Naturally I, the student council president, Beautifier of Lands, Great Leader, and Athletics Club Captain would come up with a brilliant plan!
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Peroro's snow-white feathers will match perfectly with the Red Winter Academy's brand. We could put him in a uniform and make him our PR manager.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): And adding a big white mustache will push it right over the top. What do you think? Word of Red Winter Academy's might will spread all over Kivotos.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Yes, that was my idea exactly! Excellent, Tomoe!
- Marina (Red Winter Office): But...Peroro isn't a student...or real, for that matter.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Chairman Marina, what are you mumbling about in the corner?
Please, speak up!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Or...do you have complaints about my great plan?
- Marina (Red Winter Office): O-Of course not! It's a...wonderful idea!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Isn't it? My plans are always perfect!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): The only thing we have to figure out is how to get Peroro transferred here...
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Chairwoman Marina! It'll be your responsibility to transfer Peroro to Red Winter Academy!
- Marina (Red Winter Office): What?!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Are you not happy? You should be honored by the chance to execute my plan!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): I shall award you with a Golden Mustache Medal once you successfully bring Peroro to Red Winter!
- Marina (Red Winter Office): B-But how am I supposed to register a fictional character as a student?
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): The disrespect, Chairman Marina!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): I saw Peroro dancing and singing in downtown Kivotos with these very eyes!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Are you saying you don't believe the words of Red Winter Academy's president, the Beautifier of Lands, Great Leader, et cetera?
- Marina (Red Winter Office): N-No, it's not that...
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Allow me to intervene. This is written in the Red Winter Academy rules.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Red Winter Federal Academy Rules, Article 1: President Cherino is always right.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Red Winter Federal Academy Rules, Article 2: Any person who makes a statement that violates Article 1 must be purged.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): I most certainly hope you are not violating the rules, Chairman Marina.
- Marina (Red Winter Office): O-Of course not, but...
- Marina (Red Winter Office): (Th-This is bad! I'm going to be purged, at this rate!)
- Marina (Red Winter Office): (But how am I supposed to make a fictional character into a student?)
- Marina (Red Winter Office): (Hold on. I can't make the fictional Peroro real, but what if I turn something real into Peroro?)
- Marina (Red Winter Office): (Heehee... Okay! I can make this work!)
- Marina (Red Winter Office): You can count on me, President. I will bring Peroro to Red Winter Academy as you ordered!
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Excellent. I'll be counting on you, Chairman Marina.
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): *yawn* Is it time for a nap already?
- Cherino (Red Winter Office): Tomoe... I'm sleepy...
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Your bed is already prepared. Please come this way.
- Marina (Red Winter Office): Transferring Peroro to Red Winter Federal Academy...
- Marina (Red Winter Office): If this fails, I could lose my promotion... Or worse—I could get purged. I can't let that happen. This has to work. I have to prove myself.
- Marina (Red Winter Office): President Cherino is always right!
