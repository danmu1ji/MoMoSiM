# Group Story / anzenkyoku / 1

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Group_Story/anzenkyoku/1

> Valkyrie Public Safety Bureau
> On a leisurely afternoon under the warm sun...
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): *sigh* Relaxing. So relaxing...
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): There are no open cases or special requests today at all. I can even nap while I'm on the clock. This is heaven!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): I wish Kivotos could be this peaceful every day...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): No, that can't happen!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Huh? Do you not like peace, Kirino?
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Are you saying that you want Kivotos to always be in chaos, with villains committing crimes every day and the students constantly fighting amongst themselves?
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): You can be so evil sometimes, Kirino...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Of course that's not what I'm saying.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): But there wouldn't be anything for us to do if it were always peaceful.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): We wouldn't be able to show our worth to the police school higher-ups, and we'd have to stay in the Public Safety Bureau with no hopes of ever being promoted!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): I like the Public Safety Bureau.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): It doesn't receive any dangerous or troublesome cases. All we have to do is keep the peace.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): That's my LEAST favorite thing about it!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I came to Valkyrie Police School because I wanted to catch bad guys like the cops I admired. But all I'm doing is helping lost children and directing traffic at crosswalks.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I know it's important work and has its own merits.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): And it does feel indescribably good to be thanked by a lost child, but still! It's not enough for me!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): You really are a busybody, Kirino.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Maybe it's because I'm getting older, but I always run out of energy for this job by the afternoon.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): But you're a first-year just like me, Fubuki! You need to get on your feet and be more enthusiastic!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Don't be so anxious. You said it yourself: We're first-years, so we still have plenty of time to prove our worth.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): You never know. There might be a major case that needs our help soon—
> Emergency dispatch! All Security Bureau personnel to arms. Head to the donut shop on 2nd Avenue and 3rd Street! I repeat! All Security Bureau to arms...
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): I was nearly about to blame my big mouth, but that's not us.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): And the donut shop on 2nd Avenue... Do they mean Master Donuts? That place is a classic.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I'm going to call the Security Bureau to find out what's going on!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Director, this is Kirino from the Public Safety Bureau!
- Security Director General: Yes, Kirino? What's the matter? We're busy preparing for dispatch.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I just heard the emergency call. Did something happen at the donut shop?
- Security Director General: It's not a big deal. A petty thief is holding the place up and demanding money.
- Security Director General: Your department doesn't have anything to worry—
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Let's go, Fubuki! We have to get there before the Security Bureau and stop the criminal so we can prove our worth!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Huh? But I don't wanna...
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Plus, we aren't even equipped to handle something like that—literally. We don't have the weapons, so if something goes wrong, we're going to be taken hostage too.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Why not just leave it to them so we can kick back and relax?
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Innocent civilians are being held hostage as we speak! Now isn't the time to argue about whether it's the responsibility of the Public Safety Bureau or the Security Bureau!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): And we may even receive a commendation from the higher-ups if we solve this case! Or better yet...maybe more vacation days?
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Vacation days? Now you're talking my language.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Fine. But I'm hightailing it out of there if the situation gets too dangerous.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Don't worry. Justice will always prevail no matter who stands in the way!
