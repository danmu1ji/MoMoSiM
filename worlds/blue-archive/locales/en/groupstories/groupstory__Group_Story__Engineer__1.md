> Engineering Department Workshop
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Hmm. By reducing the weight of this lower bearing connected to the drive shaft, the speed will be...
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Oh, it's lunchtime already. Utaha! You can do the engine maintenance this afternoon. Let's eat before you get your hands dirty!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Okay, give me a moment. I'm almost done welding the seams. Let me finish this, and we'll go to the cafeteria together.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): But we have soot all over our work clothes. I was so focused that I didn't even notice...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I know. It would be pretty rude of us to set foot there wearing these rags... So, should we get lunch delivered to the workshop like yesterday?
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Great idea! I've been craving greasy fast food with lots of ham and cheese.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): This calls for fast food that has a lot of variety and can be easily shared with minimal use of our hands...
- Kotori (Engineering Department): That would be pizza!
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): I agree. There's nothing like getting a pizza delivered to the workshop.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Ha. You two never get tired of pizza.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Well, I can't deny I've been craving it too. We'll get the usual pepperoni and cheese.
- Pizza Delivery Guy: Delivery from Kamehame Pizza! Did you order one pepperoni and cheese pizza?
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Yes, please put it on the table.
- Pizza Delivery Guy: Got it. Thanks for choosing Kamehame!
- Kotori (Engineering Department): *inhale* Oh, that rich, cheesy smell... I haven't even tasted it, and I'm already hungry!
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Let's dig in.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Mmm. Pepperoni pizza is the best! Nothing beats savory cheese and salty pepperoni!
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): I appreciate a good cheese pizza. The simple, light flavor of the cheese is enough for me.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Is that right? Too much cheese makes me feel kind of sick...
Kotori, could you pass the hot sauce?
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Shouldn't it be in the pizza box? It's not here.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): There's nothing but grated cheese in the box. The delivery guy must have forgotten the sauce.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): What? That's unforgivable. Everyone knows you don't forget the heat!
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Let's blow up the review section of the delivery app and teach that delivery guy a lesson!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Calm down, Kotori. We all forget things, especially when we're busy. Mistakes happen, so let's forgive him.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): There should be some hot sauce left in the club room cabinet from last time.
I can just go get it.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Huh? I was sure I left the sauce somewhere around here... Where'd it go?
- Kotori (Engineering Department): That cabinet is always full of junk. Maybe someone took it out when they were getting a tool?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Bah. Then I suppose we won't be having hot sauce with our pizza this time after all...
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): I wonder if anyone's ever made an invention that gives you sauce anytime, anywhere you want...
- Kotori (Engineering Department): ...
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Say, Utaha. We order pizza all the time. Why don't we modify a bottle of hot sauce to make it wearable?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): A wearable sauce bottle? How?
- Kotori (Engineering Department): For example...we could install hot sauce dispensers on our personal weapons so that we'll always have them on hand!
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): You would risk shooting hot sauce instead of bullets just for some pizza?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): That's the most ridiculous and frivolous idea I've ever heard.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): ...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): ...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Let's get to work.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): (nod)
- Kotori (Engineering Department): I knew it was a good idea!
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Let's start by reverse engineering the hot sauce bottle!
> That is how the Engineering Department came to the absurd conclusion to build a gun with a hot sauce dispenser...
