> Somewhere Outside Gehenna
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): *pant* *pant* Did we lose them?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): I think we're good. Not even the head prefect is stubborn enough to chase us this deep into the woods! ☆
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Sheesh. All this fuss just because we lit up that slop bucket of a dining hall? Doesn't the Gehenna Prefect Team have anything better to do with their time?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Actually, no. That's...exactly what the Prefect Team is supposed to do.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): See here, Junko. If you saw water gushing out of a tap into an overflowing sink and there was no one around, what would you do?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I guess...I'd shut it off?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Exactly. That's precisely the urge I get when I see an eatery that's no better than a feeding trough.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): You can stop there. I'll never understand the way you think.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Wait. I haven't seen Izumi in a while. Wasn't she behind us?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Izumi fell behind ages ago. I'm sure Hina nabbed her by now.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Are you kidding me?! That's serious! Why didn't you say something sooner?!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): When Izumi fell behind, she was yelling out something about conviction. Primal scream of a determined soul, that sort of thing.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): C-Conviction? N-No way! Could she have...for us...?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Her sacrifice will never be forgotten. What exactly did Izumi say?
- Izumi (Gourmet Research Society): N-No! You traitors! Don't leave me behind!
- Izumi (Gourmet Research Society): *slips* Huh?!
> Screen shakes
> *slam*
- Izumi (Gourmet Research Society): Ah! I'll get you for this. I swear, I'll get you back if it's the last thing I do!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): ...Something like that.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): ...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Heehee! ☆
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): *sniff* Do you smell that? Something smells absolutely scrumptious.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Here? In the middle of the park?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Whatever it is, its aroma is out of this world!
*sniff* This way!
> *whoosh*
> *rummages*
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Found it! It's coming from these mushrooms!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Oh, my. Is that...?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Truffles! These are truffles!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Trupples?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): They're also known as black diamonds. These mushrooms are the rarest of the rare!
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): In fact, this delicacy ranks higher than even the most delectable of high-end tunas, the Kin Maguro!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Whoooa! You don't say?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): And this rich and inimitable aroma is what makes it so unique in particular.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): We should consider ourselves lucky just to be breathing it in.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): I've heard about how rare they are. People train hogs with keen senses of smell just to find them.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Who knew Junko had such a talented snout?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): One more backhanded compliment like that and I'll bury you where I found these truffles!
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): We don't have time for this. How a truffle is handled within the first five minutes of it being harvested is vital in preserving its essence.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): We need a clean piece of cloth or fabric.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Hold on. Do you expect any of us to have that when we've been sprinting down this dirt road? We're all caked in dirt.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Would something like this work?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): What in the...?! Is that a pair of underwear?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Brand-new and never worn! Not to mention, 100 percent silk of only the highest quality! It may be underwear, but what's the difference between this and a handkerchief?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Nothing, I guess, but...what do you think, Pres? Are you good with this?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Absolutely. In fact, I'm relieved. I never imagined Akari's neuroticism would come in handy like this.
> *fwip*
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Voila. Now all that's left to do is head back and cook these truffles.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): No. I suppose we should rescue Izumi first. Even a single member of the club being absent when we taste such a prized luxury is unacceptable.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Well, then! Why don't we go put those Prefect tightwads in their place and save Izumi?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Love and violence! It's what friendship is all about, ladies! ☆
- Hina (Prefect Team): Who is it you're going to put in their place?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): What in the...?! What's the Prefect Team doing here?!
> *whoosh*
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Oh, dear... We're surrounded.
- Hina (Prefect Team): *sigh* I'm tired of all this running. End them, girls.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): (Wha...?!)
- Hina (Prefect Team): ...You're lucky I don't want to deal with the cleanup. Light them up, but leave them breathing.
- Iori (Prefect Team): *nod*
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Please don't resist. There's no point.
> *pop* *pop* *pop* *tat* *tat*
> (Everyone is promptly defeated and arrested.)
