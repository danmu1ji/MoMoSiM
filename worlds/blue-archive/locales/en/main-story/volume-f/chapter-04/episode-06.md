The Ark's self-destruct sequence begins anew. As everyone prepares to escape, students gather to help Sensei. The other Shiroko faces Sensei in a final fight over the fate of the world. ;Phrenapates Showdown 1 Arona tells Sensei about meeting another Shiroko and her encounter with Phrenapates. ;Phrenapates Showdown 2-3 The other Shiroko initiates combat once again. Nameless Priests approach Shiroko in her final moments before the Chroma's light reaches her and order her to defeat Sensei, who visits her despite being injured. But she is unable to shoot Sensei. Sensei responds to their final wish.
