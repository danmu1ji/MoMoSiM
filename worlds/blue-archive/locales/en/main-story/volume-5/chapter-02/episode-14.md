Azami, who led the Hyakkaryouran to the mansion, reveals that she's actually a snake charmer, and the Phantom Artist of Nature's Beauty. Azami uses her "Toutei's Phantom Illustrations" tome to neutralize Renge and Kikyou. As she struggles against the Artifact Spirits that suddenly materialize, Nagusa realizes that Ayame has disappeared. The new Ayame, who appeared with Kokuriko in the midst of the Artifact Spirits, wears the same cold expression she had when they last met in the snowfield.
