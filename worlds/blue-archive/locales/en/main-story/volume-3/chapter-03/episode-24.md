Official English episode synopsis (not the full scene dialogue).

Saori is defeated, and Azusa finally collapses from exhaustion. Atsuko then appears to personally admit defeat. Then, as if he had been waiting to strike, Maestro emerges and demands the doctrines hidden within Trinity's depths. Sensei pulls out their card to retaliate. Once the battle is over, the Arius Squad vanishes before everyone's eyes and disappears into the darkness.
