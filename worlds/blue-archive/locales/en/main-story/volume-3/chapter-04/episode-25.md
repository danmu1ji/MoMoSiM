Official English episode synopsis (not the full scene dialogue).

After successfully rescuing Atsuko, Saori tells Sensei that she will pay for all her sins. However, Sensei tells her to focus on living her life and leaves to find Mika. Meanwhile, Mika is fighting against Barbara and the Council, but she reaches her limit and is about to give up. At that moment, Sensei appears to Mika's surprise. Sensei is furious with Barbara and the Council for hurting their student. Just as the Arius students are in a panic because they can't get in touch with Beatrice, Trinity forces suddenly pour out from the catacombs.
