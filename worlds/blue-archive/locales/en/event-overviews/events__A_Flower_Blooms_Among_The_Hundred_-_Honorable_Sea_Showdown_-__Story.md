The Hyakkaryouran's Social Outing
Kikyou somehow ends up attending the Hyakkiyako Water Festival with the Hyakkaryouran. Can she keep her hydrophobia a secret?
The Hyakkiyako Water Festival Begins!
The first round is the water gun gauntlet. Let the games begin!
Round 1: Water Gun Gauntlet
The water gun gauntlet begins, and the Hyakkaryouran is immediately ambushed! How will they overcome this crisis?
The Hyakkaryouran advances to the next round thanks to some much-needed help from old friends.
Round 2: Jet Boat Rampage
In this game, the Hyakkaryouran must stay on a rampaging jet boat. The Council braces one another and navigates through the madness. Will they all make it to the next round?
Round 3: Inflatable Obstacle Race
While it was a close call, the Hyakkaryouran successfully advances. The next game that awaits them is a water obstacle course.
On the Brink of Elimination
The entire Hyakkaryouran is eliminated after trying to save Kikyou from the water. But hope isn't lost—there's still the revival match.
Revival Match: Beach Volleyball
Kikyou teams up with Renge for the beach volleyball round. She's determined to make up for the Hyakkaryouran's elimination.
Hyakkaryouran vs. Hyakkaryouran
The Hyakkaryouran meet each other in the revival round finals. Renge and Kikyou face off against Nagusa and Yukari. Two must be eliminated. Which team will reign supreme?
Mutual Pride
Kikyou conquers the revival match and Renge gives her a swimming lesson. The final event, a Buoy Push Game, is announced—and the competitors are Kikyou and Renge.
Final Round: Knock-Out
The Hyakkaryouran hostility continues! Kikyou and Renge fight a heated duel of honor and friendship. And the winner is...
The End of the Finals
Renge and Izuna face each other in the finals. Wakamo and her Thugs crash the volatile match.
It turns out Wakamo just wanted to join the festivities! A special round is held for Wakamo.
A Fight You Can't Back Down From
Wakamo, who wanted to prove her undeniable worth to Sensei in the audience, faces Kikyou. Who will emerge victorious in this bizarre battle?
The Story's End
Ultimately, Renge and Izuna become co-champions of the Hyakkiyako Water Festival. But the greatest winner can't be forgotten. With her friends, Kikyou finally faced her fears, and is finally ready to enjoy summer—and the sea—with the rest of the Hyakkaryouran.Category:Event stories
