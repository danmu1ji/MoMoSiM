A Letter from a Faraway Place
Cherino hears from Tomoe about the exchange meeting with Shanhaijing.
Wash Your Hands, Spruce Yourself Up
Kisaki decides to call Sensei for advice regarding Shanhaijing and Red Winter's exchange meeting.
The Divided Waters Unite
After a bit of a commotion, the visiting students from Red Winter change into traditional Shanhaijing clothing, and the exchange meeting begins.
So the King Says Farewell to the Lady
Red Winter's visiting students enjoy a Peking Theatre performance and meet with the President of the Peking Opera Club, Kaguya.
Tour the World and Gaze at the River
The visiting students go sightseeing in Shanhaijing. While they do so, the mood at Shanhaijing begins to shift.
The Clouds Don't Know of Being Torn Asunder
While the visiting students wrap up their day, Kaguya has a secret meeting with one of the Seven Prisoners, Shintani Kai.
They Claimed the Snow-Covered Bamboo Bent
Kisaki visits the Bamboo Forest with the Red Winter students, but her condition suddenly worsens.
Deep in the Yuzhou Forest
Kisaki tells Sensei about her secret. Kaguya tells Kisaki that there will be a new Peking opera performed the next day.
Listen to the Doll
At the end of a new Peking opera performance, Kaguya denounces Kisaki as president of Shanhaijing.
After You Race Up the Mountain
Mina and Marina combine their strengths to stop the Peking Opera Club.
There are too many members of the Peking Opera Club. Genryumon, caught off guard, are unable to respond properly.
Embracing the Sky from the Foothills
Cherino and the Secretariat take measures themselves to fight off the Peking Opera Club.
The Peking Opera Club's denouncement fails, and Kaguya is jailed.
When Companions Separate
The exchange meeting ends safely, and everything seems to go back to normal for now. But Kai's plot hasn't been completely thwarted yet...
