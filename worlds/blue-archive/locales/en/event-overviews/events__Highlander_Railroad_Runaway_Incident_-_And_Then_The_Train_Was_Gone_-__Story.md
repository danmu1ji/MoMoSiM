Girl! Take the Train to Hell
Like speeding trains, attacks pour in from everywhere. Just what is going on?!
Ordinary Mission
Hikari and Nozomi take on a freight transportation task. Aoba, who joined them as an engineer, sighs from seeing how loose the twins are. In midst of all this, the group runs afoul of a biker gang member, who was working as a delivery person.
In Search of a Missing Train
The freight train disappeared in the blink of an eye. The twins ask Sensei for help, and the group heads out in search of the train.
Back on the Search for a Missing Train
The train disappeared from its destination again. The group takes a bus toward the next destination.
On That Train
Apparently, the train in question had some very important cargo. It would be problematic if that information were to spread...
Vroom Vroom Race Association
Thanks to a historical special report from Kronos, everyone's eyes are on this freight train. They need to find the train before anyone else does, but first they need to settle a grudge from the biker gang.
After defeating the biker gang, the group hurries and starts the train.
Fan Club of Death
Fortunately, it seems that the crew will be able to meet the expected arrival time—that is, until a group of troublemakers, who'd heard about the train from Kronos' report, come rushing onto the scene. A frenetic chase ensues.
Trains Don't Run Alone
A new arrival appears: Iori, who was on a patrol near Gehenna. Being sick of trains already, she considers Sensei's request for help.
With Iori's assistance, the group fends off their pursuers. However, in the process, the truth about the cargo comes out...
What to Do Doesn't Change
The cargo was loaded with expensive valuables. Aoba is worried there might be a huge conspiracy behind the shipment, and the twins also share their thoughts on the matter.
Brake Down
Damaged due to the fierce battle, the brake system has malfunctioned and the train speeds have gone out of control. Not only does the operation look doomed, but everyone is in grave danger. How are they going to get out of this?
A Girl Smiles in an Unknown World
After the long journey, the group finally arrives at their destination. What's the true story behind the cargo, and all the events around it? What is the group in for next?Category:Event stories
