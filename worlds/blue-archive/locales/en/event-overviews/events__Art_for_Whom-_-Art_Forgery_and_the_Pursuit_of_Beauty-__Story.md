Beneath Unfamiliar Ceilings
Sensei wakes up with a pounding headache in a room of the Wildhunt dormitory. While there, Miyo tells them about how she wishes to disband the Free Trade Cartel.
Cloudy With a Chance of Trade Goods
Sensei learns that they ended up in the dorm after a bag fell from the sky and hit them on the head. Sensei takes the opportunity to learn more about the Free Trade Cartel.
Let's Hear You Out
Sensei observes the Free Trade Cartel's activities in person. In the process, they learn about the high-value commission that Fuyu received earlier.
The Love of Purple and Blue Light
Sensei learns that the client requested that a forgery be removed from the Wildhunt archives and replaced with the original art piece that the client has procured. Miyo accepts the request and prepares for the mission.
Alabaster Variable
The entire situation is turned on its head when the "Benevolent Thief," Akira, sends out a calling card.
Source of Color
Eri helps Miyo successfully decode half of the calling card. Miyo wastes no time in sharing the news with the other members.
Plot Changes
After a meeting, the second half of the calling card is also decoded. Miyo shares the newly modified plan.
Thieving and Smuggling Hour
The Free Trade Cartel helps the Benevolent Thief conduct her heist. Now that Akira has assisted in the reversal of the forgery, the mission is nearly concluded...
The Exchange of Truth
However, Akira successfully steals both the forgery and genuine art piece away from the Free Trade Cartel. Following this, a nearly unbelievable story unfolds...
I Don't Like This Ending
The mission was a failure and the real art piece has been taken by a seasoned thief. As a last ditch effort, Miyo proposes a final wager.
Test Results
Miyo emerges as the winner of the wager, and the client is arrested by the Prefectural Council, bringing the incident to a close.
One-Phrase Clue
Although everything seemingly ends well, the Free Trade Cartel has lost a considerable amount of money on the job. So the day ends as the Cartel frantically looks for a new commission.Category:Event stories
