No Risk, No Return
Yakumo and Takane head to Trinity to sell the Knowledge Liberation Front's "creative works."
Buy Two, Get One Free
Red Winter's creations cause a big stir at Trinity.
Cherish the Books
Nagisa rules that the illegal and disorderly "creative works" are threatening Trinity. She orders Mashiro to root out the source of these deviant works.
Sales Report
Yakumo and Takane run over their daily sales numbers and are shocked at the overwhelming profit. Meanwhile, Shimiko and Sensei assist the Justice Task Force as they investigate the source of these "creative works."
Covert Ops
Trinity students who are fans of the "creative works" provide a sales venue for Yakumo and Takane, all while evading the Justice Task Force's ongoing investigation.
Princess of the Frozen Kingdom
Yakumo and Takane continue earning astounding profits. Meanwhile, Koharu joins the "creative works" investigation.
An Empty Place
As the Justice Task Force's investigation closes in, Sensei, Shimiko, and Koharu decide on their paths forward.
One Last Chance
While evading the Justice Task Force, Yakumo and Takane find themselves hiding in Trinity's old library.
The Old Library War
Yakumo, Takane, Ui, Shimiko, Koharu, and Mashiro all meet in the library for a grand and final confrontation!
