# Secret Midnight Party ~ Oni Holds a Bell ~

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Secret_Midnight_Party_~_Oni_Holds_a_Bell_~/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

Inside the Cabinet
Trapped inside a cabinet, Koyuki and Sensei squabble. They reflect on how they got here.
Accomplice
At the end of the year, Koyuki breaks the rules again and is sent to the Self-Reflection Room. But, rather than repenting, all she thinks about is playing with Sensei. Sensei falls for her bait and soon joins her in the Self-Reflection Room.
Year End, Year Beginning
Yuuka and Noa prepare a pajama party for Koyuki. They return to Seminar and meet with Veritas for a year-end chat.
Can't Stand Another Moment
Sensei plays with Koyuki in the Self-Reflection Room, but when Yuuka and Noa pay her an unexpected visit, the two urgently look for a place to hide.
To Tell the Truth
Veritas helps Sensei as they try to escape without being caught by Yuuka and Noa.
Rattling Cabinet
Sensei and Koyuki hide to avoid the drone. But Noa slowly approaches the cabinet the two are hiding in.
Remember Me!!
Sensei and Koyuki find themselves in an old underground passage that hadn't been used in a long time. They have to fight through the Seminar drones to find a pathway out.
Sensei and Koyuki have almost successfully escaped. Unexpectedly, they end up in a dangerous situation, and Koyuki sacrifices herself for Sensei.
Privileges of the Seeker
Noa takes Sensei to the pajama party room. Everyone spends a fun holiday playing games.
One Year Ends, a New Year Begins
At night, when everyone is asleep, Sensei and Noa walk the hallway and have an unusually honest conversation.
Chase the Joker
Koyuki, who has bad luck in games, goes for a risky move to try to turn the tides. But she only makes the situation worse.
BOOM!
The students, while enjoying video games together, invite Chihiro to play a violent game. They soon see a new side to Chihiro.
The Ultimate Drink
Koyuki and Maki play around mixing drinks, but they're caught and scolded for goofing off.
Truth or Dare
Truth or Dare begins, but for some reason, Yuuka seems to be the only one receiving questions.
When Everyone's Asleep
Late at night, past bedtime, Chihiro and Noa are still awake. They end up having a reflective conversation with Sensei about the end of the year.Category:Event stories
