# Say-Bing!

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Say-Bing%21/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

Prologue
A mega water park opens for summer, and the Valkyrie Police School decides to send lifeguards for safety. However, there's an unexpected name on the roster...
Starting as a Lifeguard!
Deputy Director Konoka made the decision to assign Kanna to the water park dispatch team. As a result, Kirino, Fubuki, and Kanna begin their lifeguard mission together.
I'll Take You to the Water Slide!
The first location the trio is assigned to is the water slide, but Kanna makes a mistake.
Playing with Children!
The next location is the children's pool. Following Kirino's principles of public service, Kanna rescues a child guest who has fallen into the water, but the child ends up crying...
Marooned at the Wave Pool!
The trio is then assigned to work at the wave pool. After Kanna is accidentally swept away by the waves, she encounters an unidentified monster underwater and helps evacuate the area. And who else might be watching...?
I Don't See the Monster...
Kanna claims she saw an alligator, but the water park owner is skeptical. Following Sensei's request, security guards move out to search the wave pool.
An Alligator Appears!
Working at the food court, where she mulls over her repeated mistakes, Kanna unexpectedly comes across a familiar face.
Where Did You Come From, Mechagator?
The Mechagator finally emerges. Kanna, Fubuki, and Kirino unite to tackle the monster and put a stop to its rampage across the water park.
The Mechagator escapes, and the investigation reveals that it originated from outside the park.
Interrogating the Suspect!
The trio interrogate Rabu and the Helmet Gangsters, who are the primary suspects in the release of the Mechagator. However, Rabu maintains that they're not the culprits.
Suppressing the Alligator!
After the battle, the Mechagator attempts to escape, heading directly toward the children's pool. As everyone watches in shock, Kanna steps in to take action...
Thanks to Kanna's efforts, she, Fubuki, Kirino, and Rabu triumph in their battle against the Mechagator. To uncover its origins, they decide to open it up and investigate.
I Did It as a Lifeguard!
Utaha and Chihiro suddenly appear. They reveal that the Mechagator was a project from the Engineering Department, and ended up in the water park due to an accident during the development process. They also reveal why it stopped moving in front of Kanna...
Epilogue
Konoka welcomes the trio back as they return from their assignment. She has prepared a gift to show her appreciation for their hard work.
The Engineering Department's Mistake 1
The Engineering Department, which had been developing a flower-arranging robot, receives a request to build a Marine Leisure Robot.
The Engineering Department's Mistake 2
On the day of the Mechagator's trial run, it unexpectedly malfunctions and goes berserk.
Komakaze Rabu's Secret Vacation 1
While Rabu and the Helmet Gang Members are going about their day, they receive news about the "Helmet Gangster Grand Alliance."
Komakaze Rabu's Secret Vacation 2
Rabu opts to skip the Grand Alliance Gathering, not wanting to leave her subordinates behind. Instead, she chooses to enjoy some summer fun at the water park.
The Public Peace Bureau's Director-less Day
After dispatching Kanna to the water park, Konoka finds herself alone in the office. Unable to tolerate the boredom, she suggests a combat swimming training session.Category:Event stories
