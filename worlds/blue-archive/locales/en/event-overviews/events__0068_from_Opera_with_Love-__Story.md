Prologue
Problem Solver 68 takes on a job at the Opera House and runs into Sensei, who is there at the same time.
Here for Work
Sensei decides to visit the opera house. Aru approaches Sensei with the offer of a tour. Sensei senses something amiss about Aru's detailed knowledge of the opera house, but Aru claims she's simply there for "work."
Haruka, Mutsuki, and Kayoko narrowly escape an explosion. Sensei intervenes, and learns what really brought Problem Solver 68 to the opera house.
More Hard-boiled
Problem Solver 68's mission is to kidnap Don Arancino, the head of the gang alliance. After learning the truth about their mission, Sensei decides to not get in their way.
Monopolizing the Prey
Unknown to Aru...Haruka, Mutsuki, and Kayoko have found the operatives responsible for the bombs in the opera house. After learning that there are more enemies afoot, Problem Solver 68 decides to search the opera house with Sensei.
After searching the opera house, Problem Solver 68 obtains vital information regarding Don Arancino's arrival.
Didn't Expect That Much
Problem Solver 68 notices there's something off with the opera house audience as Don Arancino makes his entrance.
It turns out that all the opera house's guests are out for Don Arancino's head! Don Arancino flees from the operatives and calls in his "back-up plan." Problem Solver 68 meets this "back-up" face to face.
Skillful Yet Clumsy
Don Arancino's "back-up plan" is none other than Joumae Saori, working as a part time bodyguard. Saori secures Don Arancino and retreats. Problem Solver 68 gives chase.
Fearing their retreat is taking too long, Saori finds a more efficient method of transportation.
An Absurd Misunderstanding
Problem Solver 68 chases Saori. Aru frets, as they should be helping Sensei, not the other way around. Sensei encourages her, reminding Aru that students are more important than work.
Saori finally gets in contact with the Arancino gang, only for them to turn their guns on her instead.
An Absurd Conclusion
Seeing that Saori's been played, Aru helps her battle the Arancino gang.
During the battle, Saori takes cover behind a dumpster, which was filled with some very dangerous garbage. A grenade makes it way to her location.
One of Those Days
Sensei, Saori, and Problem Solver 68 enjoy a meal at a food stall, swapping stories in the dusky night.Category:Event stories
