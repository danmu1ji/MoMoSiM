# Trip-Trap-Train

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Trip-Trap-Train/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

Transport Train Car
The Arancino Family, a gangster organization, detains a suspected terrorist from a special convoy.
3:10 to Gehenna
Ichika is tasked with helping Sensei retrieve a precious Trinity relic from a ruin excavation. She's excited about the opportunity to work with Sensei and takes the job in stride.
Everything Is Under Control!
The relic ends up on a train bound for Gehenna, and Sensei and Ichika scramble to hop aboard. However, their fellow passengers don't exactly give them a warm welcome...
A sudden explosion rocks the convoy car attached to the train, and a mysterious figure reveals herself through the smoke.
To the Front
Kasumi suggests a partnership with Sensei and Ichika. Before they can respond, the train's crew and the Arancino Family attack. Ichika and Sensei are forced to join Kasumi.
Kasumi explains her plan to Sensei and Ichika and tells them to head to the front car so that they can escape with Ichika's bag.
The Good, The Bad, The Trinity
The Gehenna Prefect Team and Iori board the train and are met by Ichika, Sensei, and Kasumi.
Iori, Ichika, and Kasumi face off. Kasumi has a trick up her sleeve to corner Iori.
Express Train to Hell
Using the relic as leverage, Kasumi demands that Ichika attack Iori. While Iori and Kasumi are wrapped up in their bickering, Ichika loses her patience and goes on a rampage.
Effort over Talent
The relic is broken. Ichika blames herself, but Sensei manages to comfort her. Unfortunately, she faces another problem...
Declaration of War
Back at Trinity, Sensei and Ichika apologize, but Nagisa says it's not a big deal and explains why the relic was needed.
