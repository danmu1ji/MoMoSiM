# Bunny Chaser Aboard

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Bunny_Chaser_Aboard/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

C&C's New Member
Sensei and the members of C&C are given a mission by Seminar. They are to infiltrate the ship of another school to apprehend a Millennium troublemaker.
Bein' Smart Part
C&C and Sensei infiltrate the Golden Fleece cruise ship of the Odyssey Maritime School to capture and bring back codename White Rabbit. They're discovered almost immediately, and chaos ensues.
C&C hides in a guest room to avoid the bunny girl guards in hot pursuit. They change from their maid uniforms into their bunny girl outfits and officially begin the mission.
A Different Perspective Part
C&C and Sensei infiltrate the systems office to take control of the surveillance system, but old habits die hard, and they end up blowing it sky high.
C&C find themselves in a pickle after blowing up the systems office. Akane is beating herself up when Sensei proposes a Plan B.
Ruthless Interrogation
C&C wake up the kidnapped bunny girl and start interrogating her. Karin takes the lead, but the guard faints after being tickled for too long.
Everything as It Was
C&C busts onto the scene as usual when their best-laid plans fail. They spot White Rabbit and succeed in getting close to her but blow their cover at the last moment.
Problem Solving, the C&C Way Part
Neru and Sensei are thrown into the ship's holding cell, and White Rabbit mocks them. Akane and Karin soon follow but also as prisoners.
White Rabbit is smug and triumphant as Neru's spirits are down. However, the tables turn when Asuna becomes a VIP, and C&C resumes its pursuit of White Rabbit.
Hunting Rabbits Part
White Rabbit howls as Neru cheerfully hunts her down. Sensei and the other members stand by silently as they watch the scene.
White Rabbit attempts to escape the ship on a drone but is sniped down by Karin and finally arrested. C&C has completed another mission, albeit roughly and messily.Category:Event stories
