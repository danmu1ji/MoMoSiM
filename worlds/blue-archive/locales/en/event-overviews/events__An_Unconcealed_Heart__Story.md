# An Unconcealed Heart

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/An_Unconcealed_Heart/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

Attack of the Ninjas!
One day, the Ninjutsu Research Club suddenly visits Sensei at Schale. They want Sensei to help them in their mission to become an official club. As Michiru is trying to convince Sensei, Kaho from the Yin-Yang Club shows up at Schale.
Start of the Exchange Meeting
An exchange meeting is being held between Hyakkiyako and Gehenna Academy, and Niya wants Sensei to help her just in case. Sensei agrees on the condition that the Ninjutsu Research Club be recognized as an official club and Sensei, Michiru, Tsukuyo, and Izuna decide to help with the exchange.
Big Girls Don't Cry
The members of the Ninjutsu Research Club start to prepare for the Princess Waraku performance for the exchange meeting. Meanwhile, Sensei helps Tsukuyo out of a pickle with a bunch of thugs.
After defeating the thugs, Tsukuyo opens up and tells Sensei about what she's been thinking. The performance of Princess Waraku is quickly approaching.
When I Say Ninja, You Say Princess!
Just before the performance of Princess Waraku, highlight of the exchange meeting, Ibuki from the Pandemonium Society shows up instead of Chise, who was originally the lead role. Ibuki convinces everyone that it was unavoidable. Suddenly, the Mouryo attack.
The lead role of the show, Ibuki, is kidnapped by Mouryo, and the exchange meeting is on the verge of disaster. To prevent this, Sensei, the Ninjutsu Research Club, and Iroha go on the move to find Ibuki.
The First Obstacle: The Face and the Bodyguard
Sensei, the Ninjutsu Research Club, and Iroha chase the Mouryo to rescue Ibuki. But the Festival Operations Department stands in their way, claiming that they are the first gatekeepers of the performance.
Sensei, the Ninjutsu Research Club, and Iroha defeat the Festival Operations Department, pass the trial, and continue their pursuit of Mouryo.
The Second Obstacle: The Sleeping Beauty
Sensei, the Ninjutsu Research Club, and Iroha are still chasing the Mouryo. But Tsubaki, the Sleeping Beauty, the second gatekeeper, stands in their way.
Everyone successfully passes Tsubaki's trial and resumes chasing the Mouryo.
The Third Obstacle: The Mind Reader
Sensei, the Ninjutsu Research Club, and Iroha continue to chase the Mouryo. This time the third gatekeeper and self-proclaimed mind reader, Mimori, stands in their way.
The Ninjutsu Research Club successfully overcomes Mimori's trial in the most extraordinary turn of events. Mimori says something to Michiru on their way out, but the members go after the Mouryo without understanding the real meaning of her words.
The Fourth Obstacle: The Trick House
The last trial in the way of the Ninjutsu Research Club is the Trick House, where Kaede is the gatekeeper. When everyone is united to face the trial, Michiru, who acts hastily out of urgency, gets caught in the Trick House's trap along with Sensei.
Michiru cooperates with Sensei and successfully escapes the Trick House. The Ninjutsu Research Club passes the trial, but Kaho realizes what happened and appears before them.
What Really Stands in Our Way
Understanding the situation, Kaho announces that the Yin-Yang Club will handle of the case. Michiru is almost convinced, but Tsukuyo and Izuna insist it's the Ninjutsu Research Club that promised to save Ibuki and decide to keep fighting.
With Sensei's help, the Ninjutsu Research Club succeeds in defeating Kaho, and Tsukuyo convinces Michiru they should rescue Ibuki together. Then, Iroha and Kaede appear with a tank.
A Ninja Girl's Code
To evade the pursuit of the Ninjutsu Research Club, the Mouryo destroy a bridge. While everyone is panicking, Iroha leads the charge and successfully jumps the tank over the collapsed bridge with everyone.
Finally, Michiru succeeds in forcing the Mouryo into a corner. Despite her initial hesitation, when Michiru learns that Ibuki has been waiting for the Ninjutsu Research Club, she boldly rushes in to save Ibuki.
After the Festival
After the successful exchange meeting, everything goes back to normal. Then Niya appears in front of Sensei and confesses that she plotted everything to see Sensei's skills, and Sensei thoroughly scolds Niya.Category:Event stories
