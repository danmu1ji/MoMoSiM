# Operation record of No. 227 Hot Spring Resort

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Operation_record_of_No._227_Hot_Spring_Resort/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

A Day in the Life of Spec Ops No. 227
Nodoka and Shigure stumble upon a hot spring while uprooting tree trunks for firewood. Gehenna's Hot Springs Department visits the Former Institute to build a huge spa resort before disappearing.
Unauthorized Hot Spring
Suffering from a lack of sleep due to repeated earthquakes, Cherino indignantly heads to the Former Institute with her tank in tow upon hearing that a new hot springs resort has been built at the Former Institute.
Welcome to Hot Springs Resort No. 227!
Cherino's party is flabbergasted by the massive size of Hot Springs Resort No. 227. Now acting as the hostess of the hot springs, Nodoka offers a bribe to Cherino to win her favor and guides Sensei to a special hot spring.
A Suspicious Gaze
Sensei notices a suspicious gaze in the bath. It turns out to be the Knowledge Liberation Front's Meru, who says she visited in search of inspiration for her fan book. Displeased by this behavior, Nodoka tries to kick Meru out.
A Suspicious Deal
Meru proposes a secret deal to Nodoka which she accepts, bringing the fight to a dramatic end. Afterward, Nodoka takes Sensei on a tour of the garden.
Labor for the Labor Party!
Sensei and Nodoka head to the entrance of the hot springs resort to find the Labor Party students staging a protest. The Labor Party is unhappy the resort was built by the Hot Springs Department and wants it demolished. Nodoka resists, and a fight ensues.
Mors for the Labor Party!
Shigure offers her home-brewed mors to the Labor Party in an attempt to open conversation. Beguiled by Shigure's delicious mors and sweet talk, the Labor Party heads over to enjoy a relaxing spa bath.
The Perfect Prefect Team Vacation!
Sensei runs into Chinatsu relaxing in a massage chair in the lounge. Chinatsu warns Sensei about the Hot Springs Department's conspiracy.
The Hot Springs Department's Conspiracy
Sensei and Cherino meet on the walking trail. Cherino wants to award the Hot Springs Department with a medal. A fight soon breaks out when the Hot Springs Department reveals their intention to destroy the Red Winter district.
Tear It Down!
The Hot Springs Department's scheme is stopped in its tracks, but Nodoka is unable to accept the closing of Hot Springs Resort No. 227. The Labor Party joins the fray, and a battle ensues between the students of the Secretariat and those of the Spec Ops.
Unchanging Friendship
Nodoka cries saying that she doesn't want to return to her old life at the Former Institute. Shigure comforts Nodoka, but then the Hot Springs Department reappears and blows Hot Springs Resort No. 227 to smithereens.
A Day in the Life of Red Winter Academy
