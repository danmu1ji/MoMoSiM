# New Year March

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/New_Year_March/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

New Year's Resolution
It's just before the new year, and Maki, Kotama, and Hare are immersed in their video games. When the club members don't respond to her calls, Chihiro shows up at the club room and chastises them for their lazy antics. She then makes plans for everyone to go see the New Year's sunrise.
An Unexpected Camp
Maki and the group reluctantly gather in front of Millennium Academy. To their surprise, Chihiro announces that the plan is to go camping. Chihiro inspects everyone's packs to find...that none of the items they've brought are conducive to being in the outdoors.
Pitch the Tent
Veritas members arrive at the camp site. Unlike Chihiro, who's very motivated, the other members have a hard time doing outdoor activities for the first time in...almost ever. After setting up a tent, the members split up to enjoy some free time, and Chihiro begins reading with Sensei.
Boredom Kills Hacker
The Veritas members don't return after a long time, so Chihiro and Sensei set out to find them. While looking, they run into Hare, who's suffering from caffeine withdrawal. After sharing coffee with Hare, Sensei and Chihiro find out where Maki is. When they arrive at the cave she's in, the pair find Maki creating a huge work of art.
Dancing with a Bear
Meanwhile, Kotama, while recording environmental sounds deep in the forest, encounters members of the Athletics Training Club on special winter training. While talking with the Training Club members about the value of outdoor experiences, Chihiro warns them that they might encounter a bear preparing for hibernation... Then a bear really appears.
Dinner Prep
After returning to the campsite, the Veritas members prepare for an early dinner. While Chihiro gets a fire going, Maki, Kotama, and Hare clumsily prepare the ingredients.
Chicken Hot Pot for the Soul
Chihiro cooks up a chicken stew for the Veritas members. The rest of them add their prepared ingredients to the pot and share a meal together. After finishing, Sensei hears Hare's and Kotama's honest impressions about camping in front of the bonfire.
The First Sight
The next morning, Sensei and the Veritas members wake up early and begin hiking through the dark to see the sunrise. They run into Sumire while hiking, and she explains it might be difficult to see the sunrise because it's cloudy. The Veritas members are still unusually motivated to keep going. When they arrive at the top, the group finally sees a beautiful sunrise.
The Veritas members return after seeing the sunrise and find a number of reporters gathered around the camp. They find out that the murals Maki painted in the cave were mistaken for ancient artwork. It takes a while for Sensei and Chihiro to sort out the misunderstanding.
Short-Lived Resolution
Chihiro visits the club room for the first time a week into the new year. She immediately sees that the Veritas members are just as focused on their games as before. Angry that they learned nothing from their trip, she gives them all a severe lecture.Category:Event stories
