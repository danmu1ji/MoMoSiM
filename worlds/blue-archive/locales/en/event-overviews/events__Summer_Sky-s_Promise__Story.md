# Summer Sky's Promise

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Summer_Sky%27s_Promise/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

Too Bad, So Sad
Ichika and Kasumi reunite after their ill-fated encounter. When Ichika puts her pride aside and asks for help, Kasumi is receptive.
It's Friday
Mika, sensing Nagisa's fatigue, prepares a vacation at the Tea Party Private Beach. As she inspects the location with Hasumi and Ichika, they encounter an unwelcome intruder...
Hot Spring Crisis
The intruder is Megu from the Hot Springs Department. Hasumi, Ichika, and Mika somehow stop Megu from installing explosives, but, given her explosive reputation, they find it safest to chase her off.
The group successfully ousts the Hot Springs Department and decides to embrace their leisure time.
My Way Highway
Nagisa and Seia arrive at the Private Beach. Mika keeps up the act, but then learns that her perfectly planned lunch has been stolen. Mika tries to salvage the barbecue, all while dodging Nagisa's suspicions.
We Do Not Negotiate With Gourmets
Sensei introduces Mika to the Gourmet Research Society. After hearing her heartfelt request, the Society provides her with delicious ingredients. However, contrary to Mika's worries, Nagisa isn't suspicious about lunch being delayed...
Never Carpe Diem
Even after numerous catastrophes, Mika and Ichika manage to execute the plan. However, right as they finish their prep work and return to the group, a mysterious tremor collapses the villa.
The Devil Always Asks
Mika's on the verge of despair, so Ichika asks the Hot Springs Department to rebuild the villa. Kasumi, despite sneering through Ichika's plea, agrees to help for some reason.
Our Saturday Night
Thanks to the Hot Springs Department's help, the villa is quickly rebuilt. The group successfully wraps up the day and gathers for an afterparty that night. The Hot Springs Department mentions that the villa collapsed due to an underground water vein. Doesn't that mean...? Yes. It was a hot spring the whole time.
A Midsummer Night's Tea Party
To Mika's shock, Nagisa apologizes for her mistakes. Nagisa tells Mika that she trusts her, and Mika bursts into tears. The two share an honest conversation by the evening sea. Later that night, Seia visits Sensei and reflects on the Tea Party's past and present.
After Saturday Night
After the fun vacation, the Tea Party members return to their everyday lives and welcome Sensei as a guest. However, Kasumi from the Hot Springs Department finds a private hideout where she can be alone...
Sun, Sand, and Shenanigans
Nagisa tells Ichika of the Justice Task Force about the Private Beach and how it needs security. She's sure that this will be an easy and relaxing patrol mission... No, it needs to be!Category:Event stories
