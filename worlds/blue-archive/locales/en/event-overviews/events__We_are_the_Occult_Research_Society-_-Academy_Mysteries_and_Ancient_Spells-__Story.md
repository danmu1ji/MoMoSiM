Prophecy
A dark shadow makes eerie predictions. What could be waiting ahead?
Destined Reunion
Sensei has an unexpected reunion with Tsumugi, who invites them to tour Wildhunt Art Academy.
Wonderland of Hopes and Dreams
Sensei meets Tsumugi's roommates at Wildhunt, the spirited members of the Occult Society. They offer to guide Sensei around the school.
Occult in Art
The Occult Society encounters a devil statue in the art gallery that seems to move on it's own. What's going on?
The Bearer of the Voice...
A statue on the street seems to speak suspiciously. Who could possibly be providing its voice?
Something Accursed Appears
The girls commit themselves to summoning a demon, convinced that everything that has occurred so far has some occult connection. What they summon, however, isn't a wayward demon, but the Prefectural Council.
Each Person's Dream
Sensei and the Occult Society find themselves locked in an attic as punishment for the trouble they caused. While there, Sensei listens to them voice their dreams and aspirations.
Proof of the Legend
After being released from the attic, Sensei and the others analyze why they failed to summon anything. In the process, they learn that by connecting the academy's old facilities, they will complete a magic circle said to fulfil any wish.
Meeting
Sensei and the students decide to complete the magic circle and gather the materials needed through secret meetings.
The Operation Begins
As the magic circle reaches completion, the Prefectural Council appears again. The Society must lose them before they get into big trouble!
Right as the Prefectural Council surrounds them, a bell that normally should remain silent rings out ominously.
Wish-Fulfilling Magic
Rena, who said she would make a solo getaway, reveals herself as the bell ringer. As the Prefectural Council is thrown into chaos, the magic circle completes.
The magic circle is finally complete. Although no wishes are granted, something magical indeed happens.
