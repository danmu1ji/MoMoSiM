# Dragon & Tortoise ~ Working Together for a Better Future ~

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Dragon_%26_Tortoise_~_Working_Together_for_a_Better_Future_~/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

Welcome Guest, Joyful Hospitality
While working like any other day, Sensei receives a special invitation from Rumi, president of the Black Tortoise Promenade. What awaits them is a delicacy never experienced elsewhere.
Rare Delicacies, Tasty Treats
Sensei receives great hospitality from Rumi and the Black Tortoise Promenade members. However, it's not long before they sense the tension between the Black Tortoise Promenade and Genryumon, the major factions within Shanhaijing.
From Today, It Begins
Sensei visits Genryumon to find a solution for the "10,000-year-old Ginseng controversy" surrounding the Black Tortoise Promenade. However, evidence is presented against the Black Tortoise Promenade, and everyone puts their heads together to solve the problem.
Sensei and the rest of the group thwarts the independent actions of Genryumon's junior officer and quickly decide on the next course of action.
Diligent Study, Constant Practice
To find more information regarding the "10,000-year-old Ginseng", Sensei and the group goes to see Saya at the Eastern Alchemy Society. Although not wanting to get involved with the conflict at Shanhaijing, Saya ends up giving useful information after enjoying Rumi's special delicacy.
Longer Road, Taller Mountain
Based on the information from Saya, Sensei and the group arrive at Kunlun Mountain, where an unexpected guest is waiting for them.
Sensei and the group subdue Mina, the executive officer of Genryumon, and listen to her story. Following the ensuing debate, things take an interesting turn.
Once Taught, Thrice Learned
After finding out the truth about how the 10,000-year-old Ginseng got taken, Sensei proposes a way to solve the problem.
Open Hearts, Candid Words
To solve the case, Sensei and the group prepare a trap. However, someone had been watching the whole time...
Full Gallop, Harsher Crop
Sensei and the group engage in battle with the smugglers responsible for stealing the 10,000-year-old Ginseng.
Piercing Lies, Unveiling Truth
Sensei and the group successfully subdue the smugglers, but come to realize that they are merely pawns serving the mastermind working behind the scenes.
Good Wins, Justice Prevails
Thus, the 10,000-year-old Ginseng case that long troubled the Shanhaijing area, is resolved. Yet, the resentment between the two factions remains strong...
Full House, Joyful Family
They say that ground hardens after the rain, and thus the Black Tortoise Promenade and Genryumon hold a grand banquet in an effort to reconcile the conflict caused by the "10,000-year-old Ginseng case."Category:Event stories
