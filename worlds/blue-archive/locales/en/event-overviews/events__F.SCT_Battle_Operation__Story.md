# F.SCT Battle Operation

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/F.SCT_Battle_Operation/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

Initiating Operational Plan: Nisir's Summit
Preparations for the assault are finally complete. The countdown towards total assault begins.
Enter the First Sanctum
Abydos Foreclosure Task Force and Gehenna's Problem Solver 68 serve as the vanguard in the fight against Binah, the guardian of the First Sanctum.
Rin notifies the allies of the destruction of the First Sanctum.
Enter the Second Sanctum
Neru and Tsurugi take the lead in facing Chesed's forces. But Decagrammaton's armies keep coming, seemingly without end, and it seems the crisis may drag on forever. Then, reinforcements arrive from Red Winter Federal Academy.
Momoka notifies the allies of the destruction of the Second Sanctum.
Enter the Third Sanctum
The RABBIT Squad, the Game Development Department, and Hina serve as the vanguard in the fight against Shiro & Kuro, the guardians of the Third Sanctum.
Ayumu notifies the allies of the destruction of the Third Sanctum.
Enter the Fourth Sanctum
The Arius Squad and Trinity's Sisterhood serve as the vanguard in the fight against Hieronymus, the guardian of the Fourth Sanctum.
Rin notifies the allies of the destruction of the Fourth Sanctum.
Enter the Fifth Sanctum
Millennium's Seminar and Gehenna's Hot Springs Department serve as the vanguard in the fight against Hod, the guardian of the Fifth Sanctum.
Momoka notifies the allies of the destruction of the Fifth Sanctum.
A New Crisis
After five sanctums are defeated, a powerful, highly dangerous energy begins to emanate from where they used to stand. The allies must re-conquer five Sanctums they thought they'd defeated to prepare for the final battle at the Sixth Sanctum.
Enter the Sixth Sanctum
The Gourmet Research Society join with the familiar faces of Hifumi and Azusa to face the guardian of the Sixth Sanctum, Perorodzilla, who has appeared to terrorize D.U. Shiratori City.
The headquarters notifies the allies of the completion of the assault on the previous Sanctums.
Enter the Final Sanctum
With the five Sanctums destroyed, it seems that the energy is concentrating on the Final Sanctum. As they prepare for the last battle at the Final Sanctum, the allies' fate is dependent on Himari's secret weapon.
The Final Sanctum is destroyed. As the dust settles, the allies confirm that they suffered no casualties.Category:Event stories
