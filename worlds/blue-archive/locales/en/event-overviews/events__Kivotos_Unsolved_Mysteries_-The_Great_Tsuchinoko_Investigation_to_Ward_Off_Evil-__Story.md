# Kivotos Unsolved Mysteries ～The Great Tsuchinoko Investigation to Ward Off Evil～

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Forecast Says Cloudy Skies
Today's luck is the worst! Konoka's horoscope says that she'll have the worst day of her life in three days. While chatting with some of her social media friends, she learns of a potentially lucky charm.
Misunderstanding and Misunderstanding
Konoka goes on a trip to search for the Tsuchinoko, the snake of luck, and Sensei tags along at Kanna's behest. Together, they head to the countryside of the original sighting and meet Konoka's online friends.
She Has That Kind Of Gimmick
Konoka's friends turn out to be Rena from the Occult Society and a girl named Tsubasa, who claims the suspicious title of Mo Paranormal Research Institute Director. But Rena interrupts, claiming that she doesn't believe in the Tsuchinoko...
Ghost Bus
Ignoring the ominous old man's warning, the investigators forge onward. They find themselves in front of the bus from the ghost stories on their way to the mountain.
Living Stone Doll
The group learns that Tsubasa's club was disbanded. Meanwhile, Konoka, who had wandered off on her own, finds an ominous box buried underground.
Shadow Over the Lodge
It's late when the group, exhausted from a day of hard work, finally gets back to their lodgings. Konoka, the only person who is still full of energy, takes charge of dinner preparations and steps away. Just then, the group sees a suspicious figure around the lodge. Tsubasa raises the possibility that this figure might be one of the Seven Prisoners who holds a grudge against the Public Peace Bureau.
Phone Booth from Hell
The next day, the group gets too caught up in their search for the Tsuchinoko and misses the evening bus. Because they have no cell service, Konoka uses an old public phone booth.
The Ultimate Ghost Story
The group is almost completely certain that someone is pulling the strings behind the series of bizarre incidents. Konoka suspects Mitsui Kyoko, one of the Seven Prisoners, as the culprit. Later that night, the suspicious figure appears again, but the group fails to capture the figure for reasons they can't quite explain.
Grey Alien Appears
The group realizes that someone is intentionally isolating the lodge. Tsubasa insists the Grey Aliens are behind it. It turns out that it was all the work of thugs who held grudges against Konoka...
The group takes out all the thugs. After learning that everything from the disturbances to the Tsuchinoko rumors was because of the thugs, Tsubasa gets visibly disappointed. However, the group remains unsure of who had been lurking around the lodge.
The Tsuchinoko is Blue
Day 4 of not finding the Tsuchinoko. Konoka is scared that she'll soon be facing the worst day of her life...but perhaps the real Tsuchinoko was the friends she found along the way.
Barbecue and Chili
Now that everything's been sorted out, the group finally gets to enjoy a night of camping. Konoka teases Mirai for being overprotective when she finds her lurking around the lodge late at night, but Mirai asks about the person who had been peeking at the lodge.
Sky of Occult Society, Spring of UFO
Rena reaches out to her Occult Society seniors and proudly explains the truth behind the rumors of the Tsuchinoko. Meanwhile, Tsubasa calls everyone out late at night, saying that there's only one thing to do in the countryside at this hour...
Anonymous Who?
The person who sent Konoka the Tsuchinoko sighting story on social media was Akeboshi Himari from the Super Phenomenon Task Force. What Sensei and the students experienced was quite literally a super phenomenon.
Iron Maiden
The Valkyrie Public Peace Bureau is bustling due to thugs suddenly barging in to turn themselves in. Meanwhile, Kanna leaves the interrogation to other members and steps away to talk with a "special guest."Category:Event stories
