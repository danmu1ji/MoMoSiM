# Serenade Promenade

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Serenade_Promenade/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

Motorcycle Menace
Mari steals a Helmet Gangster's motorcycle, as if plotting something. When the Helmet Gangsters ask why, Mari answers, "It's to save Trinity."
Chance for an Image Change
Sakurako contacts Mari. She and Mine explain that they are trying to work as pop idols to remake their respective images. For the sake of these two people, who are not well-versed in what it means to be pop idols, Mari suggests that Sensei help them learn.
The Road to Pop Idol Stardom: The Costume
Mari and Sensei go to Abydos to learn about pop idol outfits. However, the Abydos students are put in a tough spot as shy Serika and Ayane resist.
Mari and Sensei persuade Serika and Ayane. For the two who helped out, Nonomi teaches them how to make outfits.
The Road to Pop Idol Stardom: The Dance
Mari and Sensei go to the Valkyrie Police School to learn pop idol dances. However, a hostage situation arises at the donut shop while they're talking, so Mari uses the easy dance moves she learned from Fubuki and Kirino to resolve the hostage situation.
Fubuki and Kirino are grateful for Mari and Sensei, and introduce them to a dance instructor.
The Road to Pop Idol Stardom: The Song
Mari and Sensei have learned to make outfits and dance. Utaha finds them and discusses the philosophy of pop idols, teaching them about choosing the right songs. Mari, who has learned all three things, relays this info to Sakurako and Mine, but she feels a strange sense of disappointment.
The Opening Act of War
Despite learning all three things, Sakurako and Mine can't manage to proceed with their rehearsal, and they claim the reason is because they don't have a leader. The two discuss choosing a leader of the pop idol unit, and rumors spread in a weird way, becoming misinformation about a reorganization happening within Trinity.
Summit of the Final Battle
During the discussions to decide the unit leader, Nagisa hears the rumors about Sakurako and Mine, so she butts in, bringing Mika along with her. A large civil war threatens to break out among the three organizations. At that moment, Mari and Sensei show up and stop the situation, and at last, the misunderstandings are resolved.
Antique Seraphim's Beginning
Sakurako and Mine deem Mari worthy of being the unit's leader, appointing her. The three of them, including Mari, come together as an idol unit.
Trinity Carnival
After the idol concert, Mari shows up in a mask, secretly giving Sensei a picture of herself. She tells Sensei that she'll give up being an idol soon, but wants Sensei to remember her the way she is now.
Foreclosure Task Force on the Stage
Sensei and the Foreclosure Task Force look around for Ayane, who has hidden on the day of the rehearsal. Ayane, who was found just in the nick of time, complains of stage fright, but Sensei gives her advice to help soothe her anxiety.
Utaha and the Kids
Sensei has come to watch the preliminaries of the pop idol contest. The Engineering Department is there as well. Utaha climbs on stage and, encouraged by the members' somewhat-questionable cheers, delivers a jaw-dropping performance.
After-School Haunted House
The After-School Sweets Club has prepared a Haunted House-themed cafe. However, it's not scary at all, and the club members debate over whether to go for scary or cute.
Public Festival, Public Safety
The Public Safety Bureau students are recruited to assist the civilians, like always. But for many reasons, they end up dressing in novel attire... What could have possibly happened to them?
Justice☆Revue
The Justice Task Force had decided to show off theater during the carnival. But on opening day, the main actress ends up stepping down. Sensei recommends the princess role to Tsurugi, who has all the lines memorized, and Tsurugi pulls off the performance, doing her best.
We're Students Too!
While wandering around the festival, Sensei notices that one street is devoid of any normal crowds. Instead, the street is full of Helmet Gangsters, who came to watch the festival.
Extroverted Exhibition
Sensei sees Nagisa, who's sitting by herself at the art center's exhibition, and talks to her about this and that, encouraging her in the process.
School Food Fighter
While looking around the carnival, Sensei ends up hearing the concerns of the students on Stall Street. They say the food definitely doesn't taste bad, but they're not getting customers. Sensei, who tries to think of a good solution, asks for help from the Gourmet Research Society, who happens to be passing by.Category:Event stories
