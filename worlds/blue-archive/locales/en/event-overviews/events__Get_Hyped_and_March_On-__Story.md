# Get Hyped and March On!

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Get_Hyped_and_March_On%21/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

Out of the Blue
Kaho and Chise enjoy the first free time they've had in a while. Niya interrupts with news that Gehenna students are visiting Hyakkiyako.
Fairness, Impartiality, and Fun
Through a fair and impartial process, the Festival Operations Department are selected to give the Gehenna students a tour. The Festival Operations Department panics when Niya makes her own declaration out of nowhere.
The Front Seat of a Rollercoaster
The Festival Operations Department's tour guides prepare to welcome the Gehenna students. Despite everyone's efforts, the tour starts off on the wrong foot.
Nice to Meet You
The tour guides scramble to calm down their chaotic Gehenna guests. Or at least, they try to...
Easy, Right?
The tour guides and Gehenna students visit the Traditional Crafts Workshop together. All seems well, until the peace is broken by a sudden intruder.
The tour guides and Gehenna students face the intruder together. In the wake of the battle, an odd friendship takes root.
Flower Viewing after a Meal
Right before a delicious dinner, the Gehenna students flee from the dining hall with the Gourmet Research Society leading the charge. Umika and crew try to chase the Gehenna students down but chaos erupts regardless...
Umika and the crew barely manage to get the Gourmet Research Society under control. But Umika detects something off about the earlier commotion.
Fantasy Nightmare
Umika and the crew prepare a test of courage for the Gehenna students. However, the unexpected appearance of real ghosts puts the tour guides in a tight spot.
Thanks to a batch of tardy Gehenna students, Umika and the crew manage to deal with their ghostly interlopers. But none can shake the feeling that things are slipping out of hand...
To Each Their Own
Both the Gehenna students and their tour guides agree to make the most of their troublesome situation. Amidst the mischief, can the Gehenna students' field trip still be salvaged?
In the Middle of a Festival
The main event of the Hyakkiyako Festival, Tree of Deities Ritual, finally begins. Umika vows to make up for all the tour's previous mistakes.
This Festival Is Ours!
The Mouryo shows up in front of Umika and the crew, derailing the field trip again. Umika and the crew fight to drive the Mouryo away.
Umika and the crew barely manage to stop the Mouryo from ruining the festival. But due to an unexpected accident, the fireworks show kicks off early.
What Can Be Done
The Tree of Deities Ritual turns disastrous thanks to an unforeseen incident. But Umika and others strive to complete the festival for their Gehenna guests.
Invincible Memories
Gehenna's rowdy field trip comes to an end. Sensei and the tour guides have a fun time swapping stories in the aftermath.Category:Event stories
