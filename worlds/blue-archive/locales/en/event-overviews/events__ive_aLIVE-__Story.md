# -ive aLIVE!

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Not Really Enough
As Airi struggles to come up with a theme for the Academy Festival, she finds inspiration from hearing a guitar performance on the street.
Let's Form A Band!!
Yoshimi, Natsu, and Kazusa are hesitant to accept Airi's sudden band proposal, but agree when they learn that participating in the Academy Festival's Opening Celebration Concert will reward them with Frederica Semla, the Dessert Queen.
What A Band Needs Most
Airi, Yoshimi, Natsu, and Kazusa take on important roles in the band: songwriting, costume design, rock, and vocals. Feeling disheartened among such passionate bandmates, Airi seeks advice from Sensei that night, sharing her concerns about her songwriting.
The Honored Guest from Wildhunt
In an effort to resolve Airi's songwriting concerns, she and Sensei spend the night searching the alleyways for the Wildhunt student Airi had spotted earlier. Without any leads, they search until dawn. Just as they're about to give up, the Wildhunt student suddenly appears before them.
A New And Different Me
Late in the evening, Sensei finds Airi practicing alone in the studio. Airi smiles and tells Sensei she plans to reveal a completely new version of herself. However, the next day, Yoshimi tells Sensei that Airi has suddenly gone missing.
For Airi
Sensei goes out searching for Airi. Yoshimi, Natsu, and Kazusa speculate that she might have left after missing the chance to join the Academy Festival's opening performance, thereby missing the chance to taste Frederica Semla. Determined to find her, they take their first steps down the forbidden path.
One Befitting the Position
Sensei finds Airi. It turns out her disappearance has nothing to do with Frederica Semla. Rather, she worries that she's too ordinary and doesn't belong. Just as Sensei is about to offer comfort and advice, they receive word from the Tea Party that three of their students have been detained.
Straight Out of a Youth Manga
When Airi and Sensei arrive, Yoshimi, Natsu, and Kazusa present Airi with a piece of their hard-earned semla. Tsumugi, witnessing the scene, is moved and gives Sensei a song she wrote.
It's Enough 1
As Airi discusses the theme of the Academy Festival, she sees her After-School Sweets Club friends waiting for her on the street. She smiles.
It's Enough 2
The After-School Sweets Club meets on the rooftop of a secluded Trinity building for a musical performance.
Shiina Tsumugi
The After-School Sweets Club attends Shiina Tsumugi's performance, and witnesses a stage show beyond their wildest expectations.
Traitors
While Sensei and the After-School Sweets Club are discussing the battle for Frederica Semla, a powerful voice of justice suddenly echoes from out of nowhere.
7 Days Ago: Do It Properly
With seven days left until the final battle, the After-School Sweets Club begins their rigorous practice.
6 Days Ago: A Classic Solution
They balance their physical practice with studies of theory.
5 Days Ago: Guppa
The After-School Sweets Club pairs up to observe and help each other's practice.
5 Days Ago: Position Switch
To make sure everyone learns all they can from everyone else, the club periodically rotates their pairings.
4 Days Ago: The Club's Identity
Since they haven't had much in the way of desserts lately, they decide to practice while indulging in some choice sweets.
3 Days Ago: A Weak Spirit
The After-School Sweets Club members' practice drills start to wear them down.
2 Days Ago: For Victory
With their practice time dwindling, they continue to give it their all.
2 Days Ago: Intrusion
Reisa shows up unexpectedly. The After-School Sweets Club members greet her warmly and enjoy desserts together.
1 Day Ago: Ready to Head Out
On the final day of practice, the After-School Sweets Club is full of determination.
Looking Back
Following their performance, and before the results come in, the After-School Sweets Club has a moment to look back on their journey. Upon receiving news of their success, they all agree to go get semla together.
"Happy, But..."
The After-School Sweets Club has made plenty of new fans with their exceptional performance.
Competitive Spirit 1
While focusing intensely on her practice, Yoshimi injured her hand. Once again, she and Kazusa are squabbling with each other.
Competitive Spirit 2
The After-School Sweets Club practices with great dedication, pushing themselves beyond exhaustion.
Operation Cram 1
The After-School Sweets Club begins to notice the effects of their theoretical study. Although their time is limited, they decide to dive deeper into cramming.
Operation Cram 2
As expected, the After-School Sweets Club members collapse from boredom, except for Airi.

The After-School Sweets Club has significantly improved their skills, but they're still slightly out of sync. Natsu decides to take drastic measures.
The Best Program 2

Group Hypnosis 1

Group Hypnosis 2
