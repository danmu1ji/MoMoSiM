Millennium Expo
Sensei and Seia arrive at Millennium for the Expo. Seia says this moment is a test for her.
Proof of Self
Sensei gets a call and goes to the Tea Party to find out what the conflict is. Nagisa, anxious about Seia's trip, eventually concedes, saying she believes in Sensei. Seia hopes to use this opportunity to prove herself.
Natural Division of Work
Sensei and Seia assume their respective roles and move out. Asuna and Karin are assigned to guard Seia.
How To Get Someone Mad
Neru and Rio talk in Rio's hiding place about their next objective.
The Raging Game Center
Worried about Seia's safety, Rio suggests a different objective, but Seia has a hard time accepting.
Negative Emotions
Neru finds out about Rio's worries that Seia may think negatively of her, so Neru shares her own thoughts.
Big Sister Room
Rio reveals her plan to take responsibility for her mistake and complete the infiltration alone to Neru and Seia. But the two propose a new method.
Epilogue
