# Sunshine Girls' Serenade

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Sunshine_Girls%27_Serenade/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

Face-to-Face
[USERNAME] Sensei visits the executive office of the Pandemonium Society and is presented with a proposal by its great chairman, Makoto.
A Cooperative Proposal
Makoto requests Sensei's help with the Gehenna Special Party which is held in Gehenna every Valentine's Day. [USERNAME] Sensei promises to help Makoto, who schemes to make the party bigger than ever to solidify the Pandemonium Society's position in Gehenna.
The First Steps
Sensei visits the new Pandemonium Society assembly hall, built as part of the Gehenna Special Party. The Hot Springs Department starts a commotion, forcing the Pandemonium Society to stop them.
The Chairman's Demand
The Hot Springs Department disperses thanks to the Prefect Team. The Pandemonium Society blames Hina for the damage done to their new assembly hall during the battle. As penance, they demand Hina perform in the piano recital, the Gehenna Special Party's main event.
The Chairman's Philosophy
Sensei arrives at the Gehenna Banquet Hall to taste the party's catering. Sensei instead encounters Makoto, who is not her usual self. The Pandemonium Society members quickly reveal why.
The Chairman's Promotional Strategy
Makoto shows Sensei a custom decoration she commissioned to promote the party, and unveils more about her master plan.
The Chairman's Decision
Sensei receives an unexpected call from Iroha. Sensei arrives at the Pandemonium Society's executive office and witnesses Makoto acting completely out of character. Sensei and the Pandemonium Society chase after this new Makoto, who seeks to disband Gehenna's leadership in the pursuit of peace.
The Day of the Party
When Hina's late to the Gehenna Special Party's opening, Makoto confesses that she knew Hina couldn't play music, and tasked her with the piano recital to humiliate her. An eager Makoto announces her victory to all of Gehenna. With the Prefect Team "defeated", shenanigans erupt all over Gehenna.
Starting the Party
