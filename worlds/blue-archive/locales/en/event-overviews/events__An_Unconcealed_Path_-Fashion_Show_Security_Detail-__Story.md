Ramen and Conspiracy
The Ninjutsu Research Club attempts to record a new episode of "Michiruchi, A Girl's Ninja Scroll" at a ramen shop. However, a group of Mouryo begins causing trouble...
An Unrefusable Offer
Niya caught the Ninjutsu Research Club taking down the Mouryo, so she secretly sends the club a new commission.
Mission Eligibility
The Ninjutsu Research Club accepts Niya's commission to provide security for a fashion show. However, there's one last thing they have to take care of before they embark...
Encountering Talent
The Ninjutsu Research Club's security guard mission begins. Shinecat, the famous designer, finds that Tsukuyo has caught their eye...
Ninja and Thief
Just as the fashion show is set to begin, one of Shinecat's signature pieces is stolen. The Ninjutsu Research Club sets out to track down the thief...
The culprit is subdued, and the outfit is recovered. But a surprise is still in store...
A Ninja's Other Side
Though the thief is apprehended and the outfit recovered, the model is injured and must be rushed to the hospital. As Shinecat searches for a substitute, his attention shifts once more to Tsukuyo...
Rising Reputation
Tsukuyo's star continues to rise, but she increasingly loses sight of her duties with the Ninjutsu Research Club.
An Unfamiliar Void
Tsukuyo no longer hangs out with Michiru and Izuna. She doesn't even answer Michiru's texts anymore...
In Their Respective Places
Tsukuyo's absence leaves a noticeable void in the Ninjutsu Research Club. Meanwhile, Michiru begins to act out of character.
Intended Coincidence
Michiru becomes despondent. Izuna and Sensei try to think of a way to cheer her up.
The End of the Show
The Hyakkiyako New Year Fashion Show finally begins, and the Ninjutsu Research Club trio is reunited. However, a sudden ambush takes things in an unexpected direction.
