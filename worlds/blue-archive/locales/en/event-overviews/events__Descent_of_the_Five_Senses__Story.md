# Descent of the Five Senses

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Descent_of_the_Five_Senses/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

Let's Get Ready for the Festival!
Sensei is invited to Shanhaijing's traditional festival, the Moonlight Festival.
An Unfortunate Accident
The phenomenon where the students become younger spreads across the entirety of Shanhaijing, with the Black Tortoise Promenade at the center of it all. The rumor about Kai starts to spread among the students.
How to Deal with Rumors
Kisaki orders control over Shanhaijing, but the getting-younger phenomenon continues to spread relentlessly. Shun, the Plum Blossom Garden instructor, approaches Kisaki, but as a younger version of herself.
One Adult, Many Children
Kisaki and Shun inspect Shanhaijing here and there, while appearing as young children
Between Past and Present
Kai finally returns to Shanhaijing.
Kai dominates the Eastern Alchemy Society.
When I Pass Your World
Kisaki eventually loses to Kai, who's become stronger through the power of a potion.
Kai stirs up the Shanhaijing students and denounces Kisaki. Extremely dispirited, Kisaki hides herself.
Us, From the Distant Future
The chaos that Kai started takes over and swallows Shanhaijing. Genryumon's hardliners try to bring about Kisaki's downfall and put Kaguya, the Peking Opera Club's president, in her position temporarily.
If You Go, Could You Meet a Qilin?
With Rumi's help, Kisaki manages to flee, then stands back up through Sensei's encouragement. Genryumon's moderates find Kisaki and pledge their allegiance once again.
To You, Who Would Waver a Thousand Times
Kisaki herself looks for Kaguya.
Kaguya seeks to recover Shanhaijing's order and tradition once more.
For All Cumbersome Things
Kisaki heads to the prison to set Saya free.
Kisaki, who's released Saya, leads all their military power toward Kai.
Balancing the Sky
The final contest between Kisaki and Kai takes place.
Kisaki wins against Kai, rising to the position of president once more.
Let's Start the Festival!
Sensei and Shanhaijing's students have fun at the Moonlight Festival, which has started without any problems.Category:Event stories
