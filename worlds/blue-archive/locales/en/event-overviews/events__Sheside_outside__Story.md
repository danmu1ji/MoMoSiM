# Sheside outside

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Sheside_outside/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

Prologue
Saori works part-time for yet another day to make ends meet. Then she receives a new offer...
Saori, the Sea, and...
Saori arrives at Outlaw Beach. But at the same time, the Squad has made their way to the beach as well.
The Squad's Situation
The Squad members, who were looking around the festival grounds, see Saori, but try to avoid being caught. Coincidentally, Saori also tries to hide herself from the Squad, and Sensei decides to help with both parties' requests.
Approaching Shadow
Saori works as the DJ's assistant-slash-bodyguard. The DJ bullies her, but Saori keeps her head down and continues to work quietly on the concert preparations. The Squad notices this and decides to punish the DJ...
Thugs appear out of nowhere, trying to kidnap the DJ, but the Squad defeats them. Humbled, the DJ starts treating Saori more respectfully.
A Bowl of Yakisoba
The Squad goes for lunch at a food stall, where they're shocked at the extremely high prices. Saori, who'd been watching them, approaches the workers who ripped them off...
Saori, having punished the food stall workers, orders food for the Squad with the money she made herself. Sensei, in turn, offers food to Saori, who looks pleased.
Helmet Gangster Water Gun Competition
Saori somehow gets roped into the Helmet Gangster Water Gun Competition. Seeing her fight alone, the Squad does their best to cover her on the sly.
Much to her surprise, Saori wins the competition. Before she can figure out how that happened, the Squad runs off.
Ambush from the Darkness
Acting on the schemes of someone called "Professor Smug," the thugs mount an ambush on the festival. However, Saori suspects danger and steps up to stop them.
She is Coming
Saori crushes the thugs single-handedly. Demoralized, they complain to the professor, but the professor only gives them a cryptic statement that "she" is coming, then hangs up on them. Meanwhile, Saori finishes the final preparations for the DJ concert, the highlight of the festival, and the Squad takes their seats to watch it. Just as the concert is about to start, a rocket flies in without warning.
The Legendary Thug
The "she" the professor referred to is none other than the "Legendary Thug" Kurihama Akemi, one of the infamous Seven Prisoners. She rampages through the concert, blasting any Helmet Gangsters she sees. Saori, in defense of her friends, her clients, and her work, steps up to oppose her.
Arius Squad, Assemble
Akemi charges into battle against Saori, while the thugs swarm in to back her up. The Squad joins forces with the outnumbered Saori.
Having lost the battle, a resentful Akemi limps away, trading terse words with "Professor Smug" as she goes. After the commotion ends, Saori, Atsuko, Hiyori, and Misaki finally greet each other.
Yo DJ Pump This Party
The uproar has ended, but the DJ ran off during the fight, leaving the concert without its star performer. Seizing the moment, Sensei and the Squad suggest that Saori take the stage in the DJ's place.
Epilogue
The morning after the festival, Saori and the Squad bid warm goodbyes to each other, then take off on their respective paths.
How the Squad Enjoys the Festival
The Squad has decided to look around the festival together. Right off the bat, Hiyori finds a game booth.
How the Squad Enjoys the Game 1
The Squad decides to work together and try to clear the game.
How the Squad Enjoys the Game 2
The Squad passes the beginner level and steps up to intermediate.
How the Squad Enjoys the Game 3
The Squad braces themselves ahead of the final battle.
