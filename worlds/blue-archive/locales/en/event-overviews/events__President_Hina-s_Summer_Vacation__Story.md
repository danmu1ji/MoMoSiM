# President Hina's Summer Vacation

Official English event-story overview. This is a synopsis, not the full scene dialogue.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/President_Hina%27s_Summer_Vacation/Story
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed

The Head Prefect Needs a Break
Ako says that Hina's complexion doesn't look well and declares that Hina will be given a vacation during the Prefect Team's summer boot camp.
This Isn't It...?
The Prefect Team arrives at the beach for summer boot camp. Ako hears of an all-out war between troublemakers on an otherwise peaceful beach. She decides to put a stop to it, without telling Hina.
Plans Are Destined to Go Awry
Sensei assumes the role of keeping Hina distracted while Ako, Iori, and Chinatsu block the troublemakers. Sensei has to go and deal with the stronger-than-expected counterattack from the troublemakers when things get out of hand.
All For A Perfect Vacation
Iori and Chinatsu believe that Hina must be informed of the current situation, but Ako strongly disagrees, claiming that it would ruin Hina's vacation. She says for Hina's sake, this situation should be resolved only among the Prefect Team and Sensei.
An Average Day at the Beach
Thanks to everyone's cooperation, the Prefect Team successfully defeated the troublemakers in secret from Hina. However, their relief is short-lived, as new troublemakers soon appear.
Gourmet Research Society...And You?! Part
The Gourmet Research Society came to sell corn to find out why the corn eaten at the beach is so delicious. The Prefect Team fights to prevent the Gourmet Research Society's disturbance.
After the fight, members of the Gourmet Research Society flee, thus ending an eventful day.
The Beach at Night
Night comes shortly after the commotion of the day. Sensei and Hina walk on the beach, and Sensei promises to teach her how to swim.
There's More?!
Just when Hina's vacation is back on track, countless troublemakers begin to assemble. Hina appears just as Ako is overwhelmed and at a loss.
Prefect Team vs. Troublemaker Part 1
The troublemaker sweep begins under Hina's command.
Prefect Team vs. Troublemaker Part 2
The troublemakers are easily defeated with Hina joining the fight. Having resolved the problem so quickly, Hina looks back at the Prefect team and calls for a reflection meeting.
Summer Isn't Over Yet
Hina lets out a sigh at Ako's excuse that she was looking out for her. Hina then smiles as she declares that the summer boot camp isn't over yet, and that the real grueling training has only begun.
The Prefect Team's Boot Camp Begins!
Ako, the acting deputy head prefect, instructs Iori to demonstrate shooting drills to set an example as a Prefect Team officer.Category:Event stories
