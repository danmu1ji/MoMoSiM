# Momiji — Relationship Story 3

> After receiving a very excited message from Momiji, I made my way to the Red Winter plaza.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Oh, Sensei! Welcome.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Heehee. I can't believe we're going to the Red Winter book festival together, Sensei.
- "What's the book festival?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Exactly what it sounds like! It happens once a year, over the course of two days!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Red Winter students bring books from all over, display them, or even buy and sell the books they like.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): That's what it's supposed to be, at least.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): In actuality, it's a kind of flea market for each club so that they can offload their leftover inventory from the last year.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Red Winter Academy doesn't have a lot of events, to be honest... So we try to make it as much like a festival as we can.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Even though it's called a book festival, a lot of students are just here for the environment and good food.
- "That makes me...a little sad."
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Of course not! That's the best part of this whole thing!
- "Wh-What?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Since most people aren't interested in the books, it's the perfect time for the Knowledge Liberation Front to go treasure hunting!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): We get to peruse a whole variety of books! Without the need of differentiating samples and the real thing! Actually, most students don't even understand the concept of a sample!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): And sometimes... We can even find some legendary publications as well!
- "I-I see..."
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): This isn't the time to dawdle, Sensei! We only have two days to make history!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Our goal for the next two days is to explore every corner of this festival!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Okay! That might be a little ambitious! But we're going to read as much as we can!
> And so, a very excited Momiji and I perused the festival.
> Red Winter Plaza
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Look over here, Sensei! It's the complete collection of Two Piece!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I can't believe I was able to find this here!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Ack! I thought it was complete, but it's missing a volume!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): That makes a lot more sense... The complete collection is a rarity that would cost an arm and a leg!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): The missing volume is one that tends to be pretty hard to find, to top it all off...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): There's no choice. For now, I'll buy this pack as a way to secure the whole eventually.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): This is...! The limited special edition of Jason and the Caramel Factory?!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Such precious knowledge, just lying around in a festival... I'm so glad to be a part of the Knowledge Liberation Front!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Huh? The hand-written foreword from the author that should be inside the cover is...cut out of it!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): What an atrocity! How dare they?! This harms not only the book, but is an insult to the author!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I'm outraged! They probably cut it out to sell the autograph that accompanies the foreword! They're the enemy of society.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Whoa, the regular print of Ishmael!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): It was super unpopular at the time of printing. Only 3,200 volumes were sold!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): The subject was very niche, and people just couldn't get through all 135 chapters.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): But more people started to read it over time, and has since been recognized as one of history's classics.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): This isn't a special edition or anniversary printing or anything, but...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Heehee. I still like it. Maybe we should get this as well.
> Momiji continued to stack more and more books in her arms as she moved from one corner to another.
