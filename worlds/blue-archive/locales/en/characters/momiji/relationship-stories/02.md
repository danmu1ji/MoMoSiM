> Black Market
> I ended up helping some Valkyrie students with patrol work around the black market...
- Valkyrie Student: Thank you for your assistance, Sensei.
- Valkyrie Student: Today's patrol went smoothly thanks to you.
- "Any time. I didn't do much though."
- Valkyrie Student: Don't say that! Just your presence makes the job easier.
- Valkyrie Student: Once word gets out that you'll be around, all the criminals tuck tail and run!
- Valkyrie Student: Of course, there are always reckless troublemakers who'll try their luck.
- Valkyrie Student: Just like her! Come on out!
> Screen shakes
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Waaah...
- "Momiji?!"
- Valkyrie Student: Oh! Do you know this student, Sensei?
- Valkyrie Student: Understood. If that's the case, I will place Akiizumi Momiji under your care, Sensei.
- Valkyrie Student: It's less paperwork for me!
- Valkyrie Student: I will leave the guidance and punishment of that individual in your hands, Sensei!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Th-Thank you, Sensei!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): If I was just handed over to Red Winter... For the crime of entering the black market without permission...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I would have been sentenced to no less than two weeks of cleaning our deepest, darkest bathrooms...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): N-No! It's not like that... I mean, yeah, I was here!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): But I promise I'm not doing anything bad. Please believe me, Sensei!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Then what am I doing here...? Ah...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Well, I heard a rumor that one of Luriel's old books was spotted on the black market.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Yeah... Those are the ones I've been collecting.
- "Hm... So did you find what you were looking for?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Yes! And in mint condition too!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Heeheehee. It was dangerous, scary, and more expensive than I thought but...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I'm so, so happy I was able to get it...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Huh? What makes it so valuable?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Um... I wouldn't necessarily call it valuable in that sense. People don't actually think much of it.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): It was Luriel's second ever published work, before she blew up in popularity. That's why there aren't a lot of copies.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Unless you're a real fan... You wouldn't even know to look for it.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Exactly! So you do get it, Sensei.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): HUH?! You've spent 100,000 yen on a transforming robot toy?!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Wow... That's a whole other level... Is that what adults are capable of...? Their power...?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I just like collecting her published works. It's a little niche, I'll admit.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): The reason? Well...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Firstly, I am a member of the Knowledge Liberation Front. I'm doing my duty to collect information, with priority given to books.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): The older publications tend to be damaged or dog-eared, so people think they're not worth much.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): For some reason... I'm particularly partial to those kind of items.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Because, you know, books are made to be read by people, right? If they've been loved, they shouldn't be in perfect condition.
- "I'm actually quite moved by your dedication..."
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Y-You don't have to be that moved, Sensei!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): There are also concerns regarding our budget.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Of course, there are other cool items that aren't just books.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): But, as you know, Red Winter students aren't exactly living under the best conditions in their day-to-day lives.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I have to be careful about what we're spending our money on, or it'll lead to riots and problems.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Trying to be so selective about what I choose, my collection ended up mostly being works that mean a lot to me.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I do try to make requests to the Secretariat Office here and there for additional books and publications.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): But they almost always get rejected! Ugh! It's so frustrating!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Even though the library is kind of isolated and no one really knows where it is...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): And even though the library doesn't exactly have a lot of foot traffic...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I still want to increase our selection for the dedicated students who do come and visit!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): *sigh* But I've learned that we can't have everything we want in life...
- "Wait, did you say you request books and other publications for the library together?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I-I did. Why?!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I'm so sorry... WAAAH!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): You're right! I'm using the Secretariat Office and library for my own personal gain!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I'm a terrible librarian! How can I even call myself a member of the Knowledge Liberation Front?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Once the others hear about this, they won't forgive me...
- "It'll be our little secret."
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...Really?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Then that makes you an accomplice, right Sensei? Heeheehee...
> I returned to Red Winter, holding the hand of an embarrassed, yet strangely happy Momiji.
