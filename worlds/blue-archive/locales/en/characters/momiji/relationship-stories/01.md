> Inside the Library
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I'm looking for the fanzine of legends!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Oh, I'm sorry Sensei! I guess I got too excited...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): It's said that somewhere in Red Winter Academy, there's a fanzine that was put together by the previous Knowledge Liberation Front.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Doesn't the thought of a legendary fanzine make your heart just race?!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): It could contain information on our previous history, culture, and so much more!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Ahem! The point is, it could help solve all the problems that the Knowledge Liberation Front is suffering through at this very moment!
- "You really think so...?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Yes! So let's go look for it, Sensei!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...Hm? Paper is pretty easily damaged? You're right! That means the longer we wait, the more moisture it's absorbing! It's being damaged as we speak!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Do I have any clues? Don't worry about the little details! I've come prepared!
- "Um, Momiji? What is...that?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): What's wrong, Sensei?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): This is how we're going to look for the legendary fanzine! Isn't it obvious?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...Is something wrong?
- "I'm just not sure...if this is the best way to go about it..."
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): What are you talking about, Sensei?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): The dowsing rod has such rich culture and history.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Normally they're used to look for veins of water...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): But utilizing the basic principles of dowsing, we should be able to find what's lost!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Shh. Look. The copper rods...are already turning.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): If we go in the direction that makes it turn faster...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Spin, spin, spin...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Here! I'm sure of it!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I'll use my weapon and blow the whole place up!
- "You'll end up destroying the fanzine you're looking for!"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Oh, really? Th-Then what do we do?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Digging...? But most of the earth under Red Winter is frozen solid, so it won't be easy.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): But I guess that's better than blowing up the legendary fanzine...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): We've been digging for a whole meter! We haven't found even a single scrap of paper... Ugh...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I wonder why? Was something wrong with my dowsing skills?
- "I don't think it has anything to do with your dowsing skills, per se..."
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): You're right! Of course it doesn't! We just need to use a different method.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): How about...a pendulum!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Right now, it's barely moving...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): But it'll start rotating in wider and wider circles as we near our desired object.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Okay, never mind! Next, we'll use a magnetic field sensor!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): We can download a free app and make use of the phone's internal magnetic sensor!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): A vein detector!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Tarot cards...!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Astrology...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Weeeeeh...
- "M-Momiji? Don't cry..."
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): But...but I can't find the fanzine no matter what tool I use...
- "Is it that important?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): T-To me it is...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): It might not seem like a big deal to other people, and if we ever find it, it might even seem silly and stupid to Sensei...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): But that fanzine is a part of the Knowledge Liberation Front's history nonetheless...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I'm sure I'd be able to see comics, artwork, and other data that I haven't been able to see before.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): That's why I can't give up.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I know it's silly, so I'm sorry for involving you, Sensei.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I can just...look for it on my own. You can go back now...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I'm sorry again... It was just a silly dream.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Thank you for today, Sensei. Please take care.
- "Where are we looking next?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): H-Huh? You don't have any reason to care about this fanzine...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): But you're still gonna help me look for it?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): From the bottom of my heart, thank you, Sensei! And I'm really sorry again...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...Normally after having such a touching moment, people tend to find what they're looking for...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): At least in manga they do...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Ugh... I guess reality can't be that easy...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): That line was straight out of some cheesy Saturday morning cartoon...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Wait, Sensei. Inside... Inside...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Sensei, could it be...?!
- "What is it, Momiji?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): To the library! Immediately!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): The deepest part of the library is being used as storage these days.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): It might just be the one place no one's thought to check!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I found it, Sensei! The Knowledge Liberation Front's legendary fanzine!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): You're right. Heeheehee. I'm so happy. This is all thanks to you.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): What? You just said the first thing that came to mind?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Well, it was still super helpful! Your words are what inspired me to come look in the storage!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): On top of that, you spent all day helping me look for it.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Okay! Let's see what's in it...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Heehee. Why is my heart beating so fast?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): This was undoubtedly made by our predecessors. It's filled with their works: some a little clumsy, some inspiring!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): But it screams with their feelings, the effort and desperation that they poured into making it!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): It's like the collective early works found in Komisen...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): What? Am I disappointed?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Of course not. How could I be disappointed in finding more history of the Knowledge Liberation Front?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Now, this doesn't hold all of the answers to our problems, or any great revelation.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): But it gives me something more important. Seeing that our older seniors from long ago made this, with the feelings we still carry today. It gives me the courage to keep moving forward.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Heeheehee. Sensei?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I'm so happy that you're here with me!
