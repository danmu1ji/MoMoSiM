# Momiji — Relationship Story 4

> I headed to the Red Winter library after receiving an invitation from Momiji...
> Inside the Library
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Oh, welcome, Sensei!
- "Huh? Is no one else in today?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Oh, about that... Today's a holiday.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): What? Why did I call you on a holiday?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Just, hurry up and come in! Come on!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): What do you think, Sensei? Isn't it warm?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): A-About that!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Well, you see! Today isn't really an official holiday. More of a temporary one.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I'm dodging the question? Fine, you got me there...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Um, Sensei...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Will... Will you...take a nap in the library?
- "What?! Is the librarian allowed to even offer that as an option?!"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Ack! Well, you see...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Sometimes...students or even President Cherino will come to the library just to nap!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): So I have to wake all of them up. I had to physically escort President Cherino out once! Not that it was very difficult, with her height and all...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Anyways! I got kinda curious why people would come all the way out here just to nap, so I tried it out...
- "You realized that the library is the perfect napping spot?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...Yes.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Wh-Why are you asking why the library is closed?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Th-That's because...!
- "I get it. You wanted to at least seem legit."
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): You make it sound bad if you put it that way!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Well, you're not...wrong...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Ugh... It's bad of me, isn't it? Maybe this was a bad idea...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): What would people say if they found out that the librarian was using it for such trifling matters?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): B-But still, I did set aside some space as a dedicated napping area.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Other academies do it too, you know! We're just following suit!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I'm sorry, Sensei. I'm just making excuses...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): R-Really?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Are you just trying to make me feel better, Sensei?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Hey! Wipe that smile off your face! You're starting to embarrass me!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Um, Sensei?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Well, if you really are that tired, do you...maybe want to nap together?
- "Yes, please. My eyes won't stay open."
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Heeheehee... Then please come this way to Red Winter library's brand new napping spot!
> The napping area Momiji prepared really was warm and cozy.
> It had a lot of cushioning and blankets, and she had arranged the bookshelves to keep the sun out of my eyes.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Are you comfortable, Sensei?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Your breathing slowed down already. You must have been so tired...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): You know, Sensei.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I... I really like people who understand me.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I hope you get some good rest.
