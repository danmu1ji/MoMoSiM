# Momiji — Relationship Story 5

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> I headed to Red Winter's library.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Oh, you're here, Sensei?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I feel bad troubling you with something this small...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Hmm? Small things can be important too? Well, um...
- "It's all right. Let's get to it!"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): A-Are you sure, Sensei? A-All right, thank you...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Hm? You're asking what I'm looking for?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I-I-I-I-It's, so, I need to find...!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): My limited reprint set of Luriel's debut manga...
- "That's not small at all, is it?!"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Well, not small to me, since I happen to be a fan, but...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...to someone else, it's only a set of manga...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): You say that makes the treasure personal and irreplaceable?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): It's kind of you to put it that way. Thank you, Sensei.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Well, let's see if we can find it, then!
> And so, we started searching every nook and cranny in the library for Momiji's collection.
> And after some time...
- "Hm!"
- "Momiji, is this it?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Hm...?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Y-Yes! This is it! Thank you, Sensei!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I was worried I'd never see it again.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): To lose a book in midst of other books. What kind of librarian could I ever hope to be?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Hmm? You're asking why it looks so new, still in its original wrapping and all?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I've been very careful storing it. Thank you for asking!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): It is my favorite collection, after all!
> After praising Momiji's skill and care as a collector, I got curious and looked the collection up online.
> Then, suddenly...!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): What's wrong, Sensei?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Hm? You found the collection's trading price? Let's see...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): *gaaasp*
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): S-Sensei? Sensei...? Are you SURE that's the right number? Maybe the page is hacked, or malfunctioning, or...
- "I checked multiple times and places. This is it."
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): W-Well, limited first editions can go for quite a bit sometimes...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...but still, this is...!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): (I wouldn't have to ask for new books. I could buy them myself!)
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): (And replace that shoddy old heater.)
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): (And maybe even build an annex, if I can convince the Labor Party...)
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): (Ooh, and pudding...)
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): (I might even have some money left over after all that! That's... That's scary!)
- "Momiji?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Y-Yes?!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): F-Forgive me, I was...lost in thought for a bit...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...!!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Wh-What am I thinking? I can't give my collectible up!
- "I see..."
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Right...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): It's the first thing I ever saved money for.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Back when the price wasn't this absurd, of course.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): So I can't treat it as a simple investment or a piece of merchandise.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): It represents my determination, in a way.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): And it represents my pride as a collector.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Most of all, it stands for what I believe, that some things can't be measured in material value.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): That's...good to believe, isn't it?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Ehehe. Thank you, Sensei. I appreciate the way you think.
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): H-Huh?! What's the trouble now?
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...Waah...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Sensei, I think I'm a bad collector...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): I've already made the decision not to sell it, but a thought still lingers in my head...
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): "Just how much pudding could I buy with that money...?"
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): R-Really?!
- Momiji (Knowledge Liberation Front): Thank you, Sensei! You're the best!!
> Afterward, Momiji and I enjoyed a fun snack time.
