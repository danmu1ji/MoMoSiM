# Michiru — Relationship Story 5

> Shopping District Entrance
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Fufu, you're here, Honorable Sensei.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Naturally, those on the path of the ninja would be drawn to such an item...
- "I'm on that path too?!"
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Fufu, why so surprised, Sensei?!
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Anyways, the ancient recording medium I brought forth is...!
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): This!
- "...A video tape?!"
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Indeed, Sensei.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): You're right. This is an...ancient recording medium that's no longer used... It's a relic called a "video tape"!
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Fufu, curious? Naturally.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): On this tape...
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): ...is...
- "A home movie of your first steps?"
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Ah! Nothing like that!
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Why would that be your first guess?
- "Oh, too bad..."
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): What are you disappointed for?!
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): A-Anyway, this video has...!
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): ...Something that Honorable Sensei, no, ANYONE who walks on the path of ninja would know...
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): It's a copy of the esteemed director Crow Nyan Nyan's earliest work...shot in black and white... You know what it is...
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): ..."The Three Ninjas of the Hidden Classroom"!
- "Oh. I see..."
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): What's with the lack of enthusiasm?
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): These are the times where true ninjas go, "So you also are familiar!" before exchanging glances, small smiles, and curt nods of recognition.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Oh... Is this the first time you've heard of it?
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): I-I get that Crow Nyan Nyan's works are a little vintage... Which is why these classics are all shot in black and white and are so rare to find.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): But still, surely you paid attention in class!
- "Is this something that we're supposed to learn in school?!"
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Hm! I can't believe you still haven't watched "The Three Ninjas of the Hidden Classroom"...
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Honorable Sensei, it's a tragedy that you've lived so long without experiencing such joy.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): It's just so...good.
- "It's that important...?"
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Of course.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Because...this is the movie that set me on the path of the ninja!
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Nyahaha! Right? Right?
- "Shall we have a screening right now?"
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): As much as I'd love to set up a screen in the middle of the street and ambush everyone with this brilliance...
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): ...It will not happen today. I'm here for another reason.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): You see, I'm here to make a copy of this tape. A true ninja is always prepared with a backup.
- "A backup?"
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): I heard that a nearby photo studio has introduced a new service...
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): ...One where they can archive and digitize old video tapes. Just think, I could back this up to my computer! To the cloud!
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): It would make this movie accessible anytime, anywhere, and with incredible ease.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): And that's why I have the tape with me.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Think of it as...a preservation process! Precious things deserve to be cherished and protected.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): You know, it's like how ninjas would smash food and desserts into tiny balls so that they would be preserved for months! Years, even.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Anyway, we call those ration pellets... They...have a taste.
- "Well then! Let's get going!"
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Ah! That's my line!
- Photo Studio Owner: I don't know about this one. It's going to be rough.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Wh-Why...?!
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Did I not bring enough money?
- Photo Studio Owner: No, it's not the money. It's the tape itself.
- Photo Studio Owner: I can't extract the data from this tape.
- "Are there any alternative methods?"
- Photo Studio Owner: Eh, the thing is, here's your problem... This tape is OLD.
- Photo Studio Owner: And it's already sustained substantial physical deterioration.
- Photo Studio Owner: I can't do anything when it's gotten this bad.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Ugh... I see.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Well, we tried. Let's get going, Honorable Sensei.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): *sigh* How disappointing.
- "You're surprisingly calm, Michiru."
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Hm? Ah, I knew this tape was broken. I've known for a while.
- "...You knew?"
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Of course. I played this tape hundreds of times.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Eventually, I noticed that the movie...wasn't playing right.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): I thought that maybe the professionals could find a way... But I guess not.
- "Aren't you...disappointed?"
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Nyahaha! Of course I am!
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Some of my fondest memories are now sealed in this rectangular scroll. Of course I'm disappointed!
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): ...And it's so hard to find another copy.
- "Michiru..."
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Sometimes I have these thoughts, Honorable Sensei...
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): If only I shared this joy with others...
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): If only I recommended it to more people and let them share the memory with me. If only I hadn't treasured it to the point that I hoarded it for myself...
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): ...You know, things like that.
- Quietly pat Michiru's head.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Heehee, thank you, Honorable Sensei.
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): ...
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): Ah! Now that I think about it, that ninjutsu shop we visited last time is only a couple blocks away!
- "That's a VERY quick pivot!"
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): I can't help it! I also heard that the store owner has both the "Wicked Muramatling" AND the real "Wicked Masamunade," the legendary cursed evil grenade!
- Michiru (Ninjutsu Research Club): The Masamunade is often criticized for copying the Muramatling under the pretext of paying homage. But, to me, it's totally different. It's iconic for what it is!
> We strolled the shopping district as Michiru excitedly recounted ninja lore.
> ...She tightly hugged the tape to her chest the entire time.
