# Iroha — Relationship Story 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Iroha/Relationship_Story/3

> As always, I was in Schale working on an endless pile of tasks.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Hello, Sensei. Are you alive and well?
- "More or less. Nice to see you, Iroha."
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): You look busy.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Those dark circles are a new look for you. Are you auditioning for a horror movie?
- "Very funny, Iroha."
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): (That was a pretty weak response.)
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I would never disturb someone who insists on working, so I guess I'll have to just work next to you.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Who knows what I'll pull today to get out of being productive.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): *nervous glance*
- "Sorry, Iroha. I don't have time to banter today. Augh."
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Sensei? Are you okay?
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Hm... Okay.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Hm... Okay.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I can work with this.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Let me borrow that table.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): This is not good. *glance*
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): This just won't do at all. *glance*
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Oh dear. What is to be done? *glance*
- "Um... Iroha? Is there a problem?"
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Oh, this?
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Don't worry about it. This is just a checklist from our last inspection.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Since I'm the Pandemonium Society's commander, I have to make sure everything's up to standard.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Based on the most recent inspection...let's just say I pretty much got an A.
- "Isn't that good?"
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): No. The highest possible grade is an S.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): As the best student in the Pandemonium Society, the number one tanker, and a top 300 second-year that the little ones look up to, I can't receive an A.
- "That's rough..."
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Sensei?
- "Oh, I mean... You can do it, Iroha!"
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Thanks, I guess.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I'm ashamed I didn't get an S though.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I guess I really do need to work harder.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Maybe I didn't get an S because some of the equipment is old. Maybe I'm just lacking.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Ugh. I guess I really don't have a choice.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Sensei. Can you spare a bit of time for a struggling student?
- "Um. Right now?"
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): This very moment. Consider it a commission from the Pandemonium Society.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Are you going to give up a chance to have a Gehenna student be indebted to you?
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Please?
- "Well... I guess if you're really desperate."
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Heehee. I need to work really hard on all my shortcomings.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): So first, shopping!
> East Gehenna Lively Shopping District
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Okay. Let's get right to it.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): This is our first stop.
- "...A doll shop?"
- Store Owner: Welcome! How can I help?
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Just a minute. I have a list, let me see.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Do you have that Gehenna teddy bear? The one with the bow?
- "Huh?"
- Store Owner: Oh! It's a limited-edition product, and we have just one left!
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Great. I'll take it. You don't need to wrap it. It's not a gift.
- "Wait, Iroha? You don't seem like the teddy bear type..."
- Store Owner: Thank you for your purchase!
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): First item, checked off. On to the next.
- "What in the world is happening?"
- Store Owner: Welcome! How can I help you?
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Do you have any Wavecat pillows?
- Store Owner: Oh! You have great taste! Yes, here's our selection!
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Okay. I'll take one.
- "Huh...?"
> Iroha went from store to store buying more strange things.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): A decorative frame...
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): The new wasabi potato chips...
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Do you have the new book from the bestselling "Love Is a Goldfish" author?
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I came after seeing your ad. Where are your Perfectly Plush pillows?
- "Are you going to explain?!"
> As the shopping spree went on, I only had more questions. After an hour and a half, Iroha was finally done with her list.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): That should be good.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Thank you for coming shopping with me today.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I really improved all of my shortcomings with this.
- "I'm so lost! I thought you were supposed to be purchasing tank equipment!"
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): You thought the checklist was for tank equipment?!
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): No way! Did you really think we'd be able to buy parts for a tank, the linchpin of the Pandemonium Society, from the shopping district?!
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): We have our own repair shop for our tanks if there are any issues.
- "Then what on earth was the point of today?"
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I was just shopping for personal reasons. To fill up my hideout with my favorite things.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I have to keep my hideout up to code, too. When I inspected it recently, all the plush cotton in the cushions was flat and my nap pillow was all worn out.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): And, of course, snacks and toys for Ibuki when she visits.
- "Why did you put me through that?!"
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Isn't it obvious?
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): You were so out of it earlier. No fun at all.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): My pride won't allow that!
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I can't skip work alone, so I used all my wiles to drag you with me.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I got you good this time, didn't I?
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): But you, Sensei... Didn't you have fun today too?
- "Iroha..."
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Now that you're rested, let's head back. I'm satisfied I skipped enough work today.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): When we get back to Schale, I'll help with your work. I know how it is to be a middle man.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Huh? I don't know what you're talking about.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Huh? I don't know what you're talking about.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Heehee. Let's go back.
> After returning to Schale, Iroha stayed up and helped me finish my work.
