# Iroha — Relationship Story 1

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Iroha/Relationship_Story/1
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> One peaceful and sunny day.
> I was enjoying a rare lazy afternoon.
- "It's such a nice day today."
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Excuse me, Sensei.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Nothing. I asked you on MomoTalk and you said you were at Schale.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Nothing. I asked you on MomoTalk and you said you were at Schale.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): So I came to see you.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): We received a mission from the Pandemonium Society. It was an order to lure you out.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): By any means possible. We've been given clearance to threaten you, negotiate, hold you hostage, or even...
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Even seduce you and turn you into an ally.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): That's pretty much it.
- "Um... Is it okay for you to tell me that?"
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): ...
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): This is so annoying.
- "You're the one that's annoyed?!"
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I hate stupid questions.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): But if you really need an answer...it's simple.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I thought it would be pointless to make something up.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Based on my intel, you're not the type to fall for sketchy tricks.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I figured my best bet would be to lay out all my cards from the start and just stick by you.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): At least then you wouldn't think badly of me.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Besides, I'm too lazy to come up with some elaborate strategy.
- "Thanks for being honest, I guess?"
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): And you wouldn't want a liar for an ally, right?
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I took the route with the least effort and most efficiency.
- "I don't really care if you want to hide something from me."
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): ...What?
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): What do you mean? I'm a little offended.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Are you really okay with someone pretending to like you, when all they want is to take advantage of you?
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): You're making me almost as angry as those Trinity morons do.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I did my research before speaking to you, but you've exceeded my expectations...
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I did my research before speaking to you, but you've exceeded my expectations...
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Hm... But if that's the case, I might have to change my strategy a bit.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): So let me explain, even though I really don't want to.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Sensei, please lend Schale's power to the Pandemonium Society. If that's not possible, please just help us.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): And if that's too much, just think of us...as a contractor.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): If you can help us, we'll make it worth your while.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I think they said something like, "On the day we dominate Kivotos, we'll give you half!"
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Well, the stuff they promise in contracts is always exaggerated, right?
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Why don't we leave it at, "You may get less than half, but what is half, anyway? It's all subjective." It's different interpretations.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): What do you say? You want to make an exclusive contract with the Pandemonium Society?
- "No thanks."
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Hm. I see. That's a relief.
- "Huh?"
- "Iroha? Why are you lying on the sofa now?"
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Just a second, Sensei. I'm trying to get comfortable.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Ahhh... This is much better.
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Oh, right...
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): Oh, right...
- "I knew there was more to it!"
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): You have coffee here, right? Can you get me a shot of espresso?
- Iroha (Pandemonium Society): I want to finish this book today, so...
- "E-Excuse me?!"
> I spent a very uncomfortable afternoon with Iroha sprawled out on the sofa in Schale with no explanation.
