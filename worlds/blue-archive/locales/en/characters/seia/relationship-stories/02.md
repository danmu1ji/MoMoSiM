- Seia (Tea Party): You're here, Sensei. Apologies for bringing you all this way.
- Seia (Tea Party): As you may have guessed, I have no particularly pressing issues at the moment. I hope I made that clear enough by asking you to come by "when you were free."
- Seia (Tea Party): Still, if I might justify my invitation after the fact...
- Seia (Tea Party): ...perhaps this will serve as a reason.
- Seia (Tea Party): Those who exhaust themselves with work must replenish their sugar levels. The Tea Party had these snacks specially ordered.
- Seia (Tea Party): Since I've taken your time and energy, I may as well help you restore the latter.
- Seia (Tea Party): It must seem like a bit of a contradiction...
- Seia (Tea Party): ...the way I wish to trouble you, while also providing for your naps and snacks and such.
- Seia (Tea Party): But so be it. Allow me to ask for your understanding.
- Seia (Tea Party): And if you find this preamble too long, please, go on and help yourself.
- Seia (Tea Party): ...Sensei? Are those...?
- Seia (Tea Party): Oh. But why...?
- Seia (Tea Party): Surely, if you saw through the ruse, you wouldn't have bothered with such a thing?
- Seia (Tea Party): Are you telling me you went out of your way to bring treats for a sick person, despite knowing that I'm not actually sick?
- Seia (Tea Party): ...Hmm? The "sickness" was a mere excuse to bring them for me?
- Seia (Tea Party): Aha... I see.
- Seia (Tea Party): So we both brought desserts, then. Different brands, of course, but nearly identical in substance.
- Seia (Tea Party): One would think we were both here for a picnic, rather than a visit to the sick.
- Seia (Tea Party): And of course, you knew from the start why I called you here.
- Seia (Tea Party): Fufu. Is that so?
- Seia (Tea Party): ...What a shame. It seems my idea wasn't quite so unique as I thought it was.
> I was actually a bit excited to give you a surprise gift.
- Seia (Tea Party): What? You still want it?
- Seia (Tea Party): You actually like it...? Why would...?
- Seia (Tea Party): ...Indeed.
- Seia (Tea Party): And only you could make such a thing so pleasant, Sensei.
- Seia (Tea Party): It's impressive. One simple phrase from your lips, and all my disappointment simply vanished. In fact, this went far better than I could have expected.
- Seia (Tea Party): We both understood the pretense from the start.
- Seia (Tea Party): And here I am, expecting you to play along for my benefit. Truly I ought to be content to enjoy your company and share a treat or two.
- Seia (Tea Party): How should I put this...?
- Seia (Tea Party): The way I see myself developing, I fear the word "brat" may be an understatement soon.
- Seia (Tea Party): But have no fear. I'm fully capable of drawing my own boundaries.
- Seia (Tea Party): You're the only one I treat this way.
- Seia (Tea Party): And, in that spirit, there are lines I will make certain never to cross.
- Seia (Tea Party): ...Does that put you at ease?
- Seia (Tea Party): ...Fufu.
- Seia (Tea Party): I can't help but laugh. Your company is as enjoyable as it is unpredictable.
- Seia (Tea Party): But back to the point: let's indulge in these scrumptious desserts.
- Seia (Tea Party): Naturally.
- Seia (Tea Party): The treats I prepared are already enough for the two of us. And they're unwrapped, so it only makes sense to eat them first.
- Seia (Tea Party): And forgive me for saying so, but all the treats you brought are mine now.
- Seia (Tea Party): I'll savor them myself, on my own good time.
- Seia (Tea Party): ...Such are the rules in my room. Even you have to follow them, Sensei.
- Seia (Tea Party): In this room...
- Seia (Tea Party): ...my word is Trinity's law.
