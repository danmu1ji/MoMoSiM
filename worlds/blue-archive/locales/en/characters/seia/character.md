# Seia

## Description

A member of Trinity Integrated Academy, Seia is the leader of the "Sanctus" student union, one of the Trinity factions. She is pedantic and difficult to please. Being a bookworm, Seia is often found in the Great Library. After recovering from her injuries, she sometimes shows her more childish side.

## Appearance

Seia has straight champagne-blonde hair extending below her waist, long front locks, fox-like ears, and a long tail decorated with white ribbons and flowers.
