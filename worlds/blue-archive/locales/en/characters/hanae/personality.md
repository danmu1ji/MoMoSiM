# Hanae — official English introduction

Newly enrolled at Trinity Integrated Academy, Hanae is a lively and cheerful girl who belongs to the Rescue Knights. She has a positive attitude towards everything and is full of energy, but has a tendency to become overexcited when she finds a patient, usually making the situation worse. Despite being quite an eccentric character, strangely enough, patients who have received her care have all recovered right away no matter their illness.
