> Schale House
> I was sleeping in on my day off, when...
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): It's time for the radio workout!
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): Sensei! Hurry up and get up!
- "Can't you let me rest on my days off..."
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): No! If you insist on sleeping every minute of your off-time, you'll become a cow.
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): You may feel sluggish now, but once you move your body you'll feel much better!
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): ...And if you keep being lazy, I'll give you a big kick in the butt!
- Reluctantly get out of bed.
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): Now let's start the radio workout! Follow the rhythm and do as I do!
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): First is stretching! One, two!
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): Not like that, Sensei! Stretch out your arms and put them next to your head!
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): If you do it half-heartedly, I won't give you a gold star.
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): Now, from the beginning! One, two!
> Afterwards, Hanae and I did the radio workout together.
