> Schale House Outdoor Terrace
> I came to Schale a little earlier than usual, and found Hanae with a watering can at the outdoor terrace.
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): Hmm, hmm...♪ Drink up and grow up big and strong! ♪
- "What are you doing so early in the morning?"
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): Ah, Sensei. Good morning!
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): As you can see, I'm watering the flowers. I wanted the terrace by my office to have more color.
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): I'm so proud of these flowers. They've grown a little every time I check them. When I come to Schale, I always head to the terrace first.
- "You don't have to come this early if you're just watering flowers, do you?"
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): You're right, but...you can't see the morning glory bloom after school.
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): You have to wake up early to see the morning glories bloom properly.
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): Look at this, Sensei. The buds of the morning glories might bloom tomorrow.
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): When the morning glories bloom, I'll pick some and bring them to the Executive Office.
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): ...Um, do you like morning glories?
- "I do."
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): Heehee. I'm happy to hear that.
- Hanae (Remedial Knights): But I'm going to make them more colorful and pretty so that you'll like them even more.
> Afterwards, Hanae and I discussed how to grow morning glories on the terrace.
