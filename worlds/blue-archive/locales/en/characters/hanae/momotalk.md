## MomoTalk 2

- Hanae: Are you scared of getting shots too, Sensei?
- Sensei reply option: I have no problem with getting shots.
- Sensei reply option: I hate shots! I hate needles!
- Hanae: Wow, and you're an adult!
- Sensei: Most students are afraid of pointy needles.
- Hanae: ...I guess even adults are scared of shots.
- Sensei: Those needles always give me a bad feeling.
- Hanae: The older students told me that skilled nurses know how to make shots painless.
- Sensei: If shots really were painless, more people would be okay with the hospital...
- Hanae: I wonder how they make shots painless?

## MomoTalk 3

- Hanae: Have you been sleeping well, Sensei?
- Hanae: Those dark circles look pretty bad, and your skin is looking pretty rough...
- Sensei reply option: My insomnia has gotten pretty bad...
- Sensei reply option: I just can't sleep at night.
- Hanae: You have to sleep at the right time every day!
- Sensei: Exhaustion is the worst! Lack of sleep will ruin your health and make you sick!
- Hanae: This won't do. I'll make something that'll help you sleep better at night.
- Sensei: Come to the infirmary during lunch tomorrow!
- Hanae: Herbal tea takes too long to get you to sleep.
- Sensei: I'll search the internet for a tea that will make you sleepy in no time.
- Hanae: Hm... What about tea brewed with wolfsbane root?
- Sensei: Are you trying to kill me?!

## MomoTalk 4

- Hanae: Where do you usually do your morning stretches, Sensei?
- Hanae: At the park? School? Office?
- Sensei: Actually, I've never seen you do morning stretches before.
- Sensei: I don't think I've done them since elementary school...
- Hanae: What? You don't do your morning stretches?
- Sensei: How do you even get through the day?
- Hanae: You should come to the park with me and stretch!
- Sensei reply option: No.
- Sensei reply option: I'm too lazy.
- Hanae: I won't take no for an answer. People like you who sit in front of a computer all day need to stretch!
- Sensei: Sensei!
- Hanae: How do you feel after doing your morning stretches, Sensei?
- Sensei: Don't you feel so refreshed?
- Sensei: I actually do.
- Hanae: Right? These morning stretches help relieve tension in muscles that you don't really use, so they're great for your health!
- Sensei: You should come to the park every day to do morning stretches!
- Hanae: If you don't come, I'm going to come pick you up from Schale!
- Sensei: Can I at least rest on my days off...?

## MomoTalk 5

- Hanae: I found an old medical book at the Trinity Library a few days ago, and it was filled with treatment methods I've never heard of before.
- Hanae: I wonder if they're taboo techniques that were never passed down to modern society?
- Sensei: Maybe they were alternative methods that were used between non-medical professionals?
- Hanae: I don't know...
- Hanae: Well, when you get hurt next time, I'm going to use one of these methods on you.
- Sensei: I'm so excited!
