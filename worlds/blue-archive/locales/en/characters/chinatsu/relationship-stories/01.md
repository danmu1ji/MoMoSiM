- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): *sigh*
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Is this ever going to end? All of this work piled up, and the others aren't doing anything to help.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): (struggle) And it's so heavy too. Though that shouldn't surprise me at this point.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Huh?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...?!
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...?!
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): S-Sensei?! What are you doing here?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): You're here because of the last text I sent?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): W-Well, yes, b-but I didn't expect you to drop what you were doing and rush over here.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): A-And the answer is no! I can handle carrying these documents just fine.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Excuse me? No, I said I was fine.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): You...really want to carry half of them?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I shouldn't be asking a teacher to do this for me...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Fine. You're offering, so it would be rude to turn you down.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Then please...and thank you.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Here you are.
- (I took half of the documents that Chinatsu was carrying.)
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Hmm?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Heavier than you thought it would be? Paper is deceptively heavy when you stack it up like this.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): You've made my life so much easier. Thanks again.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): You want to know where we're headed? Just follow me. We're going straight down the stairs here...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I promise it's not far, so I'd appreciate it if you could come with me.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): You can't believe that I was about to go down the stairs with all of these files?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I don't think it would have been that dangerous. Well, maybe a little...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Heehee...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Now that I think about it, you're the first person to say something like that to me. Thank you.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I would have managed. I'm used to things like this.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): As I'm sure you've noticed, the students of Gehenna have unique personalities, to say the least...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Many of them would never bother with this kind of menial work. They always try to avoid it.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): So it often times falls to me to get it done.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): So I have a lot of practice in dealing with these kinds of things. That's all.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Hmm? What about me?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Am I in charge of paperwork? No, I'm the head of the Medical Committee.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): *sigh* I don't get why a medical officer would have to do this either.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I wish we had more people to take care of this kind of work.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): It's not something I particularly enjoy doing. But, like I said before...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...if no one does it, then it'll just keep piling up. Someone has to do it.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Though this kind of work suits someone boring like me. Haha...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...Excuse me?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I must have blacked out for a second, because you couldn't possibly be talking about me.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): M-More importantly...! I should apologize for taking up your time like this. You came all the way here, and yet I...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): W-We should hurry.
> (Chinatsu hurried down the stairs at a brisk pace.)
- (I followed her.)
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Here we are, Sensei.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Thanks for your help. You can leave your stack there. I can take it from here.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): What was that? You want to help until I'm completely done?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): But...why?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): You don't...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I'm not sure what to say. No one's ever offered like this before.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Hmm?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I should just go with the flow? Think of us as two people who have been working together from the beginning?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): You'll help me until the job is done?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Heehee...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): It's nice of you to offer, but I couldn't agree to that. The last thing I want to do is inconvenience you.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...But I will admit this.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Today's work was much less tedious with you here, Sensei.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I won't ask you to help me every day, but as long as you're up to it...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...I may turn to you if you aren't too busy.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): That will afford us the opportunity to...catch up.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I-I'll be looking forward to it.
> (With a shy smile, Chinatsu began to organize the rest of the documents.)
> (Chinatsu and I left the classroom together after we finished our work for the day.)
