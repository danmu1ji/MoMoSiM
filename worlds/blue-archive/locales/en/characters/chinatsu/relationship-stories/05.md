> I went out to take a casual stroll.
- ???: Hmm? Sensei?
- ???: Fancy running into you here...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Y-Yes. It's me.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): What a grandiose reaction. I appreciate it.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): But I wouldn't have expected to see you here in the street so soon. Perhaps you took my message too seriously...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...Ah.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Well, don't mistake my meaning, I...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...Pardon?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Am I not on my day off? Yes, I am. Just as I told you.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...My bag?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Ah, this, you mean.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Ah. So that's what you meant. My apologies. Perhaps I didn't explain well enough.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I have a day off from the Prefect Team specifically. I was planning to use it to visit the Medical Emergency Club.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Not that I think they're in dire straits, but they are always busy, so I'm sure they could use an extra hand.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Well, I don't know if you remember, but I started out with the Medical Emergency Club.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Ever since then, I've carried this bag...and the knowledge of all the procedures I learned.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Their president, and the others, are...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...well, they're all rather odd, but their skills are unquestionable.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Fufu. I never found them particularly hard to deal with.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): And we all help each other with the work.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...Pardon?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): You want to ask me something?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Hm... What could you want to know? I can guess, given the circumstances, but...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...please feel free to ask. I'll answer to the best of my ability.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I guessed right. Fufu.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Well, no worries, I can answer that easily enough.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): You're not the first one to ask me, you know.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): To put it simply...let's say...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...someone had to.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): To be honest about it, I couldn't leave them the way they were.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...That's right.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): The Prefect Team and the Medical Emergency Club may be on good terms, but they're not the same organization.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): The two presidents are on good terms as well, but there's no chain of command between them.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): That said, the Prefect Team's work often results in casualties.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): The Medical Emergency Club always deploys whenever that happens, of course, but the response can't always be immediate.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): When I was with the Medical Emergency Club, I saw all that firsthand.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): And I thought, "The Prefect Team should have someone there who can handle medical responses right away."
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Of course, neither of the two presidents are particularly flexible, so any talk of reorganization soon died in committee.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I'm sure everyone had their reasons, of course. Both presidents know the way things are.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): But the only remaining option...was to be that "someone" myself.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): And that's how I came to be where I am. Does that answer your question?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Honestly, I don't mean to flatter myself, but the Prefect Team's resilience on the front line has improved since I transferred over...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...or so the Head Prefect has said. I'm sure it's true. She makes it a point to speak objectively, after all.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): I-I swear I wasn't fishing for compliments when I brought this up.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): But anyway, for that reason, I sometimes check in with the Medical Emergency Club.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): And that's where I happen to be going right now.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...Um...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): So...what are you up to today, Sensei? From the message earlier, I'm sure today was...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...Yes?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): You have business at the Medical Emergency Club too?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...All of a sudden?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Did...something JUST come up?
- "Well, maybe."
- "I also have a few things I can't leave alone."
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Hah. You're hopeless, Sensei.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): What can I say?
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Well...fine. Many hands make light work, right? I'm sure the Medical Emergency Club will agree.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): Once again, I feel like I'm in your debt.
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): So with that in mind...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...since you're visiting us this time, well...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...on my next day off...why don't I...
- Chinatsu (Prefect Team): ...go to Schale and help you?
