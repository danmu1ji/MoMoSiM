# Noa

## Description

Member of the Millennium Science School, secretary of the "Seminar" student council. Noa mainly records seminar decisions and meeting minutes, and is also in charge of the patent attorney's duties of identifying and registering patents for products developed by Millennium students. She has a very good memory and can almost perfectly memorize whatever she sees and hears once.

## Appearance

Noa has ankle-length white hair with blunt bangs, bright purple eyes, and small mechanical lens-like devices beside her ears.
