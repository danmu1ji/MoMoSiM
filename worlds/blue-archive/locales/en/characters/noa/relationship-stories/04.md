> Seminar Conference Room, Millennium Tower
> My appointment was canceled, so I decided to visit Noa in the conference room.
- Noa (Seminar): ...Sensei?
- Noa (Seminar): I informed you that today's meeting was canceled, no?
- Noa (Seminar): Did you not see my message?
- "I saw it."
- "But I thought I'd use the free time to come and see you instead."
- Noa (Seminar): Oh, that's good to hear.
- Noa (Seminar): I was just thinking about what to do with my newfound free time as well.
- Noa (Seminar): I've already finished the books I have with me, and I can't see any passerby out the window because of the rain...
- Noa (Seminar): If you hadn't come, it would have been a boring afternoon.
- "I'm not disturbing your quiet contemplation, am I?"
- Noa (Seminar): Not at all. I'm glad you came.
- Noa (Seminar): It's embarrassing to say, but I've always had an obsession with observing and recording situations around me beyond my duties as a secretary.
- Noa (Seminar): It's hard for me to deal with the static routine of daily life. I much prefer handling the noise and disorganization of hectic situations.
- Noa (Seminar): If you hadn't come here today...
- Noa (Seminar): I might have caused trouble to create a hectic situation just to save myself from boredom. Heehee.
- Noa (Seminar): Only because the ordinary life of Millennium students is already full of trouble and incidents.
- Noa (Seminar): Only because the ordinary life of Millennium students is already full of trouble and incidents.
- Noa (Seminar): There is plenty of chaos to be converted into records and observations without my interference.
- Noa (Seminar): ...By the way, water has condensed on the windows and is dripping down.
- Noa (Seminar): I should clean the windows before the floor gets wet.
- Write lightly on the window.
- Noa (Seminar): Sensei?
- Noa (Seminar): You're...writing on the window?
- Noa (Seminar): That never occurred to me...
- Noa (Seminar): Heehee. They're like phantom letters carved into the condensation, contrasting with the clouds above... It's all quite pretty.
- Noa (Seminar): Shall I write something as well?
- Noa: ― How strange...
- Noa: ― It's different than writing on paper.
- Noa: ― It's more...exuberant.
- Noa: ― Records
- Noa: ― carry meaning into the future.
- Noa: ― But when the rain stops and the sun rises,
- Noa: ― these words will fade, just as temporary as the moment that created them.
- Noa: ― This glass pane is too frail...
- Noa: ― to bear the weight of truth or reason.
- Noa: ―But...that's precisely why
- Noa: ― I like this large glass page.
- "What did you write?"
- Noa (Seminar): This?
- Noa (Seminar): It's inspired by a favorite poem of mine.
- Noa (Seminar): Just like that writer loved clouds, I loved this sentence, but...
- Noa (Seminar): I was afraid that if I recorded it on paper, my feelings would harden into reality and turn stagnant.
- Noa (Seminar): But the words on this window are even more transient than a person's moods.
- Noa (Seminar): So, Sensei...
- Noa (Seminar): Today's record is only in my heart.
> I wrote my own feelings on the window and talked with Noa until the rain stopped. Our conversation outlasted the window's records.
