## MomoTalk 2

- Noa: Hello, Sensei.
- Noa: My name is Ushio Noa, from Millennium. I've been appointed to help you.
- Sensei: I wanted to introduce myself before beginning my duties.
- Sensei: I look forward to working with you!
- Noa: This will be my first time doing this...
- Sensei: Is there anything I need to prepare beforehand?
- Sensei: There's no need to busy yourself with prep work.
- Sensei: Get here first and figure the rest as you go. Arrive ready to enjoy yourself.
- Noa: Enjoy myself...
- Sensei: Understood.
- Noa: I'll see you as scheduled.

## MomoTalk 3

- Noa: Sensei, while on duty yesterday...
- Noa: Were you wearing new shoes?
- Sensei: How in the world did you notice?
- Sensei: They're exactly the same as the shoes I always wear, just new...
- Noa: Ah, so I was right.
- Sensei: There were no scuffs on the front of the shoes. The truth was right in front of me.
- Noa: Your previous shoes were old and worn out, and replacing them would be far more efficient than restoring them.
- Sensei: Are you a secretary or a detective?
- Noa: Well...
- Noa: Thinking about it logically, both detectives and secretaries rely on an unfailing devotion to detailed observation.
- Sensei: For example, I'm always intently observing you...like a detective trailing a criminal.
- Sensei: Why a criminal?!

## MomoTalk 4

- Noa: The ingenuity of Millennium's students never fails to impress me.
- Noa: The technologies and ideas they come up with are beyond imagination.
- Noa: I can't help but reflect on, and maybe even doubt, my own contributions to Millennium.
- Sensei: But you work so hard!
- Noa: The cruel reality is that being a hard worker and being a productive worker aren't the same thing.
- Noa: Millennium is full of productive workers overflowing with sparkling creativity and awe-inspiring talents.
- Sensei: And what do I have? Just throwaway skills...
- Noa: ...Like being able to perfectly memorize an entire engineering blueprint by looking at it once.
- Sensei: ...If you're throwing that skill away, can I have it?
- Noa: Sensei, I have bad news.
- Noa: The patent application for the Sensei-Locating Device That Tracks Their Every Move and Thought...
- Sensei: has sadly been rejected due to objections raised by the General Student Council.
- Sensei reply option: Of course it was!
- Sensei reply option: Who would ever approve a patent like that?!
- Noa: We'll find out once they apply again after changing the patent objective.
- Noa: If they keep changing that, they can keep applying...
- Sensei: And perhaps, on a better day, to a more open-minded council, the patent will be accepted?
- Sensei: A better day for who?!
