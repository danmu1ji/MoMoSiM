# Aoba

## Description

An engineer in the Freight Logistics Department at Highlander Railway Academy. Although she is shy and easily scared, Aoba also has a side to her that casually makes piercing sarcastic remarks. Perhaps because she speaks in a hushed voice, it can be a little difficult to tell whether the words she speaks are insulting or not. She is always busy with hard work and often feels unfairly treated, and harbors a strong dissatisfaction with the world.
