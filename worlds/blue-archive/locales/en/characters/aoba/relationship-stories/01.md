# Aoba — Relationship Story 1

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Aoba/Relationship_Story/1
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> I visited a stationery store to purchase work supplies.
- Citizen (Average Person): Excuse me.
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Ah...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Ugh...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Th-This is why I hate crowded places... Too many people to beat up by myself...
- "Hi, Aoba. Nice to see you here."
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Ah...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Huh? H-Huh??? Sensei?
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): H-Hello...!
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): I... I wasn't expecting to see you...
- "Are you here to buy something?"
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): A-Ah... Yes...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): I need to write a list of all the parts we're shipping, but we ran out of paper in the office...
- "So you're picking up a requisition?"
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): H-Huh? No... I'm paying out of pocket...
- "For work stationery?"
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Y-Yeah... I-I mean I am, but...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): There's a lot of strict rules and deadlines about getting reimbursed for expenses...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): It seemed like I'd always miss something in the paperwork every time I tried...so now I just buy it with my own money.
- "That sounds unfair."
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): I-It's just how Highlander is...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Pretty frustrating, now that I say it out loud...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): There's all kinds of weird bureaucracy. It gets...stressful.
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Like, "All reports must be written on scrap paper for conservation purposes."
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): And if there is no scrap paper, I have to print something off so I can USE it as scrap paper.
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): K-Kind of funny, right? Heehee...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Then there was the time... Well, officers have to stamp documents to approve them, and this one officer lost her stamp, so we all had to wait weeks while she ordered a new one.
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Or, "When an officer orders bento, we all have to use that same menu."
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Sometimes, they drop next-day deadlines on us out of nowhere...
- "Sounds like you're putting up with a lot."
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): All the time, yeah.
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): E-Even now, I'm sure someone back at the office is suffering just like I am.
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): ...But I've more or less come to accept that's what life is.
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): If you never have expectations, you're never let down, right? Fufu...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Life is just one big long stretch of exhaustion and boredom...
- "Well, since we're both here today, would it be all right if I gifted you some office supplies?"
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): E-Eh?! Y-You'd do that, Sensei?
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): I... I mean, they have everything I need here...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): N-No! I-I can't... That's way too big of a favor...!
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): U-Uhh... Well...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Now that I think about it, I need paper... I lost almost all my writing utensils, so... Oh, and tape, I need tape...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Okay, well, I AM going to be in your debt!
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): A-And it's just for today! Thank you... Sensei...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Everything I need. It's all right here...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Th-Thank you, Sensei...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Ah...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): W-Well, maybe...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): But if I start hoping for better, I'll be let down again. I've had enough of that...
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): Thank you so much for today, Sensei.
- Aoba (Freight Logistics Department): I won't forget what you did for me.
> Aoba thanked me multiple times, then headed back.
