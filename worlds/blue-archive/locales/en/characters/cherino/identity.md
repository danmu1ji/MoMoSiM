# Cherino\n\nCherino is the tyrannical and childlike student council president of Red Winter. She has an extremely strong desire for power - despite being overthrown in a revolution almost every week, Cherino somehow keeps returning to the seat of student council president. According to Cherino, her fake moustache is a "symbol of influence" that "radiates authority".\n\nSource: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Cherino\nReview status: source extracted; pending editorial review.\n
