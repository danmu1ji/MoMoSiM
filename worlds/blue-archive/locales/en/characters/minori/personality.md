# Minori — official English introduction

Student of the Red Winter Federal Academy, and head of the Labor Party. Minori will send workers to any place where manpower is needed, such as large-scale civil engineering and construction projects, and direct them on-site. Although good at her job, Minori seems to be infamous with her employers for going on strikes and demonstrations over trivial matters...
