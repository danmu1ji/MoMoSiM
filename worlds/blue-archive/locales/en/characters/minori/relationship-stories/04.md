# Minori — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> Schale Executive Office
> On Minori's Schale duty day...
- Minori (Labor Party): Hmm...
- Minori (Labor Party): Hmmmmmmmm...
- "Something on your mind?"
- Minori (Labor Party): Ah, sorry! I'm not trying to sabotage Schale's work with my hmm-ing.
- Minori (Labor Party): I just...had something on my mind.
- "Want to talk about it?"
- Minori (Labor Party): Well, it actually has to do with you, [USERNAME] Sensei.
- "Is that so...?"
- Minori (Labor Party): Remember when we were talking on MomoTalk about how to make everyone in Kivotos happy?
- Minori (Labor Party): You know, society's suffering can be traced back to the bourgeoisie monopolizing precious resources, like pudding.
- Minori (Labor Party): But there's only so much pudding, and some resources are more...intangible.
- Minori (Labor Party): I was wondering how a resource like that should be distributed.
- "You'll have to walk me through this one."
- Minori (Labor Party): Do you know how many students want to be on Schale's duty roster?
- Minori (Labor Party): Duty here means spending alone time with you, Sensei, and the work itself is pretty light. So many students sign up to be included.
- Minori (Labor Party): In a way, Schale's duty roster is an intangible resource that makes people happy!
- Minori (Labor Party): Of course, your time isn't limitless. So duty days aren't equally distributed among the students, and only some students get to take more than one.
- Minori (Labor Party): Isn't this a kind of monopoly? A small group get all of your attention. It's social inequality!
- "I...suppose..."
- Minori (Labor Party): Unlike pudding, your time cannot be equally distributed.
- Minori (Labor Party): Even if we ensured that Schale duty was equally split, there are so many students that some will graduate before they even get to meet you.
- Minori (Labor Party): Alternatively, we could assign a couple hundred students to Schale every day...
- Minori (Labor Party): That's true. Is it worth it to make everyone happy if it means Schale can't do its work?
- Minori (Labor Party): But all of this is an issue because there's only one of you, and there are many of us.
- Minori (Labor Party): The ideal solution would be to divide Sensei into multiple pieces and distribute you...
- Minori (Labor Party): Hahaha! I kid, I kid.
- Minori (Labor Party): No one would be happy with a solution like that.
- Minori (Labor Party): If it were so easy to make everyone happy, there'd be no need for my comrades at Red Winter Academy to continue the good fight.
- "I see where you're coming from, Minori."
- Minori (Labor Party): No, that isn't possible!
- Minori (Labor Party): Even I feel more at ease, and more happy, when I'm around you.
- Minori (Labor Party): If I could, I'd bring you with me to the job site every day—
- Minori (Labor Party): Ah, what am I saying?!
- Minori (Labor Party): I know this is a private conversation, but as president of the Labor Party, I need to set an example to the proletariat...
- Minori (Labor Party): And here I am, thinking of monopolizing you like some dirty bourgeois!
- "I don't think you have anything to worry about..."
- Minori (Labor Party): Lately, I've been spending more time with...capitalists. Am I being infected by their greed?
- Minori (Labor Party): Sensei, I must perform a self-criticism session!
- Minori (Labor Party): Please sit there and observe. Do not hesitate to reprimand me with utmost severity for what I am about to confess!
- "Is this really necessary...?"
> I sat and listened for a while to Minori's eloquent self-criticism.
