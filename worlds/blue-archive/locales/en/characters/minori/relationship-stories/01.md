# Minori — Relationship Story 1

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Minori/Relationship_Story/1
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> Bus Stop outside D.U.
> I had been working all night and ended up waiting for the first bus home the next morning.
> To my surprise, there was a large group of students waiting at the stop, in spite of it being so early.
- "(Is there some kind of event today?)"
- Labor Student A: It's been ages since I've had a job. I was ready to write off this whole month.
- Labor Student B: The timing couldn't be more perfect. There are some new goods in the shop that I was eying, and I need the cash.
- Labor Student A: I just hope it's nothing too hard this time. I hate lugging sand and cement around.
- Labor Student B: I hear you. Maybe some pipe fitting, or putting together a little home extension... That would be nice.
- Wait for the bus.
- Minori (Labor Party): I need five for scaffolding, ten for steel framing!
- Labor Student A: Me! I can do it!
- Labor Student B: Oh, me too, me too!
- Minori (Labor Party): All right. They're expecting us at the job site, so everybody in!
- "W-Wait..."
- Labor Student A: Hey, you! Stop lollygagging!
- Labor Student B: You planning to pay us if we miss this gig?
- Minori (Labor Party): Everybody in? Here we go!
- "Please, wait just a second...!"
> The crowd of students carried me along with them, and off we went to an unknown location.
- Minori (Labor Party): Good morning, laborers of Kivotos!
- Minori (Labor Party): I am Yasumori Minori of Red Winter Academy. I am responsible for managing today's work site!
- Minori (Labor Party): This is the first job in a while for most of us, so let's show them what we can do!
- Minori (Labor Party): And remember, a safe environment and fair wages are the right of every worker! If you feel you haven't received that here, come see me at the office here on site!
- Labor Student: Yeah!!
- Minori (Labor Party): Before we begin, I'll lead you in a cheer.
- Labor Student: Woo!!
- Minori (Labor Party): Safety first!
- Labor Student: All right! All right! All right!
- Minori (Labor Party): All right! Everyone stay safe out there!
- "They sure do have a lot of energy for this early in the morning..."
- Minori (Labor Party): ...Oh?
- Minori (Labor Party): [USERNAME] Sensei? What are you doing here?
- Minori (Labor Party): Ah, so you were swept up by this morning's laborers...
- Minori (Labor Party): I'm sorry for bringing you here against your wishes. The Labor Party uses that bus stop as a gathering spot in the morning.
- Minori (Labor Party): But now what do we do? I can't spare any vehicles to take you home, and we aren't close to any bus stops here...
- "Don't worry about me."
- "I've been meaning to see how the Labor Party does its work, anyway."
- Minori (Labor Party): Oh? Would you like to take a tour while you're here, then?
- Minori (Labor Party): I can't replace the time that you've lost, but after the workday, I'll treat you to an extra-large katsudon from a place nearby.
- Minori (Labor Party): Their sauce is thick and the meat is plenty, so it's a hearty reward for a hard day's work.
- Minori (Labor Party): If you ask me, the workday isn't over until you have a katsudon or a beef bowl to fill your stomach.
- Minori (Labor Party): Now, before we begin... Put this on.
- "And this is...?"
- Minori (Labor Party): A hard hat and a hi-vis belt. The first most important thing on a job site is safety! And the second is also safety!
- Minori (Labor Party): Some of the equipment we use here is dangerous even for the sturdy students of Kivotos.
- Minori (Labor Party): Stay away from any structure that looks unstable. And no matter what, watch your step!
- "Got it."
- Minori (Labor Party): All right!
- Minori (Labor Party): Now then... Let's get to work!
- Minori (Labor Party): Welding team! Get those safety handrails up!
- Minori (Labor Party): Plaster team! We need 3 bags of mortar here! I want all those gaps in the plywood filled!
- Minori (Labor Party): Impressed? Huh. Everyone seems a little slow today, if you ask me...
- "Are you kidding? This building will be done in no time!"
- Minori (Labor Party): Don't be so sure. The reason the Labor Party has so much trouble getting jobs is because we're slower than other groups.
- Minori (Labor Party): Of course, our work quality is second to none in all of Kivotos...
- Minori (Labor Party): And I think the students here work at a fine pace, so I'm not sure why we're always behind schedule.
- Boss: Ah, President Minori, hello! I believe this is our first time meeting in person, yes?
- Boss: I'm the president of Magma Construction. It's a pleasure, a real pleasure.
- Minori (Labor Party): Ah, so you're our client. Thank you for giving us this opportunity.
- Boss: Oh, it's nothing, nothing at all. Why, I should thank you for coming here on such short notice!
- Minori (Labor Party): ...What brings you to the job site? Would you like to inspect our work?
- Minori (Labor Party): I can personally vouch that we are following safety regulations to the letter, so you have nothing to worry about.
- Boss: Oh, no, it's nothing like that...
- Boss: To be quite frank with you, dear... I had a teeny, tiny favor to ask of you.
- Minori (Labor Party): A favor.
- Boss: You see—and oh, this is dreadfully embarrassing—I had an unexpected expense, and my funds aren't exactly liquid just now.
- Boss: I just don't know if I have the funds to pay the Labor Party wages today.
- Boss: Oh, but don't worry! I'll pay you back with interest tomorrow, so if we could just keep this between us—
- Minori (Labor Party): Attention, everyone! Stop work! Shut it down!
- Minori (Labor Party): No labor for no pay!
- Minori (Labor Party): Labor is the fight for human survival! There can be no labor without fair and equal payment!
- Minori (Labor Party): Anyone who comes to me, trying to get free work with honeyed words...
- Minori (Labor Party): ...is a capitalist pig who doesn't know the true value of labor!
- Boss: I-I never said I wouldn't pay! I only need more time...
- Minori (Labor Party): Call it whatever you like! The Labor Party will accept no unequal terms.
- Minori (Labor Party): We will not work one minute—one second—without fair compensation!
- Labor Student: Yeah! Yeah!
- Boss: Hmph... It seems nobody wants to work these days.
- Boss: Fine, have it your way. Leave the site for today.
- Boss: But I promise I'll have the money tomorrow. And then we can put all this nasty business behind us and get back to it, yes?
- Minori (Labor Party): If you can give us fair pay, we're happy to—
- Minori (Labor Party): Wait. What about the work we've already done today?
- Boss: I told you, I don't have any money today!
- Boss: Tomorrow! We can talk this out tomorrow!
- Minori (Labor Party): Ah-ha! This is how you planned to squeeze free labor out of us, you bourgeois...
- Minori (Labor Party): I think it's time we showed you the value of our labor!
- Minori (Labor Party): Blast team! Dynamite, now!
- Boss: Dynamite?! What are you planning...?!
- Boss: You're going to sabotage the work site over a little misunderstanding...?!
- Minori (Labor Party): Sabotage is such an ugly word. We're just equaling things out.
- Minori (Labor Party): Anything we built without pay, we're going to demolish!
- Boss: Wait! If you destroy it, then you'll just have to build it again tomorrow!
- Boss: Think of how wasteful you're being!
- Boss: Don't you have pride as builders? As artists?
- Minori (Labor Party): Even capitalists understand that you have to pay for quality art.
- Minori (Labor Party): I will ask you one last time. Do you have today's wages?
- Boss: I told you! I literally have no money in the—
- Minori (Labor Party): Tear it all down!
- Boss: Noooo!
- Minori (Labor Party): Haha... I've worked up an appetite from today's hard work.
- Minori (Labor Party): Okay, everyone! Katsudon for everyone. My treat! Fill your stomachs!
- Labor Student: Three cheers for the president!
- Labor Student: I don't care what the job is, as long as President Minori is the foreman!
- Minori (Labor Party): Aaah... But why do our jobs always end up falling behind schedule? I really don't understand it.
> I thought about answering her, but in the end I bit my tongue...
> I enjoyed some hot katsudon with the Labor Party before returning to Schale.
