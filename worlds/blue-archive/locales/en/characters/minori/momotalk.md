- Minori: I recently read a very interesting book.
- Minori: The book was called, "Why Half of Kivotos is Unhappy".
- Sensei: Oh, what was it about?
- Minori: It was written by a student who worked in the General Student Council's finance office.
- Sensei: After investigating the demand for luxury items like pudding throughout Kivotos,
- Sensei: the author discovered there was enough pudding for everyone if they divided it equally.
- Sensei: But since some students eat two puddings, there is an imbalance in the distribution.
- Sensei: That's an interesting statistic.
- Minori: But since the students who eat two puddings won't give up their pudding,
- Sensei: almost half of the students can't have any pudding and are unhappy.
- Sensei: Of course the cause of unhappiness is probably not just pudding...
- Minori: What do you mean? Pudding is the source of all happiness!
- Minori: Unless we take pudding away from the bourgeoisie, the unhappiness will continue!
- Sensei: But since the current struggles within Red Winter Academy are daunting enough,
- Sensei: the fundamental solution to this problem may be...
- Minori: to cut the number of students in half...?
- Sensei: Don't be ridiculous!
