## MomoTalk 3

- Shun: Sensei, this afternoon...
- Shun: We're doing a puppet show to help the students of the Plum Blossom Garden learn to brush their teeth.
- Sensei: A puppet show sounds...fun.
- Shun: Yeah.
- Sensei: It's like a sentai puppet show about defeating cavity bacteria, and the students love it!
- Sensei: But today we don't have an instructor to play the role of the cavity...
- Shun: Could you help us out?
- Sensei: I'm not really much of an actor...
- Shun: Heehee, that's okay. I'll be right next to you to help.
- Shun: Thanks to your help, the puppet show was a success! Heehee.
- Sensei: Don't forget to brush after every meal, Sensei!
- Sensei reply option: Yes, Instructor Shun!
- Sensei reply option: I'm not a kid, though...
- Shun: Heehee, you did a great job.
- Shun: It's important for adults to brush their teeth too!
- Shun: I'll be checking for cavities next time, so keep up with your hygiene until then!
