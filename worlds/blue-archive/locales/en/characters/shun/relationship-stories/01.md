> Plum Blossom Garden Entrance
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): What a surprise, Sensei. To what do I owe the pleasure?
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Snack time? Yes, I was just about to hand out snacks to the Plum Blossom Garden students...
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Oh, dear. I must have emailed you by mistake. I'm sorry about that.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): No matter. Why not join us while you're here?
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Don't hold back. We have plenty of fruit to go around!
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Besides, I have something to ask of you... Now, now. Come on over here.
> Plum Blossom Garden Kitchen
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Doesn't everything look so fresh? The Promenade kindly donated so much fruit when we told them it was for the Plum Blossom Garden students.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): I couldn't possibly cut and prepare all of it myself. Would you mind helping me?
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Let's see... I'll prepare the persimmons and pears. How about you peel the apples, Sensei?
- "No problem."
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Well, I'll be, Sensei. These apples look delectable.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): This will be a good skill to have when you grow up, you know.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Pardon me. I'm fully aware you're already an adult. I simply get used to saying these kinds of things because I'm always interacting with the children.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Now take a gander at the fruits I peeled. How do they look?
- "Looking good."
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Does it now? It feels good to have your seal of approval. Heehee...
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Then should we taste them first? Here, Sensei. Say, "Ahhh".
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Don't be shy. Here, have a bite of this.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): ...You don't want to eat the one I picked out for you?
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Good boys and girls are never picky about their food. The same rule applies to you!
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Now, one more time. Say, "Ahhh".
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): There. That's better. How does it taste?
- "It's fresh and really sweet."
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Is it? Then I wouldn't mind a bite myself.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Don't be shy. It wouldn't be fair if I was the only one to do the feeding.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Now, it's my turn! Ahhh...
- Put an apple slice in Shun's mouth.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Mmm! It's even sweeter when you're the one feeding me.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): I may need another to form an opinion, though... Oh my, look at the time.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Snack time will be over if we take any longer. We should hurry.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): You carry the plates over there. I'll bring the ones here.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Heehee. I'm looking forward to today's snack time.
