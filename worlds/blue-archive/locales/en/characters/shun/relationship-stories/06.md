> Plum Blossom Garden Lounge
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Goodness, Sensei. Have you been getting enough sleep? There are bags under your eyes.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): You need to get your rest, regardless of how busy Schale keeps you.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): I know. The Plum Blossom students are outside right now, so why not take a nap in the lounge?
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): It's quite all right. Take your nap, and I'll be sure to wake you before the children return.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): If you keep resisting...then I'll have no choice but to bear hug you and force you to sleep!
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Heehee. That's a good sensei. Napping is essential to one's health. Now lie yourself down on this blanket here.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): I'll sing you to sleep with a lullaby.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Hush-a-bye, Sensei, on the tree top... When the wind blows the cradle will rock...
> When the bough breaks the cradle will fall...
- Shun: ― Goodness. You're awake.
- Shun: ― Today...was quite enjoyable. Heehee.
- Shun: ― Oh, dear. It's time for the children to come in.
- Shun: ― Should you head back now?
- Shun: ― No...heehee. You should rest a little more, Sensei.
- Shun: ― Don't worry about today's class... I'll substitute for you.
- Shun: ― Aw... You're so cute. Heehee.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): How do you feel? Refreshed?
- "Much better now, but I should be getting up."
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Nonsense, Sensei. You should lie down a little more.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): I'm not going to give up this precious moment... I mean, I want you to get a little more rest.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Come to think of it, I didn't get a good night's sleep either. Maybe I should lie down too.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): In that case, would you mind singing me a lullaby this time, Sensei?
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): And would you mind patting my head until I doze off? I want to hear your warm voice until I'm sound asleep.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): The children? Oh, right. I suppose I should stay awake with them coming inside, shouldn't I?
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Though I think I've earned the right to be a little lazier today than usual.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Consider me another sleepy student in your class for the time being.
