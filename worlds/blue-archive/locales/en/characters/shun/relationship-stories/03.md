> Shanhaijing Classroom
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): *sigh* I'm always reminded of my age, no matter how much I try to keep my mind off of it.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): I need to snap out of it...
- "Is something wrong?"
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Oh, it's you, Sensei.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): No, it's nothing. ...There's just something on my mind.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): I was scolding Kokona for her eating habits when she said I wouldn't understand because I'm too old.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): I know she didn't really mean any harm by it...
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): ...but I can't deny that Kokona and I have been butting heads more often as of late.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Um, this may be awkward to ask...but do you think I'm too old as well?
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): I suppose that's a silly question for me to ask an adult. Still, I've always been told I look and act more mature than other people my age.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): I can't help but think if I was a little younger and more childlike...perhaps I could better empathize with Kokona.
- "I think that's the mark of being a good sister, Shun."
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Am I? I feel as though all I do is get on Kokona's nerves with my nagging...
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Naturally, I know Kokona doesn't hate me and isn't trying to hurt me, but having to be the older sister can be very challenging.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Sometimes I wonder what it would be like to be an ordinary student and not a Plum Blossom Garden instructor.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): How it would be to spend my time with peers who weren't concerned about my age...
- "You still deserve the care that all students do."
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): C-Care... I've never considered being taken care of...
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): It does make me happy to hear you say that, though. Heehee...
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Oh, dear. Where does the time go? The Plum Blossom children must be waiting.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): Heehee. I also have students to take care of, just like you.
- Shun (Plum Blossom Garden): ...But when I get back, I may just take you up on your offer.
