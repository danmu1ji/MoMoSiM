> Near Business District 1
- Chihiro (Veritas): There you are, Sensei. I've been waiting.
- Chihiro (Veritas): I know you're busy. Thanks for coming today.
- Chihiro (Veritas): We can get to work now that you're here.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Why are you so worried, Sensei?
- Chihiro (Veritas): Why are you so worried, Sensei?
- Chihiro (Veritas): As I said before, all you have to do is stand there.
- Chihiro (Veritas): I'll be dealing with an IT company that's located in this high-rise building.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Their security has weakened due to a recent server expansion, but their president is stubborn and doesn't want to entrust their data to a student like myself.
- Chihiro (Veritas): What are you talking about? We'll be going through the front entrance.
- Chihiro (Veritas): What are you talking about? We'll be going through the front entrance.
- Chihiro (Veritas): We're not living out some kind of spy novel. This is serious business.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Hello? I'm Kagami Chihiro of Millennium Science School.
We spoke this morning.
- Chihiro (Veritas): I'm here for a consultation regarding your company's server security.
- Difficult Owner: Please. Come in.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Thank you.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Remember what I said, Sensei. You don't have to do or say anything.
Just play along and follow my lead.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Let's go inside.
- Difficult Owner: So, you believe our company's data is in danger?
- Chihiro (Veritas): Yes. The new server you installed seems to be using an old antivirus program that's already been cracked by hackers.
- Chihiro (Veritas): You're vulnerable to spoofing, and all company and partner data could fall into the hands of bad actors.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Including confidential information regarding new projects.
- Difficult Owner: We can't have that. You're right—I should have the server's security enhanced.
- Difficult Owner: But why should I entrust Veritas with my server management?
- Difficult Owner: You may project an outward appearance of professionalism,
but Veritas is still just an amateur club run by students.
- Chihiro (Veritas): We may be students, but we're hardly amateurs. Veritas has offered complete security solutions for several IT companies in Kivotos.
- Chihiro (Veritas): That includes for Sensei here, the advisor of Schale.
- Difficult Owner: Schale? You mean the investigative club that has extrajudicial authority as granted by the General Student Council president?
- Difficult Owner: Schale? You mean the investigative club that has extrajudicial authority as granted by the General Student Council president?
- Difficult Owner: To my understanding, Schale exchanges data with several academies.
If even Schale trusts Veritas, than I suppose my company can as well.
- Difficult Owner: Still, I would prefer if anyone but a student was the one in charge.
I don't know how I feel about entrusting my data to children.
- Difficult Owner: Interesting.
- Difficult Owner: All right, then. How about we sign a one-year contract?
- Difficult Owner: I wouldn't have agreed to this if I was only negotiating with students...
- Difficult Owner: ...but I'll trust the word of this Sensei person.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Thank you, Mr. President. We'll treat your company's data with the utmost care.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Phew. I'm glad that's over.
- Chihiro (Veritas): We shouldn't have to worry about our finances for a while now.
- "What was that all about?"
- Chihiro (Veritas): Hmm? I told you: I was going to install a back door before getting to work.
- Chihiro (Veritas): In programming terms, a back door is a shortcut created for convenience.
It's a basic hacking technique that allows you to bypass even the most impenetrable firewall with ease.
- Chihiro (Veritas): I'm saying your presence was the back door I needed to bypass the president's firewall—his prejudice against working with students.
I was able to complete my hack thanks to you, so to speak.
- "That doesn't count as hacking, does it?"
- Chihiro (Veritas): Of course it does. It's a completely legitimate technique.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Hacking is all about disabling the opponent's security in any way you can to procure the desired information.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Building trust through conversation is one of the best hacking techniques you can have.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Now if only I could get that through to the other members of Veritas as well...
- Chihiro (Veritas): Regardless, thanks for your help today. I'll treat you to coffee next time.
