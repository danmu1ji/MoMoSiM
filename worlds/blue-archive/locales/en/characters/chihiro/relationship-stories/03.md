> Business District Data Center
- Chihiro (Veritas): The cable and LAN connections are good, and the hardware capacity is sufficient.
- Chihiro (Veritas): No problem with the security settings. I just need to back up the database after updating to the latest kernel.
- "..."
- Chihiro (Veritas): What's wrong? Did you picture me frantically pounding the keyboard in front of a flashing-red monitor like you see hackers do in TV and movies?
- Chihiro (Veritas): Well, I suppose sometimes it can look like that during emergency response cases.
- Chihiro (Veritas): This is just routine maintenance. This is what it's like under normal circumstances.
- "But why work in the middle of the night?"
- Chihiro (Veritas): Because the server has to go offline during maintenance. Every service is different, but you can generally minimize inconvenience to the client by performing maintenance during late-night hours.
- Chihiro (Veritas): The business district is relatively quiet at night, so processing data is also faster.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Though working at night does have its drawbacks, and there are always some people that require service at any time of day.
- Chihiro (Veritas): It's impossible to please everyone, however, so this is just the best course of action to take.
- "You have a lot on your plate."
- Chihiro (Veritas): I don't mind. This is my job, after all.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Server maintenance isn't all that different from other public services like law enforcement or firefighting—the less you have to do, the better.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Every server manager's job is the same, no matter how competent they are: operate the server without issue.
- Chihiro (Veritas): It's not a glamorous job, but someone has to do it.
- "*yawn*"
- Chihiro (Veritas): You must be getting tired.
- Chihiro (Veritas): The backup should be done soon. We can go out and get some coffee once it's done.
- "Is there any place open at this hour?"
- Chihiro (Veritas): Don't worry. There's a spot I like to go to when I'm working graveyard shifts.
- Chihiro (Veritas): I told you last time that I'd treat you to some coffee, so I insist.
- Chihiro (Veritas): We'll be done soon. Just hold out a little while longer.
> The server maintenance was done soon after that. I then followed Chihiro outside.
- "Is there really a cafe open around here?"
- Chihiro (Veritas): It should be just around this corner.
- Chihiro (Veritas): There. Follow me, Sensei.
- Chihiro: ― Heehee. There's nothing like a good coffee after work.
- Chihiro: ― Hmm? You thought we were going to a cafe?
- Chihiro: ― Sorry to disappoint. Nothing is open at this hour.
- Chihiro: ― A vending machine is the only place you can get a drink.
- Chihiro: ― I come here to have a can of coffee
- Chihiro: ― whenever I'm finishing an all-nighter.
- Chihiro: ― This vending machine is always here,
- Chihiro: ― even if no one else is this late.
- Chihiro: ― Doing its job, ready to help someone in need.
- Chihiro: ― I sort of admire that.
- Chihiro: ― It reminds me of the work that I do...
- Chihiro: ― That's why, Sensei,
- Chihiro: ― it'd make me happy if you came to appreciate this coffee like I do.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Whew. Look at the time.
- Chihiro (Veritas): I got so wrapped up in our conversation that I didn't realize it's almost dawn.
- Chihiro (Veritas): The late-night trains aren't even running anymore. It will be difficult to make it back to the Millennium dorms.
- Chihiro (Veritas): Is there somewhere I can crash at Schale?
> I walked Chihiro back to the Schale residence hall and gave her an open room to get some rest.
