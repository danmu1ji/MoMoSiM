## MomoTalk 3

- Chihiro: Sensei, could you do me a favor?
- Sensei: How can I help?
- Chihiro: I'm going to present a proposal to the company delivering equipment to Millennium this afternoon.
- Sensei: But the president is very stubborn, so I want to be prepared before I bring it up.
- Sensei: I want to do this Veritas' way.
- Sensei reply option: What do you mean by "Veritas' way"?
- Sensei reply option: You mean through hacking?
- Chihiro: Yeah. I want to install a back door on the node before I work. I'd like you to help.
- Sensei reply option: Node? Back door?
- Sensei reply option: How can I help? I don't know how hacking works.
- Chihiro: It's okay. I know it sounds difficult, but you just have to be there.
- Sensei: I'll see you then.

- Sensei: I thought that's what hackers were supposed to do... Or is that just a stereotype?
