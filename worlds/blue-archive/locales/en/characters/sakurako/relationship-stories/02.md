# Sakurako — Relationship Story 2

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Sakurako/Relationship_Story/2
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> I visited the shopping district to pick up some supplies for Schale.
> In the middle of a row of shops, peering in the window of an accessory store, was Sakurako.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Um... Hmm...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Hm. This is... Hmm. Or, is it...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): *sigh*
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): ...!? H-Hm! Ah. Sensei!? H-How are you, um, how are you doing today?
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Huh? Me?
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Ah, well... I just happened to be passing through this neighborhood, to—
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Well, nevermind. I was passing through, and I noticed... I was looking at the items in this store, because...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): As a matter of fact, I was considering changing some of my accessories.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): It's not that I have much knowledge or interest about such things.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): But...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Well, I'm not sure if you'll understand, but...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): If I see everyone else pursuing a hobby, or a style, and I don't at least look into it...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I feel as if I must be falling behind. Does that...make sense?
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): You may have noticed that I rarely leave the cathedral. If there are things to take care of outside, other Sisters usually handle them.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): It's not that I don't want to join them on occasion...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): But, someone has to stay behind and handle all the administrative work.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): So I suppose it's natural that I'm a bit behind when it comes to the state of the outside world.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): The other Sisters tell me things, of course, and I try to understand and imagine it based on their descriptions.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): But, of course, sometimes my imagination differs from the truth.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Well...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): For example, like the MomoTalk I sent you this morning...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Heh, you're always so considerate of me.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Thank you, Sensei.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Anyway, as long as I'm here, I thought I'd pick out an accessory.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Do you, by any chance, know anything about which designs are in fashion?
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): And, if so, would you mind sharing that knowledge with me?
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Ah. Well, that's a shame.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I suppose you are quite busy as well.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Excuse me.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I've taken up enough of your valuable time. I'll take it upon myself to—
- Store Owner: Ah, excuse me, valued customer! I've noticed you've been looking at the show window for a little while. Can I offer you assistance? Are you looking for something in particular?
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): A-Ah. I'm very sorry. I hope I wasn't disturbing anyone.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I'm just having, um, a bit of trouble picking something out...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): As you can see, I'm not very knowledgeable about fashion.
- Store Owner: ...Aha!
- Store Owner: I see, I see! Not to worry, valued customer!
- Store Owner: I will find the perfect accessory for you!
- Store Owner: Please, come in, come in! You'd look good in anything, of course, but I have a few suggestions to get you started!
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): H-Huh? ...Uh, hold on! Um?!
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Wh... Wait!
> I followed Sakurako and the shopkeeper inside.
> The shopkeeper was making suggestion after suggestion to an overwhelmed Sakurako. I rescued her and we made our escape back outside.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Phew. What a day.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Thank you so much, Sensei.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): If you weren't here... *shudder* I don't even want to think about that.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): As I suspected. Catching up to the rest of the world will be quite difficult.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): ...Huh? You don't think there's a need to catch up?
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): B-But... But I'm a Sister. I ought to study such things in order to understand others.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Sensei...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Heehee... Heeheehee.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I must admit I feel quite relieved to hear you say that.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Oh dear. I'm still getting help from you. As a Sister, it's a bit embarrassing to be on the receiving end like this.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): ...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): But... I can't say I truly dislike it, in this case.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I wonder why? Despite the fact that my flaws and shortcomings have been on display...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Perhaps...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Well, to me, perhaps you are someone who stays the same.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): You are, as you put it, someone "stable and constant."
> I walked a smiling Sakurako back to the cathedral before returning to Schale.
