> Sakurako led me back to one of the inner corridors of the cathedral.
> That late at night, all the lights were off and there were no signs of anyone else.
- ???: Thank you for coming, Sensei.
- ???: I've been waiting.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I feel I owe you thanks, and also an apology...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Thanks for responding to my call, and an apology for the lateness of the hour.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I needed a place and a time when no one would be around.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): The reason I asked you here today is...quite personal.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I was hoping you would hear my confession.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Hearing others' confessions and helping them to pray is usually my job.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): But for this moment, today...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I wish for you to hear my secret, Sensei.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Heehee. I suppose one could say that. Or, then again, maybe not.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): And, in your case... Perhaps you've been expecting this.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): But... I admit, I'm still hesitant.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): It's something that might become yet another burden for you to shoulder. The last thing I'd want is to be a source of worry for you.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): And, just the knowledge of it...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): You can turn around and go back to Schale right now, if you'd like. You don't need to hear anything more tonight.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I swear to you, I won't be upset or disappointed, if that is your choice.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Heehee. Is that so? I admit your response is not unexpected.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I had a feeling you'd be amenable, which helped me gather my courage to try. So hearing you say that has heartened me.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): So, in that case...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Shall we take a walk? I'd like to calm my nerves.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): The truth is that I, as a member of the Sisterhood...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I know some things that I cannot speak of to the rest of the world. In some cases, there are measures in place against divulging the information.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I think, to a certain extent...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Any organization with the Sisterhood's size and longevity is bound to have secrets.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): One might call it inevitable.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Of course, even within the Sisterhood, most do not know all the secrets we hold, or only know about them within the confines of scripture.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): There are so many considerations which make these things impossible to speak of with others.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): And, unfortunately...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I can't reveal this information, even to you, Sensei. That's another reason why I'm struggling.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Ah. Yes. Just as I feared...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): It seems insensitive to tell you that I have secrets and not reveal them to you.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Sensei...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Heehee.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Now where have I heard something like that before?
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Well...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): To be honest, that was my goal in calling you today.
- Sakurako: ― I don't want to hide anything...
- Sakurako: ― Not from you, of all people.
- Sakurako: ― But, in my position,
- Sakurako: ― it's hard for me to reveal everything...
- Sakurako: ― Still,
- Sakurako: ― it is a relief just to be able to tell you
- Sakurako: ― that I have a secret to keep.
- Sakurako: ― I know
- Sakurako: ― you may not understand.
- Sakurako: ― I don't expect that...
- Sakurako: ― Nonetheless, I...
- Sakurako: ― I want you to accept me for who I am.
- Sakurako: ― Um...
- Sakurako: ― I was nervous.
- Sakurako: ― I thought you might not trust me once you knew...
- Sakurako: ― I never dreamed
- Sakurako: ― I would get that answer, and so soon.
- Sakurako: ― I sincerely thank you, Sensei.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I'm happy that I will be able to pray for you with a heart that is even lighter and more sincere than before.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): May all your future endeavors be blessed with happiness and good fortune.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I've said it before, perhaps. But I hope you can sense the strength of my emotion in the repetition.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Every hour that I am awake, every moment...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I will pray for you, Sensei.
> We walked around the cathedral a bit longer, chatting about lighter subjects, before I returned to Schale.
