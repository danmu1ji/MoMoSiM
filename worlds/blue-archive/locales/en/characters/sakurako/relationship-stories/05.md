- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I consider this a very large problem. I wish for all Sisters to feel at peace and at home in the cathedral.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): So of course, I had to investigate. I didn't want to act on a false impression.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): And I even took a Sister aside somewhere quiet a few times, so I could ask them one-on-one.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Unfortunately... yes. As you may suspect, acting on my own, I only worsened the problem.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): For some reason, this particular issue appears to be out of my capabilities to resolve. That is why I am coming to you for help.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): As a Sister, I ought not do so. I should be taking responsibility for my own inadequacies, not foisting them onto a teacher.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): However... Sometimes it's necessary to prioritize finding a solution over one's own pride.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): And this is one of those situations. Or at least, that's what I believe.
