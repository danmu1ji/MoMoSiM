> I showed up at Sakurako's office in the cathedral for the counseling session she requested.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Thank you for coming, Sensei.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I appreciate you going out of your way for me, even though it's a personal matter.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): It would've been more proper for me to seek you out, not the other way around, but unfortunately, I am unable to do so at this moment.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Ah, I see I don't need to explain. Thank you, Sensei.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Then, let me get straight to the point. I don't wish to waste your time.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Today, I wanted to ask you about hobbies.
- "Hobbies?"
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Yes. Not long ago, I was asked a question while listening to another Sister's confession.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): She was telling me that she was having a hard time because she'd had less time for her hobbies.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): She paused at one point, and asked me, "What are your hobbies, Sister Sakurako?"
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): And... I was unable to answer.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I wish I could, but I don't believe I have anything I could consider a hobby.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): To be frank, I'm not sure I know exactly what a hobby is.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Of course I don't feel that just because everyone else has a hobby, I need to as well.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): However...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): That's correct. Now that I've told you, I suppose this story isn't strictly about me.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Perhaps I struggle to understand hobbies because my day-to-day duties are set in stone, and I rarely have time left over.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): And when I do, I'm usually double-checking preparations for an upcoming event at the cathedral, or tending to the schedules of the other Sisters.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Ah, don't get me wrong. I don't mean to complain. I don't feel stressed or hounded by deadlines, and I am not unhappy with my duties. But all the same...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Sensei.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): How does it feel to have "free time"? What is a hobby?
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I've heard that you have hobbies that you occasionally spend your time and energy on.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): So I wanted to know how one obtains a hobby, and why...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Huh?
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Oh, so there's not necessarily a meaning or a goal. I see. So... Is it more like a habit?
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Indeed. That makes some sense.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I understand my mistake. I was thinking of a hobby as a conscious choice of activity, selected out of self-awareness or self care.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): But I understand now that developing a hobby is more subconscious than I thought. I think I understand a bit better now.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Then, is it possible that I do have a hobby that I'm unaware of?
- "Think about things you did recently when there wasn't work to do."
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Ah... I see.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Recently, what have I done without intention... What did I do when I had a bit of time...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Let me think. I often have some downtime between tasks, or when I'm traveling.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): So, in that time...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I believe that's the time when I've sent you MomoTalks, Sensei.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Or, when I remember something interesting, and talk to you about it...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Or, when I ponder something troubling, and request a counseling session...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Or...uh...something...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Like...that...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Hmm?
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Uh. Hm... Well.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Sensei.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): My apologies, but could I request that we end our conversation here for today?
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): As for a solution... I believe I found one. However, I find myself in need of a moment to compose myself.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): I know I'm being rather rude right now.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): But I hope you'll show your usual understanding.
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): So, please. For today...
- Sakurako (The Sisterhood): Let's end it here.
- "..."
- "...?"
> Sakurako escorted me out of the cathedral, looking uncomfortable the whole time.
