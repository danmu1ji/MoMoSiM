# Sakurako

## Description

A devout student of the Sisterhood belonging to Trinity Integrated Academy. Sakurako is widely trusted by other students for her serious attitude. However, being too serious, she sometimes deviates from trends and common sense.

## Appearance

Sakurako has hip-length silver hair with bangs and magenta eyes.
