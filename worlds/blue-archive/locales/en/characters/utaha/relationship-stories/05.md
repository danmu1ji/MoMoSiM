> Schale Suburban Shop
> As Utaha and I arrived in town to buy things for work, we came across a newly opened shop.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Huh? This place is new.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): There's a lot of young women waiting here. I wonder what they're selling...
- Nail Salon Employee: Hello. Welcome to the grand opening of Momo Nails!
- Nail Salon Employee: We're offering free nail care to celebrate our opening, so please come give us a try!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Heh, it's a nail salon.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Come to think of it, there are students at Millennium who stick gel and gems on their nails.
- "Utaha, why don't you give it a try?"
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Me? Get my nails done?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I'm fine. My nails aren't that pretty anyways...
- Nail Salon Employee: You there, lady! You have a great look about you!
- Nail Salon Employee: If you're up for it, how about some free nail care?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): You mean me? But I've never had my nails done...
- Nail Salon Employee: That's great. It's a lot more rewarding to care for nails like yours!
- Nail Salon Employee: True fashion runs from the top of your head to the tips of your toes! There's nothing like pretty nails to boost your confidence!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): But...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Well...if Sensei insists.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Then maybe just a little bit of nail care...
- Nail Salon Employee: Okay, please have a seat here!
- Nail Salon Employee: Your nails are finished. What do you think?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): It's been a while since I saw my fingers so clean.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): There are no more cracks, and all the oil has been cleaned... It's almost as if I'm looking at somebody else's hands.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Nail art is really amazing.
- "How would you like nail care every week?"
- Utaha (Engineering Department): ...No, it's fine. If I decorate my nails it'll bother me when I work on the machines.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Cracked and oily hands suit me better.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I'm not embarrassed. I might look dirty...but dirty hands are the meister's mark of honor.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): ...But maybe I'll leave my pinky finger decorated to remember this.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Excuse me, is it possible to have gel applied to just my pinky finger?
- Nail Salon Employee: Yes, that's no problem. Give me a few seconds.
> After the nail salon, I returned to Schale with a rather excited Utaha.
