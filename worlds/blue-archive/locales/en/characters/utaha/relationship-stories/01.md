> Engineering Department Workshop
> I went to the Engineering Department to pick up the pencil holder I asked Utaha to make.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): No... This won't do...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Ah, Sensei, you're here. Please wait, I need a little more time.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Ah, Sensei, you're here. Please wait, I need a little more time.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I've come across a small, unanticipated problem.
- "It's fine. Take your time."
- Utaha (Engineering Department): ...Sure. Normally this doesn't happen, but the machine seems to be in poor condition today.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I hope we don't have the same issue...
> Soon after...
> It's complete. Please inspect the finished product.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): ...
- "Wow, it's better than I expected. This should..."
> Screen shakes
- Utaha (Engineering Department): That's not it!
- "What's the matter?"
- Utaha (Engineering Department): That's not the pencil holder I was trying to make!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Look at it. The finish is sloppy, and the lamination isn't curved like on the blueprint!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): And the durability is too weak! This wouldn't even be able to deflect a .22 caliber!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): No. Even if it's fine with you, it's not fine with me. This is a defective product!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): No. Even if it's fine with you, it's not fine with me. This is a defective product!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I'd be so embarrassed if all the other students found out I gave you a defective gift...
- "Really, it's okay..."
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Don't worry, Sensei. I'll create a truly perfect pencil holder for the honor of the Engineering Department.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Please wait just a little longer!
> But with Utaha's strict standards...
> It's complete. Please inspect the finished product.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): This isn't any good either!
- "...Now what?"
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Look at this! There's a mark where we removed the support!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I guess there's a limit to extrusion printers...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Again! We'll print it again!
- "I don't care if it's defective. Please stop this..."
> After that, it took another ten pencil holders before Utaha was satisfied.
