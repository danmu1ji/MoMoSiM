> Central Kivotos Shopping Mall
> I went to the shopping mall to buy emergency tools at Utaha's request.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I can't believe there's not a single electric tool in a facility as big as Schale.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Schale is already a soft target for delinquent students, so you need to be able to do simple repairs yourself.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): You can't always depend on repairmen or Millennium students.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): ...Here's the tool shop.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Now, Sensei. Choose a tool of your liking. I'll teach you how to use it.
- Choose a 1,000-yen multi-tool.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): ...Sensei, are you really going to buy that?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): At first glance, multi-tools may seem inexpensive and versatile, but frequent use can damage a machine because the tools don't fit to the exact specifications.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): In the long run, using a cheap tool like that will cost you more.
- "What would you recommend?"
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I recommend this: Manaslu's premium tool set for experts.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): The tool's been polished via diamond sharpening. It's so sharp it can pierce a tank! And the case is made of titanium that's strong enough to withstand big explosions!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): This tool set alone should be able to handle pretty much anything that happens in the office.
- "Isn't this a little too much for a beginner like me?"
- Utaha (Engineering Department): It's not too much. You need this to be properly prepared for an emergency.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): You'll regret not buying this tool set when a tank comes roaring through the office.
- "This is 100,000 yen?!"
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Like I said, using cheap tools might cost more in the long run.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): It's cheap if you consider it an investment for the future.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Some say a true craftsman doesn't care about what tools she uses, but I don't think you can be a craftsman at all without being picky about your tools.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Here, Sensei, you'll be well on your way to mastery with this tool set!
> In the end, I bought the most expensive tool set on Utaha's insistence.
> And I had to personally pay for every yen that went over budget...
