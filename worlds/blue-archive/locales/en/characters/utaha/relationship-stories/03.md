> Schale Executive Office
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Sensei, I'm here.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): ...Oh, right, Sensei is out on a business trip.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Then I guess I'm all alone in the Schale House today.
- Puppy: Ruff, ruff ruff!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Oh my, why is there a puppy in Schale?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Oh. This must be the puppy toy Sensei mentioned.
- Puppy: *pant pant*
- Utaha (Engineering Department): It's quite sophisticated for a toy.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Sensei said I shouldn't disassemble it, but a little peek might be all right...
> Utaha picks up and hugs the puppy.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): What's this? It's so soft and warm.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Unlike artificial skin made of urethane or gelatin, it smells like real fur.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): And the temperature... Is there a separate thermal insulation system built in?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I should take a closer look.
> Utaha picks up the puppy and stares into its face.
- Puppy: *pant pant*
- Utaha (Engineering Department): There's moisture coming from its nose and mouth.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): This amount of moisture must be detrimental for a robot. How is it waterproofed?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): And its eyes are following me as if it understands my movement.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): It looks like it's equipped with advanced motion-tracking AI.
> The puppy struggles to get away from Utaha.
- Puppy: *whimper* *whimper*
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Don't move, puppy. I need to check your motor system.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Such natural movements... How many motors are there in this little leg?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Its toes are fully actuated, and there's no artificial noise when squeezing it!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): This...isn't just a simple toy. This is an extremely high-tech automaton!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): This technology even surpasses the skills of the Engineering Department...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): How could such a high-tech creation exist as just a simple toy without us knowing about it? How far behind are we in terms of technology...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): ...Ah, Sensei.
- "Was everything all right while I was away?"
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Yeah. I was able to learn a lot about my limits...
- "In such a short amount of time?"
- Utaha (Engineering Department): This toy puppy you ordered... I've lost all confidence after playing around with this machine.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Perhaps I should stop calling myself a meister of Millennium, and start learning all over again from the beginning.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Don't pretend you don't know. Look at this finely-crafted animaloid!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Don't pretend you don't know. Look at this finely-crafted animaloid!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Creating an animaloid that's indistinguishable from a real dog is difficult even with the technology the Millennium School has on hand!
- Puppy: Ruff, ruff ruff!
- "Where did you get that puppy?"
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Huh? This isn't the toy puppy you ordered? Then this...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): This is a lost puppy that wandered into Schale...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I was tricked by what you said.
- "Didn't you ever stop to consider it was a real puppy?"
- Utaha (Engineering Department): You can barely tell the difference with animaloids these days.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): It may be difficult to reproduce the touch and body temperature of a real dog, but the movement of the joints can be made extremely realistic with our current technology.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Even if it's hard now, in ten years it's likely that we'll be able to make a toy dog that's indistinguishable from a real dog...
> After that, I was forced to listen to Utaha ramble about the latest technology.
