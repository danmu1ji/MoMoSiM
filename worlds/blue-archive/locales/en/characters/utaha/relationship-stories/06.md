> I went to the shopping mall after Utaha invited me...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Hey there, Sensei.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Thank you for coming today. I'm sure you have more exciting things to do.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Don't worry about it?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Heehee. Don't say I didn't warn you!
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Only a few Millennium students even know about the hardware store we're going to.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Think of it like a secret treasure trove.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Shall we get going?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Long time no see, Mr. Owner.
- Owner: Ah, Meister Utaha. It's been so long that I almost forgot what you looked like.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): It's only because you're too good at your job.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): If your tools broke more often, I'd have more reasons to come!
- Owner: Is that so? Maybe I should start making worse tools...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): But in the end, loyalty is only as durable as the quality of service.
- Owner: Hahaha! I suppose I'll just have to live with the flaw of being dedicated to my craft. So, what can I do you for today?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): For starters, I need a set of hex head wrenches and a 100 cm vernier caliper. There's some other stuff I want too, but let me take a look around first.
- Owner: Sure, go ahead. Say, who's this with you?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): This is Schale's Sensei.
- Owner: Ah, Sensei! I've heard so much about you. If you're ever in need of tools, keep me in mind!
- "Yes, of course..."
- Utaha (Engineering Department): *whistle* I think that's it for tool replacements...
- "That's a very...impressive tool set."
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Oh, this?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Heehee, I wouldn't call it impressive, but I would call it mine.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): More specifically, I call it the Meister Tool Set.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): It might seem a bit excessively customized, but...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I'm the one using the tools, so it's all good, isn't it?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): After all, this is the very same tool set I've used ever since first becoming a meister.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Although now that I think about it... None of the tools in the tool set are actually the same.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): No matter how durable they are, I've had to replace all of them at one point or another.
- "In that case..."
- Utaha (Engineering Department): ...If everything has been replaced, can it really be called the same set of tools?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Haha. Good question, Sensei.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Let's say you have a ship. As time goes on, parts of the ship need replacing.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): And so you keep replacing parts until you reach the day that all of the ship's parts have finally been replaced. So, Sensei, is it the same ship?
- "That's a tricky question... "
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Here's what I think...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I think the replaced ship parts are irrelevant. What matters is if the people manning the ship are the same. As long as that's the case, I consider it to be the same ship.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Without someone to make use of it, isn't a ship just a hunk of deformed metal and wood? If function defines what a ship is, then wouldn't function define its identity?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): A profound interpretation? Or a convoluted one?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Regardless, that's how I view the relationship between a meister and her tools.
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Tools are created for and defined by their uses, so without someone to use them, is a lump of bent metal even a tool in the first place?
- Utaha (Engineering Department): I don't know if that sounds strange... But it definitely sounds a bit embarrassing...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): But being a meister with tools worth showing off...
- Utaha (Engineering Department): Heehee. Doesn't sound too bad to me.
> As we continued shopping, Utaha seemed to walk around with a bit more confidence in her step than usual.
