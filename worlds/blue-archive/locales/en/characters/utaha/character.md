# Utaha

## Description

The club leader of the Engineering Club at Millennium Science School. Without straying from that title, Utaha continues to develop all sorts of various robots. One she always brings along, Rai-chan, is known for having capabilities that can rival several hundred PMC Automata.

## Appearance

Utaha has very long violet hair tied at the ends and violet eyes.
