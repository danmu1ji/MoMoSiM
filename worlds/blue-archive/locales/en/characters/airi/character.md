# Airi

## Description

Enrolled at Trinity Integrated Academy, Airi is a member of the After School Sweets Club. She has an easygoing personality and treasures spending time talking with her friends while eating sweets more than enything else. She loves ice cream and has recently developed a taste for mint chocolate.
