# Miyo\n\nMiyo is a member of the Wildhunt Art Academy and the head of its unofficial smuggling group, Free Trade Cartel. Her goal is to see the club disbanded. However, being timid and unable to rein in the other members, she ends up getting dragged along for their activities.
