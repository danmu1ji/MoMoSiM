# Miyo — Relationship Story 1

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Miyo/Relationship_Story/1
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> Schale Office
> The next day.
- "Come in."
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Hello...
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Sakurai Miyo, reporting in for today's Student Rep duties.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): P-Please don't say that. I should be the one thanking you, Sensei.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): You've given me a rare opportunity to go outside...
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Ugh... It's been so long since I've experienced something new... Transcendent freedom...
- "I know it's hard to leave Wildhunt."
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Yes. They...are even more restrictive with Literature majors like me. We have mandatory cloister times.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): After all, you don't need fresh air or sun to write.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): And a lot of great classics were actually written in confinement...and from behind bars...
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...You know, maybe I should confess all the Cartel's smuggling activities and get sent to prison. It could do wonders for my writing and focus.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Wh-What are you...?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): S-S-Sensei...?! What are you doing...?
> Schale Lounge
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): This is a...lounge?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): And you want me to relax and write? Ooh, such nice stationery!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): And I can help myself to the drink fridge? And you have snacks?!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): B-But I came here as Student Rep today... If I don't help you, then I'm—
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...My only job as a writer is to write?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Well... You're not wrong...
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...Thank you, Sensei.
> I left Miyo in the lounge and went back to my work.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Hmmmm...
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): So... How should I reveal that the protagonist has an incurable, beautiful, tragic, crippling disease? How best to twist the knife?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): And the in-laws mobilize a tank unit and launch an offensive to keep the lovers apart... Heh. Oh, this is good.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Okay, maybe a little...homage...from another work?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): "RAAAH! If heaven made you, why did it also make me?!"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): BRR-at-tat-tat! Pew! "Do not give up yet! We still have 200 anti-tank shells!"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Ha, yes. That's it! It's perfect! I'm a genius!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): And next up is the steamy—
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...Sensei? Do you...require something?
- "Oh... I got this gift basket and thought you might..."
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...Were you listening?
- "...I heard nothing."
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): You heard me talking to myself, right? You. Heard. Everything.
- "...No! Not EVERYTHING. I was working, and—"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): From where?
- "...Something about a beautifully tragic disease?"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): That IS everything!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Aaaaaaaaaah...!
> Miyo skittered out of Schale and disappeared in a flurry of paper.
