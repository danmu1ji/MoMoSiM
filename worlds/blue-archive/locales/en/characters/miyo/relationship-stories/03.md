# Miyo — Relationship Story 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Miyo/Relationship_Story/3

> I arrived and waited for Miyo.
> Vintage Shopping District
- "Fufu, finally! I've been waiting forever!"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): What's that supposed to mean?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Excuse me. I am exactly on time. Just who do you think I am?
- "Umm... The queen of crime novels?"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): That is...fundamentally incorrect!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Although...I agree...that I understand certain tropes.
- "Anyway, why did you call me here?"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): I have a real job. There's a certain "something" I need to procure for a client. It's at this vintage shop.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ....And, frankly, I could use another pair of eyes. It'd be nice to wrap this up quickly.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...Just one more job. Then I'm done. I'll go legit, I swear.
- "Sure!"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Ugh...!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Spare me the sarcasm!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Ah... Permission... About that.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Well, the thing is...
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...I didn't.
- "...What?"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Wildhunt has a Free Outing Club. Unofficial, of course.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): They helped me sneak out.
- "...I really wonder what kind of academy Wildhunt is."
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Well... It's an art school. Isn't that obvious?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): So, artists can be quirky. Everyone is a little...insane, to be honest.
- "And that includes you?"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Well, time to get to work!
- "You ignored me...!"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Look at this typewriter, Sensei! It's the MilkySweet 42 model! Check out that chassis!
- "It has such a cute design."
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Yes. But it's unfortunate, really. It never caught on in the market.
- "I wonder why..."
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): See the letters? This keyboard has a custom layout. They claimed it was uniquely ergonomic!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): The only problem was that...well, people didn't want to relearn how to type. It's hard being an innovator.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): The model was retired after the initial batch of a million typewriters.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Well, that's why I have one in my collection. So I should be grateful for their ambition!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Huh? Oh! Look, Sensei! A Demington model, and in great condition. Ah! And the matching fountain pen!
> We continued window shopping at the antique store, stopping at every typewriter.
> Suddenly, Miyo stopped in front of an inconspicuous shop.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): One second, Sensei!
> Miyo left the store with a new bundle in her hands.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): That's it! Let's go, Sensei.
- "What's with the bundle...?"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Ah, it's the...certain "something" I promised the Free Outing Club.
- "Free Outing Club?"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Yes. This is in exchange for them sneaking me out and...
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...Ah.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Uh, ah, u-um...
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...I'm not allowed?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): And am I allowed to sneak out if it means seeing you?
- "Eh?"
- "Me?!"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): L-Last time you said that you wanted to see me too, didn't you?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): You said you truly, deeply desired to see me. In MomoTalk!
- "Not quite with those words, but...!"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): D-Do you understand it now? You're the bad one, Sensei. You're the corrupting influence.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): So just stop talking about it. Let's go!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): We still have many typewriters...and so little time.
> I continued browsing the market with Miyo.
