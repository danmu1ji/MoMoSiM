> Downtown
> The next morning, I went to Wildhunt for work.
- "I feel like I'm here a lot lately."
> And after hours of work, just as I got back to Shiratori City...
- ???: Hm? Sensei?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Good morning...
- "Miyo? What brings you out of Wildhunt's walls?"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): W-Well...
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): I received an offer from a publisher here. I was granted a few hours' leave because this is considered a work meeting.
- "A publication offer?!"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Yes. You may remember that I was working on a story while visiting you at Schale.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): And...I finished! A romance imprint is interested in my work.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): D-Don't flatter me! Please, I haven't even seen the contract yet.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Oh, when's the meeting?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): It's in 20 minutes, 2 o'clock. And it's in this building. I've been trying not to pace too much. I don't want to look desperate, right?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...What? You'll join me?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): I mean... That's asking so much of you. You undoubtedly had a long day, and...
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): That's true. I've never signed a legitimate contract before. And you know so much about them.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): I-In that case...
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): I'd be happy to have you, Sensei.
> Miyo and I headed to the publisher's office together.
> Publisher Meeting Room
- Publishing Staff: Ah! There she is! Our special writer with her ribbons and that eye for romance!
- Publishing Staff: But...Schale's Sensei? What are you doing here?
- "I heard you're offering her a contract. I'm here for support."
- Publishing Staff: Ah, right. So invested! How wonderful. Look at the two of us, encouraging the youthful voices of tomorrow!
- Publishing Staff: Since you're all busy, let's get to it. Now, Miyo, you're a smart girl, so you probably know all of this.
- Publishing Staff: The industry isn't what it used to be! There's so much overhead. Writing may be easy for you, but publishing is expensive!
- Publishing Staff: Sure, there's paper, but also editors, designs, localization, and MARKETING! You wouldn't believe how much marketing costs.
- Publishing Staff: And, in the unfortunately possible event that the printed books don't sell, there are storage fees. Climate-controlled warehouses! Armed guards!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Y-Yes. I am familiar with these concepts.
- Publishing Staff: Excellent. We're crystal clear. I just wanted to make sure we were on the same page!
- "But isn't this the normal cost of doing business?"
- Publishing Staff: Business! You're speaking my language, Sensei. Now, let's get to the proposal.
- Publishing Staff: Miyo... How would you like to be an equal partner?! We're giving you the opportunity to invest in your OWN book's publication.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...?
- Publishing Staff: That's right. We'll publish, and YOU get the privilege of paying for printing, editing, designing, and marketing!
- Publishing Staff: Meanwhile, we'll handle the rest!
- "Wait, that's just Miyo doing everything..."
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Wow...!
- "Miyo...?"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Oh, I bet that's not all, right?
- Publishing Staff: Huh?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): After I pay all that "investment" money and get published, naturally, I'll have to buy 500 or so copies, right? So we can game the Kronos Bestseller List?
- Publishing Staff: Oh...! You're familiar!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): And I bet I'd also have to enroll in some of your paid writing courses... You know, to make sure my next book with you is even better? Is that right?
- Publishing Staff: Absolutely! You're already up to speed. We're investing in your future, Miyo!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Yes, of course! Wow!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...You scammers need a new playbook.
- Publishing Staff: Th-The contract...!
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Let's go, Sensei. I have no more business here.
> Nearby Park
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): *sigh*
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Please don't look at me like that, Sensei. It's fine. I'm tougher than I look.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Hm? How did I spot the scam?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): It's simple. I just imagined what I would do if I wanted to...exploit some naive girl with a dream, then worked from there. It's how I come up with our smuggling ideas, too.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): So...
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...*sob*
- "Miyo?!"
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Why is this what I'm good at? Being scum?
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): I can't get anyone to care about my work, my writing, the thing I focus on the most.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...My only talent is crime.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): I'll never be a writer. No one wants my silly romances. I'm just doomed to be...THIS, aren't I?
- "Beats me."
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): How comforting. You have such a way with words.
- "I'm not worried, Miyo. You'll find your path."
- "Because you know the wrong paths better than anyone."
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Avoiding the wrong path...is just as important as finding the right path.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): Yeah. I could've been that girl who falls for scams. So cute. So naive. But I'm me.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): It's a benefit. I'm blessed to be so...clever.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): ...Although I still hate what I am.
- Miyo (Free Trade Cartel): S-Shut up!
> We talked a bit longer in the park before returning back.
