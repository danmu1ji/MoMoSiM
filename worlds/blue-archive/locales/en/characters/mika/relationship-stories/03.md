# Mika — Relationship Story 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> I arrived late, since work ran long.
- Mika (Tea Party): Wow! ☆ It's Sensei!
- "Sorry I'm so late. Did you wait long?"
- Mika (Tea Party): Hmm? No, no. It's okay. I know you're really busy, Sensei. Me on the other hand, I've been free lately.
- "But what's going on? Mika?"
- Mika (Tea Party): Hmm? Well... Um...
- Mika (Tea Party): Really, nothing actually happened...
- Mika (Tea Party): I just thought it would be nice to have some tea and dessert together. If you're okay with that, of course.
- Mika (Tea Party): B-But the stores are closing early today, since it's a holiday.
- Mika (Tea Party): It's so late that I'm not sure anything local will be open...
- Mika (Tea Party): What should we do? I don't want you to have come all this way for nothing.
- Mika (Tea Party): But I don't want to take up more of your time either, Sensei. Do you just want to go back to Schale?
- Mika (Tea Party): It's probably better for you if people don't keep seeing you with a miscreant like me anyway...
- "Oh, don't worry about that."
- Mika (Tea Party): O-Okay.
- Mika (Tea Party): Somewhere to sit?
- Mika (Tea Party): You mean like my room?
- "Your room?"
- Mika (Tea Party): Yes.
- Mika (Tea Party): I don't mean to be weird or anything! And it's just a dorm room, nothing fancy...
- Mika (Tea Party): But because it's a holiday, lights out is later than usual.
- Mika (Tea Party): And I've got some roll cakes and tea to share. If I'm being honest, I'm tired of the roll cakes, but they're going to go bad soon, so...
- Mika (Tea Party): In fact, you could take it home with you if you want, Sensei! It's better than wasting food!
- Mika (Tea Party): Ah, yeah, I thought you wouldn't have too much time to spare.
- Mika (Tea Party): Come on! The dorm is this way!
- "(That building is pretty old...)"
- Mika (Tea Party): Depressing, isn't it? It's an old building, so it's not very popular. Hardly any students stay here.
- Mika (Tea Party): Ugh! Last time I was here, I even saw a rat! Kinda makes me miss the prison cell, you know?
- "It must be hard for you."
- Mika (Tea Party): Ahahaha! ☆ No, it's not that bad. The management here is great!
- Mika (Tea Party): Anyway my room is at the top floor, so we can take the emergency stairs here.
- Mika (Tea Party): Don't worry. It's just because I'm a special case with an unusual history.
- Mika (Tea Party): Be careful now. The stairs are steep.
- Mika (Tea Party): We're here! It's the room at the end!
- "It's basically an attic..."
- Mika (Tea Party): Yeah...
- Mika (Tea Party): Right... Attic...
- Mika (Tea Party): Let's see...
- Mika (Tea Party): It's just about time for...
- "For what?"
> Screen shakes
- Student RA: Roll call!
- "?!?!"
- Student RA: The main entrance is now closed! No exceptions! Every student needs to line up outside their door!
- Student RA: Roll call will begin from the top floor!
- Mika (Tea Party): Oh no! This is no good at all, Sensei. The inspector is coming this way!
- "M-Mika?!"
- Mika (Tea Party): Go in my room and hide under the blankets! Quick! You'll be in so much trouble if you get caught in here!
> I dove under the blankets Mika pointed out just in time.
- Student RA: Mika? What's with all the noise? Is something going on?
- Mika (Tea Party): Huh? No. Not really.
- Student RA: I thought I heard somebody talking to you...
- Mika (Tea Party): Ahaha! ☆ What do you mean? It's just me here. You must've heard wrong.
- Student RA: Mm hm... Would you step aside for a moment? I want to inspect your room.
- Mika (Tea Party): Whoa! Don't you trust me?!
- Mika (Tea Party): I mean... Whatever. Do whatever you want.
- Student RA: Then, please excuse me.
- "(...!)"
- Student RA: ...
- Student RA: Hmm... I suppose I did just hear wrong...
- Student RA: Well, keep the noise down, okay, Mika? This is an old building, so the walls are very thin.
- Student RA: Get some rest.
- Mika (Tea Party): Of course. Thank you! ☆
- Mika (Tea Party): Wow. Success!
- Mika (Tea Party): Sensei. It's fine. You can come out now.
- "Mika! You—"
- Mika: ― Shhh!!!
- Mika: ― ...Sensei, I tricked you.
- Mika: ― What should we do now?
- Mika: ― It looks like we're stuck here until morning.
- Mika: ― You trusted me so easily.
- Mika: ― Really, Sensei... I told you...
- Mika: ― so many times not to trust me...
- "You are—"
- Mika: ― Hmm? If you cause a scene here,
- Mika: ― everyone will find out. Are you sure you want that to happen?
- Mika: ― Of course, it doesn't matter to me either way.
- "..."
- Mika: ― What?
- Mika: ― ...You aren't mad, are you?
- Mika: ― You should know me by now.
- Mika: ― You knew I was trouble, Sensei. Didn't you...?
- Mika: ― Hey...
- Mika: ― Hey...
- Mika: ― I didn't mean to upset you...
- Mika: ― ...I don't like it when you're mad at me, Sensei...
- Mika: ― It was harmless fun.
- Mika: ― Just for a moment...
- Mika: ― I just wanted to spend one more moment with you, Sensei.
> You waited in Mika's room until the morning, leaving her a bit flustered at your hasty exit.
> The moment the dormitory door opened, you slipped out thankfully unnoticed.
