# Mika\n\nStudent of Trinity Integrated Academy and the former leader of "Pater", one of the student unions that make up Trinity. Mika is a former member of the Tea Party and is also a childhood friend of its leader, Nagisa. However, because of their opposing political positions, they make a clear distinction between their public and private lives.
