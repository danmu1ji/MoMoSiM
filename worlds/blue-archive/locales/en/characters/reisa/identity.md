# Reisa\n\nA member of Trinity Integrated Academy's Vigilante Corps, who patrols all over Trinity and works to maintain public order. People at times take her to be a mischievous child because Reisa says things that don't make sense, but she's more serious about the vigilante activities than anyone else around.
