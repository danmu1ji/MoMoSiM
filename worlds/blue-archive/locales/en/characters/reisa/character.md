# Reisa

## Description

A member of Trinity Integrated Academy's Vigilante Corps, who patrols all over Trinity and works to maintain public order. People at times take her to be a mischievous child because Reisa says things that don't make sense, but she's more serious about the vigilante activities than anyone else around. She has a habit of referring to herself by different names.

## Appearance

Reisa has waist-length hair with uneven baby-blue and baby-pink streaks.
