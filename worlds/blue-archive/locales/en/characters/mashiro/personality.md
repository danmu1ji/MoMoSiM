# Mashiro — official English introduction

A student of Trinity General School, and member of the Justice Actualization Committee. Carrying an unwieldly large rifle, Mashiro speechlessly carries out her responsibilities in providing fire support. Normally one of few words and rarely interacting with other people, once talks of justice pick up she becomes quite the conversational partner.
