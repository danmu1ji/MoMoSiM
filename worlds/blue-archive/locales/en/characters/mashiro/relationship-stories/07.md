> I was passing through the plaza when I saw Mashiro sitting on a bench.
- "Hello, Mashiro!"
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Oh, Sensei.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I wasn't aware that you were visiting Trinity. If you had informed me, I would have come to greet you.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): ...I suppose it doesn't matter, since we've run into each other here.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Oh, my book?
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I-It's a bit embarrassing, but...
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): It's...this...kind of book.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): It teaches various proverbs and four-character idioms.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): ...It looks interesting to you?
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): ...
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Fufu, yes. I do think it's very compelling.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I recommended it to Hasumi recently as well.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I don't think she read it, though...
- "You must enjoy proverbs and old tales."
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Yes, I really do. The proverbs and four-character idioms are nice, but what really engrosses me are old stories.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Old stories contain lessons that have been passed down through generations.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): It's like seeing the future through the past, as they say. Old stories can guide us to the correct path.
- "Oh, wow..."
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): And the best part of it all is that...
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): ...most proverbs contain messages of the inevitability of justice and the reward of good deeds!
- "Ah, that's true. Most of them anyways..."
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Yes, it's a universal truth that justice always prevails.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): That's why I've always liked proverbs, ever since I was young.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Even during my club activities, I often quote proverbs or sayings...
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): They tease me for being ancient or just completely ignore me when I do, though...
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): But I knew you'd understand me.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): ...You're interested, and you'd like to borrow it?
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Yes, of course!
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Heehee...
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): To think that you'd enjoy the same thing I enjoy. It makes me feel...
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): ...
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Um... It's an amazing feeling.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Y-Yes, pretty amazing.
> Mashiro and I chatted about some of her favorite old stories before I headed back.
