> Trinity Classroom
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Oh, Sensei. It's good to see you again.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Oh, my skirt? I borrowed a sewing kit from Hasumi so I could stitch it up.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): The seam might rip again if I move too much... It will have to do until the new one arrives.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I would borrow one, but Hasumi's would never fit, and Tsurugi's are always covered in the blood of her enemies.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Getting replacement Justice Task Force uniforms can be a real pain, even within Trinity.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Are you curious as to our uniforms are black, whereas the regular students wear white?
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): It's so the blood we spill of our unrighteous enemies—like those from Gehenna—won't be noticeable on our uniforms. Again, take a good look at Tsurugi's uniform.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I can slaughter my enemies without worry so long as I'm wearing this uniform.
- Gulp nervously and look afraid.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): That...was supposed to be a joke. Did I push it too far?
- "I thought you were serious, so I got a little scared."
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I'm well aware that I have not been gifted with a sense of humor. People tend to be terrorized whenever I crack jokes.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I wonder why that is.
- "Could it be how dead serious you look?"
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): That is one possibility. Heh...
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I should clarify that we do not wear black uniforms for fear of bloodstains.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Our club places importance on hygiene. If blood is to get on our uniform during a mission, we're to quickly wash it off.
- "Haha. That's another good one, Mashiro."
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): No, I was being serious...
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): There's a price to pay for attaining justice after all. Haha...
- Gulp nervously and look even more scared.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Hmm. When I'm joking, you take it seriously, and when I'm serious, you think I'm joking.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I really don't have any sense of humor, do I?
