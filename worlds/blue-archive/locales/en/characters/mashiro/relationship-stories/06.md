# Mashiro — Relationship Story 6

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> Downtown Kivotos
> I spotted Mashiro looking down at the city from atop a building while walking downtown.
> She's watching the people below with a serious look on her face.
- "I wonder what she's up to..."
> I decide to head up to the roof to see what she's doing.
> Downtown Building Rooftop
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Sensei? What are you doing here?
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): You happened to see me while you were walking down the street?
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I see. I must have been too preoccupied to notice.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): You want to know if this is a sniper mission? No, this is just how I like to pass the time.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): You can see a lot of the city from up here.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): The cars coming and going from downtown, the people hard at work, and even the students at school...
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I find it hard to believe that injustice could exist in this city when I see how orderly it operates.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): But I know there are people lurking in Kivotos waiting to break the peace.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I belong to the Justice Task Force. I should be doing more...
- "But you already work so hard, Mashiro."
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Do I? I think my efforts are average at best.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): And I'm only doing what I can to keep up with the other members of the task force. If I didn't...
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Still. If it pleases you, then perhaps being average isn't so bad. Heh...
- "Are you going to stay here longer?"
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I usually don't stay as long as I have.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): But I wouldn't mind staying a while longer if I have you with me.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): So? Will you humor me...for just a little while longer?
> Mashiro and I watched the city from the roof of the building until sunset.
