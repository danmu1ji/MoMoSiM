> Building Rooftop
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): It's said snipers are soldiers of opportunity. Patience being a virtue is no truer for anyone than it is for a sniper.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Once you have your target, you can't move a centimeter until the enemy enters the line of fire.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): No matter what temptations or aches you may face!
- "Even if you get hungry?"
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Of course. I've had to skip several meals before due to being on a mission.
- "I can't even imagine. You're incredible, Mashiro."
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I hardly think so. It simply comes with the territory.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I also wouldn't go so far as to call myself "incredible".
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): In fact, there is one thing that tests even my patience when I'm on a mission.
- "What is it?"
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): The, um...bugs.
- "Bugs?"
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Now that the weather is warmer, there are more bugs on campus and on the roof. They crawl up my arms when I'm in position.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): It's a creepy feeling, and they affect my aim.
- "Can't you shoo them away?"
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Of course not! You have to control every action you take when you're in position—even your breathing. A few bugs is no excuse to break concentration.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): It would be a disgrace to any good sniper!
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): But I haven't trained myself well enough to not have them bother me.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): That's why I wanted to ask you for help.
- "Me? What can I do?"
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I'm going to stand still and try to maintain my composure. I'd like for you to tickle me the way the bugs do.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): All right. I'm ready whenever.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): *breathes in*
- Tickle Mashiro's arm.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): This is nothing. It will take more to break my composure.
- Tickle Mashiro's cheek.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Gah. My face? No. An elite sniper would not let this faze her...
- Tickle Mashiro's neck.
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): Ahhh! Wh-What are you doing, Sensei?!
> Screen shakes
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): You can't just tickle my neck for the sake of training!
- Mashiro (Justice Task Force): I thought...you stood for justice, Sensei!
