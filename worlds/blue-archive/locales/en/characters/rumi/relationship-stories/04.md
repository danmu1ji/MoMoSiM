# Rumi — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Rumi/Relationship_Story/4

> I returned to the Black Tortoise Promenade with a spring in my step.
> Black Tortoise Promenade
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): You're here early, Sensei.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Good timing, actually. We were just about to start steaming some dumplings.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): I'm so glad you came. I was worried this would be too early for you...
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Well then... Shall we begin?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Everyone! Light your steamers!
> Gouts of steam filled the air at Rumi's command.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): It's intense, isn't it?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): The kitchen practically turns into a sauna whenever we're steaming dumplings.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Believe me, I wish I could step out...
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): I need to keep an eye on the steamers.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Too much heat and the dumplings rupture, but not enough, and they don't cook all the way through.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): You can watch from the door, Sensei. But I'm staying right here.
> I took my place by the door as Rumi suggested...
- "Uh... Hey, Rumi?"
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): What is it, Sensei? Is something wrong?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― Aah... Look at that. It got a little...see-through.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― Oh, don't worry about it. A little steam won't kill me.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― ...If I knew this was going to happen,
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― I would have worn cuter underwear.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― It's a real shame, you know?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― Haha! You don't have to look away.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― I don't mind at all.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― I didn't know there was such a side to you, Sensei...
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― Haha! I like it!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― It's not like this happens very often, so...
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― shall I make you even more uncomfortable...?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Whew, all right. The dumplings are done.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): I made plenty, so please have a bite.
> Screen shakes
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Hmm? What's the matter?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): You see, I tried something called a "sardine pie" at Trinity the other day, and it was pretty good.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): So I thought I'd try making a sardine dumpling!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): It turns out that the exact recipe for the pie is some kind of Trinity General School secret...
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): I made this just for you, so please, enjoy!
> Screen shakes
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Is there a problem? The recipe supposedly had something to do with Trinity's history and traditions...
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Come on, just one bite! Do you know how hard it is to get a dumpling into that shape?
- "...Looks like I have no choice!"
> I fearfully took up the sardine dumpling...
> I trembled at the unimaginable horror I was about to unleash on my palate, but...
> It actually wasn't that bad.
