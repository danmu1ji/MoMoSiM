# Rumi — Relationship Story 2

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> Black Tortoise Promenade
> Rumi invited me to visit her at the Black Tortoise Promenade.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Welcome, Sensei. I have been expecting you.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Are you excited? Hahaha... Don't be. This is actually kind of embarrassing for me...
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): About that...
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Normally, I would get help from the others, of course!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): But everyone's too busy to help me right now. That's why you're here!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): They're all hard at work studying, but the club is important, too. At least, that's how I feel.
- "(Something smells fishy...)"
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Here's the first dish. Please try it and tell me what you think.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Don't hold back. I want your honest opinion.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Yes, yes, I know it looks a little green. That's...um...that's how it's supposed to look!
> I gave Rumi's cooking a taste.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Okay. That's all right. I'll make a note about this dish...
- "There's nothing 'all right' about any of this!"
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): No need to get upset. There are more dishes for you to try.
> I tried Rumi's next dish.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): I see... Just as I expected. Okay, the next dish is the last one.
- "Listen, I don't know if I can survive another dish..."
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Hahaha! Oh, Sensei. You can't back out now.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): You've already come so far, and you want to give up halfway?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): In for a penny, in for a pound, as they say! It's only fair...right?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Life is full of trials like this one. You're an adult, Sensei. I'm sure you've had your fair share.
> I tried...Rumi's cooking...again!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Finally...success.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Bitterness isn't an easy flavor to use well.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): You could cut it with salt, but then you risk the whole dish becoming too salty.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): So I tried to balance it with fat and spiciness...
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): The idea is to coat the tongue to cover the bitterness and give your taste buds a little thrill. And it gives the dish a pleasant savor!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Then the spiciness helps cut through the grease quickly, giving you a sense of relief.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Based on your reaction, I'd say it worked.
- "So this is how you come up with new recipes."
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): It is. Maybe it's a bit stubborn of me...
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Flavor is such a delicate thing. You never know what combinations are going to work out.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): So that means lots of trial and error.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Hahaha... Well, it's not like I want to torture my fellow students.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): You don't want to be a taste-tester, either? Hm... Well, I still say it all worked out in the end!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): I've got 5 more recipes in mind, based on that last success.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): I'm sure they'll all be just as delicious as the dish you just tried.
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): So sit tight, because the next course is coming right up!
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): You don't want any...?
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): But I'm inspired NOW. If I try making these dishes later, it won't be the same...
- Rumi (Black Tortoise Promenade): Sensei? Where are you going?
> I tried to get away, but in the end, she caught me...
> And once again, I was fed plate after plate until I was bursting at the seams...
