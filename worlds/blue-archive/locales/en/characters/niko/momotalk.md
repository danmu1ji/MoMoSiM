## MomoTalk 4

- Niko: Sensei
- Niko: Have you heard the adage, "Anything that can go wrong, will go wrong"?
- Sensei reply option: You mean Murphy's Law.
- Sensei reply option: I hear it at work a lot.
- Niko: Yes. I think most people regard it as true.
- Sensei: Once, I forgot to do our daily equipment checks...
- Niko: And on THAT day, an accident happened, and I got in trouble.
- Sensei: I seriously couldn't believe a safety failure occurred on the ONE day I forgot.
- Sensei reply option: Don't worry, it happens to the best of us. Even me!
- Sensei reply option: That's why they say there's no such thing as a coincidence!
- Niko: But I read this recently in a book:
- Sensei: It seems this way, because we tend to remember bad memories over the good ones.
- Niko: So the times you make a mistake and nothing happens, you're less likely to remember it.
- Sensei: Ah! So there's a scientific explanation.
- Sensei reply option: Still, it's a bit of a shame...
- Sensei reply option: I wish good memories lasted longer.
- Niko: But I think there's good reason for us to remember the bad ones.
- Sensei: You can learn from your mistakes and do better next time that way.
