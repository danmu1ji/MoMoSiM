> I was curious about the preserved food Niko had mentioned, so I popped into the kitchen for a moment during my lunch break.
> Schale Building Kitchen
> As soon as I opened the kitchen door, a sizzling sound, like the pitter-patter of raindrops, filled the air, along with the strong, savory smell of oil.
- "This smell..."
- Niko (FOX Squad): Ah, Sensei. You're here.
- Niko (FOX Squad): You're a bit early. There's still a way to go until the food is ready.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Yes, that's what these are.
- Niko (FOX Squad): What I'm making right now are traditional combat rations that soldiers carried with them long ago...
> Screen shakes
- Niko (FOX Squad): It's inarizushi!
- "Inarizushi can be a combat ration?"
- Niko (FOX Squad): Yes. Inarizushi meets the criteria for combat rations perfectly.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Not only is it deep-fried in oil, making it calorically dense, but it also has low moisture content, so it doesn't spoil easily. As an added bonus, it contains plenty of vinegar, which has antibacterial properties.
- Niko (FOX Squad): If you're in a situation where you can't cook or start a fire, nothing beats an inarizushi for missions.
- Niko (FOX Squad): I suppose the problem with mine is that they're so delicious, you'll want to eat them all up right away. Fufu.
- "Now that I think about it, you're right..."
- Niko (FOX Squad): Yes. The inarizushi I'm making today is completely homemade, meaning everything was done from scratch, from the fried tofu slices to the filling.
- Niko (FOX Squad): The inarizushi I usually pack for FOX Squad's bento boxes use store-bought fried tofu, but...
- Niko (FOX Squad): I've always wanted to find the time to make the fried tofu slices myself!
- Niko (FOX Squad): Oh, would you look at the time?
- Niko (FOX Squad): Excuse me for a second...
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― Fufu. Cooked to perfection.
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― The perfect puff. For the perfect slice of fried tofu!
- "It looks delicious."
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― Ah, it's not ready yet.
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― I'll need to fry it again a little more later.
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― It needs to be fried at a lower temperature, and then again at a higher one.
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― That'll ensure all the moisture is removed, creating the perfect deep-fried tofu!
- "I didn't realize deep-frying tofu was so labor-intensive..."
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― Yes.
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― Deep-fried tofu slices take a lot of time and effort.
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― That's why
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― they were offered when praying for good health and a rich harvest.
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― All I wish for
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― is the happiness and well-being of those around me.
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― And if that feeling, packed into this dish, reaches them...
- Niko (FOX Squad): ― I'd be very happy.
- Niko (FOX Squad): The double-fried tofu is done!
- Niko (FOX Squad): Of course, it'll take a little more work to become inarizushi.
- Niko (FOX Squad): But if you don't mind, would you try a bite of the tofu first, Sensei?
- Niko (FOX Squad): It's okay. Actually, I would be really happy if you tried it, Sensei.
- "Okay, here I go..."
- Niko (FOX Squad): Yes. That's the advantage of making it by hand: You can customize the thickness and size however you like.
- Niko (FOX Squad): I might've gotten a little overboard, though. It's more thick-fried tofu than deep-fried.
- Niko (FOX Squad): But it's not bad for a first try!
- Niko (FOX Squad): Ah, Sensei. You said you don't have any plans for the rest of the day, right?
- Niko (FOX Squad): Well, since I'm going to make some inarizushi with this fried tofu, would you like to eat them with me for dinner?
- "Sounds good!"
- Niko (FOX Squad): Okay. I'll get it ready right away, so please take it easy and wait as you would on a normal day off, Sensei.
- "But..."
- Niko (FOX Squad): No, no! I insist. After all, this was all just something I wanted to do.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Besides, this is my first offering to you, Sensei.
> Afterward, we spent a pleasant evening eating the homemade inarizushi that Niko had made.
> It had a very smooth and warm flavor. That must have been because Niko made it with such sincerity.
