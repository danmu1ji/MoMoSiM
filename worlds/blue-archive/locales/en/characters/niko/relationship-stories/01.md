> A few minutes after receiving a strange MomoTalk from Niko...
> ...the bell to my office rang. I wasn't expecting any visitors.
- ???: "Hello, this is FOX EATS!"
- ???: "Your food delivery is here!"
- ???: "Oh my. The door appears to be locked this time too..."
- ???: "It can't be helped. I'll have to breach the door..."
- "I'm coming! I'm coming!"
- Niko (FOX Squad): Hello, Sensei. This is FOX EATS.
- Niko (FOX Squad): You haven't had dinner yet, right?
- Niko (FOX Squad): No matter how busy you are, you shouldn't skip your meals. It's bad for your health.
- "No, I haven't eaten yet."
- Niko (FOX Squad): Fufu, there are many ways for a special forces unit to gather intel.
- Niko (FOX Squad): For starters, your refrigerator is basically empty.
- Niko (FOX Squad): And no food delivery person visited Schale any time after lunch.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Finally, your status on your PC has been online all night, so I figured you haven't left your desk.
- "...I have no response."
- Niko (FOX Squad): You need to eat, but you also shouldn't be sitting in one place for so long.
- Niko (FOX Squad): I'll get everything ready, if you'd like to take a break in the meantime.
- "It'd be nice to step away for a little bit..."
- "So, what's for dinner?"
- Niko (FOX Squad): Fufu. It's not premade, but I did bring all of the necessary ingredients.
- Niko (FOX Squad): After all, there's nothing better than a freshly-made meal.
- Niko (FOX Squad): I can make most basic dishes with these ingredients. Do you have any requests?
- Niko (FOX Squad): As long as it's my repertoire, I'm at your service.
- "I'd be happy with anything you make..."
- "Let's go with the chef's special, then."
- Niko (FOX Squad): Hm, the "chef's special"?
- Niko (FOX Squad): My best dish is ordinary...but it'd be the inarizushi I always make for missions.
- "That sounds perfect to me."
- Niko (FOX Squad): Sounds like a plan. I'll just need a moment to simmer the fried tofu.
- Niko (FOX Squad): ...Making inarizushi in front of you like this reminds me of the past.
- "Back when we first met?"
- Niko (FOX Squad): Yes. I also made you lots of inarizushi back then.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Although my intentions were entirely different at that time...
- Niko (FOX Squad): But I think that my inarizushi is always filled with my sincere feelings.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Because it's a cherished staple amongst my squad members now.
- "What prompted you to start making inarizushi?"
- Niko (FOX Squad): Hm. I wonder when it was...
- Niko (FOX Squad): It must've started when FOX Squad first came to be. I thought it would be good to pack emergency rations, in case our missions went long.
- Niko (FOX Squad): I was pretty new to cooking, and inarizushi was one of the few things I could make.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Not that I was even very good at it. Almost all the fried tofu pouches split because I didn't stuff them properly.
- Niko (FOX Squad): All my squad members said they were delicious, though. So making inarizushi eventually became a habit of sorts.
- Niko (FOX Squad): It's funny how things work out. I didn't even like fried tofu that much back then.
- "I think I get it."
- Niko (FOX Squad): ...Yes. It's a special dish for me.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Aaand, the inarizushi is ready!
- Niko (FOX Squad): It really is a simple dish. I just have to fill up the fried tofu slices.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Frying the tofu can take a little while, but otherwise it's easy to make.
- "Thank you for the meal!"
- Niko (FOX Squad): Wait, Sensei.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Fufu. You said you wanted the chef's special for tonight, right?
- Niko (FOX Squad): I made some fried tofu udon on the side too. Please eat this for dinner tonight!
- Niko (FOX Squad): Warm soup will fill you right up, since it's cold tonight.
- "I don't mind, but what should I do with the inarizushi you made?"
- Niko (FOX Squad): Inarizushi is actually one of those dishes that tastes better the next day.
- Niko (FOX Squad): ...Sensei. Was your plan to heat up some microwavable food for breakfast or skip breakfast entirely?
- "...Is that more intel you gathered on me?"
- Niko (FOX Squad): That one was just a hunch. Heehee.
- Niko (FOX Squad): I can read you like a book, Sensei.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Have the inarizushi for breakfast tomorrow. I'll put it in the fridge for you.
- Niko (FOX Squad): And don't overdo it tonight, okay?
- Niko (FOX Squad): Good night, Sensei.
> Niko's homemade bowl of fried tofu udon warmed me from my head to my toes.
