> Schale Executive Office
> Niko was at the Schale Executive Office for her Student Rep duties.
- "...Oh?"
- Niko (FOX Squad): Is something the matter, Sensei?
- Niko (FOX Squad): Oh no... What a waste. Accidents happen, though.
- Niko (FOX Squad): How many did you order?
- Niko (FOX Squad): So there ARE students that even you are afraid of, Sensei.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Still, since staples don't expire anyway, there's nothing wrong with having a stockpile.
- Niko (FOX Squad): It reminds me of SRT. We'd have times when we'd forget to check inventory and end up accidentally reordering supplies we already had.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Once, we'd just put it another order for mines. Later, it turned out we'd had a whole crate of them in the basement, completely untouched—
- "How is that safe?!"
- Niko (FOX Squad): It's okay. Finding abandoned boxes of explosives in Kivotos is actually pretty common.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Ideally they wouldn't originate from SRT, however.
- Niko (FOX Squad): To help rectify this, SRT Academy made it a rule that we had to put nametags on everything.
- "...A rule?"
- Niko (FOX Squad): We had standard-issue supplies, so it was easy for people's belongings to get mixed up.
- Niko (FOX Squad): ...Underwear got switched doing laundry pretty often.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Actually, I believe that may have only been in the FOX Squad specifically. I wonder why that is.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Ah, Sensei. While we're at it, why don't we apply the same rule to Schale's supplies?
- Niko (FOX Squad): I actually have some nametags here. I had some labeling to do after I finished my Student Rep shift.
- Niko (FOX Squad): If we clearly label where each item is located, and who it belongs to, we can avoid situations like today's. And then you won't have any more supply problems!
- Niko (FOX Squad): Here, I bought a bunch, so I'll give you some too, Sensei.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Yes!
> After that, I went around putting nametags on Schale's various supplies with Niko.
> During the process, I ended up finding a lot of items that I'd previously thought were lost for good.
- "...That took a lot longer than expected."
- Niko (FOX Squad): Yes. Thank you for your hard work.
- Niko (FOX Squad): With this, now we can tell at a glance where things are and who they belong to.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Check this drawer for writing utensils. Printer toner and copy paper are all stored in the box next to the printer...
- Niko (FOX Squad): Oh? There's one nametag left over.
- Niko (FOX Squad): I wonder if there's anything that hasn't been labeled...
- Niko (FOX Squad): ...Right.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Then, I'll put this nametag on something that belongs to me.
- Niko (FOX Squad): There.
- "D-Did you just pat my back?! Why?!"
- Niko (FOX Squad): Heehee, no reason.
- Niko (FOX Squad): It's important to label your things. That way, there's no confusion over who the owner is.
