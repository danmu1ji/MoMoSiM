> In the evening, I headed over to meet with Niko and help with her operation.
> I had been nervously anticipating what an SRT special mission would look like...and if it'd be dangerous.
> But I got to the location Niko sent me and found myself in front of a crowded grocery store.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Yes. This is the target location for the FOX Squad's special supply mission. Point Mike.
- Niko (FOX Squad): This may appear to be a normal crowd on a peaceful street...
- Niko (FOX Squad): But don't let your guard down, Sensei. It's actually a warzone.
- Niko (FOX Squad): No, nothing that serious.
- Niko (FOX Squad): I think it would be easier to just show you.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Here, Sensei. Follow me.

> A while later...
- Store Clerk: It is now 6 PM. Therefore...
- Store Clerk: We'll now begin our closing sale!
> Screen shakes
- Crowd: RAAAAAAH!
- "Gah! The crowd...!"
- Niko (FOX Squad): We can't panic here, Sensei.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Let's make our way to Delta 3, the vegetable section, and secure carrots first!

- Citizen A: All vegetables are on sale for 99 yen starting now?
- Citizen B: Ugh, I can't hesitate! There are too many people!
- Citizen C: Which one would be better? This one, or this one?
- Citizen B: I'll just start with whatever I can grab—
- Citizen A: Did anyone see that just now?!
- Citizen B: That student! She slipped right through the crowd and picked out a plump, juicy carrot!
- Citizen C: The carrots are covered in dirt! How did she pick such a perfect one, and so quickly?
- Niko (FOX Squad): You'll generally find that dirtier carrots are fresher than the cleaned ones, as those tend to be treated with preservatives. But that also means you can't judge on color alone.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Instead, just check the core of the carrot stalk! That'll tell you how fresh it is.
- Niko (FOX Squad): You give me too much credit. This is nothing compared to our usual missions.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Next up is Delta 5—the potato section.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Please guard the shopping basket with everything you have, Sensei!
- "I won't let you down, Niko!"
- Citizen A: Augh! I was eyeing that potato! How'd she grab it so fast?!
- Citizen B: That's no ordinary student!
- Citizen C: We need to act fast, or she'll get all the good stuff before we can!
- Niko (FOX Squad): Phew! That should cover the vegetables.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Next up is Sector Charlie 1—the meat section...
- "Am I on basket duty again?"
- Niko (FOX Squad): No. The meat section alternates sales on pork and beef every 10 minutes starting at 6:10, so if we try to buy one at a time, we won't make both of them.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Sensei, I'll handle the beef at Point Bravo, so please take care of the pork at Point Papa while I do that!
- Niko (FOX Squad): Your objective is a 200-gram pack of pork belly...
> Screen shakes
- Niko (FOX Squad): Sensei, under no circumstances should you make a move before the clerk puts the half-price sticker on it!
- "U-Understood...!"
> It was easier said than done without Niko there. The crowd around Point Papa was ruthless.
- Citizen A: Move, move, move! I've had my eye on that pork shoulder this whole time!
- Citizen B: Pork ribs are 30% off? Now that's a deal I simply can't resist!
- Citizen C: Excuse me. I'll be passing through here first.
- Citizen D: Then I'll also squeeze through...
- "Ugh..."
> I was swept to-and-fro by the crowd, but never got any closer to the display.
- Store Clerk: Next up, we're kicking off a sale at the meat section!
- "(Now...!)"
- Citizen A: Hehe... First time at this supermarket, whippersnapper?
- Citizen B: The 30% off stickers at this supermarket look a lot like the 50% off stickers, so it's easy for beginners to mix them up!
- Citizen C: What are you looking at? That's the frozen meat section!
> Overwhelmed by the energy of the seasoned shopping veterans, I ended up grabbing the wrong items.
- Niko (FOX Squad): FOX 2 has returned.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Sensei. Have you secured the objective?
- Niko (FOX Squad): That's no worries! I managed to get beef at 70%, so we even out!
- Niko (FOX Squad): Fufu, some would call it "the art of negotiation."
- Niko (FOX Squad): After all, conducting successful negotiations is a required skill in the special forces.
- "I'm once again blown away by SRT's skills..."
- Niko (FOX Squad): No, meals are a part of our responsibility on missions. I can't accept your help.
- Niko (FOX Squad): While making sure we're properly fueled is obviously important, there's no guarantee that we'll always have access to good ingredients.
- Niko (FOX Squad): That's why we learn a variety of survival skills, like securing stable food sources no matter where we are. Whether we're in the mountains or behind enemy lines, we have to be able to keep ourselves fed.
- Niko (FOX Squad): After all, no operation can proceed smoothly if we're skipping meals.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Ever since being paroled from the Corrections Bureau, we've been receiving a budget from the General Student Council, but it's not enough for missions.
- Niko (FOX Squad): I want to make sure my squad members get all the nutrition they need, so I've gotten pretty used to coupon clipping.
- Niko (FOX Squad): I suppose it may all look a bit pitiable from your perspective, doesn't it, Sensei?
- "Now I understand why they call you the 'squad mom.'"

- Niko (FOX Squad): I don't think I quite qualify for that role.

- Niko (FOX Squad): Ahaha...
- Niko (FOX Squad): That's too kind of you, but thank you.
- Niko (FOX Squad): It looks like the sale is almost over now.
- Niko (FOX Squad): I need to go back and get some onions, and we'll need curry powder too.
- "The spice section looks quiet, so I'll go get that."
- Niko (FOX Squad): Ah, Sensei. Just a second.
- "Are there special tricks to buying curry too?"
- Niko (FOX Squad): No, nothing like that... This is a secret, okay?
- Niko (FOX Squad): Industrial products like curry are cheaper at another supermarket.
- Niko (FOX Squad): Then, let's get our items and check out!
> Niko and I stopped by the last supermarket to get curry powder, then we returned to Schale.
> The curry Niko made was so delicious, you would never believe the ingredients all came from a supermarket's closing sale.
