# Kaede\n\nA student of Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy, an energetic member of the discipline club. Originally, she was a mischievous child from a rural Hyakkiyako countryside, but the moment she met Tsubaki and Mimori, Kaede decided to join the discipline club saying "I want to be such a wonderful lady!". She is studying every day to get closer to her ideals, but most of the training leads to trouble for some reason.\n\nSource: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Kaede\nReview status: source extracted; pending editorial review.\n
