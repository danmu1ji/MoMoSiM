- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): Yes! Don't get too excited! Ta-da!
- "This card protector... It radiates old age..."
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): You see...this is the first card protector I ever bought. I saved up all my pocket money so I could buy it.
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): When I was but a novice battler, I didn't even know I would need something like this.
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): It was before I realized just how truly precious a single card is, so I would grab my cards with all my might and crumple them!
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): Before I knew it, I had ruined these irreplaceable treasures.

- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): It wasn't my proudest moment as a battler...
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): Gaaah! The more I think about it, the more I can't comprehend what I was thinking!
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): I'm cringing recalling my shameful past, Sensei!
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): I don't want to "be" growing up! I just want to be grown already!
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): ...Huh? Impatience is the enemy of progress? Hm... What an interesting perspective.
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): ...What do you mean it's like a card battle? How can that be?
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): You're correct, there's an optimal order you should play your cards. But what does that have to...?
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): It's similar to how I'll ruin my strategy if I'm impatient and try to force my hand?

- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): Hm... You may be on to something!
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): You never fail to impress! I guess it's because you're an adult. I'll just have to wait my turn to become a proper lady...and I'll be able to say cool things too, just like Sensei!

- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): Ah, think about it, Sensei! If you use a card protector with an opaque back, then you can cover the back of your card!
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): That can help prevent cheating from happening in the first place, so card sleeves are required for almost every tournament.
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): Never forget, Sensei! Cards are the very soul of a battler! They hold our memories and dreams, and our hearts!
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): If someone doesn't treat their cards with the utmost care, why, they can't even call themselves a battler!
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): And that's why card protectors are so important! They're a battler's armor!

- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): Ah! Did I manage to say something cool? Just like you, Sensei? Right?!
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): ...You think so?
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): Heehee... Th-Thank you, Sensei. To hear those words...

- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): Ah... That's actually an old story...
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): It's a little bit embarrassing, truth be told...
- "(The dissonance of someone Kaede's age saying "old story"...)"
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): I don't know if you know this, Sensei, but I'm actually from the countryside.
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): I might be stating the obvious, but there's not much to do out in the fields! Definitely not a whole lot of stuff to play with...
- Kaede (Inner Discipline Club): I would catch crawfish by the river...or herculean beetles in the forest...
