# Kotori

## Description

Enrolled at Millennium Science School, Kotori is a member of the Engineering Club. She loves to talk, and will not stop when telling people about physics and the mechanisms of machinery. Whenever there's machine trouble at Millennium, she'll be the first on the scene to explain the situation.
