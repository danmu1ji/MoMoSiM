- Kotori (Engineering Department): I told you I would answer any question, so that's what I will do! Pull yourself together, Kotori!

- Kotori (Engineering Department): ...It's not as big of an issue as I'm making it seem.

- Kotori (Engineering Department): This unique shape makes it difficult to get a lens replacement!
- Kotori (Engineering Department): But you see, these glasses have a special effect that ordinary glasses do not!
- Kotori (Engineering Department): To an extent, it's a very intentional design on my part!
- Kotori (Engineering Department): As you well know, I'm prone to giving a lot of lectures on different things. And when I explain things, I can get a little long-winded.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): That's why these glasses are designed to give people who are listening to these lectures something to focus on!
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Basically, it's a focus point for your eyes. Surprisingly, it actually works quite effectively!
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Fu. Fu. Fu. You understand it now, Sensei!
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Even small features like this can help people concentrate and subsequently absorb the information I'm delivering!
- Kotori (Engineering Department): ...Although these days, people seem to run away whenever they see my glasses.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): Yes! And regardless, it's still quite effective for newbies who've never heard one of my lectures before!
- Kotori (Engineering Department): When my glasses broke this time, I did think about going back to a regular pair of glasses that are easier to maintain...
- Kotori (Engineering Department): ...Heehee.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): You know what, it's absolutely the best option to keep them.
- Kotori (Engineering Department): After all, if I hadn't, this wonderful situation never would've happened!
