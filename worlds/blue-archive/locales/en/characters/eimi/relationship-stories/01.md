> Inside a Millennium Building
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): *sigh* Great job again, Sensei.
- "Not too shabby yourself."
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Now, then...
> Eimi suddenly started undressing.
- "Wh-What are you doing?!"
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Hmm? I'm taking my clothes off. Why?
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Why am I taking them off...? Well, because it's hot.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): I can't just take my clothes off because it's hot? How else am I suppose to cool off, then?
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): The most efficient way to dissipate heat inside one's body is to undress.
- "Aren't you embarrassed to take your clothes off in public?"
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Embarrassed...? Is embarrassment a valid reason to not successfully carry out a mission?
- "You still can't take your clothes off."
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Then what would you suggest I do to cool down without undressing?
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Hmm...
- "How about eating ice cream?"
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Ice cream? Yes, ingesting something cold may work... That should prove an effective way of lowering my internal temperature.
- "Let's go to an ice cream shop out in the suburbs. It'll be my treat."
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Very well, on the condition that I buy you your ice cream.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Why...? Because that's the sensible thing to do.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Heehee... We should hurry, Sensei. Staying here will only make us overheat more.
> Eimi and I then went out for ice cream.
