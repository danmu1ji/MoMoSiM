> City of Kivotos
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Hello, Sensei. I just discovered something new.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): According to my school friends, singing together at karaoke has become quite popular.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): I don't really understand how singing together in a small room could be fun...
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): ..but it'd be inefficient to jump to conclusions before trying it myself.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Now, Sensei. Let us go to karaoke together.
- "Wouldn't it be more fun to go with your friends?"
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): That isn't an option. Karaoke is a dangerous place.
- "And what exactly is dangerous about karaoke?"
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): It's a small, confined, soundproofed space. We'll be cut off from the outside.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): If we were to be attacked there...I wouldn't stand a chance.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): So it's only right that I go with Sensei, my trustworthy comrade.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Now, let's go sing... I mean, investigate this karaoke stuff.
> After that, I sang my heart out with Eimi at karaoke.
> I was surprised Eimi could sing so well...
