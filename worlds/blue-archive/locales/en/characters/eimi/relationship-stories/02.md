# Eimi — Relationship Story 2

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> In Class
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): You're here, Sensei.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): I'm glad you could make it. I know you must be busy.
- "What's been troubling you lately?"
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Well, honestly...
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): What do you think...girls are like these days?
- "...Why are you asking me?"
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Well, because you're always surrounded by girls.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): So I thought you'd be an expert...
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Was I mistaken?
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Hmm...
- "What brought this on?"
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Well...I've been having a hard time seeing eye to eye with my friends.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): For example, they wanted to go to a new candy shop that just opened near the school.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): If junk food is what they're after, wouldn't it be more economical to buy cheap greasy fries rather than expensive candy?
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): That also doesn't account for the time spent standing in line. I just can't understand their reasoning.
- "Sometimes candy just hits the spot."
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): That is a valid point...but there are other instances.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): For example, why do girls find occult things so interesting? You know, like tarot cards, horoscopes, and fortune telling?
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Why in the world would anyone trust some fortune teller on TV? I mean, there's no logic there. I just don't get it.
- "I don't understand it either..."
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): So, there are some things you don't know about girls. Color me surprised.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Maybe I do have a chance, after all...
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Hmm? Oh, I didn't say anything.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Anyway, I need to know what girls like if I'm to be friends with them.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Does that make me sound like I'm not a real girl my age? I could hardly blame you if it did.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Sometimes I forget that I'm a student. Sensei, you strike me as more a...comrade than a teacher.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): But thinking like that will only make graduating harder. And that wouldn't be efficient.
- Eimi (Super Phenomenon Task Force): Now, Sensei. Let's read that magazine I brought and ponder the nature of girls.
> Afterward, I read women's magazines with Eimi, but it did us little good...
