# Eimi\n\nEnrolled at Millennium Science School, Eimi is a member of the Supernatural Phenomenon Task Force. She's a quiet girl who gives a denpa-like impression. It's hard to know what she's thinking. She can often be seen standing around vacantly for no reason.
