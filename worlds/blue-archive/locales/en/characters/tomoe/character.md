# Tomoe

## Description

Tomoe is a student of Red Winter Federal Academy, the secretary general of the Red Winter Secretariat, and the first member of Cherino's inner circle. She is a master storyteller who can make anything sound plausible, and she regularly infuses her loyalty to Chairman Cherino into various speeches. Cherino herself believes that Tomoe is inspired by her charisma and has a sense of loyalty to her, but in reality Tomoe is just blindly adoring Cherino.

## Appearance

Tomoe has knee-length light-magenta hair in two long braids tied with fluffy white ornaments, and peach-pink eyes.
