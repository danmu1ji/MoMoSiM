# Tomoe — Relationship Story 3

> (On the way back to the Red Winter Office after the speech tour...)
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Heehee, I think the speeches were a success.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Even the skeptical students questioning the president's legitimacy were cheering and shouting slogans by the end of the speech.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): For a little bit at least, I can relax even if Cherino does something crazy. The students are thoroughly convinced of the greatness of the president.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Heehee, thank you for the compliment, Sensei.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): What do you mean almost believed? It is undeniably true that President Cherino is a great student council president with an amazing mustache.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Since Red Winter Academy is so huge, there are quite a few students who have never actually seen President Cherino and only know of her by reputation.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): This is why it is necessary to go on periodic speech tours, so that the ignorant students do not succumb to false rumors.
- "Are you finished with the speech tour, then?"
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): No, I still have the Former Institute and the Red Winter Library left.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): But I'm going to wrap up for today and return to the Secretariat.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): I checked the weather forecast, and it seems a huge snow cloud is approaching the district.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Wow, speak of the devil. The winds are so strong!
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Let's hurry before the snow gets any worse!
> (A few hours later...)
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): This is weird... We've been walking for a while now.
- "I think we lost our way in the snow."
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): You're right. It looks like we're lost.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): We need to find somewhere we can stay warm for now.
- "Looks like there's a wooden shack over there."
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): That's... The storehouse where materials for mountain road repairs are stored!
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Great job, Sensei. We can wait out the snow in there.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Come on. Let's go inside and warm ourselves up!
- Tomoe: ― Whoa, that's a huge snowstorm.
- Tomoe: ― Come on, Sensei, take off your outerwear right now.
- Tomoe: ― You might catch a cold if you
- Tomoe: ― keep those wet clothes on.
- Tomoe: ― Sensei, what've you been
- Tomoe: ― looking at this whole time?
- Tomoe: ― Are you also...
- Tomoe: ― interested in
- Tomoe: ― President Cherino's great achievements?
- Tomoe: ― Well, this timing works out well, so that's great.
- Tomoe: ― Then, let's talk about the greatness of the president
- Tomoe: ― until the snow stops!
> (After that, Tomoe lectured me about President Cherino until the snow stopped.)
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Whew, it finally stopped snowing. I didn't expect the blizzard to last so long.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): It's a good thing time always passes quickly talking about President Cherino's greatness.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Don't you agree, Sensei?
- "..."
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Huh? Your expression...
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Looks like you still have a lot of questions about President Cherino's great achievements!
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): It's okay. We still have a lot of time.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): When we get back to the Secretariat, I'll continue my story!
- "...End my suffering."
