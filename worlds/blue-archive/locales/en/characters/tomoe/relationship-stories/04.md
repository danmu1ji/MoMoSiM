# Tomoe — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Tomoe/Relationship_Story/4
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Welcome, Sensei.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Thank you for coming to help, Sensei.
- "You threatened me. I had no choice..."
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Threatened you? I requested your assistance just like I normally do.
- "You said we would both be purged if I ran away."
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Yes, of course. You and I are comrades who share the same destiny.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): My mistake is your mistake...
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): In other words, joint responsibility!
- "Why did you ask me to meet you in the kitchen?"
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Because today's mission is to develop a new sweet snack.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): ...Do you happen to know how to make a sweet snack with bell peppers?
- "A sweet snack with bell peppers?"
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): You see, last month, the school lunch manager accidentally ordered a large number of bell peppers. A very large number.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): The school lunch manager who made the grave mistake was immediately purged, but President Cherino declared she would never eat a bell pepper, no matter what.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): But I believe if I can develop a sweet snack with them, our picky-eating president might enjoy it.
- "That's something a parent or guardian would worry about."
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Heehee, I'm always happy to see President Cherino enjoying delicious food.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): But how do you make this bitter and pungent vegetable into a sweet snack?
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): I'd love to hear your thoughts, Sensei.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Indeed. Dehydrated vegetables have a crunchy texture like crackers.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Plus, it gives them a little bit of a sweet taste.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Indeed. The sugar's sweetness could mask the bell pepper's bitterness.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Plus, when the moisture evaporates from a pickled vegetable, its texture turns soft.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): What an amazing plan. I knew I could count on you, Sensei!
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): I'll start preparing right away.
> We tried various methods to cook the bell peppers.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Hmm... There's something missing.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): No matter how sweet you make it or how nice the texture is, you can't purge the bitter taste.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): As a picky eater, Cherino would never try and enjoy this.
- "Still, we worked so hard on it. I'm sure she'll understand."
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Wh-What?! You want to report our failure to the president?!
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Nothing done in the president's name is a failure. The president is always right!
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): *whisper* If Cherino asks about the whereabouts of the bell peppers, you must distract her with the luxurious pudding I've prepared for her.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Do you understand, Sensei? As far as I'm concerned, this never happened. Eradicate all evidence of failure.
- "But there's a lot of bell pepper snacks left."
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): We just have to dispose of them one way or another.
- Tomoe (Red Winter Office): Let's get to work, Sensei.
> Afterwards, I ate the leftover bell pepper snacks with Tomoe.
> ...But there were too many left, so we handed them out to the students at Red Winter Academy.
