## MomoTalk 2

- Tomoe: Hello, Sensei.
- Tomoe: If it's not too much trouble, could you spare some time in the afternoon?
- Sensei: Sure. What's going on?
- Tomoe: I have to visit the shopping district near Schale to buy things for the Red Winter Office's work, but...
- Tomoe: It's been a while since I left the Red Winter district, and so I'm embarrassed to admit I'm not used to the roads around here.
- Sensei: If it's okay, I was hoping you could accompany me.

- Tomoe: Thank you, Sensei.
- Sensei: Then I'll see you in front of the shopping district in the afternoon.

## MomoTalk 4

- Tomoe: Recently, there seems to be a lot of propaganda slandering President Cherino all over Red Winter Academy.
- Tomoe: I think it's time to go on the Periodic Speech Tour.
- Sensei: Periodic Speech Tour?
- Tomoe: Are you interested in our academy's speech tours, Sensei?
- Tomoe: The Red Winter Academy's speech tour is an event where President Cherino spreads her greatness by touring different parts of the district and giving speeches.
- Sensei: However, it does take a while to tour the entire academy since the district is so large.
- Tomoe: Would you like to join us on the upcoming speech tour, Sensei?
- Sensei: I'm fine...
- Tomoe: No, I insist.
- Sensei: I still have many wonderful stories about President Cherino I would love to share with you!
- Sensei: Am I being forced to participate?!
