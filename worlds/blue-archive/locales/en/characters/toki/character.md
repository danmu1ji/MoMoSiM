# Toki

## Description

A member of the Millennium Science School, the fifth agent of its secret "C&C" agency. Although she belongs to C&C, Toki has been working as Rio's exclusive maid and bodyguard until now, so few people are familiar with her. A superior agent who fights based with high-tech weaponry. Until now, Toki has mainly acted alone, but she actually has a lonely side.

## Appearance

Toki is petite, with blond hair in a bun, navy accents along the sides, a white maid ribbon with aquamarine lace, blue eyes, and pale skin.
