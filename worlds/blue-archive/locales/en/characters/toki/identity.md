# Toki\n\nA member of the Millennium Science School, the fifth agent of its secret "C&C" agency. Although she belongs to C&C, Toki has been working as Rio's exclusive maid and bodyguard until now, so few people are familiar with her. A superior agent who fights based with high-tech weaponry.
