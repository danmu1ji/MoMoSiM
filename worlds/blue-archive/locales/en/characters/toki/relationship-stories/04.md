> Near Toki's Workshop
> I was a bit concerned, so I went to visit Toki's workshop.
> I didn't want to intrude, so I gave her a call first.
- Toki (C&C): Hello, Sensei. As I mentioned earlier, I'll be a bit late today.
- Toki (C&C): Hm? You were worried about that, and you're on your way here?
- Toki (C&C): Ah, my apologies. It seems I should've explained myself a bit better.
- Toki (C&C): I didn't realize you'd do that, but that doesn't excuse negligence.
- Toki (C&C): Huh...? Why are you apologizing to me?
- Toki (C&C): Ah... So you're already almost here.
- Toki (C&C): Uh... Hm. I'm not sure what to do.
- Toki (C&C): However, I know it's not right for me to keep you waiting. Please come in.
> I felt a bit bad about it, but I headed inside.
- Toki: ― There's not much to see, but welcome, Sensei.
- Toki: ― I was checking my equipment, as usual.
- Toki: ― I'm sorry to cause you concern.
- "You always do this much maintenance?"
- Toki: ― Yes. I always try to maintain peak condition.
- Toki: ― To honor the name of C&C,
- Toki: ― I want to be a great maid.
- "That's amazing, Toki. You work so hard every day."
- Toki: ― Do you...really think so? It doesn't seem that special to me.
- Toki: ― But hearing you say that makes me think...
- "Think what?"
- Toki: ― I'm determined.
- Toki: ― Today's maintenance will be performed even more effectively than usual.
- Toki: ― The goal is to become the best maid in Kivotos. The maid of all maids.
- Toki: ― Well, Sensei...
- Toki: ― Please watch carefully, all right?
> I stood to the side and watched as Toki calmly worked on her equipment.
- Toki (C&C): Thank you again for your patience, Sensei.
- Toki (C&C): Let's begin today's work.
- Toki (C&C): I, Asuma Toki, will be the perfect assistant.
> As we returned to Schale together, Toki was beaming.
