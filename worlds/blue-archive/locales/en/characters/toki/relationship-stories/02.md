> Millennium Campus
> I headed to Millennium at Toki's request.
- Toki (C&C): Hello, Sensei.
- Toki (C&C): Thank you for taking the time to see me.
- "So what's the problem that you mentioned?"
- Toki (C&C): Ah...
- Toki (C&C): Well, I'd appreciate it if you'd take a look at this letter.
- Toki (C&C): It's from the school, but I'm unsure if I understood it correctly.
- Toki (C&C): So I wanted to seek the advice of an expert. That'd be you, Sensei.
> I took Toki's letter and unfolded it.
- Toki (C&C): I see. It's just as I understood it. In that case...
- Toki (C&C): It's nothing to worry about.
- Toki (C&C): Let me begin escorting you today, then.
- Toki (C&C): I've already been held back once. I don't think anything will change if I am again.
- Toki (C&C): In fact, it'll allow me to escort you more, and stay an active member of C&C for longer.
- Toki (C&C): So, in that case, wouldn't you say it's a good thing?
- Toki (C&C): In the past, I received special attendance credits.
- Toki (C&C): However, it seems this is no longer the case, and so my attendance has been deemed insufficient.
- Toki (C&C): Hold on! I just had an idea.
- Toki (C&C): What if Millennium were to deem escorting you a special exception, as well?
- Toki (C&C): Ah, now that I've said it, it sounds like a good idea. Sensei, while you're here, why don't we file the paperwork for your full-time transfer to Millennium Science School?
- Toki (C&C): Is that right?
- Toki (C&C): Ah, so the letter says that if I begin attending classes, lectures, and keep up a normal daily routine at the school...
- Toki (C&C): Then, in consideration of my previous circumstances, they'll grant me leeway on my current attendance.
- "I see! What you need is a normal academy life!"
- Toki (C&C): Academy...life?
- Toki (C&C): What are you getting so excited about?
- Toki (C&C): ...
- Toki (C&C): I've heard that it's natural to feel that way about new things.
- Toki (C&C): To be honest, I...don't have a clue how I'm supposed to behave.
- "I'll help you!"
- Toki (C&C): S-Sensei?
- Toki (C&C): You can't just drag me around by the arm. Sensei? Sensei?!
> That was the beginning of Toki's normal academy life.
> Since I couldn't join her classes, I decided to observe as she attended.
> Classroom
- Instructor: And that's the last topic we'll cover in today's remote lecture.
- Instructor: Thank you all for your focus and attention.

- Toki (C&C): ...

- Toki (C&C): When an unsolvable problem stands in your way...
- Toki (C&C): An elite agent will always know how to charge through it head on.
- Instructor: Wh-What are you doing?! What are you doing to the screen?
- Instructor: No, Toki! You can't use your C&C tools in class!
- Instructor: Especially not a hacking tool that can take down all of Millennium's firewalls at once!
- Instructor: If you keep this up, I will not be able to count your attendance today!
- Toki (C&C): ?!
- Toki (C&C): Sensei, what should I do?
- "Can't you just solve the problem without any tricks?"
- Toki (C&C): If that were possible, there wouldn't be conflict in the world. We would finally know peace. It's such a shame.
- "This is exactly what I'm talking about!"
- Toki (C&C): ...Very well. If there's no other way, I'll do my best to solve the problem.
- Millennium Student: Hey, Toki. I'm going to the cafe after class, do you want to come?
- Toki (C&C): ?!
- Toki (C&C): Sensei, what do I do?
- "Have fun. Don't overthink it."
- Toki (C&C): But...
- "Hanging out with a friend in a cafe is normal academy life at its finest!"
- Toki (C&C): I-Is that right?
- Toki (C&C): In that case, will you join us, Sensei?
- "It would be terrible for a teacher to interfere in students' camaraderie."
- Toki (C&C): What does that mean?
- Toki (C&C): Sensei? Sensei?!
> I saw off Toki and her pleading face with joy in my heart.
> Until...
> Screen shakes

- Thug A: Wh-What's with this maid...

- Toki (C&C): ...

- Toki (C&C): It's just like the book I saw in the C&C club room.

- Toki (C&C): ...
- Toki (C&C): Hm... If you say so, Sensei.
- Toki (C&C): A normal academy life...
- Toki (C&C): I honestly didn't expect it to be so difficult, Sensei.
> Toki stood in front of me, looking somber.
