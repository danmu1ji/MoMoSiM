# Toki — Relationship Story 5

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Toki/Relationship_Story/5

> Schale Office
> I received a call from an unknown number.
> I picked up with the intention of politely declining if it was a telemarketer, but then...
- Toki (C&C): Hello, Sensei.
- "Toki?!"
- Toki (C&C): Connection confirmed.
- Toki (C&C): A successful attempt. I'm glad. I truly am excellent.
- Toki (C&C): With this, I've also confirmed that I am connected to you 24 hours a day, Sensei.
- "That could be easily misunderstood..."
- Toki (C&C): But it is simply the truth.
- Toki (C&C): After all, are you not currently talking to me using an unknown communication line at this very moment?
- Toki (C&C): An educator should never deny obvious truths.
- Toki (C&C): In fact, is it not an educator's duty to lead students to correct conclusions that are based on facts?
- "I can't say...that you're wrong..."
- Toki (C&C): As expected, you are a true teacher. Being able to adapt your thought processes quickly is an excellent thing.
- Toki (C&C): In any case, I contacted you to arrange a meeting. I have something to give you.
- "Something for me?"
- Toki (C&C): That's right. I, a kind and perfect maid, am giving you this digital shackle—
- Toki (C&C): ...I mean, symbol of our relationship.
- "I thought I heard you say something really ominous..."
- Toki (C&C): You misheard me.
- Toki (C&C): ...You don't think so? It's true. You did mishear me.
- Toki (C&C): Protest as much as you'd like, but my opinion will not change.
- Toki (C&C): Listen, Sensei. It is said that a person swallows an average of eight spiders a year.
- Toki (C&C): If that's the case, wouldn't it also be true that a person mishears things an average of 100 times a year?
- Toki (C&C): ...Hm? How are those two things related?
- Toki (C&C): ...
- Toki (C&C): They aren't in any way, but you still misheard me. I'm positive.
- "Okay... Let's just say you're right..."
- Toki (C&C): Thank you.
- Toki (C&C): So on that note, I would like to visit you.
- Toki (C&C): I will come and give you this token of our connection. Okay?
- "Okay, sure... Where are you...?"
- Toki (C&C): That agreement was not very enthusiastic...
- Toki (C&C): No. Of course you would want me there. In fact, I am in front of Schale's office right now.
- Toki (C&C): Correct.
- Toki (C&C): Hello, Sensei. It's good to see you again.
- Toki (C&C): I could have, but this was my strategy to gain your sympathy.
- Toki (C&C): Furthermore, to make passersby think...
- Toki (C&C): ..."How could Sensei make such a perfect, wonderful maid wait outside? How terrible."
- Toki (C&C): ...There was one significant problem, though.
- Toki (C&C): What is that, you ask? It was that there was significantly less foot traffic in front of your office than I originally expected.
- Toki (C&C): Truly a blunder. Next time, I will be more diligent in finding methods to properly induce sympathy from both you and civilians.
- "Why were you looking for sympathy?!"
- Toki (C&C): Oh, no. You misheard me.
- "Okay... Let's just say you're right..."
- Toki (C&C): Moving on. Allow me to reveal the purpose behind today's visit.
- Toki (C&C): Yes, after all, there is no point in subterfuge now. This is a headset, perfect for both a maid and a perfect agent.
- Toki (C&C): The soundproofing is thorough, and although it is an in-ear type, it has special acoustic processing to prevent hearing damage.
- Toki (C&C): Naturally, basic channel interference and hacking prevention are in place. However, in the unlikely event that the channel is breached, it has more safeguards.
- Toki (C&C): It will automatically bypass the channel and generate a new one.
- Toki (C&C): There are various other features, but I will skip them due to time constraints.
- Toki (C&C): The point is that with this, a separate, secure communication channel is available to us.
- Toki (C&C): It means you can contact me directly at any time.
- Toki (C&C): One for you, and one for me. Two makes a pair.
- "Is it okay for me to accept something like this?"
- Toki (C&C): Yes. It is what I want.
- Toki (C&C): This is a unique, special piece of equipment that someone I used to serve developed especially for me.
- Toki (C&C): ...I should save it for if that person returns?
- Toki (C&C): Why is that necessary? Not utilizing all available resources is unreasonable. That person would say the same.
- Toki (C&C): Therefore, I believe that the correct decision is for you to use it. That person will also feel the same way.
- Toki (C&C): Furthermore, this headset will also be pleased that it's being used.
- Toki (C&C): The lifespan of tools are shortened when they are left to idle.
- Toki (C&C): Therefore, Sensei, please put on the headset and stay connected with me 24 hours a day.
- "That's a bit much..."
- Toki (C&C): ...Why is that? Have you perhaps found someone else?
- Toki (C&C): Our relationship...
- Toki (C&C): ...was it all just a game to you?!
- "Toki, what does that even mean?!"
- Toki (C&C): I apologize, Sensei. It was a joke intended to lighten the mood.
- Toki (C&C): More specifically, it was a line from a drama.
- Toki (C&C): My knowledge of dramas and movies is still lacking, but I am doing my utmost to learn.
- Toki (C&C): That was a line I was able to recall.
- Toki (C&C): Anyway, what I would like to say is simple.
- Toki (C&C): Please accept it. And always be with me.
- Toki (C&C): That's all I ask of you.
- "I'm grateful for the thought...so I'll accept it."
- Toki (C&C): Thank you, Sensei.
- "But I don't think I can wear it 24/7."
- Toki (C&C): Why not? I'm disappointed. No, I'll show you disappointed.
- Toki (C&C): Look, Sensei. Look at the way my shoulders are drooping. I demand that you take responsibility. Quickly.
- Toki (C&C): I see. If that's the reason...
- Toki (C&C): Yes. I can understand that reasoning.
- Toki (C&C): Anyway, with this, we...
- Toki (C&C): ...now have a couple's headset.
- "Where did you learn things like that, Toki...?"
- Toki (C&C): From a movie.
- "Word choice is important. You can cause unwanted misunderstandings..."
- Toki (C&C): What do you mean? I want others to misunderstand...
- "Toki, please. Hello? Toki...?"
> Toki stayed and played for a while before she finally felt satisfied and left.
> ...I decided to keep Toki's headset safely stored in the drawer.
