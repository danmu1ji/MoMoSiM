# Toki — Relationship Story 1

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Toki/Relationship_Story/1
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> Schale Executive Office
> Screen shakes
> I had barely sat down at my desk for the day...
> When suddenly...
- Toki (C&C): Good morning, Sensei.
- Toki (C&C): ...
- Toki (C&C): I see. You forgot about me completely, Sensei.
- Toki (C&C): That's quite all right. It's fine, really. I'm not the type to be hurt by something like this. I forgive you for forgetting me.
- Toki (C&C): But I wonder... Will my pristine maid uniform and spotless rocket launcher forgive you as well?
- "N-No! I'm sorry! I didn't forget. I was just surprised!"
- "Toki! It's Toki, right? Asuma Toki!"
- Toki (C&C): Oh, you remembered. Thank you, Sensei.
- Toki (C&C): Thank you for remembering, Sensei.
- Toki (C&C): I didn't realize I would surprise you so much, though. I'm a bit worried... Did I do something incorrectly?
- "N-No, that's fine. Students should feel free to seek out their teacher whenever they want!"
- "But next time... I'd appreciate it if you contacted me first, at least."
- Toki (C&C): I did contact you.
- "You did...?"
- Toki (C&C): I just didn't reveal my identity.
- "Do you even hear yourself?"
- Toki (C&C): So, you wanted to know what I'm doing at Schale so early in the morning.
- Toki (C&C): So, you wanted to know what I'm doing at Schale so early in the morning.
- Toki (C&C): Well, it's because my schedule suddenly freed up.
- "...That clarified nothing."
- Toki (C&C): Is that so? Hmm... This thing called explanation is quite difficult.
- "(Is it really?)"
- Toki (C&C): Let me try again. I had someone who I used to serve.
- Toki (C&C): I was her bodyguard and her aide.
- Toki (C&C): But then, something happened.
- Toki (C&C): With only a few orders for me, she disappeared.
- Toki (C&C): Suddenly, I was without responsibility, without duty. I pondered what I should do next.
- "It's great that you could think through a problem and find the solution."
- Toki (C&C): Yes. I concluded that I would go about my daily duties for you, Sensei.
- "Why?"
- Toki (C&C): I believe, as the one responsible for creating my situation, it would likewise be your responsibility to fix it.
- Toki (C&C): You should see this not as a problem, but as an opportunity to consider the consequences of your actions.
- "Now that's going too far!"
- Toki (C&C): No. It's eminently logical and fair. A very reasonable conclusion, if I do say so myself.
- Toki (C&C): Anyways, that's how it is. So, from now on, I'll be taking care of you, Sensei.
- "H-Hold on! Toki? Wait, Toki?!"
> Despite my protests, Toki's caretaking began...
> Downtown Street
- Passerby A: Hey... is that Schale's Sensei? Are they really making a student wear a maid uniform and follow them around?
- Passerby B: Schale's Sensei seems nice, but I heard that they sometimes make their students do messed up things. Were the rumors true after all?
- Toki (C&C): Ah, I can hear passing citizens praising my performance.
- Toki (C&C): Don't you think that I'm the epitome of a perfect maid, Sensei?
- "I think, instead of you being praised, I'm being criticized..."
- Toki (C&C): I see. Then I will take this opportunity to show you another side of my flawless service, the fact that I can protect you from any threat.
- Toki (C&C): Raising alert level. Shifting to combat mode.
- Passerby A: Oh dear! Now they're even having the student change her outfit?!
- Passerby B: And in the middle of a crowded street, too... What a disgrace!
- Toki (C&C): Disappointing. Lowering alert level by one stage. I will continue to protect you.
- Millennium Student: Ah, Sensei. Do you have a moment?
- Toki (C&C): Please wait. If you'd like to speak with Sensei, you must clear it with me first.
- Millennium Student: Huh?
- Toki (C&C): On what subject would you like to speak with Sensei? For what reason? What do you expect the tone of your conversation to be?
- Toki (C&C): How long do you expect it to take? What goal do you hope to accomplish with that time?
- Millennium Student: I, um, I just...
- "Hold on, hold on. Wait!"
> And that's the way Toki "took care" of me all day.
> It was, in many ways, an...interesting day.
- "Toki, thank you for...your hard work today..."
- Toki (C&C): Hm. You look fatigued. Are you well?
- Toki (C&C): You don't want me to worry? But...
- Toki (C&C): ...
- Toki (C&C): Did you, perhaps, find my service to be tiring, Sensei?
- "Not really, but... It was maybe a bit much."
- Toki (C&C): Oh? But, if anything, my conduct today was less intense than usual.
- Toki (C&C): What? You think I'm kidding?
- Toki (C&C): Please look me in the eyes. Do I look like a student who tends to crack jokes?
- "I said that because you don't!"
- Toki (C&C): Oh, thank goodness. It's a relief to see proof of your good judgment, Sensei.
- "Anyway, you don't need to go to such lengths when you're helping me."
- Toki (C&C): Really?
- Toki (C&C): You ought to be more aware of the importance of your position, Sensei.
- Toki (C&C): But, if you insist... I understand.
- Toki (C&C): Casual. I see...
- Toki (C&C): I understand, Sensei.
- Toki (C&C): Kay, Sensei. Lookin' forward to working with you, eh?
- Toki (C&C): ...Just kidding. Imitation is one of the skills of an excellent maid. Don't be so shocked.
- "I thought you didn't joke!"
- Toki (C&C): I do sometimes.
- "You betrayed me! You've hurt my feelings!"
- Toki (C&C): ...
- Toki (C&C): That's right. I have hurt your feelings. With that, I am victorious.
- "And I've become a loser... Can I even call myself a teacher anymore...?"
- Toki (C&C): Well, this is a problem. You must remain a teacher so that I can continue to assist you.
- Toki (C&C): This is your duty, so that I can continue to do my duty.
- "Out of curiosity, is this your way of being casual...?"
- Toki (C&C): ...Heh.
- Toki (C&C): If I said yes, what would your response be?
- "Well, if it makes you feel comfortable... Hah. Hahaha..."
- Toki (C&C): Wonderful. Thank you, Sensei.
> Toki left with a smug look on her face.
> And then, the next day...
- Toki (C&C): Good morning, Sensei.
- "I had a feeling this was going to happen."
- Toki (C&C): Thank you for remembering, Sensei.
- Toki (C&C): I'll take care of you again today.
- Toki (C&C): Shall we go, Sensei?
> I had a sneaking suspicion that it was going to be another lively day.
