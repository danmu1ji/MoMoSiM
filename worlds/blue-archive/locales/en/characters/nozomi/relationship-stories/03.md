> I was on my way to take my usual subway ride.
- "Eh?"
- Nozomi (CCC): Wait, what? Sensei?
- Nozomi (CCC): Pahyahya! I didn't expect to see you here!
- Nozomi (CCC): I'm here to take the subway, of course.
- Nozomi (CCC): Are you taking this one too, Sensei?
- Nozomi (CCC): That's great! Let's go together.
- Nozomi (CCC): Lucky! Got a seat!
- Nozomi (CCC): Sensei! Sit here!
- Nozomi (CCC): Pahyahya! I take it sometimes, since some places don't have train tracks installed.
- Nozomi (CCC): It's not that different from a train, so I like it, you know?
- "I suppose they do feel similar."
- Nozomi (CCC): They hold a lot of people, they're usually on schedule...
- Nozomi (CCC): ...plus, they feel open, so I don't get motion sickness.
- Nozomi (CCC): Pahyahya! So yeah, most of the pros are very train-like!
- Nozomi (CCC): Cons: it's smaller, it can get cramped, and it stops more than trains do.
- Nozomi (CCC): I guess that means trains are better. Don't you think?
- Nozomi (CCC): Hmm. I guess.
- Nozomi (CCC): They CAN be more practical than trains, depending on where you're going.
- Nozomi (CCC): Maybe I'll get to drive a subway sometime.
- "Ever thought about piloting a ship?"
- Nozomi (CCC): A ship? I've never sailed before, but I hear the motion sickness gets really bad.
- Nozomi (CCC): It's probably not for me!
- Nozomi (CCC): Oh? You get less motion sick on bigger ships? Heh, I guess you've been on all kinds of vehicles, haven't you?
- Nozomi (CCC): Pahyahya! Well, maybe someday, I'll take a boat trip somewhere.
- Nozomi (CCC): But you know what'd be really cool? A sea train!
- Nozomi (CCC): We've just got to keep building new tracks, right? It'll happen eventually. Pahyahya!
> We talked about different transportation methods for a while...
> ...and that's how I missed my stop.
