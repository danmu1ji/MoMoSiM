> Nozomi asked me along to a railway construction site field survey.
- Nozomi (CCC): Good morning!
- "Morning, Nozomi!"
- Nozomi (CCC): Thanks for coming! Pahyahya!
- Nozomi (CCC): It was a job for two, but I ended up going solo.
- Nozomi (CCC): And it'd be boring by myself, you know?
- Nozomi (CCC): Pahyahya! That's nice, then!
- Nozomi (CCC): All right, let's go have some fun...
- Nozomi (CCC): ...I mean, get some work done!
- "That sounded like you meant it just now."
- Nozomi (CCC): Okay! This is the first thing to check.
- "What am I checking, exactly?"
- Nozomi (CCC): We just need to see if this written report is accurate. Pretty simple, yeah?
- Nozomi (CCC): Preliminary feasibility study. CCC is doing a survey before construction starts.
- Nozomi (CCC): For example, this place was approved for the opening of a new train line during the first survey.
- Nozomi (CCC): If we build an express line here, it'll be connected to the D.U. district. That'll help streamline the funding allocation process.
- "Nozomi..."
- Nozomi (CCC): What? What's with that look?
- "You look so capable right now, you know?"
- Nozomi (CCC): Heh, I'm not ALWAYS jokey.
- Nozomi (CCC): ...
- Nozomi (CCC): Pff...
- Nozomi (CCC): Pfft! Pahyahya!
- Nozomi (CCC): Sensei, your face looks so funny!
- Nozomi (CCC): Did I have you believing I understood a word of that?!
- Nozomi (CCC): I just read it straight off the report!
- "Yeah, I thought so."
- Nozomi (CCC): Fufufu... Well, you know, when you're dealing with higher-ups, you have to at least LOOK like you know what you're talking about.
- Nozomi (CCC): But somebody else can take care of all that complicated stuff. I just drive the trains.
- Nozomi (CCC): Although I did sound pretty convincing just now, didn't I?
- Nozomi (CCC): Today's another field survey, so we can goof around some more. Call it a change of pace.
- Nozomi (CCC): You know, even if we did take this seriously, and write up all our opinions, it wouldn't matter.
- Nozomi (CCC): There are multiple factions in Highlander. If one side says, "do this," the other one's pretty much always going to say, "No, do that."
- Nozomi (CCC): All I've gotta do here is go through the motions.
- "I do wonder if that's okay..."
- Nozomi (CCC): Well, it's worked so far.
- Nozomi (CCC): Most students are busy doing maintenance and operations.
- Nozomi (CCC): As long as everyone does their own job, we're good.
- Nozomi (CCC): Although...if I could, I'd love to just lay tracks across all of Kivotos, and not have to bother with the paperwork.
- Nozomi (CCC): Then I could go where I wanted.
- Nozomi (CCC): Parks, ramen shops, rooftops...
- Nozomi (CCC): ...You look like you want to say something. Pahyahya!
- Nozomi (CCC): You know, speaking of factions, I bet you can relate to that, huh, Sensei?
- "Hm? What do you mean?"
- Nozomi (CCC): Well, Schale also works across all these different academies.
- Nozomi (CCC): You've got to balance all their needs, their wants... That must get complicated.
- Nozomi (CCC): Hmm.
- Nozomi (CCC): So you treat every student fair and square?
- "Yeah. I need to."
- Nozomi (CCC): Well...
- Nozomi (CCC): ...then that's unfair to me! Pahyahya!
- Nozomi (CCC): Pfft, what're you saying all of a sudden? Pahyahya! You're funny!
- Nozomi (CCC): Yep, you're learning on the job, huh?
- Nozomi (CCC): Well, like you said...
- Nozomi (CCC): I'll make you care about me the most today! Come on, hurry!
> And so, I went around all day following Nozomi's lead.
