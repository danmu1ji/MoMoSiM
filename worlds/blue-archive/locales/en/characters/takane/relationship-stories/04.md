# Takane — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

- "I can't believe I'm seeing an aurora..."
- Takane (Publishing Department): Yes, it occasionally shows itself over Red Winter.
- Takane (Publishing Department): We even have an aurora forecast app.
- "I see you like taking photos."
- Takane (Publishing Department): I...suppose?
- Takane (Publishing Department): ...Though, I am more interested in capturing scenic landscapes than taking portraits.
- Takane (Publishing Department): I feel that, in a way, that taking photographs and forming critiques share considerable common ground.
- "I never considered that."
- Takane (Publishing Department): Ahem. Please allow me to expand on my thesis, Sensei.
- Takane (Publishing Department): The purpose of critique is to observe a cultural work, and then use one's own logic and words...
- Takane (Publishing Department): ...to transcribe one's feelings through the act of writing.
- Takane (Publishing Department): In a way, you seek to capture the feelings and impressions of a certain moment, and articulate those to another.
- Takane (Publishing Department): And your impression, or "critique," is then read by your peers, then reinterpreted and edited to convey an air of objectivity.
- Takane (Publishing Department): To seek "objectivity" is, perhaps, the fruitless effort to represent a universal point of view.
- Takane (Publishing Department): That's how I see the link between photography and critique.
- "So you're saying that a photo conveys objectivity?"
- Takane (Publishing Department): To an extent. Nothing is truly "objective." The photographer makes dozens of decisions, after all. Consider the lens, the framing, the shutter speed...
- Takane (Publishing Department): All these micro-decisions impact the resulting photograph and how it will be interpreted.
- Takane (Publishing Department): Any attempt to secure objectivity will inevitably involve a multitude of subjective decisions.
- Takane (Publishing Department): The creator's fingerprints will naturally show on any work.
- Takane (Publishing Department): Don't you find that intertwined tension interesting?
- Takane (Publishing Department): At least... I think it is.
- Takane (Publishing Department): I feel the same could be said about the relationship between creative works and critique.
- "Creative works...?"
- Takane (Publishing Department): What? Have I ever considered making my own..."more creative" works?
- Takane (Publishing Department): Uh... Well...
- Takane (Publishing Department): I...
- Takane (Publishing Department): Ah...!
- Takane (Publishing Department): Sensei! An aurora!
- Takane (Publishing Department): ...Nature is amazing, is it not?
- Takane (Publishing Department): Whenever I witness nature's grandeur, I can't help but consider that we as people are mere motes of dust floating in a grand, vast universe.
- Takane (Publishing Department): Sometimes I even ponder why I bother writing critiques or even consider being creative...
- Takane (Publishing Department): Yet, at the same time, I believe creative expression is one of the most beautiful endeavors a person can attempt.
- Takane (Publishing Department): You know what they say: "people may not be beautiful, but what they create could be."
- Takane (Publishing Department): ...Unless one finds something to be truly breathtaking, they will never be inspired enough to attempt the act of creation.
- Takane (Publishing Department): Perhaps "critique" is my form of a creative expression...
- Takane (Publishing Department): ...My own unique way of celebrating beauty.
- Takane (Publishing Department): Wh-What?! You, taking a picture of me...? I-I mean, I would be grateful and happy but... Why all the sudden?
- Takane (Publishing Department): Whaaaat?! You want me to create a "theme" for my pose? Wh-What kind of theme did you have in mind...?
- Takane (Publishing Department): Y-You want me to come up with one? Spontaneously? This is so SUDDEN, Sensei!
- Takane (Publishing Department): What? Adding something pretty on top of something else pretty would just be additive, not harmonious!
- Takane (Publishing Department): Do you also enjoy pouring mint chocolate syrup on hot takoyaki?
- Takane (Publishing Department): Above all, creation...isn't part of the...a critic's repertoire...
- Takane (Publishing Department): Y-Yes, of course I read and appreciate a bevy of different works! But still! This feels like I'm over-stepping into someone else's world...
- Takane (Publishing Department): I don't want to undermine the struggle of those who are courageous enough to create...
- Takane (Publishing Department): ...I've seen plenty of incidents where someone was haphazardly creative, and it resulted in widespread pain and suffering.
- Takane (Publishing Department): What? You really think it's permissible? If I'm with you? It can be a secret?
- Takane (Publishing Department): ...To capture a single, magical moment...just for me. How aspirational.
- "Yes. Let me be your critic, Takane."
- Takane (Publishing Department): ...!
- Takane (Publishing Department): Th-Then...
- Takane (Publishing Department): Could you inspire me with some...keywords...or something...?
- Takane (Publishing Department): ...Why do people keep telling me things like that...?
- Takane (Publishing Department): All right. If it's something you came up with, Sensei...
- Takane (Publishing Department): Ahem.
- Takane (Publishing Department): I had three older sisters, making me the youngest in a quite well-respected family.
- Takane (Publishing Department): I was beloved by the people, but ever since I was a child...I was struck by a tragic frailty.
- Takane (Publishing Department): I was never allowed to have fun outside, so I always instead chose to pull pranks on the people around me...
- Takane (Publishing Department): Because of that, my sisters called me "little menace," and I was particularly close with my third sister.
- Takane (Publishing Department): Whenever I wasn't sick, we would share a pillow on our cot. On mornings, we'd bathe together in cold water.
- Takane (Publishing Department): I never liked cold baths, but my sister insisted it would help with my health...
- Takane (Publishing Department): But...due to a number of unfortunate events, our house came to ruin...
- Takane (Publishing Department): And we, the four sisters, scattered and said goodbye, without ever knowing if we'd find each other again.
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― Sensei, look...
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― An aurora.
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― Old stories say
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― that the light is of...
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― guardians of the heavens,
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― enjoying a banquet.
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― The ones who I thought would be with me forever...
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― ...can no longer be found.
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― And the world
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― continues to feel cold and far.
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― But I can still look up at the aurora
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― and have hope because...
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― My "partner," who will stay by my side until the end...
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― ...is right here.
- "Oh, that would make me the partner, then?"
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― Fufu. Yes...
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― That's the prelude to this story.
- Takane (Publishing Department): ― Please take a good photo, okay?
- Takane (Publishing Department): ...
- "Wh-Why are you looking at me like that, my lady...?"
- Takane (Publishing Department): I'm not your lady! We're done with that! I'm just Miyoshi Takane, and always will be!
- Takane (Publishing Department): Really... If someone else wrote that story, I would only need to read one page to form a verdict...
- Takane (Publishing Department): "A tale so derivative and hackneyed that its first line moved me...in record time...to slam it shut and throw it in the bin!"
- "But you wrote it yourself."
- Takane (Publishing Department): Only because you told me to! I wasn't prepared!
- Takane (Publishing Department): Ugh... Just show me the photograph, please.
- "So?"
- Takane (Publishing Department): ...
- Takane (Publishing Department): It came out wonderfully...
- Takane (Publishing Department): Thank you, Sensei.
- Takane (Publishing Department): I would like to ask one thing of you.
- Takane (Publishing Department): This photograph, and whatever...else...happened here...
- Takane (Publishing Department): Please don't mention it to anyone else.
- "Is it really that embarrassing?"
- Takane (Publishing Department): Well, that's certainly a part of it.
- Takane (Publishing Department): I want to keep some things between you and me, Sensei.
