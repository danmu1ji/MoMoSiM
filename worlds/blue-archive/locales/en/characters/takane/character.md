# Takane

## Description

A member of the Publishing Department at Red Winter Federal Academy. A refined, elegant young lady who values good manners. She is skilled as a critic, but her strong personal opinions often lead to conflicts. She appears to be locked in a rivalry with the Knowledge Liberation Front.

## Appearance

Takane has thigh-length wavy red hair partly resting on her shoulders and golden eyes.
