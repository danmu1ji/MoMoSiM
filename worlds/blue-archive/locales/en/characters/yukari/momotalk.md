## MomoTalk 2

- Yukari: Sensei! Hi, hi!
- Sensei: Hi, Yukari. You're energetic, as usual.
- Yukari: Yup! I consider my boundless energy one of my best qualities!
- Sensei: But... I can only really be like this around you.
- Sensei: Why is that?
- Yukari: It's just...it's been pretty tense at home lately.
- Sensei: I have to live up to the Kadenokouji name and study harder.
- Yukari: But I don't know how much harder I can try...
- Sensei: At this rate, I might die and be resurrected as an academic ghost who has to carry an abacus and a calculator around for eternity!
- Sensei: How did you even come up with that?!
- Sensei: Anyway, it's important to take a breather every once in a while.
- Yukari: Take a breather?
- Sensei: Take...a breather... Hm, indeed.
- Yukari: I understand now!
- Sensei: I'll take your advice! I'm on my way over!
- Sensei: Huh? R-Right now?
- Yukari: Yes!
