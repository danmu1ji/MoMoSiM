# Koyuki\n\nGenius girl from the Millennium Science School, Koyuki is a troublemaker member of the "Seminar" student council. Koyuki is a prodigy when it comes to analyzing probabilities, although she herself is completely unaware of it. Koyuki likes games of chance, and constantly tries her luck in various ways.
