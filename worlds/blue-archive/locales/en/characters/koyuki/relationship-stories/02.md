> Koyuki showed up at Schale to work.
- Koyuki (Seminar): So this is Schale! Ooooh... I've never seen it before!
- Koyuki (Seminar): Hm, but it's actually just a normal office...?
- Koyuki (Seminar): Haha! Just messing with you. Of course the place where you work would be like this, Sensei.
- Koyuki (Seminar): So, what should I do? I heard it was my turn on the roster, so I showed up, but...
- Koyuki (Seminar): I don't have any bizarre computational abilities like some cold-hearted accountant I won't name.
- Koyuki (Seminar): Oh, I can run errands, though! Since it's for you, Sensei, I'll work super hard!
- Koyuki (Seminar): Haha... Well, uh...of course!
- Koyuki (Seminar): The truth is...no matter what it is... It's gonna be way, way, WAY better than doing whatever stuff the upperclassmen at Millennium make me do!
- Koyuki (Seminar): ...Huh?
- Koyuki (Seminar): You think work will be equally boring for Schale or for Seminar?
- Koyuki (Seminar): Haha... Sensei. You don't know what you're talking about, do you? This isn't just about being bored! Not at all!
- Koyuki (Seminar): What I do there... It's basically just like stamping documents... Document after document, with no end in sight, and I don't know anything about what's in them...
- Koyuki (Seminar): Sitting in one spot, doing the same thing over and over and over... My brain can't take it!
- Koyuki (Seminar): You want to know what I do, specifically? Why?
- Koyuki (Seminar): I mean...it's nothing important, really. Just, like, recovering lost passwords for other students, or unlocking security systems that have been locked accidentally...
- Koyuki (Seminar): Or, figuring out how to fix code that's so old that we don't even know who made it... A bunch of useless stuff like that, I guess.
- Koyuki (Seminar): ...No! Dang it! Stop! That's not what you're supposed to say!
- Koyuki (Seminar): It's totally boring! Not even a bit meaningful! Not fulfilling at all!
- Koyuki (Seminar): I sit there, someone puts a problem in front of me, I write the first thing that pops into my head, then it just solves itself! I don't even know what the problem's about before they bring the next one, and the next one, and on, and on...
- Koyuki (Seminar): They treat me like a vending machine, but I'm a person! A whole person! I don't wanna do it anymore!
- Koyuki (Seminar): If that's what life is like, how could I not search for freedom? Even if I'm caught every time!
- Koyuki (Seminar): And then, on top of everything, they even throw me in solitary confinement, whether it's an empty workroom or the Self-Reflection Room... Are there no depths the Millennium Seminar won't sink to? No torments they won't inflict?
- Koyuki (Seminar): Sensei, that's so mean! You're supposed to be on my side! A poor, downtrodden soul is telling you her tragic tale and this is how you respond?
- Koyuki (Seminar): Ugh, anyway...
- Koyuki (Seminar): The point is, whatever you have me do, it's at least gonna be more interesting than the Seminar stuff.
- Koyuki (Seminar): So, what is it? What's my assignment? Or, oh no... Do I have to work with passwords here too?
- Koyuki (Seminar): ...Huh?
- Koyuki (Seminar): You don't have any work like that? Oh, you definitely don't want me cracking passwords here?
- Koyuki (Seminar): Haha, why?
- Koyuki (Seminar): Hmm.
- Koyuki (Seminar): ...So? I...don't get it. Or...
- Koyuki (Seminar): No, I'm missing something. Huh? Is there something I'm not supposed to know?
- Koyuki (Seminar): What do you mean, there's a whole lot I'm not supposed to know?
- Koyuki (Seminar): Hmph... Fine. I guess.
- Koyuki (Seminar): ...Hmmmm...
- Koyuki (Seminar): No! I mean, um, uh, nothing! I'm not thinking anything at all!
- Koyuki (Seminar): I definitely wouldn't think oh, I should look at it!
- Koyuki (Seminar): I wouldn't think anything like that at all. I wouldn't, and I didn't!
- Koyuki (Seminar): Definitely!
- Koyuki (Seminar): Anyway! It seems like there's nothing for me to do here today, so...
- Koyuki (Seminar): Um...
- Koyuki (Seminar): I'll just...
- Koyuki (Seminar): I'll be...
- Koyuki (Seminar): Going for today... Sensei... I'll see you later...
- Koyuki (Seminar): Okay! Have a good day!
- "..."
