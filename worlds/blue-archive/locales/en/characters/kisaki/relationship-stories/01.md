> Genryumon Main Room
> Screen shakes
- Kisaki (Genryumon): I've been confined here!
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Hm. It's due to internal Genryumon matters...complex political issues. It'd be a difficult endeavor to explain it all to you.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): It includes factions like Black Tortoise Promenade, as well as Shanhaijing's dignity, in its position as an academy of Kivotos.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): In addition, the continuous increase of interest rates and taxes, the lack of recovery in people's incomes, as well as the collapse of public education have all contributed. It is a menagerie of issues, all collapsing upon themselves.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): We've reached the depths of this conflict, without an exit in sight.
- "I don't get it at all."
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Hm. As expected.
- "Even that answer. Don't get it."
- Kisaki (Genryumon): At times, action is more important than comprehension. Do you agree?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): We must gaze towards the sky and climb the steps of the high mountain.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): To put it simply, you, as an educator, must rise to your duty and help me overcome these circumstances. Sensei!
- "In short, you want me to get you out of here...?"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Hm. What a blessing, to have a friend who can ascertain my needs with such quickness.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): However, secretly escaping Rikkagaku, the headquarters of Genryumon itself, would be a fool's errand. Not without a plan.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): So, naturally, I've prepared one.
- "Is that...?!"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Yes. Here it is.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Now then, let us leave here, quietly.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Our escape from the main room was successful.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): But this area appears to be even more dangerous?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): While the criticism stings, you are correct, Sensei. This is the very center of Rikkagaku.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): The patrols here operate on very strict schedules and rotations.
- Genryumon Member A (Genryumon): Huh...? I'm the only one on duty right now.
- Genryumon Member A (Genryumon): What's going on? Did I misread the schedule?
- Genryumon Member A (Genryumon): No... A Genryumon member entrusted security would have never made such a rookie mistake!
- Genryumon Member A (Genryumon): I may not know every detail about every member, but I don't recognize you! Is this a Black Tortoise Promenade covert mission?!
- Kisaki (Genryumon): U-Um...!
- "(Kisaki...is in danger!)"
- Genryumon Member A (Genryumon): Oh, Sensei? You're here too...
- Genryumon Member A (Genryumon): A-Apologies! Please take no offense at my failure to recognize you! I tunnel-visioned on the fact that there appeared to be a mistake in our patrol rotation!
- Genryumon Member A (Genryumon): ...But if I think about it more, it's a bit strange. Why would you need Genryumon protection in the very heart of Rikkagaku, Sensei?
- "That...That's because...!"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): It's an extra security measure, under the guise of protection.
- Genryumon Member A (Genryumon): Security measure...? Oh!
- Genryumon Member A (Genryumon): So that means, this is a secret order, directly from the president...?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): That's correct.
- Genryumon Member A (Genryumon): I-I'm sorry, Sensei!
- Genryumon Member A (Genryumon): I'm sure you've gotten acclimated already, but please enjoy everything Genryumon has to offer, to the fullest!
- Kisaki (Genryumon): You've helped us overcome the first obstacle. Fufu.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Good work, Sensei. If we're able to avoid detection and escape Rikkagaku...
- Kisaki (Genryumon): ...that would be quite the victory for us.
> Kisaki's journey to escape confinement(?) continued along that vein...
- "The head of security is headed this way!"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): The news that a Genryumon member is attending to you should have spread all over Rikkagaku by now, Sensei...
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Which means that it will be nigh impossible for said Genryumon member to leave the premises.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): We must employ a different methodology for this case.
- Genryumon Member B (Genryumon): What?! What's a Black Tortoise Promenade member doing here?!
- Genryumon Member B (Genryumon): Get out of here immediately!
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Fufu. Just as expected. Shall we run?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): We've somehow escaped the heart of Rikkagaku.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): But a Black Tortoise Promenade member roaming around Rikkagaku Street would garner much suspicion.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): I must become someone whose presence by your side wouldn't garner a second glance, Sensei.
- "Is that even possible?!"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Fufu. Of course. It's...
- Kiki: Hello, Sensei! I'm Kiki! Today, we're walking around outside of the Plum Blossom Garden, right, Sensei?
- Kiki: Yay! Kiki likes Sensei best!
- Genryumon Member C (Genryumon): Um, Sensei? Why are you here...? Oh, I see. You're playing with the Plum Blossom Garden children.
- Genryumon Member C (Genryumon): Thank you for your hard work. And you, don't give Sensei a hard time!
- Kiki: Don't worry! Instructor Shun already told me the same thing!
- Genryumon Member C (Genryumon): All right, thank you.
- Genryumon Member C (Genryumon): (...Huh? But...)
- Genryumon Member C (Genryumon): (Why did a chill just run down my spine...?)
- "Now...are we reaching our goal? Bit by bit?"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Hm.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Fufu. Great work, Sensei.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): I sincerely appreciate your assistance in my escape play— Ahem! I mean, in escaping my confinement.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Huh? Was I really confined?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): ...
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Why would you ask such a thing, Sensei?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): It didn't seem like the Genryumon members were really looking for me, in the Rikkagaku or nearby streets...?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): I suppose it's no use attempting to hide the truth from you, Sensei.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): This was all part of my grand play.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): But it's been quite some time since I've been so entertained.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Fufu. Yes, it would appear so.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): But, Sensei. Genryumon's president...or rather, the student council presidents that reside in each academy...
- Kisaki (Genryumon): ...they're all confined in their own right, are they not?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Following such logic, every now and again, these presidents require guidance of the "educational" sort.
- "You can't just throw in the word 'educational' and make everything better!"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Fufu. I suppose you could perceive this as a manipulation to further my interests.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Worry not. I shall be sure to repay your kindness—
- "No. I had fun, too."
- Kisaki (Genryumon): I-Is that so...?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Occasionally, you say things that I can find no fitting response to.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Then, Sensei...
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Just for this day...could we continue our charade?
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): President! President! Where are you?! President!
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Ah. It would appear the absence of Genryumon's president was quickly noticed.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): I had assumed it would happen, however...
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Well then, Sensei. Shall we go?
- "Huh? Where are we going?"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Back to Genryumon, of course.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Fufu. Does that invalidate my request to continue our charade?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Of course...if you were to deny me and return, Sensei, I would have no way nor reason to stop you.
- "You don't play fair, Kisaki!"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Fufu. I'm aware. But I must take advantage of my presidential privileges, when the circumstances allow for it.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Now, then.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): For our remaining time...I look forward to working with you, Sensei.
