# Kisaki — Relationship Story 2

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Kisaki/Relationship_Story/2

> Schale Office
- Kisaki (Genryumon): ...So, this caused quite a stir.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): It goes against tradition for Genryumon's president to collaborate with outsiders. Which puts you in a rather difficult position.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): I, however, simply desired to abandon my post.
- "Really?!"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Fufu. I am making a joke.
- "I knew it..."
- Kisaki (Genryumon): However, behind every joke lies a grain of truth.
- "Which is it?!"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Fufu. Deciphering that is another aspect of the fun.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Now, let us return to our work.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): I've never had the opportunity to divest my efforts into tasks that were not related to Genryumon. I am rather excited.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): But before we begin...
- "What's this?"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): I was told that it is a medicinal incense, intended to calm the nerves and promote healing.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): The Eastern Alchemy Society secretly prepared it, when they heard that I would be the student rep.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Now, let us begin. Time is a precious commodity, after all.
> That's how working with Kisaki started...
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Hm... It's become popular to record personal broadcasts, and these "challenge videos," in abandoned buildings of the old city.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Local residents have expressed a growing sense of anxiety about the noise and threat to security.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): They appear to be requesting additional cooperation, including a statement, from Schale?
- "What would you do, Kisaki?"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): It's clear. The root of the cause is the abandoned building.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): With the help of Genryumon, we will simply demolish it, down to its foundation.
- "That's a very aggressive tactic!"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Is that so? Such would be the norm in Shanhaijing.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): ...We need to consider the context?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): I suppose...but I still believe that these kinds of situations cannot be resolved without strong and commandeering leadership.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): What's next? Large protests, held by students upset by the lack of firearm and ammo supply.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): As it should be. A constant and efficient supply of firearms, as well as their maintenance, should be regarded with the utmost importance in Kivotos. Guns are as essential as food, clothing, and shelter.
- "(Really?!)"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Students hold an inherent right to protest, as well as the responsibility to endure the resulting criticism.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): There is no need for further investigation. There appears to be an inappropriate use of funds here, as this ledger provided by the supplier appears to be manipulated.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): It is the way of Genryumon to respond with quick and absolute force—
- "We can't just use physical force willy-nilly!"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): ...Is that so? In Shanhaijing, if you so desire, you may execute wrongdoers within the same day...
- Kisaki (Genryumon): *sigh* Naturally, it would be ideal if people were to simply abide by the law.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): However, you must understand, Sensei. In this world, people walk a thin line that must be meticulously managed.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Simply determine whether it is a false accusation or an infraction of the law, and apply the death penalty, if required.
- "N-No to the death penalty!"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): I do mean that in a figurative sense—
- "I don't care! No!"
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Is that so...
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Hm. That completes today's tasks, Sensei.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Your usual work time was nearly halved? Fufu. I'm pleased I could be of service.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Hm? My speed and judgment were impressive, but my suggested resolutions were...extreme?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): How saddening. It would appear the world has not grown quite big enough to understand the ways of Shanhaijing, as of yet.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Then, Sensei. What are you going to do now?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Hm? Things ended much sooner than you expected, so you don't have any plans?
> I kept smelling a wonderful fragrance coming from somewhere.
> I realized it was the censer Kisaki had brought with her.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Did something happen, Sensei? You appear exhausted.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Ah. You were so inundated with work, you were unable to sleep for three days?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): That's unacceptable. Someone as important as yourself must take care with their body. Shall we change locations, then?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Lay down here. I shall lend you my knee.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Yes, yes... Give yourself to the fragrance, and rest your eyes.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Breathe in, breathe out... That's right...
> At some point, it was hard to discern whether it was a dream or reality.
> But in the midst of it all, I felt the touch of a small hand, patting me gently.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): ...Are you dreaming, Sensei? You have a calm expression.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Although, they say dreaming is proof of not being in a deep sleep.
> On my forehead...
- Kisaki (Genryumon): There's an old tale that goes like so.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): There was once a person who dreamt they were a butterfly, flying about the world. They were thrilled, content with life.
> On my cheeks...
- Kisaki (Genryumon): The butterfly was so pleased, it knew not whether it was a person or something else entirely.
- Kisaki (Genryumon): But, suddenly, they awoke from their dreams. They had reverted to their human form, no longer a butterfly.
> On my neck...
- Kisaki (Genryumon): They couldn't help but wonder. Is the butterfly me? Or am I the butterfly?
- Kisaki (Genryumon): Or was there never any distinction? Something so insignificant...
> When I opened my eyes, both Kisaki and the censer were gone.
> I wasn't even sure if Kisaki had ever actually been there.
