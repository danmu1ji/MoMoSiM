# Miyu

## Description

A sniper of the RABBIT platoon belonging to SRT Special Academy. She enrolled SRT Special Academy because of her talent as a sniper, only to find her school suddenly closed. She is now living in the park with fellow platoon members. Miyu has a weak presence, which in itself is useful for sniper missions, but she is often forgotten not only by the enemies but also by her allies. Her callsign is "RABBIT 4".
