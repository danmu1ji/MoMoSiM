> Trail near Kousagi Park
> I found Miyu crouching down, picking at something.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Umm... Not this rock...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Not this one either.
- "What are you doing?"
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): S-S-Sensei?!
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Wh-When did you get here?
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): I didn't hear you at all.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Have you been with me too much? D-Did you lose your presence too?!
- "I was just walking quietly."
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Oh... That's good.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): I was scared you were l-like me now...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): If you became invisible too...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): No one could open the automatic door for me when I go to work...
- "...That's your concern?"
- "Anyway, what were you doing?"
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Well, um...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): I was picking some p-pebbles.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Collecting pebbles is my hobby...
- "Neat. Did you find anything interesting?"
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): N-Not like that.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): I like things no one would normally care about, I guess...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): L-Like a normal pebble that's been kicked around a lot.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Pebbles like those...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): I really relate to them.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): So I wanna take care of them.
- "I think I can understand that."
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): R-Really?
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Th-That makes me happy.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): S-Sensei. Do you...wanna join me?
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): There's lots of normal pebbles here. I-It's my favorite spot.
- "Sure."
> I found a normal pebble and handed it to Miyu.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): This one...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Sensei, this is a broken brick piece.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): It has a role already...it's not very normal.
- "What about this, then?"
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): It's so smooth...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Even people who don't like rocks would pick it.
- "What about this?"
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): This is granite, a volcanic rock... It's rare...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Sensei. You only pick not normal ones.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Pretty and rare stones are easier to see, b-but that's not what we're looking for.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): P-Please take it seriously!
- "You're stricter than I thought you'd be."
> Miyu and I worked hard together to find normal pebbles until the sun went down.
