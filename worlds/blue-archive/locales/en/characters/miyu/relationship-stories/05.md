# Miyu — Relationship Story 5

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Miyu/Relationship_Story/5

> A building rooftop near Kousagi Town
> I went to the specified building rooftop to help out Miyu with her mission.
> It was still early in the morning, so the cold wind on the rooftop chilled me to the bone.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Current time is 0830. Temperature, seven degrees Celsius. Wind speed is 20 knots, from the southwest...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): No sightings of the target, Tango Charlie, yet...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Oh, S-Sensei.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Y-You see...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): We received a request from locals to eliminate a crow that's been going through trash cans in the area.
- "A crow?"
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Y-Yes. They said that a crow in the area has been ripping up trash bags in search of shiny things to steal.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): The problem is, crows are r-really smart. They're hard to get.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): They know to hide from guns and even what times areas are less crowded and safe to pillage.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): S-So, I'm going to hide on this rooftop with this tranquilizer gun all day, and try to catch the crow alive.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Given all that... Do you really want to wait with me, Sensei?
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Sniper missions tend to be a lot of waiting... We have no idea when the target will appear.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Well, that's a relief, if you mean it...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): I-In that case, I'll continue my surveillance.
> ...But the cold only got worse, and it was getting unbearable.
- "Ugh..."
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): S-Sensei... Are you cold?
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): You've been shivering this entire time.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): I-I've always been more sensitive to high temperatures rather than the cold...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): My ghillie suit is p-pretty thick as well, so that helps too.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): A warm drink...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): W-Wait, Sensei. Just a moment... I have it... Right here! You can have this.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): It's hot cocoa. Please, help yourself.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): We've been up here for a while, but I think the thermos should've kept it warm...
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): O-Of course. I'm really happy I could do something for you, Sensei.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Actually, snipers avoid drinking liquids during missions as much as possible.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): It'd be a problem if we lost the target because we had to go to the bathroom.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): So, we hold off on drinking anything until the end of the operation, no matter how thirsty we are.
- "Then what's the purpose of the thermos?"
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): That's... My squad members made this hot cocoa.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): Whenever we have a mission during cold weather, they make me some hot cocoa or tea to help keep me warm.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): So, I use the thermos to warm my hands throughout the mission.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): It's nice, because it's almost as if my squad members are right here with me, even though I'm by myself.
- "You really treasure your squad members."
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): ...Of course.
- Miyu (RABBIT Squad): After all, together, we all make up the RABBIT Squad!
> For the remainder of the mission, we passed the thermos of hot cocoa back and forth, courtesy of the RABBIT squad's warm love.
