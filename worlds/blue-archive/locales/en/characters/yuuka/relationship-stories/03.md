# Yuuka — Relationship Story 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> Schale Sensei's Executive Office
- Yuuka (Seminar): I heard something interesting, Sensei! Is it true that you recovered a ton of equipment after raiding those hoodlums' armory?
- Yuuka (Seminar): That should put them out of commission for a while. Good work!
- Yuuka (Seminar): You don't look very happy, though. Is there something wrong with the equipment?
- Yuuka (Seminar): You're saying the equipment is fine but something's wrong with the inventory? What do you mean?
- Hand the equipment inventory to Yuuka.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Let's see here...200 gallons of tank fuel, 600 pounds of TNT, 0.3 caliber cartridges...
- Yuuka (Seminar): It might be due to their age, but most of these measurement units are from Trinity's old system.
- Yuuka (Seminar): So you're having a hard time organizing the ledger because you're unfamiliar with these units?
- Yuuka (Seminar): Since it's handwritten, you wouldn't be able to easily convert it with a computer either.
- Yuuka (Seminar): The calculation shouldn't be too difficult. All this will take is a little mental math.
- Yuuka (Seminar): I should have this done in about 30 minutes or so.
- Yuuka (Seminar): What? Don't believe me? Just you watch.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Give me a little time.
- Yuuka (Seminar): All done. I've reorganized the inventory according to customary units.
- Yuuka (Seminar): I rounded some of the units up or down that couldn't be calculated exactly, so make sure to read my footnotes.
- "Did you really do all that in 30 minutes?"
- Yuuka (Seminar): Heh heh. What did I tell you?
- Yuuka (Seminar): This was nothing. I wouldn't be Millennium's treasurer if I couldn't handle something as simple as that.
- "Then can you do this for me too?"
- Yuuka (Seminar): A document? Is this a...?
- Yuuka (Seminar): Sensei! This is a regular report from Schale meant for the General Student Council!
- Yuuka (Seminar): This bill is three months overdue!
- Yuuka (Seminar): I know I'm great with numbers, but you can't hand things over to other people just because you're lazy! Do it yourself!
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...And don't look at me like that!
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...
- Yuuka (Seminar): *sigh* Fine. I'll just help you out a little bit.
- Yuuka (Seminar): I have to stop being such a pushover...
- Yuuka (Seminar): Why are you just sitting there? Those other papers have YOUR name on them, so keep your pen moving!
> After that, Yuuka and I organized the ledger in peace.
