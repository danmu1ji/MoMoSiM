> Millennium Seminar Office
> I offered to play poker with Yuuka while we wait for the others to arrive.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Poker? Where's this coming from?
- Yuuka (Seminar): Though it's not a bad idea. I was staving off boredom.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Heh heh... You'd better be ready. I've always been good at gambling.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Gambling is a process where you bet on an expected value based on the probability of your hand versus the probability of your opponent's hand.
- Yuuka (Seminar): A skilled gambler knows how to control their emotions and objectively analyze the probabilities to minimize risks.
- Yuuka (Seminar): In other words, the best gamblers are also those that best understand math.
- "Prove it."
- Yuuka (Seminar): Heh heh. Then deal the cards and let's play a hand.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Um...
- Yuuka (Seminar): (I have three of a kind in hand... A hand that appropriately only has a three percent chance of being attained.)
- Yuuka (Seminar): (Granted, it's not the strongest hand in the game, but the odds are in my favor to win.)
- Yuuka (Seminar): Call! I have three of a kind.
- "Flush."
- Yuuka (Seminar): What? You have five cards of the same suit? The probability of getting that hand is only 0.2%!
- "Anything can happen on the cards table."
- Yuuka (Seminar): That's true, but...
- Yuuka (Seminar): You got lucky this time. Let's go for another round!
- Yuuka (Seminar): (Great. I'm definitely going to win this time!)
- Yuuka (Seminar): Straight!
- "Four of a kind."
- Yuuka (Seminar): Four aces?! I can't believe this!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Ugh... One more hand!
- Yuuka (Seminar): (The probability of getting this hand is only 0.0015%... Surely Sensei can't beat this hand.)
- Yuuka (Seminar): (My victory is assured!)
- Yuuka (Seminar): Straight flush!
- "Royal straight flush."
- Yuuka (Seminar): No way!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Ugh. How did I lose three times in a row...? All three were good hands too!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Did I make a miscalculation?
- "It takes more than calculations to win at poker."
- Yuuka (Seminar): But the chances of getting certain hands are always set in stone. What am I supposed to rely on if not the calculations?
- Yuuka (Seminar): I have to rely on my intuition and instincts instead?
- Yuuka (Seminar): How does one use instincts in a game of chance? It doesn't make sense to me, though I can't argue with the results.
- "I'd recommend not gambling in the first place."
- Yuuka (Seminar): Yes, I can agree with that.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Allow me to put away the cards.
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...Sensei. Why are there two aces of hearts on the table?
- Yuuka (Seminar): There's only one per deck, and poker is played with one deck of cards...
- Yuuka (Seminar): Were you hiding cards up your sleeves?!
- Yuuka (Seminar): You are unbelievable!
