> Millennium Seminar Office
- Yuuka (Seminar): There you are, Sensei.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Today's agenda pertains to Millennium's finances, so I can't really discuss it with the other students. I've been waiting for you to arrive.
- Yuuka (Seminar): I've been mulling it over all day, but haven't come up with a suitable solution.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Could you take a look at this picture?
- Yuuka (Seminar): What do you think it looks like?
- Yuuka (Seminar): *sigh* So you agree.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Huh. I never thought of it that way.
- Yuuka (Seminar): I know it's abstract, but would it kill you to be a little considerate?
- Yuuka (Seminar): Let me get to the point. This abstract painting was given to us as collateral by an entrepreneur who owes the Millennium School money.
- Yuuka (Seminar): According to this entrepreneur, it was painted by a genius painter named Matsumoto from Wildhunt Art Academy, and it's worth at least 100 million yen.
- Yuuka (Seminar): But no matter how much I stare at it, it looks like it was scribbled by a toddler.
- "Some art is just like that."
- Yuuka (Seminar): I'm aware, but I can't shake my disappointment. That's all.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Taking all supplies into account, it couldn't have cost more than 10,000 yen to produce.
- Yuuka (Seminar): But it becomes thousands of times more valuable just because the artist was supposedly famous...? I can't fathom why that would be.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Then again, I'm only Seminar's treasurer. It's not my job to appraise art. I only wonder whether or not this art qualifies as collateral.
- "You can't calculate the value of art with conventional math."
- Yuuka (Seminar): If it can't be calculated with a formula, then how DOES one calculate it?
- Yuuka (Seminar): Are you saying that when appraising art, you need to examine the artist's effort and creativity?
- Yuuka (Seminar): Interesting... I suppose I'd be angry if a fully functional robot and a pile of scrap metal were considered equal in value just because they weighed the same.
- Yuuka (Seminar): But that brings us back to square one. Since I can't evaluate art, I want you to appraise this painting.
- Yuuka (Seminar): How much do you think it's worth?
- "Zero yen."
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...What? How can that be?
- Yuuka (Seminar): Wildhunt Art Academy doesn't have a famous artist named Matsumoto?
That would mean this artwork is just a counterfeit?
- Yuuka (Seminar): Then why didn't you tell me that from the start?!
> Though the painting is worthless, we decided to hang it up anyway since there was nowhere else to store it.
