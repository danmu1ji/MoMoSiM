> Schale Sensei's Executive Office
- Yuuka (Seminar): Hello, Sensei. I brought the invoice that I mentioned in my message. Please review it when you have time.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Oh! You were eating. I'm so sorry to bother you.
- "It's okay. Don't worry about it."
- Yuuka (Seminar): Then let me tidy your desk while you eat.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Sensei...is bread all you're having for lunch?
- Yuuka (Seminar): Sorry if this too forward...but shouldn't you be eating something more nutritious?
- Yuuka (Seminar): A diet of exclusively bread can't be good for your health.
- Yuuka (Seminar): What?! You're out of money and won't be able to afford meals until the end of the month?
- Yuuka (Seminar): I had no idea Schale's faculty were paid so little... I'm shocked.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Wait a moment. Isn't this receipt from that famous toy store in central Kivotos?
- Yuuka (Seminar): It's from last week, and you spent...100,000 yen on limited edition transforming robot toys?!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Sensei! Explain this receipt! This is a month's worth of food!
- Yuuka (Seminar): You gave up meals for childish toys? You must be out of your mind!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Nonsense! You need to start spending more wisely!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Nonsense! You need to start spending more wisely!
- Yuuka (Seminar): I've had enough. I'm going to start reviewing your expenses myself! Where do you keep your checkbook?
- Yuuka (Seminar): What? You don't keep a checkbook?
- Yuuka (Seminar): That's why you spend so impulsively!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Sensei, gather all your receipts from the past month.
- Yuuka (Seminar): From now on, I'll be your bookkeeper and the one monitoring your expenses.
- Yuuka: ― Seriously... If you're an adult, you should know better than to make impulsive purchases.
- Yuuka: ― You're not like those kids who spend all their pocket money as soon as they get it, are you?
- Yuuka: ― On top of that, I'm the only one who helps you sort your receipts like this...
- Yuuka: ― If you keep this up, I'm not going to help anymore!
- Yuuka: ― Convenience store chicken sandwich two weeks ago...500 yen.
- Yuuka: ― New manga volume...450 yen.
- Yuuka: ― Huh? You spent 20,000 yen at Club Smoochie the other night?
- Yuuka: ― S-Sensei! Is this receipt what I think it is?!
- Yuuka: ― You're a teacher! You're supposed to set an example for your students! How could you...?
- Yuuka: ― Despicable! You're the absolute worst!
- Yuuka: ― Huh? Club Smoochie is the name of a gacha game?
- Yuuka: ― Geez. I thought you were visiting a...
- Yuuka: ― Wait a minute! That doesn't excuse you blowing 20,000 yen in a night!
- Yuuka: ― No more impulse purchases! You need to budget!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Look here. Putting aside the transforming robot, you could have saved 50,000 yen!
- Yuuka (Seminar): If you start keeping tabs on your expenses from time to time, you won't have to live off of plain bread anymore.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Anytime you want to buy something that costs over 5,000 yen, you have to consult with me first!
- "But why should I have to report my expenses to you?"
- Yuuka (Seminar): Well, I... If I want to take care of you, then I have to stay on top of your spending habits...
- Yuuka (Seminar): Anyway! It's for your own good, so please just do it!
> Yuuka continued to nag me for a while before leaving.
