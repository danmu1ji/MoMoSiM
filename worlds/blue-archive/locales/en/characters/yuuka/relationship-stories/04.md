# Yuuka — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Yuuka/Relationship_Story/4

> Millennium Seminar Office
- Yuuka (Seminar): Why is everyone at this academy always so late? Even the teachers.
- Yuuka (Seminar): If they just went to bed on time, they wouldn't oversleep. How hard is it to follow one simple rule...?
- Yuuka (Seminar): My workload would be half what it is now if Millennium's students just knew how to control themselves.
- Yuuka (Seminar): *yawn* Look at the time...
- Yuuka (Seminar): If I stay up any later, I might miss the morning meeting at Schale.
- Yuuka (Seminar): I should...hurry up...and finish...
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...Huh? Morning already?
- Yuuka (Seminar): Did I fall asleep while doing Student Council work? Oh, no...
- Yuuka (Seminar): What time is it...?
- Yuuka (Seminar): Already 11 AM?! I-I'm late!
- Yuuka (Seminar): I have to do my hair... No, I have to organize the documents for the meeting first...!
> Schale Executive Office
- Yuuka (Seminar): I-I'm late!
- "Good morning, Yuuka."
- Yuuka (Seminar): I-I'm so sorry, Sensei. I was working late and overslept...
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...
- Yuuka (Seminar): Hold on. Were you on time today?
- "Of course. Didn't you tell me to never be late yesterday?"
- Yuuka (Seminar): ...I'm sorry!
- Yuuka (Seminar): I feel like such a hypocrite after yesterday... I have no excuse.
- Yuuka (Seminar): I'd understand if you held it against me.
- Yuuka (Seminar): I'll do anything to make it up to you—just say the word!
- "It's fine, Yuuka..."
- Yuuka (Seminar): No! Rules are rules! What kind of treasurer would I be if I didn't follow the rules that I make?!
- Yuuka (Seminar): Don't hold back. I'll do anything!
- "In that case, your punishment is to go to the Residence Hall and take a nap."
- Yuuka (Seminar): What...? But that's not a punishment.
- Yuuka (Seminar): I have to be punished if I'm going to learn my lesson.
- "What you really need is rest. You're working too hard."
- Yuuka (Seminar): But...
- "Didn't you say you'd do anything?"
- Yuuka (Seminar): *sigh* I should have seen this coming.
- Yuuka (Seminar): Have it your way. I'll take a break in the lounge, then.
- Yuuka (Seminar): But this doesn't mean you get to be late from now on. I'll be monitoring your attendance like a hawk.
- Yuuka (Seminar): And I won't be late either!
> While Yuuka rested in the lounge, I took care of some tasks for Seminar in her place.
