# Yuuka\n\nEnrolled in the Millennium Science School, Yuuka is the treasurer of the student council known as "Seminar". A prominent mathematical genius even amongst Millennium's STEM-rich student base, she supervises the management of the school's budget. Yuuka's specialty is the abacus, and she has a habit of counting on one in order to compose herself when faced with complex and troubling matters.\n\nSource: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Yuuka\nReview status: source extracted; pending editorial review.\n
