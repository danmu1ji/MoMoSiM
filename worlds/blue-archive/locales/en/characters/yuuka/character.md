# Yuuka

## Description

Enrolled in the Millennium Science School, Yuuka is the treasurer of the student council known as "Seminar". A prominent mathematical genius even amongst Millennium's STEM-rich student base, she supervises the management of the school's budget. Yuuka's specialty is the abacus, and she has a habit of counting on one in order to compose herself when faced with complex and troubling matters.

## Appearance

Yuuka has waist-length navy hair in twin tails, each tied with a triangular device resembling her halo, and bright-blue eyes with red slit-shaped pupils.
