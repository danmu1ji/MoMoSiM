# Hanako

## Description

Trinity's Supplemental Lessons Club's paragon of sensitivity. At first glance, Hanako appears graceful and ladylike... Or so one may think. In reality she can be quite the problem child who can't stop speaking of sex. Her club members, aware of this, are often at a sense of unease every time she speaks.
