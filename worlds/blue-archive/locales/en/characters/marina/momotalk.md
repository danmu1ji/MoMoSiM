## MomoTalk 2

- Marina: Suspicious...
- Sensei: What is?
- Marina: Snacks are mysteriously disappearing from the School Lunch Club.
- Sensei: Most notably, the pudding count is significantly less than what's recorded on the ledger.
- Sensei reply option: Maybe you miscounted?
- Sensei reply option: Maybe someone ate them?
- Marina: That's impossible.
- Sensei: I've done the math several times, along with other Security Council members.
- Marina: That's impossible.
- Sensei: The Security Council has been watching the School Lunch Club facility like hawks!
- Marina: So if it's not a miscount, and nobody ate them...
- Marina: There must be a spy trying to sabotage Red Winter Academy!
- Sensei: A spy?
- Marina: Red Winter Academy is very large. There are students we don't know much about because our student records haven't been fully digitized.
- Marina: It's very possible there's a spy from another academy among the students.
- Marina: The spy must be trying to cause internal conflict by going after our weaknesses. How disgraceful.
- Sensei: So starting today, a special spy search operation begins!
