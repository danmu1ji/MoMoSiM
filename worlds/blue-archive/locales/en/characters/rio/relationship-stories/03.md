# Rio — Relationship Story 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

- Delivery Man: Got an order here for a 3D printer and...powder based materials, right?
- Delivery Man: If you could just sign here...
- Rio (Seminar): Hello, Sensei.
- Rio (Seminar): Feels like...I've gotten somewhat used to this. It feels fresh.
- "Looks like you ordered a 3D printer."
- Rio (Seminar): That's right. I'd also like a spacious place with good ventilation.
- Rio (Seminar): Since 3D printers tend to generate lots of waste products during the process.
- Rio (Seminar): ...Which is why I couldn't do the work from my hideout.
- "I'm sure I could find a place if I looked for one..."
- "How about here?"
- Rio (Seminar): Here looks good. There's a nice big window right there...
- Rio (Seminar): I'll be borrowing this space, then.
- Rio (Seminar): All right. If I start this now, then...
- Rio (Seminar): Well, let's step outside. You're going to need some fresh air.
- "Are you making things for a particular project?"
- Rio (Seminar): Not as such. I just happen to experiment with new industrial designs sometimes.
- Rio (Seminar): It helps get the kinks out early, and it plays a part in aesthetics too.
- Rio (Seminar): It's work, but it's...not unenjoyable work.
- "So it's a hobby, then?"
- Rio (Seminar): Hm? A hobby?
- Rio (Seminar): I-Is it?
- Rio (Seminar): I never thought of it that way.
- Rio (Seminar): But...hobbies are inherently irrational.
- Rio (Seminar): They're purely for recreation. There's no study, or work, or training involved, no profit or utility in them...
- Rio (Seminar): Meaning the input-to-output ratio invariably converges at zero.
- Rio (Seminar): Could I even consider spending my time that way?
- "Eh. Nothing says you can't."
- Rio (Seminar): So...getting a hobby isn't necessarily a bad thing? You'd actually recommend it?
- Rio (Seminar): ...
- Rio (Seminar): Well, there is one other problem with that.
- Rio (Seminar): The designs I've done... You might already know this...
- Rio (Seminar): Other people...don't tend to appreciate them.
- Rio (Seminar): I strive for rationality and functional beauty, and most people don't seem to understand that.
- Rio (Seminar): ...Maybe I'm just doing it wrong.
- Rio (Seminar): No, no "maybe" about it. I must be doing it wrong.
- Rio (Seminar): Statistically, the probability of EVERYONE else being wrong about my work is infinitesimal. The fault is definitely mine.
- "Whoa!"
- Rio (Seminar): ...Hm?
- Rio (Seminar): Art...?
- Rio (Seminar): I'm...an artist?!
- Rio (Seminar): I-I never thought of it that way.
- Rio (Seminar): Art is entirely subjective, of course. There's no room for objectivity there...
- Rio (Seminar): There are no specific parameters that define art's quality...and in that sense, no one can truly say it's wrong!
- Rio (Seminar): ...
- Rio (Seminar): I see...
- Rio (Seminar): It is art.
- Rio (Seminar): I've been doing art. ...Which means I CAN do art.
- Rio (Seminar): I...wasn't wrong at all!
- Rio (Seminar): Thank you, Sensei!
- "Huh? Well, sure..."
- Rio (Seminar): You always see what I've been missing.
- Rio (Seminar): I suppose that's why you're "Sensei."
- Rio (Seminar): Sensei MEANS "one who's lived ahead," after all. One who can lead others, who can explain what others don't understand yet. Of course. That's why...!
- "R-Rio? Are you okay?"
- Rio (Seminar): I don't know. I...feel like it's my first time being this happy.
- Rio (Seminar): I've always felt like I'm in some constant struggle just to keep pushing forward.
- Rio (Seminar): But...
- Rio (Seminar): ...if I'm by your side, I could just stay like this...!
> I heard the sound of the 3D printing being completed.
- "Is it done? I want to see how it turned out."
- Rio (Seminar): And...!
- Rio (Seminar): ...Um, Sensei?
- Rio (Seminar): Sorry. Don't turn around, please just stay there and listen.
- Rio (Seminar): ...Thank you for what you said.
- Rio (Seminar): It really made me happy...hearing that.
> As I watched Rio walk away, I thought more about what art is.
