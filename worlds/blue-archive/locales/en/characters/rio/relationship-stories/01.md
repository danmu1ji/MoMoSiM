> I went where Rio asked me to go.
> The process of getting there, through all the security, was quite a hassle.
- Rio (Seminar): Are you here, Sensei? Have you been waiting long?
- Rio (Seminar): You just got here? I see. I'd mistakenly assumed you would follow the optional route.
- "Let me ask you something..."
- Rio (Seminar): Hm? What do I usually eat?
- Rio (Seminar): Well, of course, discount dinner bento and frozen food. That's a very logical choice.
- Rio (Seminar): For one thing, that bento can reach up to 50% off, and the taste is acceptable.
- Rio (Seminar): Especially the katsu bento and kaarage rice bowls. In fact, those are amazing.
- Rio (Seminar): Unfortunately, the place I usually get it from took the day off.
- Rio (Seminar): On a normal day, they have people fighting for their food. That's how good it is.
- Rio (Seminar): With frozen food, it lasts a long time, and you can store it in bulk.
- Rio (Seminar): Again, a logical choice.
- Rio (Seminar): I can't order delivery. It's irrational. Delivery orders can be tracked.
- Rio (Seminar): If a delivery driver happened to remember my face, I'd be in a world of danger.
- Rio (Seminar): Not only that, but they offer perks for leaving reviews. Discounts and such.
- Rio (Seminar): It would be irrational of me not to take advantage of that...
- Rio (Seminar): ...but again, any review could be traced back to me.
- Rio (Seminar): In the vernacular, it's a dilemma.
- Rio (Seminar): And I prefer to avoid any such dilemmas whenever possible.
- Rio (Seminar): As is, again, logical.
- "That food's cheap now, sure, but hospital bills are expensive."
- Rio (Seminar): H-Huh? Well, bento and frozen foods ARE often fried...
- Rio (Seminar): ...which means they're too high in carbohydrates and fat, and they lack fiber and vitamins...
- Rio (Seminar): ...That is indeed a factor, yes. But I can always take vitamin supplements.
- Rio (Seminar): And as for fiber...
- Rio (Seminar): Well, my body has been fine without fiber so far.
- "That isn't fine."
- Rio (Seminar): How significant could plant fiber possibly be for anyone's body? Plus there are all kinds of supplements...
- Rio (Seminar): ...besides, I can't cook.
- Rio (Seminar): Huh? What if I make something like the Avant Guard, but for cooking? As in, with online recipes?
- Rio (Seminar): Well, I can't say I've ever tried that...
- "...But?"
- Rio (Seminar): ...But I'm scared...
- Rio (Seminar): No, their prices are too high. It's an irrational choice.
- "I'm buying. Let's go."
- Rio (Seminar): A-All right...
> I dragged Rio off to the supermarket.
- Rio (Seminar): S-Sensei, what IS that? Some white vegetable in a fractal structure...? The Hausdorff dimension...seems to be about 2.7...
- Rio (Seminar): It's called "cauliflower"? I-It looks scary to eat... Hmm? It tastes like potato when it's boiled properly...?
- Rio (Seminar): ...So here we have potatoes, carrots, onions, and...tomatoes. Yes, those I know. On a related note...
- Rio (Seminar): Are you sure you want to buy that much meat...?
> And so, I taught Rio, who knew nothing about grocery shopping, how to shop for herself.
> Honestly, I didn't know much about grocery shopping either, so I got a lot of help from the internet...
- Rio (Seminar): ...
- Rio (Seminar): I see, it's based on protein and vegetables. With less carbohydrate content than before.
- Rio (Seminar): So what now, Sensei?
- "I'll cook for you at Schale."
- Rio (Seminar): H-Huh? You know how to cook?
- Rio (Seminar): You don't, at all? Then how...? ...Your plan is to follow some recipes off the internet and hope something edible comes out of it?
- Rio (Seminar): ...Listen, far be it from me to put any stock in premonitions...
- Rio (Seminar): ...but I do have a bit of a bad feeling about this.
- "That's fair. It hurts, but it's fair..."
> After a bit more meandering, we made it back to Schale.
- "Go relax for a bit. I'll...try to make this work."
- Rio (Seminar): A-All right.
- Rio (Seminar): Is this...?
- Rio (Seminar): Stew, hmm? So...anyone can cook stew, relatively speaking?
- Rio (Seminar): ...
- Rio (Seminar): Thank you for the food, Sensei.

- Rio (Seminar): Somehow, it...

- Rio (Seminar): ...doesn't taste bad.

- "I see..."
- Rio (Seminar): ...
