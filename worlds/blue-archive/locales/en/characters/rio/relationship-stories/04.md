- Rio (Seminar): As I mentioned before, I have multiple hideouts.
- Rio (Seminar): Consider yourself invited to one of them.
- Rio (Seminar): It can be our shared secret place, in a sense.

- Rio (Seminar): ...then I'll know, no one else but you could have told anyone.
- Rio (Seminar): Fufu. If that happens, I'll need proper compensation.
- Rio (Seminar): Not that I'm expecting it to happen, of course. I trust you.
- "A little MORE trust than that would be nice..."
- Rio (Seminar): Duly noted.
- Rio (Seminar): I'll look forward to seeing you.

- Rio (Seminar): But...
- Rio (Seminar): W-Wait, where are you going all of a sudden?!
- "We're going to need some 50L trash bags."
