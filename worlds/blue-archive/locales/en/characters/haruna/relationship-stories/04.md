# Haruna — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Haruna/Relationship_Story/4
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> D.U. Outskirts District
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Oh, Sensei?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): I didn't expect to run into you here.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Out on another one of your walks?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Same here. I'm glad I ran into you.
- "What were you up to?"
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): I've been scoping out new restaurants to try.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): There was a place I wanted to check out that people have been raving about on social media...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...but it was nothing like what I expected.
- "Please don't tell me you blew up another restaurant."
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Heh heh. Where would be the fun in doing that every time?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): The thing is...I have been thinking about food more deeply lately...
- "You must have a lot on your mind."
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...Thanks for always looking out for me.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Now that we're here, want to go on a walk together?
- "I'd love to."
> (Haruna and I talked about food as we walked.)
> On our way, a taiyaki store caught my eye.
- "Care for some taiyaki?"
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...Oh, taiyaki.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Heh. I'd love some, Sensei!
- Taiyaki Store Owner: Thank you!
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...Heh heh.
- Haruna: ― Heh. I'm enjoying walking home together.
- Haruna: ― We have a bunch of taiyaki now too.
- Haruna: ― Don't I think it's a bit underwhelming?
- Haruna: ― No... To me, this moment is the pinnacle of luxury.
- Haruna: ― Here, Sensei. This is for you.
- Haruna: ― No. Open your mouth. Say "ahhh."
- "Ahhh."
- Haruna: ― Eating my favorite food with my favorite person.
- Haruna: ― This is...
- "I'm starting to feel a little bashful..."
- Haruna: ― Hmm? Why are you looking at me like that?
- Haruna: ― Heh... Still, it doesn't particularly bother me.
- Haruna: ― Aren't I cold?
- Haruna: ― No. I feel warm, actually.
> (Haruna and I cheerfully walked back home while sharing the taiyaki.)
