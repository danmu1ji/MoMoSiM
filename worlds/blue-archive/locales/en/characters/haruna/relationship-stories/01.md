# Haruna — Relationship Story 1

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> Gehenna Student Dining Hall 17
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Heh heh heh. The dining hall is closed. There will be no more students to interfere.
> *click*
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Good thing the hall still has electricity and running water after hours.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): This is the perfect spot for a little epicurean research. No one will bother us here!
- "Why am I here...?"
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Is that a rhetorical question?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): I need an adult here to take responsibility in case we're caught by the Prefect Team.
- "That's just cruel..."
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Heh. No one said life is fair. Now, let's get down to business.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Today's epicurean subject is coq au vin!
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): You should already know, but allow me to explain coq au vin...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Coq au vin.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): It's an easy stew made by boiling chicken and vegetables. You just put the ingredients in the pot and boil it all together.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): However...if it were really that simple, it wouldn't be worth researching.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): What makes coq au vin different is that instead of water, wine is used to boil the chicken.
- "Wine...?"
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Yes, as in the alcohol.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Naturally, it's off limits here. No student in Kivotos can buy wine.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): The upscale restaurant that serves this dish doesn't allow students inside...so there's no way for me to try it for myself.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Instead...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...I prepared ethyl alcohol and grape juice as a substitute!
- "What?!"
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Heh heh heh. I've been searching for a combination that would act as a good substitute for the taste of wine.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): All I need is grapes and alcohol, no? This should be perfect. With this combination, I can make my own coq au vin.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Just watch, Sensei.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): First, grill the bacon in the frying pan.
> *sizzle*
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Next, fry the breaded chicken in the bacon grease.
> *sizzle*
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): After that, put the cooked chicken, mushrooms, carrots, and onions in the pot.
> (pours)
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Then, pour the grape juice.
> (pours)
- "I don't know about this..."
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Heh heh. Don't worry, Sensei. The plan is foolproof.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Finally, it's time for the alcohol...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Hmm... How much should I put in?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): I should have thought about this beforehand...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Well, I'm sure it'll be fine. The book I read said that alcohol evaporates when it's boiled...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): I'll just dump it in!
> *pours*
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Heh heh heh. A true epicure is never stingy with ingredients. I like to put in more than enough...
> *pours*
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Okay, maybe I went a little overboard...but I'm sure it will be fine..
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Let's turn up the heat...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Huh?
> Screen shakes
> *bang*
> (The pot exploded.)
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...?!
- "Are you okay, Haruna?!"
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Yes, I-I'm fine...but the coq au vin is...
> *braaang*
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): We set off the fire alarm?!
- Gehenna Student Voice 1: There was an explosion in the closed dining hall!
- Gehenna Student Voice 2: Could it be an attack?! Report to the Prefect Team right away! Have them converge all forces on the dining hall!
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Whoops... Looks like we're busted.
- "Quick, let's run!"
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): B-But...what about the food?
> (We left the dining hall with me having to drag Haruna by the hand.)
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Oh... This is bad.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): It wasn't a big explosion, thankfully...but now the secret research lab is gone.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): *sigh*
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...It's okay. I can always find another place.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): The ratio of alcohol played a bigger part than I thought. I'll have to be more careful next time.
> (There was more to it than that, but it's pointless talking to Haruna when she gets like this...)
> (I'll have to visit Gehenna's faculty office later and apologize.)
