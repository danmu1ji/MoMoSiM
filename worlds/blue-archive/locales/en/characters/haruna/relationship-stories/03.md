> Pierre Garnish, Restaurant
- Manager: Welcome to Pierre Garnish. Do you have a reservation?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Yes. Table for Kurodate Haruna.
- Manager: Very well. Right this way.
- Manager: Allow us to take your bag.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Oh, thank you.
> (sneaks)
- Manager: There's something you must know. Our restaurant specializes in full multi-course meals, so we don't accept single customers.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Huh. I didn't know that was your policy. I reserved a table for one, though...
- Manager: It was implemented just yesterday.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...
- Manager: But as you said, you made the reservation before the policy changed, so we'll make an exception.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Wow, thank you! In that case, what should I order...?
- Manager: I recommend the Pierre. It's the best set on our menu.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Oh... Okay. I'll get that.
- Manager: Please wait a moment.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...Hmm.
- Waiter: Here's your bread, served with a foie gras appetizer.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Hmm...
- Waiter: And here is the oyster salad, part of your entree.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Mmm...
- Waiter: Here's your main course, the chateaubriand steak.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...
- Waiter: And for dessert, the fruit sorbet.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...
- Manager: ...Yes. Here is your receipt. Thank you.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...
- Manager: How was your dinner? Did you enjoy each dish?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): The dishes...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...Heh heh heh heh heh heh.
- Manager: ...?
- Manager: We'll bring you a to-go box.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Heh. You don't need to do that.
- Manager: Pardon?
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): *strains*
- Manager: A d-detonator?
> *baaaaaang*
> ...
- "Are you telling me you blew that place up because you didn't like their food?!"
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): It wasn't just because of that, Sensei.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): When you serve food, it's not just about the taste.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Their arrogance when they said they didn't accept reservations for one.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Their audacity when they didn't allow me, the customer, to choose my own meal.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): There was no aperitif—no cocktail before my meal. There wasn't even an amuse-bouche. A meal NEEDS a bite-sized appetizer.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): They didn't even ask how I'd like my steak cooked. How rude!
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Everyone knows chateaubriand has to be cooked medium rare!
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): I expect the service AND food to be luxurious at a gourmet restaurant.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): ...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): But I'll give them one thing. I don't enjoy eating good food alone.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Heh heh.
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): I should go back and take a shower. I smell like smoke...
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): I feel like overcooked yakitori!
- Haruna (Gourmet Research Society): Okay, then...Sensei. I'll be a new me next time we meet.
> (Haruna staggered away, looking a little lonely.)
