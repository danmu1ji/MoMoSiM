# Nodoka

## Description

Nodoka is a student of Red Winter. However, she is currently suspended from school, instead being confined to Class No. 227 in the old school building. The reason for her suspension is "using a telescope to engage in stalking". Nodoka has a desire to observe all of the beautiful things in the world with her own eyes (including Sensei). The long hardship she has endured as part of Class No. 227 has given her a deep-running sense of frugality.

## Appearance

Nodoka is petite, with waist-length blonde hair braided at the end and tied with a small red band, and red eyes.
