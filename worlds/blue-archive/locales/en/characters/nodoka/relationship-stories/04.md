# Nodoka — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Nodoka/Relationship_Story/4
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> Schale Building Entrance, Late Night
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): I've been waiting for you, Sensei!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): I was so excited for today that I couldn't sleep... I almost overslept.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): I'm so glad that I'm able to watch the meteor shower like this.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Come on, let's hurry to the roof before it's too late!
> Roof of the Schale Building, Late Night
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Phew, it looks like it hasn't started yet.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Are you cold, Sensei?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): It's quite chilly late at night, so it's a good idea to bundle up.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Heehee. Would you like a cup of tea?
- "Thank you."
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): For some reason...I feel a bit shy being alone with you like this in the middle of the night.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): We won't need the astronomical telescope I brought with me to observe the meteor shower...
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Wh-What should we talk about to pass the time?
- "Nodoka, look up."
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): ...What?
- Nodoka: ― Wow, it's a shooting star! Oh... Over there! And over there!
- Nodoka: ― The sky is full of shooting stars! Meteors are falling like rain!
- Nodoka: ― I can't believe I'm seeing this with my own two eyes... It's like a dream.
- Nodoka: ― A wish? No... I'm okay for today.
- Nodoka: ― I'm content enjoying this beautiful view with someone important...
- Nodoka: ― If I wish for something more, the stars might grow angry with me...
- Nodoka: ― I'm totally satisfied for now.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): But I wonder how the story of wishing on a shooting star came to be.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): It's a beautiful concept, but it's just a celestial event that has nothing to do with people's wishes...
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): What do you think, Sensei?
> (I didn't answer. I was already falling asleep.)
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Hey, you can't fall asleep here!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Seriously! Sensei!
> I watched the night sky with Nodoka until the meteor shower ended.
