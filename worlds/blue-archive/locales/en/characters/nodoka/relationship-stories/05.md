> Kivotos Subway Station
> Nodoka was walking with a large telescope on her back.
- "Hello, Nodoka."
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Oh! Hello, Sensei. Are you on your way to work?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): The weather is so nice today. If I go up somewhere high on a day like this, I'd be able to see everything!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Red Winter Academy isn't on this route?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Ahaha... Yes, you're right. Actually, I wasn't going to the academy. I was going to a station past the mountains to see the clouds.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): When I woke up in the morning and looked up at the eastern sky, I saw a beautiful cloud hanging on the mountainside.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): I thought the view would be prettier from the top of the mountain, so I was on my way over there with my telescope.
- "Isn't that telescope heavy?"
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): This one?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Of course it's heavy, but I can't fully admire the beautiful scenery without it.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): As long as I can see grand views with the telescope, the weight of it isn't a concern.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Sensei, would you like to go with me to see the clouds?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Oh, are you too busy with work at Schale?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Well, I guess there's nothing I can do about that!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Instead, I'll feast my eyes on all the beautiful sights I can see!
- "Would you like me to carry the telescope for you?"
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Oh, thank you, Sensei.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Now then, on to the station platform!
> I saw Nodoka off at the station platform and returned to Schale.
