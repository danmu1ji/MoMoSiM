> Roof of a Building
> Nodoka was looking through her telescope, preoccupied by something.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): That's weird... Sensei was sitting in the Executive Office earlier...
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Did Sensei go to the restroom?
- "Who are you looking for?"
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Agh! S-Sensei?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Wh-When did you get here? You were in Schale just a minute ago...
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): You came because you were worried after seeing my email?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Ugh, if I knew this would happen, I wouldn't have sent that message...
- "Were you watching me through the telescope?"
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Uh, so...I wasn't watching you through the telescope...
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): But I can't help it. I need my daily dose of Sensei to be motivated!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): This is all because it's so hard to see you, Sensei!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): I can call you directly if I want to see your face?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Th-Thank you...but I'd rather not do that.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Your face shines brighter when I'm spying through a telescope than when I'm looking straight at you!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): I love watching you from afar as you go about your day... *sigh*
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Ah! That was kind of messed up... F-Forget what you just heard!
> After that, I asked the Red Winter Office to keep a closer watch on Nodoka.
> ...And Nodoka's detention length in the Spec Ops was extended.
