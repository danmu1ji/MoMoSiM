> Kivotos Residential District.
> I spotted a 500-yen coin while walking down the street.
- (Pick up the 500-yen coin.)
- ???: Waaait!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): *pant* *pant* ...Sensei, that's my coin!
- "Did you drop this coin?"
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): No, I didn't drop it, but...
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): I found it first!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): From one kilometer away!
- "From a kilometer away?"
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Yeah! If I look through this telescope, I can even see ants crawling from hundreds of kilometers away!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): My morning horoscope was auspicious, so I was looking through my telescope the entire morning to see if something good might happen today...and I got lucky!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Supply distribution is terrible at Spec Ops No. 227, so we don't get enough snacks...
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): But 500 yen will buy me a pile of pastries and drinks from the convenience store!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): So, Sensei, please give me the coin!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Why, Sensei?!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Why, Sensei?!
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): What? It doesn't matter who saw it first? Whoever picked it up first is the rightful owner?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): You're mean! You're stingy! You're unreasonable!
- "Instead, I'll buy you a cake from L'Oiseau Bleu downtown."
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Wh-What? Isn't L'Oiseau Bleu that...fancy sweets store where the cheapest cake is over 1,000 yen?
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): A-Are you sure?
- "Fine with me."
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Th-Thank you.
- Nodoka (Spec Ops No. 227): Seriously...this is one lucky day.
> After all that, although I used my card to buy the most expensive cake...
> Nodoka couldn't even eat half of it because she felt so intimidated by the cost.
