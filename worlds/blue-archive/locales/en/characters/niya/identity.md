# Niya\n\nPresident of Yin-Yang Club at the Hyakkiyako Academy. Outsiders may know Niya as the Student Council President, but she is better known as the Non-Working President as due to the structure of Hyakkiyako Academy she is usually tied in much more complicated matters… or so she claims; the dislike of work is her second nature.
