# Azusa

## Description

The ice witch of Trinity's Supplemental Lessons Club. For various reasons, she had dropped out of school and re-enrolled at Trinity. One to mainly keep to herself, she tends to voluntarily distance herself from others so as to not be a bother. This has led to others in the Supplemental Lessons club worrying for her.
