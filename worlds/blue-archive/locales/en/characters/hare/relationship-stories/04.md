> Millennium School Rooftop
- Hare (Veritas): I've been waiting, Sensei.
- Hare (Veritas): As I said in my message, I finished modifying the drone. So I'm gonna do a test flight on the roof.
- Hare (Veritas): This modified drone not only has enhanced flight capabilities, but also has a voice recognition system and a simple AI logic unit, which allows me to control it with my voice.
- Hare (Veritas): Its name is...Athena-3!
- Hare (Veritas): Named after the goddess of wisdom, this drone is designed based on an empirical algorithm which enables it to perform cognitive tasks at a high level.
- Hare (Veritas): In other words, through repetitive machine learning, this drone can figure out the optimal solution to any task, no matter what command I throw at it.
- Hare (Veritas): Hmm...my bad. I guess I was bombarding you with jargon.
- Hare (Veritas): How about we start off by testing a few simple functions?
- Hare (Veritas): Athena-3, take a picture of Sensei using your camera function.
- Athena-3: Picture mode active. Taking pictures of subject "Sensei."
- Athena-3: Collecting selfies from social media to analyze recent trends... Calculating brightness of area to estimate optimal angle...
- Athena-3: Analysis complete. Please hold still while the picture is taken. 3, 2, 1...
- Hare (Veritas): How's that? Good, right?
- "Looks even better than the one I took at the photo studio..."
- Hare (Veritas): A machine sees better than human eyes can. That's why this picture looks better than one taken by a handheld camera.
- Hare (Veritas): In addition, it comes with a bevy of other features. It's got schedule management and an alarm, plus fitness functionality like workout assistance.
- Hare (Veritas): Athena-3, activate workout assistance now.
- Athena-3: Workout assistance mode active. Measuring the physical activity of subject "Omagari Hare."
- Hare (Veritas): All right then, shall we take a quick jog for testing purposes?
- Athena-3: *beep* The subject's body temperature and heart rate increased by a small amount. Analyzing effectiveness of workout... I recommend an increase in workout intensity to burn more calories.
- Hare (Veritas): *pant* As you can see, it can automatically analyze the physical condition of the user and serve as your own personal trainer.
- Hare (Veritas): Whew...that should be good enough. Athena-3, turn off workout assistance mode.
- Athena-3: ...
- Athena-3: *beep* Cannot comply. Continuing in workout assistance mode.
- Hare (Veritas): Huh? That's odd. The drone's decision-making protocol shouldn't have the capability to reject a human's command... What could've gone wrong?
- Hare (Veritas): Athena-3, initiate emergency stop. Run a system self-diagnosis.
- Athena-3: *beep* Unfortunately, your command cannot be executed. This program is designed to suggest optimal solutions at all times.
- Athena-3: Subject "Omagari Hare" is facing risk of severe body malfunction due to lack of exercise. Proceeding with exercise.
- Athena-3: If the subject does not move within thirty seconds, this program may employ electric shocks for compliance.
- Athena-3: Three...two...one...
- Hare (Veritas): W-Wait... Wait just a second...!
- Athena-3: Sufficient calorie consumption for subject "Omagari Hare" confirmed. Ending workout mode...
- Hare (Veritas): *pant* *pant* It's finally over...
- Hare (Veritas): Athena-3...initiate emergency shutdown. You can rest now...
- Athena-3: Understood. Shutting down.
- Hare (Veritas): *sigh* Today's drone test was a total disaster.
- Hare (Veritas): I didn't expect the logic unit to prioritize carrying out tasks over obeying user commands and shutting down... I guess I underestimated the power of its learning algorithm...
- Hare (Veritas): I better get back to patching her programming from the ground up.
- Hare (Veritas): ...And I should definitely get rid of that unnecessary workout assistance function.
- "Why don't you leave the workout assistance function as is?"
- Hare (Veritas): Not a chance!
- Hare (Veritas): One should only exercise when they feel the need to, not when they're forced to.
- Hare (Veritas): Well, it wasn't wrong to point out that I was in dire need of some exercise...
- Hare (Veritas): Still, humans shouldn't live their lives controlled by machines!
> Hare looked more determined than usual while saying that...
