> Downtown Kivotos
- Hare (Veritas): Thanks for shopping with me today. You helped me snag two new graphics cards!
- Hare (Veritas): It's hard to get my hands on graphics cards these days because people are wasting them on dumb things like mining cryptocurrency.
- Hare (Veritas): They're the reason why honest hackers like me, who use computers as intended, are suffering.
- Hare (Veritas): ...Okay, "honest hackers" might sound strange, but what I mean is that we don't make other people's lives difficult for our profit.
- Hare (Veritas): Well, my business here is done, so let's go back to the academy.
- Hare (Veritas): Looking at how cloudy the sky got, it could rain at any sec...
- Hare (Veritas): ...Me and my big mouth.
- Hare (Veritas): What do we do? I didn't bring an umbrella with me, since there was no rain in the forecast today...
- Hare (Veritas): At this rate, my precious graphics cards I just bought are gonna get wet... Is there any place nearby to avoid the rain?
- (Point at the nearby internet cafe.)
- Hare (Veritas): An internet cafe? I heard about those from Maki. It's where you get a dark, tiny, partitioned room to use a PC, right?
- Hare (Veritas): ...
- Hare (Veritas): We don't have any other options. I don't want to ruin my hard-earned graphics cards before I even get a chance to unbox them.
- Hare (Veritas): And honestly, I've been curious about this internet cafe for quite a while.
- Hare (Veritas): Shall we go in, then…?
- Hare: ― *sigh* That rain shower came out of nowhere.
- Hare: ― It's hard to trust the weather reports these days.
- Hare: ― So, what should we do now?
- Hare: ― (Oh, no... Was that a weird thing to say?)
- Hare: ― (This is my first time at an internet cafe.
- Hare: ― I don't know what you're supposed to do at a place like this.)
- Hare: ― (Besides, this place is so small...
- Hare: ― and Sensei's face is so close...)
- Hare: ― You're gonna go freshen up?
- Hare: ― What do you mean?
- Hare: ― Oh, you're gonna wash the rain from your hair...
- Hare: ― ...No, forget I asked.
- Hare: ― Do internet cafes have showers?
- Hare: ― Hey, Sensei. I was told the drink bar is free.
- Hare: ― I wonder if they have energy drinks.
- Hare: ― Not only is the internet free, but the drinks are free too.
- Hare: ― Amazing! This is the perfect place for someone like me!
- Hare: ― I could stay here forever.
- Hare: ― Oh... I hope I'm not coming off weird.
- Hare: ― Don't get me wrong...
