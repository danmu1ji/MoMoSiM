> Veritas Hacker's Lounge
> I stopped by Veritas' club room on my way back from taking care of Millennium School business at Seminar.
- Hare (Veritas): Hmm...
- Hare (Veritas): Oh! Hey there, Sensei.
- Hare (Veritas): Yeah, I just got back from watching Eliminator 4 at the theater.
- Hare (Veritas): But something about it was...a bit...
- "What's that look for? Was the movie not good?"
- Hare (Veritas): Should I say that the movie wasn't fun? Or perhaps it was more that there was a part I didn't like.
- Hare (Veritas): The movie itself was all right. The special effects were flashy, the characters stood out, and the audience enjoyed it.
- Hare (Veritas): Yeah...it was mostly fine, except...
- Hare (Veritas): The way they depicted hackers in the movie sucked!
- "What did they get wrong?"
- Hare (Veritas): Almost everything! But if I had to point out a few things...I'd start off with the costume design.
- Hare (Veritas): It's true that hackers prefer comfy clothes, but what's with that sloppy hoodie that the hacker in the movie was wearing? They made it look like every hacker is a computer nerd who doesn't know how to dress.
- Hare (Veritas): Also, the hacking scenes in the movie looked so fake.
- Hare (Veritas): Do you think it makes sense that a huge corporation's security, locked down with all kinds of security programs, could all be breached at once by a single hacker? Even the most talented hackers have their limits.
- Hare (Veritas): On top of that, who puts up an ASCII skull after a hack? Talk about corny.
- Hare (Veritas): I find it hard to believe that a franchise as popular as Eliminator would make such rookie mistakes.
- "...Is it really that important?"
- Hare (Veritas): Absolutely! It concerns the very nature of Veritas.
- Hare (Veritas): Popular movies showing hackers in such inaccurate ways is the reason why the public image of white-hat hackers like us is going down the drain.
- Hare (Veritas): We don't do our hacking for grudges or personal gain. All of Veritas seeks only truth and freedom.
- Hare (Veritas): But they portrayed hackers as some third-rate script kiddies, simply because that's easier for them to show in their movie. Unforgivable...
- "You totally lost me..."
- Hare (Veritas): ...It's no good. I better leave a pointed warning for the company that produced Eliminator.
- Hare (Veritas): Oh, I'm not going to contact the production company directly. It's simple, really. I just need to hack into their servers.
- Hare (Veritas): I'll use this opportunity to show them how real-life hackers differ from their fictional ones.
- Hare (Veritas): Let's see, the address of their home page is...
- Hare (Veritas): It's protected by a powerful security program, as expected. But it's got no chance of stopping me.
- Hare (Veritas): ...There we go. What should I edit first?
- Hare (Veritas): I'll start by lowering the movie's review to one star, then lock them out of making any edits...
- Hare (Veritas): Rewrite all the scripts to warn them about misrepresenting hackers...
- Hare (Veritas): And put in an ASCII version of the Veritas logo for good measure... Perfect.
- "..."
- Hare (Veritas): Hm? Sensei, is something wrong?
- Hare (Veritas): ...You think what I'm doing right now is not too far off from what the hackers did in the movie?
- Hare (Veritas): Don't be ridiculous! Unlike the movie, I'm not hacking for personal gain, nor using ASCII art...
- Hare (Veritas): ...Okay, maybe I am.
- Hare (Veritas): Let me make this clear again. Not every hacker does this!
> Afterwards, I convinced Hare to restore the homepage she hacked.
