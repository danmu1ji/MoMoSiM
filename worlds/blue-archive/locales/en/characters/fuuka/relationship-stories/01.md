- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I have a long grocery list to get through! ♪
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Wow. Supermarkets always manage to get me excited.
- "You must really enjoy them."
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I suppose I do...
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): There's something about a cool breeze coming down aisles of fresh food that makes my heart race.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): All the colorful fruits and vegetables on display are so captivating.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): It's like a treasure trove for chefs!

- "I'm not sure about that one."
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Oh! Look, Sensei. This cucumber still has its spines intact.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I can tell this one is good because it's firm and the tip is still moist.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Oh! This watermelon is a winner too! Just listen to the sound it makes when I tap it!
- "You really know your way around produce, Fuuka."
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): S-Sorry. I guess I got a little carried away. Ahaha...
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I give it my all when cooking meals for the academy...
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): ...but it's challenging to keep the innate flavors of the ingredients when cooking such large portions.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): That's why so many students complain about the food having no taste.
- "I know you put a lot of work into it, Fuuka."
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Stop. You're going to embarrass me...
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): M-More importantly! That was the last of the ingredients I need.
- "Time to check out, then?"
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Not at all, Sensei.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): The real shopping has only just begun.
- "Just begun...?"
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Excuse me! I'd like to buy something here.
- Shop Owner: How can I help you, ma'am?
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I appreciate how fresh your bell peppers and cucumbers are. Good size, good quality...
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): These are the kinds of ingredients that I know I can make something delicious with.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): They're so impressive that I keep coming back for more.
- Shop Owner: Oh, so you're a regular. Would you mind if I asked where this is going...?
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): You see, I'd like to start buying in bulk if possible.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I need enough to feed every student at Gehenna and then some.
- Shop Owner: Ah, I see! Did you have a price in mind?
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Since I'm trying to buy this many items at once...
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): How does this price seem to you?
- Shop Owner: Uh-huh. I'll have to consult with my manager about that price...
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I'll tell you what. At this price, I'll also buy a month's worth of food for our dining hall.
- Shop Owner: *gasp*...!
- Shop Owner: V-Very well. We have a deal.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Glad to hear it. Pleasure doing business with you.
- "Fuuka, that was amazing..."
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I'm the president of Gehenna's School Lunch Club. That was nothing!
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Well, let's head back, Sensei!
