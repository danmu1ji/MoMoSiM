# Fuuka — Relationship Story 5

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> (I'm supposed to help Fuuka at Gehenna's dining hall today.)
> (But when I arrived...)
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): The students filed another complaint today!
- "F-Fuuka?"
> (Is this...mirin wine?)
- "Don't tell me you've been drinking this, Fuuka!"
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): It was only a sip! I had to taste test the recipe!
- "But there's alcohol in it..."
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): That's not the problem here, Sensei!
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I pour my soul into making delicious meals for everyone!
- "I-I know, Fuuka. Everyone recognizes that."
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): But cooking large quantities is always harder. The food doesn't turn out as well, and then the students complain.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): And, no. Not everyone recognizes my hard work like you do...
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I know it's unrealistic to expect everyone to. I accept that.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): BUT STILL!
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): They're the ones who mixed up karashi mustard instead of the regular kind just because they're both yellow!
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Where do they get off complaining that my food is too spicy?!
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Try cooking for yourself if you're just going to complain everyday!
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Don't they realize how thankless being the School Lunch Club president is?!
- "I-I know it can be a lot to handle sometimes."
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Wait. I've got an idea.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): ...What if I replaced mustard with karashi in every recipe? That would teach them a lesson about complaining.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): It would give a whole new meaning to student lunches from Gehenna...
- "L-Let's not do anything crazy!"
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I'm only kidding. It would be blasphemous for the School Lunch Club to sabotage food like that.
- "That's better. No one puts more love in their cooking than you!"
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): In the end, School Lunch Club drama isn't something to lose sleep over.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I choose to cherish the things that make me happy—like when someone tells me they like my food. And best of all...
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): ...you came to help me, and being with you is enough to warm my heart.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Heehee. Heeheehee...
> Screen shakes
- "Fuuka?"
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): ...
- "F-Fuuka...?"
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Zzz...
> (The back label of the mirin bottle then caught my eye.)
> ("Contains 0.00003% alcohol.")
> (I promised myself I'd never let Fuuka near alcohol again.)
