> Fuuka reached out to me at the end of a several night stretch of doing Schale work.
> I went straight to the meeting place as soon as I received her text.
> But just as I arrived, the sky felt like it had begun to spin around me.
> Gehenna Academy Infirmary, Room 12
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): S-Sensei? Are you all right?
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): You're awake! Can you hear me?
- "Fuuka? What happened?"
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I found you collapsed on the ground at the meeting place.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): The school nurse told me that you fainted from fatigue.
- "Now I remember. I had been up working late again last night."
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): This...is all my fault.
- "That isn't true."
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): But it is. I should have taken your schedule with Schale into consideration.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Had I not asked you to meet me for dinner, you wouldn't have fainted.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I'm so sorry...
- "It's not your fault, Fuuka."
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): It's not...?
- "I agreed to it because I love your cooking, Fuuka."
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Every time...
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): You always say things like that to me.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I've been jumping to conclusions while I waited for you to wake up.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): I got so caught up in serving you what I wanted you to eat that I failed to consider what you wanted.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): So this time, I want you to choose.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Sensei, is there something you would like to eat?
- "I get to choose...?"
> Schale House
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): All you want is a home-cooked meal? Are you sure you don't want anything fancier?
- "It's not just a home-cooked meal. It's home-cooked meal that you made for me."
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): If that's what you want, Sensei. It's up to you.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): All right, I'll get right on it!
- Fuuka: ― I knew it. You've been eating frozen food again!
- Fuuka: ― They're not good for you. You have to think about your health.
- Fuuka: ― Making something better won't even take long, so just wait a moment.
- Fuuka: ― Seriously, Sensei. Your worst habit is the way you ignore your health.
- Fuuka: ― Huh? You want me to cook for you every day?
- Fuuka: ― Oh, hahaha. I... If you really want me to, I don't mind...
> Schale House
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Let me know if you're ever craving another home-cooked meal.
- Fuuka (School Lunch Club): Feel free to ask as much as you want... I'll always be happy to cook for you.
