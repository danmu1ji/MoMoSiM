# Hibiki — Relationship Story 7

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> There was a hubbub of maintenance and work going on in the Engineering Department.
- "I'm here, Hibiki!"
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Ah, Sensei. Just a second...
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Here. Your newly repaired bike.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): I really considered adding some mods.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): But then I remembered how many times you explicitly insisted that I "only do repairs," so I stopped myself.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Although I really would have liked to add a high-velocity grenade launcher or flamethrower. For safety reasons, of course...
- "I think that creates more safety hazards!"
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): You could at least switch to airless, titanium tires.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): I mean, what will you do if you run into spike strips on the road? Your tires would get completely shredded.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Well, I guess if that's really what you want...I'll let it go. Just this once.
- "Thanks for being so considerate, Hibiki."
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): But...modifications are every engineer's dream. Why are you so afraid of them?
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): You think that sometimes purity is best...? Hm...
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): There's a certain beauty there that I can respect. Respecting something's origins can have meaning, too.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): However! Adding more functions through modifications is also beautiful! The act of maximizing efficiency to its ultimate level...
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): And it's unwieldy to juggle a whole menagerie of tools. Isn't it better to combine multiple different features into one?
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): The more I think about it, the more I think we should spice up your bike.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Heehee. You get it, Sensei!
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): For example... Let me see...
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Ah, yes. Behold these beauties.
- "These are...your goggles, Hibiki?"
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): You could call them that. But now that they've undergone modification after modification...
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): ...they're a priceless masterpiece. The embodiment of versatility and functionality.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): They have so many uses now that my work would suffer if I didn't have them.
- "A meister's proud work, I see."
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Exactly. Naturally, they have the inherent feature of eye protection.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): But not just from welding sparks. They can also withstand the harsh gusts of the desert or wind pressure from a freefall.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): These goggles also protect against blue light, UV rays, and bullets.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Oh, and its zoom feature is really useful for when I'm sewing.
- "Sewing...cosplay costumes?"
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Precisely. All good cosplay begins with sewing, after all.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Starting with fabric cutting to seam treatments, you have to determine and implement the appropriate method on a case-by-case basis.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Even with the newest sewing machines, threading a tight stitch in a small area again and again is a battle of the senses. Crystal clear vision is essential.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): That's why I added a 15-level multifocal lens. At maximum level, you can even view the stitches and fabric on a molecular level.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): I technically haven't had the need for it yet, but it's important to know the capability is there.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Can you guess at some of the other functionalities, Sensei?
- "I'd guess...something combat-related?"
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Heehee. You hit the mark. I've added a survey feature, which includes a GPS-system.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): You'll never need to work with another operator to fire mortars again, as long as you have these goggles.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Infrared navigation? Of course. Night operations are no obstacle.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): On my pride as a meister, I can confidently say I've added every possible feature a student would need.
- "Then, do these goggles also have..."
- "...your favorite feature? A self-destruct?"
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): O-Oh... Uh... Self-destruct...?
- "That's an essential mod for any invention, right?"
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Um... For these goggles...
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): No, I didn't install a self-destruct feature.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): You are correct that it's almost a given to add it to a meister's inventions.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): But...for some strange reason, I didn't feel the same urge with this particular masterpiece.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): ...
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): I can't put my finger on it. Maybe I still haven't fully matured into the mindset of a true meister.
- "I think I know why."
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Why's that?
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): ...
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): You're right, Sensei.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): I've been using these goggles for...a long time now.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): At first, I just liked their aesthetic. But then, I put a lot of work into them, modifying them whenever the situation arose.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): After all this time...
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): You're right. They became really important to me, somewhere along the way.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): These feelings...stopped me from adding a self-destruct feature.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): I didn't want to even consider a situation where they could explode. I guess they're pretty sentimental to me.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Even if I would never use it, I was averse to the hypothetical.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): ...Yeah. I hope so too.
- "Hibiki?"
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): Hm... It's nothing.
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): I was just thinking...
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): You don't need a self-destruct feature either, Sensei.
- "Isn't that a given?!"
- Hibiki (Engineering Department): So please stay with me, for as long as I want.
