# Chiaki — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Chiaki/Relationship_Story/4
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Hello!
- "Hi, Chiaki."
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): How are you feeling today, Sensei?
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Did you sleep well last night? You're not sick or anything, are you?
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Heehee, well...
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): It's because of this new Weekly Pandemonium Society's feature I'm working on!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ...And I really need your help with it, Sensei!
- "Help...eating more desserts?"
- "I don't know if my stomach can handle more desserts right now."
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Ahaha! No, no, not that!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): I was actually thinking about the headline for the next Weekly Pandemonium Society feature article!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): How does this sound: "On-site Reportage! Schale Undercover Mission!"
- "Schale...Undercover...Mission?"
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Heehee, correct!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Schale's [USERNAME] Sensei, the celebrity everyone's talking about!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): An up-close-and-personal report of Sensei's day at Schale!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): What do you think? That title just reaches out and grabs you, right?
- "Umm..."
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): I mean, I'm here as a student rep today, and I DO have a code of journalistic ethics.
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): I wouldn't write this story without your permission, of course!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): No pressure or anything. If it's a no-go, I can always find something else to write about.
- "Well, I don't mind the coverage, but..."
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Really? Sweet, thank you!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ..."But"? Is there a problem?
- "Not exactly a problem, but..."
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Don't worry about that! Who do you think I am?
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): I'm Motomiya Chiaki, the secretary of Gehenna Academy's student council, Pandemonium Society, and "Weekly Pandemonium Society's" reporter-editor-publisher!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): No matter the place, there's always news going on!
- "Um...should I be doing something, then?"
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): No, not at all! Just act normal!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): It's my job to FIND the news!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): And acting normal is how you make the most interesting stuff happen!
- "Well, if you say so..."
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Yup! So I've got your permission for this, right?
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Is it okay if I go around and take a few pictures of Schale?
- "Sure, as much as you like."
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Thanks!
> I left Chiaki to it and got started on some urgent work.
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Ah, I'd recognize this brand of chairs anywhere. You can't slip anything past me, Schale!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Hmm, I need more than that for a news article, though...
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Hup! Maybe there's something interesting under the sofa!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Umm... Nope, just dust...
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Ah, this is a gamepad?! Maybe I can find out what kind of games Sensei likes!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Huh? It's not yours, one of the other student reps left it here? I see...
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Mmm, good news is hard to find sometimes...
- "Told you this wouldn't be exciting..."
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): I-I won't give up, though!
> Moments later...
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Hey, Sensei? I'm sorry to bother you at work...
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): But do you usually just sit there and type all day?
- "I happen to have a lot of urgent deadlines today."
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Ugh, this is turning into my worst article ever.
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): M-Maybe I should find a different topic?
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Ha, my razor-sharp reporter's instincts...
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ...will always find the most amazing exclusives!
> A few hours later...
- "Zzz..."
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ― Ahaha! Yes! Got it! Perfect shot!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ― Sensei's dozing face! It'll be great for next issue's cover!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ― That's a wrap for this special issue of "Weekly Pandemonium Society!"
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ― I, Chiaki, have scored myself yet another exclusive report!
- "By crawling under my desk."
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ― Heehee! Never underestimate a secretary's resolve!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ― Ooh! Maybe while I'm at it, I can find out your secrets too...!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ― Like, why do you have holes in your socks?
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ― What?! Your secret is you lock naughty students up under your desk...?
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ― I-I'm sorry! Please forgive me!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Ugh... Undercover work is dangerous sometimes...
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): I could have been locked up for that...
- "Are you really going to put my sleepy face on the cover?"
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Heehee, well, I WAS going to...
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ...
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): ...but I can use something else!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): As much as I enjoy making "Weekly Pandemonium Society," I want the readers to enjoy it! And that includes you, Sensei!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): So yeah, I'll find a better picture!
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Heehee, actually, maybe you could help me find it?
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): I think I'd have better luck with you around.
- "Yeah, it'll help me stay up too. Let's go see what's happening out there."
- Chiaki (Pandemonium Society): Yay! Thank you!
> Afterwards, Chiaki and I visited various places around Schale, looking for stories.
