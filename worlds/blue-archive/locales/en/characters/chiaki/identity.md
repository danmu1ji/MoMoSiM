# Chiaki\n\nChiaki is a member of Gehenna Academy's "Pandemonium Society" council and its secretary. A girl with a bright and cheerful personality. She publishes the "Pandemonium Society Weekly" newspaper as a hobby. Chiaki constantly writes articles in an effort spread the word about Ibuki's cuteness to as many students as possible.\n\nSource: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Chiaki\nReview status: source extracted; pending editorial review.\n
