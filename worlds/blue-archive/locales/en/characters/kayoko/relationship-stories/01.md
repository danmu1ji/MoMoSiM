> Kivotos Streets
> Kayoko is being questioned by the police in front of a dark alleyway.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): I told you already! I didn't do anything!
- Cop: Don't lie to me! How do you explain such a suspicious outfit...?
- Cop: And who would wander around a high-crime area for no reason?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): It's because...
- Cop: ...Because?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): ...Never mind. I have nothing to say to you.
- Cop: Hmph. I knew it. You are hiding something from us!
- Cop: First, I'll need your student ID to confirm who you are.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): (The Prefect Team won't be happy if they find out I'm from Gehenna...)
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): (I'm in big trouble now.)
- "She's my student. Is there a problem?"
- Cop: Hmm? And who might you be?
- "I'm an advisor with Schale."
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Advisor?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): What are you doing here...?
> I did my best to cover for Kayoko.
- Cop: Hmm... Everything here seems to check out. So you do work for Schale.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): ...Why did you help me?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): I looked like I was in trouble? Well, I guess I was in a tight spot...
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): I'm used to people calling me suspicious, though. It's nothing to get worked up about.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Being interrogated by the police is practically a Tuesday...
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): I guess I don't give off a great first impression. I look pretty intimidating, so that lands me in hot water.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): What, you wanna know what I was actually doing here?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Too bad. It's none of your business.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): ...
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): *sigh* Fine. I was feeding a stray cat. Though it ran away as soon as the cops showed up.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Why didn't I just tell them that?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Because they would have thought I was lying, obviously.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): You can hardly believe it yourself, I bet.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Do I look like the kind of girl who feeds stray cats for fun?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Wait...you don't think it's strange? Oh, uh...thanks.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): I guess I owe you one, even if I don't want to.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): But I'll make good and pay you back. Promise.
> After that, I escorted Kayoko back to the Problem Solver office.
