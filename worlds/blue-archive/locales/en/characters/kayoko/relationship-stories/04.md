> Schale Entryway
> The rain started coming down hard as I was leaving from work.
- "It was sunny just a few minutes ago..."
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Sensei? Something wrong?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): What? You forgot to bring your umbrella?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): I mentioned it in the MomoTalk I sent you this morning, but you still forgot...
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Here. Your umbrella.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Why am I carrying an extra?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Because I work with a lot of clumsy people. These kinds of things always come in handy.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): I swear, I'm surrounded by klutzes...
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Where am I headed?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Well...there's someone else who needs my help on rainy days like these.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): It's not important. Don't worry about it.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): What...? You want to tag along?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): What...? You want to tag along?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Well, it's not like I particularly mind or anything.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): But don't blame me if you end up thinking it's a waste of time...
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Especially with all this rain. It's gonna be muddy.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): *sigh* You're really not going to let this go, huh?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Fine. Do whatever you want.
- Follow Kayoko.
- Kayoko: ― You really didn't need to follow me all this way, Sensei.
- Kayoko: ― I guess it's not here...
- Kayoko: ― I only came here because it was raining.
- Kayoko: ― It'll be fine. Cats can look after themselves.
- Kayoko: ― Not being tied down by someone, not being hurt by others...can be a good thing...
- Kayoko: ― But still, having no one to rely on can feel a bit lonely...
- Kayoko: ― A part of me can't just leave it alone. That's all.
- Kayoko: ― That's all there is to it. Got it, Sensei?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): ...The rain stopped.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Giving the umbrella back already?
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Don't forget flash floods can strike out of nowhere. Hold on to it for now.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): Besides, we see each other often enough. You can always return it later.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): I know I objected to it a while back...but being in debt to someone isn't the worst thing in the world.
- Kayoko (Problem Solver 68): I'll see you tomorrow, Sensei.
