# Yuzu — Relationship Story 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Yuzu/Relationship_Story/3

> Game Development Department Club Room
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): *sigh* I can't keep relying on the other members. This is a big problem.
- "What's bothering you?"
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Oh, Sensei.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): The Game Development Department is developing a new game, but we're short on development funds...
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): The whole team is working part-time to help with the development costs, but I can't even go out to look for a job because I'm too afraid of people...
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): I'm the president of the Game Development Department, so it's really upsetting to me that I can't be of more help.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Is there a good way for me to help the other members?
- "Do you want me to tell you about a part-time job that I know of?"
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): ...Huh? A p-part time job?!
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): No way! I can't even talk to people that well!
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): ...
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): I'm scared of...going out in front of people, but...
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): ...Yes, I can't keep running away.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): If it's a part-time job that you recommend... It should be okay!
> Angel 24, Kivotos Central Branch
> The next day, at the Angel 24 Convenience Store in downtown Kivotos...
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): No way...
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): I did say that I would do a part-time job, but you didn't tell me it was a customer service job! I don't think I can be a convenience store clerk!
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Sensei, I trusted you... I trusted you!
- "Don't worry too much. I'll be right by your side to help you through it."
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): R-Really? Will you stay with me while I work part-time?
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): I think I'll be able to bear it if you help me...
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Ugh, but what if a scary customer comes in?
- Store Manager: Yuzu, can you display the snacks on the empty shelf in front of the cash register?
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): H-Huh? A-Are you talking about the drinks?
- Store Manager: No, the snacks, not the drinks. The drinks are already in the fridge...
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Y-Yes! I-I will display the drinks on the shelf!
- Store Manager: *sigh* Is she gonna be okay?
> Moments later...
- Customer A: Please heat up one fried chicken lunch box for me.
- Customer B: I would like a copy of this month's new manga magazine.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Yes, yes!
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Here's your heated-up manga magazine and hamburger lunch box!
- Customer A: I ordered the fried chicken lunch box...
- Customer B: The manga magazine isn't supposed to be heated...
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): H-Huh?! I-I'm sorry! I-I'll fix that right away!
- Customer C: Hello, I would like to order—
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): (Ugh, there are too many people... I'm scared... I want to run away!)
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): (But Sensei is helping me out, and if I don't want to be a nuisance to the members of the Game Development Department, then I shouldn't run away!)
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): (A little bit longer...just a little bit longer...)
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): J-Just a minute! I-I will help you right away!
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Th-Thank you for your hard work...
- "You worked hard too."
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): N-No, that's not true...
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Ugh...I made mistakes all day...and I probably offended some customers... Just more proof I'm a loner and a mess who can't do part-time jobs...
- "That's not true. You did your best."
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): But...
- Store Manager: You worked hard, Yuzu.
- Store Manager: Man, we had more customers than usual today... You don't know how helpful it was to have a new part-timer come in.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): R-Really?
- Store Manager: I mean, you did make a lot of mistakes, but you immediately apologized and dealt with them.
- Store Manager: The customers seemed satisfied with your sincerity.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): That's a relief... I'm glad I was of some help.
- Store Manager: On that note...there's a big event planned in front of the store next weekend.
- Store Manager: Can you come and work part-time then?
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): ...Eeek! I can't do any more!
