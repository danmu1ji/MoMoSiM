> Game Development Department Club Room
> I heard that the Game Development Department was preparing to release a new game, but when I got to the club room, no one was there.
> I decided to wait patiently until the members returned.
- Open the metal cabinet door to hang your outerwear.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Huh?!
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Y-You scared me, Sensei! Please knock before opening the cabinet!
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): ...Oh, even to me, that sounds weird.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): M-More importantly, why are you here? D-Do you need me for something?
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Oh. You came to see the Game Development Department's new game?
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): I-I see...but the new game we're working on is on Momoi's laptop, so I can't show it to you right now.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Please wait for the other members to return.
- "So why are you in the cabinet anyway, Yuzu?"
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): It's because...
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): No one notices me when I'm in here.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): When I just sit in the club room like normal, people come and talk to me all the time.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): In particular, Yuuka from Seminar always gets angry, gives me scary looks, and tells me to attend the club president meetings... When Yuuka comes up to me like that, I want to run away.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): But no one can find me when I'm in here.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): At least for now, this cabinet is my little sanctuary. Heehee...
- "Isn't that cabinet cramped?"
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): No, it's actually pretty comfy. Kind of a perfect fit for me.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Besides, on a hot day like today, its cold metal walls feel cool to the touch.
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): If I lean against the wall and listen to the sounds coming from outside... Ah, I could just stay here forever.
- "Then is it okay if I go in there with you?"
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Wh-What?!
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): B-But the cabinet only fits one person!
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): O-Of course, there's no official limit to the number of people who can fit inside the cabinet, but...wouldn't it be too small for two people?
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): You think that it'll feel more cozy because the space will be tighter?
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Um, if you say so. It might be okay...
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): W-Wait, Sensei! Don't force yourself in here!
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): Um, you're too close. Uh...
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): This isn't working!
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): This is impossible! It's impossible to fit two people in one cabinet!
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): What? You want me to get out so you can stay in the cabinet by yourself?
- Yuzu (Game Development Department): No! This is my sanctuary!
> After that, I fought over the cabinet with Yuzu until the other members returned...
