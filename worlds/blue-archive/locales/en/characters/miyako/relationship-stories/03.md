> Kousagi Park
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): In popular media, rabbits are portrayed as liking carrots. But they actually aren't very good for them, especially when they're young.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Bun Bun is still less than six months, so I've been feeding her white clovers.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): ...Is that her name?
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Yes! Don't you think it's perfect?
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Bun Bun... Heehee. How cute.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): And she's very smart! Bun Bun even recognizes and greets me when I return from missions.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): I feel guilty for leaving her alone every time I go on a mission though...
- "Maybe we should've gotten two rabbits?"
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): I don't think so. A lot of people say rabbits will die if they're lonely but, in fact, they're very particular and shy. It takes a lot for them to bond with another rabbit.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): The situation could get even worse.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Kind of like how the RABBIT Squad was having trouble getting along before you came, Sensei...
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Ah. Please forget I said that.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Speaking of the RABBIT Squad, they're actually my biggest problem right now.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): We never have enough food...and sometimes Saki and Moe look at Bun Bun like she's their next meal.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Sensei, would you mind keeping an eye on her during my next mission?
> I agreed and spent more time with Miyako and Bun Bun before returning to Schale.
