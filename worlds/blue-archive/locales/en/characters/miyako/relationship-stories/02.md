> Downtown Kivotos
> Miyako looked to be deep in thought in front of a shop window.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): ...
- "Miyako?"
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Hmm...
- "Miyako!"
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Augh! Oh... Hello, Sensei.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Apologies. My head was in the clouds.
- "What were you looking at?"
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Oh, these animals at the pet shop.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): I like to stop by and see them when I'm dispatched in the area.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Look, they added some rabbits recently.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Don't you think rabbits are adorable?
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Those stubby little legs, their floppy ears... I could watch them all day.
- "Why don't you adopt one?"
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): That's...
- Pet Shop Employee: Welcome back, customer!
- Pet Shop Employee: Are you interested in adopting a rabbit?
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Wh-What? Oh, no... I just...
- Pet Shop Employee: Oh... I see. You stop by often to visit them, so I thought you were thinking of taking one home.
- Pet Shop Employee: You always look at them with so much love. They're very friendly and easy to train. Why don't you consider adopting one?
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): W-Well... I would like to... But I don't have the money or circumstances. It would be irresponsible...
- "We'd like the white one in the front."
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Sensei?! What are you doing?
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): A gift? I don't know if I'm ready for a pet...
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): And the RABBIT Squad is operating out of a park right now... I wouldn't be able to give it a proper home.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): S-Sensei...
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): It would almost be like a rescue operation.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): S-Sensei...
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): It is sad to see it living behind a store window.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Thank you, Sensei. Really.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): I'll make sure to take good care of her.
> Miyako looked happy as she returned with her new rabbit in her arms.
