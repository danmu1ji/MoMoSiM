> Schale Lounge
> I was helping Miyako fold the RABBIT Squad's laundry in the Schale Lounge.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Thank you for your help, Sensei.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Now our laundry should be nice and dry.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): If you had not let us use the dryer here, we would be stuck with damp clothes in the middle of the rainy season.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): I cannot imagine how uncomfortable that would be. Or what the cold weather and wet clothes would do to the squad's health.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Thank you, Sensei.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): When we first started camping, our only worry was the weather. Needing to take showers or do laundry never crossed our minds.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): I guess life isn't as straightforward as the operations in our textbooks.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Seems I have a lot to learn before I can become a full-fledged squad leader.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Ah, the rest of our laundry finished drying.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): I'll fold those. Feel free to take a break, Sensei.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Oh...
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): You're quite observant, Sensei. It must have fallen from the laundry basket.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): No, it's fine. I never wore this name tag on my uniform...
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): I always keep it tucked away in my pocket.
- "Why carry a nametag that fell off?"
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): This is my trainee nametag. I received it when I first came to SRT Academy.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): It was meant for our old trainee uniforms. After I was officially assigned to a squad, I never had to use it again.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Holding it brings me back to the old days.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Our unyielding justice even in the harshest conditions. Looking upon my seniors, so bold and brilliant.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Even when I'm feeling down, when I remember those times, I find the strength to pull through.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): That is why I keep this nametag in my pocket, as my personal forget-me-not.
- "That's pretty thoughtful..."
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): Don't be cheeky, Sensei.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): At the time I thought you were just getting in the way... Honestly? I didn't trust you yet.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): I remember the good moments too, Sensei.
- Miyako (RABBIT Squad): So don't worry. How I feel right now won't change...
> Miyako and I had a nice time reminiscing and folding clothes.
