# Subaru

## Description

A student from Arius Branch School, who leads the remaining Arius students. She is gentle, kind to everyone, and easy to get along with. She is also trusted by her juniors. For the sake of her juniors, she is always prepared to pull the trigger without hesitation.

## Appearance

Subaru has long black hair with one side braided; the ends fade to purple. Her purple eyes have dark vertical pupils, and she has a mole on her neck.
