> D.U. Suburban Mountain Pass
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Phew, there's a lot more than I thought there'd be.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): There were so many fatsia sprouts. I need a bigger basket.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): These'll last us at least a week... Actually, maybe even a month!
- "Won't they go bad?"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): We'll portion out some to eat now, and preserve the rest.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): We can pickle them with salt, dehydrate them... They're really versatile.
- "It seems like you really like fatsia sprouts."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Does it? I mean, I think they're okay.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I'd say I like most of the wild vegetables and mushrooms that're around.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): If supplies ever run out, common vegetation can be life-saving.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): But some are poisonous, so you need to be well-versed in the different kinds before harvesting anything.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): It's all a part of survival training!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Also...um... I think we might have wandered too far.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Do you know...where we are?
- "...Are we lost?"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Don't worry, Sensei. I brought a map and compass!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Just a little map reading, and I can find our way back no problem.
- "Map reading?"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): It's the process of decoding a map and where all the depicted features are. That way, I can use the compass to see how to get home.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): It's a basic skill that any SRT student would know.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Let's see. The mountain peak over there is Shiraishi Peak...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Okay, our current coordinates...calculate the angle between the grid north and magnetic north...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Should be here. We're at Charlie-9 on the map.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): The nearest road is north, three kilometers away...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): This way, Sensei! Don't get lost!
> A few hours later...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I feel like...we've walked a lot more than three kilometers.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): My calculations definitely weren't wrong. I don't get it.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): W-W-Wait. Was that Kuroishi Peak, not Shiraishi Peak?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I can't believe I made such a stupid mistake... I'm sorry Sensei.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): It's too dark for me to find a reference point.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): We'll have to camp out until the sun comes up.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Seriously? Are you playing with your phone, Sensei? At a time like this?
- "I found our location with my GPS."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): ...GPS?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): There's a bus stop just five hundred meters east?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): You had our location this whole time? Why didn't you say anything?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Huh?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Huh?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): That's why we walked around for half a day?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): What is wrong with you? Are you stupid?!
> Saki and I finally found the bus stop and got back to Schale.
> ...We ended up deep-frying the fatsia sprouts we'd gathered and ate them as a late-night tempura snack.
