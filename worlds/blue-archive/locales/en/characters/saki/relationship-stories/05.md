> The book with Saki's name on it was on the table.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Sensei, have you found it?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I tried looking around the park, and it wasn't there...
- "Is this the one you're looking for?"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Yes! That's it!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): That's the one!
- "What kind of book is it?"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Well, you might be surprised...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): It's not just any ordinary book.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Here, look!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Incredible, right?
- "...How's it not ordinary again?"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): What the...?! This is a manual for SRT students!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): It has all the basics and the fundamental tactics for living in SRT.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Most people don't get to see it, but you're allowed to, Sensei!
- "It's got lots of sticky notes and writing in the margins..."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Well, I couldn't wait for the semester to start, so I read the whole thing. Ten times so far.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I wanted to make sure I understood the fundamentals.
- "Why's it say, 'Always walk in step with others in campus'?"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Ah. That's SRT etiquette.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): They frown on walking at your own pace. We all try to stay in step there.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I got scolded a lot about that, back before I got used to it.
- "I've seen you walk out of step sometimes, though."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Like back when we were walking on the bridge, right?
- "...Right, I think that was it."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Fufu... The manual talks about that too!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): See, on a bridge, too many people walking in step might trigger the bridge's resonant frequency.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): That could make the bridge collapse, so we change up the pace when we're crossing one.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): SRT's rules and regs cover everything, don't they?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Right? Right? There's a looot of interesting stuff like that in here. You should read it, Sensei!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Eh?!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): (Ah!! I forgot I used that page for my diary!!)
- "'I never trust adults.'"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): W-Well, you CAN'T really, right? Not most adults. It made sense back then!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Anyway, why are you still reading that?!
- "'This one acts nice, but I'm sure they've got some other agenda.'"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Eeek!! Give it back!! Gimme!!
- "'Well, I guess they MIGHT actually be nice.'"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Stop reading that!!
> By the time I came back to my senses, Saki had taken the manual and left.
> She'd written "Idiot!" on my face while I was unconscious. It was hard to wash off.
