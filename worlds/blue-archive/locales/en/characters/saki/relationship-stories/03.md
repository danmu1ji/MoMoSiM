# Saki — Relationship Story 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Saki/Relationship_Story/3

- "You're in a good mood."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Sensei!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Yes, you're right!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I scored a really good find at the flea market this morning.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): It's...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): A 37mm spade mortar!
- "...A what-now?"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Oh, I guess you wouldn't have seen one before.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): It makes sense you haven't. It's really rare and almost all production records are gone.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Some weirdo from the Red Winter Academy made one a long time ago! And look. It doubles as a trenching shovel!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Look, you can hold the gun barrel like a shovel handle! But then, hold it here...and voila! Now it's a mortar!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): It's so innovative! Its multi-functional nature is amazing!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): This single piece of equipment can handle both close and long-range combat!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I don't get why they stopped producing something as cool as this.
- "Maybe it wasn't as useful as it seems?"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Huh? No way.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Trenching shovels and mortars are both essential pieces of equipment! Only snotty higher-ups who've never dug a trench before don't understand that.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): This mortar was probably discarded by one of those of high-ranking officials who didn't understand its value in infantry warfare.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): But I'll prove its true worth.
- "It's super rusty."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): That's expected. The seller said it was collecting dust in storage for decades.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): It'll be a cinch to fix up though. It has a pretty simple structure.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Never give up on equipment that can be fixed with some cleaning, tightening, and greasing!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Okay, Sensei. Let's repair this mortar!
- "I'll try my best."
- Saki: ― ...Ugh, ugh!
- Saki: ― ...Hmph.
- Saki: ― It's so stiff. It won't move at all.
- Saki: ― It needs more grease...
- Saki: ― Sensei, can you hand me the lubricating oil over there?
- Give her the lubricating oil.
- Saki: ― Whoa, it's moving pretty well now!
- Saki: ― Ahh... I love taking off rust.
- Saki: ― It feels so good.
- Saki: ― My hand movements are making you uncomfortable?
- Saki: ― H-Huh?!
- Saki: ― This is the holy process of maintaining a muzzle!
- Saki: ― What are you talking about, you idiot!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): All done!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Look at this shiny barrel and shovel blade. How beautiful.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): You can't deny the classic charm of a classic weapon.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Okay, let's try a test shot!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Aim the barrel toward the target...load the shell...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Fire!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): ...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): What was that? Was it a dud?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Must've been. One more try.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Fire!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Is it just too much to shoot a 37mm high explosive with a howitzer?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): This won't even pierce a helmet, let alone a Tochka...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): That's okay! It's not very powerful, but this mortar still has an advantage since you can dig with it.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): For example, I can easily move this pile of dirt here!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Very...easily...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): This shovel sucks. The thick barrel gets in my way. And it's so heavy!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I'd rather dig a trench with my bare hands than use this!
- "I guess there's a reason no one is using this weapon."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): B-But it's such a rare piece of equipment.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): There has to be some use for it!
> Saki stubbornly carried around the spade mortar for a long time.
> But one day, the mortar was found in a nearby barn, covered in dust.
