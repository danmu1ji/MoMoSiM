# Saki — Relationship Story 1

> Schale Office
> One hot, midday afternoon.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Ugh. It's so hot.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I swear it just gets hotter every day.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Well, at least Schale has AC!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): ...Why is it so hot in here?
- "The AC's broken. They have to fix it."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Seriously? Today of all days?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): The main reason I came to Schale was to cool off.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): ...I guess I'll just suffer.
- "If you're that hot, why don't you take off your helmet?"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): What are you saying, Sensei?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): My helmet protects my head, the most vital organ in the body. I can't just take it off during a mission!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): You need to look up the statistics of soldiers being sniped after taking their helmet off during a mission.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): The likelihood of bullets might be low, but Schale work is still a mission.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): And imagine what other students would say if they saw an SRT agent expose her head because it was a little hot?
- "Probably nothing. Most of them don't wear helmets."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Just because they're lax doesn't mean I should be too.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Anyway! I absolutely will not take my helmet off while on the job! No matter what!
- "No matter what?"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): No matter what!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Do your worst!
> I tried tactic after tactic to get Saki to take off her helmet, so she could cool off.
- "Saki! There's a bug on your helmet!"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Huh?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Weird. I didn't feel it up there. Is it gone?
- "...Did you just try to catch it with your hand?"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Yeah, why?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Did you think I'd throw a fit and cry over an insignificant bug?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I'm the squad's point woman. I stand at the front lines when no one else will. What could a bug do to me?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Forget the bug, weirdo. Next on the to-do list is...
- "A recent study found that handstands are good for your health."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): ...That's cool.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): So, the lifespans of the carabiners and holding pins used in Schale are...
- "Maybe you should try a handstand."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Right now? Here?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): ...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I heard you were a strange adult, but...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Why would you ask a female student wearing a skirt do a handstand?
- "Th-That's not what I meant..."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): None of your excuses, Sensei. I know exactly what you're thinking.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Stop messing around and focus.
> (...I was forced to try my last resort.)
- Sneak up behind Saki and take off her helmet.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): ...Sensei. What is wrong with you?!
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): You took advantage of my blind spot and snuck up on me.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Are you testing my situational awareness?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I'm trying to work, Sensei. Stop bothering me.
- "S-Sorry..."
> I gave up on trying to get Saki to take her helmet off...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): *sigh* We're finally done.
- "...Great work."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): You too, Sensei.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): You look exhausted, though. Is it because of the heat?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): I can't blame you. I'm pooped.
- "Saki, did you just...?"
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Huh?
- "You said you never take your helmet off during missions."
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Well, yeah. It's protocol to wear a helmet during missions.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): But we finished work, so...
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): Are you okay, Sensei?
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): ...You really are a weirdo.
- Saki (RABBIT Squad): ...You really are a weirdo.
> ...Saki gave me one last, mistrustful look even as she returned to camp.
