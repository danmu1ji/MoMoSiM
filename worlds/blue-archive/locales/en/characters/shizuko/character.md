# Shizuko

## Description

A student of Hyakkiyako, Shizuko is the president of the festival management committee and the owner and attraction of the classic-style cafe Momoyo Hall. Usually the clutz while on the job, she's never one to overdo the attraction role. In reality, she's the professional-minded kind to do whatever necessary to see the success of her efforts for the festival and Momoyo Hall. This isn't to say she isn't one to show her true emotions when she lets her guard down; both to her close friends, and to Sensei as well.

## Appearance

Shizuko has long brown hair reaching her legs, tied into two short pigtails, and lavender eyes.
