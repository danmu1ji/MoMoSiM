- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Oh, Sensei!

- "Uh, yeah... I'm here now."

- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Why not order something off of the menu?
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): I recommend the strawberry anmitsu.

- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Okay! Coming right up!
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Okay! Coming right up!

> (Shizuko still manages to shine amidst the crowd.)
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Here's the strawberry red bean bun set you ordered!
- Cuteness-Obsessed Female Student: How do you recommend eating it, Shizuko?
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Oh! I know a special trick!
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): If you take a sip of tea along with a bite of the red bean bun...
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): ...the tea's bitterness and red bean's sweetness make a fantastic combination! Heehee.
- Cuteness-Obsessed Female Student: Wh-Whoa!
- Cuteness-Obsessed Female Student: Shizuko is so cute!

- Momoyodou Regular: Huh? This isn't what I ordered.

- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Huh?
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Oh...! I-I'm so sorry, Master!
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): I must have made a mistake.
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): I'll have it out in a minute!
- Momoyodou Regular: Heh heh.
- Momoyodou Regular: It happened! One of Shizuko's famous scatterbrain moments!
- Momoyodou Regular: That look of concern on your face, your cheeks flushed red with embarrassment...
- Momoyodou Regular: You're so adorable.
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): S-Stop. Don't tease me...
> (Shizuko's little mistakes seem to be a real crowd pleaser.)
> (The guests must be here for Shizuko and not the cakes.)
- "Business is certainly booming."

- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Thank you for your patience, Master!

- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): ...!
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): That's it?
- Nod.
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Okay, th-then I'll be right back with the next part of your order!
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Oh, Master! P-Please get out of the way! C-Coming through!
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Th-That was fast!
- "Are you okay, Shizuko?"

- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): I-I'll bring you your next order...
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Ah! Stupid Shizuko! Stop making mistakes!
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Sensei—I mean, Master—I accidentally put hot sauce in the drink I made for you!

- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): D-Do you still want it, Sensei? (peek)
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Ah! Shizuko, you scatterbrain!

- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Gah. I can't take this anymore!

- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): What do you think you're doing, Sensei?!
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): What do you mean? I'm just...
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): What do you mean? I'm just...

- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Exactly! The customers eat it up.
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): That's my act and they love it.
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): What kind of response is that?!
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Don't you have anything else to say?!
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): Anyway! The customers call me the idol of Momoyodou!
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): They find my mistakes charming, and I love seeing them smile.
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): It's a win-win business model!

- "I like every side of you."
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): You mean that...?
- Shizuko (Festival Operations Department): I don't know what to say.
