# Saya

## Description

A genius inventor from Shanhaijng. Having already acquired 6 degrees by the time she was in Elementary school, the nicknamed genius is known for bullying and causing trouble for a lot of people. Saya brings along with her a rat assistant that she's been with since childhood, they're so close that she considers it to be like family.

## Appearance

Saya has hip-length light-gray hair with twin tails at the sides and a loose back section, red eyes, and two mouse-like ears atop her head.
