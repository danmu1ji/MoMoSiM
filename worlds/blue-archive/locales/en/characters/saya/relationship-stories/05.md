> Saya's Research Lab
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): There's been something on my mind. I've begun to feel strange about us spending so much time together...
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): If I had to explain it... My heart starts pounding and I start blushing whenever I look at you. I feel like I'm no longer myself.
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): I won't be able to focus on my research at this rate. I asked my assistants for advice, and they told me that I'm in love!
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): Love... Loath as I am to admit it, I don't know anything about love. So...
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): So this is...
- "This is...?"
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): It's an elixir that makes someone fall in love with you! A love potion!
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): Hahaha! Every brilliant scientist knows that bold claims need to be backed up with data.
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): To verify what I'm experiencing truly is love, I must first observe the behavior of someone I KNOW is in love.
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): That's where my love potion comes in. One whiff of this and you'll fall head over heels for the first person you lay eyes on!
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): In other words, if their behavior becomes the same as mine right now, that would be proof that I'm in love! I'm a genius. Hahaha!
- "So who's going to take the elixir?"
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): You, of course!
- Run away.
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): Wait, don't run away! I swear this elixir is safe! You won't turn into gold or start growing fur or anything like that.
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): I'm begging you. Please help me with my experiment!
- Point at Nezusuke.
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): Nezusuke? Sorry, but the potion has no effect on animals. It has to be you, Sensei!
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): Now...let's see what happens!
- Try to escape.
- Saya (Eastern Alchemy Society): W-Wait! Don't shake the bottle like that!
