# Shimiko\n\nShimiko is the librarian of Trinity Integrated Academy's Library Committee. An absolute bookworm, she is one of the few students who has read the huge book collection in Trinity Library. She also loves to recommend books to others as much as she loves reading them, such that the first thing that comes to her mind when meeting someone new is "What book would suit this person's taste?"\n\nSource: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Shimiko\nReview status: source extracted; pending editorial review.\n
