# Shimiko — Relationship Story 2

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Shimiko/Relationship_Story/2
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


- Shimiko (Library Committee): And this book goes over here... Heehee. I'm almost done categorizing.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Oh. Hi, Sensei! What are you doing here at this hour?
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Oh! Wasn't I raising my voice? Don't worry—we're past closing time.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): You and I are the only ones here.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): That's right, I spend a lot of time here after hours. Someone has to organize the books, after all.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): And besides...
- Shimiko (Library Committee): I spend so much of my day helping others that I don't get any time to read for myself.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Now is usually when I get in some quality reading time!
- Shimiko (Library Committee): So I'm practically at the library 24 hours a day.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Anyway... That text I sent you wasn't supposed to be serious. I'm surprised you came here so late.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Do you still have work to do?
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Ahaha...
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Ahaha...
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Well, suit yourself. Wait here for a little bit. I'll set up a study space for you.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Oh, don't worry. I'm a pro when it comes to pulling all-nighters.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): I'll even put on a pot of coffee for you. I hope you don't mind drinking it with a straw and lid, though.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): I'll put this cushion in the back of the seat and place this pillow right here...
> Shimiko spent some time arranging the chair and desk, making them look cozier than ever.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Ta-da! What do you think? I set it up just the way I like it!
- Shimiko (Library Committee): You can stay up all night without feeling the least bit tired in this arrangement!
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Sensei... There you go saying embarrassing things like that again!
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Sensei... There you go saying embarrassing things like that again!
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Come and sit here. I'll take the seat next to you, so just let me know if you need anything!
> Shimiko and I sat side by side, focusing on the papers in front of us until the wee hours of the night.
> The library is even quieter at night. All I can hear is the sound of pages turning...
> ...along with Shimiko's breath and the sound of her lightly touching her hair.
> I lost track of the hours as we each worked late into the night.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): ...
> (When I looked up, I saw Shimiko staring at me.)
- "...? Shimiko?"
- Shimiko (Library Committee): ...Huh? Oh, I'm sorry. I didn't mean to stare. Was I bothering you?
- Shimiko (Library Committee): I-I just wanted to make sure you're comfortable and see if you needed anything...
- Shimiko (Library Committee): It also dawned on me that I invited you here and all we've done is read and work.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): I know we've both been so immersed in what we're reading.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Just knowing that we're in the same space together feels so...
- Shimiko (Library Committee): It's a pleasant feeling, but...
- Shimiko (Library Committee): ...conversely, I can't help but feel a little selfish.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): I'm enjoying myself, but I never asked you whether this is how you like to spend an evening.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): It feels as though...I was being inconsiderate by forcing you into something I like.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Was that...childish of me...?
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Perhaps I've been immature this entire time without realizing it.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Or maybe it's my imagination. I dunno. Ahaha...
- Shimiko (Library Committee): ...!
- Shimiko (Library Committee): ...!
- Shimiko (Library Committee): Ugh. You didn't even deny that I was acting like a child. Now I'm all embarrassed.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): ...Heehee.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): I can accept that. There's no point in denying me being childish sometimes.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): As long as you promise to keep it a secret, okay?
- Shimiko (Library Committee): And I won't tell anyone you were getting your work done here instead of your office.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): ...Besides, this time of night is perfect for keeping secrets.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): So thank you for keeping me company, Sensei.
- Shimiko (Library Committee): If it's all right with you...
- Shimiko (Library Committee): ...
- Shimiko (Library Committee): ...I hope you'll indulge my childishness more often.
> I sat and read with Shimiko late into the night, even after I had finished my work.
