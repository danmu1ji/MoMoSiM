> Not long after getting Satsuki's message...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Heehee, so this is Sensei's office... Like Makoto said, it's more plain than you'd think.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Oh, you weren't busy, were you?
- "I'm always busy. Anyway, what brings—"
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Well, it doesn't matter.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): What I'm about to tell you is top priority, no matter what you're up to.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Hmph, I think you know, Sensei.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): You don't have to pretend. We're both professionals, after all.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Now, per the Pandemonium Society's grand plan...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Stage one of Makoto's Kivotos Media Takeover is finally underway.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): We want your cooperation, as the Pandemonium Society's partner.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Oh, no, no. The Pandemonium Society Intelligence Committee's information CANNOT have been wrong about this.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Are you telling me you DIDN'T lick Makoto's shoes and promise unconditional cooperation for a thousand years, in front of everyone, in Gehenna Plaza?
- "No!! Where'd that kind of baseless rumor even come from?!"
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Hmph, so our intel was flawed. I made an executive decision to skip the verification process, because you know how much of a pain THAT is, and, well, now it seems we have a problem.
- "That was a bold decision. Are you regretting it yet?"
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): ...Hmm. I suppose I should properly introduce myself.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): I am Kyougoku Satsuki, heir to the Gehenna Student Council Intelligence Committee.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): My organization inherited all the expertise of the previous Gehenna Student Council Intelligence Committee, which you're no doubt familiar with from history.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Not that I OFFICIALLY hold the original title, but I think of that as a small formality.
- "Or so you decided yourself."
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Well, enough pleasantries. Let's get to the point.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): If you're not willing to cooperate...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Then I have no choice but to inform you of the leverage our committee has obtained on you.
- "What leverage?"
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Your "weakness," Sensei.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Heehee. How about it? Are you prepared to hear all about it?
- "..."
- "(I have so many weaknesses, I can't even guess what she's going to say...)"
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): First.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Sensei, we happen to know that you...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): You have a hobby of running through the fields of Kivotos completely naked. Yes?
- "Okay, that DID happen, but it's not a hobby or anything!!"
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): You admit it so willingly. Hmm.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Clearly, you feel no shame about it. As we'd expect of you, Sensei.
- "It WAS embarrassing, but—"
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Hold on. We're not finished yet.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): I know dozens of your weak points in all.
- "That many? Just what kind of life have I been living?"
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): If you're uncomfortable, we can stop at any time. Simply pledge your cooperation with the Pandemonium Society for the next thousand years.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Now, your second weakness...
- *gulp*
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): As I understand it, Sensei, you...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): ...help raise funds every month for students who can't afford meals. Heehee.
- "..."
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Oh, no. Not at all.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Kivotos is a wild land. Survival of the fittest is the only law.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): To show sympathy or mercy in a world this cruel is to leave yourself entirely vulnerable, is it not?
- "I'm a teacher. I look out for my students."
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Heehee, so even that revelation doesn't faze you, hmm?
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Clever, Sensei. If you admit to the shameful truth, it's no longer leverage.
- "It's not 'shameful' in the first place..."
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Oh, but we're just getting started.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Your third weakness, Sensei...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): When cooking instant ramen noodles, you shake the pack to get all the crumbs out!
- "..."
- "Isn't that normal...?"
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Heehee, well, a "normal" person could get away with that, but you?
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Schale's Sensei, the noble paragon, scrounging like a commoner?
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): What would the students say, if they knew? How quickly would they turn from you in disappointment?
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): But the Pandemonium Society...oh, we're different. We're willing to embrace even the—
- "No, seriously, that's normal. It's fine."
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): I don't think you fully grasp the situation. Need I spell it out?
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): This is blackmail, Sensei. I'm blackmailing you.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): I have the power to expose every one of your enormous weaknesses to the entire world.
- "I'm not sure if that's going to be a problem."
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): So even now, you stand tall, do you? Heehee...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Well, you're stronger than I thought. I'll say that.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): I guess this isn't going to work.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): But this isn't over. Far from it.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): I'll do whatever it takes to make SURE you cooperate.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Until next time, then.
- "O-Okay... Bye..."
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): And I assure you, next time, I won't be so nice to you.
- "At least one of us has a big misunderstanding here."
