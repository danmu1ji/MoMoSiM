> As soon as I got Satsuki's message, I hurried to the Pandemonium Society.
- Pandemonium Society Member (Pandemonium Society): Ah, Schale's Sensei. What brings you here today?
- Pandemonium Society Member (Pandemonium Society): Ah, Satsuki is...
- Pandemonium Society Member (Pandemonium Society): W-Well...
- "Did something happen?"
- Pandemonium Society Member (Pandemonium Society): Well, yes, you might say that...
- "So you're telling me..."
- "...she doesn't like vaccination shots, so she's hiding behind the door?"
- Pandemonium Society Member (Pandemonium Society): Well, you never know who might be afraid of needles, I suppose...
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): Satsuki? Satsuki!
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): You're the only one left!
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): I'll make it quick and painless, so please come out!
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Don't lie to me! In what world is there a "painless" shot? I'm fine! Just go away!
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): All Gehenna students are required to take this shot. I know you know that.
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): You want to end up bedridden with a miserable cold?
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): That won't happen! I've never gotten a cold in my life!
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): That's because you're...
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): A-Anyways! That's not the issue here!
- Pandemonium Society Member (Pandemonium Society): So as you can see...yeah.
- "She's got it rough..."
- "Uh, hi. You want me to try talking to her?"
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): Oh, Sensei.
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): Please go ahead. She won't listen to me at all...
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): I'll leave it to you, then.
- "Satsuki? It's me."
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): ...Sensei? Is it really you?
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): ...No. There's no reason for Sensei to be here.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Heehee, you do a great impression of Sensei, but you can't fool me. I'm the Pandemonium Society's Intelligence Committee Director, master of psychology...
- "It's really me, Satsuki. Could you please open the door?"
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): ...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): ...Sensei, are you really there?
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): ...It is you. So then why are you here...?
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Ah, you saw my MomoTalk?
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Heehee, I suppose you saw right through my "emergency rescue request."
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Well, you see the problem. An unprecedented crisis, same as always...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Now get me out of here.
- "Just because you don't want the shot?"
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): I really, really, REALLY don't want the shot!
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): I don't like getting hurt! Especially shots, they're the worst!
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): That sharp little needle, piercing through my poor, delicate skin like a spear...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Then they press the syringe, and some mystery drug slips into my veins, into ME...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Sensei...it's not that simple.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Do you think you could endure it, Sensei?
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Not just the physical pain, but the terror of seeing that needle get closer and closer...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): And then that cold alcohol swab, rubbing against your skin, marking you for death!
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Tch, even so...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): ...What's with that face?
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Anyway, I just don't want to! As a student, I exercise my right to decline.
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): That's not a thing!
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Oh, can't you just check my name off the list and not worry about it?
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): That would be medical fraud, so no!
- "Um, by any chance, do you happen to have any extra doses?"
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): Hmm, yes. I do have a few...
- "Satsuki, how about if you get it with me?"
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Huh...?
- "Would that help you get through it?"
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Well...aren't you afraid of shots too, Sensei? It hurts a lot, you know!
- Medical Emergency Club Member (Medical Emergency Club): You know, if Sensei were going that far for me, I'd just get the shot.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Will you shut up?! I'm thinking...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): ...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): ...Together. At the same time.
- "Yes. At the same time."
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): ...Tch...
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Fine.
- Satsuki (Pandemonium Society): Give me the shot.
- "Now you're talking!"
> She trembled through the whole ordeal, but we both got through it in the end.
