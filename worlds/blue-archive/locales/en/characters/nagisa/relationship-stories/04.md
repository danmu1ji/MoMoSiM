> After taking care of business on the outskirts of campus, I headed to a nearby shopping district to get a late lunch.
> Trinity District Outskirts Shopping Complex
- "One chocosora bread, please."
- Baker: Oh dear, this is unfortunate...
- Baker: We had such a big lunch rush today that we sold out of all the bread we have baked.
- Baker: I'm afraid we don't have any left. I'm very sorry...
- "Are there any other bakeries around here?"
- Baker: I'm afraid there aren't. We're the only store in this neighborhood that sells bread... Hence why we frequently sell out at mealtimes.
- Baker: I'm very sorry. Can I interest you in any other baked goods...?
- "I was really in the mood for bread..."
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Oh, Sensei! Hello!
- Nagisa (Tea Party): What a surprise to see you all the way out here!
- Nagisa (Tea Party): What brings you to this remote shopping complex this afternoon?
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Oh, dear. You were visiting a bakery for lunch, but they were sold out?
- "Yeah. And I really wanted some bread, too..."
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Heehee... Luckily, that's a simple problem to solve!
- Nagisa (Tea Party): If there's no bread, you can eat cake.
- "That sounds a bit...despotic."
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Heehee, don't misunderstand me. I meant it literally!
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Cake is a baked mixture of flour, liquid, leavener, and other ingredients, so in form and texture it's a fairly good substitute for bread.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): And it can be quite filling. I've often found that after a tea party that serves cake, I have no need for another meal.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): And it just so happens that there's a made-to-order sweet shop in the neighborhood that's quite underrated. I've just picked up a whole cake from them.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): And, even more coincidentally... The tea party I scheduled for this afternoon was canceled due to unforeseen circumstances, so I was left wondering who I could share this huge cake with...
- Nagisa (Tea Party): If you'd like, please split this cake with me.
> Nagisa and I had a pleasant tea party and enjoyed the cake together.
