- Nagisa (Tea Party): Good afternoon, Sensei.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): I'm happy to say the new tea leaves we've been anticipating have arrived.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): I was introduced to this brand by students who attended teatime a short while ago. They'd brought leaves that they said were leftovers from brewing kombucha. I tried them, and the taste was quite good!
- Nagisa (Tea Party): My usual stock of tea leaves ran out, so I ordered black tea from that brand as a change of pace.
- "I can't wait to try it!"
- Nagisa (Tea Party): The crisp flavor of bergamot should make it a bit more refreshing than a traditional black tea.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): I wasn't sure what the proper pairing would be, so I've prepared a variety of light refreshments.
- "...That's quite a large variety."
- Nagisa (Tea Party): This teatime is meant to be an apology for my previous behavior. It wouldn't be proper to skimp on cakes and desserts.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Please help yourself, and enjoy them.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): In the meantime, I'll brew the tea.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Oh, the design of this box is slightly different than the one I saw before.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Wait a minute. Is this...?
- Nagisa (Tea Party): (This is dried kelp, not tea leaves!)
- Nagisa (Tea Party): (Why!? Who puts kelp in a tea box?)
- Nagisa (Tea Party): (Could the company have misunderstood my order this badly?!)
- Nagisa (Tea Party): (Even for a busy store, this kind of mistake is unforgivable.)
- Nagisa (Tea Party): (But... I have no tea leaves left. And of course it'd be outrageously rude to send Sensei away without a single cup of tea...)
- Nagisa (Tea Party): (So I guess I have no choice...but to serve Sensei...this kelp tea...)
- "Is something wrong?"
- Nagisa (Tea Party): O-Oh, no! Absolutely nothing at all.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): As this type of tea is new to me, I had to research the perfect temperature to brew it at.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): T-Thank you for waiting. Please enjoy this royal blend, steeped just for you.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): This tea should be sipped slowly and steadily.
- Nagisa: — Heehee...the subtle scent of bergamot
- Nagisa: ― is so refreshing.
- Nagisa: ― (This is definitely kelp...
- Nagisa: — The smell is unmistakable...)
- Nagisa: — And its unique, crisp aftertaste
- Nagisa: — goes well with snacks...
- Nagisa: — (The kelp is too strong...
- Nagisa: — and tastes are colliding in my mouth...)
- Nagisa: ― Sensei, is it your first time
- Nagisa: — tasting something like this?
- Nagisa: ― I-I see...
- Nagisa: — This kind of tea isn't easy to come by. Hee...heehee...
- Nagisa: ― (You haven't discovered my little deception, have you?
- Nagisa: — The smell may be...significantly off, but at least the color is right...)
- Nagisa: ― (If I knew this would happen,
- Nagisa: — I would've just ordered my normal black tea.)
> Nagisa and I enjoyed tea and refreshments for a while.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Whew...it really wasn't as bad as I feared.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): It wasn't correctly paired with the snacks, but at least it was better than offering plain water...
- Nagisa (Tea Party): What did you think, Sensei? Was it to your liking?
- "Yup, it was good!"
- "That was some very tasty kelp."
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Yes, that was certainly the taste of a perfect kelp infusion—
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Wait! Wait a moment, Sensei! You knew it wasn't black tea? When did you realize?
- "From the start...?"
- Nagisa (Tea Party): ...
- Nagisa (Tea Party): The purpose of this tea party was to make amends for my previous poor conduct, but instead you just witnessed another embarrassing moment.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): There's nothing else for it...
- Nagisa (Tea Party): I have no choice but to forcibly erase your memory.
