> To make it to my meeting with Nagisa in the Tea Party Room, I had to walk through what felt like endless hot, sun-drenched corridors.
> Trinity Tea Party Room
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Welcome, Sensei. Thank you for taking the time to meet with me.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): You're very punctual. I just finished preparing our afternoon tea.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): ...That is...quite a bit of sweat, though.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Did you run all the way here?
- "I didn't want to be late!"
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Still, there's no need to overdo it.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): And I find myself with a conundrum, as well. The only tea I have ready is hot black tea, which certainly will not help you cool down.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Please give me one moment. I will call a student to fetch some iced tea.
- "It's all right. I have a drink with me."
- Take out a bottle of black tea.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): What...kind of drink is that?
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Oh, it's tea? Black tea? The kind that's sold already brewed, I see...
- Nagisa (Tea Party): That's interesting. I've never sampled this brand before.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): ...Sensei, if you can spare any, could I try some of your tea?
- Nagisa (Tea Party): You see, I take pride in knowing every single black tea brand in Kivotos.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Every one, from popular flavored black tea brands like Lepton, to high-end brands that are difficult to get in Trinity, like Brinings of Kivotos.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): And I thought I'd tried every one, but that brand is new to me. So it seems I still have more to learn.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): So, in the interest of continuing my studies, would you share some of your tea?
- Pour some tea into Nagisa's teacup.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Thank you, Sensei.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): And now, I'll take a sip.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Hm...
- Nagisa (Tea Party): (...It's so sweet!)
- Nagisa (Tea Party): (What is this? It's so sweet it makes my teeth hurt! I nearly spit it out!)
- Nagisa (Tea Party): (Of course, there are many sweet black teas. I quite enjoy Russian tea... But this one is so sweet it's like drinking sugar water.)
- Nagisa (Tea Party): (And the artificial citrus is so overpowering that you can't even taste the tea!)
- Nagisa (Tea Party): (I really, truly wish I had a proper cup of tea to wash this down...)
- Nagisa (Tea Party): (But that would be unforgivably rude, after Sensei shared their tea with me. I've just got to keep drinking...)
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Hah...haha...it's got a pretty...pretty impressive taste.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): The flavor of black tea can be divisive, so it's been minimized and an emphasis was put on sweetness instead. This certainly is a new, modern taste...
- Nagisa (Tea Party): It's clearly been designed so even students from Millennium and Gehenna who don't usually like tea can enjoy it. Hee...hee...
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Yes...it certainly was a unique experience.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Yes...it certainly was a unique experience.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): I didn't think I'd be treated to such an, um, such an interesting black tea today!
- Nagisa (Tea Party): I'm quite glad you agreed to meet with me. Haha...hahaha...
- "I knew you'd say that, so I brought another kind!"
- Nagisa (Tea Party): What? Another bottled black tea?
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Oh, and it's...quite a large bottle, I see.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Hm, don't you think drinking more bottled black tea than this could be...
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Well, when you put it that way...
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Well, when you put it that way...
- Nagisa (Tea Party): I suppose... Pour me another cup, if you please.
> Nagisa drank almost five cups of bottled black tea before fleeing, claiming a stomachache.
