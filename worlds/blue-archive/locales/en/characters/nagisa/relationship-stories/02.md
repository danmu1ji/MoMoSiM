- Nagisa (Tea Party): Using abbreviations, for one.

- Nagisa (Tea Party): Yes. I'm sure you know that messengers like MomoTalk have been increasingly popular these days. Students have been shortening words or, in some especially outrageous cases, entire sentences, just to make them easier to type.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Things like "HBD" for "Happy Birthday" and "TY" for "Thank You".

- Nagisa (Tea Party): No matter how well-mannered you may otherwise be, overuse of slang makes you sound flippant and unserious. It's a foolproof way to lose the confidence of your peers.
- Nagisa (Tea Party): Students at Trinity General School have the respect and even the envy of all the other academies of Kivotos. In order to be worthy of that, one must speak with the utmost class and dignity.

- Nagisa (Tea Party): Absolutely not. Perish the thought! If the host of the Tea Party, who is meant to be setting an example for all other students, was seen using uncouth language, how would we look to the other academies?
