## MomoTalk 2

- Nagisa: Long time no see, Sensei.
- Nagisa: I hope this message finds you well.
- Sensei: It's been a while, Nagisa.
- Nagisa: I'm contacting you today for a reason.
- Sensei: I would like to discuss a matter with you regarding the Trinity students.
- Nagisa: Nothing too serious, though. Please don't be overly concerned. In fact, perhaps we could enjoy tea and a meal as we speak?
- Sensei: Yeah, sure.
- Nagisa: Very well. I'll select a suitable black tea.
- Nagisa: It's very hot out today. Please be cautious and stay out of the sun.
- Nagisa: I hope you'll forgive my abrupt departure, Sensei.
- Nagisa: I'm afraid I felt my health suddenly fail me.
- Sensei: Don't worry about it.
- Sensei reply option: I'm sorry. Perhaps it was that black tea I selected?
- Sensei reply option: I'm really sorry about that...
- Nagisa: Don't be. It was a unique experience.
- Nagisa: But next time I will be more cautious about the amount.

## MomoTalk 3

- Nagisa: Hello again, Sensei.
- Nagisa: I hope this message finds you well.
- Sensei: How are you feeling?
- Nagisa: Oh, are you still concerned about last time?
- Sensei: Please don't be. It wasn't too bad.
- Nagisa: In other matters, might I impose upon you to stop by the Tea Party room when you have a spare moment?
- Sensei: I was unable to tell you the conclusion of the story last time. I'd like to continue it.
- Nagisa: I have a particularly lovely blend of black tea I would like to brew for you this time.
- Sensei: Sounds good. I'll bring my favorite black tea, too!
- Nagisa: Of course. As long as it's not in a plastic bottle.

## MomoTalk 4

- Nagisa: I'll be waiting for you in the usual spot.

## MomoTalk 5

- Nagisa: Good morning, Sensei.
- Nagisa: Did you eat breakfast before you went to work?
- Sensei: Yeah. How about you, Nagisa?
- Nagisa: Yes. I had miso soup a while ago.
- Sensei: ...I didn't expect that.
- Sensei: I thought Tea Party students ate pastries three meals a day.
- Nagisa: That's simply not possible.
- Nagisa: We may enjoy sweets at our gatherings, but it wouldn't be healthy to eat that and nothing else.
- Sensei: I certainly don't eat only cakes and cookies.
