# Kirino — Relationship Story 4

> Police School Entrance
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Hello, children! Welcome to Valkyrie Police School!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I'm Kirino from the Public Safety Bureau, and I'll be your guide today.
- Kindergarten Student A: How come you're not a Security or Investigation Bureau student?
- Kindergarten Student B: I wanted to hear a bunch of stories about arresting scary criminals...
- Kindergarten Student C: You dummy. The Security and Investigation Bureau students are busy. Of course they'd send someone from Public Safety. They have nothing better to do.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Th-That isn't true! Sure, we're not as busy as Security, but the Public Safety Bureau still has plenty on its plate!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): We find the owners of lost items and conduct regular patrols to find dangerous facilities in the city center!
- Kindergarten Student A: What the heck? That's so boring.
- Kindergarten Student B: Who cares about your busywork?
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): B-But... Well, the thing is...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): R-Right! Everyone, follow me!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I will guide you through the buildings and explain the history of Valkyrie Police School!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): This is the dorm where the cadets reside!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Most Valkyrie students reside in the school dorms in order to quickly respond to every incident.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): At the entrance, you'll find a cornerstone where the first president of the school's maxims are engraved. Students entering the building are imparted with their responsibilities as officers and given an opportunity for self-reflection.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): "It's the police's duty to think of the civilians' safety above all and treat your fellow students with a servant's heart."
- Kindergarten Student A: Boo...! Boring!
- Kindergarten Student B: What's so important about this boring lecture?
- Kindergarten Student C: We want to know how to look cool when you take down bad guys!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): B-But understanding the responsibilities of the police is very important.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Officers that have no regard for the public's safety are no better than the criminals they're stopping!
- Kindergarten Student A: Yeah, okay. Now tell us stories about the scary criminals!
- Kindergarten Student B: Have you ever shot your gun at a crime scene?
- Kindergarten Student C: Let me guess. You're not even allowed to shoot your gun because you're with the Public Safety Bureau, huh?
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Th-That isn't true at all! Shooting is a basic skill that all Valkyrie students must have regardless of department!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): And it just so happens that I recently resolved a hostage situation with my gun! Hahaha...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): (D-Don't look at me like that! The children might suspect something!)
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): (T-Try not to sound so insincere! The children might suspect something)
- Kindergarten Student A: You fired your gun during a hostage situation? Weren't you afraid of the criminal?
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Well, of course I was scared, but they had a hostage...
- Kindergarten Student B: I always thought the Public Safety Bureau only did boring stuff, but that's so cool!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): (Technically, that wasn't a lie, but I still feel like I'm being dishonest with these innocent kids.)
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): (At least I got their attention now. The field trip should go much smoother now.)
- Kindergarten Student A: So what's that bottle hanging from your waist? Is it another weapon?
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Oh, this? It's a smoke bomb. It's not as powerful as a grenade, but it comes in handy during hostage extractions because it obscures the culprit's vision.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): In fact, it played an important part in the hostage case that happened last month in Shiba Town!
- Kindergarten Student A: You mean when those heavily armed schoolgirls infiltrated the pudding shop?
- Kindergarten Student B: Wow! So you were part of that?!
- Kindergarten Student A: Amazing! So cool!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Ahaha...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): (To be fair, my smoke bomb didn't exactly help matters...)
- Kindergarten Student C: What about those scrunchies you have?
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): These aren't scrunchies—they're handcuffs. I use them to arrest criminals by placing them around their wrists.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): There's no hope of getting out of them without a key, so you have to be very careful when using them.
- Kindergarten Student C: Really? They look like they could fall apart just by tugging at them...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Not at all. It has hidden gears on the inside that make it impossible to loosen them by hand!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Why don't I show you with a demonstration?
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): First, you restrict and immobilize the criminal and cuff one arm...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): ...then you loop the other end of the handcuffs around a sturdy structure and cuff the other arm.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Ta-da! They've been apprehended!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): As you can see, there's no escaping once a criminal has been apprehended.
- Kindergarten Student A: Wow, that's awesome!
- Kindergarten Student B: This is the first time I've ever seen anyone use handcuffs!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Haha. I'm glad they're such a hit.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Now, time to use the key to undo the handcuffs...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Wh-What the...?
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): (M-My hand slipped and I lost the key for the handcuffs!)
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): (And to make matters worse, it rolled into the sewers!)
- Kindergarten Student A: Hey, what's wrong? Is there some kind of problem?
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Uh. Um, well...
- Kirino: ― (Wh-What should I do...?)
- Kirino: ― (Hngh, hmph! It won't...come off?!)
- "Don't tell me you lost the key."
- Kirino: ― H-Huh? No, no!
- Kirino: ― An upstanding officer like me would never lose the key!
- Kirino: ― This is just a, uh...
- Kirino: ― It's an escape training demonstration!
- Kirino: ― Just in case you find yourself wrongfully placed in handcuffs.
- Kirino: ― Yours truly will show you how to escape!
- "Are you allowed to share that kind of info with the public?"
- Kirino: ― Don't fret! I'm a trained police officer!
- Kirino: ― This is nothing for someone like me!
- Kirino: ― Ghhhhhh! Ahhhhhh! *sigh*
- Kirino: ― What did I do to deserve this...?
- Kirino: ― Please...save me Sensei...
> Kirino remained locked in her handcuffs until another Valkyrie student arrived to lend her some help.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): *sigh* Today's field trip was a total failure...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I spent half the day sitting around handcuffed like a lowly criminal instead of showing the students how cool the police can be.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): What kid would ever look up to the police as role models after that?
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): But...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): But...
- Kindergarten Student A: Miss Officer!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): You're one of the students from the field trip. Is something wrong?
- Kindergarten Student A: I wanted to thank you for being our guide. I had a lot of fun!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): But...I messed everything up today.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I didn't even get to show you all the great things that Valkyrie Police School has to offer, and you watched me embarrass myself on top of that...
- Kindergarten Student A: But that only happened because you were answering our questions.
- Kindergarten Student B: It was so cool how you answered everything we asked, no matter how trivial!
- Kindergarten Student C: The older girls at the other schools we went on field trips to didn't even give us the time of day because we're little kids.
- Kindergarten Student A: I want to grow up to become a nice police officer just like you some day!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Wow... That's so sweet of you!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I, Kirino, solemnly swear that I'll always do my best to protect and serve the public and treat them with respect!
- "I'm glad they had a good time."
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Me too. I'm grateful they could tell how sincere I was.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): But still, mistakes were made. I still have a lot to learn if I want to avoid repeating those mistakes again.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I'm going to work real hard on my escape training so I can free myself right away the next time I lose the key to my handcuffs!
- "Is that really what you need to improve on?"
