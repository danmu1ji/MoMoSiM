> Police School Shooting Range
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Strange. It's not that far off, but I keep missing the target...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): My breathing isn't the problem, and I don't lack upper body strength.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): But whenever I pull the trigger, the bullet fires in a different direction than expected—as if my aiming point is off.
- "Are you sure the sight is installed correctly?"
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Of course I am. I give my firearm maintenance every morning.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I would have noticed something a long time ago if there was something wrong with the sight.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): ...Firearms CAN be sensitive though, so I can't rule it out.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Then I'll do some light zeroing shots!
> The paper targets returned from the firing lane once Kirino was done with her shooting.
> Screen shakes
- "..."
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): ...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Th-The aiming point must've been set incorrectly after all!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): There's no other reason the bullets would've kept flying directly at the hostage! Hahaha...
- "Adjust the sight and let's try firing again."
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Right. I'll do that and give it another go.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Adjusting the sight to the right three clicks and up three clicks...
> The paper targets returned from the firing lane once Kirino was done with her shooting.
> Screen shakes
- "..."
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): ...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Th-That's weird. I'm sure I aimed directly at the culprit's head and pulled the trigger...
- "Are you sure you aimed at the culprit?"
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): O-Of course!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): S-Surely you don't think I hit the hostage on purpose, do you, Sensei?
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): A Valkyrie Police School student would never intentionally harm a civilian!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): The fact that my bullets keep hitting them is an unfortunate coincidence...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Anyway! This all happened because the sight was broken!
- "Kirino."
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Huh?
- "How about we try aiming at the hostage to begin with before firing?"
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I'm sorry? You want me to aim the muzzle at the hostage, not the culprit?
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I can't aim my gun at an innocent civilian, even if this is just shooting practice!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): My pride as a police officer will never allow it!
- "But you hit the hostage every time you aim for the culprit."
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Th-That's true, but...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Please forgive me, Mr. Paper Civilian!
- "..."
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): ...
- "It looks like you'll have to aim at the hostage from now on."
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I-I can't do that! What kind of police officer intentionally aims at a hostage?!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): There must have been a mistake. If I correctly adjust the sight, then surely...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I'm going to fire again!
> Kirino kept aiming at the culprit, but the bullets hit the hostage each time without fail.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Ahhhhhh! Why...? Why?!
> Kirino eventually relented and aimed at the hostage while crying each time. She passed the marksmanship exam a few days later.
