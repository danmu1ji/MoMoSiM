# Kirino — Relationship Story 1

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Kirino/Relationship_Story/1
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> Kivotos Business District 4
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): There you are, Sensei.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): We can get our patrol underway now that you're here.
- "What do we need to patrol?"
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Good question!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): The police have a duty to protect the public and prevent major crimes before they happen.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): In other words, we're going to monitor and investigate anything that looks suspicious in the vicinity!
- "But this area looks peaceful..."
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Looks can be deceiving! You never know where the seeds of crime will take root.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): A pro detective can sniff out a case even at peaceful times like this.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): *sniff* Let's see here... Something suspicious, something suspicious...
- Citizen (Average Person): Heh. I've got it. But it's going to take more than this!
- Citizen (Average Person): Now we just have to...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): (Sensei, do you see that over there?)
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): (That person looks awfully suspicious, don't they?)
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): (They're smiling so ominously, and what's with that suspicious-looking jar they have? Like it's carrying the most precious thing in the world.)
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): No. That gait looks too suspicious for someone carrying something fragile.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): No. That gait looks too suspicious for someone carrying something expensive.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): It can only be...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): ...a bomb! I'm sure of it!
- "A bomb?!"
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Of course. Kivotos' business district is home to every important administration in the city.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): This suspect must have been coerced into becoming a malicious terrorist planning to detonate a bomb and halt the city in fear!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Thank goodness I sniffed this plot out before it became a large-scale incident.
- "Are you going to move in for the arrest?"
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Not yet. Simply possessing a bomb isn't enough to constitute a crime in D.U.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): We have to covertly follow the suspect until they plant the bomb.
We'll catch them red-handed and then make the arrest!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): *gasp* They're starting to make their move.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Let's hurry and follow them, Sensei.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Hmm. This is strange.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): We've been pursuing the suspect for a while now, but they still haven't planted the bomb.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Could the target location be somewhere else entirely?
- "Did we profile someone who's innocent?"
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): That can't be it. My gut is never wrong!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): If we wait just a little longer...
- Citizen (Average Person): Wh-What?!
- Petty Thief: Haha. I'll be taking that expensive-looking jar of yours!
- Citizen (Average Person): Th-Thief! It's a thief!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Another criminal is robbing our suspect of their bomb!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): This is bad. I have to hurry and capture the thief, or else...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Hey there, stop! If you don't stop, I'll shoot!
> Kirino pursued the thief.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Ugh... I can't believe I missed every single shot from that distance.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): But at least the thief dropped the jar as they were fleeing. The bomb is secured!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Now we just have to capture the original suspect and—
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Huh? What's that smell?
- Citizen (Average Person): Ah! You got it back! Thank goodness!
- Citizen (Average Person): I thought I'd lost the homemade pickled soybean paste that was sent to me before I'd gotten a chance to taste it.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): P-Pickled soybean paste?!
- Citizen (Average Person): Yes, my family usually sends me some around this time of year.
- Citizen (Average Person): I haven't had much of an appetite these days. This is just what I needed!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I can't believe it. The jar didn't contain a bomb after all.
- Citizen (Average Person): What's this about a bomb...?
- Citizen (Average Person): Well, whatever. I'll share some of my pickled soybean paste with you since you helped me. I'm sure you'll love it!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): Th-Thank you...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): We spent all that effort chasing after a suspect who turned out not to be a terrorist. That's somewhat demoralizing...
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I was overconfident with my gut instinct, and that led me to make decisions too hastily this time.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): True. It won't be enough to transfer me to the Security Bureau, but at least we were able to get back one citizen's property.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): N-No! I have to always think of the citizens' safety first as a student of Valkyrie Police School!
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I should think of this pickled soybean paste they gave me as a badge of honor.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): But...it's too much to eat all by myself.
- Kirino (Public Safety Bureau): I know! I'll garnish some of my dinner with it once my patrol is finished!
> Kirino and I patrolled the area more before returning to Schale and enjoying the pickled soybean paste together.
> The badge of honor was a bit salty without any rice.
