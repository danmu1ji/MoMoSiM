## MomoTalk 5

- Kirino: Ugh. I'm so nervous...
- Sensei: What's the matter?
- Kirino: Kindergarten students will be visiting Valkyrie in the afternoon...
- Sensei: And I'll be their guide.
- Kirino: I'm worried that I might get too nervous and make mistakes...
- Sensei: I'll help you out, then.
- Kirino: You're going to come help me in person?
- Sensei: That's a relief. Thank you, Sensei!

- Kirino: Of course. This is the best opportunity to show the good side of police to the young students.
- Sensei: It was a field trip just like this that inspired me.
- Sensei: I want to come off as cool as the officers did back then.
- Sensei: Kirino.
- Kirino: Huh?
- Sensei: Whatever you do, don't pull out your gun. Even in a panic.
- Kirino: I-I know that! Give me some credit!
