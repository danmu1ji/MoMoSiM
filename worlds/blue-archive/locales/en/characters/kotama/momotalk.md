## MomoTalk 2

- Kotama: Sensei! Sensei!
- Kotama: Is there something that you particularly like?
- Sensei: That's a very broad question...
- Kotama: Like something you collect?
- Sensei: Or something you carry around all day long?
- Sensei: Like if you leave it behind, you feel anxious all day?
- Sensei: Not really...
- Kotama: Really? Then that might make things difficult...
- Sensei: What will be difficult?
- Kotama: Never mind.

## MomoTalk 3

- Kotama: Have you heard of a ghost story called Mary's Calling, Sensei?
- Sensei: Never heard of it.
- Kotama: Mary's Calling is a scary story about a little girl with a doll named Mary. When the girl throws the doll away, the doll starts calling for her.
- Sensei: The scary part is that each time Mary calls the little girl, she gets closer and closer to her. At the end, the doll appears right behind the little girl.
- Sensei: And why are you telling me this?
- Kotama: I don't know. I was just curious if you'd heard of it.
- Kotama: If you could trace back her calls, I wonder if you would see where Mary is calling from?
- Sensei: I wish Mary would call me sometime.
- Sensei: How spooky!

## MomoTalk 5

- Kotama: Yes, human hearing is surprisingly vulnerable to repetitive stimulus.

- Kotama: The music I listen to?
- Kotama: Well, that is...
- Sensei: ...
- Kotama: It's a secret.
