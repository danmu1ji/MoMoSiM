> Kivotos Hub Electronics Shopping District
> While shopping for new earphones, I spotted Kotama listening to music.
- Kotama (Veritas): ...
- "Hey, Kotama."
- Kotama (Veritas): ... ♪
> Kotama seemed too immersed in the music to notice me.
- Approach Kotama.
- Kotama (Veritas): ― Oh, Sensei.
- Kotama (Veritas): ― Sorry...
- Kotama (Veritas): ― I was caught up in the music.
- "No worries. What music were you listening to?"
- Kotama (Veritas): ― This music?
- Kotama (Veritas): ― Heh heh. Sensei,
- Kotama (Veritas): ― are you really that curious?
- "These are, um...noises? In a cafe?"
- Kotama (Veritas): ― It's not really music.
- Kotama (Veritas): ― It's just...people talking, and the noise that comes with that.
- Kotama (Veritas): ― It's called "Urban Ambiance".
- Kotama (Veritas): ― There's no rhythm or melody...
- Kotama (Veritas): ― But there's plenty to listen to. The beats of everyday conversation, the chorus of a crowd...
- Kotama (Veritas): ― These sounds don't come from instruments or vocalists.
- Kotama (Veritas): ― They come from people, real people. To me, that's better than any musical arrangement.
- Kotama (Veritas): ― And I wish
- Kotama (Veritas): ― I could listen to it forever.
> I spent the rest of my time listening to albums Kotama recommended.
- Kotama (Veritas): So, Sensei, how do you like it?
- "Who'd have thought there could be music in everyday sounds?"
- Kotama (Veritas): They're pretty popular these days. There's all kinds of different ambient noise albums to evoke certain moods.
- Kotama (Veritas): For example, say you wanted to focus more on work. The sound of the rain or a busy cafe might help.
- "Do you have a favorite sound?"
- Kotama (Veritas): Well, things like the sound of rain or sitting in a cafe might be nice, but...
- Kotama (Veritas): I think I like your voice the best.
- "My voice?"
- Kotama (Veritas): Yes, listening to your voice calms me and clears my head.
- Kotama (Veritas): The only downside is that I can't buy a soundtrack of your voice...
- Kotama (Veritas): So, Sensei, do you think we could record one now?
- "No."
- Kotama (Veritas): No fair!
> I then spent the rest of my time stopping Kotama from recording me. I never ended up getting those new earphones.
