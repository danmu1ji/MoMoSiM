> Schale Executive Office
> Screen shakes
> Kotama brought a large teddy bear to Schale's Executive Office on her duty day.
- "What's with the teddy bear?"
- Kotama (Veritas): Well... Uh...
- Kotama (Veritas): It's a gift. A gift for Sensei.
- "A surprise gift?"
- Kotama (Veritas): I did receive a lot of help from you.
- Kotama (Veritas): I thought about how I could repay you, and decided to give you a gift.
- "This is unexpected.... But I'll take it. Thank you."
- Kotama (Veritas): Sure.
- Kotama (Veritas): Sensei. I'd like for you to keep this teddy bear in Schale's Executive Office if possible.
- Kotama (Veritas): I don't have any ulterior motives.
- Kotama (Veritas): I just...I thought it'd be best for the bear.
- Kotama (Veritas): I'd prefer it to be placed somewhere in the sunlight where it can overlook the entire Executive Office.
- Kotama (Veritas): Don't place anything in front of it which might block its sight, and be sure to keep it away from water and electromagnetic fields that might be bad for electronics...
- (Examine the teddy bear carefully.)
- Kotama (Veritas): S-Sensei? Why are you...
> (There was a suspicious device behind a button on the bear.)
- Kotama (Veritas): Th-That's... I wouldn't worry about that. It's just a simple...
- Kotama (Veritas): It's just a device that records sound!
- "I'm sorry, but I can't accept this teddy bear."
- Kotama (Veritas): Yes. I guess it can't be helped if you don't like it...
> But Kotama's wiretapping attempts continued...
- Kotama (Veritas): Sensei, I brought a new wall clock.
- Kotama (Veritas): You won't find anything strange this time.
- (Examine the clock.)
> (A wiretapping device was tucked between the hands of the clock.)
- Kotama (Veritas): Oh, it's just...a clock with a recording device. Other than that, there's nothing unique about it.
- "I still can't accept this clock."
- Kotama (Veritas): *sigh* ...Foiled again.
- Kotama (Veritas): Sensei, I've prepared a meal for you.
- Kotama (Veritas): Please eat it before it gets cold.
> (A wire-tapping device was floating in plain sight.)
- ...
- Kotama (Veritas): ...It seems you already know.
- Kotama (Veritas): I guess sneaking in an eavesdropping device with some food isn't a viable option either...
- Kotama (Veritas): The teddy bear, clock, and meal all failed. What next...
- "Why are you so insistent on wiretapping us?"
- Kotama (Veritas): It's not about wiretapping. I just want to record your voice.
- "In that case, you don't need to be so secretive about it."
- Kotama (Veritas): I'm trying because...
- Kotama (Veritas): I want to know a secret of yours that nobody else knows.
- Kotama (Veritas): I thought if I wiretapped the Executive Office, I might be able to record you slipping up here and there...
- Kotama (Veritas): No, that's not true!
- Kotama (Veritas): No, that's not true!
- Kotama (Veritas): I just want to have a normal conversation with you...
- Kotama (Veritas): In my eyes, Sensei, you're a perfect adult with zero flaws.
- Kotama (Veritas): Of course, I've gotten so much help from you thanks to your perfection, but that makes it harder for me to talk with you...
- Kotama (Veritas): I thought I'd be able to finally have a normal conversation with you if I knew a secret others didn't.
- Kotama (Veritas): But it's not easy to get a wiretapping device into Schale. I'll give up on trying to uncover your secrets.
- "I'm actually afraid of heights."
- Kotama (Veritas): What?
- "My weakness is that I'm afraid of heights."
- Kotama (Veritas): I see. So Sensei is afraid of heights...
- Kotama (Veritas): Haha. That's funny. You work in an office in a tall building, and yet you're afraid of heights.
- "That's why it's a secret."
- Kotama (Veritas): Indeed. Even a person who seems perfect has an unexpected weakness...
- Kotama (Veritas): I think I understand you better now, Sensei.
- Kotama (Veritas): Thank you for playing along with me, Sensei.
