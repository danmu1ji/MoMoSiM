# Kotama — Relationship Story 2

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> Schale House
> I lay down on a cot to rest my eyes after a late night of work at Schale.
> Just when I was about to fall asleep, I heard a strange sound.
- Unidentified Voice: Haha, hahaha...
- Unidentified Voice: My name's... I'm...in...
- "...Where's that voice coming from?"
- Unidentified Voice: I'm coming straight at you... Two...!
- "That voice... Mary?"
- Unidentified Voice: One...!
> Screen shakes
- "?!"
> I couldn't fall asleep because of the mysterious laughing...
> Schale Executive Office
- Kotama (Veritas): So, you heard a mysterious voice at the Schale House which you believe to be Mary?
- Kotama (Veritas): That's interesting... Actually, it's been a dream of mine to record audio from a ghost.
- Kotama (Veritas): Would it be okay if I stayed at the Schale House with you tonight? I'll discover where the sound is coming from.
- "Kotama, aren't you afraid of Mary?"
- Kotama (Veritas): No need to be afraid. Most phenomena can be explained with science, and unexplained phenomena must be researched. I'm actually quite excited.
- "...You really are a Millennium student, after all."
- Kotama (Veritas): Of course. I'm also a member of Veritas, the seekers of truth.
- Kotama (Veritas): I'll have to set up the sound equipment in the Schale House in advance so that I can record right away when we hear something.
- Kotama (Veritas): Then I'll go to Veritas' club room to get the recording equipment.
> Kotama returned to Millennium to get the recording equipment.
> Schale House
- Kotama (Veritas): So this is the Schale House Sensei uses when on duty.
- Kotama (Veritas): I tried so hard to install a wiretapping device here... Who'd have thought I'd end up getting it in like this?
- Kotama (Veritas): That's a joke, of course. Please don't worry about eavesdropping.
- "Saying that makes me worry even more..."
- Kotama (Veritas): First, we'll listen carefully to see if there are any strange sounds.
> Soon...
- Unidentified Voice: Haha, hahaha...
- Unidentified Voice: My name's... I'm...in...
- "There it is again!"
- Kotama (Veritas): This voice is perhaps...?
- Kotama (Veritas): Sensei, could you turn on the Executive Office radio?
- Kotama (Veritas): If I'm right, this is probably...
- Radio DJ: Haha! Did I keep you waiting? This is DJ Mary, coming to you live from Kronos!
- Radio DJ: If you send me a story, I'll hook you up with some prizes.
- Radio DJ: Looks like there are...two stories today!
- "This voice is..."
- Kotama (Veritas): That's right, it's the late-night radio show.
- Kotama (Veritas): Occasionally when the radio frequency gets picked up by an unconnected speaker, this strange voice might result.
- Kotama (Veritas): Mary's voice must've been from this radio broadcast.
- "I'm glad it wasn't a ghost."
- Kotama (Veritas): But it's a little disappointing. I was looking forward to recording a real ghost's voice.
- Kotama (Veritas): ...Maybe I should use a 10 yen coin to give Kokkuri a call.
- "Stop it, now you're really scaring me!"
> After that, I had a hard time convincing Kotama not to try summoning ghosts.
