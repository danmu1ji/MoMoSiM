- Rabu (Helmet Gang): Sensei, do you not get tired of running like that all the time?
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): What if you fall and get hurt? Your skin is so sensitive, after all.
- "I can't make my precious student wait!"
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): (P-Precious student...)
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): It's not a big deal.
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): The weather's nice. How about we go for a walk?
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): So, she walked up to me, chest puffed out, and said...
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): "Captain! You'll have to go through me first!"
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ...So I did! You should have seen it!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): But this other time, my helmet split. It was a good thing I was wearing it, obviously!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): And, even though we barely have a spare yen between us, they saved up. They took it in for repairs!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): So, yeah! I care about my crew. Obviously. They're not all bad.
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ...And she came back, bruises all up her arms, cut lip...
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): So I said, "Where are they?! I'll teach them to—"
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): Huh? What's that?
- "Looks like ducks."
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): They're so small. I usually can't tell the difference between them and geese.
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): But this girl has some swagger, huh?
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): And...six little babies following her. Aw.
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): They are!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): Ah! The little ducklings fell!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): Lady! Be careful! You should have known that your babies are too small! You gotta watch out!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): *sigh* I guess there's no helping it.
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): Sensei, hold the grate up!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― One, two, three, four, five, six. Perfect!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― Rescued all six of them!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― You should listen to your mommy,
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― okay?
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― Momma Duck also seems to be doing fine now,
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― so that's another problem solved!
- "You're not a typical student, Rabu."
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― Wh-Wh-Wh-What are you talking about?!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― Sensei, isn't that a bit rude?
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― Huh?...Ah?! That's...
- "I meant that the way you lead the Helmet Gang is also similar."
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― Ah, ah! So that's what you meant!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― Of course, a leader should
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― have a compassionate side too!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― (Is Sensei doing this on purpose?)
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― (Maybe they're just clumsy?)
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― (Even if they are, it pisses me off.)
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― (But it's worse if it's intentional!)
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): ― (...There's no way that was on purpose... Right...?)
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): Okay, let's stay on the grass from now on!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): And watch where you're going next time!
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): Okay then, what were we talking about again?
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): Did I already tell you how we took on 100 thugs with just the 3 of us?
- "By the way, are you okay with wearing that helmet?"
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): !
- Rabu (Helmet Gang): You're the worst, Sensei! I hate you!
