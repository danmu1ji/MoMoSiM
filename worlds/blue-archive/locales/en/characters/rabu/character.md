# Rabu

## Description

The captain of the Splash Helmet Gang. She is trusted by her subordinates and has a big sister personality. However, she doesn't seem to have much luck.

## Appearance

Rabu is pale, with long red hair and red eyes; her sharp shark-like teeth are also distinctive.
