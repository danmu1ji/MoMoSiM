> Millennium School Classroom
- Maki (Veritas): Studying has nothing to do with me failing the test...
- Maki (Veritas): Millennium's tests are too hard to begin with! None of the problems are meant for average high school students!
- "You could solve anything if you would just apply yourself."
- Maki (Veritas): Sensei, you're starting to sound like a teacher... Wait. I guess you are one.
- Maki (Veritas): Besides, studying the textbooks doesn't help me understand the questions.
- Maki (Veritas): Especially with literature! How am I supposed to know what the narrator is feeling just from reading a paragraph? I can't read minds, you know!
- Maki (Veritas): What? How can I be such a good hacker if I can't answer such simple questions?
- Maki (Veritas): I don't get what you mean. Hacking is way easier than this.
- Maki (Veritas): Pouring too much water in a bucket will make it overflow. When you don't have the key you need to open something, you ask the person who does. It's all so simple!
- Maki (Veritas): But solving literature questions is supposed to be easier than hacking? Let's see about that...
- Maki (Veritas): Oh, so this part means that the character is happy. Right, I think I get it!
- Maki (Veritas): What about this part, Sensei? What does this mean...?
- Maki (Veritas): Thanks, Sensei! I'm going to ace the make-up exam at this rate.
- Maki (Veritas): It's amazing how easy you make it to understand complex questions.
- Maki (Veritas): Huh? You think it's because I'm a fast learner?
- Maki (Veritas): Haha. Don't flatter me.
- Maki (Veritas): I may not be the smartest Veritas member, but the last thing I want is to fall behind the curriculum.
- Maki (Veritas): And it's not like Veritas sees me as a troublemaker. It's one of the only places where I feel like my talents are understood.
- Maki (Veritas): Besides, you mean a lot to me too, Sensei. So... I'm going to try harder.
- Maki (Veritas): Haha! That came out way too embarrassing. Forget I said anything!
- Maki (Veritas): Ahem. Did I memorize the notebook you gave me?
- Maki (Veritas): Here it says the notebook is 100 sheets of 80-gram A-type white paper cut to 297 by 210 millimeters...
- Maki (Veritas): Hmm? What am I reading? It's the beginning of the notebook you gave me.
- Maki (Veritas): *gasp* I'm not supposed to memorize that part? I thought you told me to memorize everything inside...
- Maki (Veritas): E-Excuse me? You can't tell whether I'm brilliant or brainless? What's that supposed to mean, Sensei?!
