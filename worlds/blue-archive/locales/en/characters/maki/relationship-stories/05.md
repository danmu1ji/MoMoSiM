> Downtown Kivotos
- Passerby: Young lady! You with the red hair!
- Maki (Veritas): Hmm? Me?
- Passerby: Yeah, you're the one who spray-painted that wall, right?
- Maki (Veritas): Oh, well...
- Passerby: I'm the owner of that building.
- Maki (Veritas): I'm so sorry!
- Maki (Veritas): I wasn't trying to cause trouble. It's just, the alley was so dirty—
- Maki (Veritas): Please, don't call the police!
- Passerby: I wasn't going to.
- Passerby: I've been so tired of people dumping trash back there and tagging the wall with ugly graffiti.
- Passerby: But there's been less litter and tagging ever since you painted it with your art. I don't know how to thank you.
- Maki (Veritas): O-Oh. I only did it because i-inspiration struck. H-Haha...!
- Passerby: Would you be interested in painting the other side too?
- Passerby: You don't have to if you don't want to, though...
- Maki (Veritas): Of course I want to! I'd be happy to!
- Maki (Veritas): Haha! I can't believe I have the green light to do whatever I want with this wall.
- Maki (Veritas): Graffiti artists are almost never given the freedom to paint whatever they want for however long they want. It's so exciting!
- "I can't wait to see what you're going to paint."
- Maki (Veritas): Where's this coming from, Sensei? You promised to be my apprentice, remember?
- Maki (Veritas): You're going to be painting with me, obviously. Here, take my spray can, Sensei.
- "What should I paint...?"
- Maki (Veritas): Don't overthink it. Let your heart paint the lines, then fill in the colors.
- Maki (Veritas): You can always paint over any mistakes... So don't worry. Just pick up the spray can and get started.
- Maki: ― So, is this good enough?
- Maki: ― What do you think, Sensei?
- Maki: ― Hmph... At least try to look impressed.
- Maki: ― I need you to give me feedback like a professional art critic!
- Maki: ― ...
- Maki: ― So what did you paint, Sensei?
- Maki: ― Uh...
- Maki: ― Hahaha! What is that supposed to be? It looks so silly!
- Maki: ― Aren't I scared of heights? Not when I have you by my side.
- Maki: ― And I'll be by yours whenever you need me.
- Maki: ― The wall, Sensei. I'll paint anything if it's for you!
- Maki (Veritas): I hope this wall stays here forever so I can always remember this moment with you.
- Maki (Veritas): I, uh... Pretend you didn't just hear that!
