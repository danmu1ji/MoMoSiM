# Maki — Relationship Story 1

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Maki/Relationship_Story/1
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> Police Station
- Maki (Veritas): Waaaaaah! Sensei!
- Maki (Veritas): The police arrested me even though I'm innocent...
- Cop: You know exactly why we arrested you!
- Maki (Veritas): Waaah, Sensei! The cops are bullying me!
- Cop: Are you this child's guardian?
- "I'm her teacher."
- Cop: Are you aware of what this young lady has been doing?
- Maki (Veritas): I didn't do anything wrong! I did what I had to do!
- Cop: Why you!
- Cop: This girl was caught trespassing on private property...
- Cop: ...not to mention vandalizing it!
- Maki (Veritas): Graffiti isn't vandalism. It's an art form!
- Cop: Try explaining that to the property owner, young lady.
- "Vandalism?"
- Cop: Precisely—or "graffiti" as this miscreant refers to it. She was caught painting images on the property's wall.
- Maki (Veritas): But the owner said it was fine!
- Cop: He only said that he forgave you, not that you were given permission in the first place. This also isn't the first instance.
- Cop: The overpass on the KV highway, the back alley of the Kuro Neko market...
- Cop: You're always tagging places where there aren't a lot of people around. Do you have any idea how hard it is to clean up the messes you leave behind?
- Maki (Veritas): They're not messes—they're art!
- "...I'm sorry about her."
- Cop: She should be the one apologizing, not you.
- Maki (Veritas): What?! All I'm doing is adding some liveliness to this gloomy city!
- "You really should apologize, Maki."
- Maki (Veritas): But... Ugh...
- Maki (Veritas): Fine. I'm...sorry about all of this.
- Cop: Hmph. I'll let you off with a warning this time. Don't think you'll get so lucky if I catch you again!
- Maki (Veritas): Hey, Sensei. Are you...angry with me?
- "Why would I be?"
- Maki (Veritas): Don't lie. You are angry, aren't you? Um...
- Maki (Veritas): But that gloomy gray wall was calling out to me! It wanted to be tagged in bright, pretty colors!
- Maki (Veritas): I-I know that tagging someone else's property isn't right, okay...?
- Maki (Veritas): But I couldn't contain the burning artistic passion inside me...
- Maki (Veritas): I'm sorry. The last thing I wanted was to make you angry.
- Maki (Veritas): Graffiti means a lot to me but not more than you do.
- Maki (Veritas): This is a predicament. How can I continue expressing myself with graffiti but not make Sensei angry...?
- "We could always get you a huge painting canvas."
- Maki (Veritas): ...A huge canvas?
- Maki (Veritas): Wait, I get it. If we stacked several canvases together, I could use that instead of the side of a building!
- Maki (Veritas): Maybe it won't be the same as using a real wall...but anything is fun as long as I'm doing it with you, Sensei.
- Maki (Veritas): So...if I put a huge canvas together, will you be there to help me paint?
- "Sure. We'll work together."
- Maki (Veritas): You promise, right? Thanks, Sensei!
