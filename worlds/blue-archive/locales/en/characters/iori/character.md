# Iori

## Description

Enrolled in Gehenna Academy, Iori is a ruthless specialist of the Public Morals Committee. As the leader of its assault corps, she punishes students that she finds breaking the rules with overwhelming force. While she may be quick-thinking, good at her job and possessing a decent sense of combat, Iori is also prone to losing sight of her surroundings once she spots an enemy. This recklessness can lead to her falling into even the simplest of traps.
