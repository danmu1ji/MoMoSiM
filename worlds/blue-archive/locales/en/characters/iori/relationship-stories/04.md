> Gehenna Academy Infirmary
> ...
- "Is everything all right, Iori?"
- Iori (Prefect Team): Wah! Oh, it's just you, Sensei. You scared me!
- Iori (Prefect Team): How is my injury? Oh, it's not too bad.
- Iori (Prefect Team): Prefects expect to take damage in the line of duty. Don't worry about it.
- Iori (Prefect Team): Huh? Why would I need to disinfect it? I already treated it long before you showed up.
- Iori (Prefect Team): Um...
- Iori (Prefect Team): Only thing is, I can't quite reach around and finishing tying up the bandage...
- Iori (Prefect Team): Would you mind helping me, Sensei?
- Iori (Prefect Team): Undoing the back of my uniform is a little tricky... Can you close your eyes for a bit?
- Iori (Prefect Team): And don't get any funny ideas...
- Iori (Prefect Team): I'm done. You can open them now.
- Iori: ― Why are you making that face? You had better not be thinking anything lewd!
- Iori: ― How long are you just going to stand there staring? Hurry up and help me.
- Iori: ― Ugh... Guh Argh...
- Iori: ― Did it hurt? I'm a Gehenna prefect. This is nothing!
- Iori: ― It just...kind of tickled... That's all.
- Iori: ― What are you doing? The bandage is coming loose.
- Iori: ― If it's you...I don't mind it at all.
- Iori: ― So hurry up and wrap it tighter!
> I wrapped Iori's bandage for her and helped her get dressed again.
- Iori (Prefect Team): Thanks for all of your help today.
- Iori (Prefect Team): You've always struck me as a weirdo, but you do have a sweet side to you.
- Iori (Prefect Team): You would help me treat my wounds again if I asked...right?
- Iori (Prefect Team): You idiot! I didn't say I'd get hurt on purpose just so you can help me!
- Iori (Prefect Team): But if and when it does happen...I hope I can count on you.
> Iori left the infirmary with a pleased look on her face.
