# Tsubaki — official English introduction

Enrolled at Hyakkiyako Academy, Tsubaki is president of the discipline club. Notorious as a serial sleepyhead, Tsubaki is always taking naps during the day, so her responsibilities as club president are effectively entirely handled by her friend Mimori. Contrary to her lazy daytime habits, at night Tsubaki is engaged secretly defending Hyakkihakou Academy.
