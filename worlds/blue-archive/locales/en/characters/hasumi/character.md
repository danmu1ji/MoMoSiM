# Hasumi

## Description

Enrolled at Trinity Integrated Academy, Hasumi is the Justice Actualization Committee's No. 2. In place of its president, Tsurugi, who embodies wildness, Hasumi also acts as the Justice Actualization Committee's tactical commander. At a glance she appears composed and intellectual, like the archetypal Trinity student. However, Hasumi too is a fully-fledged member of the Justice Actualization Committee, and will sometimes act recklessly while retaining her composed nature.
