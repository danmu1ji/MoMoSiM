> Trinity Suburban Park
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): What's this..?
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Hello, Sensei. I didn't expect to see you here.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): You just came here to get some fresh air?
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Me too. I wanted to make time to stop by the park...before getting around to today's club activities.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): What a pleasant surprise that I ran into you here. Heehee.
- "Is something going on?"
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Nothing in particular. I just needed some time to myself.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): This is the perfect time of day to be alone in the park.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): I can look up at the sky without a care in the world...
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): ...and watch as the sky turns purple with the setting sun.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): ...Look.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): The sky is this color for only a few minutes before the sun sets. You have to savor it.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): I've never been much in the way of a conversationalist...
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): ...but a sight like this speaks for itself, doesn't it?
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): ...
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): ...I must sound like a whimsical old woman right now, don't I?
- "No. Not at all."
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Pardon me. It's a bad habit of mine.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Brief though it may be, I'd like to share it with you.
> I sat in the park with Hasumi until it got dark before returning to Schale.
