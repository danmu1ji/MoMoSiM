> Trinity Suburban Shop
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Hey, Sensei.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): We have to stop meeting like this.
- "Hasumi...? What are you doing here?"
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Isn't it obvious? Am I not allowed to shop here too?
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): ...Did you upset me?
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Oh... I'm sorry. I've been weaning myself off of desserts.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): It's making me very stressed and high strung.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): The Justice Task Force's current workload isn't helping matters...
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): I've even begun to develop headaches.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): ...
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): ...But I'm fine.
- "You don't look fine to me."
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Do I not...?
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): If I keep worrying the people around me, then what's the point of my diet?
- "Why not drop the diet if you're so stressed out?"
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Do you think I should?
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): But, I...
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): I'm a little embarrassed to admit it, but I lose confidence when I see other ladies with their dainty uniforms...
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Um... If I may ask...
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Do you not want me to go on a diet, Sensei?
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): ...
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): ...
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): I see.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): ...That's how you feel?
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Heehee. My headache suddenly went away.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): Let's go. I know of a delicious bakery nearby.
- Hasumi (Justice Task Force): You're the one who told me to quit my diet. Therefore, you have a responsibility to tag along and try desserts with me.
> We then went and stuffed ourselves at the bakery.
