- Yakumo (Publishing Department): You know, Sensei.
- "What is it, Yakumo?"
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Anything Schale says eventually spreads through all of Kivotos, right?
- "Well, in a sense, it is pretty official."
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Of course. But even taking that into account...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): I recently realized how rare it is to find an influencer with this level of intangible power and reach.
- "Hearing you talk like that makes me really anxious."
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Just hear me out, Sensei.
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Don't you think it's a shame to waste such influence and trust?
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): You might not. But I do. I can see it.
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): So, while I was thinking about that, I came up with an excellent pitch. What if we launched a "Let's Read Together" campaign, supported straight from Schale?
- "That seems like it would benefit the public."
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): And then we carefully select and include the Publishing Department's special selection of books. Heh, heh, heh.
- "Please behave yourself, Yakumo."
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Ah, now that I think about it, Trinity Library would take a hit, I guess...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): No. If I play my cards right, it'll just boost the Publishing Department's credibility even more... We could use it to our advantage...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): People do say that only a true entrepreneur can turn crises into opportunities...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Sensei. Could you support the Publishing Department? As an advisor or something like that?
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): As for the justification... Hm. "For the welfare of the frozen academy of the wastelands, and for the destitute students who suffer there."
- "No."
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Why?
- "You know why."
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): You're right... Fiddlesticks. If only that annoying neutrality clause didn't exist.
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Well, what else is there...?
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Schale... D.U....
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): A universally recognized zone of political neutrality...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): In other words, no academy can rashly assert their sovereignty here...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): ...even the Red Winter Federal Academy's weak voice could be amplified within Schale...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Hm, hmmm. And the student from said Academy just happens to be me, Aramaki Yakumo...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Sensei!
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): For the sake of equality and fairness, the unofficial motto of our Red Winter Federal Academy, all academies—
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Well, not all of them, but the so-called "top 3 academies"...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): How about we make a...gentle suggestion to directly, or even indirectly, assist with Red Winter's pudding production?
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): And we'll add some fine print stating that the whole endeavor was sponsored by the Publishing Department.
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): This will temporarily alleviate the chronic shortage of pudding, elevate Red Winter's standing externally, and internally...
- "Deeeniiied."
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Why?
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Ugh... Fine...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Wait. I've only been focusing on Red Winter until now... Is that shortsightedness the cause of my failure?
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): By leveraging the fact that Schale possesses extrajudicial authority...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): ...Don't look at me like that. This time, the aim is to create a virtuous culture. Trust me. It's the truth!
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Anyway, that extrajudicial authority, although it's only ever been used in emergencies...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): In this case, for the common good...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Supposing, for example...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): ...We'll work out the specifics a little later.
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Either way, let's carry out public projects that benefit everyone in the name of Schale.
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): And then just announce that you're collaborating with the Publishing Department!
- "No."
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): So cold...
- "That being said, you seem to really enjoy this sort of thing, Yakumo."
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Well, that's right. Writing business proposals is a hobby of mine, of sorts.
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): How do I explain this? It's the excitement of exploring and manipulating the endless possibilities of any given scenario.
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): It doesn't matter if it's out of the question or actually feasible.
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Besides...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): When it comes to making money, you have to exaggerate everything you can (within reason), without outright lying!
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Even more so when it comes to the Red Winter Office...
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): For example, let's say that pudding rations this month would double if a project goes well...
- "Has that ever worked out?"
- Yakumo (Publishing Department): Er... I'm sure it could, one day...
