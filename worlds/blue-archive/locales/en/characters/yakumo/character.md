# Yakumo

## Description

Head of the Publishing Department at Red Winter Federal Academy, Yakumo also serves as the editor-in-chief of "Red Bear." She has a stubborn side, and never backs down once she makes up her mind. She will use any means necessary to benefit the Publishing Department, demonstrating her unrivaled drive. However, it seems that she has a pure-hearted side as well...

## Appearance

Yakumo has medium-length, slightly wavy straw-colored hair in two messy low ponytails, green eyes, and lowered white dog ears with gray edges.
