# Akane\n\nAn agent of Millennium's secret society "C&C". Having a callsign of Zero-three, she is an essential member of C&C specializing in "cleaning up". Akane has earned her nickname of "Master Cleaner" for her ability to infiltrate enemy lines with a deceivingly tender impression and gracefully yet explosively "clean out" the enemy.\n\nSource: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Akane\nReview status: source extracted; pending editorial review.\n
