# Kazusa — official English introduction

A member of Trinity Integrated Academy's After School Sweets Club with a laid-back and quiet personality. Preferring to avoid the spotlight, she usually follows along with the other club members. Kazusa is always having a hard time getting caught up in the eccentricities of those around her, but she also cherishes those moments.
