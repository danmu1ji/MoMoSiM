# Juri — official English introduction

A student of Gehenna Academy and member of the School Lunch Club that manages the academy cafeteria. While working alongside Fuuka to prepare meals for fellow students, because her cooking talent is said to exceed catastrophically bad levels, it's hard to say her food provisions are of particular use. However because of encouragement and direction from Fuuka, Juri works hard everyday to make better meals.
