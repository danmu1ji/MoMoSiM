> Gehenna Dining Hall No. 8
> Before I knew it, the weekend arrived.
> I visited the Student Dining Hall, like I had promised Juri I would.
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Oh, Sensei! I'm so glad you finally made it!
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Oh, you mean all of this?
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Isn't it obvious? It's a shovel, a sickle, and a hammer!
- "Yes, but what do you plan on doing with those things...?"
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Well, to dig with the shovel like THUD, cut with the sickle like SWISH, and beat it with the hammer like BANG!
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Ah! Don't worry. These aren't the only tools we have.
- Juri (School Lunch Club): I have all different kinds besides just these.
- Juri (School Lunch Club): So, you don't have to worry about whether we'll have enough tools, Sensei.
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Trust that School Lunch Club member Juri is on the job!
- "Wait, wait. What kind of 'job' are you going to do...?!"
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Oh. Isn't it obvious.
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Gardening!
> I followed Juri to the back side of the Gehenna Student Dining Hall.
> She led me to a large garden filled with many different kinds of food.
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Heehee. Do you like it?
- Juri (School Lunch Club): This is the School Lunch Club's pride and joy: our garden!
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Well, you see... The School Lunch Club is always working on a tight budget, and it can be hard to get enough food to make hundreds and hundreds of lunches for the students...
- Juri (School Lunch Club): So we've been trying to solve the problem by growing some of the ingredients ourselves!
- Juri (School Lunch Club): To make things even easier, there's a volcano right by Gehenna.
- Juri (School Lunch Club): The soil in our region is very fertile because of it!
- Juri (School Lunch Club): We just plant the seeds, water and prune them as needed, and then ta-da! It's like magic!
- Juri (School Lunch Club): And now we can source the freshest garden-to-table vegetables and ingredients that we all worked together to grow!
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Heehee. Isn't it pretty incredible!
- Juri (School Lunch Club): H-Huh?! R-Really? Do you think so?
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Heehee. I suppose you're right. Cooking will always have my whole heart, but I do love to garden as well.
- Juri (School Lunch Club): I have a long way to go in my culinary journey... I still can't make an amazing dish that makes everyone go "Yum!" with just one bite...
- Juri (School Lunch Club): But I thought helping us find a way to source high-quality ingredients at an affordable cost was something I could do to contribute.
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Really good ingredients are delicious even before they're cooked! That's what I wanted to bring to the School Lunch Club.
- Juri (School Lunch Club): This doesn't mean I'm giving up on becoming a master chef! I just wanted to do what I could, right now, with the skills I currently have, to make the students happier and healthier.
- "Yup. I was right. You're just incredible, Juri."
- Juri (School Lunch Club): M-Me...?! Incredible? I-I'm just another normal student...
- Juri (School Lunch Club): I just wanted to do what I could to help, since I'm still a novice cook.
- Juri (School Lunch Club): B-But for you to give me such a huge compliment, Sensei...
- Juri (School Lunch Club): W-Well...! What do I do?!
- Juri (School Lunch Club): I-It doesn't matter! We're here for a job!
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Let's get to gardening! Before we begin, I-I brought you a straw hat, arm sleeves, and sunscreen to protect you from the sun! Please be sure to use them.
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Th-Then, shall we, Sensei?
- Juri: ― Ah. Look, Sensei.
- Juri: ― That ripe tomato
- Juri: ― looks so delicious, doesn't it?
- Juri: ― Heehee. This is all the result of everyone's hard work at the School Lunch Club.
- Juri: ― Growing this garden was really worth it!
- Juri: ― Oh, the tomatoes over there look really good too…
- "Huh? Where?"
- Juri: ― Um, uh, it's over there, b-but...
- Juri: ― Aren't we really close right now…?
- Juri: ― If you're this close, then, uh, um…
- Juri: ― (Ah! Sensei's so close I can smell them…)
- Juri: ― (M-My heart is beating faster for some strange reason…!)
- "Juri... You're flushed. Are you too hot?"
- Juri: ― I- I'm okay! P-Please don't worry…!
- Juri: ― But, it's just that, you see... Um…
- Juri: ― My face feels so hot… Eek…!
> We were gardening together for quite some time.
> Juri was usually very focused when doing tasks, but she seemed distracted and was fidgeting constantly.
- "Hey, Juri…?"
- Juri (School Lunch Club): (I garden like this all the time, but it's a different experience doing it by Sensei's side, with my heart beating out of my chest...)
- Juri (School Lunch Club): So, um... The thing is..! You see!
- Juri (School Lunch Club): H-Huh...?!
- Juri (School Lunch Club): Augh! I need space— I mean, water! We need water to water the plants! Y-Yes. I'll go get us some!
> I tended the garden by myself for a while after Juri scampered off in a hurry. She took a very long time getting back for some reason.
