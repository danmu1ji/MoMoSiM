# Neru

## Description

The leader of Millennium's secret agency "C&C". Looking indisputably like a delinquent with her attire, she's actually prideful of being the strongest agent, boasting a 100% mission success rate. Because of this, she's earned the callsign "Double O" which is seen as a guarantee of certain victory.

## Appearance

Neru has short vermilion hair with a crooked cowlick, a side braid and a tied section accented with Millennium tags. She has sharp red eyes and wears a maid headdress with her distinctive sukajan.
