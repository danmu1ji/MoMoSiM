> I headed over to Millennium Square to meet Neru.

- Millennium Student B: That's gotta be it. So we'd better do what she wants and go the other way.

- Neru (C&C): Unless...

- Neru (C&C): ...Why do I wear it?

- Neru (C&C): ...

- Neru (C&C): So that's why I tensed up. But you're definitely not doing that.

- Neru (C&C): So, how should I explain it?
- Neru (C&C): Hm?

- Neru (C&C): No, it's not a secret, or really anything special. Honestly, I thought no one would believe me because the reason is so simple.
- Neru (C&C): But, if you really want to know, Sensei, I'll tell you.

- Neru (C&C): Was that what you were looking for?

- Neru (C&C): You thought it was to make up for being short, or...

- Neru (C&C): Usually they're trying to get into a fight, so they're not listening to the answer, or they don't want to push me when I say there's no reason.
- Neru (C&C): ...The truth must be pretty disappointing, though, huh?
- Neru (C&C): ...Oh!
- Neru (C&C): I'm...surprised. You'd really rather that answer than whatever wild story people make up?
- Neru (C&C): I literally just told you there was no meaning. Why would that be better?
- Neru (C&C): *sigh*
- Neru (C&C): Sensei, this...
- Neru (C&C): This is exactly why...
