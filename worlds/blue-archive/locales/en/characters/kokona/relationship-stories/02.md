> City Street
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): You bought another cup of coffee?

- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): So? I didn't say I don't like the smell.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I'm an instructor. I can handle a cup of coffee.

- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): So. I'll just go ahead and drink it now...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): One. Two. Three!

- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Ugh! Coffee is gross. I'd rather have ice cream.
