# Kokona — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Kokona/Relationship_Story/4
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> Downtown
> Kokona and I arrived at the carrot restaurant.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I can't believe you actually brought me here.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Or that a carrot restaurant is even a thing...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Whoa. They REALLY like carrots, don't they?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): They have carrots on the curtains, the stairs...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Even the sign is a giant carrot!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Sensei, we don't have to do this. It's not too late to leave.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): In fact, you're so determined that I'm starting to worry you've been brainwashed by Big Carrot!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): That's it! You've been brainwashed! Don't worry, Sensei! I'll save you!
> I laughed and reassured Kokona as we entered the restaurant together.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Wait! Wait!!! Please don't make me go in there! You can't do this to a proper lady!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I know you're trying to be nice, but really—
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Waaaahhhh!
> Carrotopia
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Ugh. Literally EVERY dish has carrots in it.
- Waiter: What would you like?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Umm...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): You should order, Sensei.
- "Who, me?"
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): You brought me here, which means you know what's good.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): So you should order for both of us.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I'll hate it no matter what, but at least I'd only kind of hate it.
> I smiled to myself and then ordered from the secret menu.
> The food came out quickly.
- "Dig in!"
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Okay... Ugh...
> Kokona reluctantly raised her fork to her mouth.
> She chewed for a moment. Then...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): It's so...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Sweet! And somehow savory at the same time? And it's soft!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): There's no way this is a carrot!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): So they steamed them to maintain the sweetness and fried them in butter... Huh!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): You want me to try this sauce? Okay...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Oooh! It's so tasty!
- Kokona: ― But...
- Kokona: ― Just so you know...I still hate carrots.
- Kokona: ― I'm just eating them for you...
- Kokona: ― ...and because I'm an instructor. It's not because I want to.
- Kokona: ― Understand?
- Kokona: ― It's not my fault you're so stubborn.
- Kokona: ― I guess...
- Kokona: ― If I absolutely have to eat them—
- Kokona: ― Carrots, I mean...
- Kokona: ― I'll come here.
- Kokona: ― This is the first place I ever visited with you.
- Kokona: ― So it's special now.
- Kokona: ― Know what I mean? It's not the carrots. It's the company.
- Kokona: ― Okay?
- Kokona: ― Anyway...
- Kokona: ― I guess... Carrots aren't as bad as I thought.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I ate the whole serving!
- "And? How was it?"
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): It was...good.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I guess you proved me wrong...this time.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): But someday I'll get you back for making me eat carrots.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Which means you have to hang out with me again.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Okay?
