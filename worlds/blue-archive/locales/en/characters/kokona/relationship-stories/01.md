- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Because I'm the instructor!

- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I—yes, but—
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Sensei, will you say something please?!

- Plum Blossom Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): Hey, Sensei said "Instructor Kokona"!

- Plum Blossom Student D (Plum Blossom Garden): Well, it's a little weird, but... I guess we should call her Instructor Kokona then.

- Plum Blossom Students (Plum Blossom Garden): Instructor Kokona?
- Plum Blossom Students (Plum Blossom Garden): Instructor Kokona!

- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Ahem! Now you all know.
