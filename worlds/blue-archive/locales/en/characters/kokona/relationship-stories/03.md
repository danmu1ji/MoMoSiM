# Kokona — Relationship Story 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Kokona/Relationship_Story/3

> Downtown
> I ran into Kokona while walking around downtown.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Oh! Hello, Sensei.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I'm glad I ran into you. I've got a lot on my mind right now.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I've been thinking about ways to get the students to call me Instructor Kokona.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): It helped a little when you talked to them, but they went back to just Kokona pretty quickly...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): But I hate carrots!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): But I hate carrots!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I don't care what you say.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I've never liked them, and I never will!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): It's not like I chose to hate them.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I just... How can people stand eating them?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): They're bitter. They smell weird. They're hard as rocks...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): They're not fit for human consumption.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): What even possessed people to eat them in the first place?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): It's a clear regression of culture.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): We must move forward. We must eat better than that. BE better than that.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Carrots are so...unsophisticated!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): And don't even get me started on bell peppers!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Not only are they bitter and smelly—just like carrots—but they're spicy too!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): What?!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): What?!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): What kind of choice is that?!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Carrots or bell peppers? That's it?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I'd rather starve than eat either!
- "Come now. You can't really think they're that bad..."
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Carrots. And bell peppers. Of course I can.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Ugh. I suppose if I HAD to choose...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Then...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): ...I'd pick carrots. Grudgingly.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): At least they aren't spicy like bell peppers...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Bleh. Why are you so determined to know?
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): You want me to eat carrots on purpose? And at a restaurant no less...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): You want me to eat carrots on purpose? And at a restaurant no less...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Look, I really appreciate the offer and the fact that you're willing to pay.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): But could we go somewhere else? There are nicer restaurants than that one.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): And I'd rather have something more sophisticated.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Why go to a carrot restaurant when we could have something better?
- "Kokona. You said you'd choose the carrot."
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I-I know I did, but...
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): That was only because you made me choose between carrots and bell peppers.
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): What—Why would you take me to a bell pepper restaurant instead?!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): Sensei, you're so mean!
- Kokona (Plum Blossom Garden): I thought you of all people would understand!
> Kokona burst into tears, and it was ages before I could calm her down.
