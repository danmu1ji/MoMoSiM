# Konoka\n\nA student from Valkyrie Police Academy and the Deputy Director of the Public Peace Bureau. ​Though her appearance sometimes leads people to mistake her for a suspect, she is a highly capable officer. She can trace the psychology and behavior of criminals better than anyone else in the Bureau, though she doesn't seem to be doing it consciously. ​Rumor has it that she is the student who has been promoted the most—and demoted the most—in the history of the Public Peace Bureau.\n\nSource: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Konoka\nReview status: source extracted; pending editorial review.\n
