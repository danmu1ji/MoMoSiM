# Konoka — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> I took a bus to the countryside village Konoka asked me to meet her at.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): Over here, Sensei!
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): Thanks for coming. I couldn't take any chances doing this solo after what my horoscope said.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): There's basic training today, so I couldn't bring anyone from Valkyrie.
- "What's the job?"
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): That's a good question! We'll be canvassing.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): Someone caused a bit of trouble in D.U. and took off. The last reported sightings of her placed her getting on a bus heading in this direction.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): I'm sure the suspect will stick out like a sore thumb, so a few friendly locals will be sure to point us in the right direction.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): Forecast said rain, so let's wrap this up with a bow before it starts coming down!
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): Let's get going then!
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): Blaaah. This sucks.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): We can't find a single witness! Maybe she grew wings and flew off into the horizon? Or burrowed deep underground?
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): It's too late to try to question any more people...
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): Maybe she went into mountains...? I WILL sniff her out, even if I have to rip out every tree here, one by one!
- "Think about the environment! The animals!"
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): Bah, humbug! I'll replant them after I find her! I'm still a law-abiding police officer.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): Before the trail gets too cold, we need to—
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): Eh?!
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): KYAAAH!! A cold downpour!
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): Let's take shelter over there!!! Gaaah, I can't believe this!!
> Konoka and I hurried to find shelter from the rain together.
> Konoka, who'd been moping in the corner from how the day had turned out, got up out of nowhere and took her jacket off.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― Ah, ah, I'm soaked.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― Ugh... I'm cold.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― It's really coming down.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― Apparently, it's not going to stop until later tonight. *sigh*
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― Well, I guess it's nice to take a beat and regroup.
- "Are you okay? Aren't you disappointed?"
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― Hm? Ah, well...
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― I'd be lying if I said I wasn't disappointed.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― I wasted my time, and I'm drenched...
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― But still, today went just like my horoscope said it would.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― It ended up being a nice day with a buddy by my side.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― ...It's all thanks to you.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― So, if I had to say...
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― I'm happy.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― ...Ah, wait, I mean!
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― It was really lucky that we found shelter from the rain so quickly! That's why I'm happy!
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ― Th-That was the...gist of it...
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ...
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): It's just a good thing that I didn't have any bad luck today!
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): That's all I meant...
- Suspicious Passerby: Whoa?! I didn't know someone was here already.
- Suspicious Passerby: My bad! Don't mean to intrude on y'all's private moment or nothin', but lemme scooch in and stay dry.
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): ...?
- Suspicious Passerby: You bein' chased too? Hah! Looks like we're in the same boat.
- Suspicious Passerby: You ain't seen any Valkyrie cops nearby, have ya?
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): Is this for real...?
- "I have, actually..."
- Suspicious Passerby: Uh...
- Suspicious Passerby: Ah, I suddenly want to go dancing in the rain!! The clouds are calling to me!!
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): Hold it right there!!
- Suspicious Passerby: C'MON!! There's no way you're a cop!! Aren't there rules to being undercover?!
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): You're getting an aggravated sentence!!
- Suspicious Passerby: What?! But WHY?!
- Konoka (Public Peace Bureau): For ruining a good time!!
> Konoka rushed through the rain and quickly arrested the suspect.
> The entire way back, she was staring quietly out the window.
