> In Front of Kivotos Arena
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): I bought everything we need. Let's see...
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): Hmm? What's with that big crowd over there?
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): Sensei, look over there! It's Peroro!
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): And Wavecat and Pinky-Paca too! I think they're doing a puppet show!
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): I never thought I'd see a Momo Friends puppet show in a place like this...
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): S-Sensei! Can we watch the show before we leave?
- "Fine with me."
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): You're the best, Sensei!
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): Sensei, look at that! The bad guy brainwashed Angry Adelie and they're about to attack Peroro!
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): Look out, Peroro!
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): *sigh* That was amazing!
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): My palms were all sweaty when Peroro took down the bad guy with his signature licks and catchphrase. "Peropero!"
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): I got to see Peroro live and in the feathers... This is the luckiest day of my life!
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): Wait a minute... Are they going to do an encore or something?
- Performance Staff: The line for the Peroro meet and greet starts here!
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): A chance to meet THE Peroro!
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): I could reach out and touch those fluffy wings myself...
- "Why not get in line?"
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): Ahaha... That sounds fun, but I'd feel so awkward.
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): Almost everyone in line is a little kid. Someone my age getting in line would look like a weirdo...
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): Seeing the puppet show was enough for me.
- "If Peroro is your one true love, then who cares what people think?"
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): True love...!
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): You have a point. I feel like I'd be betraying Peroro if I didn't go.
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): Let's go stand in line, Sensei!
- "You want me to come too?"
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): Of course! It's not every day you get the opportunity to meet Peroro!
- Hifumi (Make-Up Work Club): I promise he'll be fluffy and smell great!
> I stood in line with Hifumi and shook hands with the Peroro puppet.
