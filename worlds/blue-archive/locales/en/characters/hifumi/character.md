# Hifumi

## Description

A sensitive girl, Hifumi is a student of Trinity Integrated Academy and a member of the Supplementary Classes Club. Despite her average grades and appearance, Hifumi's gentle and kind personality has gained her popularity. While she will gladly lend an ear to those with problems, she has a tendency to be swept up in the moment and unintentionally cause trouble.
