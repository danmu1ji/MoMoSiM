- Children: Thank you!

- Children: She patched me up when I got hurt playing ball! She's the best!

- Serina (Remedial Knights): Helping people and putting smiles back on their faces...
- Serina (Remedial Knights): ...is all the satisfaction I need.
- Serina (Remedial Knights): I don't understand it entirely myself. Maybe it's just part of who I am.
- Serina (Remedial Knights): But enough about me. You've seemed fatigued lately. Are you okay?
- Serina (Remedial Knights): You're fine, huh? Nope—I don't think so!
- Serina (Remedial Knights): You can't hide it from me. You work too many hours, you skip meals to eat snacks...
- Serina (Remedial Knights): ...and you're always putting yourself in dangerous situations to help others. You're liable to collapse if you keep this up!
- Serina (Remedial Knights): I always carry first aid supplies, but that doesn't mean I want to use them.

- Serina (Remedial Knights): I'm so disappointed in myself.
- Serina (Remedial Knights): But I told you that being an adult doesn't mean I can neglect you!
- Serina (Remedial Knights): You say you understand, but you still...
- Serina (Remedial Knights): It's not much, but would you like to have this? It's apple juice.

- Serina (Remedial Knights): Well, then. I'm happy to be of service.
- Serina (Remedial Knights): ...Heehee.
