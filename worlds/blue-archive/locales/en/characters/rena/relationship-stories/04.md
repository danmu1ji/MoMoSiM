> It was the afternoon.
> I headed to the Occult Society's dorm with the materials Rena asked for...and a chocolate-banana crepe.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Ah. Welcome, Sensei.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): The others are out right now.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): They're probably doing something silly, like trying to find occult symbols in a mural, or something.
- "Why didn't you join them?"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): And pass up an opportunity to get some work done in peace?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): That was my plan...until I saw that the fabric shop was closed while they scrub off the unauthorized "artwork."
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Seriously, can anyone be normal at Wildhunt? I can't believe I have to survive three more years here.
- "Here! Maybe this chocolate-banana crepe will cheer you up."
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I told you not to, but you bought it anyway.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Well, thank you...
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Hmm! Th-This is good! Where'd you get it from?
- "Well, I didn't buy it. I made it myself."
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): What? R-Really?!
- "What do you think? It's good, right?"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Yeah... I-It's good...
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ...
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): It's...definitely...good.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Shyaah! I don't know! Why do you have to spring all these questions? What is this, the inquisition?! Idiot Sensei!
> Despite her angry outburst, Rena happily finished the crepe.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Mmm. Now that I've had some sugar, I can start working.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): A ribbon hair clip.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Well... It's not like I LOVE them...
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I had one at first, then I added another for balance...and then I ended up covered in them before I even realized it.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Now, I feel sort of "off" if I'm not wearing a bunch of them when I go out.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Shyaah! Shut up! It's not like that!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): This is all about the fashion! Fashion!
- "Why are you making more when you're already wearing so many?"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Since they're so small, I tend to lose a few if I'm out all day. So...I make a few spares, just to be safe.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I'm going to start working now, so you should start on your work, too.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Didn't you come here today because you had some business to take care of?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ...Huh?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Th-This is mundane work... So it might be pretty boring to watch.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): But if you still want to observe...
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): F-Fine. Feel free to.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Just don't bother me, okay?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― You know, I've always been good with my hands, ever since I was a kid.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― I would make things like ribbons and give them to my friends.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― I was happy to see that everyone liked them.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― ...And that's how I learned
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― that even something small
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― can make someone feel happy.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― That's why I started dreaming of becoming a fashion designer.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― Because it makes me happy to see others enjoy
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― the clothes and accessories I design.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― Sensei, you know how that feels, right?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― I think that's also why you keep looking out for me.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― Huh...?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― How was today...?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ― Shyaah! D-Don't make me say it! Idiot Sensei!
> Afterward, I kept talking with Rena as she crafted ribbons.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Ah... Look at the time.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Sensei. Don't you have to get going? It's getting dark.
- "I should head out soon."
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Yeah. Good luck with your work...and everything.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Ah, right. Before you leave...
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Here. Come closer.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Heehee. This ribbon looks good on you!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Consider it a gift! For keeping me company!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): H-Huh? You don't have to go that far...
- "But it's the first ribbon Rena ever made for me."
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ...!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Wh-Whatever! Keep it on, if you must!
> As Rena insisted, I kept wearing the ribbon while I worked...
> ...continuously thinking of how happy she looked when she clipped it to me.
