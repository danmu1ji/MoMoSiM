# Rena — Relationship Story 2

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> I went to the meeting spot late at night.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): O-Over here! Sensei!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Well, the thing is...
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Starting now...we're going to...
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ...investigate a school. An abandoned one. And people say it has ghosts.
- "A haunted school?!"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I-I have to!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I told my seniors, the Occult Society, that I would prove that ghosts don't exist.
- "That sounds up their alley. Why didn't they tag along?"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I told them not to.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I'm sure they would pull some stupid prank again and try to scare me.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Also... I'm the only one with an approved outing pass.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): But well... It's so late. And it's a little sketchy, you know. So I figured I shouldn't go alone...
- "So you called me?"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Well, I doubt anything will happen, but still. No ghosts will show up.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): If you want to pay me back for your..."pants incident," this would help.
- "Sure! I'll protect you!"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): *sigh* How did it come to this...?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Ah. There's the bus.
> It dropped us off in a secluded area.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): The abandoned school is down this street.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): It could be a coincidence, but the rumors say that its halls are plagued with mysterious screaming...and have been since the day the school opened.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ...They say you can still hear the screams, even though there are no more students.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): A few kids have even passed out while exploring it. That's why no one goes over here anymore.
- "Such diligent research."
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): N-Naturally! You should always research where you're going.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): We should get to it. Come on, let's prove that there's no such thing as ghosts!
> We entered the abandoned school.
- "Huh... Isn't this place a little creepy?"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): This school is said to house the "Seven Mysteries."
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): First is loud knocking on the window in the middle of the night.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Second: there's a thirteenth stair that...also doesn't exist.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Third is an eerie shadow wandering around the faculty hallway.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): A bloodstained cherry blossom pressed against a classroom window—that's the fourth mystery.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Fifth: the broken classroom speakers play the sound of a ringing bell.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Sixth, which we've already talked about—is general suspicious screaming originating from the basement's mechanical room.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): And the seventh mystery...
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): It's so mysterious that no one knows what it is.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): They say that if you so much as LEARN what this mystery is, you'll be inflicted with a terrible curse.
- "These sound like pretty typical ghost stories."
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): R-Right? Just...everyday ghost stories, right?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): And that's why I'll investigate all these silly rumors, take some photos, and show the others.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): And, because NOTHING will happen, I'll finally prove that ghosts don't—
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ...?!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Wh-Wh-What the...?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Eek...!
- "Loud knocking on the window! In the middle of the night...!"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): B-But we're the only ones here!
- "Let's check it out."
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ...!!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I-I wasn't worried!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): F-Fine! L-Let's go see!
- Crow: Caw! Caaw...!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Huh, what? It was just a crow?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Why was it pecking at the window? It's not like there's any food in here.
- "What if it wasn't the crow? What if it was actually a ghost...?!"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Shyaah! No way!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Let's take a photo. I need evidence that all this uproar was just a bored crow looking for snacks.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Here. How's that look? I didn't mess it up or anything, right?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): One down. Let's keep it up and wrap up the alleged Seven Mysteries!
> Despite her enthusiasm, Rena never let go of my hand...
- "(I shouldn't let go of hers, either...)"
> Rena and I investigated all the mysteries.
> The suspicious shadow slinking around the hallway...
> The classroom with "so-called" bloodied cherry blossoms...
> A scream-free basement mechanical room...
> We visited all the places mentioned in the Seven Mysteries and collected photographic evidence.
> As expected, not a single ghost appeared.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Phew, that's what I thought. I knew there were no such thing as ghosts.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): They were all misunderstandings originating from stray animals or run-down facilities.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Good! Since we have everything, we should head back. I can't wait to show my seniors!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Th-That's...
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ...n-not because I'm scared, or anything!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I-It's to stop us from falling! It's dark in here! Someone could trip.
- "Yeah. You wouldn't want to get injured here."
> I safely escorted Rena back to Wildhunt.
> And, just as I thought things had wrapped up smoothly...
