> As promised, I arrived at Wildhunt that afternoon.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Ah, Sensei.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): You really came.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): How are you feeling?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): You know... Are you sleeping all right? Any sudden shivers or...night terrors?
- "Nothing like that. Why?"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): It's nothing... I was just curious.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): My roommates keep yapping about the "consequences" of seeing a ghost, signs of being haunted, things like that.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): *sigh* How annoying.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Here.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): It's an omamori I made. You know, a protective amulet.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I was just going to buy one, but my seniors insisted that I make one myself.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I don't know if it'll protect you from anything, since I made it, and I don't believe in the occult, OBVIOUSLY, but it can't hurt, right?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Take it.
- "Oh, this is so intricate. Did you embroider this yourself?"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Of course. It was easy.
- "I really like it! I'll treasure it forever!"
- "I feel guilty just taking this from you. Would you like a...treat, perhaps?"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Are you buying? Ha...
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Yeah! There's actually a snack I've been eager to try.
- "Like a...chocolate-banana crepe?"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): ...H-How did you know?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I don't crave it all the time, you know?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I-I just happened to be in the mood today!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Really, Sensei? You also enjoy chocolate-banana crepes?
- "Yes! Just like you!"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): U-Ugh... I-I knew it. You're teasing me...
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Well, whatever. Let's go.
> And just as I was about to enter the store...
> ...I stepped on something. I pulled my shoe up to see a massive piece of gum.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Gross... That's a big one.
> I quickly scraped off the sticky goo.
- "L-Let's go inside."
> Screen shakes
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Wh-What was that?!
- Cafe Manager: Sensei... It says "PULL."
- Cafe Manager: I'm sorry, but you need to cover the replacement cost.
- "I-I'm so sorry!"
> After paying the damages, we tried to buy the chocolate-banana crepes.
> ...But they sold out right before we arrived.
> And, as we departed the cafe in disappointment...
> ...I slipped and fell on a banana peel someone left on the sidewalk!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): What's going on...? Why are all these bad things happening?
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Sensei. Hand me the omamori I gave you earlier. Something's off.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): A-Ahh...!!!
- "What's wrong?"
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I-I made them in bulk... A-And I think I gave you an empty one! It was a mistake!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I knew something was weird. No wonder you've been cursed with bad luck!
- "So you don't believe in the occult...but you DO believe in omamori."
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I-It's just in case! Only a precaution.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): I'm so sorry I gave you an empty one.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Hold on for a minute. I'll get you a new one. A good one!
> A short while later...
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Here...! This one's fine! Take it!
> I felt something inside it, but I couldn't tell what it was.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Just in case you didn't know...you can't peek inside.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): They say that an omamori loses its power once you open it.
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): G-Got it? No matter what, you CANNOT OPEN IT.
- "Okay! I understand!"
> To test it out, Rena and I tried going to a different cafe.
> They were selling chocolate-banana crepes!
> And the chef said that they only recently added them to their menu!
- Rena (Occult Investigation Society): Wonderful. Thank you, Sensei!
> Perhaps my luck had turned around.
