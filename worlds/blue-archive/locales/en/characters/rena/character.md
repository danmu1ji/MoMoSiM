# Rena

## Description

A member of the Occult Investigation Society at Wildhunt Art Academy, which studies the paranormal aspects of art. She was assigned to room 404 in building 4, which led to her involvement in the Occult Investigation Society's activities. While she claims anything paranormal is a sham, it seems she herself is unknowingly becoming immersed in the occult world.

## Appearance

Rena has shoulder-length light-brown hair styled in twin tails with yellow accents and small ribbons, each tied with a large black bow.
