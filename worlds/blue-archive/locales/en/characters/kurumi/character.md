# Kurumi

## Description

The pointwoman for the SRT Special Academy's FOX Squad, callsign "FOX3". Kurumi possesses excellent physical ability and strong on-site adaptability, specializing in combat as the squad's pointwoman. She has intense emotional swings and a straightforward personality, but those traits have turned to her advantage in countless operations. Though she rarely speaks of it, she harbors a complex about her physique.
