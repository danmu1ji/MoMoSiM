> FOX Squad HQ
- "Is everyone in their seats?"
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Wait, [USERNAME] Sensei?
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): I don't think there were any planned operations today... What are you doing at HQ?
- "I received a gift from the farm with the seawater issue today."
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Oh, thanks! There are plenty of uses for potatoes.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): You can roast them, boil them, fry them... They're extremely versatile.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): I'll share them with the other squad members.
- "Anyway, are you alone today?"
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Yeah. Everyone's away on personal business.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Did you have some business with them?
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Ugh! Don't say gross things, and completely unprompted too!
- "So what were you up to, Kurumi?"
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Huh? As you can see, I've been working on my shield.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): I've been busy lately, so it's been getting a little beat up.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): I'll work out the scratches with an abrasive, and then repaint it.
- "I see. You're hard at work."
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): It feels a bit unnecessary, repainting it like this all the time, doesn't it?
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Shields were made to block things. It's a given that it's going to get dinged up and scratched.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Regardless, I always like to clean my shield before our next mission.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): It's the first thing an opponent sees during an operation, right?
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): This shield right here is like the first impression our squad makes, so I want to make sure it's up to standard whenever I deploy to the field.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Honestly speaking, this isn't the kind of thing you notice when you're in the thick of battle...
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): So it's mostly just my own self-indulgence.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): R-Right? I knew you'd understand, Sensei!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): I need to display my conviction with confidence, or I won't feel confident. Even in front of my enemies!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): So sometimes I even put stickers on it, to show off a little.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Look. I got this sticker for free from a magazine. Isn't it cute?
- "Oh, it's cute!"
- "Come to think of it, I have a sticker I won as a prize, too."
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Aw! It's a little cat sticker.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): How cute...
- "If you want to, you can put it on your—"
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): There's no way I could put this on my shield!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): You want me to put this adorable little cat on my shield, so it can be barraged by a rainstorm of bullets? Are you actually completely heartless, Sensei?
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): The only impression the FOX Squad will leave with this sticker on my shield is that we're a bunch of ruthless monsters!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ...I refuse.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): You gave it to me, so I won't give it back.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): I have plenty of other places to stick it on other than my shield. Like my diary or military notebook...
- "Kurumi. By any chance..."
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): No! I-I definitely don't want to keep it safe because it's technically a gift from you!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): That's not it! Just... Just look at this cat! It's adorable!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Don't let your ego get ahead of you! You big idiot!
