# Kurumi — Relationship Story 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Kurumi/Relationship_Story/3

> Inside a subway station near Schale
> I'd finished up some work and had to take the subway to get back to Schale.
> At the subway station, I saw Kurumi taking pictures in a self-service booth.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Ah... Don't be nervous...
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): One, two...
> ...For some reason, Kurumi looked extremely anxious as she tried to take her pictures.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ...Kyaaah!
- Walk up to Kurumi.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ― Ugh, bah!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ― Wh-What? When did you get here, Sensei?
- "Why are you so nervous in front of the camera...?"
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ― I-I'm not! I'm not nervous at all!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ― It's just... I'm having a bit of a tough time getting the timing right...
- Announcer Voice: ― Look over here... One. Two. Three.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ― H-Haha.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ― Ugh... I closed my eyes again.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ― For some reason, the counting makes me nervous.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ― N-No, wait. I never get nervous!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ― I just closed my eyes without thinking...
- Announcer Voice: ― "Look over here"
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ― Here I go...!
- Announcer Voice: ― "One. Two. Three."
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ― Haha.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ― Why?! Why do I keep
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ― closing my eyes?!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Ugh, how many times have I failed so far?
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): I don't have much cash left...
- "Do you think the counting is making you close your eyes?"
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): The counting isn't the problem... I think the problem is thinking the flash is going to go off.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): As point woman, I have to handle flash attacks a lot. The shield I use also has a flash device on it.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): I've always been trained to turn my head and close my eyes when I think a flash is coming.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): I guess muscle memory is kicking in.
- "Does this happen when you're taking pictures normally?"
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): No, it's really only when I start THINKING about the flash going off.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): With a booth like this, you know the flash is about to go off, and it tells you exactly when it's going to happen.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): So it instinctively puts me on edge.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Ugh... This is why I didn't want to use a self-service photo booth.
- "Kurumi, can you stand in front of that wall for a moment?"
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Wall? Like this...?
- "No, get in front of that white wall, right by it."
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): I don't really understand what standing in front of a wall is going to do.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): This doesn't solve my problem at—
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ...Gah!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Wh-What are you doing? You just took that photo of me on your phone without my consent!
- "Sorry, sorry. I should've warned you."
- "But then the photo might not have come out so well."
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): I doubt it did! Especially because I wasn't ready for it!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ...Huh? Oh. It looks nice.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): What do you mean "pretty"...?!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Don't think that smooth talking is going to get you out of this one! What are you planning to do with that picture?
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): What? You should've told me about this method sooner!
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): ...No. It's partially my fault for not explaining in detail.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Anyway, today turned out to be a waste of time.
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Wasted money and time...
- "Yet I feel like I've come out ahead."
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): How did you come out ahead?
- "I got a photo of Kurumi."
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Th-That's...
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): Ugh... Aughhh...
- Kurumi (FOX Squad): That photo. If you use it for anything other than an ID photo, don't think I'll go easy on you!
> After, I returned to Schale with a curiously shy Kurumi.
