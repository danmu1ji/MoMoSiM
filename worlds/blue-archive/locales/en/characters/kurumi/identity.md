# Kurumi\n\nThe pointwoman for the SRT Special Academy's FOX Squad, callsign "FOX3". Kurumi possesses excellent physical ability and strong on-site adaptability, specializing in combat as the squad's pointwoman. She has intense emotional swings and a straightforward personality, but those traits have turned to her advantage in countless operations.
