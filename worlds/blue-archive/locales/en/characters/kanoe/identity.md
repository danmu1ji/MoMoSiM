# Kanoe\n\nA member of the Occult Research Society at Wildhunt Art Academy, Kanoe explores the occult that resides in art. Despite being the only third-year student in the Occult Research Society, she is not the president, opting instead to back other members. Rumors circulate that she herself may be an occult being due to her suspicious appearance and behavior.\n\nSource: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Kanoe\nReview status: source extracted; pending editorial review.\n
