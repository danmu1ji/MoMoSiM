> Gehenna Academy Classroom
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Still not enough...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I have to try harder...!
- "Did something happen during lunch?"
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Oh, Sensei.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): No. It's not that I was unhappy with the food. The cake was rather delicious, and they had so many different kinds...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): The real problem is...I couldn't eat nearly as much as my clubmates!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I was full after my third serving, but Akari shoveled down dozens of plates' worth of cake!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): What if it had been a buffet I paid to enter? If I couldn't eat nearly as much as the others, I'd be getting less value for my yen.
- "I'm more concerned about the buffet owner's wallet..."
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): This isn't fair! I want to eat as much as everyone else!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): What should I do, Sensei?
- "Then, you'll just have to train your stomach!"
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Train my stomach? Hmm... By nature, the stomach can expand to accommodate large amounts of food...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): So in theory, consistently overeating would train it like a muscle and I'd be able to eat as much as I want!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): But what would be a good training food for this?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Hmm... I know! Dried kelp will do the trick.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I can eat dried kelp endlessly, and since it's low in calories, I don't have to worry about gaining weight!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Let's start with a little test run...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Ugh...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I've never been so stuffed with kelp. I think I'm going to be sick. It feels like my stomach is going to explode.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I can't do this, Sensei...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Bleughhh!
> I proceeded to pat Junko's back until she was done throwing up all the kelp she ate.
