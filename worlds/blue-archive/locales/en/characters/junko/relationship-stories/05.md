> Chinatown Street near Shanhaijing Academy
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Whew! That was so good.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): This street lives up to the hype. Everything here is so delicious!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Fried rice, char siu, meat dumplings... They're all winners!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): It was worth coming all the way here for. Heehee.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Huh? Don't I want to take a break after all of that?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): No way! We haven't even gotten to half of the restaurants!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): If we don't pick up the pace, we won't be able to taste every dish by the end of the day!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Still...I do feel a little queasy after all of that greasy food.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Okay. Why don't we head to a cafe for a break?
- Junko: ― Huh? Wh-What? Why are you looking at me like that?
- Junko: ― (Seriously...)
- Junko: ― We're going to the spots after we're done taking a break, right? What should we eat next?
- Junko: ― Delicious! ♪
- Junko: ― What?! Drink it together?
- Junko: ― Don't be silly! Do you have a death wish?
- Junko: ― (Sensei must be teasing me, right? Right? There's only one straw...)
- Junko: ― (But if it's with Sensei...maybe I wouldn't mind...)
- Junko: ― Forget it! No juice for you!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Ah... That was good!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Today was the best. I got to have so much good food!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Being with you made it taste even better...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): W-Wait, don't get the wrong idea! I can eat delicious food by myself like it's nothing!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): But I'll admit that...it tastes better...with someone else there with me.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Will you grab a bite with me again sometime?
- "Of course."
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): You will? Then it's a promise! I'll hold you to that!
> I then returned to school with an excited Junko in tow.
