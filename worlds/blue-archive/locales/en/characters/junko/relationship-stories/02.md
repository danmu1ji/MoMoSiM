# Junko — Relationship Story 2

> Gehenna Academy Dining Hall
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): You're late, Sensei!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I offer you delectable food, and you have the nerve to be ten minutes late? This is death penalty-worthy!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): You're lucky that letting you live is the only way you'll be able to try what I brought.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): You're pardoned just this once, but it's the firing line next time!
- "Didn't you say something about seafood when you invited me?"
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I hope you weren't thinking I meant real seafood. I can't afford that with my kind of paycheck.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): What we have here today is...a tasty substitute that's going to deceive your taste buds!
- "And how is it going to do that?"
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): The human tongue is very sensitive. Surely you know what it's like to experience a new flavor by combining several different ingredients.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): According to my online research, it should be possible to imitate the flavor of expensive seafood by combining much cheaper ingredients.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Allow me to introduce you to...imitation sea urchin eggs!
- "That's just...soy sauce and pudding from the convenience store?"
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Exactly. From what I read, pudding with soy sauce on top tastes just like sea urchin eggs.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Don't ask me how or why...but if the combo works, it works! I'm sure we're in for an interesting flavor!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Here, Sensei, you try it first!
- Take a bite of the pudding smeared with soy sauce.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): How is it? Can you taste the sea urchin egg?
- "...I'm not sure."
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): That's weird. People swore it tasted just like it... *slurp*
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): ...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Salty... Sweet... Weird...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): What the heck?! This doesn't taste anything like sea urchin eggs!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Ugh... Why would anyone go on the internet and lie? Who does that?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Th-That's okay! I still have plenty of recipes to try!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Here's the second one on deck.
- "Avocado?"
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Same basic concept. They say if you eat avocado with soy sauce on it, it should taste like tuna sashimi!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Avocado is fatty just like tuna is, so this one HAS to work!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): All right... Here goes nothing!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): ...I feel like an idiot.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I've been bamboozled! What the heck was THAT?! None of this tastes like what people said it would!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Avocado and soy sauce for tuna sashimi, tangerine and soy sauce for salmon roe... They all come out tasting gross!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): The only person who'd like this stuff is Izumi!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): *sigh* All I wanted was luxury seafood at an affordable price...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Wait. You think I could have afforded seafood with the money I used on all those different ingredients?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Let me do the math... Pudding set, soy sauce, avocados, tangerines...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): You're right! That comes out to the same price as going to a conveyor belt sushi bar!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I got tricked again! Why would people go on the internet and just tell lies? Who does that?!
