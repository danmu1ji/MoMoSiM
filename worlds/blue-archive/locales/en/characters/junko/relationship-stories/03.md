> Schale Executive Office
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I'm back...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Ugh. That investigation was the worst. I was looking forward to going to a buffet with all-you-can-eat wagyu beef.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Nobody told me that they're closed today! How was I supposed to know?!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I skimped out on lunch to make room for bulgogi. Ugh, I'm starving...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Would you happen to have anything to eat, Sensei? I'm so hungry...
- "I just so happen to have some food right here..."
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): You do? Let me see... Mitoshin's chicken ramen?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I've heard of this brand but never tried it. This is the instant ramen you put in a cup and pour hot water over, right?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I know I said I was starving, but I'm supposed to be a gourmet. Eating cheap instant food would be a little...
- "No one's forcing you to. It's fine."
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I-I never said I didn't want it! Give it back!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Hmm. Put the noodles in a cup, pour boiling water, and wait for three minutes...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): It doesn't even have a broth base or garnish. It can't possibly be tasty...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): (What in the...?! It's delicious!)
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): (The fried noodles have an al dente texture. I can't believe my taste buds...)
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): (Not to mention the combination of noodles and sauce DO make for a savory broth... It exceeds my wildest expectations!)
- "So? What do you think?"
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I-It's edible, I suppose! Even if it falls short of the lofty standards of a gourmet like myself! H-Haha!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Not to mention it was hardly enough to be considered filling. You wouldn't happen to have more, would you?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): D-Don't get the wrong idea! I'm not asking for more because I like it or anything!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): This is purely because I require calories! Don't you dare get it twisted!
> Junko would go on to finish another two chicken ramen cups after that.
