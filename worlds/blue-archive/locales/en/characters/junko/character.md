# Junko

## Description

A prim yet gluttonous member of Gehenna Academy's Cuisine Research Society. Differing from her other club members who often overeat everyday food, Junko is one to take part in a certain extent of enjoying reasonable quality cuisine. However she is known to lose her sense of reason when delicious foods are placed in front of her.

> I decided to visit a sweets shop on a drowsy afternoon.
> L'Oiseau Bleu Confectionery
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Welcome to L'Oiseau Bleu!
- "Junko?"
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Sensei? What in the world are you doing here?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): You just came to buy snacks? This place is pretty pricey for an impulse purchase, though...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I guess that's the advantage of being an adult with a credit line.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): You don't know how good you have it. Adults can have dessert whenever they want!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): What do you mean me? What am I doing here?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Isn't it obvious? I work here part-time.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I need it to fund my spending habits. Breakfast, lunch, dinner, not to mention all the snacks between meals...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Why do high-quality ingredients have to be so expensive? How is a student supposed to feed herself?!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I don't work part-time because I want to...but that's the price I have to pay for epicureanism.
- "Is it hard work?"
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Not particularly. It's not like I'm the one doing the baking.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Besides, L'Oiseau Bleu lets its employees take the leftover sweets home.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): So I get to eat sweets I'd never be able to afford for free. What more of a reason do I need to work here?
- "So any recommendations?"
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Recommendations? Let's see...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): The whole menu is good if you ask me, but my personal favorite is the Mont Blanc right in front of you.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): We don't sell many due to the price, but it's worth trying for the rich flavor of creamy chestnut paste in harmony with the crispy meringue!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): I was lucky enough to get a leftover for free...and it changed my life!
- "One Mont Blanc, please."
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Great choice. You won't regret it!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Come again? If I sell too many, there won't be any leftovers for me to have at the end of the day, and then I'd be taking a net loss?
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Th-That's a good point. In that case, I should slack off so that there are more leftovers...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): No, that's not right! If I don't make any sales, then I won't get paid and can't afford to buy the other foods I want!
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Hmm. But I do want those leftovers. What should I do...?
- Hand Junko the Mont Blanc you purchased.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Huh...? But you bought this for yourself, Sensei.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): You want me to take it as a gift for working so hard? Th-Thanks...
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): Still, I know it's not right to slack off and take advantage of people's kindness.
- Junko (Gourmet Research Society): So I'll work extra hard today as my way of paying you back!
> I then bought some bread to snack on while I returned to Schale.
