# Izuna — official English introduction

A member of Ninjutsu Research Club of Hyakkiyako. Her dream is "to become the greatest Ninja of Kivotos". Because of pursuing a dream not many people can reason with, Izuna is often seen alone despite her bright and cheerful personality. In her pursuit to become a proper ninja, she continues to refer to Sensei as "Lord" while "escorting" him (simply tailing Sensei around Kivotos) and devoting herself to various other activities.
