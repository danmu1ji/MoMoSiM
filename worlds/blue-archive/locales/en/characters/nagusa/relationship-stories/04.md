# Nagusa — Relationship Story 4

> Early in the morning, at Schale, I sat at my desk, doing my work and waiting for the student rep.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Apologies for my lateness, Sensei.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I got a bit lost on my way here...
- "Welcome, Nagusa."
- "...Before we get started, are you feeling sick or anything?"
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): No... I'm all right.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): (grimace) I'm all right. What can I help with?
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Nothing happened, I merely... I stepped off the train at the wrong station on the way here, and got a bit lost, as I said.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): And it's...quite sunny out there.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): No, no. I came here to help, not to cause you more troub—
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...Excuse me. I'll take a small break before I start, then. Just a small one.
> I brought Nagusa a cold drink, and kept an eye on her until she felt better.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Phew... I feel like myself again.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Thank you, Sensei. I do feel much better now.
- "Happy to help."
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Well, not "heat advisory" hot, per se, but...
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Well...hot weather is a weakness of mine.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Cold weather I can stand, but summers always make me sick.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): When I was younger, people often called me "the Snow Maiden."
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Flattering, in a sense. The myths always describe the Snow Maiden as a mysterious beauty. I suppose I could see the resemblance.
- "Heh, you really are feeling better."
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): All the same, I never liked those stories.
- "Why not?"
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Because they never end happily.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): The Snow Maiden always tries to express her affection to someone, but no matter how strongly she feels it, no matter how poetic her words, that someone always rejects her because of her cold appearance.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): So I didn't always appreciate being compared to a lovelorn maiden who vanishes or melts when the world rejects her.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Even if the stories are...true in a sense.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): When winter comes, and the ground is barren, all most people can feel about it is...longing for the summer sun.
- "...That drink, is it still cold?"
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Indeed. You put in so much ice, it's still chilling my hands.
- "On days like this, ice is important."
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I don't disagree, but why do you bring that up?
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...Perhaps you're right. I've never heard stories of a Snow Maiden living through the summer.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): But here I am. And perhaps I'm not without hope for it.
