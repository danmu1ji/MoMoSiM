> Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council Club Room
> The next day, I arrived at the Hyakkaryouran club room early, as my work at Hyakkiyako ended earlier than expected.
> Behind the club room, in the courtyard, I found Nagusa training hard.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Hup... Haa!
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...Oh, Sensei.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I haven't kept you waiting, have I?
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Forgive me. I was immersed in my training.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I see... Very good, then.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Though you've caught me in a bit of a disheveled state...
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): If you're willing to wait, I could stop and wash up.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): But...
- "I'm curious about the famous 'Hyakkaryouran Marksmanship.'"
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Curious, you say?
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Well, there's nothing extraordinary about our training techniques.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): We simply shoot, replace the target, and repeat. It may not be exciting to watch.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...Sadly, I lack my predecessors' talent at it.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): So you may come away disappointed, Sensei.
- "It's okay. You don't have to impress me."
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...Thank you for saying so.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Very well...allow me to demonstrate our basic forms.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Oh, I don't recommend you watch from behind, on account of all the stray bullets.
- "Stray bullets?"
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Well, as the name implies, the Resolution Council often takes on the task of resolving disputes between students. That often pits a single one of us against a full mob.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): And of course, this is Kivotos, where most people own guns. A shot could come from anywhere at any time.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Hyakkaryouran Marksmanship was developed over the years to counter that.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Through its forms, one can evade and snipe multiple enemies at once.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Essentially, the goal is to calculate the enemy's line of fire, slip around it, sight each target, and respond with precise, rapid shots.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Of course, enemies may come from behind, or from out of sight, so we prepare for that as well.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I know little of kung fu, myself...though if I ever have the chance to visit Shanhaijing, I would love to learn more.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Now, shall I begin?
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Let us suppose that the four targets around me are enemies, and shoot them in order.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I do recommend you keep your distance.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): *inhale*
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...Haa!
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Was that acceptable? Pardon me if it seemed a bit unpolished. I don't often perform for an audience.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ..."How?" I simply shot them as they approached. Why do you ask?
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Well, to explain step by step...
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): The enemy at twelve o'clock moved first, so I sidestepped and fired at three o'clock, then turned and fired under my armpit to neutralize the enemy at nine o'clock.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I then executed a quarter turn and threw my gun, neutralizing the enemy behind me. That caused them to fumble their rifle, so I simply flipped into the air, caught it, shot the enemy at twelve o'clock while upside down, then landed.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Four enemies neutralized.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Of course, that method only works for that particular formation. There are other techniques, depending on the enemy's number and position.
- "...And you call that 'simple.'"
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Oh, no need to flatter me, Sensei.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Though your compliment does give me a certain...well, rush, one might say.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): We have time, if you'd like to see a few more examples.
> I continued to watch Nagusa practice basic Hyakkaryouran Marksmanship.
> None of which was basic at all.
