> I ended up visiting the Market Alley festival at Hyakkiyako.
- Merchant A: Try our special-sauce yakisoba! We also have okonomiyaki!
- Merchant B: Melon bread, filled with real Ebisu melon cream!
- Merchant C: Special cotton candy! Chise's favorite!
- Hyakkiyako Club Member A (Rakugo Fitness Club): The Rakugo Fitness Club will be holding a performance in the plaza soon. Come join us!

> I enjoyed various performances and street food. Before I knew it, it was already evening.
> Just before closing time, I ran into Nagusa, who was organizing materials near the back of the festival grounds.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Ah, [USERNAME] Sensei. You're here.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Did you enjoy the festival?
- "Yeah, thanks to you, I had a fun time."
- "By the way, I didn't see you out there at all today."
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): You wouldn't have. I was so busy grilling skewers and moving things about, I hardly left the stall.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): But it was worth it to see the customers enjoy the food I'd made for them.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Everyone worked their hardest today to make it all a success.
- "Still, you deserved to enjoy it too."
- Merchant B: Oh, my. Thank you for all your hard work today, Miss President!
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Uhm, I'm actually...
- Merchant B: Oh, are you the vice president? Well, regardless, we couldn't have done it without you. Would've been a disaster!
- Merchant B: When our grill volunteer called in sick, I thought we'd have to sit the whole festival out.
- Merchant B: But thanks to you, we got to serve all kinds of customers.
- Merchant B: And your culinary skills are amazing. You said it was your first time? Did you learn it from somewhere?
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I hadn't made skewers before, but I do enjoy eating them.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Honestly, mine weren't nearly as good as some others I've had. You have my apologies for that.
- Merchant B: Haha! And you're humble on top of it all.
- Merchant B: Oh, hey, I almost forgot to tell you...
- Merchant B: We're having an afterparty. If you don't have any plans, would you like to join us?
- Merchant B: Everyone's excited that it went so well today, after all the work we put into it.
- Merchant B: It'd be even more fun with you there, Nagusa!
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): In that case...I...
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...Oh, I'm sorry. I do have plans. I don't think I can make it.
- Merchant B: You do? Ah, well, that's a shame.
- Merchant B: In that case, please drop by our shop later when you can. The skewers are on us!
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I'll certainly look forward to that.
- "You have other plans?"
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Truthfully...no.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I lied. I simply didn't wish to attend the party.
- "Why? Is there something bad going on?"
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): No, not at all. In fact, I enjoy that sort of rowdy atmosphere.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): But...my own rather cold demeanor is not the best fit for it.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I rarely know what to say, and people often misread my expressions and take my coldness personally.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): When it comes to helping other people enjoy themselves, I'm...inexperienced at best.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): In that kind of atmosphere, warm as it is, I would only lower the temperature. So I declined.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Hm?!
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): B-But...
- "You don't need experience to express your feelings."
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...I hadn't thought of it that way.
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Would you pardon me, Sensei?
- Nagusa (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Let me catch up with them and ask if I can still join.
- "Of course! Go for it. Enjoy!"
> With a bit of a spring in her step, Nagusa left and followed the laughter.
