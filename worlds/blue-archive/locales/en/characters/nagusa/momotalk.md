## MomoTalk 3

- Nagusa: Sensei.
- Nagusa: There is a Market Alley Festival being held in Hyakkiyako this weekend. Will you attend?
- Sensei: Barring work, I'll be there!
- Sensei: The merchant guild asked me to be there!
- Nagusa: I see.
- Nagusa: Then perhaps our paths will cross.
- Sensei: The merchants requested my assistance.
- Sensei: Awesome! What're you going to be doing?
- Nagusa: I will be...grilling skewers at a food stall.
- Nagusa: They're apparently lacking in cooking staff.
- Sensei: Oooh! Nagusa-grilled skewers! I need some!
- Nagusa: They'll be selling a large variety of street food.
- Nagusa: I have high hopes that it will be an invigorating festival for all.
- Sensei: And I hope you enjoy yourself as well, Sensei.
- Nagusa: Thank you, Sensei.
- Nagusa: Thanks to your efforts, I had an enjoyable time speaking with people in the shopping district.
- Sensei: Sounds like it was a lot of fun!
- Nagusa: Yes.
- Sensei: I spoke from the heart, as you directed, and my words were well-received.
- Nagusa: In fact, they all burst into laughter as I spoke.
- Sensei: Even though I did not tell a joke.
- Sensei: What did you say?
- Nagusa: I may appear to be a mysterious, cool, unapproachable beauty, but please treat me as you would anyone else.
- Sensei: Nagusa.
- Sensei: You're actually a very funny person. Did you know that?
- Nagusa: Is...that so?

## MomoTalk 5

- Nagusa: Sensei, have you yet heard tales of the snow maiden?
- Nagusa: She is depicted as a blossoming beauty in many a folklore tale.
- Sensei: I am curious as to why she's described that way.
- Sensei: A lot of reasons...but I can think of one in particular.
- Sensei: Maybe because "beauty" tends to draw people's attention?
- Nagusa: Because "beauty" is appealing... I see.
- Sensei: People will not fall for a trap with no honey. As such, a "blossoming beauty" would be an appropriate lure.
- Nagusa: I find myself grateful that I am not a youkai.
- Sensei: I like that confidence!
