- Mine (Remedial Knights): Of course, I would prefer a peaceful Kivotos over one where people get hurt.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): And so, I started thinking again.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): What if, whether or not you intended it to be so, you are the cause of all of these incidents?
- "Excuse me, Mine?"
> Screen shakes

- Mine (Remedial Knights): With zero involvement from any other organization, there's no room for any political conflicts or any other kind of disagreement!

- Mine (Remedial Knights): And this is just my intuition speaking, but...

- Mine (Remedial Knights): I heard that, recently, you have been helping the Remedial Knights frequently.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): I wanted to thank you for that, and vent a little bit as well.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): Heehee.
