> Unexpectedly, you received an invitation to Mine's room...
- Mine (Remedial Knights): Welcome, Sensei. This is my room.
- "So... This is what it means to collect medical supplies."
- Mine (Remedial Knights): It's nothing amazing.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): Some people collect books. Others collect bottle caps, stamps, or memorable coins...
- Mine (Remedial Knights): I simply collect medical supplies.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): It's quite practical. They're so useful.
- "Even so, isn't this all a bit much?!"
- Mine (Remedial Knights): It does seem like it's grown quite a lot...
- Mine (Remedial Knights): If I were to look at it from an outside perspective, I suppose I could understand.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): But, Sensei, we are the Remedial Knights.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): Shouldn't we be ready whenever?!
- Mine (Remedial Knights): No matter what we face!
- Mine (Remedial Knights): Duplicate materials...?!
- Mine (Remedial Knights): I can't ignore that statement!
- Mine (Remedial Knights): Speaking of just the bandages, this one and that one are definitely not the same!
- "They look like the same wraps to me...?"
- Mine (Remedial Knights): They're different! The intricately textured one is a famous bandage from Saint Ann Medical.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): They profit by producing only the most premium medical supplies. We have no choice but to buy some of these bandages, since there are no good alternatives!
- Mine (Remedial Knights): They are specialized for emergency treatment, so are excellent for stopping bleeding and sanitizing wounds. They have enough tension to apply good pressure on the wound, as well!
- Mine (Remedial Knights): It seems like the perfect bandage...
- Mine (Remedial Knights): Well, it's also priced at five to seven times the price of regular bandages!
- Mine (Remedial Knights): On the other hand, this bandage here is from Rosieu.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): It has a dense weave that makes it specialized for holding things in place, and its large width allows for a wide range of compression.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): The price is low to mid-range, so anyone can afford to have a stock of them!
- Mine (Remedial Knights): It's a product that could be said to be closest to the true value of a bandage!
- "Y-Yeah! I see!"
- Mine (Remedial Knights): These healing ointments are also different!
- Mine (Remedial Knights): Here, this ointment with the red wrapper is formulated to prevent germs from multiplying.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): It is particularly effective in controlling gram-positive bacteria that are often the primary cause of skin infections.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): That ointment in the green wrapper, on the other hand, is a little less effective at inhibiting bacterial growth.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): But instead, it promotes cell regeneration, allowing the wound to heal comparatively faster.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): It also has some steroidal properties, so it's great for fighting inflammation.
- Mine (Remedial Knights): Of course, there are dangers to abusing it because of its steroidal properties, but...
- Mine (Remedial Knights): Hmmm...
- Mine (Remedial Knights): No matter how I try to explain it, it seems that you can't understand their differences and beauty...
- Mine (Remedial Knights): It's all right. There's got to be a way!
- Mine (Remedial Knights): I know! Let's try wounding my own body!
- "Why?!"
- Mine (Remedial Knights): I've heard that if you don't know something, it's most effective to learn when it happens to your own body!
- Mine (Remedial Knights): If we compare the efficacy of these medical supplies on my body,
- Mine (Remedial Knights): I'm sure that even you'll be able to gain a deep understanding of the differences between them, Sensei!
- "Stop that!"
- Mine (Remedial Knights): Just a little bit will do! I could just poke my fingertips with a needle, or maybe slice my hand with a scalpel just a bit...!
- Mine (Remedial Knights): Huh? In front of your eyes... You can't see a student get hurt...?
- Mine (Remedial Knights): If that's true... I'm sorry. It looks like I got too excited, Sensei...
- Mine (Remedial Knights): But if you ever need emergency treatment...
- Mine (Remedial Knights): Please seek me out.
> Even after that, as we returned to Schale, Mine passionately explained medical supplies.
