- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): As promised, I've arrived.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Your reaction is rather lackluster.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Is a student's unannounced visit unwelcome here?
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I highly doubt that. I have heard that Schale opens its arms to any and all students.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Then, by process of elimination...
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): You're displeased with my presence in particular?
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...I see.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Um.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): You need not indulge in such ostentatious reassurances.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I was joking.
- "R-Really? I think your jokes might be a bit too advanced for me..."
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): That sounds rather like mockery, but I will let it pass.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Enough pleasantries.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I have come here on business.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Are you curious as to my motives?
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): It's rather simple.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Your repairs to our club room have been received with resounding approval.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Students have been exclaiming things such as, "We won't have to set out buckets when it rains anymore!" and "I can walk without being scared of falling through the floor!"
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): One in particular said, "Wow! I didn't know doors were meant to actually stop rain and wind!"
- "Um, what kind of life have you been living?!"
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Perhaps our students indulged in a touch of drama in their descriptions. Regardless, they are grateful. As am I.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): As I said previously, I am not one to neglect my debts.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Therefore, I, Kiryuu Kikyou, will assist Schale's Sensei with your tasks today.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): This is perhaps a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to give orders to the Hyakkaryouran's operations officer.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Will this properly repay our debt to you?
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...Lies.
- "...Huh?"
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I liken you to the boy who cried wolf. A liar.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): There is no being on this planet so selfless as to be satisfied with a mere dinner as payment for a long day's work.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): You needn't fake such modesties.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Human relationships are give and take.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): For one party to only give and the other to only take... It is no relationship at all.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): People are greedy. They use others to achieve their own ends and are used in return.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Act as beguiling and innocent as you please. You cannot trick me.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Let's not continue the song and dance of proprieties. I despise wasting time.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I have used Schale as a means to my ends. It is your opportunity to employ Kiryuu Kikyou for yours.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I do not bestow this upon just anyone.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...And things are as they should be.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I, Kiryuu Kikyou, will fulfill my duty and accomplish any task you assign to me to perfection.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): (...This should be enough.)
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): (I will fulfill Sensei's request, and my debts will be cleared.)
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): (The scales of power in our relationship will once again be balanced.)
- "My task for you is to take a break today."
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...Excuse me?
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Is this a pathetic attempt at a joke?
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Do you require me to clean out your ears instead?
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I am offering you the opportunity to employ the esteemed services of Hyakkaryouran's operations officer.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Are you mocking me again?
- "No! I'm sincerely assigning you the task of taking a break."
- "Aren't you an operations officer? You should know how important rest is!"
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): O-Of course I know that!
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): One could even argue that rest is more important than efficient processes in an operation.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I won't allow your misdirection to distract me.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): You are grasping at technicalities.
- "You told me to give you a task. So I did. What happens next is up to you."
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): And so the agency returns to me?
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): You are incomprehensible.
- "It makes no difference to me if you follow through on your word or not."
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...!
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Hah.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): If that is your will...
- "W-Whoa! Kikyou! Please don't hurt me! I'm sorry!"
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Don't be ridiculous.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I am doing as you say and selecting a book from your shelf.
- "Do you mean..."
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I will acquiesce.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Per Sensei's request, I will quietly rest at Schale today.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): I will lay claim to this book and sofa to achieve those ends.
- "Please do! I just brewed some coffee, if you'd like some."
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): ...Yes.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): For you to refuse to put me to work...
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): You are truly an enigma, Sensei.
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Truly...
- Kikyou (Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council): Just a strange, strange teacher.
