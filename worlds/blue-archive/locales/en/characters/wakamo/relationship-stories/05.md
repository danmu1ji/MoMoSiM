# Wakamo — Relationship Story 5

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Wakamo/Relationship_Story/5
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> I had a bad feeling after my conversation with Wakamo on MomoTalk, so I immediately left to meet with her.
- Wakamo: Sensei? I'm thrilled beyond measure at your unexpected visit, but...
- Wakamo: I don't want to be a bother to Sensei...
- "No, never, Wakamo."
- "I just wanted to spend time with you today."
- Wakamo: Your kindness knows no bounds.
- Wakamo: The serendipity of us meeting on that fateful day never ceases to amaze me, no matter how much time has passed.
- Wakamo: What would you like me to do?
- Wakamo: I'm prepared in body and soul, in mind and resolve, to fulfill your every desire and request.
- Wakamo: Just say the word, and—
- "Wakamo."
- Wakamo: Yes, Sensei?
- Wakamo: Wh-What?!
- Wakamo: C-Could it be I am currently in a beautiful dream...?!
- Wakamo: Ah! AHHH!
- Wakamo: This cannot be a dream, for this feeling exceeds even my wildest hopes! I am happy, but I am not yet prepared for such a request, Sensei!
- Wakamo: O-One moment!
- "W-Wakamo?! Wait up!"
> I started chasing the fleeing Wakamo as fast as I could.
- Wakamo: Wh-Why do you give chase?!
- "Because I don't know why you're running!"
- Wakamo: Y-You don't understand, Sensei! As I am right now...!
- Wakamo: I just need more time, I beg of you!
- "I'm just asking for company, Wakamo! That's it!"
- Wakamo: You said it again?! From the deepest crevice in my soul, I feel only gratitude for your sentiment! But I cannot bear the weight of your feelings!
- Wakamo: All I know is that I am not READYYY!
- "Wakamo! Ack?!"
- Wakamo: O-Oh, dear!
> Maybe it's because I was in such a rush to go after Wakamo, but I tripped and fell unceremoniously on my face.
- Wakamo: Oh, dear! Oh, DEAR! Are you injured? My precious Sensei! I'm sorry! So sorry! I'm sorry, I'm sorry, I'm sorry, I'm sorry! I may as well have tripped you myself!
- "I'm completely fine! I tripped on my own feet!"
- Wakamo: Oh, no, no, no. Sensei, is that...is that b-b-b-blood?!
- "From a small scrape! Please calm down!"
- Wakamo: I...cannot... This kind of tragedy...should never have occurred...
- Wakamo: If I could take every ounce of your pain for myself, if only I... No! I should never have allowed even the smallest scrape upon Sensei's body in the first place!
- Wakamo: This is my cross to bear. I will atone with my very life, Sensei.
- "All right! Let's do some breathing exercises, Wakamo!"
- "Are you feeling better?"
- Wakamo: Yes...
- "You still have your mask on..."
- Wakamo: ...!
- Wakamo: I'm sorry, Sensei...
- Wakamo: To think you approached me and asked me to stay by your side, forever and always, day in and day out... And this is how I react? I'm ashamed all the way to the tips of my ears...
- Wakamo: How could I ruin such a momentous occasion...
- Wakamo: *sob* I'm so sorry Sensei... This is all my fault...
- "There, there. No more tears."
- Wakamo: I had fully steeled my resolve to remove my mask, to proudly display the parts of me I can only show to you!
- Wakamo: But, instead, I was a coward. I hid my shame and embarrassment behind my mask, rather than facing you honestly.
- "Well, of course it's hard. You've had that mask since before I met you."
- Wakamo: ...
- Wakamo: This mask is one of my most precious possessions.
- Wakamo: It was my protection against the evil and tribulations of the world. But now...
- "What about now?"
- Wakamo: ...!
- Wakamo: You can be very straightforward you know. But alas, even that is a side of you I have come to adore...
- Wakamo: Despite my many shortcomings, you never stop holding out your hand for me to take. You've always believed in me.
- Wakamo: Not only that, you asked for nothing more than my company today. No task or mission, just me...
- "I did want to clarify something about that..."
> I told her the truth: that I asked for her company today because I was worried about her.
- Wakamo: Y-You were concerned I would attempt to fulfill my duty at the cost of my own safety?
- Wakamo: I had... I had just said that in the hopes of a fateful event occurring between you and I...
- Wakamo: Heeheehee. You can be quite silly as well, Sensei.
- Wakamo: Even the fact you were so concerned about me is overwhelming. I'm... Eek!
- "Well, since we're here, is there anything you want to do with me, Wakamo?"
- Wakamo: !
- Wakamo: I see... So even this coincidental meeting was nothing short of a miracle! Fate itself!
- Wakamo: I, Wakamo, will follow you everywhere... To the ends of the earth and the very mouth of hell, with nothing but joy in my heart...! Lead the way, Sensei!
- "Yes, ma'am..."
> Wakamo and I spent a somewhat peaceful rest of the day together.
