# Serika

## Description

A student of Abydos High School, and the grouchy secretary of the Abydos Countermeasure Committee. One with many complaints, Serika is the type hesitant to show her emotions. Despite her daily habit of saying "It's fine if this school goes bankrupt", she has a deep love for her school and works tirelessly behind the backs of her clubmates to pay off her school's debt.

## Appearance

Serika has navy hair in long twin tails with cyan ribbons, dark-blue cat ears, reddish-pink eyes, and a small fang.
