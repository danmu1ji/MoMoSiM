- Serika (Foreclosure Task Force): Our task for today is pulling weeds in the park! It sounds simple enough, but it'll be hard work.
- Serika (Foreclosure Task Force): We need to focus on the patch here and the one over there.
- Serika (Foreclosure Task Force): That part especially is super overgrown, so the weeds will grow back quickly if they aren't uprooted.
- Serika (Foreclosure Task Force): It feels like for every weed we pull, two more will pop up in its place... *sigh* This reminds of that old tale with the guy endlessly rolling a boulder up a hill.
- Serika (Foreclosure Task Force): If only we had some professional gardening equipment. All I have are my gloves and a sickle...
- "We'll find a way to manage."
- Serika (Foreclosure Task Force): Here goes nothing, Sensei! Let's get to work!
> (Serika and I pulled weeds all day.)
> We managed to finish our work just before sunset.
- Serika (Foreclosure Task Force): All done! This should be okay.
- Serika (Foreclosure Task Force): You did a pretty good job! I guess you're used to manual labor by now?
- Serika (Foreclosure Task Force): My back...isn't as big of a fan.
- Serika (Foreclosure Task Force): This job is nice because I can do it after school...but the pay isn't very competitive.
- Serika (Foreclosure Task Force): ...It's already getting dark out. Let's head back, Sensei.
> (Serika and I hobbled home with our aching backs.)
