- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): Over here, Sensei. It's Suzumi.
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): It's okay. I just got here myself.
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): Pardon me? Why would I not be okay with you calling me...?
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): If this is about my Vigilante Crew workload, then don't concern yourself.
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): It isn't as though you're tearing me away from a job I love.
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): Am I forced to do it? No, that's not it. No one's making me do anything.
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): I simply don't...particularly enjoy it. That's all.
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): Truthfully, I hope the day comes where this region won't need a Vigilante Crew anymore...
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): You think that makes me kind?
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): You must be mistaken. Others would rather stay away because I seem cold.
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): Not that I don't understand. It simply comes with the territory of this line of work.
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): ...Thank you for saying that, though.
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): Let's go, Sensei. I'll lead—I mean...
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): Let's walk together.
- (I start walking alongside Suzumi.)
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): This is a cafe I enjoy. Would you care to join me?
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): I have a preference for lattes, and this cafe's are...

- Troublemaking Student B: It's the perfect job! We scouted for weeks so we could strike while the Vigilante Crew is off duty!
- Troublemaking Student B: So shut your mouth and empty the cash register. Get moving!
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): *sigh*
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): If only the ruffians here put this much energy into studying.
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): (This is supposed to be my day off...and I'm with Sensei.)
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): ...
- Clerk: I-I told you! That's all the money we made this week!
- Troublemaking Student B: You mean the money WE made. We promise not to spend it in one place.
- Troublemaking Student A: I hope you weren't hoping to play the hero. The last thing you want is to land in the hospital after getting fired for this! Hahaha!
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): ...
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): Um...
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): Sensei...I...
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): It's nothing. I'm sorry.
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): ...!
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): ...!

- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): Thank you, Sensei. I won't be long. I'll take care of this as quickly as possible.

- Troublemaking Student B: Yeah. What was that? It sounded oddly familiar...
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): Flash grenade incoming!
> *bam* *riiiiiiinnnng*

- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): You...don't' mind? You had fun today?

- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): That's simple...
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): It's because they're non-lethal and their effects wear off.
- Suzumi (Trinity's Vigilante Crew): Flash grenades are the quickest and safest way to neutralize the situation and subdue the enemy.
