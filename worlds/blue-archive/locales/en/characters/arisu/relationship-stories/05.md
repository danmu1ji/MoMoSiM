> Millennium Game Development Department
- Aris (Game Development Department): Good evening, Sensei.
- Aris (Game Development Department): I was just having some quiet time with a game.
- "Oh, yeah? What were you playing?"
- Aris (Game Development Department): It is a classic RPG.
- Aris (Game Development Department): RPGs are my favorite. Other games are fun too, but RPGs always hit the spot.
- "Swords, magic... You like that kind of stuff?"
- Aris (Game Development Department): Yes, there are things like that in RPGs.
- "They have fantasy elements that don't exist in reality."
- Aris (Game Development Department): Is that what you think?
- Aris (Game Development Department): I see it differently.
- Arisu: ― I believe magic exists in Kivotos as well.
- Arisu: ― It must. How else could you make me so happy, Sensei?
- Arisu: ― Games are fun because they reflect the beauty of the world.
- Arisu: ― You taught me that.
- Arisu: ― Games, cats, friends...and even this moment.
- Arisu: ― The world is full of beautiful things.
- Arisu: ― And I want to know more of them. Together with you, Sensei.
- Arisu: ― I want to learn more about this world!
