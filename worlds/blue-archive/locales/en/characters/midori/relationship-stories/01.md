> Millennium Game Development Department
> (I got a call from Midori and headed over to the Game Development Department.)
> When I arrived in the club room, I saw Midori concentrating with a sketchbook in the middle of the room.
> Then...
- "Midori?"
- Midori (Game Development Department): Hmm, this isn't it.
- "Hello...?"
- Midori (Game Development Department): Hmmm. This isn't it either...
- "Can you hear me?"
- Midori (Game Development Department): Hmmm... None of these would work.

- Midori (Game Development Department): Oh, Sensei! Wh-When did you get here?!

- Midori (Game Development Department): ...
- Midori (Game Development Department): Waaah, Sensei! I-I'm in trouble!
- "Trouble?"
- Midori (Game Development Department): The deadline is just around the corner, but I can't think of anything!
- "Well, do you feel better now?"
- Midori (Game Development Department): Yes, I'm embarrassed about how I acted. I'm sorry.
- Midori (Game Development Department): I've been agonizing over this for a few days...
- "So, what is it that you needed my advice on?"
- Midori (Game Development Department): You know...in the Game Development Department...
- Midori (Game Development Department): We don't know when or how we'll get to make a new game.
- Midori (Game Development Department): So I've been brainstorming all sorts of different ideas.
- Midori (Game Development Department): The deadline for the next conference is fast approaching, so I was trying to come up with something new by then...
- "And you couldn't think of anything all night?"
- Midori (Game Development Department): Yeah. The deadline is just around the corner, but I've got nothing...
- "If you're having that much trouble, couldn't you ask to push back the deadline?"
- Midori (Game Development Department): Push back the deadline?
- Midori (Game Development Department): Sensei, a deadline is like a promise. Wouldn't it be irresponsible for me to ask it to be pushed back because I can't keep it?
- Midori (Game Development Department): As an illustrator, and the person in charge of all game visuals in the Game Development Department, I won't allow myself to do that!
- Midori (Game Development Department): U-Um, I didn't realize how worked up I got.
- Midori (Game Development Department): U-Um, I didn't realize how worked up I got.
- Midori (Game Development Department): Anyway, no. I can't push back the deadline. No way.
- Midori (Game Development Department): That's why I texted you, Sensei. I needed your advice.
- Midori (Game Development Department): Can you think of anything?
- "What do you usually play?"
- Midori (Game Development Department): I mostly play puzzle games and casual games, like Tetness and Poyoyopo.
- Midori (Game Development Department): Oh, so shifting my perspective... You mean, I could stimulate my mind by playing games from genres I don't usually play.

- Midori (Game Development Department): I think that's a good idea!

> So Midori got a competitive game that she'd never played before.
> At the time, I had no idea...
> ...what kind of catastrophe that choice would bring.
- Midori (Game Development Department): Ah! Again, I can't go anywhere because my path is blocked by bombs!
- Midori (Game Development Department): Ugh! Again! Let's play again, Sensei!
- Midori (Game Development Department): Wha?! Why did I just die?!
- Midori (Game Development Department): I planted that bomb! Why did I die?!

- "Midori, it's time to..."

- "I mean, it's not that..."
- Midori (Game Development Department): It's not over till it's over!
- Midori (Game Development Department): The game is only over when I give up!
- Midori (Game Development Department): Be prepared, Sensei. You can't leave until I win.
> Taking a break from the brainstorming, Midori and I played the game all night.
