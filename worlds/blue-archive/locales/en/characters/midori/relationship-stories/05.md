> And so, I joined Midori...
> ...and we got deep into a monster-hunting game!
- Midori (Game Development Department): Now, Sensei! It's staggered! Attack its core and cast—
- Midori (Game Development Department): HUUUUH?!
- "Midori? Did you just disconnect?!"
- Midori (Game Development Department): I did! My console just turned off!
> Despite her blowing on the console and inverting it, it didn't turn back on. We took it in for service...
- Repair Center Staff: Hmm... This will be rough. I haven't seen a model this old in a while.
- Repair Center Staff: You need some replacement parts, and I don't think they even MAKE compatible ones anymore.
- Midori (Game Development Department): So, there's...nothing you can do?
- Repair Center Staff: Let me call the warehouse and check the stock. I'll be right back.
- Midori (Game Development Department): Thank you...
- "And we were so close to beating the monster!"
- Midori (Game Development Department): Yes. I thought we were going to get it for sure this time.
- Midori (Game Development Department): And it was so fun to play, just you and me. It was going to be so special...
- Repair Center Staff: Thank you for waiting. But...they told me they couldn't find the part.
- Midori (Game Development Department): A-Are you sure?!
- Repair Center Staff: Perhaps I can interest you in a new model? Look at all these new features!
- Midori (Game Development Department): But...
- Midori (Game Development Department): Really? But...what if I found some parts at a second-hand store and brought them in? Or 3D-printed something?
- Repair Center Staff: Miss, since we're a company-run store, we cannot provide "unauthorized modification" services...
- Repair Center Staff: And even if we were to do such thing, we're not responsible for unforeseen damage that could result in data loss.
- Midori (Game Development Department): D-Data loss?! No, anything but that! We're so close!
- Repair Center Staff: How about this?
- Repair Center Staff: This new model's on sale right now. If you purchase it here, I'll transfer your data for free, and upgrade the memory!
- Midori (Game Development Department): ...
- "..."
- Midori (Game Development Department): I guess it's the only way...
- Midori (Game Development Department): Okay. I'll do that.
- Midori (Game Development Department): Please send the invoice to the Millennium Science School Game Development Department.
- Midori (Game Development Department): That's okay? Because it's for research, it should come out of the activity fund, right? Seminar would understand!
- Repair Center Staff: Thank you, Miss. We'll get started. The transfer will take a while, so please hang tight!
- Midori (Game Development Department): Sure. Thanks for your help.
- "By any chance, did you buy that console with Momoi?"
- Midori (Game Development Department): Yes? H-How did you know?!
- "Just a hunch..."
- Midori (Game Development Department): Well... Now that I think about it, I guess it was obvious...
- Midori (Game Development Department): She got the pink, and I got the green. We swapped controllers...
- Midori (Game Development Department): Of course, hers broke down a while ago. I was so careful with mine...
- Midori (Game Development Department): ...But that's how it goes...
- Midori (Game Development Department): Don't make that face. It's not a big deal. It's normal, in fact. Electronics grow obsolete, after all...
- Midori (Game Development Department): I'm sometimes disappointed with how QUICKLY they go obsolete, but still...
- Midori (Game Development Department): The thing is, Momoi worked hard to buy me this...
- Midori (Game Development Department): ...so that she could corrupt me.
> Screen shakes
- "C-Corrupt you?!"
- Midori (Game Development Department): It's true!
- Midori (Game Development Department): I was just an artist, initially. I didn't even game, and now look at me!
- Midori (Game Development Department): Still, I play less than her, so I guess I'm not fully corrupted.
- Midori (Game Development Department): And when we DO play together, I beat her more than half the time. So, who's the real winner?
- "If that's what counts..."
- Midori (Game Development Department): ...Momoi still impresses me. She never gives up, and is always bursting with energy...
- Midori (Game Development Department): She's so curious and never shies away from a challenge. Everything she does becomes a part of her story...
- Midori (Game Development Department): It's a very admirable mindset, and especially valuable for a creator.
- Midori (Game Development Department): That said, she's always causing trouble and dragging me into it!
- Midori (Game Development Department): ...Still, I admire her courage. I used to be so envious of video games and subconsciously avoided them.
- "Envious?"
- Midori (Game Development Department): Yes.
- Midori (Game Development Department): How should I put it?
- Midori (Game Development Department): She'd play, and I'd see such incredible artwork out of the corner of my eye.
- Midori (Game Development Department): I tried not to, but I always compared my drawings to the professionals'.
- Midori (Game Development Department): And, because I was even more of an amateur back then, it hurt all the more.
- Midori (Game Development Department): I now know that it was all part of the journey.
- Midori (Game Development Department): But at the time I couldn't stop thinking about how mediocre I was!
- Midori (Game Development Department): ...My sister's never been like that.
- Midori (Game Development Department): She reads her old work and talks about things she likes, never dwelling on what she finds embarrassing.
- Midori (Game Development Department): She's like that naturally, too. She says that life is short, so we should focus on all the good things it has to offer.
- Midori (Game Development Department): Ah, I'm sorry, Sensei! What am I going on about? It's just an old console...
- "It's fine. I enjoyed listening to you, Midori."
- Midori (Game Development Department): Y-You did?
- Midori (Game Development Department): That's a relief...
- Repair Center Staff: Thank you for waiting! The data transfer is complete! Here's your old console, and I've re-boxed the new one!
- Midori (Game Development Department): Okay. Thank you so much.
- Midori (Game Development Department): By the way... What should I do with the old one?
- Midori (Game Development Department): What? W-We don't have to go that far...
- Midori (Game Development Department): You're saying that it's precious? Because of all the memories?
- Midori (Game Development Department): I mean, you're right, but...
- Midori (Game Development Department): You'll build me a clear display shelf?!
- Midori (Game Development Department): N-N-N-N-No can do. That's way too much. I couldn't have you—
- Midori (Game Development Department): What? You have some spare ones? And a stand?
- Midori (Game Development Department): ...
- Midori (Game Development Department): What kind of glamorous life are you living, if you just have that stuff hanging around?
- "What? So I can't have hobbies?!"
- Midori (Game Development Department): You know that's not what I meant!
- Midori (Game Development Department): ...
- Midori (Game Development Department): Still, Sensei, actually...
- Midori (Game Development Department): Momoi displays her old console in the same way.
- Midori (Game Development Department): Is it all right if I do something similar...maybe even better?
- Midori (Game Development Department): ...She's going to accuse me of copying her, and that means more points to Momoi...
- "Ah, I know that feeling..."
- Midori (Game Development Department): Thanks for getting it...
- Midori (Game Development Department): The thing is, I'm a believer of a certain theory...
- Midori (Game Development Department): ...The theory that sisters are programmed to fight each other from birth!
- "Yeah, but not all the time...?"
- Midori (Game Development Department): It's okay! She gets this round!
- Midori (Game Development Department): But I'll beat her in the next match!
> Midori plugged in her new console, and we continued from where we left off.
> This time, we finally completed the hunt!
