# Hinata\n\nHinata is a serious and earnest Sisterhood member from the Trinity Integrated Academy. She is responsible for the "management of the cathedral and other items", a job she performs faithfully every day. However, she is a bit of a dilettante, and often loses confidence after making a series of mistakes. She carries with surprising ease a large suitcase with various items in it, including a grenade launcher.\n\nSource: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Hinata\nReview status: source extracted; pending editorial review.\n
