# Chise — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> After receiving a MomoTalk from Chise, I arrived at the Yin-Yang Club Lounge.
- "Chise?"
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Oh, Sensei.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Over here.
- "What are you doing on the floor?"
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): The sun is sizzling, so...
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): I was just sitting here.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): If I don't, I might melt.
- "It's true. Even tiny movements can make you sweat sometimes."
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Wait here with me, Sensei.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): You'll hear it soon.
- "The 'sound of the incoming wind' you were telling me about?"
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Yes. It's a pretty sound.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Refreshing, too.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Listen closely. Can you hear it?
- "..."
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Sensei, just sit still...
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Listen closely, wait quietly...
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Did you hear it?
- "...Bells?"
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Not quite.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): It's actually...
- "Wind chimes?"
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Yes, that's it.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): I hung them up, so I could hear the wind coming in.
- "Well, you'll always know the wind is blowing when you hear those."
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Yes, the sound is clear.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): I've been putting these chimes up every summer.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): They're my favorites.
- "They look like regular chimes to me."
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): They may look that way, but...
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): ...the sound they make is special. It's bluer.
- "Bluer...sound?"
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Yes, that's why I hang them right there, each year.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): I ask to hear their clear, blue sound.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Listen with me, Sensei.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Just lie down on the floor, here.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Like this.
> I laid down next to Chise.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Good, Sensei.
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Now listen carefully.
- "..."
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): That's right!
- Chise (Yin-Yang Club): Oh, hmm. I just thought of a poem.
> A hot summer day
> I lie down upon the floor
> Wind against my skin
> Afterwards, I laid there next to Chise for some time, listening to the calm, blue wind.
