# Chise\n\nChise is a naturally-airheaded girl belonging to the Yin-Yang club of Hyakkiyako. She joined the Yin-Yang club due to her interest in traditional cultural elements such as haiku. Exuding a mysterious atmosphere from her appearance and gestures, she is the envy of other Hyakkiyako students.
