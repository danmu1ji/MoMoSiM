- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Oh, Sensei! Over here!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): I'm so glad to see you!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Hmm...
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): I hope I'm not bothering you. I feel a little guilty that I get clingy sometimes...
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Oh, thank goodness! You had me worried!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Sensei! Should someone in your position be saying that?!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Well, regardless.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Now that you're here, I want to make sure you have fun!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): And to put your mind at ease: No, we're not going to see only one movie today.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): We have 21 movies to marathon through! You're bound to enjoy at least one of them!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Heh. You look surprised. How can we watch 21 movies with only two tickets, you may ask?
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): These aren't just any movies tickets...
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Drum roll, please... These are binge passes! Ta-da!
- "Binge passes? That's a thing?"
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): I knew you'd be surprised. They have events where they screen a bunch of movies one after the other.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): It's a great way for people who love movies to see a bunch of films of the same genre!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): And it goes without saying that the movies we'll be binging are all mobster and noir films!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Hurry! There's no time! We have to make it to the first movie! Let's go!
> Pina grabbed my hand and dragged me into the theater.
> (Surprisingly, the first movie wasn't too bad. All the violent scenes were tastefully edited.)
> (It wasn't exactly a masterpiece, but it was good enough to keep the audience entertained.)
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Wow. That was...
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): ...an amazing movie! Boss—I mean, Sensei! Er, I mean, Boss...
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): N-No, it's Sensei. Right? Sensei... Sorry.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): What did you think? Did you like it?
- "You know what? You can call me Boss from now on."
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): ...!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): R-Really?! You mean it, Boss?!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Okay! Whatever you say, Boss! Let's go straight to the next movie!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): The second movie is called Loyal Cat's Fist!
> (Every movie that followed was incredible.)
> (All of them were suitably hard-boiled, sometimes lyrical and sometimes passionate.)
> (...)
> (But...)
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Wow! That was amazing, Sens—I mean, Boss!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Then, let's watch the next movie!
> (It doesn't matter how great the movies are. When you watch 21 films of the same genre all in a row...)
> (...and there's someone chattering away in your ear nonstop and keeping you awake the entire time...)
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): *gasp* This is the one I've been waiting for! The next movie is...!
> (...most people...)
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): No way!
> (...would consider that a form of torture.)
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Simply.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Amazing.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Don't you agree?
> (I must have fainted during the 21st and final movie.)
> When I came to, I was on a grassy riverside outside the theater.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): ...
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): S-Sensei...? Are you okay?
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): O-Oh, no!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): O-Oh, no!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Come on, Sensei! Stay with me! I'm so sorry!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): *whimper* I'm so sorry... I was afraid of this happening.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): I'm used to watching mobster movies for days on end... I didn't consider how hard it might be for you.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): This is all my fault. Please forgive me.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): I won't ask you to watch movies with me anymore...
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): *sniffle* *sob*
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): ...?
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): ...?
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Huh? Are you sure?
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Shouldn't I be worried about you?
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): You didn't faint... You just fell asleep?
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): That doesn't sound right... I could have sworn you were out cold.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Excuse me?
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): No...talking back? Of course not! I would never talk back to you, Boss!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Everything you say is right. You must have fallen asleep! I must have been mistaken!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Still, I-I...
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): ...
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Heehee.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Wahahahaha! Ahhh!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Haha! I'm crying. I'm really crying! Hahahaha!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Haha...ha... My goodness!
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): ...
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Thank you, Sensei. You're always so kind to me.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Even though I make you put up with so much... I don't know how to repay you.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): It's hard to explain, but the gangster spirit I sense from you is different from mine. It's very warm and tender...
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): There's something unique about you that's comforting and inspires trust.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): That's the righteousness I sense from you...and I would never lie.
- Pina (Festival Operations Department): Heehee.
> (Afterward, Pina and I spent a long time talking about the movies we had seen that day.)
> (And we also promised that next time, we'd go see a single movie together rather than a marathon.)
