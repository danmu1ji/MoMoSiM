# Pina

## Description

A student at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy, Pina is a strong admirer of chivalry and heroism. Always cheerful and happy to help out other people, she learned of her yearning of said chivalry entirely through movies. All oddities aside... She is a straight-forward girl with nothing but the chivalry she yearns for on her path.

## Appearance

Pina has long bright-blonde hair reaching her waist, with loose side bangs and a ponytail, and bright blue eyes.
