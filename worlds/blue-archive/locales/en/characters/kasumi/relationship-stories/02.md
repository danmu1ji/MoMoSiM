> It was well into the night the next time I met up with Kasumi...at yet another abandoned factory.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): As usual, Sensei, you're right on time!
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): And yes indeed! Welcome to secret hideout number 89!
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): I know, I know. It's three numbers higher than last time.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): What can I say? Two didn't agree with me, and one didn't agree with my explosives, so here we are.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): But that's all debris in the dump now. Hideout numbers don't matter in the grand scheme of things.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): No, siree! We have far more important topics to discuss than my nomadic existence.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Now, follow me, Sensei. I have a fantastic plan I want to share with you!
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Mm hm! Yes, this is just the spot.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): I'm sorry the locale's so...unsavory, but I need somewhere tucked away if I want to stay hidden.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): I'm quite popular these days—infamous even. There are people looking for me everywhere.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): The Prefect Team, the Helmet Gangsters, arms manufacturers, clubs impacted by past demolitions...
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): But such is life, right? I know everything will work out once I hit a hot spring!
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): ...Hm? What's with that face, Sensei?
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Huh?
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): ...I wouldn't say it's easy to keep going in the face of adversity, no...
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): And, sure, it's not exactly encouraging when I fail at something.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): ...Haha.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): But if you don't mind me saying it, Sensei, you're looking at this all wrong.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Creating an amazing new hot spring isn't my only measure of success.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Yes, that's my goal. But that doesn't necessarily mean that failing to do so is a loss.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Sensei...
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): What I really enjoy—what I love—is demolition.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Any opportunity to work with explosives, successful or not, is a win in my book.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Doesn't matter what it is. Big, small, successful, unsuccessful...
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Blasting that gets you chased, blasting that gets sabotaged, blasting that works despite sabotage...
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): I see amazing sights thanks to demos I perform in unique and far-flung locales.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Even when the Prefect Team sabotages a demo I spent months planning and preparing, I still find joy in the process.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): As long as there's potential for a hot spring, I'm happy! In fact, even a vague hope is good enough!
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): You're looking at me like I'm crazy. Listen, what I'm trying to say is, it's not the destination, it's the journey.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): From finding a worthy spot to planning, prepping, and the demo itself, I love it all! You can understand that, right?
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Oh, and speaking of worthy spots, you should know...
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): ...I've got my eye on the Schale building.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Wahahaha! The look on your face!
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Don't you see, Sensei?
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): I want you to be more involved in my plans.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): And I figured revealing that might keep you coming back...if only to ensure I don't make good on my threat!
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): I know you all probably think I'm a pain in the neck, but that doesn't matter to me.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): I've known what I wanted for a while now, even before you got here, Sensei!
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): So, knowing that, I hope you'll take me more seriously in the future.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Can you understand that, Sensei? It's really important to me that you do...
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): ...for both of us.
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Now, why don't we discuss my next idea?
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): And if necessary...
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): You can stay late with me!
- "..."
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Wahaha! Good, good! I wouldn't have it any other way!
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): Now, let's do some serious searching, shall we?
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): We need to find the perfect spot!
- Kasumi (Hot Springs Department): A spot that will make me, you, and everyone else happy!
> I stayed with Kasumi late into the night, poring over a map and discussing her plans.
> We argued passionately over where the best locations would be.
> It was nearly morning before we decided on a good spot, at which point Kasumi fell asleep right on top of the map.
