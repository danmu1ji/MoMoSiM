# Kasumi\n\nA student of Gehenna Academy, and manager of its Hot Springs Development Department. Kasumi insists that any land with a natural spring must be developed into a hot spring, and once a suitable location has been found she will do whatever it takes to blast through the ground with complete disregard for the surroundings.
