# Shiroko

## Description

Enrolled in Abydos High School, Shiroko is the sports-loving field captain of the Countermeasure Council. Generally a girl of few words and few emotions, she gives off an aloof aura but holds the interest of Abydos above all else. In order to revitalize the school, she is willing to do anything, and occasionally comes up with ridiculous plans.

## Appearance

Shiroko has a shoulder-length gray bob with wolf ears and prominent white tufts inside them; a cyan accessory sits below her left ear.
