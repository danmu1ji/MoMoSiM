> Inside an Abydos Classroom
> (Shiroko is waiting nervously.)
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): Umm...
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): I came like you told me to, Sensei. So, what is this all about?
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): Does this have to do with Schale? Or is it related to Abydos?
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): You want me to sit down? Okay...
> ...
- Shiroko: ― Is there something you want to talk to me about?
- Shiroko: ― (What could Sensei be thinking? I wish I knew.)
- Shiroko: ― (What could they be up to?)
- Shiroko: ― I just want to be clear that I'm your ally, Sensei.
- Shiroko: ― When you...pat me on the head like that...
- Shiroko: ― No, it doesn't bother me or anything.
- Ask what she was doing last night.
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): Last night? I went riding, then I took a shower, and I had grilled fish for dinner...
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): Before that? I was on top of the KV highway billboard and pretended to hijack a transport van...
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): Oh. You saw that?
- Tell her you're worried about her getting into danger.
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): U-Uh...
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): I know where you're coming from...but like I said, this is just a silly hobby of mine.
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): Um... I know anything I say will just sound like an excuse...
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): ...
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): I get it, I get it. You're only saying this because you're worried about me. I'll take your advice.
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): I'm...sorry. I don't mean to cause so much trouble.
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): I really made you worry, huh?
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): ...
- Shiroko (Foreclosure Task Force): ...Thanks.
> (Shiroko left the classroom in better spirits.)
