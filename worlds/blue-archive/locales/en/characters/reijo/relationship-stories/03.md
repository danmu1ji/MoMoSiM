> Black Tortoise Promenade Main Restaurant
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): What's with the Black Tortoise Promenade?! A Genryumon member personally comes all the way here for an inspection, and you don't even serve her tea?

- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): What're you saying?
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): If we boil the tea, we'll have to use the utensils that we prepared for the hygiene inspection.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Then you'll have to start the inspection from the top, or reschedule entirely.
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): ...!
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): F-Fine. I'm busy, so I don't have time for your stupid tea, anyway.
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): I'll reveal every last dirty secret the Black Tortoise Promenade is hiding!
- "Uh... Isn't this bad, Reijo...?"
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): They're just looking for problems.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): No matter what we say or do, the inspector is going to nitpick every little detail.

- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Here. Everyone, show your hands.
- Black Tortoise Promenade Member (Black Tortoise Promenade): ...Yes. Look to your heart's content.

- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): Cooking gloves are present, and contaminants are separated... Tsk.
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): Right! What about the back door? You're not leaving it open, are you?!
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): If you're carelessly leaving it open to air out the heat, unsanitary air or even BUGS can come through!
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Naturally. We at the Black Tortoise Promenade have installed a state-of-the-art ventilation system to circumvent any such concerns.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): If you activate the system, like so...
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): Wh-Who told you to activate it here! My hair!
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Trust and confirmation are important parts of inspections.
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): ...Fine. Next is facility and utensil hygiene!
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): Expiration dates of ingredients...!
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): Managing cross contamination...!
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): U-Unhygienic behaviors, like cigarettes or alcohol...!
- "I mean, I don't think students are really doing that..."
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): (Why...is everything up-to-code?!)
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Are you done with your inspection? This might be a little presumptuous of me...
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): ...but I don't think you're going to find any infractions.
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): Hmph! It's not over yet! Don't look down on Genryumon!
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): For now... I know! Guess the menu item I'm thinking of right now!
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Excuse me?
- "That's unreasonable!!"
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): You can't even manage that, and you call yourself a restaurant?!
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): How can you satisfy your customers, heart and soul, if you can't anticipate their every want and need?
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): ...
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): In addition! Why isn't the Genryumon emblem proudly displayed on the main doors of the restaurant?
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): You're exposing your rebellious feelings towards Genryumon! That's exactly what it is!
- Genryumon Member (Genryumon): Upon my return, I will immediately report the Black Tortoise Promenade's traitorous intentions towards Genryumon! No, towards Shanhaijing itself!
