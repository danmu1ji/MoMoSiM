> Early in the morning, I headed to where Reijo asked me to meet.
> When I got there...
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― Ah. You're here.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― I'm almost done with my morning training, so please wait.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― You see, this is an aspect of continuous movement as well.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― Stopping the flow, maintaining poses...
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― It also helps with training the mind.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― ...But why are you just standing there, Sensei?
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― Huh? If I'm training...
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― ...I shouldn't be getting distracted? I need to maintain my composure?
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― Yes, that's true, but...
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): ― ...B-But this is way too much!
> After she finished her training, I could feel Reijo's cold gaze on me.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): It really is embarrassing to show someone my kung fu.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): ...Although the reason for today's embarrassment had an entirely different factor mixed into it!
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Why do I feel like you're trying to subtly change the subject?
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): What? I'm imagining things? Just as you believe in me, I should also believe in you...?
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): All right, Sensei. If you say so.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): I guess I was the one who told you about this spot.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Huh? No. Even I don't train every single morning.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): I rest my body on the weekends. And I have no choice but to skip when it rains or snows.
- "Regardless, it's nice to see something you're so passionate about."
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Something I'm passionate about...
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): N-No, it's nothing. It's just...
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): You don't seem to think it's strange, me doing kung fu training.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): What? Oh... You did technically come from outside of Kivotos...
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Yes... Here, people view honing your martial arts skills as a little eccentric.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): After all, it won't help you much in a fight. Things are usually solved with a few gun shots.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): It's more or less just some morning calisthenics, at this point.
- "But you're still going to train, right, Reijo?"
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Well, if you forced me to choose, I suppose I'd say yes.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): I pursue kung fu training for a few reasons...
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): First, it benefits both your mind and body.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Moving your body helps center yourself, allowing you to clear your head and calm your thoughts. And, of course, the training increases your stamina.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Work for the Promenade is more difficult than you'd think. On top of that, I have to keep up with studying, and exams feel never ending...
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Huh? My grades...?
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Do you really need to ask me that right now?
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Well, that sums it up. Kung fu has a lot of great benefits.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Additionally, I love the proverbs that it teaches!
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): For example, "Patience is not passive nor idle, but proactive and intense."
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Only someone who's had their patience truly tested can relate to that. Like when I have to deal with Genryumon members throwing tantrums over inconsequential details!
- "L-Let's take a deep breath."
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Another great saying is, "Face your mistakes, and they will hold no power over you."
> Reijo continued animatedly talking about kung fu and its proverbs for a while.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Um. Sorry, Sensei. I've embarrassed myself again, making you listen to me ramble...
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Huh? You mean like the Martial Arts Research Club or the Modern Martial Arts Club? I do remember seeing those names around.
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): But that's a strange thing to say, Sensei.
> Screen shakes
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): The first rule of kung fu is that it must be performed by a waiter or a chef!
- "And there has to be a big, open space in the center of the restaurant!"
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Yes! And there should be a second floor!
- "Then a person breaks the handrail and falls down!"
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Exactly, Sensei! A huge fight breaks out as people come rushing down the stairs!
- "While the main character nonchalantly finishes their meal!"
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): But then they provoke the main character into joining the fight!
- Reijo (Black Tortoise Promenade): Yes! It's perfect! The strongest chef handles everything!
> Reijo's eyes positively sparkled as we carried on the conversation...
> We kept it going in perfect sync, both equally invested in the scenario.
> We carried on the enthusiastic banter until the day was almost over...
