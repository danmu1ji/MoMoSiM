# Reijo\n\nA member of Shanhaijing Senior High School, Reijo is the manager of the main branch of the Genbu Trading Company, Rumi's assistant, and a security guard. She is quite a kung fu fanatic, and often tries to imitate phrases and actions of a certain famous martial artist.
