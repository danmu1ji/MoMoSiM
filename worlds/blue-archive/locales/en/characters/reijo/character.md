# Reijo

## Description

A member of Shanhaijing Senior High School, Reijo is the manager of the main branch of the Genbu Trading Company, Rumi's assistant, and a security guard. She is quite a kung fu fanatic, and often tries to imitate phrases and actions of a certain famous martial artist. Contrary to her cool and detached appearance, she sometimes shows a fiery passion.

## Appearance

Reijo has shoulder-length violet-red hair that fades to white, styled in a bun with two ahoge; she has red eyes and a mole beside her left eye.
