# Mimori — Relationship Story 5

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Welcome, Sensei!
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Thank you for coming! I wanted to show you the results of my recent training.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): If you please, would you mind accompanying me to the kitchen?
> The Inner Discipline Club Kitchen
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): This is the Inner Discipline Club's kitchen.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): It's used for cooking practice, meal prep, training of the mind and body and many...other...things?
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Why is the kitchen such a mess?!
- Kitchen Manager (Hyakkiyako): Ah! Um... H-Hey Mimori! Well... The Mouryo had a little celebration in the kitchen last night...
- Kitchen Manager (Hyakkiyako): So I'm afraid the kitchen is closed until we get it cleaned up. You'll have to come back tomorrow.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): ...
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): I'm afraid I cannot do that.
- Hyakkiyako Inner Discipline Club Member (Inner Discipline Club): Mimori?!
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): All this calls for is some good, old-fashioned elbow grease!
- "If that's the case, I'd love to help."
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): What?! You're going to clean, Sensei?!
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): I absolutely cannot abide by that! Please allow me to take care of the cleaning! This kitchen is the responsibility of the Inner Discipline Club!
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): A real, proper lady would have been able to predict the Mouryo's festivities and the state of the kitchen before Sensei arrived!
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): And knowing that, a proper lady would have arrived at the scene of the crime in the early hours and made sure it was sparkling before your visit!
- "That's asking a lot of yourself!"
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): But... but...
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): It's due to my inaction that this mess occurred...
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): I could hardly ask you, my guest, to help with my mistake...
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): ...
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): If you insist, Sensei, then... I will accept.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Thank you for your help, Sensei.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Th-Then! I must match Sensei's dedication!
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): I, Mizuha Mimori, as the Vice President of the Inner Discipline Club...
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Will pour my heart and soul into this! Serious-Mode Mimori!
- "You look plenty serious normally though!"
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): You have yet to see me serious! I must embody the proper lady with everything I am!
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Which means, I must use "that."
> Screen shakes
- "Is it a high-tech cleaning tool?!"
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Ta-da! Let us begin!
> Mimori in serious mode(?) was truly...beyond amazing. Her energy and zeal bordered upon terrifying.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Whew... Thank you for your hard work, Sensei.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): ...What? I did all of the work? Haha...
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): It was an even effort, Sensei! And you were just an assistant anyways!
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Since we're all done, allow me to take this off.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): ...I sh-shouldn't take it off? Wh-What do you mean you think it's "cute"?
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Such joking is not proper behavior, Sensei!
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Oh... It's not a joke...?
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): ...
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): I-I'm sure you're tired! Why don't we go relax?
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Please wait here. I'll bring out some tea.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): I hope you enjoy your drink, Sensei. I've brought some complementary wagashi as well.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): This wagashi was actually the reason I invited you over. I've been working very hard on the recipe.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): What do you think? Do you like the taste?
- "It's incredible! And it smells so good too!"
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): What a relief! I'm happy to hear that.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): What? My...apron?
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): ...
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): It was the day I decided to become a proper lady, actually. I was inspired by a manga I was reading and went out and bought it.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): It's a bit old now, but it still does its job very well.
- "And it's still in really good condition!"
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Heehee. I'm glad it looks that way, but you can see all the patches and frayed edges I've fixed if you look a little closer.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): But to me...to me it's still perfect.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): I can't quite explain it, but I find it comforting when I have it on.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Cleaning, cooking, laundry, tailoring, mind reading... Its uses are endless.
- "...I'm sorry, did you say mind reading?"
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Ah! D-Did I?
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): You must have misheard! Or I misspoke!
> I kept pestering Mimori about what she knew about mind reading, but she was too skilled at changing the topic.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Ahem! Anyway, I bought this apron many years ago, and I actually had to fold it up because it was too big!
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): I made a promise to myself, then. I promised myself I would be a proper lady by the time I could fit my apron properly.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): I'm a little embarrassed, saying that out loud.
- "It's a great sentiment. Don't be embarrassed."
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): ...Do you truly think so?
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): You see, at that time, I believed in my heart that I could achieve anything with the proper mindset and hard work.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): But here I am, big enough to fit this apron perfectly.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Am I the proper lady I hoped to be by this time? Would the younger Mimori be proud of the current me?
> As I was opening my mouth to reassure Mimori she was giving it her all...
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Now then, Sensei. You weren't going to tell me I'm giving it my all and that's what matters, were you?
- "H-How did you know?!"
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Heehee... It's right out of the Sensei playbook! Although I appreciate it nonetheless.
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): H-Huh?! I never said anything like that!
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Do not wink at me, Sensei! I have no secret mind-reading powers!
- Mimori (Inner Discipline Club): Sensei? Sensei?!
> Afterwards, a very flustered Mimori tried to explain herself while we both enjoyed the rest of the tea and wagashi.
