## MomoTalk 2

- Kaho: My name is Kuwakami Kaho. I have the honor of being the vice president of Allied Hyakkiyako Academy's Yin-Yang Club.
- Sensei: I am also the Lead Strategist of Hyakkiyako's Tourism and Culture Public Relations Support Team.

- Sensei reply option: Strategist... Public...relations...?

- Sensei: Huh? What matter...?

- Sensei: Immediately...? Huh?!
