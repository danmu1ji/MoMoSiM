> I wrapped up work at the Yin-Yang Club faster than expected, so I headed over to the break room a little early.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Ah, Sensei?
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Hadn't we agreed to meet a little later? You're early...
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): No concerns at all. Tidying up took a little longer than expected today.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): I was cleaning with more vigor than usual, since I knew you were visiting.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Although, I erred on the side of overly vigorous, and time seems to have gotten away from me.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): My apologies for the mess.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): I could never impose...
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): ...Hm? The quicker we finish, the faster we can get to drinking tea?
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): I see... It'd be difficult for you to relax knowing the break room is in this state...
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): In that case, I'll accept your helping hand.
> We were hard at work cleaning the break room, when...
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): I must take...extra care with this one.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Ah, yes.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Perhaps, as the vice president of the Yin-Yang Club, I shouldn't fall victim to the temptations of merchandise and material goods...
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): But this particular item is incredibly special to me.
- "Special? You've piqued my interest."
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Ah. Would you like to see it, Sensei?
- "Really? Can I?"
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Of course. It's of no issue.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Here you go. Please be careful... It's somewhat heavy.
- "You got this from Hyakkiyako students?"
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Heehee. Yes.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): It was around when I first began my work as the Yin-Yang Club's Lead Strategist.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): In the shopping district, they held a festival that I assisted with...
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): I wasn't quite used to the work yet, so I felt I was more of a liability who didn't understand what the job required of me.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): But just as the festival ended, a group of students and merchants who participated gifted me this.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): They told me, "This event went so smoothly because of the Yin-Yang Club's help!" and, "It was such a relief to have you here," and even, "As expected of the Yin-Yang Club." And so on...
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): I... They were so kind and appreciative. It was overwhelming.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): I was very flustered at the time. After all, I hadn't done anything particularly praiseworthy.
- "I'm sure you put your all into helping, Kaho."
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): J-Just as I do today, I was only striving to help host successful events to further spread Hyakkiyako Academy's values and culture.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Regardless, looking at this certificate of appreciation instills a sense of pride in me.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Receiving it made me feel that all my efforts until now weren't in vain...that I'd been able to share my passion with everyone, even if just a little.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): I was simply relieved that I was doing my job as our lead strategist.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): I never want to forget that feeling, so I take extra care in polishing it whenever I do my daily cleaning.
- "It really does have a lot of meaning for you!"
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Heehee. It absolutely does.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): We've finished cleaning the break room.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Thank you for helping me, Sensei.
- "In that case, should we have that cup of tea?"
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Heehee. I believe the tea will taste even more delicious than usual today.
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): I'll be enjoying it in a clean, organized room, and together with Sensei, on top of that...
- Kaho (Yin-Yang Club): Now then, allow me a moment to prepare.
> Kaho and I enjoyed a very relaxing tea break in the room we had cleaned to perfection.
