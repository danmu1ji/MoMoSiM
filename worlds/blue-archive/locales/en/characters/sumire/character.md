# Sumire

## Description

Sumire is the leader of the Training Club at Millennium Science School. One who is typically working on her training, a muscle-brained girl who believes all matters in the world can be solved with muscles and exercise.

## Appearance

Sumire has long black hair tied into a ponytail with a purple scrunchie and purple eyes.
