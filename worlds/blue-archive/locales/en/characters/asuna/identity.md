# Asuna\n\nAn agent of Millennium's secret agency "C&C". With a callsign of Zero-one, Asuna is a veteran who has frequently overcome many difficulties with brute intuition. On missions, she's very openly trusting of people and breaks any sense of doubt or uncertainty.
