> I arrived at Gehenna's downtown district for a late-night meeting with Hina.
> No one was around at that hour.
> It was almost creepy how empty the streets were.
- "(What could Hina want at this time of night?)"
- Hina (Prefect Team): Sensei.
- Hina (Prefect Team): H-Hiniature? As in, miniature?
- Hina (Prefect Team): Don't call me that ever again. It makes me uncomfortable.
- Hina (Prefect Team): (nod)
- Hina (Prefect Team): I apologize for calling you here this late.
- Hina (Prefect Team): I am...rather famous around Gehenna. I get recognized wherever I go.
- Hina (Prefect Team): Sometimes, I feel like my every move is being watched.
- Hina (Prefect Team): If I were spotted with you, it would only cause trouble.
- Hina (Prefect Team): Hence why I asked you to meet me at this hour.
- "It's almost like you're an idol, Hina."
- Hina (Prefect Team): A-An idol...? I wouldn't say that...
- Hina (Prefect Team): I'm not the cute or glamorous type.
- Hina (Prefect Team): ...
- Hina (Prefect Team): I can't say the analogy is completely incorrect. I know what it's like to have no time to myself and be constantly aware of my surroundings.
- Hina (Prefect Team): You really do know how to put your foot in your mouth, don't you?!
- Hina (Prefect Team): You really do know how to put your foot in your mouth, don't you?!
- Hina (Prefect Team): Being the adult here doesn't give you license to say whatever you want.
Learn to behave!
> Hina kept yelling at me.
- Hina (Prefect Team): ...Huh?
- Hina (Prefect Team): ...Huh?
- Hina (Prefect Team): Why are you...acting so serious about this?
- Hina (Prefect Team): ...
- Hina (Prefect Team): I-I wasn't trying to call you a liar. It's just...
- Hina (Prefect Team): ...I know better than anyone that I'm not cute.
- Hina (Prefect Team): ...Enough. I didn't call you to talk about this kind of nonsense!
- Hina (Prefect Team): Let's get down to business.
- Hina (Prefect Team): (I always lose my focus when I'm around Sensei.)
- Hina (Prefect Team): Allow me to explain why I asked you here.
- Hina (Prefect Team): I wanted to discuss Gehenna's security for this quarter, and ask for advice on developing a coordinated investigation plan for next quarter.
- "You mean to talk business."
- Hina (Prefect Team): But of course. Why else would I ask you to meet me secretly in the middle of the night?
- "I thought it was a date."
- Hina (Prefect Team): Sensei...
- "I mean, let's get to work!"
> I read the document Hina brought and discussed it with her.
- Hina (Prefect Team): That's an interesting perspective. Thanks for the insight.
- "I feel like I didn't help much..."
- Hina (Prefect Team): Nonsense. Outsider perspectives help us stay objective.
- Hina (Prefect Team): Of course I'd find your opinion to be valuable.
- Hina (Prefect Team): It's not often I get the unfiltered feedback of someone with experience.
- "Does that mean you trust me?"
- Hina (Prefect Team): Wh-Why wouldn't I...? Granted, you are somewhat flaky for someone your age.
- Hina (Prefect Team): Though not to the extent that would dissuade me from asking your advice again. Given that we only meet at night, of course.
- Hina (Prefect Team): Thank you, Sensei.
- Hina (Prefect Team): Thank you, Sensei.
- Hina (Prefect Team): *sigh* It's getting late. I should head back and get some rest.
- Hina (Prefect Team): See you later, Sensei.
> A tired-looking Hina said her goodbyes and returned home.
