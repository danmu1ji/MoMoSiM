> Schale Office
> The next day.
> Ritsu had been scheduled to come in 30 minutes prior, but still hadn't appeared.
> Where could she have gone?
- "She's not picking up her phone..."
> As I worriedly paced, I looked out the window.
- "...Ritsu?"
> I could see her outside.
> Ritsu was tapping on a boulder in the front yard with a hammer and chisel.
- "...What is she doing?"
> Schale Front Yard
> I rushed outside.
> And I saw Ritsu chiseling away at a rock that dwarfed her frame.
- "Uh, Ritsu...?"
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Oh! It's Sensei!
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Hello!
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Oh! I'm the Student Rep today, right?
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Yeah! This rock spoke to me!
- "The rock spoke to you...?"
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Yes! Every rock has its own shape underneath.
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): This one told me that it had true beauty lying within!
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Which is why...
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): ...I broke it!
- "You what?!"
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Its true form can only come out after you chip away at the outer shell.
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): So I had to work through the rough and bumpy surface to reveal its true form!
- "Anyway... Is this within school rules?"
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Hm? Of course n—
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): ...
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): So, it's good to see you, Sensei!
- "You dodged my question!"
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Can you boost me up?
- "But you're already climbing on me!"
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Higher!
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Heehee. I wasn't tall enough to hit the top of the rock!
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Now I can sculpt properly! Thank you, Sensei! Close your eyes!
> Ritsu continued to sculpt the rock while positioned on my shoulders.
> An endless stream of stone dust fell from above.
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Okay, just have to refine this part...!
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): And, finally, this side needs a little more...
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Oh my. Sensei, are you okay? Your face is covered in dust! Don't lick it!
- "Pfff. Pfff."
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Wh-What should we do?
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Ah! What if we rotate? I'll sit on your shoulders, but you face the other way?
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Then the dust will fall on your back!
- "No! We shouldn't!"
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Eh, really? I thought it'd be a good idea.
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): I'll wrap this up, then!
> At last, she finished the sculpture.
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Okay! And my work is...done!
- "Looks great, Ritsu."
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Thank you, Sensei. Heehee.
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Are you trying to tell me how you feel about it?
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): You don't have to do that!
- "Hm?"
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): You just saw it for the first time, and I'm right here.
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): I think that would make speaking your feelings difficult, huh?
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Give yourself time to think about how it makes you feel.
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Especially how it makes you feel when I'm not staring at you!
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): And, however long it takes you to think it out is fine. Once you have the right words...
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): ...Then you can tell me how you feel, Sensei!
- "That's very considerate, Ritsu."
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Speaking of which, would you like me to move it? I can put it in your office.
- "It's an interesting offer..."
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): Ah...
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): ...Thank you, Sensei.
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): I think this has been a very fulfilling day!
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): ...Ah.
- "It'll be fun. Let's embark on a journey of paperwork!"
- Ritsu (Free Trade Cartel): ...Wah!
> I assisted Ritsu with the Student Rep work until night fell.
