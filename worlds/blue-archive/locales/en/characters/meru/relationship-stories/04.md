# Meru — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Meru/Relationship_Story/4

> While visiting Red Winter Academy, I decided to pay a visit to the Knowledge Liberation Front.
> Meru's MomoTalk had concerned me a bit...
> Inside the Library
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Huh? Sensei?! You're actually here?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): If I knew you were coming, I would've prepped at least a little!
- "I didn't mean to be a nuisance! I'll just head back..."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): No, no, no! Don't say that, I'll feel bad.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...What? That was the point?! What a rotten adult...!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Ahem! Anyways... What to do? I don't have anything I can recommend to you in the Knowledge Liberation Front's library.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): It's filled to the brim with things you won't like...or maybe, knowing Sensei, you'll actually really like them...
- "Now you have my attention."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): I-I can't show them to you! I'd rather...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Oh, yeah! I have an idea!
> I followed the suddenly excited Meru deeper into the library.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): I welcome you...to the Knowledge Liberation Front's workshop!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Well... It's more like my rehabilitation center rather than a battlefield.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): What do I mean by that? Um... Well...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): I brought you all the way here, so I guess I should tell you.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...It's a bit embarrassing, but sometimes the act of creation doesn't go as I imagined. Most of the time, actually.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Whenever that happens, I get discouraged, like I'm backed into a corner.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): The more trapped I start to feel, the more I feel like it's time to lay down the pen.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): In a way, I guess it is a battlefield. I'm a soldier who just lost a battle. Her morale is low, and she doesn't have the energy to keep moving forward. But her superiors demand she pick up her weapon and go on fighting.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): When that battlefield gets to be too much, I come here to start all over again.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): It's kind of training and mental rehabilitation all in one.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Does that sound totally crazy? Creating is what brought me down, but the only way to bring myself back up is through art!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): How is it that creative types all have a screw loose? Hahaha!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Don't congratulate me, Sensei! This is where original sin itself was born!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): The original sin of doing everything because I love to do it.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): What? The things we love to do...can still be hard...?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): You're really hitting the mark today, Sensei.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Geez...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― I'm not doing anything that amazing.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― I just don't have anything else I can do,
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― or anything else I'm good at...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― Maybe that's why...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― Why I keep wanting to create.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― But this, right now... It doesn't feel wrong or bad.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― Here, in this workshop,
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― I feel like I can really talk to you.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― Because...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― Creation necessitates change, right?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― And you, you're someone who helps me change, Sensei.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― Ugh...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― I just said something super cringey.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― Uh, just pretend you didn't hear anything! Forget it! Forget it right now!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ― Ahahaha!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Didn't I just tell you to drop it?!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Ugh, have it your way! How do I explain this...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): These days, I've been noticing things that would normally fly under my radar.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Things that would barely catch my attention, incite interest and sketchable moments in my mind.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Yeah... If that makes sense. I've been wanting to draw a...wider variety of things.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): And I think a lot of that...is thanks to your influence.
- "Who? Me?"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Yes, you! So I need you to take responsibility for changing me into the person I am today.
- "I really don't know about that one, Meru."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): *sigh* Well, I knew you would say something like that.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Regardless of how predictable that response was, it was still an incorrect one. Bzzzt. One warning.
- "A warning? Since when can you give out warnings?"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Since now! So you have to help me with my manuscript!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...? How else are you going to atone for the warning I just gave you?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Are you really going to tuck your tail behind your legs and go back to Schale with a WARNING?
- "I mean, I don't think I would care—"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): I'm sure that would ruin your day, right?
- "Like I said, I don't really—"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Right? RIGHT?
- "...Yeah, I guess you're right."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): I knew it! Now then, let's see what I can ask you to do first!
> And so I spent the rest of my day with Meru, helping her with her manuscript...
