# Meru — Relationship Story 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): What is my evil plan? Good question!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Heeheehee... Last time, you showed me a glimpse of your world.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): So this time, I'll be bringing you into my world!
- "I'd like to invoke my right of refusal, your honor."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Overruled! No right to remain silent either!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Your normal rights won't be recognized in my court! Hahaha!
> Despite my protests, Meru grabbed ahold of my arm and pulled me along into "her world."
> Arcade
- "This is...?"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Heh. Heh heh. It may seem like an average arcade...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): But if we walk down the stairs hidden behind the crane game...!
- "I see. A secret figurine shop."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Huh? You know what figurines are, Sensei?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): How unexpected... I didn't know you had these kinds of interests.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Take your time and peruse the shop! This place was originally used for trading or selling used manga.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): But over time, people started trading other, similar items. Eventually, it just became a general place for otaku-related goods.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Plastic models, figurines, toys, tokusatsu scripts, animation stills, manga drafts...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): But the area that really gained popularity was the figurines section.
- "That's cool, actually... Wow, look at the modeling of this dress...!"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...Don't have too much fun now.
> Shopping Mall
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Sensei! You're beginning to imitate my mannerisms. Adults really are fast learners.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): But this isn't just any mall! It's different from other malls!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): It has a pop-up store!
- "I'm not well-versed in the latest trends like that..."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): It's a limited-time, brick-and-mortar shop that sells specialized goods!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): They're called "pop-ups" because they're only around for a limited time.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Pop-up stores for tv shows, music, and other fancy goods have been...popping up all over the place!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): But this one is the good stuff! It handles limited time goods that only us and those like us can truly appreciate!
- "I see. I noticed that the customers are all very well-mannered."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Good eye, Sensei!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Everyone is here to purchase what they want, and get out! They don't want to waste energy or time talking to others!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Such behavior is the cornerstone of otaku culture!
- "Oh. That person is buying a lot at once."
- Bulk Purchase Customer: Heh heh... I can probably list this item online for one-point-five... No! Maybe even twice the retail price! My profits will skyrocket!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): It's a scalper! Get 'em!
> Cafe
- "Haven't we been here before?"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): It is indeed a cafe, but this one is different from the last one we went to.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): You can feel the difference, can't you?
- "The interior design is...made for otakus?"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Indeed! They're doing a collaboration event!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): They have life-sized stands, special gift cards, a sneak preview of the upcoming movie, merch...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Oh, and the menu also has special collaboration items! What would you like?
- "Let's see... I'll have the 'Recharging Officer's Americano Set.'"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Then I'll have the "Pink Energizer Frenzy MAX Special!"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Whew, I feel so at home.
- "Quiet, isn't it?"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Of course. These kinds of places tend to attract more introverted, quiet types.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Okay. I have something to tell you, Sensei. But I'm only telling you!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): One of my dreams...is creating something and getting to collaborate with a cafe, just like this.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Most of my works are derivatives right now, but one day an original work of mine will be flying off the stands!
- "That's an amazing dream."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...It absolutely is! Although I know the path there will be treacherous to navigate.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): I wish art was as easy as just sitting down and creating something every once in a while.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): I know, I know. Life isn't that easy, so you don't have to give me that look... It's not like every act of creation can always be fun.
- "That was an unexpected answer."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): In my case, no matter how many obstacles I face, it's harder for me to not create an idea I've had, than to just let it go. It's not always because I like doing it.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): What does that mean? Hm...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Think of it this way. Sometimes, a scene or an idea will blossom in my brain.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Like the sketchable moments! Something will trigger an idea: a book, an anime, anything really, and that moment becomes a scene in my head!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): I can't help but to then start building the next scene, or even the entire plot! I suppose letting the idea go would be an option...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): But it feels almost impossible to just set them free, you know?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Let's say you come across a small bird with its head stuck in a tin can. What would you do?
- "I guess... I'd help it?"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Exactly! That's how I feel about my sketchable moments! They're just like the bird with the tin can.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): I can't just walk by and let it go! I have to rescue the bird, and see it take glorious flight.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Huh? Don't stories and scenes pop up in my head all the time?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Duh. That's the point Sensei! That's why my artist's soul can never stop burning!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): If I can't express these sketchable moments, these scenes that play in my head, I'll go insane!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): But lately, I've been feeling something strange... Like something new, stronger than a sketchable moment, is trying to burst forth in my mind. It's been a real struggle to...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Wait, what am I saying...?!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): W-We've been here for way too long! Let's get out of here!
- "What? But we haven't even been here an hour..."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): An hour is way too long for an otaku! We're leaving! Now!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Whew... I can finally cool down.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Am I doing all right? Well... O-Of course! I'm with you, after all!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...M-Maybe it's the other way around, actually...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Th-That's okay! I found my way here so I can get back by myself too!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Wh-What? Don't be so distant? It's fine! I just want to be by myself for a bit!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): I did this last time as well? And you think...it may be your fault?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): No! It's definitely not your fault, Sensei! This is...um... It's none of your business! I mean! Not like that!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Anyways, I gotta go! Bye, Sensei!
> Despite being entertained by the rare sight of Meru being flustered, I let her go and waved until her quickly receding back disappeared from sight.
