> I didn't know which pen would be good, so I bought one that the clerk recommended.
> Maybe it's because I talked with the clerk for so long, but I ended up walking out with a bunch of freebies...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Welcome, Sensei! You really kept me waiting!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): All of my ideas and creativity nearly wilted away!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): So, so? Did you get the tablet pen? Did you?
- "Of course. Here you go! One tablet pen!"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): All right! Now I can...can...?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): S-Sensei...?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): No, it's actually the opposite? I don't know... This is too much...!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): A tablet pen of this quality... I've only heard of rumors of such things at Red Winter.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Are you sure this is for me? Something this expensive? Really? Is it?
- "As long as you use it well, the price doesn't matter."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Then I couldn't possibly decline! A personal gift from Sensei!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): What else is in here? Huh?! So many spare pen nibs?!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Do you think we're struggling at Red Winter or something?! Well... I can't completely deny that, actually...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): We can find grenades, excavator blades, and hydraulic equipment super easily!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): But things like high-quality tissues, soap, ball-point pens, or really good pudding are really hard to come by!
- "Oh yeah... I can see that being the case at Red Winter..."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): A-Ahem. Anyways, thank you.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): I feel like I need to repay you somehow...
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): What do teachers like as gifts?
- "Don't worry. It's natural for a teacher to help their students."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Come on! If you want to play that game, I can say that it's only natural for a student to want to repay a teacher's favor!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...You're treating me like a child, Sensei! I won't allow it!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Have it your way! I'll utilize your gift of spare pen nibs, and I'll make you something myself!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): ...What's my plan? Heeheehee.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Sensei's very own custom portrait!
- "Oh! Oh... No, it's really okay."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): No, it's not! Just think of my services as one of those street vendors who draw caricatures for ridiculous prices!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): But way better! Because it's free, and from me!
- "I appreciate it, really. But I don't want to put you through all that trouble."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): What do you mean? Why would this be trouble for me at all?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Quite the opposite, actually! It's always good to practice with new subjects.
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): This once-in-a-lifetime chance may never come again! So just sit still and accept my gift!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Do you intend to resist to the end?! That only makes my artists' soul burn brighter and stronger!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Forget about repayment! Just let me draw you!
- "Somebody, help! A sketch monster is trying to draw me!"
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Heeheehee. Cry all you want! There's no one around to hear your pleas!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): The library of the Knowledge Liberation Front is secluded from the rest of the campus! It's just you and me!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Huh?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Wait. I'm stumped. I can only have one or the other? The pen or Sensei's sketch?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): But I can't even sketch you without the pen in the first place!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Ugh! Is this how you adults handle things? Manipulative! Unfair! Meanie-head!
- "Heh... Sometimes adults need to be mean from time to time."
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Is this the end of the road for my artists' soul? Is my flame flickering out?
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): Fine! You've won this battle! Hand over the tablet pen and the extra nibs! You live to see another day!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): But as long as I have my pen, I won't give up. I WILL sketch you, Sensei!
- Meru (Knowledge Liberation Front): To achieve my dreams, I will stalk your every breath! I will study every line of your face!
> I spent the rest of the day running from Meru as she attempted to "study" me. Before we knew it, the sun had begun to set.
