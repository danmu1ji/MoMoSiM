# Meru

## Description

A student of the Red Winter Federal Academy, and a member of the "Knowledge Liberation Front" Library Committee. Unlike Momiji, a fellow Knowledge Liberation Front member, Meru not only enjoys consuming literary works, but also creates them herself. Her pen name is "Meruri". Meru's namamono doujins are often inspired by the relationships between students who either seem to be on good terms or especially at odds with with each other. Her works are particularly known outside the school for featuring rivalries between Trinity and Gehenna students.
