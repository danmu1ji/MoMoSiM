# Mina

## Description

Executive Officer of Genryumon, Shanhaijing's student council, and the Student Council President's personal bodyguard. Despite her calm and collected demeanor, she's a hardcore classic noir movie buff. She likes to quote scenes and lines from movies, although often incorrectly. She gets into trouble trying to be cool at inappropriate times, but Genryumon members do their best to mitigate the damage.
