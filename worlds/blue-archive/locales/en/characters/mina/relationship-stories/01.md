- Mina (Genryumon): So I got to thinking how to make the coolest entrance possible...
- Mina (Genryumon): I've got to give you the right impression, after all...
- Mina (Genryumon): And I was thinking so hard I lost track of time, and here we are...

- Mina (Genryumon): But you've got to understand, Sensei. This could have happened to anyone.

- Mina (Genryumon): We've got to figure out what we're going to call each other.

- Mina (Genryumon): The titles two people use for each other is more important than you may realize.
- Mina (Genryumon): They reveal how those two people view each other.
- Mina (Genryumon): I'm sure most students are happy calling you 'Sensei'...
- Mina (Genryumon): But that's not enough for me.
- Mina (Genryumon): Shouldn't our relationship be more special than the ones you have with just anyone?
- Mina (Genryumon): That's my thinking.
- "I-Is that so?"
- Mina (Genryumon): It is.
- Mina (Genryumon): I want something that doesn't feel too specific to Genryumon. Something that shows the bond we share...
- Mina (Genryumon): It's no simple matter.
- Mina (Genryumon): But the harder the case, the more satisfaction when you crack it.
