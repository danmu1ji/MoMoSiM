> I was on my way back after wrapping up some administrative work at Shanhaijing...
- "Those clouds..."
> Screen shakes
> *rain pouring*
- "Whoa, it's pouring!"
- Mina (Genryumon): Looks like you could use an umbrella.
- "Mina!"
- "You knew it was going to rain?"
- Mina (Genryumon): Heh... My old war wounds ache before a storm.
- "(Old war wounds, huh...?)"
- Mina (Genryumon): I'll walk you home, Sensei. Shall we?
> And so, Mina and I strolled along the rainy streets of Shanhaijing.
> Her expression was one of barely concealed joy...
- Mina (Genryumon): Hm?
- Mina (Genryumon): Well, you see...
- Mina (Genryumon): ― That's right... I love the world of rainy days.
- Mina (Genryumon): ― Everything becomes so simple.
- Mina (Genryumon): ― Black and white.
- Mina (Genryumon): ― The world of noir...
- Mina (Genryumon): ― The real world is
- Mina (Genryumon): ― so bright and colorful, it's blinding.
- Mina (Genryumon): ― But most people don't realize this truth...
- Mina (Genryumon): ― And I'm no exception.
- Mina (Genryumon): ― But the world of noir is not like that.
- Mina (Genryumon): ― It's easy to understand.
- Mina (Genryumon): ― But...then again...
- Mina (Genryumon): ― An umbrella, at least, should be bright and colorful
- Mina (Genryumon): ― so you never lose your way.
- Mina (Genryumon): Right? That's how I feel about Madam Kisaki.
- "How's that?"
- Mina (Genryumon): Madam Kisaki is the reason I joined Genryumon.
- Mina (Genryumon): I don't care about money or fame. Those things fade away in the end.
- Mina (Genryumon): But the president... She's a hard-boiled operator to the core!
- Mina (Genryumon): She's got charisma to bring you to your knees, but she's always willing to hear you out!
- Mina (Genryumon): And she's got a great build for giving a nice big hug. Isn't that the best?
- Mina (Genryumon): It's not just me. Most of Genryumon have come together because of her.
- "(I didn't realize Genryumon was a Kisaki fan club...)"
- Mina (Genryumon): Ahem. I lost my cool for a moment there. Apologies.
- Mina (Genryumon): But yes, you need a brightly colored umbrella in your life.
- "Yes. Even in the noir of a rainstorm."
- Mina (Genryumon): ...You're pretty sharp, Sensei.
- Mina (Genryumon): You always have something interesting to say.
- Mina (Genryumon): And we get to talk about all kinds of things I'd never think of, or wouldn't be able to say normally...
- Mina (Genryumon): Huh...? Where are you going, Sensei? The umbrella...!
- Mina (Genryumon): ...
- Mina (Genryumon): I get it...
- Mina (Genryumon): I saw this in an old movie once.
- Mina (Genryumon): Sensei, you really do know your stuff.
- Mina (Genryumon): Ah... What a great day.
- Mina (Genryumon): A great day for tap dancing!
- Mina (Genryumon): Hahaha. Hahahaha!
> Mina's face lit up as she ran towards me.
> We wiled away the time singing and dancing in the rain.
