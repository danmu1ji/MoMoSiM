> Plum Blossom Garden
- Plum Blossom Garden Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): Hey! It's Mina!
- Plum Blossom Garden Student B (Plum Blossom Garden): Mina! Give me a piggyback ride!
- Mina (Genryumon): Heh... How're you doing today, my cute little soldiers?
- Plum Blossom Garden Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): She said something weird again...
- Plum Blossom Garden Student B (Plum Blossom Garden): I don't wanna play if you say things like that!
- Mina (Genryumon): No no no! What're you talking about? I don't talk weird!
- Mina (Genryumon): Oh, I know... Everyone, line up. I brought gifts!
- "I didn't know you like kids, Mina."
- Mina (Genryumon): ...Huh?!
- Mina (Genryumon): A-Actually! The Plum Blossom Garden kids are...
- Mina (Genryumon): ...the future of Shanhaijing!
- Plum Blossom Garden Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): That's what Rumi says.
- Plum Blossom Garden Student B (Plum Blossom Garden): She always brings us fruits and snacks, and she lets us have as much as we want!
- Mina (Genryumon): Black Tortoise Promenade snakes... Trying to win the kids over with food...!
- Mina (Genryumon): What an underhanded tactic. I won't stand for it!
- "Isn't that a bit much? Everyone loves kids..."
- Mina (Genryumon): Sensei, are you taking their side? Are you here to spy on me for them?!
- "I would never spy on my students..."
- Mina (Genryumon): Hmph... If you say so.
- Mina (Genryumon): Anyway, kids... Here you go! New clothes for you!
- Plum Blossom Garden Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): Mmm... This looks like Genryumon's costumes.
- Plum Blossom Garden Student B (Plum Blossom Garden): Look at the flowers on these shoes... I never saw any like that before.
- Mina (Genryumon): Heh. Genryumon had these made just for you.
- Mina (Genryumon): And, unlike the gifts from the Black Tortoise Promenade, you can enjoy these for a good long time.
- Plum Blossom Garden Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): This is lame. Got anything else?
- Plum Blossom Garden Student B (Plum Blossom Garden): I want pretty things. Gimme something pretty!
- Mina (Genryumon): What?!
- Mina (Genryumon): You don't understand what you're saying here. Sit down, and let me lay it all out for you...
- Mina (Genryumon): These clothes were hand-stitched by the best tailor in Genryumon.
- Mina (Genryumon): The cloth carries within it the spirit, traditions, and values of Shanhaijing and Genryumon!
- Mina (Genryumon): Every stitch, every design, is heavy with symbolism.
- Mina (Genryumon): And the shoes! Black is the color of Genryumon, because it represents resoluteness, determination, integrity, and fairness.
- Mina (Genryumon): We made this to help you, the children of the Plum Blossom Gardens, grow into great members of Shanhaijing someday.
- Plum Blossom Garden Student A (Plum Blossom Garden): Wake me up when you're done talking...
- Plum Blossom Garden Student B (Plum Blossom Garden): Um... I heard that black roses mean goodbye. Are you going away, Mina?
- Mina (Genryumon): Th-That's not what I mean at all!
- Mina (Genryumon): Oooh... My back...
- "Did you have fun playing with the children today?"
- Mina (Genryumon): It was time well spent.
- Mina (Genryumon): They crowded around me, begged for hugs...
- Mina (Genryumon): Pulled my hair... Screamed in my ear... Ignored me when I talked... But hey, they're kids.
- "They give me a run for my money sometimes, too."
- Mina (Genryumon): In a way, it's like paying back a debt, right?
- Mina (Genryumon): We were all kids at some point, after all.
- "..."
- Mina (Genryumon): What's with the look, Sensei?
- Mina (Genryumon): Surprised to hear such wisdom coming from me?
- Mina (Genryumon): Heh. I'm just glad you got to see what I'm made of today.
- Mina (Genryumon): When I see those kids, I start thinking...
- Mina (Genryumon): What kind of students will they be when they grow up? Will I be someone they can look up to?
- Mina (Genryumon): Ah, but you're an adult. You probably gave up those kinds of thoughts long ago.
- Mina (Genryumon): Really? I didn't know that.
- Mina (Genryumon): I guess I do see that side of you sometimes.
- Mina (Genryumon): But you always seem like you know what you're doing.
- "I'm glad I seem that way!"
- Mina (Genryumon): You're saying you don't?
- "On the inside, I'm like...a duck trying not to sink."
- Mina (Genryumon): Most ducks would probably just call that swimming.
- Mina (Genryumon): Oh, what's the matter? Did I strike a nerve?
- Mina (Genryumon): A dame once told me we're all children still, deep inside. No matter how many years go by...
- "You've gotta make peace with yourself. It's the only peace any of us get in this mad, mad world."
- Mina (Genryumon): By the time that first drop hits your head, it's too late. You've just got to accept the rain.
> Mina and I shared this and other hard-boiled wisdom as we strolled along the street back to Schale.
