# Megu — official English introduction

A student of Gehenna Academy, Megu is at the heart of the Hot Springs Development Department, and a work team leader at the development site. She believes from the bottom of her heart that "demolition" is an answer to any problem. Megu is trusted by fellow members of the Hot Springs Development Department for her lively personality and outstanding demolition skills.
