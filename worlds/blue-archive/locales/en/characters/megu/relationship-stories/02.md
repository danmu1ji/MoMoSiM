> I found Megu in a gloomy corner of a back alley.
> Megu, fidgeting, was looking around the alley nervously.
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Sensei! You're here. Good. I've been waiting!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Oh? You think I look serious?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Well, I am! This is very very serious!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Oh, you wanna know what's going on?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Um... So...
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): ...
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): ...Nothing! Nothing is going on! Forever!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Right! I knew I could count on you, Sensei!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): You understood right away!
- "..."
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Anyway, before the nothing, we were trying to build a hot spring in a spot the president found.
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): But as soon as we started blasting, a whole bunch of troops came rushing at us from all directions. More than usual, even!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): I just assumed we'd have to fight again, but the president told us to scatter and hide.
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): So... Now I'm hiding. Like this.
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): And then... And then...
- "And... What happened next?"
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): I stayed... Hiding, like this. They told me not to get caught, so...
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): So I'm just supposed to stay put here... But... It's really hard! It's hard to do!
- "..."
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Sensei... How are you supposed to stay put? I really don't get it!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Hmm.... Is that right?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Maybe!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Time's passing so slowly! I feel like I'm trapped!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): This is worse than when we get put in the Prefect Team's cells sometimes! At least then I had things to do!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): And I was with some other club members, too.
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): But now! Now...!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Ughhhhh!!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): ...What?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Sensei? What did you say?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): ...Oh, this?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Um, it's just a decoration that we were going to put in the hot springs when it was done...
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): What was the word, again? Ken...dama?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): I think that's what she called it...
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): I don't know much about it! I was just holding onto it so we could use it when we were done.
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Why do you ask?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Sensei, do you know something about it?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): What? ... Oh?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): This is a toy? I didn't know that! How does it work?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Oh, that would be great! Please show me!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): ...!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Wow! Wow wow! What is this?!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): That's amazing! How are you doing that?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Can I try? Sensei, I wanna try! Let me see it!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Okay, here goes!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): ...Ugh. It's not working... Why can't I do it?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Hey, Sensei, how come you could do it and I can't? Is there a trick to it?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): You think I'll get better with practice?
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Hmm... Well, if you say so, Sensei, I'll trust you.
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Okay!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Watch, Sensei! I'm going to figure it out!
- Megu (Hot Springs Department): Let me try again!
> Megu practiced for the next few hours until she figured out the kendama.
