- "So... I guess I'll have to pretend to be your friend today."
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): God! Why do you have to say it like that?!

- Clerk: Thank you for waiting. What would you like to order?
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): We'll take the new Coco Paw Mille-Feuille, for two.
- Clerk: No problem. Two Coco Paw Mille-Feuilles... That'll be 6,800 yen.
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): Uh, excuse me? ...6,800 yen?
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): (Wh-What?! I never expected it to be that expensive!)
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): (Does that mean it's 3,400 yen each?! That can't be!)
- Clerk: ...Ma'am?
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): Uh...Well...
- (Take out your bank card and pay.)
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): ...What?!
- Clerk: Thank you. The payment has been accepted.
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): Sensei! What are you doing?! Why did you pay?!
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): Are you buying because you're the teacher? B-But I'm the one who invited you!
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): ...Ugh!
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): Okay... I'll let you get away with that today. But next time, I'm buying!
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): You got it?! Next time, I'm paying! Understand?!
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): ...!
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): ...!
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): ...Haha.
- Yoshimi (After-School Sweets Club): Okay, thanks Sensei. See you next time! Call you later!
> (Then Yoshimi wandered off happily after getting her mille-feuilles.)
