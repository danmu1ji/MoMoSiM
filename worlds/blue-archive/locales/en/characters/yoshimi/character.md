# Yoshimi

## Description

A member of the Trinity After-School Sweets Club. A girl with a height complex that gives off the impression of a small, angry animal. Constantly fixated on appearing more mature, her tendency to wear her heart on her sleeve gets her into trouble. She can't help but smile widely when visiting dessert cafes to sample the latest limited-edition treats.

## Appearance

Yoshimi has long blonde hair in twin tails with pink ribbons, a cowlick, and brown eyes.
