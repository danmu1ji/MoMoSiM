> One day, I paid a little business visit to the Foreclosure Task Force.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): La dee da dee da!
- "Hey, Nonomi!"
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): S-Sensei?!
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): When did you get here?!
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): I didn't even hear the door open!
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): I wish I'd known. I could have gotten us some snacks.
- "What was it you were so focused on?"
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Ah, well...
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): I-It's a secret.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Aww... You're giving up just like that?
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): It's not like it's a deep dark secret or anything.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Here, I'll show you. But for your eyes only!
- "...It's just a note?"
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Kinda. It's my bucket list, to be exact.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): I've written down everything I want to do with the Foreclosure Task Force one day.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Sometimes when I'm alone and no one's looking, I work on it!
- "Shouldn't you share it so you can knock out the list?"
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Mmm, no.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): If the others found out I had this, they'd drop everything and make me complete all of it.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): We all have a lot of work to do managing the debt situation. I couldn't put even more pressure on them.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): So, it's better that I work on it quietly, and complete it at my own pace.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Or at least I was working on it until you caught me, Sensei.
- "I see... Sorry for that..."
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): It's all right. I knew my secret would be exposed one day. ☆
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Well, since we're talking about it... Do you want to give it a read?
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Of course. I told you, it's not a deep dark secret!
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Here, we can read it together.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): I've marked the things we've already done in red.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Eat dessert together, decorate the academy together... Some items were easy to knock out, while for others, everyone had to really give it their all.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): I feel happy even just remembering some of these.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Oh remember this one? Try the level 5 spicy pizza.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Serika's whole face turned beet red. I wish I could see it again! ☆
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Oh, over here are the things we haven't done yet.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): ...It'd be nice if we could cross out everything one day.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): But as soon as we finish one item, I think of two more things.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Instead of shrinking, the list keeps growing even bigger than before.
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): At this point, there are over 100 things we haven't done yet... Ahaha, what a nightmare!
- "Is that really a bad thing?"
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): ...That's right!
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): We may still be struggling to pay off our debts, but...
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): There's no reason we can't make some more happy memories!
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): So I'll work even harder to work through our bucket list. Yup yup!
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Speaking of which, would you like to help me cross off another item?
- "Sure, what is it?"
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Skydiving!
- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Imagine the freedom of leaping out of a plane at 4,000 meters! Aren't you thrilled just thinking about it?

- Nonomi (Foreclosure Task Force): Heehee. Just teasing. ☆
