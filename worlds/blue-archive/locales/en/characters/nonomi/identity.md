# Nonomi\n\nA member of the Countermeasure Council of Abydos. She has an emotional and gentle personality, and plays a role as a mental support bringing together the members of the Countermeasure Council, which has many extreme personalities. Though she doesn't say it herself, she is the daughter of a very rich family.
