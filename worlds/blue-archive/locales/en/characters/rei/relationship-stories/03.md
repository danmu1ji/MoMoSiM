- "What an incredibly heated game..."
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Of course it is! These two teams have a long history of being rivals!
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): And their fans have a weird love-hate relationship with each other.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): It's one of the best classic match-ups in baseball history!
- ???: Waah... Waaah...!!
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Huh? Did I hear someone crying in the middle of all that cheering...?
- Lost Child: Mom...Dad...where are you...?
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): S-Sensei, look...
- "He must've lost his parents in the crowd."
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Wh-What should we do?
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Ah, right! I should tell the stadium first. I don't know why I didn't think of that right away.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Here, follow me, Sensei. I know where to go.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): And hey, we'll stay with you until you find your parents, okay?
- Lost Child: Really...?
> And so, we notified the stadium of the lost child.
> And we decided to wait for the child's parents with him.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): So, do you like baseball?
- Lost Child: Baseball...? I don't know. Mom and Dad do, I guess, or they wouldn't have brought me here.
- Lost Child: But I don't know where they are now... Waah...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): I-It's fine. It's okay! We'll stay with you.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Huh? Is that a foul ball coming this way...?
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Oh, here we go...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Got it! Woo, my lucky day!
- Lost Child: ...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Would you like to have it?
- Lost Child: A baseball...?
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): See, baseball's a lot different from other sports that use a ball to score with.
- Lost Child: How so...?
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Well, with other sports, the ball itself is what scores the points. So they don't usually change it out in mid-game, and they don't give it away to the fans like this.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): But baseball's different. In baseball, people score the points. So the ball's less important, and we don't hang onto it as much.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): And even in the middle of the game, if a ball goes flying out to a fan, the fan gets to keep it. It's a game that gives.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): The ball just follows the fan home. Isn't that amazing?
- Lost Child: Eh, I guess.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Y-You guess?!
- Referee: Strike three! You're out!
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Ah, did you see that? That foul ball made the batter impatient.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): The pitcher took advantage of that, and faked like they were going to throw a falling curve ball...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Then, boom! Fastball straight to the center! What a showdown!
- Lost Child: I don't even know what any of that means!
- Lost Child: ...But I bet Mom and Dad do. They must REALLY care about this game. They haven't even come to find me yet.
- Lost Child: Why DO they care, anyway? This game's boring. It's just a bunch of people standing around. Not like basketball or soccer...
- Lost Child: Even when they catch the ball, they just run a little bit and then stop again...
- Lost Child: This sucks! I hate Mom, I hate Dad... I hate baseball!
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): ...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): (Baseball is boring...?)
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): (Now that I think about it, I might have said the same thing at that age.)
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Here's the thing. Baseball's SUPPOSED to be boring.
- "That's an interesting statement, Commentator Rei."
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): H-Hang on, Sensei...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): *cough* So it's like this. When you're playing defense in baseball, you want everything to stick to the same routine.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): If all three batters hit their first pitch as grounders or flyouts, that's what's best for you.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): If that doesn't happen, someone's made a mistake. The trick is to keep things "boring" by not making any.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): So when it looks boring, it's because the whole team is doing their absolute best to keep it that way.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): You have to struggle as hard as you can to look as nonchalant as possible.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): And I kind of love the irony of that.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): You know swans, right? Up above the surface, they're just floating like clouds...but under the surface, their feet are always going like crazy.
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Having to do your best to seem ordinary and not get noticed...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): ...that's commendable, I think. There's something cool about it.
- Lost Child: ...
- Lost Child: ...Yeah?
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Yeah, I'd say so.
- Lost Child: Huh...
- Lost Child: That IS pretty cool. Maybe baseball's okay...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Right?
- Announcer: Sixth batter gets a GREAT hit on that falling curve ball!
- Announcer: The ball lands in center right field! The batter gets past first base. They're heading for second!
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Run! Run! Make it a double!
- Lost Child: Look at that speed! Defense throws to second base. The baseman stretches out for the catch...
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): It's a slide! And the call is...?!
- Studio Announcer: Safe! What a sprint, turning a single into a double!
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Yeah!
- Lost Child: Woo!
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): You like that?
- Lost Child: Yeah, that was cool!
- Rei (Athletics Training Club): Right?
> The game heated up more and more.

- Lost Child: Okay, Sensei, Sis! I've gotta go! I had a lot of fun! Thank you!
