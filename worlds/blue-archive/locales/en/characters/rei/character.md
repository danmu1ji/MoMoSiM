# Rei

## Description

Rei is a member of the Millennium Science School and is the No. 4 batter who loves baseball. She is determined to reform the Millennium Baseball Club, which is currently resigned to studying physics and ball dynamics. She seems to get along well with the fitness nut Sumire... except when it comes to actual competition.

## Appearance

Rei has thick, shoulder-length peach-orange hair, with two long strands falling below her chest, magenta eyes, and a small white bandage on her nose.
