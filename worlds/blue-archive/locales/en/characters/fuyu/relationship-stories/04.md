# Fuyu — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Fuyu/Relationship_Story/4
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> I went to the place Fuyu mentioned...
- Visitor A: Wildhunt students always make such interesting art.
- Visitor B: Fufu! We're in for a treat!
> There was quite a crowd.
> Then I saw a poster for an exhibition.
- "Wakasa Fuyu's Media Art Exhibition...?"
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): You read right! A solo feature.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): This is a private exhibition hall, dedicated to immersion into MY world.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): I'd like to escort you in, if you'd please.
- "...Sales Manager, you're sounding like a real artist!"
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Heehee, you think?
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Well, I didn't get into Wildhunt by sheer coincidence, Sensei!
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): So, at least for today, don't think of me as the Free Trade Cartel's exemplary Sales Manager...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ...but think of me as an artist. Can you do that, Sensei?
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Um, well...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ...Just Fuyu, I guess?
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Anyway! Shall we head inside?
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Not everyone gets an artist to escort them around her exhibition, you know!
> The exhibition hall was very dark.
> It was only lit by screens projecting media art.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Let's start here, shall we?
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): If we were to think of this exhibition as a collection of poems...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ...this work can be considered a prologue.
- "So, it's a primer for what we'll be seeing as a whole? Right?"
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Precisely!
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): That's why I put this at the entrance. It's a bit of a maze in here, but, regardless...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ...you'll see this piece first!
- "So, you also think about the order of the pieces?"
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Absolutely. I think of the pieces' location, angle, luminosity, hue, brightness, even pathing structure...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Every element in an exhibition should carry the artist's intent.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Now then, shall we continue?
> We gradually walked through and experienced Fuyu's works.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ...And so, you might be finding a recurring motif...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ...Oh, are you getting hungry, Sensei?
- "Wasn't that YOUR stomach growling, Fuyu?"
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Fufu, I suppose we must stop.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): As an artist, I can't ignore my VIP's ravenous and loud call of hunger.
- "But it wasn't me...!"
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Shh.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Just follow me, okay? Sen...sei?
> And Fuyu took me through several doors...
> ...until we were hidden backstage in a corner of the exhibition.
- "Th-This place is...?"
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ― We are behind the screens.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ― Thus, behind the artwork...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ― A place where only the artist may enter.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ― The only way to enter this place...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ― is to get permission from me, the artist.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ― So, right now, in this place...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ― There's only you and...me...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ― Uh no no no no!
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ― Th-That's not what I meant...!
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ― Anyway!
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ― I packed lunch...
- "You made me lunch?!"
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ― Um, if you're fine with it...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ― ...Would you like to have some?
> We enjoyed the packed lunch.
> Fuyu wasn't lying. She's a wonderful cook.
