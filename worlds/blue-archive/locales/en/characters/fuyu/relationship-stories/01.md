> The next day, I returned to Schale after a business trip.
> Screen shakes
- "Hm?!"
- "What's happening?!"
- Visitor A: Huh... What a fascinating finale.
- Visitor B: It's quite profound. She has such a way of communicating so much without words.
- Visitor C: I don't get it. Why is this happening? Are we under attack?
- Visitor D: It's performance art! It's cultured!
- "Performance art...?"
> And, as I approached...
> ...I found broken televisions, monitors, radios, and cameras strewn about.
> At the center of the clutter was...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Oh, look who it is! Good to see you, Sensei!
- "Fuyu, what is this mess?"
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): A mess...or a curated wreckage?
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): To put it simply...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Suspending objects, dancing, poetry, music...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ...are all art in their own ways. Yet they resist simple definition.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): So, I was exploring the ambiguity and embodiment of the personal with the political, the body with the machine...
- "...Can you say that a little more plainly?"
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): It's media art, Sensei. It's a performance, and we're all part of it.
- "You could have started with that...!"
- "So, that means when you said you were going out, it was for..."
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Yes. I was granted an outing pass for this performance.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ...Of course, I'm synergizing and multi-tasking with a "side job" too.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): In case you can't tell, making art like this is expensive. I can't be like Miyo and just type in some dark corner.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): I need a constant revenue stream to enable me to procure electronics, tools, things of that nature...
- "So that's why you're in the Free Trade Cartel."
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): The money helps, but I have other reasons.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Anyway, what a shame. You came right as I ended. You missed all the storytelling, the struggle!
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): I put a lot of thought into this. You wouldn't believe how much planning it takes.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Hm? What was this performance about?
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Fufu, it'd be no fun to just TELL you.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Performance is about SHOWING, about expressing something that can't be told with mere words.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): And if I told you what it meant, it would diminish the experience.
- "I suppose...?"
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): I have an idea. Try imagining it.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Look at the wreckage. Every item is in a position—thoughtfully placed, or a random occurrence? Imagine how the audience felt after, the sudden silence, the clearing of smoke...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Put all that together and imagine what my performance was about.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): You might just discover something about yourself, Sensei!
- "The world of art sure isn't simple..."
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Well, that said, it would have been best if you...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ...could have watched from the front row. Fufu.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Or, better yet, you can join me on stage next time.
- "I'll make sure to come watch next time!"
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Haha! I got it! I won't push.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): I respect your preference. But I heard what I wanted...
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ...That you'll be here next time.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): You promised! That said, I think I better get moving.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): I need to sweep up and pack, so you should head back to work, Sensei!
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ...What's that? You want to help with the cleanup?
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): You REALLY don't have to... That's not what I was implying.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Sensei...
- "S-So it's not like I-I'm trying to help you or anything!"
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Pfft! Ha!
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): ...Very well. Let's clean up together.
- Fuyu (Free Trade Cartel): Thank you, Sensei.
> I cleaned up the performance site with Fuyu.
