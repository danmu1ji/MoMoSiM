# Fuyu

## Description

A member of the Wildhunt Art Academy, Fuyu is the sales and technical specialist for unofficial smuggling group, Free Trade Cartel. She serves as the liaison for the Free Trade Cartel, handling requests and negotiating directly with clients. Fuyu seems confident and composed, but facing unexpected situations can leave her shaken and unsure of herself.
