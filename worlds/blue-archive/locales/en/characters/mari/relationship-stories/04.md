# Mari — Relationship Story 4

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> (I followed Mari's instructions and arrived at the meeting spot, a secluded shooting range in Trinity.)
> (It was a vast, sprawling area without a single soul in sight, even quieter than the cathedral.)
- Mari (The Sisterhood): You're here, Sensei.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I've been waiting for you. I was worried I may have pressured you with my sudden call.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): And it wasn't even for anything important...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Oh... I'm sorry. I suppose this was sudden after all.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Oh... I'm sorry. I suppose this was sudden after all.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I didn't come here because of the guns exactly. It's just quiet here. That's all.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Since this place is full of silence and empty of people, I can focus on my prayers.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): At times like these the range can be quieter even than the cathedral or the confessional.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): So when I'm troubled or want to pray by myself, I visit here.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Hmm. Am I weird after all? For a Sister to come to a shooting range like this...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Thank you. I was worried you might misunderstand me.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Thank you. I was worried you might misunderstand me.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Honestly, I didn't even know this existed at first.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I just heard it was a place that other Sisters occasionally go to... So I came here to emulate them.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I found myself entranced by the solitude, the deep silence...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): When you want to clear your head of idle thoughts... Perhaps seeking self-reflection or encouragement...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): When you want to forget the things that trouble your mind... I find answers here.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...Forget.
- "...Mari?"
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Well...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): To be honest, after I took that day off, I decided to seek some guidance.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): The other Sisters kindly told me it's a natural thing, but...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I don't want it to happen again. I guess it's a matter of self-management in a way...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Then they said, "It's good to always have a heart for service, but it's also important to take the time to do what you want every once in a while."
- Mari (The Sisterhood): So I thought about it. What it was that I wanted to do.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): So I came here, to pray and organize my thoughts. Then it came to me.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): But what came to mind was unexpected. I'm a little confused.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Sen...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...Sensei.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...Sensei.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Um. Once in a while... It doesn't have to be often...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I-If it's not too much trouble...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...E...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Could I...borrow some of your time again? You don't have to do anything in particular.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I don't know how to say it but...when you helped me, it brought me so much joy... and, um...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): O-Of course I understand. Talk like this is unbecoming of a Sister...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Asking others to take care of me or spend time with me...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I know it goes against my duty of taking care of others.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I know that, but still...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Huh?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Y-You don't mind?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): How could you say that so easily?!
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Are you really okay with that?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...!
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...!
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...Sensei.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Heehee. Maybe I was thinking too seriously about this.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I do want to become an excellent Sister, but at the same time, I'm also a student.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Since I'm a student...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...and you're a teacher, maybe it's okay for me to be a little childish with you.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Strange. I suddenly feel so refreshed.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Thank you so much, Sensei.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Then, going forward, I wish the best of luck...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): to the teacher...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): who will have to deal with a childish student like me.
> (Afterward, I prayed with Mari before returning to Schale.)
