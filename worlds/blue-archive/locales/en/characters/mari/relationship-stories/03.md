# Mari — Relationship Story 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Mari/Relationship_Story/3
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> (I was worried and headed to Mari's room for a visit.)
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Who is it?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...?!
- Mari (The Sisterhood): S-Sensei?! What brings you here?!
- Mari (The Sisterhood): W-Wait just a second! Please, give me a moment.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): P-Please come in, Sensei.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I'm sorry. I haven't had a chance to clean my room...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): You don't mind?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): B-But...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): What? You want me to stay in bed and rest? B-But you're already here, Sensei. How could I?!
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Well...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Well...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I-I understand. You came all the way here, so I shouldn't make things harder for you.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Then, just as you asked...
> (Mari went back to bed and lay down with a blanket.)
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I hope you didn't worry too much. It's only a small sickness.
- "How are you feeling?"
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Ahaha... I made you worry after all. I'm sorry.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Now that I've had some rest, I feel much better. I will be back to my duties tomorrow.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): It feels a little strange for a Sister like me to be receiving encouragement. I should be encouraging others.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Aha...ha...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Well... Um...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Honestly...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Lately, I've been feeling a little fatigued from work...so I thought it'd be nice to take a day off.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I think the current state of my health...may be due to that desire.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): The other Sisters are all working hard as well, so maybe I'm just being a baby.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Is it because I was harboring thoughts unbecoming of a Sister that my body is punishing me like this?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Huh?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): You think it isn't punishment, but assistance? From my body?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Um, I'm not sure I follow. How is this assistance?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Heehee. I understand what you meant. I suppose that's one possible perspective.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Thank you, Sensei. I somehow feel refreshed thanks to you.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Not just my body...but my spirit too.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Even while resting, I couldn't relieve myself of my guilt, but you resolved it so easily.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): You're really very adept at putting me at ease, Sensei. I feel I must learn from you.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): If I ever get the chance and if it's all right with you, Sensei, then...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Hm?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Oh. I obviously didn't mean today.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Like you said, I should only focus on resting today.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): But tomorrow, and from now on...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I'll look forward to it.
> (Afterward, I kept Mari company and took care of her while she rested.)
> (Once I saw that Mari was indeed asleep, I returned to Schale.)
