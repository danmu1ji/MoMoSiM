> I met up with Mari at the shopping mall, to help her buy supplies for the Sisterhood.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Wow. This is quite the list.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Bake sale, philanthropy, and outreach supplies...as well as this month's snacks.
- "Yup. It's a good thing we came together."
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Absolutely. It would have been rather difficult without your assistance.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): As usual, you have my gratitude.
- "Do you always have to run these errands, Mari?"
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Ah, no! Not at all. We have a rotating system.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): However, I volunteer quite often, as everyone has rather busy schedules.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I'm not usually alone, but no one else was available today.
- "You really are a hard worker, Mari."
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Heehee. I'm always grateful whenever I can lend a hand, however small the task may be.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Big malls like this one always have much to see, so it can actually be rather fun!
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Over there, for example. Look at those adorable dolls, Sensei!
- Mari (The Sisterhood): And that store has beautiful clothes.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): These accessories are quite cute as well.
- "(I've never seen this side of Mari before.)"
- Merchant: You two! You're having a great time together, aren't you?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): E-Eek? A-A-Are you speaking to us?
- Merchant: Do you see anyone else around? Hohoho!
- Merchant: Come take a look at our accessories! All the girls love them!
- Mari (The Sisterhood): A-Accessories...
- Merchant: They're all the rage these days! C'mon! I'll even give you a discount!
- "Why don't you pick something out?"
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Eh?! M-Me? An accessory?!
- Mari (The Sisterhood): A-Ah....
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I-I can't.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): U-Um... I truly appreciate your thoughts and feelings, but...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): As a Sister, I cannot indulge in unnecessary extravagances and luxuries...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): O-Of course, the accessories here are very pretty, and I must admit that I would love to have one, but...
- "But what about the corsage you're wearing?"
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Ah... Y-You mean, this...?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): This corsage...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I purchased this as a reminder to never forget the vows of the Sisterhood.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I wanted to encapsulate the feelings I had when I embarked on this journey, with this white flower that would never wither.
- "I see..."
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...Sensei... May I speak freely?
- "With me, you always can."
- Mari (The Sisterhood): This may not be a confession booth, but I still feel compelled to honesty.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): This corsage...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I-I...did buy it thinking that it was pretty.
- "Hm?"
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Its primary purpose was a reminder to stay true to my heart!
- Mari (The Sisterhood): But I also liked the way it looked...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): As I mentioned earlier, the virtues of a Sister are frugality and selflessness...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): But I still have interest in accessories and the such. Sometimes, I even feel the desire to dress up, as other students do.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Wh-Which is why I like wearing this corsage.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): *sigh* Not only am I giving into my worldly desires, but I'm also making excuses for them...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I should be forced to denounce my vows.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Although, I do...feel slightly more at ease, having told you my secret.
- "So that corsage is a huge luxury to you."
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Yes...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): P-Please tell me what you're thinking, Sensei. Are you...disappointed in me?
- "Ugh. So cute..."
- Mari (The Sisterhood): E-Excuse me?!
- Mari (The Sisterhood): T-Truly? It's...okay?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I...I see.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I'm so relieved to hear you say that.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): What's acceptable and what's not...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...it appears I'm still figuring that out. Heehee.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): For now, I'll simply have to accept and appreciate your words.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): This one corsage will be enough.
- "How about I treat you to something delicious instead, then?"
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Truly... You never stop trying to give. Even after I've received so much from you.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): In that case, yes. Please.
> Mari and I ate a delicious meal together, and then I returned home.
