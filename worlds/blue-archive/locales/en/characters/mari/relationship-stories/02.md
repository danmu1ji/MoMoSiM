# Mari — Relationship Story 2

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Mari/Relationship_Story/2

> (When I arrived at the cathedral, there was no sign of other people.)
> (The empty cathedral felt both magnificent and clean.)
- ???: You're here, Sensei. This way.
- Mari: ― Thank you for coming, Sensei.
- Mari: ― Yes. I was just saying a prayer for you, Sensei.
- Mari: ― A quiet place like this is great for praying.
- Mari: ― When I pray, it feels as though my heart is being cleansed.
- Mari: ― There are paths you've taken so far,
- Mari: ― and paths you must still take.
- Mari: ― May peace and happiness find you
- Mari: ― wherever you choose to go.
- Mari: ― Hm? Have I ever prayed for myself?
- Mari: ― That's... No, it's okay.
- Mari: ― I am happy if you are happy, Sensei.
- Mari: ― In other words, this can be a prayer for us.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): To be honest, I wanted to offer this prayer much sooner, but I've been kind of busy with my cathedral work lately...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Hmm. I suppose that's just an excuse.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): A Sister mustn't develop a habit of making excuses.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Please forgive me for only now attending to your needs, Sensei.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Wh-What? I-Is that so?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Wh-What? I-Is that so?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Y-You're teasing me again, aren't you? Honestly, Sensei!
- Mari (The Sisterhood): It's not good to lie, Sensei. Last time, you almost got yourself in big trouble with that.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I pray every day such a thing won't happen again, but...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Huh?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): You don't think what happened last time was because you lied?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I-Is that so? Then what was it?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): A-Anyway...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Sensei. If it's all right, could you come a little closer?
- Mari (The Sisterhood): There are several different ways to pray.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): One of them...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): is where you hold your hands together and lift them in prayer like this.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I personally believe this prayer is more easily heard.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): And for people like Sensei, who tend to put others first...
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Well, I think you would probably pray for the students before yourself.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Which is why I think it's necessary for someone to stay and pray with you like this.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): I will take on that role because I want to be a great Sister one day.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Now then, if you would please close your eyes, Sensei.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): Let's pray together for your happiness once more.
- Mari (The Sisterhood): ...And for me, who wishes for that prayer to come true.
