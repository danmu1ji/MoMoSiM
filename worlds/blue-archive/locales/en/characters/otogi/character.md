# Otogi

## Description

The sniper of SRT Special Academy's FOX Squad, callsign "FOX4". Among snipers, who are often lone wolves, Otogi is a rare type who can quickly get along even with people she has just met. Within the squad, she plays the role of a mood-maker, and on the battlefield, she serves to put pressure on the enemy morale. Otogi is skilled at handling electronic equipment, and can even handle repairs for most mechanical equipment malfunctions.

## Appearance

Otogi has very long, light ash-brown hair gathered into a thick braid that reaches her knees, light-green eyes, and fennec-like ears matching her hair.
