> Corgi Town Entrance
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Sensei! Over here.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): It feels a little weird, having non-mission related plans with you.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Ah, I didn't get in the way of your weekend, did I?
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Come to think of it, you've been super busy this week.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): ...Huh? You've never seen sniper disguises before?
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Ah. I'm sorry, but actually...
- Otogi (FOX Squad): The store I was going to take you to isn't some special military shop. It's just a regular clothing store.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): It's more effective to modify regular clothes for a disguise. I think you're thinking of camouflage outfits we use out in the field.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): I got a gift certificate that's good in Corgi Town on my last mission, so I thought I'd do some shopping.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Fufu. I'm excited to buy all kinds of outfits for different environments.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Ah, you mean a ghillie suit.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Since a ghillie suit is basically covered in netting, you can customize it by weaving in various types of fabric to suit your mission.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): It's better suited for open terrain or out in nature, since you generally use leaves and the such. But there are opportunities in urban areas where you can blend in, like bright billboards, so we'll adjust depending on where we are.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Sometimes we'll even tear up old uniforms to use, but...
- Otogi (FOX Squad): I don't like to do that, with SRT Academy closed down. There are only so many uniforms left.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): I actually haven't made a ghillie suit in a while.
- "...So it takes more handling than normal clothes."
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Of course. Mission preparation should always be perfect.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Now, should we do some shopping?
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Hm. This shirt's way too bright.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): I like this pattern a lot...
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Oh, this dress is cute. And the color will match my hair.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): What do you think, Sensei? Do you think it will look good on me?
- Otogi (FOX Squad): What's with that response?
- Otogi (FOX Squad): ...You're right. Flowy dresses like this really aren't my style.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Oh well. Then I'll play it safe with something like denim or cordu—
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Then?
- "Isn't it a shame to rip it up and turn it into a disguise?"
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Eh?
- Otogi (FOX Squad): What are you...
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Gah. That's my bad. I might've explained that badly.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): I'm just looking for casual clothes, for regular infiltration missions.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): If you dress in a way that's not familiar to the locals, it draws attention to you.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): So that's what I mean by a "disguise." Just something that lets me blend in with the crowd.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): I did say mission prep should be perfect, but I'm not going to rip up brand new clothes for the sake of one...
- "Phew. I'm glad I misunderstood."
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Oh? I like the sound of that.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Can I take back what I said?
- "Nope."
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Kidding, kidding.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Anyway, so. What do you think of this?
- "I actually think the outfit before was better."
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Oh, you have unexpectedly distinct taste.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): All right. Then this way we go!
