# Otogi — Relationship Story 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

> After seeing Otogi's cryptic MomoTalk, I headed to the Fox Squad's underground HQ.
> FOX Squad HQ
> But no one was home, not even Otogi.
> I wandered around the general area for about ten minutes.
> Then, I finally found Otogi, sitting on a bench at a nearby park. She seemed lost in thought as she stared at a streetlight.
- "Otogi!"
- Otogi (FOX Squad): ...Oh? Sensei...
- Otogi (FOX Squad): What are you doing out here so late?
- Otogi (FOX Squad): You shouldn't be wandering in places where there's no one else around.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Just wanted a change of pace.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Ah, you saw my MomoTalk.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Sorry. I didn't mean to worry you.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): It really wasn't anything serious. I feel bad for making you come out this late.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): I really did just come out here for a change of scenery, so you can head back, Sensei.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): You have an important meeting tomorrow, right? Don't you need to sleep early?
- Otogi (FOX Squad): ...You really just do whatever you want.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Ah. Maybe that's me, actually.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Do you see that streetlight at the entrance?
- Otogi (FOX Squad): That one on the right... The one that's slightly fainter than the other lights.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): I learned this on a mission before. That streetlight has older circuits and breaks down pretty often.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): These days, streetlights are automated, and their sensors can tell if the sun is up or not.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): But that streetlight's sensor is worn out, so it'll turn off about once an hour.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): So, the park's night-shift worker monitors the cameras, and if the lights are off, they have to turn the power back on manually. Isn't that a hassle?
- Otogi (FOX Squad): The other day, when I was passing by here, I saw it light back up.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): It made me think, ah, there's someone on the other side of that streetlight.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): That person isn't sleeping either, keeping watch to make sure the road stays well-lit.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): ...So when I feel lonely, I come to this park and just stare at that light flickering off.
- "Did you feel lonely today?"
- Otogi (FOX Squad): It happens sometimes. The other squad members are all out late on missions today.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): It's blackouts...or radio silence. But cutting communication, depending on the situation you're in, can be pretty standard.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Snipers, especially. We'll cut comms when we need to hear better.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Remember when I told you that communication is important for snipers? I think what's more important, and harder, is being okay with the solitude.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Sometimes, I can't see the enemies at all, let alone talk to my allies. It makes me feel like I'm the only person left in the world.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Even when it feels that way, a sniper's duty is to wait for their target in that isolated spot.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): The only reason I chose to be a sniper is because I have good vision and steady aim.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): But I get lonely easily. And when I feel that way...
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Sometimes I wonder if I chose the wrong career path.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): If I end up deciding sniping isn't for me, doesn't that mean everything I did up until now was a waste of time?
- Otogi (FOX Squad): If I'd chosen correctly, I would've grown more, and faster. Instead, I could be holding onto something that's not even meant for me.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): ...The thought of it kind of drives me crazy.
- "It's okay. You guys have all the time in the world."
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Not really. I'm already a third year.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): It's too late for me to change, isn't it?
- Otogi (FOX Squad): I can't be a student forever.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Doesn't that mean you could be one too?
- Otogi (FOX Squad): That's a funny logic.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): You definitely are a weird adult.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): ...But thanks. I feel less lonely.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Ah, the streetlamp is off again.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): ...And there it goes.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): For now, I'll be faithful to my orders.
- Otogi (FOX Squad): Even if it's a simple task of turning the lights on and off...
- Otogi (FOX Squad): I'll keep on doing it. Maybe it'll spark a light in someone's heart.
> Otogi and I watched the street light going on and off, chasing away each other's loneliness in the quiet of the dark.
