# Atsuko

## Description

A mysterious Arius Squad girl who belongs to the Arius Branch School and always wears a gas mask. While Atsuko doesn't use the gas mask expressly for the purpose of hiding her face, she lives mostly wearing the mask, except when eating or sleeping. She is called "princess" by the squad leader Saori, but her background is a mystery as Atsuko doesn't share much about herself.
