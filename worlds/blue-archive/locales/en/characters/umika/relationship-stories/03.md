# Umika — Relationship Story 3

Original English wiki story transcript. No Korean dialogue is translated.

Source: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Umika/Relationship_Story/3
Status: source-extracted-unreviewed


> After receiving Umika's message, I arrived at Momoyodou.
- "Excuse me."
- ???: Huh? Is someone there?
- ???: Who cares! Today, we're the customers!
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): How exciting!
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): Yeahhh!! This is awesome!
- "Wh-What's going on?!"
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): S-Sensei?! Why did you have to come now of all times?!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): I-I know I asked! And I really did hope you would come, but...!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Seriously?! Mouryo?! You're really ticking me off!
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): What are you saying?! We're here at Momoyodou as customers, remember?
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): We even paid in advance!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Ugh... I know that, and I know that the customer's always right...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): But that's a little hard to buy serving "patrons" like you!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): At this rate, Momoyodou's reputation will be ruined...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): So you're saying...as a server, it is vital that I offer "guidance" to disrespectful customers?
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): For if I failed to do so, it would hamper the service we provide, and disrupt the peaceful experiences of other customers?
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): We can't have that! In that case...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Mouryo! For Momoyodou, its traditions...and the sake of all future customers!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): With all my heart, let me "teach" you the full power of Momoyodou's hospitality!
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): Huh? Wh-What are you going on about?
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): I have a bad feeling about this...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Please take your seats!
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): Uh, uhh? What?!
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): Huh! You can't force me into a chair!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Now then. We have teacups, green tea, oolong, saucers, tissues, and napkins! Everything's ready! Is there anything else you need?!
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): That was so fast! And somehow it was still perfect?
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): Hmph! There's no wet towels!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): I figured you'd mention those. I was just preparing them!
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): Isn't this too many?!
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): Y-Yeah. Two should be enough?
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Seeing as you personally requested them, I trust you'll make use of them all!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Also, taking items outside of Momoyodou is prohibited.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Is there anything else you need?
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): Um...no, thank you.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): In that case, I'll take your order! Our menu at Momoyodou includes—
> Umika quickly recited the entire Momoyodou menu with detailed descriptions of each item.
> Whether Mouryo, or rather, any of the customers, fully understood her was another matter...
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): Ugh. I can't understand any of this. It's giving me a headache...
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): I can't place an order if I don't understand what they have...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Oho, are you still confused? Let me start over from the beginning—
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): No, no! That's not necessary! We'll just go ahead and order!
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): More than that— Uh... We're sorry... We won't trouble you again...
- "So you've learned to respect the staff and the establishment?"
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): Yes...
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): We got super excited about the festival happening here, and things got out of hand...
- "Huh? There's a festival at Momoyodou?"
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): Yeah, festival items started showing up three days ago, right?
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): Plus, the merchant guild, equipment managers, street vendors, and event planners...
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): All of them have been seen hanging around here.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Have you all been stalking them?
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): No. We're Mouryo. We know about almost everything related to the festivals held at Hyakkiyako.
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): There weren't any festivals scheduled for a while. Then, we heard something was being planned at Momoyodou...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): N-No way! You Mouryo must have been misinformed!
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): No way! That's not possible! We may have a bad reputation as troublemakers...
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): But we take great pride in the festivals here at Hyakkiyako! It's part of being Mouryo!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Ughhh!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): ...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): When I say you're wrong, just admit that you're wrong!
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): Huh? Did we hit a real nerve?
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): Look at her face, what do you think?!
- Mouryo A (Street Mouryo): ...Should we leave?!
- Mouryo B (Street Mouryo): Why not?! I think we've had our meal and our fun!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): ...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): I'm sorry, Sensei. I didn't mean to show this side of myself...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): I guess I was a little harsh...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Okay, maybe a lot harsh, but...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): It just isn't right!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): My festival preparations shouldn't have to tolerate interruptions like that!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): ...!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): I mean... That's...um...!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Never mind! Forget it!
- "But Mouryo..."
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): It's okay, really! By the way, Sensei! Is there anything you'd like to order?
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Today, it's my treat! Just let me know what you'd like!
- "What about that festival?"
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Can we not talk about the festival right now?!
- "I've never heard you speak that way about a festival!"
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Even I can admit that this is out of character for me!
> Afterwards, I cautiously brought up the topic of "festivals" with Umika a few times, but...
> ...she became flustered and stumbled over her words.
