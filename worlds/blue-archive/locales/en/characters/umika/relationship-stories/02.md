- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Ah, Sensei! Don't cry! I'll feel really awkward if I make you cry!

- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Sensei, why are you crying even harder?!

- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Yep, they're coming along smoothly.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Heeheehee... But that doesn't mean there's less work to do.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Still, work is just another part of what makes a festival!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Hmm? Why do I love festivals so much?
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): ...Heehee.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Well, they just bring back memories.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): When I was young, I lived in the countryside.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): The air was clean, and everything was tranquil...but there wasn't really much to do.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): It was relaxing, but you know, as a kid you want something exciting!
- "Hey, adults want excitement too!"
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): I-Is that so?!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Well to a child raised in the country, the festivals that happened once or twice a year were the most exciting thing around.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): About a month before the festival, the entire village would begin preparations. We'd cut bamboo and crafting paper, dye fabrics, and build the palanquin...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): I think back then was when it all started. Before I realized it, I was the kid who worked harder and faster to prepare for festivals than anyone else in the village.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Eventually I went to Hyakkiyako. I've learned a lot, and many things have changed, but...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Festivals are still one of my favorites.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): I can research festivals in Kivotos whenever I want. And if I plan ahead, I can experience them myself under the banner of a field trip.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Planning and preparing a festival is no cakewalk though. I still have a lot to learn at Momoyodou, but...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Whenever I hear the word festival, I still feel a rush of excitement!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Though, a lot of folks say my fixation makes me a little ditzy. Or that I kill the mood with festival talk...
- "Is that a problem?"
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Shouldn't it be...?
- "If no one's getting hurt, I don't think so."
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): But every time I get festival fever, I lose track of my surroundings and my mind goes blank...
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): ...Really?
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): If Sensei says, it must be true...right?
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Heeheehee. Thanks, Sensei. I feel a little better now.
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): O-Of course, now that I'm working at Momoyodou, I'll do my best here too!
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): ...Though, we still haven't had many customers today...
- Hyakkiyako Student A: Hello! What's today's special?
- Umika (Festival Operations Department): Oh, a customer! I'm off to work now, Sensei!
