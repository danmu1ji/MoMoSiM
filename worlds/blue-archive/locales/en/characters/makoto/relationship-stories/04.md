- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Kiheheh. On our last tour, I showed you our financial operations, Sensei.
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): The Pandemonium Society's exclusive printing office... The monumental soon-to-be-built Makoto Tower... Great Makoto character merchandise, our resort operation...
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Many roads to a single destination: the expansion of the Pandemonium Society's influence over Kivotos and increasing the renown of the Great Makoto!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Enough time has passed that the results of these projects should start making themselves clear.
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Kiheheheh! The Great Hanuma Makoto's masterpiece will soon unfold before your very eyes!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): I'm on tenterhooks waiting for the results! Aren't you excited, Sensei?
- "...It'll be interesting to see how your plans work out, Makoto."
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Kiheheheh! I knew you'd get into it, Sensei! Play aloof all you want—I'll win you over in the end!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): It's inevitable!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): It took admirable resolve to deny my magnanimous offer after I took you on a tour of our operations, Sensei! But even you won't be able to deny the results that are about to unfold...
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): You'll be overwhelmed by the urge to throw yourself at my feet and cry, "I should have joined forces with the Pandemonium Society long ago! I'm so sorry, Makoto—I've wasted so much time!"
- "...I wouldn't go that far."
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Fear not, Sensei! The Great Makoto has a compassionate heart!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): No matter how much you regret your mistake, I would never allow you to beg at my feet, Sensei! I'll always accept you!
- "Th-Thanks..."
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): There's no time to waste! Let us return to the operations and...
- Pandemonium Society Member A (Pandemonium Society): C-Chairman! We have a big problem!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Hm? What's the matter?
- Pandemonium Society Member A (Pandemonium Society): W-Well... The Pandemonium Printing Office just called... They won't be able to publish your biography today!
- "...Biography?"
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): What sort of problem? I planned and approved every aspect of the production myself!
- Print Shop Employee: W-Well, Chairman...
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Spit it out!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): I filled that book with the drama and struggle of the countless conflicts I've overcome, the comradery of fighting alongside my club, and the sage advice I know will touch the hearts of students across Kivotos─
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): My biography is guaranteed to become this year's best seller!
- "Wow, Makoto! That sounds incredible!"
- Print Shop Employee: Th-The problem is...
- Print Shop Employee: They've done the math...and there's just no way to make your intended unit price work, Chairman!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): ...Unit price?
> An employee from the printing office soon arrived to report on the situation personally.
- Print Shop Employee: You commanded us to make your biography perfect in every respect, so we put a lot of time and effort into its design and publication...
- Print Shop Employee: We commissioned a famous artist for the cover art and bound each book in the finest leather we could buy...
- Print Shop Employee: The prototype alone depleted most of the budget...and printing costs will grow astronomically at this rate. The sad truth is that each book we sell will push us further and further into the red.
- Print Shop Employee: Publishing the biography at all may be impossible at this point.
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Wh-What?!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): I just wanted to make the greatest book I could... This is the historic release of my first biography!
- "So you charged ahead without thinking..."
- Print Shop Employee: I believe we need to overhaul this project from top to bottom.
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Tsk...! Blast!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): ...Well, finding massive success with every project might have overwhelmed our staff, so it's good our other endeavors have room to grow!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): It's a shame... But problems like these are unforeseeable...
- Pandemonium Society Member B (Pandemonium Society): C-Chairman! It's an emergency!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Again?!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): What is it this time?!
- Pandemonium Society Member B (Pandemonium Society): We received word that the construction of Makoto Tower was complete, but when we went to look...!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Wh-What?!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Wh-Wh-Wh—
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): What is this?!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): This building...is Makoto Tower?!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): What happened to my beautiful skyscraper...? What happened to the monument to my authority, my beacon to all Kivotos...?
- "This looks more like a warehouse than a monument..."
- Pandemonium Society Member B (Pandemonium Society): Here... We have a letter from the site manager!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): What? Why isn't the manager here to report in person?
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): No, more importantly, what does the letter say?!
- Pandemonium Society Member B (Pandemonium Society): Uh, well... The letter says...
- Construction Site Manager: To Chairman Makoto, our great charismatic leader and holder of a peerless instinct for investment.
- Construction Site Manager: Chairman. Thank you for trusting us with a project so close to your heart.
- Construction Site Manager: As you requested, Makoto Tower stands at an impressive 400 inches—a perfect expression of our agreed-upon design.
- Construction Site Manager: Although we deeply regret that we cannot celebrate the completion of our contract in person, unavoidable circumstances have called us away. Please forgive our absence, but know that we're rejoicing with you in spirit.
- Construction Site Manager: We eagerly anticipate working with you again in the future.
- "Don't tell me...that by 400 inches they..."
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): B-Bring me the tower contract! As well as its blueprint!
- Pandemonium Society Member B (Pandemonium Society): Yes, Chairman!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): *gasp*
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Th-The contract and blueprint do have their units in inches...
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Urgh... I saw the number 400...and thought I would finally achieve my great dream... I left the piddling details to the construction company and trusted them with the work!
- "Isn't the scale the most important part of building a massive skyscraper?"
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): ...How dare they betray my trust!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): They won't get away with this!
- Pandemonium Society Member A (Pandemonium Society): C-Chairman! W-We have a problem!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): H-Huh? Wh-What is it this time?!
- Pandemonium Society Member A (Pandemonium Society): We got a report claiming that the CEO of the business creating merchandise in your likeness has embezzled project funds and fled the city!
- Pandemonium Society Member A (Pandemonium Society): That's not all! The Hot Springs Department attacked the Pandemonium Resort after they discovered it was built over a hot spring! They've driven off all attempts to recapture the building!
- Pandemonium Society Member A (Pandemonium Society): A-And there's even a report from the tourism development team—one of our package deals was double-contracted by mistake...! The clients are demanding that we pay the steep penalty fee!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Ugh. Urgh... Ahh... Um...
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Tch!!!
> Screen shakes
- "M-Makoto...are you okay?!"
- Pandemonium Society Members (Pandemonium Society): C-Chairman!!!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Just breathe...!
- "M-Makoto?"
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): If...you...thought...
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): If you thought that the Great Makoto would collapse in shock from mere trifles like these, you're gravely mistaken!!!
- Pandemonium Society Members (Pandemonium Society): C-Chairman Makoto!!!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): *inhale*...Assemble our club members!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Starting with the construction site manager, track down every company that has dared to betray the Pandemonium Society! We'll teach them the gravity of their mistake!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): It's time to unleash my full power...
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): I'll make them pay for mocking me! Let them flee to the ends of the world...when they look over their shoulders, they'll see me pursuing them!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): ...Everyone! Do you understand?!
- Pandemonium Society Members (Pandemonium Society): Yes, Chairman!!!
- "...You're amazing, Makoto."
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Hm?
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Ah. I've been through this so many times now. I'm used to it.
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Kiheheheh! I mean, what kind of leader would I be if I keeled over once a week?
- "Just how often does something like this happen?!"
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): I'll never be held back by piddling setbacks like these!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): The Great Makoto's takeover of Gehenna—no, the entirety of Kivotos—cannot be stopped!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): I'll smash through any obstacle that stands in my way!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): First we'll root out every false and fraudulent business in the district!

- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Kiheheheh! Very well—we'll leave such plans for the future! Let us enjoy the battle that lies ahead of us here and now!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Let us return! Summon the limousine!!!
- Pandemonium Society Member (Pandemonium Society): A-Ah, Chairman... I'm sorry, but we accidentally allowed the lease payments to lapse while we were funding all the new projects... The limousine was repossessed by the car company.
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): ...
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Um, Sensei... Would you possibly lend us some money for public transportation...
- "S-Sure, take whatever you need..."
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Kiheheheh! I knew our partnership would be invaluable! I owe you a debt!
- Makoto (Pandemonium Society): Everyone! It's time for the Pandemonium Society to move out!
