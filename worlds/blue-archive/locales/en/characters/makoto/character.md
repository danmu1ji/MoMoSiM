# Makoto

## Description

Chairman of the Gehenna Academy's Pandemonium Society. Makoto is an ambitious schemer who is willing to follow her desires and do anything to get what she wants. Although her attitude often causes Makoto to suffer and fail, she is a strong-willed girl who quickly recovers. She loves Ibuki like a younger sister, and also treats Iroha like one as well.
