> After I was done, I decided to go meet with Erika.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Ah, Sensei. You're here.

- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Ah, right? The weather's so nice.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Anyway, Sensei, do you have time to...hang for a bit?
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): It's nothing serious. I just wanted to take you somewhere.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): You do? Awesome.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Then follow me.
> I followed Erika up a quiet building.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): It's nice and quiet, right? Pretty much everyone's gone by now.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): No one likes staying late at school, especially not in Gehenna.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): I like it when everyone's cleared out like this.
- "Erika, did you change your perfume?"
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): H-Huh??
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Uh, yeah...
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Someone recommended this one to me.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Well, I'm glad you think it's nice. Yeah... It makes it worth it.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): People don't normally notice stuff like that. I can't tell if you're just sharp or something else, Sensei.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Anyway, this way.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Here, give me your hand.
> Erika led me to the place she wanted to show me by hand...
- Erika (Go-Home Club): ― Welcome, Sensei.
- Erika (Go-Home Club): ― This is my secret base.
- Erika (Go-Home Club): ― This is where I come when I need some alone time.
- Erika (Go-Home Club): ― Fufu. Maybe it's a bit too big to call it a "secret" base.
- Erika (Go-Home Club): ― I just...wanted to share it with you, Sensei.
- Erika (Go-Home Club): ― The views I see, the things I feel...
- Erika (Go-Home Club): ― If you're wondering why, I don't have a good answer...
- Erika (Go-Home Club): ― I guess...telling someone about a secret place...means that they're special too.
- Erika (Go-Home Club): ― You're a special person to me, Sensei.
- Erika (Go-Home Club): ― ...Fufu...

- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Not a lot of people come this way.

- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): It's nice to have somewhere to yourself in this busy school.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Do you like it here?
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): I'll give you permission to take up space here too, then.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Phew...
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): This isn't my first time seeing the sunset, but it feels different today.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): I know why, but experiencing it first-hand is nice.
> Erika and I watched the sunset for a while.
> By the time we came down from the rooftop, it was already dark.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Wow, when'd it become night?
- "Do you usually stay this late?"
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): No, never.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): We have curfew, and the hallways are kinda scary once it gets dark.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): I normally try to come down before the sun sets completely.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): But I pushed it a little, since you were here today.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): You being here is comforting in itself, so time really slipped by.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): C-Come to think of it, is it too late for you? Did we stay too long?
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Hah. You really always say the right thing.
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): That side of you is...
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): Anyway, the dark hallway is pretty scary...
- Erika (Twinkle Twinkle Club): So here, give me your hand.
> So down the dark, quiet hallway and stairs we went.
> But even when we exited into the moonlit night, Erika never let go of my hand.
