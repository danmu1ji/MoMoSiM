# Akari — official English introduction

Enrolled in Gehenna Academy, Akari is a member of the Gourmet Research Society. People may think she is just a quiet young girl, but she is actually the long-reigning champion food fighter of the Kivotos Eating Tournament. While fundamentally a calm and good-natured girl, like a true Gehenna student she is occasionally willing to cause great trouble for others.
