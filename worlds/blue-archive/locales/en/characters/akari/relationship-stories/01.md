> Gehenna Student Dining Hall
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Sensei, we don't have much time left before the eating contest downtown!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): This time, it's gonna be a hamburger-eating contest. I've never challenged myself to competitively eat hamburgers before, so I'm a bit nervous about this one.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): I can't give up yet, though! I need to keep my winning streak going for the third year in a row!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): That's why I'm going to practice eating burgers in advance, here at the Gehenna dining hall.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Though... There's a risk of not pacing myself right if I do this solo. Please help set my pace and make sure I don't overextend myself!
- "Set your pace?"
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Huh? You've never had to set pace before?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): In marathons, there are runners who run alongside others just to keep them on an even pace.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): If you only focus on running fast without considering the remaining distance, you'll run out of steam too quickly.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): An eating contest is just like a marathon. If you don't keep your pace and start off inhaling food, you'll get tired of its taste sooner. Even worse, you might get an upset stomach!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): That's why I want you to keep up with me when I'm eating burgers, Sensei!
- "But I can't eat nearly as much as you do, Akari..."
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Don't worry! I knew you'd say that, so I prepared some sliders for you instead.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Every time you eat one of these tiny burgers, I'll eat a big one. That way I can match my pace with yours.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Well, then, let's get started!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): That's thirty-one hamburgers down!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Come on, time for hamburger number thirty-two...
- "Urgh..."
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Sensei, haven't you finished your slider yet? It's bite-sized, so just swallow it whole.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): We still have a long way to go, and I need you with me. Our goal is a hundred! Let's move, chop-chop!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): ...You wanna know what the world record for hamburger eating is?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): If I remember correctly, there was a competitive eater who ate thirty burgers in one go, and that's still the world record. Since that monumental occasion, burgers have never been selected as the subject of an eating contest.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): What, do you think we should stop since I already set a new record?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): No way! We should give our all in every competition, regardless of records!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): You have to understand...this battle is a battle against myself!
- "I'm a lost cause already..."
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Come on, Sensei! Less whining and more eating! I'm still far from finished.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): If you can't feed yourself, then I'll just feed them to you. Open your mouth, okay?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): All right, Sensei. Say "Ahhh..." ♥
> After that, Akari talked me into eating more sliders, but I tapped out before taking down forty...
