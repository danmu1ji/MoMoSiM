> Eastern Restaurant Near Gehenna
> I stopped by a Chinese restaurant on my way back from work at Gehenna Academy. While there, I happened to see Akari.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Wow, Sensei! What a coincidence. Are you here to grab lunch?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Cool, same here! This place has a special "gourmand challenge" where you can eat as much mapo tofu as you want.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): If you're up for it, would you like to try the mapo tofu gourmand challenge with me?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Wait, you just want a regular order of mapo tofu? But eating a mountain of mapo tofu is pure euphoria...
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): If you're not interested, suit yourself. Should we order now?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): We'll have a regular mapo tofu and a gourmand mapo tofu, please.
- Chinese Restaurant Staff: Is the gourmand order of mapo tofu for the adult here?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): No, it's for me. I may not look it, but I'm no slouch when it comes to gourmand challenges!
- Chinese Restaurant Staff: Haha. Our mapo tofu makes for a tough challenge no matter how much you normally eat...
- Chinese Restaurant Staff: Please give us a moment. Your food will be ready soon.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): (Is it just me...or did the server have a strange expression there?)
- Chinese Restaurant Staff: Thanks for waiting. Here's the regular mapo tofu and gourmand mapo tofu you ordered.
- Chinese Restaurant Staff: If you can finish this ten-serving mapo tofu in thirty minutes, we won't charge you for it. If you finish before the time is up, you can eat even more. That's an unlimited amount!
- Chinese Restaurant Staff: ...Take your time and enjoy.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Whoa...I can smell that spicy aroma already. I can tell how delicious it is just by looking at it!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Time to dig in, then!
- "Thanks for the food."
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): ...Hmm? How odd. This mapo tofu doesn't taste the same as I remember.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Sensei, do you mind if I have a bite of your regular mapo tofu?
- Share your regular mapo tofu with her.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Nom, nom. ...I see what's going on here. It's a bit disappointing to see that there are still some restaurants that resort to cheap tricks like this.
- "Is there something wrong with your mapo tofu?"
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Compared to yours, mine has an excessive amount of spicy sauce on it. This must be their way of rigging the gourmand challenge in their favor.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Because I can eat so much of anything, people tend to forget that I'm still a member of the Gourmet Research Society. I'm a tough critic when it comes to the quality of food.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Even in a gourmand challenge, where the focus is on the amount of food served, there's a baseline quality a dish should meet. And this mapo tofu...falls below the line.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): If someone plays dirty tricks with their food to make a quick buck...I'll punish them harshly.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): ...Thanks for the grub! And I'll have another bowl of mapo tofu, please.
- Chinese Restaurant Staff: (Th-This is impossible. Not even ten minutes have passed since she started, and she's done with her third bowl already?)
- Chinese Restaurant Staff: (Each gourmand order has ten servings of mapo tofu!)
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Hello...are you listening? One more bowl of gourmand mapo tofu, please!
- Chinese Restaurant Staff: Uh, um, ma'am...you already completed your challenge. You ate the full ten servings of mapo tofu... Shouldn't you consider stopping now?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Excuse me? What do you mean by that? Didn't you clearly state that the challenger can eat an unlimited amount of gourmand mapo tofu within the time limit?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): For a gourmand challenge to be fair for both parties, the initial rules must be strictly followed. Wouldn't you agree?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): And I didn't even complain about the extremely spicy sauce they poured all over the gourmand mapo tofu... Heeheehee.
- Chinese Restaurant Staff: I-I'm so sorry! I won't charge you two for the meal... Please forgive us!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): No! I came here to stuff myself with mapo tofu, not to listen to your apologies.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Bring me my next bowl of mapo tofu, please!
> Afterwards, Akari ate sixty servings of mapo tofu in half an hour, setting a new record...
