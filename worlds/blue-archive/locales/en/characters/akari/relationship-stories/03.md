> Kivotos Dining District
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Which restaurant should we go to today?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): My wallet's pretty light today, so I think I'll go to an all-you-can-eat restaurant and load up.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): For tonight's dinner...it's not all-you-can-eat without yakiniku, and it's not yakiniku if it isn't all-you-can-eat!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): It's a bit early, but I'm gonna go have some grilled short ribs.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Hello. I'd like one order of short ribs, please.
- Yakiniku Restaurant Staff: Welco—*gasp*
- Yakiniku Restaurant Staff: (Isn't that Wanibuchi Akari, the competitive eating champion?!)
- Yakiniku Restaurant Staff: (I heard that she devoured thirty servings of short ribs in an instant at the last event...)
- Yakiniku Restaurant Staff: (This is bad... If I let a customer like her in, our restaurant will go out of business for sure!)
- Yakiniku Restaurant Staff: Uh, um...I'm terribly sorry, ma'am, but we've run out of short ribs for today. We can't accept any more customers.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Really? But I just saw another customer walk in.
- Yakiniku Restaurant Staff: That customer was the last one we could let in... I'm so sorry!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Well, I guess there's nothing I can do if you ran out. Man, I was really hoping to have some yakiniku today.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): I should look for something else to eat...
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): All right, there should be an all-you-can-eat sushi joint nearby...
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Yakiniku is still my top priority...but I don't get to eat sushi so often these days.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Tonight's dinner shall be sushi!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Hello, one order of tuna sushi please!
- Sushi Restaurant Staff: (I-Is that Wanibuchi Akari, the competitive eating champion?!)
- Sushi Restaurant Staff: (This is bad! If I serve her, we'd need a whole tuna and then some to satisfy her appetite!)
- Sushi Restaurant Staff: I-I'm sorry! Our tables are fully booked for today. We can't accept any walk-in customers!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Really? No way...
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Well, if you're booked up, I guess that's that. I'll find another place...
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Ugh...I'm starving...
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): For some reason, every place I stopped by today was either out of food or fully booked.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): I guess that makes sense... But seriously, did the all-you-can-eat restaurants band together against me or something?
- "Why the long face?"
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Ah, Sensei. Are you here to grab dinner?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): I was planning to fill up at an all-you-can-eat place, but every place I went refused to serve me...
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): So I still haven't gotten to sit down at a restaurant and eat dinner.
- "Why don't you just go to a regular restaurant instead of an all-you-can-eat place?"
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Unfortunately, that means I won't be able to have a full meal considering how light my wallet is at the moment.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Urgh... I'm so hungry, I can hardly move... I should give up on finding an all-you-can-eat place and just get whatever I can from a regular restaurant.
- "If you don't mind conveyor sushi, I'll let you eat as much as you want."

- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): But you promised that you'll let me eat as much as I want... You really think you can handle it, Sensei?
