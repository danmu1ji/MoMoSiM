> Schale Sensei's Executive Office
> After lunch, Akari returned with a happier face than usual.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Hmm, hmm... Hmm... ♪
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Ah, today's lunch was delicious. I got to try a tasty new restaurant, and it put me in a great mood.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Did you eat bread for lunch again?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): That's not okay. You ought to fill up for lunch if you want to stay focused through the whole afternoon. You should eat something more nutritious than that.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Even if you don't want to, you ought to eat at a restaurant instead of a convenience store. I know some good places near Schale you can try!
- "Did you go to a ramen joint for lunch, Akari?"
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Eh? How did you know?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): That's right, I had a bowl of ramen for lunch. There's this new ramen restaurant that just opened in front of the Schale building.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): It says on the menu that 10 servings of the jumbo ramen are free if you finish them within 30 minutes. I ate two bowls in a row, and they were great!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): They served authentic tonkotsu ramen, with rich broth and plenty of toppings.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): ...By the way, how did you know that I ate ramen for lunch, Sensei?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Wait...did you smell garlic on me?
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Wow... I brushed my teeth as hard as I could, but I guess the garlic scent really clings to me.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Now that I think about it, there were three whole bulbs of garlic in the ramen, and I ate it all... No wonder I smell like garlic.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): I don't want to smell like garlic in front of you, but I don't want to eat tonkotsu ramen without garlic, either... Oh, what to do?
- "I don't care if you smell like garlic."
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): But this is a serious issue for me!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): I love eating all kinds of delicious foods, but I don't want you to remember me as that one student who smelled like garlic.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): I need to come up with a solution...
> The next morning, the pungent stench of garlic bombarded my nose as soon as I stepped into the Schale Executive Office...
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Welcome, Sensei! I've been waiting for you!
- "What's with this smell...?"
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): I looked up solutions on the internet yesterday, and apparently the human nose cannot distinguish a smell if it's exposed to a specific one for too long.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): So, my solution is this: I'll bombard you with garlic smell, so much so that you won't smell anything odd when I'm nearby!
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): All right, now. I've made some food with plenty of garlic in it for you, Sensei.
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): Garlic bread, garlic fried rice, garlic dumplings, garlic pickles...
- Akari (Gourmet Research Society): If we eat lots of garlic together, its scent will be nothing to us!
> Akari's food was great, but it made the Executive Office smell like garlic for four days straight...
