> After finishing some work at Valkyrie Police School, I stopped by the Public Safety Bureau's office to check on Fubuki.
> Public Safety Bureau Office
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): I'm telling you, that's not our responsibility.
- Little Girl: But you said come to the Public Safety Bureau when I have a problem.
- Little Girl: I know it may seem trivial, but this is very important to me!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): I get what you're saying, but this is kind of...
- Little Girl: Please, PLEASE help me out. There's only one hairpin like it in the world.
- Little Girl: My grandma made it for my birthday. It's irreplaceable!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Lost and found management IS a Public Safety Bureau job, but that's not the same as going out to look for a lost item.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Where did you lose the hairpin anyway?
- Little Girl: I swear it was in my hair when I left home...but it was gone by the time I arrived at school.
- Little Girl: It must have fallen out while I was on my way.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): I get you're broken up about this, but that's still too wide of a search area.
We can't go looking for something when you have no clue where it could be.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): We're shorthanded as it is. We don't have the manpower to be assigning cadets to things like this.
- Little Girl: You can't be serious! All you Public Safety Bureau students do is nap all day!
- Little Girl: And you're saying you don't have time to look for my hairpin? That's not fair!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Hmph. Okay, you got me there when it comes to the napping...but!
I'm serious about us not leaving the office for too long.
We have to stay on standby in case of an emergency.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): All I can promise is that we'll give you a call if something matching your hairpin's description turns up. Mind leaving your number?
- Little Girl: The police are so useless!
> Screen shakes
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): *sigh* Another scolding from a civilian.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Sensei? Have you been standing there this whole time?
- "I saw pretty much everything."
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Haha...ha. Well, this is embarrassing.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Don't worry. This kind of thing happens all the time when dealing with civil complaints.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): We get all kinds of requests to find lost items, open locked doors,
find missing dogs... The list goes on and on.
- "You can't help find the hairpin?"
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Maybe I could, if she knew exactly where she dropped it.
I don't have time to search her entire commute route.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): It would take all day to cover the span of an area like that.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Besides, it's a slippery slope. If we make an exception or two for requests like that, then we'll be drowning in similar requests when word gets out.
That's why it's better to turn these things down.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): ...
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Even I have to be a professional sometimes. The police aren't supposed to be swayed by emotional pleas like that!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): ...
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Oh, yeah. I just remembered that I'm out of donuts.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Think you can look after this place while I go out to get more?
I'll be back before my next shift. Just tell people whatever if they come.
- "I thought you were serious about being on standby?!"
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): K-Kirino or another student can take care of those things...
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Buying donuts is an important part of Public Safety Bureau work!
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Anyway, I'll be right back. I'm counting on you!
> Some time passed, but Fubuki didn't return before her next shift was supposed to start.
- Search for Fubuki.
> Near the D.U. Public Elementary School

- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Hmm. Nope, this is just a bottle opener.
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): This isn't it either.

- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): S-Sensei?! What are you doing here?
- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): Oh. You came looking for me when I didn't return in time for my shift.

- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): I'm sure it's around here somewhere, but I can't seem to find it.

- Fubuki (Public Safety Bureau): No, no. You don't have to do that. Because I...
