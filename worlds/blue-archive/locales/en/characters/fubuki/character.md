# Fubuki

## Description

A lazy student of the Community Safety Bureau of Valkyrie Police Academy. She enrolled in the Valkyrie Police Academy pursuing the carefree life of a civil servant. Treats any incoming complaints with utter indifference, and enjoys the life of a window-gazer who's not motivated by money nor honor. Her favorite thing to do is to listen to the radio while eating a doughnut.
